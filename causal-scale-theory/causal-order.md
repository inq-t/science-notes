# Causal Order and Metric Scale

Causal order determines which events can influence which others and, under standard regularity hypotheses, determines the conformal geometry of spacetime. It does not determine a metric calibration. Causal Scale Theory begins from this exact distinction and asks whether the missing scale can be represented by distinguishable state-space displacement.

Let $(M,g)$ be a time-oriented spacetime. Metrics related by a positive Weyl factor,

$$
\widehat g_{ab}=\Omega^2 g_{ab},
\qquad \Omega>0,
$$

have the same null cones. For distinguishing spacetimes with the usual causality assumptions, the chronological or causal order recovers the conformal class $[g]$ rather than a unique representative. This is the theorem-backed content behind the slogan

$$
\text{causal order}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad [g],
$$

not

$$
\text{causal order}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad g.
$$

## Scale as a section

In conformal geometry, a positive scale section $\sigma$ of weight one selects a representative metric. Relative to a fiducial representative $g$ one may write, conventionally,

$$
g^\sigma_{ab}=\sigma^{-2}g_{ab}.
$$

A simultaneous Weyl transformation of $g$ and $\sigma$ leaves $g^\sigma$ unchanged. Thus $\sigma$ is not an additional causal relation. It is the calibration that converts conformal comparisons into lengths, durations, curvatures, temperatures, and densities.

For a flat FLRW representative with $a_0=1$,

$$
\sigma=\frac1a,
\qquad
N:=\ln\frac a{a_0},
\qquad
x:=N-N_c
=-\ln\frac{\sigma}{\sigma_c}.
$$

The centered coordinate $x$ is a logarithmic ratio, so it is invariant under a common rescaling of $a$ and $a_c$. This ratio structure is what later permits the conditional affine soldering in [[causal-scale-theory/scale-soldering]].

## The programme's additional step

The reconstruction theorem does not say that scale is quantum information, modular flow, entropy, or gravity. That new physical bridge is owned by [[scale-as-state-principle|the observable-scale principle]]. “Identity of indiscernibles” first licenses a quotient by empirically empty differences; the quotient alone produces neither a state metric nor a conserved quantity.

## Boundary of the claim

- The causal reconstruction statement needs causality and regularity hypotheses; pathological spacetimes are not covered automatically.
- Conformal structure contains local angles and null cones, but not absolute intervals.
- Choosing $\sigma$ is not yet explaining why one scale is realized.
- CST does not replace local metric dynamics merely by redescribing the conformal split. [[causal-scale-theory/scale-tractor]] shows how the split interfaces with Einstein gravity; [[causal-scale-theory/closure-stack]] lists the extra premises needed for a cosmology.
