# State Coordinate, Comparison Datum, and Holonomy

The scalar $\theta$ is used both as a coordinate labeling states and as the coefficient obtained by comparing states across scales. Those roles coincide only when cross-fiber transport is path-consistent and its relevant holonomy is trivial or controlled.

A labeled family may be written

$$
N\longmapsto\omega_{\theta(N)}.
$$

A transported comparison is instead relational:

$$
(N_2,N_1)\longmapsto\Theta(N_2:N_1).
$$

To infer

$$
\Theta(N_2:N_1)=\theta(N_2)-\theta(N_1)
$$

one needs a composition rule and vanishing relevant holonomy. Otherwise, comparison around a loop can contain a connection-dependent term even when the endpoint labels agree.

[[causal-scale-theory/scale-soldering]] assumes the required path independence in order to prove logarithmic affinity. A wall construction would settle the issue by defining the transport maps, their gauge equivalences, and the curvature of the resulting state bundle.
