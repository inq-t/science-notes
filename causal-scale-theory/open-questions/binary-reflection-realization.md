# Realizing Binary Reflection in the Full Algebra

The reduced binary model may use an abstract reflection $J_{\mathrm{refl}}$, but its realization inside a local operator algebra is not settled. Tomita conjugation cannot flip the same noncentral generator inside a factor, so a completed wall must supply genuinely typed reflection data.

The reduced relation is

$$
J_{\mathrm{refl}}QJ_{\mathrm{refl}}^{-1}=-Q.
$$

If one instead sets $J_{\mathrm{refl}}=J^{\mathrm{TT}}$, Tomita--Takesaki theory gives

$$
J^{\mathrm{TT}}\mathcal AJ^{\mathrm{TT}}=\mathcal A'.
$$

For $Q\in\mathcal A$, the transformed operator lies in $\mathcal A'$. Equating it with $-Q\in\mathcal A$ forces a noncentral $Q$ into $\mathcal A\cap\mathcal A'$, which is impossible in a factor.

Two live routes remain:

1. construct a geometric reflection acting on the net or region data that is distinct from Tomita conjugation;
2. use a doubled standard-form realization with
   $$
   Q_L\in\mathcal A,
   \qquad
   Q_R\in\mathcal A',
   \qquad
   J^{\mathrm{TT}}Q_LJ^{\mathrm{TT}}=-Q_R.
   $$

Either route must also explain why the selected reference weights are balanced. Until then, [[causal-scale-theory/binary-geometry]] is exact only as reduced-channel algebra.
