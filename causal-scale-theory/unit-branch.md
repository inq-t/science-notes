# The Unit Branch

The unit branch evaluates the generalized CST background at $\nu=1$ and $\mathfrak R_c=1$, chooses the smallest positive flatness root, and sets the residual floor to zero. Its response profile and benchmark numbers are conditional outputs, not definitions of the general theory or independent evidence for the unit principles.

The two unit principles give

$$
\rho_X(x)
=\frac12\rho_{\mathrm{crit},c}\operatorname{sech}^2x,
$$

$$
w_X(x)
=-1+\frac23\tanh x,
$$

and

$$
9(1+w_X)^2+6w_X'=4.
$$

At $x=0$:

- the binary polarization vanishes;
- the normalized susceptibility and the positive density profile are maximal;
- separate conservation gives $w_X=-1$;
- unit amplitude gives $\rho_{X,c}=\rho_{\mathrm{ordinary},c}$.

These are not four independent predictions. The first two are binary geometry, the third follows from conservation at the stationary point, and the fourth is the cosmological meaning of $\mathfrak R_c=1$.

## Why acceleration ends although the response dominates

For $\nu=1$,

$$
w_X\to-\frac13,
\qquad
\rho_X\sim a^{-2}.
$$

At every finite late $x$,

$$
1+3w_X=-2+2\tanh x<0,
$$

but this factor approaches zero as $a^{-2}$. Consequently the negative response active mass

$$
\rho_X(1+3w_X)
$$

falls as $a^{-4}$, while positive matter active mass falls as $a^{-3}$. Matter therefore retakes control of the sign of $\rho+3p$ at a finite scale factor even though $\rho_X/\rho_m$ continues to grow. The expansion then approaches coasting from the decelerating side.

The numbers above show internal rigidity and supply a benchmark for regression testing. They do not constitute a full cosmological likelihood.
