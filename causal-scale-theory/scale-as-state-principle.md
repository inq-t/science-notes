# The Observable-Scale Principle

CST's central physical proposal is that a realized change of metric scale is represented by a distinguishable horizontal displacement through an admissible family of states. Causal reconstruction does not imply this map; it is the new bridge that turns scale calibration into a possible observable of the arena of facts.

The principle may be stated as

> A physically realized scale difference must have an invariant image in the distinguishability geometry of transported states.

Schematically,

$$
\frac{\sigma_2}{\sigma_1}
\longmapsto
\bigl[\omega_{N_2}:\omega_{N_1}\bigr]_{\mathrm{transported}}
\longmapsto
\text{state-space displacement}.
$$

This is **[PRINCIPLE — OBSERVABLE SCALE]**. The first ratio is conformal scale data; the middle object requires a common algebra or cross-fiber transport; the last object may be a relative entropy, BKM tangent norm, cocycle coefficient, or another invariant returned by the construction. The principle does not stipulate in advance which one succeeds.

## Why it is not contained in causal reconstruction

[[causal-scale-theory/causal-order]] establishes that causal order fixes a conformal class rather than a metric calibration. It does not say that the missing calibration is quantum state data. The identity of indiscernibles first gives a quotient by redundant descriptions; a quotient alone supplies neither a state metric nor a conservation law.

The principle is therefore a new categorical claim:

$$
\text{scale section in conformal geometry}
\stackrel{?}{\longleftrightarrow}
\text{horizontal direction in state space}.
$$

[[causal-scale-theory/modular-flow]] explains why the right-hand side is horizontal rather than the fixed-state modular automorphism group.

[[causal-scale-theory/state-variable-typing|The type of the horizontal variable]] then distinguishes a family label, a transported comparison datum, and a local spacetime field. The theory presently requires one of the first two; it has not derived the third.

## Explanatory criterion

The state family and transport must be specified independently of the cosmological history they are intended to explain. Otherwise one can reconstruct a coordinate from $H(N)$ and merely rename an effective fluid. [[no-gos/background-reconstruction-is-not-wall-construction|The reconstruction no-go]] and [[wall-construction-interface/elimination-test|the interface elimination test]] formalize this requirement.

## Upgrade and failure

The principle would be upgraded by an explicit wall construction that derives invariant scale-comparison data and whose local restriction preserves imported GR and QFT. It fails as an explanatory principle if no such transport exists, if all admissible displacements are gauge artifacts, or if the return values can only be defined by solving backward from the target background.

The more specific claims—binary reduction, affine soldering, unit width, and areal capacity—are downstream and can fail independently.
