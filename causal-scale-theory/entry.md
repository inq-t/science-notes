# Causal Scale Theory

Causal Scale Theory is a versionless, conditional programme for relating cosmological scale to distinguishable displacement through quantum state space. Its exact core separates causal order from metric calibration and derives the geometry of a balanced binary channel; its physical model then adds scale--state soldering, an extensive-capacity definition and unit principle, a constitutive source law, and a horizon normalization. Those additions produce a rigid homogeneous response family, but they do not derive local GR or QFT, construct the required wall state, or yet determine perturbations.

The organizing question is not whether an extra fluid can be fitted to an FLRW history. It is whether the otherwise-undetermined calibration of causal structure can be represented by an observable state-space displacement, and whether the residual capacity of that displacement can appear gravitationally. The programme is therefore a proposed theory of the **arena of scale-bearing facts**, with homogeneous cosmology as its first calculable sector.

## The ordered argument

[[causal-scale-theory/causal-order|Causal order]] fixes a conformal class, not a unique metric. A positive scale section chooses a representative, so the missing scale is a calibration of already-present causal relations rather than another causal relation. [[causal-scale-theory/scale-tractor|Scale-tractor geometry]] makes that split covariant and rewrites the trace-free Einstein equation as transport of the scale tractor. It does not eliminate the independent trace or vacuum channel. On flat FLRW backgrounds, [[causal-scale-theory/flrw-kinematics|the scale section]] also gives exact identities relating its convexity to acceleration and apparent-horizon motion.

[[scale-as-state-principle|The observable-scale principle]] is the new step: realized scale difference should have an invariant image as distinguishable displacement through states. [[causal-scale-theory/modular-flow|Vertical modular flow]] evolves observables relative to one fixed state, whereas the proposed cosmological motion is horizontal. Comparing states at different scales therefore requires an actual cross-fiber transport, not just a derivative symbol. The common construction obligation is owned by [[wall-construction-interface/entry|the wall-construction interface]] and remains open.

If that construction returns a reflection-odd binary channel with normalized generator $Q^2=\mathbf 1$ and satisfies [[balanced-channel-premise|the balanced-reference premise]], then [[causal-scale-theory/binary-geometry|binary information geometry]] is exact:

$$
\eta(\theta)=\tanh\theta,
\qquad
G^{\mathrm{BKM}}_{\theta\theta}=\operatorname{sech}^2\theta,
\qquad
\eta^2+G^{\mathrm{BKM}}_{\theta\theta}=1.
$$

The last identity is the rank-one Casimir balance: a fixed representation norm is divided between resolved polarization and residual susceptibility. It is the cleanest mathematical precursor of a causal-charge conservation law. Calling either term entropy, spacetime, or gravity would already be an additional identification.

Under ratio dependence, rank-one composition, negligible holonomy, and measurability, [[causal-scale-theory/scale-soldering|scale soldering]] is affine:

$$
x:=N-N_c=\ln\frac{a}{a_c}
=-\ln\frac{\sigma}{\sigma_c},
\qquad
\theta=\varrho_\perp x,
\qquad
\nu:=|\varrho_\perp|.
$$

The sign records orientation; the width $\nu$ is physical once $Q^2=\mathbf1$ fixes the binary normalization. [[width-principle|The unit choice $\nu=1$]] is a proposed principle, not a theorem about conformal weights.

The extensive horizontal norm is not fixed by the normalized binary curve. [[causal-scale-theory/scale-capacity|Entropy-normalized scale capacity]] introduces the coordinate-invariant peak ratio

$$
\mathfrak R_c
:=\frac{k_B}{S_c}G^\perp_{NN}(N_c)
$$

while [[unit-amplitude-principle|the unit-amplitude principle]] separately proposes $\mathfrak R_c=1$. [[relative-entropy-hessian|The local relative-entropy Hessian]] motivates [[causal-scale-theory/free-energy-source|the source law]], which identifies the all-history BKM profile with a homogeneous energy density. The extrapolation is constitutive; a fixed-reference free-energy difference does not derive it.

Four logically different horizon and vacuum statements must remain separate. [[causal-scale-theory/horizon-clock|The horizon clock]] is an exact contextual identity, not a premise of the pulse. The closure itself uses [[horizontal-temperature|an open horizontal-temperature identification]] and, given it, [[causal-scale-theory/hawking-friedmann|the Hawking--Friedmann conversion]] from horizon data to critical energy. [[vacuum-residual-sector|The residual sector]] then records that choosing zero residual vacuum is a global branch choice, not a consequence of local trace-free blindness.

With those premises exposed, [[causal-scale-theory/closure-stack|the closure stack]] yields the generalized response

$$
\boxed{

=\frac{\mathfrak R_c}{2}\rho_{\mathrm{crit},c}

Separate conservation then gives

$$

\qquad
9(1+w_X)^2+6\frac{\mathrm dw_X}{\mathrm dN}=4\nu^2.

These are conditional deductions. [[causal-scale-theory/generalized-background|The generalized background]] distinguishes the full $(\nu,\mathfrak R_c)$ family, its closure roots, and its future classes. [[unit-branch|The unit branch]] evaluates $\nu=\mathfrak R_c=1$ and records the old benchmark as a reproducible arithmetic example rather than as evidence for the principles.

The theory's other exact structures do not silently become cosmological dynamics. [[causal-scale-theory/witten-pair|The Witten--Darboux pair]] is an internal factorization of the binary profile, not a derived spacetime perturbation operator. [[no-go-register|The no-go register]] preserves calculations that exclude tempting but invalid derivations. Local GR and QFT enter through [[compatible-with-existing-physics/entry|the local-physics interface]]: CST may surround an imported local sector without reconstructing it, but it must preserve that sector wherever both descriptions apply.

The empirical programme is ordered by discriminating power in [[observables|observables and tests]], not by algebraic elegance. The most characteristic background predictions are the finite transition shape, its linked density and expansion histories, and the future asymptotic class. Growth becomes a prediction only after a covariant response sector is supplied. Several deeper proposals remain deliberately separable in [[causal-scale-theory/conjecture-ledger|the conjecture ledger]], while [[causal-scale-theory/quarantine|the quarantine]] holds live typing tensions that should not be resolved by rhetoric.

## Present standing

CST currently supplies a mathematically controlled **conditional homogeneous model** and a sharper research architecture. It does not yet supply the wall construction, a covariant response stress tensor with perturbations, a likelihood against full cosmological data, or a derivation of the unit laws. Failure of one of those bridges would localize the failure: it would not undo the conformal, tractor, binary, or FLRW identities that precede it.
