# The Unit-Amplitude Principle

The unit-amplitude principle proposes that, at the self-dual crossing, the squared BKM norm of the unit cosmological scale tangent equals the horizon entropy in natural information units. It fixes $\mathfrak R_c=1$ and, on the flat zero-residual background with no additional crossing sector, selects equality between the response and ordinary matter plus radiation.

The principle is

$$
\boxed{
\mathfrak R_c
=\frac{k_B}{S_c}G^\perp_{NN}(N_c)
=1.}
$$

Equivalently,

$$
G^\perp_{NN}(N_c)=\frac{S_c}{k_B}.
$$

This is a statement about the **local squared norm of the unit $N$-tangent**. Because the metric changes away from $N_c$, it is not a statement about the finite geodesic distance traversed in one e-fold.

After the constitutive and horizon bridges are granted,

$$
\Omega_{X,c}=\frac12.
$$

If the background is spatially flat, response then equals the total non-$X$ complement. It equals ordinary matter plus radiation only on the zero-residual branch with no additional crossing component. The equality is thus a consequence of unit amplitude within those premises, not an independent third coincidence.

## Independence from the width law

$\nu$ controls how quickly the normalized binary susceptibility changes with $N$. $\mathfrak R_c$ controls the peak extensive norm. Neither fixes the other:

$$
\text{width}\quad\nu
\qquad\perp\qquad
\text{amplitude}\quad\mathfrak R_c.
$$

That separation is why the generalized canon carries both parameters and [[unit-branch]] applies both unit principles only at the end.

## Upgrade and failure

The principle would be upgraded by a microscopic derivation of a renormalized wall BKM measure whose Radon--Nikodym density with respect to causal area gives precisely the entropy density at the crossing. It fails if an independently constructed wall returns a finite, scheme-stable value $\mathfrak R_c\ne1$.

Fitting a background amplitude near one would support the effective branch but would not by itself establish the microscopic identity, because the source and horizon identifications remain separate premises.
