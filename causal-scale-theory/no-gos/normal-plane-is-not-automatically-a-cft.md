# A Lorentzian Normal Plane Is Not Automatically a Two-Dimensional CFT

The two-dimensional Lorentzian normal plane of a codimension-two surface does not by itself define a local $1+1$-dimensional conformal field theory. Cardy or Virasoro formulas therefore cannot be imported solely from the dimension and signature of that plane.

A normal bundle supplies local boost geometry. A two-dimensional CFT additionally requires, among other data, a field algebra, locality, a stress tensor, conformal symmetry, representations, boundary conditions, and a controlled central charge.

The implication

$$
\text{Lorentzian normal plane}
\Longrightarrow
\text{two-dimensional CFT}
$$

is therefore invalid. Consequently,

$$
\text{normal-plane geometry}
\not\vdash
\text{Cardy state count or unit CST amplitude}.
$$

A genuine chiral or conformal edge theory may still exist in a completed wall construction. If so, it must be constructed and its anomaly and central-charge data calculated. [[causal-scale-theory/conjectures/boost-charge-capacity|The boost-charge route]] records that stronger possibility without using the invalid shortcut.
