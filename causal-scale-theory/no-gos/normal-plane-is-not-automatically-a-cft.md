# A Lorentzian Normal Plane Is Not Automatically a Two-Dimensional CFT

The two-dimensional Lorentzian normal plane of a codimension-two cut supplies boost geometry, not a \(1+1\)-dimensional conformal quantum field theory. Its dimension and signature alone therefore cannot justify Virasoro or Cardy formulas, an entropy count, or the unit capacity ratio.

The invalid implication is

$$
\boxed{
\text{Lorentzian normal plane}
\not\Longrightarrow
\text{1+1-dimensional conformal thermal sector}.}
$$

A conformal field theory additionally requires a constructed operator algebra, locality, stress tensor, conformal action, positive-energy representation, state or ensemble, boundary conditions, central extension, and a controlled thermodynamic regime. [[wall-construction-interface/binary-channel|The binary-channel obligation]] also explains why two null orientations do not make the microscopic wall Hilbert space two-dimensional.

A genuine horizon algebra remains possible, but it must be built. [[deriving-value-of-g/horizon-algebra-capacity|The horizon-algebra conjecture]] states the constructive route and its failure conditions; [[deriving-value-of-g/noether-capacity-theorem|the Noether--capacity theorem]] obtains a unit ratio only after an actual \(1+1\) conformal thermal sector and the required tangent and entropy identifications have been supplied.
