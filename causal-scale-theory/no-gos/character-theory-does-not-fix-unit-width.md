# Character Theory Does Not Fix Unit Width

Measurable scale characters imply logarithmic affinity but leave a continuous slope. Neither real conformal weights nor the multiplicative Cauchy equation quantize the CST width to one.

The conditional soldering theorem gives

$$
\Theta(r_1r_2)=\Theta(r_1)+\Theta(r_2)
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\Theta(r)=\varrho_\perp\ln r
$$

under measurability or comparable regularity.

But

$$
\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathrm{meas}}(\mathbb R_+,\mathbb R)
\simeq\mathbb R,
$$

so every real $\varrho_\perp$ defines a character. The theorem fixes the logarithmic form, not a primitive integer generator.

Likewise, conformal densities can carry real weights. Their existence does not supply an integrality theorem for the horizontal state character.

Thus

$$
\boxed{
\text{affine scale soldering}
\not\vdash
\nu=1.}
$$

The unit value belongs only to [[width-principle|the unit-width principle]] unless a stronger representation-theoretic selection theorem is constructed.
