# Modular Rescaling Is Not the Binary Tangent

Rescaling the modular Hamiltonian of the balanced binary state produces a tangent norm different from the BKM norm of translation in the binary exponential coordinate. The calculation excludes their direct identification.

For

$$
\rho_\theta=\frac{e^{\theta Q}}{2\cosh\theta},
$$

the centered modular Hamiltonian is proportional to $\theta(Q-\langle Q\rangle)$. Hence

$$
\operatorname{Var}_{\rho_\theta}(-\ln\rho_\theta)
=\theta^2\operatorname{sech}^2\theta.
$$

The binary translation tangent instead has

$$
G^{\mathrm{BKM}}_{\theta\theta}
=\operatorname{sech}^2\theta.
$$

At the self-dual point the first quantity vanishes while the second equals one. No constant normalization can identify them along the whole curve.

Therefore

$$
\boxed{
\text{binary horizontal displacement}
\ne
\text{temperature rescaling of one fixed modular Hamiltonian}.}
$$

The result does not exclude a more elaborate cross-fiber cocycle whose transported generator has a $Q$ component. It says that [[causal-scale-theory/modular-flow|vertical modular rescaling]] cannot simply be renamed as the required horizontal scale tangent.
