# Conjecture: One Isometry Underlies the Unit Laws

The two unit principles may be shadows of one isometry between physical scale translation and the entropy-normalized horizontal BKM metric at the self-dual wall. Such an isometry would derive unit amplitude directly and could constrain unit width only after the binary generator is independently normalized.

Let $\Phi$ denote the proposed map from the cosmological scale line into transported wall states. Seek

$$
\boxed{
\left.\Phi^*G^{\mathrm{BKM}}\right|_c
=\frac{S_c}{k_B}\,\mathrm dN^2.}
$$

This equation immediately gives

$$
\mathfrak R_c
=\frac{k_B}{S_c}G^perp_{NN}(N_c)
=1.
$$

It does not by itself give $\nu=1$. That conclusion additionally requires the horizontal tangent to lie in the fundamental binary representation with $Q^2=\mathbf1$ and the exponential coordinate conjugate to that $Q$.

## Upgrade criterion

Derive the pullback metric from an independently constructed wall transport, prove its extensive normalization and renormalization are canonical, and show that the physical scale action uses the fundamental binary generator.

## Failure criterion

The conjecture fails if the calculated peak norm differs from $S_c/k_B$, if the result is regulator dependent, or if unit amplitude and unit width arise from inequivalent structures. It must not be rescued by redefining $N$, $Q$, or the metric after the calculation.
