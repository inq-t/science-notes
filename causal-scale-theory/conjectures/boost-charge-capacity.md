# Conjecture: Local Boost Charge Fixes Capacity

Local causal horizons possess a canonical boost structure, and modular theory supplies a $2\pi$-normalized generator in suitable wedge-like settings. CST conjectures that an independently normalized boost susceptibility could fix the entropy-normalized wall capacity and possibly the unit amplitude.

The proposed route is schematic:

$$
\text{geometric boost generator}
\longleftrightarrow
\text{modular generator}
\longrightarrow
\text{BKM susceptibility}
\longleftrightarrow
\text{horizon area density}.
$$

Its attraction is that the same local boost normalization appears in the canonical temperature of [[horizontal-temperature]] and in horizon entropy. But equality of normalizations is not equality of physical generators.

## Upgrade criterion

A successful construction must specify a local algebra and state, calculate the transported horizontal generator, prove its relation to geometric boosts, control edge terms and anomalies, and derive the extensive capacity without inserting the Bekenstein--Hawking coefficient as an input.

Half-sided modular inclusions or affine-group relations may provide useful structure, but their usual modular parameter is vertical. The missing theorem must connect that structure to horizontal cosmological state comparison.

## Failure criterion

The route fails if the boost susceptibility is state-, regulator-, or region-dependent in a way that cannot yield the proposed universal peak ratio, or if it fixes only a dimensionless ratio while leaving the area density entirely free. The invalid shortcut from normal-plane dimension to a CFT is excluded in [[causal-scale-theory/no-gos/normal-plane-is-not-automatically-a-cft]].
