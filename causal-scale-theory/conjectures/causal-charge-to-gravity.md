# Conjecture: Causal Discernibility Is Represented Gravitationally

The strongest candidate conservation concept in CST is the fixed binary representation norm $1=\eta^2+G$. The conjecture is that the residual BKM capacity $G$, after a covariant extensive completion, is represented physically as gravitational response rather than as a second independently conserved substance.

The exact reduced identity is

$$
1
=\underbrace{\eta^2}_{\text{resolved polarization}}
+\underbrace{G^{\mathrm{BKM}}_{\theta\theta}}_{\text{residual susceptibility}}.
$$

Under affine soldering, fixed extensive normalization, and the constitutive source law,

$$
\frac{\rho_X(N)}{\rho_{X,c}}
=\operatorname{sech}^2(\nu x),
$$

so the same normalized identity becomes

$$
\boxed{
\eta^2+\frac{\rho_X}{\rho_{X,c}}=1.}
$$

This equation is a conditional re-expression of the binary Casimir. It is not ordinary energy conservation: the two terms are dimensionless, and $\rho_X$ still obeys its own background continuity equation.

## Proposed meaning

The state may acquire a discernible orientation while the representation invariant remains fixed. The capacity **remaining as residual susceptibility at that state** is proposed to have a geometric image in the scale response of spacetime; as polarization grows, both that susceptibility and the modeled response shrink. On this reading,

$$
\text{gravitational response}
=\text{constitutive image of residual discernibility capacity},
$$

not an extra addend inserted beside that capacity.

This is the typed version of the poetic intuition that facthood has a geometric price. [[conservation-of-causal-charge/entry|The conservation-of-causal-charge module]] develops the wider philosophical interpretation.

## Upgrade criterion

An upgrade requires a covariant construction that:

1. generalizes the rank-one Casimir to local wall patches or a larger representation;
2. produces a positive, additive or appropriately local BKM measure;
3. maps that measure to a conserved gravitational source independently of the solved cosmology;
4. recovers the homogeneous pulse as a restriction.

For generators $T_a$ in a representation with quadratic Casimir

$$
\kappa^{ab}T_aT_b=c_R\mathbf1,
$$

the desired extension would resemble

$$
c_R
=\kappa^{ab}\langle T_a\rangle\langle T_b\rangle
+\text{positive susceptibility norm}.
$$

For noncommuting or noncompact groups, ordinary covariance, the BKM form, and positivity need not coincide; proving the correct replacement is part of the conjecture.

## Failure criterion

The conjecture fails if a constructed wall produces the binary balance but no covariant geometric source, if the extensive capacity is not localizable, or if the required source violates local conservation or established gravitational phenomenology.
