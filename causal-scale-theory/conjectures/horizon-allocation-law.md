# Conjecture: Horizon Allocation Is a Physical Exchange Law

The exact FLRW identity $\mathrm dN=\mathrm d\widehat\eta_A+\tfrac14\mathrm d\ln S_A$ may be the homogeneous shadow of a deeper law splitting scale transport between reversible modular motion and growth of observable capacity. At present the two terms are reconstructed from one background and do not constitute independently established conserved charges.

The exact starting point is

$$
\mathrm dN
=\mathrm d\widehat\eta_A
+\frac14\mathrm d\ln S_A.
$$

The conjectural strengthening is that a constructed horizontal connection admits a decomposition

$$
\text{scale transport}
=\text{reversible state transport}
+\text{capacity growth}
$$

whose flat-FLRW restriction is the displayed equation.

## Upgrade criterion

One must independently construct the wall state path and identify:

- a signed generator whose homogeneous value is $\widehat\mu_A$;
- an entropy or capacity variation whose geometric value is $\tfrac14d\ln S_A$;
- a closed law or Ward identity relating them before solving for $H(N)$.

The result must also explain whether the binary coordinate $\theta$ is identical to, projects onto, or remains distinct from horizon rapidity.

## Failure criterion

The conjecture is reduced to a useful kinematic mnemonic if both terms can only be reconstructed from the same solved $H(N)$, or if a wall calculation gives a different decomposition. The exact identity in [[causal-scale-theory/horizon-clock]] would remain true.
