# Conjecture: The Infrared Wall Channel Is Balanced and Binary

Homogeneity and reflection of the two null-normal orientations may make the infrared reflection-odd wall response a balanced two-outcome sufficient statistic even when the microscopic local algebra is type III. This would explain why the reduced binary geometry is universal rather than merely convenient.

The proposed descent is

$$
(\mathcal A_N,\omega_N)
\longrightarrow
\mathcal E_N(\mathcal A_N)
=\operatorname{span}\{\mathbf1,Q\},
\qquad
Q^2=\mathbf1,
$$

with equal reference weights in the $Q=\pm1$ sectors. Once this descent is granted, [[causal-scale-theory/binary-geometry|the binary geometry]] is exact. The conjecture concerns the descent, not the algebra afterward.

## Upgrade criterion

Construct a canonical conditional expectation, sufficient channel, or infrared quotient onto the $Q$ sector; show that geometric reflection enforces [[balanced-channel-premise|balanced weights]]; and bound corrections from every discarded reflection-odd mode.

## Failure criterion

The conjecture fails if additional reflection-odd modes remain unsuppressed, if no canonical channel exists, if the reference is intrinsically unbalanced, or if the reduced BKM form is not the binary susceptibility.

The full-algebra typing obstruction is isolated in [[causal-scale-theory/open-questions/binary-reflection-realization|the reflection-realization question]] and [[wall-construction-interface/binary-channel|the shared binary-channel obligation]].
