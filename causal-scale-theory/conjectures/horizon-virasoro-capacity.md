# Conjecture: A Genuine Horizon Algebra Fixes Capacity

A dynamically realized near-horizon conformal or Virasoro algebra could place the horizontal wall sector in a regime where entropy and capacity follow from one partition function. This route is meaningful only if the algebra is constructed; the two-dimensional normal plane alone is not a CFT.

The hoped-for chain is

$$
\text{wall modes and boundary conditions}
\longrightarrow
(c,L_0,\text{ensemble})
\longrightarrow
S_c\ \text{and}\ G^perp_{NN}(N_c)
\longrightarrow
\mathfrak R_c.
$$

## Upgrade criterion

Construct the mode algebra, Hamiltonian, central extension, state or ensemble, boundary conditions, and valid asymptotic regime for the dynamical wall. Compute entropy and horizontal susceptibility from the same partition function and recover a finite, scheme-stable capacity ratio.

## Failure criterion

The route fails if no such algebra exists, if the relevant state is outside the Cardy regime, if the horizontal generator is not among its charges, or if the calculated capacity differs from the proposed unit value.

The invalid dimensional shortcut is permanently excluded in [[causal-scale-theory/no-gos/normal-plane-is-not-automatically-a-cft|the normal-plane no-go]]. [[causal-scale-theory/conjectures/boost-charge-capacity|The boost-charge route]] is related but does not require a Virasoro realization.
