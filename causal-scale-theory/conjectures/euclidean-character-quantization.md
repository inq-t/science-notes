# Conjecture: Euclidean Wall Periodicity Quantizes the Scale Character

Euclidean continuation of a normal boost may produce a genuine $2\pi$ angular action whose single-valued representations discretize a wall character. This is a serious possible route to unit width, but ordinary real scale characters and conformal weights do not supply the quantization.

The temptation is to relate

$$
\theta=\nu N
$$

to a Euclidean angular generator with integral or half-integral monodromy. The categorical obstruction is that angular periodicity naturally constrains a **vertical** rotation generator, whereas $\nu=\mathrm d\theta/\mathrm dN$ is a **horizontal** scale-to-state slope. No equality between those generators is presently known.

## Upgrade criterion

Construct the Euclidean wall and its state bundle, show that horizontal transport extends consistently around the angular circle, identify its monodromy representation, and derive the map from angular charge to the canonical $Q^2=\mathbf1$ scale character.

## Failure criterion

The route fails if quantization applies only to vertical modular rotation, if the horizontal connection has unrelated holonomy, or if admissible wall states realize a continuous family of slopes.

[[causal-scale-theory/no-gos/character-theory-does-not-fix-unit-width|The character-theory no-go]] remains the baseline: without this additional Euclidean construction, $\nu$ is continuous.
