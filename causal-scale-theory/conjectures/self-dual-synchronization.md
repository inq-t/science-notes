# Conjecture: Wall Dynamics Synchronizes the Self-Dual Event

Binary self-duality, maximum normalized susceptibility, a stationary response density, $w_X=-1$, capacity saturation, and equal cosmological partition currently meet through several independent premises. CST conjectures that a completed wall dynamics makes them one physical event rather than a coordinated choice of definitions and unit laws.

The exact binary statements are

$$
\theta=0
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\eta=0
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
G^{\mathrm{BKM}}_{\theta\theta}=1.
$$

Affine soldering dates this event by $N=N_c$. The density maximum and $w_X=-1$ require the fixed extensive profile plus separate conservation. Equal partition additionally requires unit amplitude, flatness, zero residual, and declared matter content. Those are presently different edges.

## Upgrade criterion

Derive a wall evolution or variational principle for which the reflected-state self-dual cut is also the extensive-capacity saturation cut, and show that the gravitational constraint enforces the corresponding partition without separately imposing $\mathfrak R_c=1$.

## Failure criterion

The conjecture fails if an explicit wall places self-duality, peak extensive capacity, density stationarity, or gravitational equality at distinct cuts. Such a result would preserve the binary identities while dissolving the claimed cosmic coincidence.
