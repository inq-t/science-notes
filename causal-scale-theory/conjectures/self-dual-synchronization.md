# Conjecture: Wall Dynamics Grounds the Coincident Crossing

The balanced binary fixed point, maximum normalized susceptibility, maximum extensive capacity, stationary homogeneous density, \(w_X=-1\), weak unit matching, and equal cosmological partition are not presently one theorem. CST conjectures that an independently constructed wall dynamics derives the premises that place these events on one cut, rather than coordinating them by definition and separate unit assumptions.

Within the granted balanced binary family, [[binary-information-geometry/balanced-exponential-family|the exact theorem]] gives

$$
\theta=0
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
m(\theta)=0
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
g^{\mathrm{bin}}_{\theta\theta}=1.
$$

Everything after that equivalence uses another bridge. [[basic-concepts/soldering/affine-scale-state|Affine scale--state comparison]] places the binary center at \(N=N_c\) only under its transport and rank-one hypotheses. The extensive capacity peaks there only if its tangent normalization and channel factor do not shift the peak. [[causal-scale-theory/theorems/rigid-sech-response-identities|The rigid-response theorem]] then gives the density maximum and \(w_X(N_c)=-1\) only after the constitutive pulse and separate conservation have been assumed. Finally, [[causal-scale-theory/theorems/dimensional-crossing-partition|the crossing-partition theorem]] obtains equal partition only after horizon conversion, flatness, the weak principle \(\mathfrak R_c=1\), and restrictions on the complementary sector.

The conjecture is therefore not that these statements are already equivalent. It has two gates. **Mathematical synchronization** constructs all event loci over one wall or scale moduli object and proves that their intersection or equalizer is one selected cut. **Physical synchronization** proves that this same cut realizes gravitational matching, \(w_X=-1\), sector partition, and an independently normalized \(\mathfrak R_c=1\). Category theory can formulate the first gate only after the wall object exists; it cannot infer the second. The full conjecture is that the wall action, constraint system, or variational principle supplies the missing balance, transport, extensive normalization, state--geometry matching, and global-sector selection as consequences of one construction.

## Upgrade criterion

Construct the wall independently of the target FLRW history and prove that its reflected balanced state selects a cut \(\Sigma_c\), that the physical horizontal capacity is extremal there, that the covariant gravitational response reduces to the rigid homogeneous source there, and that its constraint equations imply the required integrated matching and sector partition without inserting \(\mathfrak R_c=1\) as normalization data.

## Failure criterion

The conjecture fails if a valid wall construction places binary self-duality, peak extensive capacity, density stationarity, or gravitational matching at distinct cuts, or returns a nonunit \(\mathfrak R_c\) while satisfying the other conditions. Such a result would preserve the exact binary fixed point and whichever conditional background theorems still apply; it would refute only their proposed dynamical synchronization.
