# Conjecture Ledger

CST's bold extensions are kept as individually composable conjectures with explicit upgrade and failure criteria. None is needed to establish the earlier conformal, FLRW, binary, or Darboux identities; each would strengthen the physical interpretation or microscopic closure.

[[conjectures/causal-charge-to-gravity|Causal discernibility is represented gravitationally]] proposes that residual BKM susceptibility has gravitational response as its physical image.

[[conjectures/areal-capacity-measure|BKM capacity has an areal measure]] makes the “spatial residue” idea precise as a measure-density theorem and a possible route to $G$.

[[conjectures/horizon-allocation-law|Horizon allocation is a physical exchange law]] asks whether the exact FLRW clock identity descends from one independently constructed state-space law.

[[conjectures/covariant-response-sector|A covariant response sector descends from the wall]] is the gate between the homogeneous background and physical perturbations.

[[conjectures/boost-charge-capacity|Local boost charge fixes capacity]] explores whether canonical modular and geometric boost normalization selects the extensive wall response.

The unit-width and unit-amplitude proposals are owned separately by [[width-principle]] and [[unit-amplitude-principle]]. The zero-residual choice is a sector question in [[vacuum-residual-sector]], not another unit law.
