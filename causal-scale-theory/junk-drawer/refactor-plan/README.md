# Refactor-Plan Archive

This directory preserves the AI-authored inventory, salvage ledger, receipt proposal, quarantine, and migration plan that preceded the reviewed synthesis. They are historical process documents rather than current theory nodes; [[causal-scale-theory/junk-drawer/refactor-plan/refactor-audit|the synthesis audit]] records which recommendations were adopted, corrected, or rejected, and [[causal-scale-theory/junk-drawer/refactor-plan/retirement-audit|the retirement audit]] records the final provenance disposition.

The files remain useful for tracing discarded routes and proposed migrations. Their universal wording about a $\nu=2$ existence bound is superseded, and their migration commands are not live instructions. Retirement was performed under the narrower preservation rules in the retirement audit: exact legacy trees remain local, while active ownership passes to the versionless canon.
