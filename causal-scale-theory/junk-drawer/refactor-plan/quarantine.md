# Quarantine Register

These are tensions that are neither resolved nor dismissible. Each is load-bearing somewhere in the argument, and each has at least two incompatible readings alive in the archive.

Quarantine is not the junk drawer. Junk-drawer material is dead and kept for provenance; quarantined material is live and blocking, and every entry below carries what would resolve it and what would kill it. An entry leaves by being answered, not by being restated more confidently. Entries that graduate into a real answer should become their own notes; entries that die should be recorded as dead in [[salvage-ledger|the salvage ledger]]'s do-not-recover section, so they are not rediscovered.

## Q1 — Which reflection realizes the binary channel

The archive asserts three things that cannot all hold: that $J$ in the relation $JQJ=-Q$ is the Tomita conjugation; that $Q$ is noncentral; and that the algebra is a factor. Together they force $Q\in\mathcal A\cap\mathcal A'=\mathbb C\mathbf 1$.

[[scale-as-modular-observable/misc/scale-as-a-modular-observable|Revision 2]] asserts all three explicitly, justifying the relation on the grounds that modular conjugation exchanges the two null directions. [[causal-scale-theory/sources/legacy/causal-scale-master/binary-geometry|v7's binary-geometry]] carries the same conflation into an exact-algebra note. [[causal-scale-theory/sources/legacy/causal-scale-master-v8/wall-state-construction|v8]] identifies the obstruction and offers the doubled alternative $Q_L\in\mathcal A$, $Q_R\in\mathcal A'$ with $J^{\rm TT}Q_LJ^{\rm TT}=-Q_R$, but does not settle which is intended. The argument and both realizations are now stated in [[wall-construction-interface/binary-channel|the binary channel obligation]].

**Why it blocks.** The two readings give different meanings to self-duality. Under a purely geometric reflection, $\theta=0$ is a distinguished point of the reduced family. Under the doubled realization, it is a statement about a left–right pair, and what "the state at the crossing" means changes with it.

**Resolves if:** the wall construction exhibits $Q$ and its reflection explicitly, and one realization follows. **Dies if:** no reflection-odd generator survives the reduction at all, in which case the binary channel goes with it.

## Q2 — What type of object the state coordinate is

Two live readings. Revision 2 §23 types it as a collective constitutive coordinate — the same type as inverse temperature or an order parameter — a state *label*. [[wall-construction-interface/cross-fiber-transport|The transport note]] types it as the noncentral coefficient of a relative modular comparison — a *comparison datum* between two fibers.

These need not be the same object. A state label is intrinsic to one state; a comparison coefficient is a function of a pair, and becomes a label only after a base point and a path are fixed. The soldering law $\theta=\nu x$ silently supplies both.

**Why it blocks.** The constitutive source law treats the coordinate as a label, integrating a local Hessian along a path with fixed crossing data. If it is really a comparison datum, the "all-history" extension is not an extension of a function but a choice of base point, and [[scale-as-modular-observable/claim-audit|the claim audit's]] free-energy counterexample is the first symptom of the confusion rather than an isolated slip.

**Resolves if:** the transport is shown to be flat in the relevant sector, so that comparison-to-a-base-point *is* a label, with holonomy bounded. **Dies if:** holonomy is essential, in which case there is no scalar $\theta(N)$ and the profile must be rebuilt from a path-dependent object. [[cosmodynamics/registers-and-type-discipline|Type discipline]] is the right frame for the question.

## Q3 — Why the horizontal channel uses the boost-normalized temperature

The source law uses $k_BT_{\rm hor}=\hbar c/(2\pi R_A)$. The dynamical horizon's own temperature is $T_{\rm KH}=\mu_A T_{\rm hor}$, and these differ at a generic FLRW horizon. [[causal-scale-theory/sources/legacy/causal-scale-master-v8/horizon-and-vacuum|v8]] tags the choice open and keeps the two symbols apart, which is correct as far as it goes.

What makes this a tension rather than a gap: Revision 2 §17 *argues for* the horizontal normalization on the grounds that the alternative yields an amplitude excluded by data. That is selecting a structural ingredient by the target observable — the failure mode [[wall-construction-interface/elimination-test|Test I]] exists to forbid. So the archive contains both an open question and an answer obtained the wrong way, and the answer happens to be the one in use.

**Resolves if:** the horizontal modular temperature is derived for a dynamical wall from the construction. **Dies if:** the derivation returns $T_{\rm KH}$ or another running normalization, which changes the amplitude and falsifies the principle as stated. Either way the data-based argument must not be cited.

## Q4 — Whether the amplitude has a tractor reading

[[salvage-ledger|The salvage ledger]] records an orphaned proposal: because $w_X=-1$ exactly at the crossing, the trace-free stress vanishes there, and at unit amplitude the energy budget splits one-to-one between the tractor-parallel and tractor-source sectors. If sound, this reads the amplitude law off [[causal-scale-theory/sources/legacy/causal-scale-master/scale-tractor|tractor geometry]] rather than off horizon capacity.

**Why it is quarantined rather than salvaged.** It would be a second, independent route to the same number, which is either a real bridge or a coincidence of the unit branch — and the archive's history with independent-looking confirmations of $\operatorname{sech}^2$ is a reason for caution. It also rests on a citation whose description [[scale-as-modular-observable/claim-audit|the claim audit]] flags as mismatched, so its literature basis is unverified. And it exists only inside a receipt script, never having been reviewed as prose.

**Resolves if:** the split is derived for general $\mathfrak R_c$ and the one-to-one point is shown to *be* the unit value rather than to coincide with it at the benchmark. **Dies if:** the relation holds only at $\mathfrak R_c=1$ by construction, in which case it is a restatement.

## Q5 — Whether the crossing is an intrinsic event or solution data

Revision 2 §18 holds that the crossing epoch is fixed intrinsically, as the unique minimum of a modular functional. [[causal-scale-theory/sources/legacy/causal-scale-master-v8/generalized-background|v8]] holds that it is solution data fixed by present flatness, not a new universal constant, and further that the closure equation may have several roots so a branch must be declared. [[scale-as-modular-observable/claim-audit|The claim audit]] gives the reconciliation for the unit branch: the functional identifies $\theta=0$, and the *cosmic date* of that event follows only after the amplitude law, flatness, present abundances, and a branch choice.

That reconciliation is right and probably ends the dispute as stated. What survives is sharper and unresolved: the theory wants the state-space event and the cosmological event to be the same event, and nothing yet explains why the wall should reach capacity saturation exactly at self-duality. [[causal-scale-theory/sources/legacy/causal-scale-master-v8/conjecture-ledger|The conjecture ledger]] carries this as a conjecture, which is the right status; it is repeated here because it is the conceptual core of the coincidence the theory advertises, and a reader of the background notes alone would not see that it is open.

**Resolves if:** wall dynamics place the minimum of the reflected divergence at the capacity-saturation event, with the gravitational constraint enforcing equal partition. **Dies if:** self-duality, peak capacity, and gravitational equality fall at three different cuts.

## Q6 — Whether the three rank-one reductions are one reduction

Three distinct claims travel under one name across the two programmes: one noncentral horizontal generator; one common material clock shared by all species; one spin-zero form factor in the stress two-point decomposition. [[causal-wall-spectral-theory/causal-scale-interface|The spectral interface note]] states plainly that none implies the other two.

Only the first is an obligation of [[wall-construction-interface/entry|the shared interface]]. The second and third are consumer-side — the second belongs to [[causal-wall-spectral-theory/cosmological-descent|cosmological descent]], the third to [[causal-wall-spectral-theory/spectral-dictionary|the spectral dictionary]]. This module inherits the tension only through its own use of the first.

**Why it stays listed here.** If a single microscopic reduction did explain all three, that would be a substantive unification claim and it would belong to both programmes at once — which means neither will naturally own it. Recording it here keeps it from falling between them.

**Resolves if:** a construction derives all three from one channel with controlled errors. **Dies if:** the reductions are shown independent, in which case each needs its own justification and the shared vocabulary should be retired.

## Not quarantined

Three things that look like tensions and are not, recorded so they are not re-litigated.

**The shape invariant's testability.** Not a tension but a settled power result: the structural argument in [[salvage-ledger|the salvage ledger]] shows background data cannot constrain the shape exponent, and the fix is to attach a power statement to the test rather than to argue about it. It becomes a defect only if a test ledger keeps ranking it first.

**The two invalid closure arguments.** Settled and dead. Conformal weights are real, and a two-dimensional normal plane is not a conformal field theory. [[scale-as-modular-observable/claim-audit|The claim audit]] is the disposition of record; [[causal-scale-theory/sources/legacy/causal-scale-master-v8/conjecture-ledger|the conjecture ledger]] keeps the serious remnants — Euclidean character quantization, a genuine near-horizon algebra — as routes with kill conditions, which is where they belong.

**The width ceiling.** Settled: an artifact of dropping radiation from the evolving density, with the true existence bound at $\nu=2$ and independent of the matter fraction. The correction is recorded in [[scale-as-modular-observable/claim-audit|the claim audit]] and the corrected atlas is in [[causal-scale-theory/sources/legacy/causal-scale-master-v8/generalized-background|generalized-background]]. What remains is a branch prior, declared, not a tension.
