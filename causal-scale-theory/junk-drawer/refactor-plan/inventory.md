# Inventory of Master Documents

Five generations of master document exist, plus two distilled libraries and one autopsy layer. This note records what each is uniquely authoritative for and what is wrong with it, because the consolidation cannot be done by taking the newest of everything: on at least three axes an earlier document is the more reliable one, and two arguments survive only in the earliest.

Ordering below is by logical generation, which for the chat material is not the order the filesystem suggests.

## G1 — Attempt at Closure

[[scale-as-modular-observable/chats/01/attachments/attempt-at-closure|The attachment to chat 01]].

**Uniquely authoritative for:** the honest statement of the gap. It builds the durable structure — the separation of the binary score variance from the full entanglement capacity, the modular free-energy source functional, the chosen-temperature horizon conversion, the radiation-corrected crossing ratio $1/(1-2\Omega_{r,c})=1.000395$ — and then names the four-clause proof obligation it has *not* discharged, treating capacity saturation and capacity-equals-entropy as an Einstein-capacity conjecture rather than a theorem.

**Defects:** superseded arithmetic; a jerk sign error corrected downstream.

**Verdict:** the epistemically cleanest document in the archive. It contains no ceiling argument, no integrality claim, and no Cardy argument, because it had not yet reached for them.

## G2 — The forced closure

[[scale-as-modular-observable/chats/02/outputs/the_closure|the_closure]] and [[scale-as-modular-observable/chats/02/outputs/rubles_equations|rubles_equations]].

**Uniquely authoritative for:** the impossibility argument for a dimensionful constant, and the framing of the amplitude as a measured constant of nature rather than a derived one — the fallback position that survives if the capacity chain fails. Also the momentum-space Gudermannian identity.

**Defects:** this is where both invalid closure arguments enter. Conformal-weight integrality is promoted from identification to theorem, and Cardy's formula is applied to the normal plane. The two files also contradict each other in the same directory, one declaring two constants of nature and the other zero.

**Verdict:** the generation where confidence outran the mathematics, in direct response to a prompt asking for closure.

## G3 — Chat-03 master

[[scale-as-modular-observable/chats/03/outputs/scale_as_a_modular_observable|scale_as_a_modular_observable]].

**Uniquely authoritative for:** its morgue. It carries thirteen withdrawn claims plus two caught analysis errors — a trapped optimiser that produced a spurious significance, and a single-bracket root finder that produced a spurious ceiling near $1.57$ — and it describes its observational comparison as partly retrodictive.

**Defects:** inherits both invalid closures.

**Verdict:** on epistemic hygiene this is *better* than its successor, and that is the archive's most counterintuitive fact.

## G4 — Revision 2

[[scale-as-modular-observable/misc/scale-as-a-modular-observable|the Revision 2 monograph]].

**Uniquely authoritative for:** four computed no-go results, the typing of the state coordinate as a collective constitutive quantity, the Gudermannian half-turn, the demonstration that background data cannot see the shape invariant, the negative control that disqualifies its own lensing evidence, and the Komar-shutdown account of why acceleration ends. [[salvage-ledger|The salvage ledger]] itemizes these.

**Defects:** retains both invalid closures; adds the radiation-artifact ceiling; conflates the algebraic modular generator with the density-matrix modular Hamiltonian; writes the reflection relation with a Tomita conjugation while asserting the generator is noncentral; brands the Riccati flow a saddle-node normal form; sells one fact as a triple coincidence; calls the local cosmological-constant problem dissolved. It also *deletes* G3's morgue, its retrodictive caveat, and its own caution about a neutrino residual, while promoting that residual.

**Verdict:** the richest document and the least trustworthy set of labels. Its status is correctly recorded in [[scale-as-modular-observable/claim-audit|the claim audit]], and the reasoning about which parts survive is in [[scale-as-modular-observable/misc/research-history|the research history]].

## G5a — v7, and its distilled library

[[causal-scale-master/latest/Causal_Scale_Dynamics_Master_v7_0|the v7 master]], distilled into the thirteen leaf notes of [[causal-scale-master/entry|causal-scale-master]].

**Uniquely authoritative for:** the repair. v7 discards both invalid closures, states unit slope as a representation choice, and introduces the entropy-normalized peak

$$
\mathfrak R_c:=\frac{k_B}{S_c}G^{\perp}_{NN}(N_c)
$$

as an explicit principle rather than a derived quantity. Its leaf notes are the best-factored prose in the archive: one concept each, with a claim-status section and a provenance line.

**Defects:** it hardwires $\varrho_\perp=1$ and $\mathfrak R_c=1$ through the leaves, so the generalized family is invisible; [[causal-scale-master/binary-geometry|binary-geometry]] writes the reflection relation as $J_{\mathrm{mod}}QJ_{\mathrm{mod}}=-Q$, carrying G4's conflation forward into an exact-algebra note; and its reported background comparison is not reproducible from the loose snapshot, whose figure links and reproduction paths refer to the archive layout rather than the flattened files.

**Verdict:** correct structure, over-specialized content.

## G5b — proposed v8, and its review

The raw proposal at [[inbox/causal-scale-dynamics-proposed-v8/causal-scale-dynamics-proposed-v8|inbox/causal-scale-dynamics-proposed-v8]], reviewed into the nine notes of [[causal-scale-master-v8/entry|causal-scale-master-v8]].

**Uniquely authoritative for:** the generalization. It makes $(\nu,\mathfrak R_c)$ central, gives the generalized invariant $9(1+w_X)^2+6w_X'=4\nu^2$ and the CPL locus, separates the signed horizon index from the surface-gravity magnitude, distinguishes the horizontal normalization from the Kodama–Hayward temperature, corrects the flow classification, and supplies the exact matter-plus-radiation root atlas in [[causal-scale-master-v8/generalized-background|generalized-background]] that exposes G4's ceiling artifact. [[causal-scale-master-v8/closure-stack|The closure stack]] is the archive's clearest statement of what the premises actually are.

**Defects:** it is a *review of a proposal*, not a master document, so the canonical argument is split across two modules by construction. Several of the proposal's quantitative claims are withheld as unreproducible, correctly, in [[causal-scale-master-v8/revision-audit|the revision audit]]. And its [[causal-scale-master-v8/observational-programme|observational programme]] promotes reconstruction of the shape invariant to the highest-value test without recording that G4 had already shown that test to be underpowered on structural grounds.

**Verdict:** correct parameterization, incomplete as a home.

## The autopsy layer

[[scale-as-modular-observable/entry|scale-as-modular-observable]] is not a competing synthesis and should not be retired. It is the provenance and autopsy layer for the repair: [[scale-as-modular-observable/claim-audit|claim-audit]] is where the two invalid closures were caught, and it ends by naming v7's principle as the clean repair. [[scale-as-modular-observable/misc/background-model|background-model]] is a correct, generalized derivation of the background that predates v8 and reaches the same generalized invariant.

Two of its arguments are sharper than what replaced them in v8 and are flagged for lifting in [[salvage-ledger|the salvage ledger]].

Its one genuine staleness is that it was synced to v7 and never updated for v8; the radiation-ceiling correction has since been recorded there directly.

## Reading the generations together

| Axis | Best generation | Worst |
|---|---|---|
| Honesty about the gap | G1 | G4 |
| Withdrawn-claim hygiene | G3 | G4 |
| Computed negative results | G4 | G5a |
| Prose factoring | G5a | G2 |
| Parameterization | G5b | G5a |
| Root-structure correctness | G5b | G4 |
| Fallback position if the amplitude law fails | G2 | G4, G5a |

No single generation dominates. That table is the argument for a consolidation that selects per axis rather than per document, and it is why [[synthesis-plan|the synthesis plan]] is organized note by note rather than as a merge of the two newest files.
