# Relative Entropy and the Local BKM Hessian

For a smooth comparable family of faithful states, relative entropy has the BKM metric as its coincidence Hessian. This exact local identity motivates CST's source proposal but does not determine an energy density along a finite cosmological history.

Let $\omega_N$ be a smooth family of faithful states transported to a common algebra, with a finite or consistently renormalized relative entropy. For neighboring states,

$$
S(\omega_{N+\delta N}\Vert\omega_N)
=\frac12G_{NN}^{\mathrm{BKM}}(N)(\delta N)^2
+O((\delta N)^3).
$$

The absence of a linear term is a coincidence property: the two arguments meet at $\delta N=0$. It is not a statement about the derivative of a divergence from one fixed reference far along the path.

For fixed physical dynamics generated by $\mathcal H_c$ at temperature $T_c$, let

$$
\omega_c=\frac{e^{-\beta_c\mathcal H_c}}{Z_c},
\qquad
\beta_c:=\frac1{k_BT_c},
$$

and use the dimensionless von Neumann entropy

$$
s_{\mathrm{vN}}(\rho):=-\operatorname{Tr}(\rho\ln\rho).
$$

Define

$$
F_c(\rho)
:=\operatorname{Tr}(\rho\mathcal H_c)
-k_BT_c\,s_{\mathrm{vN}}(\rho).
$$

Then, in a finite or suitably controlled setting,

$$
F_c(\rho)-F_c(\omega_c)
=k_BT_cS(\rho\Vert\omega_c).
$$

At the equilibrium point, the second variation along an admissible family is therefore

$$
\left.\frac{\mathrm d^2}{\mathrm dN^2}
F_c(\omega_N)\right|_{N_c}
=k_BT_cG^{\mathrm{BKM}}_{NN}(N_c),
$$

provided the first variation vanishes there and the same KMS dynamics is held fixed.

## The finite-history obstruction

A coincidence expansion compares neighboring states; it does not equal the change of relative entropy from one fixed reference along a finite path. [[no-gos/fixed-reference-free-energy-does-not-give-the-pulse|The binary counterexample]] owns the explicit calculation and shows that the fixed-reference quantity has a nonzero first derivative away from the crossing.

## Domain boundary

The wall programme has not yet constructed the common type-III algebra, transport, faithful state family, or renormalization that would make the displayed quantities finite and physical. The exact statement here is an interface theorem **if** those analytic prerequisites are satisfied. [[causal-scale-theory/free-energy-source]] begins only where that theorem stops.
