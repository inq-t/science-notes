# Quarantine of Live Tensions

These questions are unresolved and load-bearing enough that they must remain visible, but none is licensed as a premise merely because the homogeneous formulas are attractive. Each issue is routed to the note that states the exact obstruction and the evidence required to remove it.

## Full-algebra realization

[[open-questions/binary-reflection-realization|Binary reflection]] is exact only in the reduced model. A full wall must choose between a genuinely geometric reflection and a properly doubled Tomita realization, and it must explain the balanced reference weights.

[[open-questions/state-coordinate-and-holonomy|State coordinate and comparison datum]] may differ when cross-fiber transport has nontrivial connection data. Affine soldering is conditional on the comparison being path-consistent in the relevant direction.

[[wall-construction-interface/entry|The wall construction]] remains absent for dynamical FLRW regions. Without it, the binary channel, finite renormalized BKM norm, and microscopic return values $(\nu,\mathfrak R_c)$ are uncalculated.

## Extensivity and physical conversion

[[open-questions/extensive-channel-normalization|The extensive channel factor]] may depend on scale even when the normalized binary susceptibility is exactly $\operatorname{sech}^2\theta$. The rigid shape invariant requires its constancy or an equivalent theorem.

[[horizontal-temperature|The horizontal temperature]] is stipulated to be the canonical $2\pi$ horizon temperature. A derivation must distinguish that choice from the Kodama--Hayward temperature.

[[causal-scale-theory/free-energy-source|The all-history source law]] remains constitutive. The exact local BKM Hessian does not fix the finite-history density or its factor of one half.

## Coincidences and global sectors

Binary self-duality, maximum normalized susceptibility, $w_X=-1$, and ordinary-sector equality do not all have the same origin. [[unit-branch]] decomposes them; a deeper synchronization theorem remains conjectural.

[[vacuum-residual-sector|The zero residual]] is a global sector choice. Local trace-free blindness neither selects it nor proves its radiative stability.

## Covariant descent

[[open-questions/three-rank-one-reductions|Three uses of “rank one”]]—one wall generator, one clock, and one scalar stress response—are not interchangeable.

[[conjectures/covariant-response-sector|The covariant response sector]] has not been constructed. Until it is, stability, growth, lensing, and CMB predictions are not properties of the homogeneous pulse alone.
