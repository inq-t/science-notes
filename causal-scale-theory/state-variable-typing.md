# The Type of the Horizontal State Variable

The horizontal coordinate $\theta$ is a collective state-space variable or transported comparison datum, not automatically a local spacetime field. This typing explains why a canonical scalar action is not required by definition, while preserving the separate obligation to construct a covariant gravitational response.

Three possible types must be distinguished:

$$
\begin{array}{rcl}
\theta(N)&:&\text{a label on a chosen family of states},\\
\Theta(N_2:N_1)&:&\text{a relational coefficient returned by transported comparison},\\
\theta(x)&:&\text{a local spacetime field with its own kinetic operator}.
\end{array}
$$

The first two may coincide after a flat comparison datum and negligible holonomy are established; [[open-questions/state-coordinate-and-holonomy|that equivalence remains conditional]]. Neither implies the third.

Collective thermodynamic variables provide the right type analogy. Temperature, inverse temperature, chemical potential, and an order parameter can label states, vary across a system, and affect stress without being fundamental canonical scalar fields. Likewise, CST intends $\theta$ to label or compare the scale-dependent state of a causal region.

This analogy does no dynamical work by itself. A physical theory must still specify how the collective variable contributes to a conserved covariant stress tensor, how inhomogeneous variations propagate or remain constrained, and which observables measure them. [[conjectures/covariant-response-sector|The covariant-response conjecture]] owns that burden.

## Two exclusions

First, $\theta$ cannot be defined only by rearranging the target expansion history. That would make it a renamed effective-fluid coordinate rather than an independent state observable. [[causal-scale-theory/scale-as-state-principle|The observable-scale principle]] and [[no-gos/background-reconstruction-is-not-wall-construction|the reconstruction no-go]] require an independent wall construction.

Second, treating $\theta$ as a regular minimally coupled positive-kinetic field fails across the pre-crossing branch. [[no-gos/positive-kinetic-field-crossing|The positive-kinetic no-go]] excludes that direct realization. It does not exclude a collective, constrained, nonlocal, multi-component, or geometric completion.

The wider rules for keeping labels, comparison data, fields, and observables in their proper categories are developed in [[cosmodynamics/registers-and-type-discipline|register and type discipline]].
