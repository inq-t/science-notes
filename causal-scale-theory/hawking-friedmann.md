# The Hawking--Friedmann Conversion

For the flat-FLRW apparent horizon, the product of the canonical horizon temperature and Bekenstein--Hawking entropy equals both the Misner--Sharp horizon energy and the critical energy inside the horizon volume. This is exact algebra once the horizon definitions and the horizontal temperature choice are stipulated.

Use the energy-density convention

$$
\rho_{\mathrm{crit}}
:=\frac{3c^2H^2}{8\pi G},
$$

and define

$$
R_A:=\frac cH,
\qquad
V_A:=\frac{4\pi}{3}R_A^3,
\qquad
\frac{S_A}{k_B}:=\frac{\pi c^3R_A^2}{G\hbar}.
$$

With the canonical temperature from [[horizontal-temperature]],

$$
k_BT_{\mathrm{hor}}
=\frac{\hbar c}{2\pi R_A}.
$$

Multiplication gives

$$
k_BT_{\mathrm{hor}}\frac{S_A}{k_B}
=\frac{c^4R_A}{2G}.
$$

The right-hand side is the Misner--Sharp energy at the apparent horizon. Independently,

$$
\rho_{\mathrm{crit}}V_A
=\frac{3c^2H^2}{8\pi G}
\frac{4\pi}{3}\left(\frac cH\right)^3
=\frac{c^4R_A}{2G}.
$$

Thus at the crossing,

$$
\boxed{
k_BT_c\frac{S_c}{k_B}
=E_{\mathrm{MS},c}
=\rho_{\mathrm{crit},c}V_c.}
$$

## Conversion of the source law

Substituting this identity into [[causal-scale-theory/free-energy-source|the anchored source law]] yields

$$
\rho_X(N)
=\frac{\mathfrak R_c}{2}\rho_{\mathrm{crit},c}
\operatorname{sech}^2(\nu x).
$$

At $x=0$,

$$
\Omega_{X,c}
:=\frac{\rho_{X,c}}{\rho_{\mathrm{crit},c}}
=\frac{\mathfrak R_c}{2}.
$$

The factor one half here is inherited from the constitutive source normalization; it is not generated by Friedmann algebra.

## Boundary of the identity

- The displayed equality uses the flat-FLRW apparent horizon and $3+1$ dimensions.
- [[causal-scale-theory/dimensional-horizon-closure|The $d$-dimensional extension]] gives the corresponding crossing fraction and its precise dimensional assumptions.
- Choosing $T_c=T_{\mathrm{hor},c}$ is the open physical step isolated in [[horizontal-temperature]].
- The identity contains $G$ and does not derive its numerical value.
- Converting one homogeneous scalar does not construct a covariant response stress tensor.
- The stationary black-hole Smarr formula is a distinct homogeneity identity, reviewed in [[conservation-of-causal-charge/black-hole-saturation-boundary|the black-hole saturation note]].
