# Claim Status in Causal Scale Theory

CST treats logical status as part of every claim. The same formula may be exact as binary algebra, conditional as a cosmological deduction, or conjectural as a statement about a physical wall; this note fixes the local vocabulary used to prevent those levels from sliding into one another.

## Status classes

**[DEFINITION]** introduces notation or stipulates the meaning of a term. It is neither evidence nor a physical law.

**[STANDARD]** imports an established result with its ordinary hypotheses. Examples include causal reconstruction of conformal structure and flat-FLRW kinematics.

**[EXACT]** is an identity, equivalence, or theorem inside a declared mathematical regime. A qualifier may name that regime: `[EXACT — REFORMULATION]` adds no physical content, while `[EXACT — AFTER REDUCTION]` is exact only inside a granted reduced model.

**[CONDITIONAL THEOREM]** follows from named hypotheses that are stronger than definitions but need not be laws of nature. Measurable solutions of the multiplicative Cauchy equation provide the affine soldering theorem.

**[PRINCIPLE]** is a proposed fundamental law. The unit-width and unit-amplitude statements are separate principles.

**[ASSUMPTION]** supplies a model premise not asserted as a fundamental law, such as constancy of an extensive channel factor.

**[IDENTIFICATION]** maps one already-defined object or normalization to another physical role. An `OPEN` qualifier means that the map is stipulated but awaits derivation.

**[CONSTITUTIVE]** maps a mathematical quantity to a physical one. Such a law can be precise and falsifiable without being derived from the mathematics that motivates it.

**[BACKGROUND ASSUMPTION]** imports a dynamical setting such as flat GR--FLRW, separately conserved sectors, and declared matter content.

**[SECTOR]** chooses a global solution class, boundary condition, or residual datum.

**[CONDITIONAL OUTPUT]** is derived from the assembled stack. It is not evidence for its own premises.

**[RECEIPT]** is a symbolic or numerical recomputation. A receipt tests arithmetic and implementation; it does not test a physical identification.

**[REPORTED FIT]** records an empirical result whose data, likelihood, and reproducibility envelope must be stated.

**[CONJECTURE]** proposes a mathematical or physical extension together with an upgrade criterion and a failure criterion.

**[OPEN CONSTRUCTION]** names a missing object without assuming it exists.

**[NO-GO]** proves that a specified route fails under specified premises. It does not exclude neighboring theories with different premises.

Text after an em dash is a scope qualifier, not a new status class. Thus `[PRINCIPLE — WIDTH]`, `[REPORTED FIT — LIMITED]`, and `[NO-GO — POSITIVE-KINETIC SCALAR]` remain instances of the base classes above.

## Composition rule

The status of a conclusion cannot exceed the weakest load-bearing edge in its proof. In particular,

$$
\text{exact algebra}+\text{constitutive identification}
\Longrightarrow
\text{conditional physical output},
$$

not an exact physical theorem.

Likewise, a successful receipt promotes “unchecked arithmetic” to “checked arithmetic”; it does not promote a principle to a theorem or a background fit to a microscopic construction.

## The three most important type separations

1. **Imported versus derived.** Local GR or QFT may be accepted as the effective fiber theory without being reconstructed by CST.
2. **Normalized versus extensive.** The unit binary identity fixes a normalized susceptibility shape; a finite physical BKM norm requires channel multiplicity and renormalization data.
3. **Local versus all-history.** A coincidence Hessian constrains infinitesimal neighboring states; an energy-density profile along an entire cosmological trajectory requires a constitutive extension.
