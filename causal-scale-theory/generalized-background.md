# The Balanced-Binary CST-B2 Homogeneous Background

The CST-B2 background composes the balanced-binary response member, a constitutive homogeneous source, present-flatness branch selection, and ordinary GR--FLRW evolution. It is a **[CONDITIONAL MEMBER OUTPUT]** whose response has two independent parameters—the scale-state rate \(\nu\) and the integrated reference ratio \(\mathfrak R_c\)—while abundances, root branch, and residual-sector data remain additional background inputs. Neither response parameter is a universal constant merely because the unit branch proposes the value one, and no result in this note applies to every member of [[response-family-interface|the CST response family]].

Let

$$
x:=N-N_c.
$$

The CST-B2 types are fixed by [[program-core/ruble-equations|the programme core]] and [[response-law|the balanced-binary member]]:

- \(m:=\langle Q\rangle\) is the normalized binary polarization;
- \(\nu:=|\mathrm d\theta/\mathrm dN|>0\) is the constant scale-state rate, hence an inverse profile width, after affine scale--state soldering has been granted;
- \(\mathfrak R_c\) is the integrated state-to-gravitational-entropy ratio evaluated at the distinguished reference or candidate crossing cut.

After balanced binary reduction and the response-orientation choice \(\theta=\nu x\), with \(\nu=|\mathrm d\theta/\mathrm dN|\), the imported member data are

$$
m(x)=\tanh(\nu x),
\qquad
g^{\mathrm{bin}}_{\theta\theta}(x)
=\operatorname{sech}^2(\nu x),
\qquad
m^2+g^{\mathrm{bin}}_{\theta\theta}=1.
$$

[[binary-information-geometry/balanced-exponential-family|The balanced-family theorem]] owns the reduced identity, while the response-family interface owns its assembly with the state coordinate and constant extensive factor. The normalized identity does not supply an extensive wall norm or a cosmological density. Those require the independent construction, constant-extensivity assumption, anchored source law, and horizon conversion declared in the core.

With those bridges, spatially flat \(3+1\)-dimensional GR--FLRW, separate response conservation, and a residual-sector choice, the homogeneous interface is

$$
\boxed{
\rho_X(x)
=\frac{\mathfrak R_c}{2}\rho_{\mathrm{crit},c}
\operatorname{sech}^2(\nu x),
\qquad
w_X(x)
=-1+\frac{2\nu}{3}m(x).}
$$

[[causal-scale-theory/theorems/rigid-sech-response-identities|The CST-B2 rigid-sech theorem]] owns the equation-of-state derivation and all differential identities. The result is exact given this member profile and separate conservation, but the physical profile itself remains downstream of constitutive and identification premises.

On the zero-residual branch with no additional reference-cut component, present flatness yields the CST-B2 branch equation in [[causal-scale-theory/theorems/present-flatness-closure|the closure theorem]]. A nonzero residual or additional sector changes that closure. A chosen root then fixes [[causal-scale-theory/future-asymptotics|the CST-B2 expansion history and future class]], while [[causal-scale-theory/theorems/acceleration-condition|the acceleration theorem]] tests the zero-residual active-mass balance. The rate \(\nu\) fixes this member's transition shape and inverse width as well as asymptotic dilution; \(\mathfrak R_c\) fixes the reference amplitude; the root places that shape relative to the present epoch.

The two unit principles are logically independent:

$$
\nu=1
\not\Longrightarrow
\mathfrak R_c=1,
\qquad
\mathfrak R_c=1
\not\Longrightarrow
\nu=1.
$$
