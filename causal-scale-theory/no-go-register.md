# No-Go Register

The no-go results are durable constraints on specific derivation routes. Each linked note owns one calculation and states its scope, so a failed route cannot be quietly reused as a premise and cannot be exaggerated into a rejection of all neighboring theories.

[[no-gos/fixed-reference-free-energy-does-not-give-the-pulse|Fixed-reference free energy does not derive the pulse]] separates the exact local BKM Hessian from the all-history constitutive source.

[[no-gos/modular-rescaling-is-not-the-binary-tangent|Modular rescaling is not the binary tangent]] excludes identification of horizontal $Q$-translation with temperature rescaling of one fixed modular Hamiltonian.

[[no-gos/positive-kinetic-field-crossing|No regular positive-kinetic scalar realizes the full crossing]] blocks the simplest canonical field and positive-definite sigma-model lifts.

[[no-gos/character-theory-does-not-fix-unit-width|Character theory does not fix unit width]] preserves the exact affine theorem while removing an invalid integrality argument.

[[no-gos/normal-plane-is-not-automatically-a-cft|A Lorentzian normal plane is not automatically a CFT]] blocks an unsupported Cardy shortcut.

[[no-gos/background-reconstruction-is-not-wall-construction|Background reconstruction is not wall construction]] rules out circular explanatory derivations from the history they are meant to predict.

Two further exclusions are carried directly by their exact owners: [[causal-scale-theory/binary-geometry|the reflected divergence is even]] and cannot orient cosmic evolution, while [[causal-scale-theory/witten-pair|internal Darboux positivity]] does not imply spacetime perturbative stability.
