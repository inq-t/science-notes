# Hawking–Friedmann Conversion

For a flat $3+1$-dimensional Einstein-FLRW apparent horizon, the area-law entropy and a chosen canonical horizontal modular temperature multiply to the Misner--Sharp energy. Dividing by the areal volume yields the critical density, providing the dimensional bridge used by the scale--capacity closure.

## Conversion in \(3+1\) dimensions

At the crossing,

$$
R_c=\frac{c}{H_c},
\qquad
V_c=\frac{4\pi}{3}R_c^3.
$$

Use the Bekenstein--Hawking entropy and canonical horizontal temperature

$$
\frac{S_c}{k_B}
=\frac{\pi R_c^2c^3}{G\hbar},
\qquad
k_BT_c=\frac{\hbar c}{2\pi R_c}.
$$

Their product is

$$
k_BT_c\frac{S_c}{k_B}
=\frac{c^4R_c}{2G}
=E_{\mathrm{MS},c}.
$$

Flat Friedmann marginality gives

$$
E_{\mathrm{MS},c}
=\rho_{\mathrm{crit},c}V_c,
$$

and therefore

$$
\frac{k_BT_c}{V_c}\frac{S_c}{k_B}
=\rho_{\mathrm{crit},c}.
$$

In $d$ spatial dimensions, the corresponding algebra gives

$$
\frac{k_BT_c(S_c/k_B)}{V_c}
=\frac{2}{d-1}\rho_{\mathrm{crit},c}.
$$

## Claim status

- **Algebraically exact once stipulated:** flat Einstein-FLRW apparent-horizon geometry, the area law, Misner--Sharp marginality, and the canonical $2\pi$ horizontal temperature normalization.
- **Important distinction:** $T_c$ is not silently interchangeable with the dynamical Kodama--Hayward temperature or a stationary Schwarzschild Smarr temperature.
- **Open physical applicability:** the canonical horizontal normalization has not been derived for a dynamical FLRW causal-wall state.
