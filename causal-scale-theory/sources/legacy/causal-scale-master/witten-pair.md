# Binary Witten–Darboux Pair

The binary log-partition potential canonically generates a Witten--Darboux factorization with one localized mode and a reflectionless continuum. This is exact internal horizontal geometry after the binary reduction, but it is not yet a scalar, vector, or tensor perturbation theory for FLRW spacetime.

## Factorization

Let

$$
\eta_Q(\theta)=\tanh\theta,
\qquad
\eta_Q^2+\frac{\mathrm d\eta_Q}{\mathrm d\theta}=1.
$$

Define

$$
\mathcal A=\partial_\theta+\eta_Q,
\qquad
\mathcal A^\dagger=-\partial_\theta+\eta_Q.
$$

Then

$$
\mathcal H_-
:=\mathcal A^\dagger\mathcal A
=-\partial_\theta^2+1-2\operatorname{sech}^2\theta,
$$

$$
\mathcal H_+
:=\mathcal A\mathcal A^\dagger
=-\partial_\theta^2+1.
$$

The normalized zero mode is

$$
\psi_0(\theta)
=\frac1{\sqrt2}\operatorname{sech}\theta,
$$

so its density equals half the binary BKM metric:

$$
2|\psi_0|^2
=\operatorname{sech}^2\theta
=G^{\mathrm{BKM}}_{\theta\theta}.
$$

For continuum energy $1+k^2$, a scattering state is

$$
\psi_k(\theta)
=(-ik+\tanh\theta)e^{ik\theta}.
$$

It contains no reflected $e^{-ik\theta}$ component asymptotically, so $R(k)=0$. The continuum is transmitted with a phase shift; it is not absent.

## Claim status

- **Exact after binary reduction:** the factorization, partner potentials, one bound mode, and reflectionless continuum.
- **Canonical in a limited sense:** generated by $\Psi(\theta)=\ln(2\cosh\theta)$, not canonical as a spacetime perturbation operator.
- **Not established:** identification with physical FLRW scalar, vector, or tensor modes, or with the ordinary matter-growth equation.
- **Negative result:** an ordinary canonical single scalar cannot realize the full history because $1+w_X<0$ before the crossing and the crossing develops critical inverse-square behavior.
