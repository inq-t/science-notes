# Scale–Capacity Equivalence Principle

The Scale--Capacity Equivalence Principle is the new amplitude-closing law of Causal Scale Dynamics. It sets the entropy-normalized squared BKM norm of the horizontal tangent generated by one Weyl e-fold to unity at the self-dual crossing; it is a postulate with controlled precedents, not a theorem about dynamical FLRW horizons.

## Extensive horizontal norm

After binary reduction and affine soldering, define the peak state-coordinate metric coefficient

$$
C_{\perp,c}:=G^\perp_{\theta\theta}(0),
$$

and write the physical pullback metric coefficient, or squared BKM speed, as

$$
G^{\perp}_{NN}(N)
=C_{\perp,c}\varrho_\perp^2
\operatorname{sech}^2\!\left[\varrho_\perp(N-N_c)\right].
$$

The dimensionless entropy-normalized peak squared speed is

$$
\mathfrak R_c
:=\frac{k_B}{S_c}G^{\perp}_{NN}(N_c).
$$

It is invariant under reparameterization of the auxiliary state coordinate $\theta$ when the physical Weyl coordinate $N$ is held fixed: the state-space metric and the slope $\mathrm d\theta/\mathrm dN$ transform oppositely.

The principle states

$$
\boxed{\mathfrak R_c=1}.
$$

With the additional fundamental-character choice $\varrho_\perp=1$, the peak-normalized binary profile is

$$
\frac{k_B}{S_c}G^{\perp}_{NN}(N)
=\operatorname{sech}^2(N-N_c).
$$

The full profile uses both the peak principle and the binary unit-slope shape; the principle alone fixes only the peak normalization.

## Claim status

- **Physical equivalence principle:** $\mathfrak R_c=1$.
- **Physical representation choice:** $\varrho_\perp=1$.
- **Motivation, not proof:** capacity/entropy equality in controlled Einstein-holographic settings and related causal-horizon proposals.
- **Not established:** that a generic quantum BKM metric equals ordinary modular variance or that the full FLRW wall saturates capacity equals entropy.
