# Modular Flow and State Deformation

Vertical modular flow and horizontal state deformation are different tangent directions. Keeping them separate prevents a modular clock parameter from being mistaken for a coordinate that compares different states, a category error that affected earlier versions of the programme.

## Vertical motion

For a local von Neumann algebra $\mathcal A$ and faithful state $\omega$, Tomita--Takesaki theory supplies

$$
\sigma_s^\omega(A)
=\Delta_\omega^{is}A\Delta_\omega^{-is}.
$$

This is an automorphism at fixed $(\mathcal A,\omega)$. Its parameter $s$ is a vertical modular-flow coordinate.

## Horizontal motion

A family

$$
N\longmapsto\omega_N
$$

moves through state space. Comparing its fibers requires relative modular data, such as a Connes cocycle or modular Berry connection. Schematically, a changing modular Hamiltonian can be separated as

$$
\partial_NK_N
=[\mathscr C_N,K_N]
+(\mathcal D_NK_N)_\perp
+\frac{\mathrm dc_N}{\mathrm dN}\mathbf 1,
$$

where $\mathscr C_N$ is a modular-frame connection and the terms represent vertical frame change, physical horizontal noncentral deformation, and a central normalization shift.

The source distinguishes a vertical horizon rapidity $\eta_A$ from a horizontal polarization $\theta$:

$$
\frac{\mathrm d\eta_A}{\mathrm dN}=\mu_A(N),
\qquad
\frac{\mathrm d\theta}{\mathrm dN}=\varrho_\perp.
$$

Neither parameter is Newtonian time, proper time, or causal order.

## Claim status

- **Standard:** modular automorphism flow at fixed algebra and state.
- **Mathematical distinction:** vertical frame motion versus horizontal motion through states.
- **Conditional physical interpretation:** identifying a cosmological horizon flow as geometric modular flow and identifying scale evolution with a particular horizontal state family.
