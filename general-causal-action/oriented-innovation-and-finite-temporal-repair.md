# Oriented Innovation and Finite Temporal Repair

A one-sided innovation on the selected vacuum chain has an explicit finite repair and a concrete intertwiner obtained by extending its slab at both ends. The extension changes the endpoint-pair law; an exact variance ledger separates conditional loss from the cost of that change. A uniform excess of loss would give a polynomial gap certificate. Conditioning on both endpoints adds a separate reconstruction burden that orientation avoids, while the required non-Abelian strict estimate remains open.

**Status: exact finite-word identities on the stated stationary chain; conditional polynomial certificate; open uniform non-Abelian estimate.** [[two-slice-innovation-geometry/inq|Two-slice innovation geometry]] and [[global-local-response-reconstruction/vacuum-boundary-gluing-and-wall-response|VBG3]] already own the one-ended variance identity. The new constructions here are its finite repair and actual three-slab intertwiner, with the corrected variance estimate needed to make the repair uniformly effective.

## Use the selected chronological law

Let \(P\) be the ground transform of the normalized supported transfer returned by [[reflection-sewing-and-the-auxiliary-boundary-carrier|RS10–14]]. Work on its gauge-invariant supported carrier, equivalently the site-gauge orbit space. The vacuum probability, written in auxiliary representatives before this quotient, is
\[
d\pi(\xi)=|\varphi(\xi)|^2m(\xi)d\xi,
\qquad
P=P^*,\qquad 0<P\le I,\qquad P1=1.
\tag{OI1}
\]
Fix a positive integer \(k\), put \(\ell=ka_t>0\), and write \(A=P^k\).
The actual stationary pair and triple laws are
\[
\pi(dx_-)A(x_-,dx_0),
\qquad
\pi(dx_-)A(x_-,dx_0)A(x_0,dx_+).
\tag{OI2}
\]
The endpoints are separated from the middle by \(k\) original temporal steps. These laws retain the selected vacuum and the original sewing. They do not define a new configuration sampler.

Let \(\mathscr H_{\rm frame}\) be the closed cyclic sector generated by the returned neutral frame-source multipliers and powers of \(P\) from \(1\), as in [[determinant-response-sewing-and-relational-rigidity#Two-boundary rigidity: a sufficient stronger route|DS10e–g]]. It is \(P\)-invariant and, since \(P=P^*\), reducing. Write
\(\mathscr H_0=\mathscr H_{\rm frame}\ominus\mathbb C1\).
The simple top eigenvalue in the finite RS theory implies
\(\ker(I-P)=\mathbb C1\). A family of such theories still needs uniform control; simplicity at each regulator supplies none.

## One orientation identifies the exact transfer defect

On the pair law let \(J_-f=f(x_-)\), \(J_0f=f(x_0)\) be endpoint pullback isometries. Then
\[
J_-^*J_0=A,\qquad
E_-=J_-J_-^*,\qquad
\delta_+=(I-E_-)J_0=J_0-J_-A.
\tag{OI3}
\]
Thus
\[
(\delta_+f)(x_-,x_0)=f(x_0)-(Af)(x_-),
\]
with adjoint \(J_0^*-AJ_-^*\). Every map is obtained by conditional integration in (OI2). In particular
\[
\boxed{
R_+:=\delta_+^*\delta_+=I-A^2=I-P^{2k}.}
\tag{OI4}
\]
This is TSI3–4b instantiated on the actual RS carrier. Positivity of \(P\) also gives
\[
\ker R_+=\ker(I-P),\qquad
R_+=I-e^{-2\ell H}\le2\ell H,
\qquad H=-a_t^{-1}\log P,
\tag{OI5}
\]
where the last inequality is a quadratic-form statement on the energy form domain.

For precise source typing, use
\(\mathscr K_0=\overline{\delta_+\mathscr H_0}\)
as the innovation carrier. The full endpoint adjoint sends \(\mathscr K_0\) into \(\mathscr H_0\): on its dense set \(\delta_+f\), its value is \(R_+f\), and boundedness extends the assertion. No independently inserted projection onto the vacuum complement is needed in the repair below.

## The extra endpoint removes a separate piece of information

Embed the pair observables in the triple law and let
\(E_{-,+}=\mathbb E[\,\cdot\mid X_{-k},X_k]\).
The two-ended discrepancy used in DS10e is
\[
\delta_{\rm br}=(I-E_{-,+})J_0.
\]
Because \(E_-\le E_{-,+}\) as nested orthogonal projections,
\[
\delta_+=\delta_{\rm br}+(E_{-,+}-E_-)J_0
\]
is an orthogonal decomposition. Consequently
\[
\boxed{
R_+-R_{\rm br}
=J_0^*(E_{-,+}-E_-)J_0\ge0,
\qquad R_{\rm br}=\delta_{\rm br}^*\delta_{\rm br}.}
\tag{OI6}
\]
Its scalar value on \(f\) is exactly
\[
\mathbb E\left|
\mathbb E[f(X_0)\mid X_{-k},X_k]
-\mathbb E[f(X_0)\mid X_{-k}]
\right|^2.
\]
This is the additional predictive information supplied by the future endpoint, conditional on the past. It is not determined by the one-sided transfer norm alone.

A lower bound on \(R_{\rm br}\) is sufficient for a physical gap by (OI5)–(OI6). Conversely, a gap gives a lower bound on \(R_+\), but gives no automatic lower bound on \(R_{\rm br}\) through these identities. To recover that stronger route one could independently prove, for example,
\[
J_0^*(E_{-,+}-E_-)J_0\le\theta R_+,
\qquad \theta<1,
\tag{OI7}
\]
uniformly on the source sector. Then \(R_{\rm br}\ge(1-\theta)R_+\). This extra future-information estimate is precisely the burden the oriented response avoids.

[[two-endpoint-recovery-and-a-rare-state-escape|The rare-state escape]] supplies a positive reversible transfer family in which the centered transfer contracts strongly while a normalized middle source is recovered almost perfectly from its two endpoints. Its discrete-transfer scope is explicit. It rules out deriving (OI7), or an equivalent uniform bridge floor, from positivity and a finite-step chronological transfer gap alone.

Reversing time gives the analogous future-to-middle one-sided discrepancy with the same Gramian \(I-P^{2k}\), by reversibility. Selecting one orientation changes the declared response; it does not change the underlying time-reversal-invariant path law.

## A finite chronological word repairs the oriented response

For \(m\in\mathbb N_{>0}\), define the polynomial and the return map
\[
S_m(A^2)=I+A^2+\cdots+A^{2m-2},
\qquad
\boxed{\mathcal B_m=S_m(A^2)\delta_+^*.}
\tag{OI8}
\]
This uses finitely many slab compositions and the actual endpoint adjoint. The definition contains neither a logarithm nor an inverse of a gap, transfer, or response.

The geometric telescoping identity gives
\[
\boxed{\mathcal B_m\delta_+=I-A^{2m}=I-P^{2km}.}
\tag{OI9}
\]
Moreover, commuting positive polynomials give
\[
\mathcal B_m\mathcal B_m^*
=S_m(A^2)^2(I-A^2)
=S_m(A^2)(I-A^{2m})
\le mI.
\]
Hence
\[
\boxed{\|\mathcal B_m\|\le\sqrt m.}
\tag{OI10}
\]
The same statement holds with the source-restricted innovation adjoint, by the carrier observation following (OI5). The repair bound is independent of the number of auxiliary coordinates and spatial links. Its residual has not been estimated away.

If the additional uniform statement
\[
\|P^{2km}|_{\mathscr H_0}\|\le\rho<1
\tag{OI11}
\]
is available with \(m\) bounded and physical \(\ell>0\) fixed, (OI9)–(OI10) give the concrete QD7 repair with \(C=\sqrt m\). The number of physical slab blocks is bounded; \(k=\ell/a_t\) may grow as the microscopic duration is refined. In particular
\[
\|\delta_+f\|^2\ge\frac{(1-\rho)^2}{m}\|f\|^2,
\qquad
\langle f,Hf\rangle
\ge\frac{(1-\rho)^2}{2m\ell}\|f\|^2.
\tag{OI12}
\]
Since (OI11) is already a chronological contraction bound, direct functional calculus gives the sharper
\(H|_{\mathscr H_0}\ge-\log\rho/(2m\ell)\).
The merit of (OI8) is the explicit repair map, not a proof of (OI11) from weaker premises.

The related transported-innovation ledger is
\[
\sum_{j=0}^{m-1}\|\delta_+A^jf\|^2
=\langle f,(I-A^{2m})f\rangle.
\tag{OI13}
\]
This is the homogeneous instance of [[transported-response-observability-solder/inq|transported response observability]]. For the actual past filtrations \(\mathcal F_t=\sigma(X_j:j\le t)\), the Markov property gives
\(\mathbb E[f(X_0)\mid\mathcal F_{-jk}]=(A^jf)(X_{-jk})\).
Their successive differences are orthogonal, with squared norms equal to the summands in (OI13). That existing ledger does not supply a lower bound on its sum without an additional estimate.

## A specific new boundary relation would supply a polynomial certificate

There is a more structural test than simply naming (OI11). Construct a map \(\mathcal K\) on \(\mathscr K_0\) from the actual RS boundary feature contractions, their adjoints, Haar projections, and a fixed finite temporal sewing word. Seek the relations
\[
\boxed{
\delta_+A=\mathcal K\delta_+,
\qquad
\eta^2I_{\mathscr K_0}-\mathcal K^*\mathcal K
=\sum_{\alpha=1}^{N}\mathcal F_\alpha^*\mathcal F_\alpha,
\qquad 0\le\eta<1,}
\tag{OI14}
\]
where the \(\mathcal F_\alpha\) are also explicitly supplied finite boundary words. A uniform inequality
\(\mathcal K^*\mathcal K\le\eta^2I\)
can replace the displayed finite sum if proved geometrically. The words, \(\eta\), and their validity on every source direction must be specified independently of the desired spectrum.

If (OI14) holds, then the existing response \(R_+=I-A^2\) obeys the exact finite-word certificate
\[
\begin{aligned}
R_+^2-(1-\eta^2)R_+
&=\eta^2R_+-AR_+A\\
&=\delta_+^*(\eta^2I-\mathcal K^*\mathcal K)\delta_+\\
&=\sum_{\alpha}
(\mathcal F_\alpha\delta_+)^*(\mathcal F_\alpha\delta_+)\ge0.
\end{aligned}
\tag{OI15}
\]
Thus the spectrum of \(R_+\) lies in
\(\{0\}\cup[1-\eta^2,1]\). Its already identified vacuum kernel gives
\[
A|_{\mathscr H_0}\le\eta I,
\qquad
P^{2km}|_{\mathscr H_0}\le\eta^{2m}I.
\tag{OI16}
\]
For \(0<\eta<1\), the physical energy edge is at least
\(-\log\eta/\ell\). The endpoint case \(\eta=0\) would force \(A=0\) on \(\mathscr H_0\); the injective finite RS transfer excludes that on a nontrivial source sector. A strict contraction with \(0<\eta<1\) remains compatible with injectivity and does not impose the rejected exact finite-tour idempotence in [[cut-tour-absorption-and-the-injective-exchange|CT3 and CT10–11]].

The strict estimate in (OI14) is the new content. There is always an abstract contraction on the innovation carrier, defined densely by
\(\delta_+f\mapsto\delta_+Af\), because
\(AR_+A\le R_+\).
Merely assigning that contraction the name \(\mathcal K\) constructs no independent explanation. Its norm can equal one when normalized soft sources survive. The proposal requires an actual finite boundary-word realization and an independently justified strict loss, or the explicit positive remainder in (OI14).

The most immediate chronological word can be tested exactly. On the same two-sided stationary path, let
\[
\mathcal F_j=\sigma(X_i:i\le jk),\qquad
\mathsf D=E_{\mathcal F_0}-E_{\mathcal F_{-1}},
\qquad
(\mathsf S F)((X_i)_i)=F((X_{i+k})_i).
\tag{OI16a}
\]
The history shift \(\mathsf S\) is unitary, and \(\mathsf D\) is the projection onto the difference of the nested past spaces. On a slice function, \(\mathsf Df(X_0)=\delta_+f\). Advancing its innovation gives
\[
\mathsf S\delta_+f=f(X_k)-(Af)(X_0),\qquad
E_{\mathcal F_0}\mathsf S\delta_+f=0,
\qquad
\boxed{\mathsf D\mathsf S\delta_+=0.}
\tag{OI16b}
\]
The first conditional expectation vanishes by the Markov property; its conditional expectation onto \(\mathcal F_{-1}\) therefore vanishes too. Thus the candidate \(\mathcal K=\mathsf D\mathsf S\), restricted to the current innovation range, annihilates that range. It cannot satisfy (OI14): for every nonzero \(f\in\mathscr H_0\), injectivity of \(A\) and the vacuum-only kernel of \(\delta_+\) imply \(\delta_+Af\ne0\).

This tests an actual oriented word rather than an arbitrary spectral construction. Transporting the observed function by \(A\) before taking its innovation is different from advancing a realized innovation into the next chronological interval and projecting it back onto the old one. The needed boundary word must retain the former operation; the latter loses it exactly.

Generic positivity and sewing cannot supply it. Along any centered spectral sequence with \(Af_n\) close to \(f_n\), \(R_+f_n\) is small, the residual \(A^{2m}f_n\) remains close to \(f_n\) for every bounded \(m\), and no uniformly bounded repair of \(\delta_+\) can reconstruct those vectors with an error below one. The Abelian soft modes in [[reciprocal-coefficients-and-the-field-gap-test|the field-gap control]] retain this obstruction. A successful non-Abelian relation must say exactly why that control changes on the complete physical source sector.

## Every mark stays in the original amplitude

The repair is a linear diagnostic on the existing stationary history, not a replacement stochastic kernel. Choosing \(\delta_+\) rather than \(\delta_{\rm br}\) changes which prediction is subtracted. It neither discards the future from the full amplitude nor renormalizes a conditioned action.

For any bounded joint mark \(N(X_{-k},X_0)\), its actual innovation form is
\[
\langle\delta_+f,M_N\delta_+g\rangle
=
\mathbb E\left[
N\,
\overline{f(X_0)-(Af)(X_{-k})}\,
\bigl(g(X_0)-(Ag)(X_{-k})\bigr)
\right].
\tag{OI17}
\]
All four terms use (OI2), with the source-free conditional denominators fixed. General joint history marks are retained on a larger actual cylinder before integration. Marked forms need not remain positive for complex \(N\), and they do not automatically retain the source-free polynomial simplification (OI4).

For bounded auxiliary slice multipliers \(F_j\), the chronological source law is still
\[
\left\langle
1,M_{F_0}P^{n_1}M_{F_1}\cdots
P^{n_s}M_{F_s}1
\right\rangle_\pi.
\tag{OI18}
\]
The factors in \(\mathcal B_m\) are actual powers from this same sewing law; its additions and subtractions are ordinary linear combinations of those amplitudes. Multiplication sources cannot be commuted through the powers of \(P\). Original frame sources at a common slice retain their joint RS15 integral, including [[auxiliary-boundary-sufficiency-and-the-wilson-source-algebra|AS's finite conditional-covariance correction]]; separate conditional averages must not be substituted for that product.

Undoing the ground transform retains the original \(\varphi\), \(\lambda_0\), temporal durations, and endpoint half-weights. Thus orientation does not erase any sourced normalization or assert that a changing finite source algebra has already become the physical continuum algebra. Its concrete advantage is narrower: the repair residual in (OI9) measures actual chronological persistence, without also requiring a uniform inability of two endpoints to decode their middle.

## Parallel slab extension supplies an actual intertwiner

There is a concrete boundary word beyond the failed shifted-innovation candidate. Keep the inner pair \((x,y)\) at separation \(\ell\), and extend its actual stationary path by one slab at each end. Conditional on \((x,y)\), its new outer boundaries \(x',y'\) are independent, with laws \(A(x,dx')\) and \(A(y,dy')\). Reversibility identifies the four-boundary law as
\[
\begin{aligned}
d\Gamma(x',x,y,y')
&=\pi(dx)A(x,dy)A(x,dx')A(y,dy')\\
&=\pi(dx')A(x',dx)A(x,dy)A(y,dy').
\end{aligned}
\tag{OI19}
\]
The chronological order is \(x'\to x\to y\to y'\). This is three successive actual slabs, not a stationary update of the inner pair. Its inner and outer pair laws are
\[
\mu_1(dx,dy)=\pi(dx)A(x,dy),
\qquad
\mu_3(dx',dy')=\pi(dx')A^3(x',dy').
\tag{OI20}
\]
In particular, replacing the outer law by \(\mu_1\) would change the amplitude.

The conditional descent
\[
(Q\zeta)(x,y)
=\int A(x,dx')A(y,dy')\,\zeta(x',y'),
\qquad
Q:L^2(\mu_3)\longrightarrow L^2(\mu_1)
\tag{OI21}
\]
is a contraction by conditional Jensen. Retain the original source function
\(h_f(x,y)=f(y)-(Af)(x)=\delta_+f\).
Evaluating that same function on the outer pair keeps \(A\) in its second term. It is not the fresh three-slab innovation \(f(y')-(A^3f)(x')\).

The same-function inclusion from these original innovations to the wider pair has the exact norm
\[
\begin{aligned}
\|h_f\|_{\mu_3}^2
&=\langle f,(I+A^2-2A^4)f\rangle_\pi\\
&=\langle f,(I-A^2)(I+2A^2)f\rangle_\pi
\le3\|\delta_+f\|_{\mu_1}^2.
\end{aligned}
\tag{OI22}
\]
The cross term uses the actual outer conditional transport \(A^3\). This estimate makes the inclusion well defined even for equivalence classes: a zero-norm original innovation also has zero norm on \(\mu_3\). It extends uniquely to
\[
\iota:\mathscr K_0\longrightarrow L^2(\mu_3),
\qquad \|\iota\|\le\sqrt3.
\]
No bounded inclusion on all of \(L^2(\mu_1)\) is claimed.

Now compose the actual extension and descent:
\[
\boxed{
\mathcal K_\parallel=Q\iota,\qquad
\mathcal K_\parallel\delta_+f
=J_0Af-J_-A^2f
=\delta_+Af.}
\tag{OI23}
\]
Thus the first relation in (OI14) has an explicit realization. The image belongs to \(\mathscr K_0\), and the sharper bound
\[
\|\mathcal K_\parallel\delta_+f\|_{\mu_1}^2
=\langle f,A^2(I-A^2)f\rangle_\pi
\le\|\delta_+f\|_{\mu_1}^2
\]
extends it to a contraction on that carrier. The word consists of two independently integrated outer slabs, the original pair readout evaluated on their boundaries, and conditional descent. It contains no inverse response or spectral reconstruction of \(f\).

## The exact variance ledger identifies the missing strict estimate

For \(h=\delta_+f\), write \(R=I-A^2\). The conditional-variance loss in the descent \(Q\), the cost of changing the pair separation, and their difference are respectively
\[
\begin{aligned}
V_Q(h)
&:=\|\iota h\|_{\mu_3}^2-\|Q\iota h\|_{\mu_1}^2
=\langle f,(I-A^4)f\rangle_\pi,\\
C_{\rm lag}(h)
&:=\|\iota h\|_{\mu_3}^2-\|h\|_{\mu_1}^2
=2\langle f,A^2R f\rangle_\pi,\\
\boxed{V_Q(h)-C_{\rm lag}(h)}
&=\boxed{\|h\|_{\mu_1}^2-\|\mathcal K_\parallel h\|_{\mu_1}^2
=\langle f,R^2f\rangle_\pi.}
\end{aligned}
\tag{OI24}
\]
Here
\[
V_Q(h)
=\mathbb E_\Gamma\operatorname{Var}
\bigl((\iota h)(X',Y')\mid X,Y\bigr).
\]
All three identities extend to the innovation closure by boundedness. Both quantities being subtracted are nonnegative. A lower bound on conditional variance by itself does not bound their difference.

The concrete additional relation to test is now
\[
\boxed{
V_Q(h)\ge C_{\rm lag}(h)+c\|h\|_{\mu_1}^2,
\qquad c>0,\qquad h\in\mathscr K_0,}
\tag{OI25}
\]
uniformly at fixed physical \(\ell\) through the required limits. It would give
\(\|\mathcal K_\parallel\|\le\sqrt{1-c}<1\)
and \(R^2\ge cR\). A finite boundary-word sum of squares for the remainder in (OI25) would implement the second relation in (OI14). The diagram and the ledger have been constructed; that strict estimate has not.

There is no spectral gain hidden in this construction. The polar factorization
\(\delta_+=V R^{1/2}\)
has \(V:\mathscr H_0\to\mathscr K_0\) unitary, because the already identified kernel of \(R\) is only the vacuum. Commutation of \(A\) with \(R\) and density give
\[
\mathcal K_\parallel=VAV^*,
\qquad
\|\mathcal K_\parallel\|=\|A|_{\mathscr H_0}\|.
\tag{OI26}
\]
This identity verifies the spectral meaning of the constructed boundary word. It was not used to define that word or to invert \(R\). Proving its strict norm bound still requires a non-Abelian estimate with content beyond the transfer-gap restatement.

For a complete finite control, take \(x,y\in\{-1,1\}\),
\(\pi(\pm1)=1/2\), and
\[
A_\lambda(x,y)=\frac{1+\lambda xy}{2},
\qquad 0<\lambda<1.
\]
Every centered slice source is proportional to \(f(x)=x\). The innovation is \(h(x,y)=y-\lambda x\), and direct four-boundary integration gives
\[
\mu_1(x,y)=\frac{1+\lambda xy}{4},
\quad
\mu_3(x,y)=\frac{1+\lambda^3xy}{4},
\quad
\mathcal K_\parallel h=\lambda h,
\quad
\|h\|_{\mu_1}^2=1-\lambda^2.
\tag{OI27}
\]
Thus the complete innovation carrier has contraction factor \(\lambda\). In units of \(\|h\|_{\mu_1}^2\), the variance loss is \(1+\lambda^2\), the separation cost is \(2\lambda^2\), and their difference is \(1-\lambda^2\). As \(\lambda\to1\), both positive terms remain large while the difference closes. This exact control tests the strict margin in (OI25), rather than treating a sizeable uncorrected conditional variance as rigidity.

## The four-boundary word retains every source and normalization

Write \(m(d\xi)=m(\xi)d\xi\), let \(t_k\) denote the RS kernel of \(\widetilde{\mathsf T}^{\,k}\) relative to this measure, and normalize \(\varphi\) in \(L^2(m)\). The complete law in (OI19) is
\[
d\Gamma
=\lambda_0^{-3k}\,
\varphi(x')\varphi(y')\,
t_k(x',x)t_k(x,y)t_k(y,y')\,
m(dx')m(dx)m(dy)m(dy').
\tag{OI28}
\]
The ground-vector ratios in the three conditional transitions cancel to the two displayed outer factors. For any bounded joint four-boundary mark \(N\) and admitted input/output functions, all sourced matrix elements are their integrals against \(N\,d\Gamma\). Sources on variables inside one of the slabs are retained inside that slab's actual marked kernel before integration.

Equivalently, the marked conditional descent is
\((Q_N\zeta)(x,y)=\mathbb E_\Gamma[N\zeta(X',Y')\mid X=x,Y=y]\),
with the source-free conditional normalizer fixed. General marks do not preserve the source-free intertwining or its positivity, and they need not map back into \(\mathscr K_0\). Their full amplitudes are nevertheless retained by (OI28). Returning original spatial frame sources keeps the same RS/AS coincident-source integration already specified after (OI18).

The executable next test is therefore one diagram: derive (OI25) from the actual local non-Abelian sewing and conditional-source blocks of the three-slab extension (OI19), retaining its complete source law (OI28). Taking a positive \(c\) as another spectral assumption would restate the target. The constructed map supplies the exact carrier and defect on which that independent physical rigidity estimate must act.
