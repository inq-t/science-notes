# The Radius–Orientation Chronological Gram Changes Sign

The complete first mixed chronological response of the fixed radius and orientation sources is a sum of nine exact exponentials. Its zero-time Gram coefficient is positive, while its slowest exponential has negative weight, so the response changes sign at a finite scaled duration. Both signs belong to the actual compact theory at sufficiently strong confinement. The innovation source pencil retains every signed coefficient and the changing Gram; the previously positive exterior contribution alone does not determine it.
