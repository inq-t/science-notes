The primitives may be algebraic, categorical, analytic or a new combination. A candidate can declare a pairing, preparation or processing rule without deriving every axiom from nothing. The stronger foundational ambition is to constrain more outputs with fewer independent choices; it does not add an ontology, a unique gauge group or a derivation of four dimensions to the admission conditions for a valid Yang–Mills theorem.
