The side-chat review correctly identifies a mismatch between the programme's foundational ambition and its recent choice of next tasks. Finite preparation and vacuum constructions have advanced, but the law selecting their inputs has remained largely unresolved. The correction is to investigate one explicit constitutive conjecture and its consequences, using the existing constructions as tests. This assessment changes the research order without revoking the finite results or weakening the Yang–Mills target.

## One active conjectural package

The review's three commitments sharpen the existing [[general-causal-action/determinant-response-sewing-and-relational-rigidity|determinant–response proposal]] and [[global-local-response-reconstruction/quantitative-descent-and-the-shape-of-a-gap|quantitative-descent programme]]. They need no new master framework:

- **Selection:** specify an elementary positive evaluation and allowed comparisons, so state and dynamics cannot be independently changed while preserving that law.
- **Rigidity:** derive a finite-word positivity certificate or an explicitly assembled bounded repair from its relations.
- **Realization:** construct the required four-dimensional Yang–Mills theory from the same law and identify the rigidity on its complete physical vacuum complement.

The active candidate is now **conditional boundary exchange**, stated explicitly in DS10a–c. The determinant's full joint law on frames and auxiliaries determines each actual cut conditional map \(C_p\). The constitutive rule admits \(T_p=C_p^*C_p\), their adjoints and inherited compositions, with one equal weight per declared physical cut occurrence. It forces
\[
\langle f,(I-T_p)f\rangle
=\mathbb E\operatorname{Var}(f\mid\eta_p).
\]
The determinant state, the conditional correlations and these relaxation rates consequently cannot be independently retuned. The conditional-exchange rule is postulated and falsifiable; positivity alone does not select it. Its remaining family inputs are named in the owner. The conjectural Yang–Mills return may be assumed while exploring consequences, provided no conditional consequence is reported as an established physical theorem.

The first test is the full sourced comparison under two cut/elimination orders, retaining the inherited memory. A disagreement revises this rule. It does not trigger an independently fitted extra pace. Universal autonomous Markov closure under spatial readout is not required and has already failed in narrower families.

The actual physical boundary-recovery response remains QD2,
\[
R_{\partial}=J_C^*(I-E_{\partial})J_C.
\]
It must be distinguished from the proposed sum of cut defects. [[global-local-response-reconstruction/boundary-frozen-heat-and-conditional-fisher-response|Boundary-frozen heat]] already supplies a constructed neutral response below the recovery defect. The categorical neutral action remains another conditional mechanism, not an additional prerequisite that must be completed before this chosen route can be explored.

## An algebraic certificate must explain its own constant

The certificate mechanism is sound. [[library/noncommutative-real-algebraic-geometry-of-kazhdans-property-t/inq|Ozawa's theorem]] supplies a precise group-algebra precedent, verified against its primary abstract. It does not supply a Yang–Mills comparison action or vacuum identification. The theorem concerns finitely generated groups with property \((T)\), not arbitrary non-Abelian operations.

For
\[
R^2-\kappa R=\sum_j B_j^*B_j,
\]
the \(B_j\) must be explicit finite words in the declared comparisons. Choosing a spectral square root after assuming the interval exclusion merely rewrites a gap assumption. QD6a–c now makes an existing negative control explicit: the neutral character sum in the sharpened \(SU(2)\) overlap family has a positive eigenvalue \(r_k\to0\). Its expectation of the proposed polynomial is \(r_k(r_k-\kappa)<0\) eventually. A stronger relation must therefore exclude or alter that concrete family for an independently stated reason.

A second qualification survives refinement. QD9–10 already proves that a completely positive readout \(\Phi\) introduces the subtracted remainder
\[
\Phi(R^2)-\Phi(R)^2\ge0.
\]
An algebraic certificate is preserved by an admissible representation; a coarse readout is not automatically such a representation. The full comparison must retain or control this remainder. This is where BF's prohibition against resetting the experiment serves the proposed rigidity route directly.

The Abelian control must also retain its stated carrier and scale. A finite compact Abelian rotor can be gapped. The relevant free-field comparison is the large-volume Maxwell or quadratic response at fixed physical calibration. [[general-causal-action/reciprocal-coefficients-and-the-field-gap-test|The existing quadratic test]] already supplies its soft neutral sequence.

## A concrete law is tested immediately

This review round tests an alternative elementary rule rather than adding another matched cell. [[general-causal-action/commutator-word-comparisons-and-neutral-soft-escape|NW1–13]] takes actual word moves
\[
x_i\longmapsto x_i[x_j,x_k],\qquad i,j,k\text{ distinct},
\]
with source-containing Haar evaluation and one symmetric jump rule. The moves preserve the state, change a neutral Wilson character, and become trivial for an Abelian group. They therefore avoid the simple failure of ordinary gauge transformations, which fix neutral observables.

Nevertheless, the moves fix the flat configuration and have identity first derivative there. Normalized neutral bumps of radius \(\varepsilon\) have response \(O(\varepsilon^2)\). Their actual joint-boundary recovery defect also tends to zero at every fixed slab half-width. Thus this precise law cannot have both a positive rigidity certificate and vacuum-only invariants. The proof does not assume ergodicity or claim to classify its full invariant subspace.

This is a useful failure because it identifies a relation the next primitive proposal must change. Simply making comparisons non-Abelian and observable is insufficient; their displacement may still vanish relative to a localized physical distinction. Conditional boundary exchange must face this test and the sharpening witness, not inherit a presumed gap from its name.
