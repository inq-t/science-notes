## The useful inversion retains more than one boundary

Obtaining symmetry and physical response from a prior asymmetry remains a legitimate direction of the programme. A canonical return determined by an inclusion and a state is a concrete object for that investigation. Contravariant recovery composition can organize a tower once its states, restrictions and support conventions are compatible. Neither construction alone supplies a contraction rate or a relativistic theory.

[[general-causal-action/paired-boundary-return-and-the-same-wall-identity|PB1–11]] gives the decisive comparison. The actual stationary chronological pair has two inclusions and their state-preserving retractions:
\[
E_-\iota_-=E_0\iota_0=I,\qquad
E_-\iota_0=P,\qquad E_0\iota_-=P^*.
\]
The same-wall returns are identities, while the cross-boundary return and source loss are
\[
D_{0\to-\to0}=P^*P,\qquad
\delta^*\delta=I-P^*P.
\]
These remain nontrivial for a commutative history algebra. For the selected reversible physical slab \(P=e^{-\ell H}\), the return is \(e^{-2\ell H}\). Replacing this composite by \(E_-\iota_-\) changes the diagram.

The [[general-causal-action/prepared-sources-and-the-modular-wall|prepared-source modular-wall theorem]] already proves that equal-time multiplication has trivial modular flow and its unrestricted dynamical completion has a nonseparating pure vacuum. It leaves proper regional modular algebras as a further construction. It does not exclude return of noncommutative physical dynamics from a positive Euclidean amplitude. [[general-causal-action/cycle-moments-and-the-pure-gauge-vacuum-return|CM14–21]] now returns the finite Wilson spectrum, selected vacuum histories and fixed physical-source transport from the same determinant family. Its gap bound is uniform in sufficiently large auxiliary-copy number at fixed graph and coupling; physical refinement remains open.

## What the receipts establish and what they change

The Gaussian matrix is an established specialization. Swingle and Wang's recovery matrix, with \(B\) the restriction to \(A\), immediately gives the small returned channel
\[
X=\left[\sqrt{I+\Gamma_R^2}\right]_{AA}
\left(\sqrt{I+\Gamma_A^2}\right)^{-1}.
\]
Their paper also treats singular matrices. This is a useful consequence of an existing theorem, rather than a new general recovery formula. [Swingle–Wang, Eq. (7) and Appendix C](https://arxiv.org/html/1811.04956v3)

The finite verification uses a pure five-site state and outer regions of three, four or five sites. Schmidt rank gives
\[
\operatorname{rank}\rho_R\le 2^{5-|R|}<2^{|R|}
\qquad(|R|\ge3).
\]
Thus **every tested outer restriction is nonfaithful**, including the apparently successful cases. When the inner restriction is faithful, the displayed formula can still define a unital completely positive small-algebra map. That is a nonfaithful extension of the calculation, not the faithful outer wall stated in the prose. In the final case the inner three-site restriction is also singular; its ordinary inverse is invalid.

Separately, if \(\Omega\) is cyclic and separating for finite-dimensional \(M\), then \(a\mapsto a\Omega\) is bijective and \(\dim\mathcal H=\dim M\). A proper subalgebra \(N\) has smaller dimension and cannot also be cyclic on that carrier. Faithful matrix-state examples are legitimate for Accardi–Cecchini maps in appropriate standard representations. They are not common-vector half-sided modular inclusions. Neither observation establishes the commentary's claimed necessity of type III\(_1\) for every state-inclusion tower.

The rate scripts diagonalize **parity-odd Majoranas**. For the one-site even observable algebra, the centered occupation direction has eigenvalue \(\det X=\lambda_1\lambda_2\). This follows from the Gaussian covariance action. The stored one-site results have \(\lambda_1=\lambda_2=\lambda\), so
\[
\lambda_{\rm even}=\lambda^2.
\]
If the proposed odd asymptotic \(\lambda\sim R^{-1/2}e^{-2R/\xi}\) holds, the even one-site asymptotic is \(R^{-1}e^{-4R/\xi}\); its critical power also doubles. This does not select a neutral Yang–Mills rate. The scripts' Jordan–Wigner representation also attaches strings to odd Majoranas, so local CAR and local spin regions must not be silently identified.

The asserted \(2/\xi\) rate remains numerical: the control script extrapolates two rounded local slopes with an assumed \(1/R\) correction, without an asymptotic remainder or certified quadrature/eigenvalue error. Gaussianity does provide a degree filtration with exterior powers of \(X\) on its diagonal blocks, supporting the multiplicative spectrum; the limited numerical checks alone would not prove that general statement.

A clock distinction is visible in this control itself. Fourier transformation of the script's quadratic matrix gives
\[
\varepsilon(k)=2|h-Je^{ik}|,\qquad
\inf_k\varepsilon(k)=2(h-J)\quad(h>J).
\]
The comparison rate is \(2\log(h/J)\), a spatial inverse length. The expressions differ at general lattice coupling. A relativistic scaling limit and space/time calibration are required to compare them as masses. More generally, \(H\mapsto cH\), \(c>0\), preserves its vacuum and every state-only wall channel while scaling excitation energies. Structural scale ratios remain possible outputs; an absolute energy scale requires a clock bridge.

## The missing comparison is mathematical

The same-wall Accardi–Cecchini map uses the KMS square-root mean. The relative-entropy Hessian uses the BKM logarithmic mean. [[general-causal-action/paired-boundary-return-and-the-same-wall-identity|PB12–15]] computes the difference for the faithful qubit \(\rho=(I+r\sigma_x)/2\), restricted to its diagonal algebra:
\[
\lambda_{\rm KMS}=\sqrt{1-r^2},\qquad
\lambda_{\rm BKM}=\frac{r}{\operatorname{artanh}r}.
\]
The normalized entropy-Hessian loss is \(1-r/\operatorname{artanh}r\), not \(1-\sqrt{1-r^2}\). Identifying the wall defect with entropy susceptibility therefore requires another comparison.

The proposed “gap implies grain” proof has missing arrows. Fawzi–Renner bounds recovery fidelity from conditional mutual information of a declared tripartite state. JRSWW supplies a universal recovery construction involving modular rotations. Neither directly bounds the centered operator norm of the specific nested-wall channel in the receipts. [Fawzi–Renner, main theorem](https://arxiv.org/html/1410.0664v3), [JRSWW, Theorem 2.1 and Remark 2.2](https://arxiv.org/html/1509.07127v3)

Covariance clustering does not automatically supply the needed conditional-information estimate. The faithful three-bit state
\[
\rho_t=(I+tZ_AZ_BZ_C)/8,\qquad 0<t<1,
\]
has product two-body marginals but
\[
I(A:C\mid B)=\log2-h_2((1+t)/2)>0,
\]
where \(h_2(p)=-p\log p-(1-p)\log(1-p)\). This identifies a missing conditioning step, not a counterexample to a suitably strengthened gap-to-recovery theorem. Covariance, mutual-information and mixing bounds have distinct hypotheses and boundary-size dependences. [Bluhm–Capel–Pérez-Hernández, introduction](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12477101/)

Nor does the largest eigenvalue on one fixed small algebra identify the softest physical excitation by definition. Its carrier must cover the relevant physical source directions, with quantitative control as regions and source complexity grow. Fixed-state fidelity and all-normalized-source contraction are different conclusions. The claimed RG inequality likewise requires a state-compatible channel diagram before comparing the selected return norms.

## Keep the spatial test, with a possible quantum extension

The immediate two-plaquette proposal must use the existing [[general-causal-action/regional-preparation-algebras-and-vacuum-support|regional support results]]. The genuine four-link/three-link cut is not separating at any magnetic coupling. At the balanced cut, actual positive-coupling support is established for the charged construction, but its descended multiplicity-one physical algebra is abelian. Loop coordinates are not a disjoint regional tensor partition. Flux-resolved return becomes an executable observable only after its charged or neutral algebra, boundary center and support are specified; a finite gauge graph is not automatically a finite matrix carrier.

A constructive extension of PB is a **paired quantum boundary span**
\[
N_-\xrightarrow{\iota_-}(M,\omega)
\xleftarrow{\iota_0}N_0.
\]
First test it where both boundary ranges admit state-preserving conditional expectations. Their GNS embeddings are isometries, the cross map is \(C=E_-\iota_0\), and its source loss is \(I-C^*C\). State-compatible inclusions remain GNS isometries without those expectations. For general Accardi–Cecchini spans, however, the KMS embeddings need not be isometries: their inclusion Gram terms must be retained, and the preceding GNS formula cannot simply be reused. The next construction must preserve ordered joint sources, prove four-boundary sewing identities and identify the cross map with the same physical chronology. This extends the boundary programme without presupposing an unrelated modular clock.
