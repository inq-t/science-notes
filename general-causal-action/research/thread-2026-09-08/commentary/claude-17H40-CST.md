## What the rounds actually built, in standard names

Strip the vocabulary and the sewn-overlap construction is this: a bivariate law π(x,y) = q(xy⁻¹)dxdy on a compact group with Haar marginals, and the operator L = 2 − P_x − P_y, which is the two-block Gibbs sampler for that law. Its spectrum is governed by the angle between the two conditional-expectation subspaces, and the gap 1 − ‖C_q‖₀ is "one minus the squared maximal canonical correlation." This is Halmos two-projection theory as applied to Gibbs samplers; Liu–Wong–Kong 1994 is already in your library, and Diaconis–Khare–Saloff-Coste 2008 compute exactly this kind of spectrum for exponential families. The note says no novelty is claimed for the underlying mathematics, which is right, but it may not be obvious from inside the vocabulary how completely standard the object is.

The binary member's gap being sech² is the identity 1 − tanh² = sech². The correlation r_k = tanh(k ln3/2) is the magnetization of a single Ising spin in a field of strength k ln3/2, and 1 − r_k² is its fluctuation. Every two-state system with an exponential weight produces this. The "flat-partner theorem" (∂ + tanh gives the Pöschl–Teller sech² potential) is the same logistic function appearing for the unrelated reason of SUSY shape invariance. The connection between them is that both are one-dimensional and logistic. This is exactly the situation PC1 was written for.

The refinement result is the statement that pointwise powers of a kernel peaked at the identity concentrate to a Gaussian of covariance 12/k, so that convolution by them is approximately the heat semigroup e^{−6D_Q/k}, and k(I − C_k) → 12 D_Q. The character-convolution closure under sewing, with Fubini making the order irrelevant, is the machinery of the 1D chain and Migdal's exact 2D solution; the note's own remark that a single face weight does not close under four-dimensional elimination is the precise reason the 2D solution does not extend. That sentence in the proposal is the entire Yang–Mills problem, stated in passing.

## The "two-clock obstruction" is, I think, a misreading

This is the point most worth your attention, because round 3 is planned around it. The checkpoint says no common scalar pace keeps both the collective and relative sectors finite, and concludes that the composition law must be revised.

Read x and y as successive Euclidean time slices, with p the one-step transfer kernel and u the intermediate slice integrated out. Then q = p∗p is the two-step kernel, and the physical Hamiltonian is H = −(1/a) log C_p with a ∝ 1/k. I checked: −k log b_{k,n} → 6c_n, so H → 6D_Q, the free rigid rotor on SU(2), whose gap is the Casimir. The "accelerated collective clock" 12D_Q is exactly twice this, because 1 − b² ≈ −log b² for two steps. So the collective branch is not an analogue of something physical; it is the continuum-time limit of a single link with zero magnetic term, the strong-coupling electric Hamiltonian. And the "relative" mode x − y between adjacent time slices, with rate ∝ k ∝ 1/a, is a UV mode. Its expulsion from the positive-time heat limit is what a correct continuum-time limit does. Nothing is obstructed. Under this reading the plan to revise the primitive so as to retain finite relative dynamics is a plan to keep a lattice doubler.

There is a second reading that the proposal's "presentations, not spacetime points" stance permits: x and y as adjacent spatial links. Then xy⁻¹ ≈ exp(aA) is the connection, and "no common pace makes both sectors finite" is the statement that the electric term scales as g²/a and the magnetic term as 1/(g²a), so that a finite continuum limit needs g to run. That is the Kogut–Susskind Hamiltonian and asymptotic freedom, in other words the RG. Under either reading the obstruction has a name, and in neither case does it point toward a new composition law. It points toward the transfer-matrix reading in the first case and toward the coupling flow in the second. I would write the transfer-matrix interpretation into the module before round 3, because it changes the next action from "revise" to "reinterpret."

One further consequence of the Gibbs-sampler identification: the L_θ retuning counterexample, which you correctly use to show that positivity and sewing alone do not select the clock, is Parisi–Wu's basic observation. Any reversible dynamics with a given invariant measure reproduces its Euclidean correlators; the fictitious-time gap is not the physical gap. The physical gap is a property of the state (exponential clustering along one direction), not of the refresh dynamics. Your architecture "conditional refresh → generator → gap" is the stochastic-quantization route, and the vault should say so and then decide which gap it is talking about.

## What is good about the recategorization

The discipline is the best thing in the module, and it is not decoration. The independent-retuning test and the disguised-input test are exactly the two tests that fail nearly every "proof" of the mass gap that defines a positive operator and calls its gap the mass. The insistence that the gap is a rate in a properly calibrated clock, not a floor, survives from the 09-05 Q* discussion and is right. The restart note's negative control (a forgetting map cannot select the spectrum of what it forgets) is the correct kill for the idea that directedness by itself produces mass. And the observation that the joint law across contexts carries information absent from either marginal, and is therefore where a new primitive must sit, is structurally sound; it is a finite shadow of the fact that the gap in a gauge theory is a statement about clustering between separated regions, which in the operator-algebraic setting is Buchholz–Wichmann nuclearity and the split property.

The "joint realization" itself is, as a mathematical type, functorial QFT: a category of boundary presentations, sewing as composition, † as Osterwalder–Schrader reflection, positivity of Z(p†∘q) as the Hilbert space. That is Segal's axioms with unitarity, sharpened by Kontsevich–Segal. It is a legitimate and fully respectable recategorization of what a QFT is. It has been spectacularly successful for topological theories and for 2D conformal ones, where the sewing constraints are strong enough to determine amplitudes, and it has produced no nonperturbative four-dimensional construction, because in four dimensions sewing constrains amplitudes without supplying them. Your proposal's demand for "closure under composition of complete boundary amplitudes and responses" is equivalent to demanding the full theory as the primitive. The categorical dress does not reduce the analytic work; it relocates it. The 09-06 memory said the same about the fourth method, and it applies here unchanged: this characterizes given the theory, and existence still needs a construction.

## Where it does not touch the problem

Every object in the thread lives on a compact configuration space or a finite lattice, where a gap is automatic by compact resolvent. The Clay gap is the survival of a gap through the joint limit L → ∞, a → 0, g → 0 in which the naive scaling sends the gap to zero or infinity and only dimensional transmutation, m ∝ (1/a)e^{−1/2b₀g²}, gives a finite answer. Nothing in the module has a parameter from which e^{−1/g²} could emerge; the refinement parameter k is a UV cutoff on a single group variable, and the result that the collective mode has a uniform gap is the statement that in lattice units at fixed coupling the gap is O(1). Everything here is in the regime the 09-03 assessment identified for the earlier work: strong coupling, small volume. The "certified Yang–Mills interface benchmark" is a seven-link, two-plaquette Kogut–Susskind quantum mechanics at κ = β = 1. The interval enclosures to 10⁻¹² are real craft, but the object is not Yang–Mills in the member/family sense your own category-error checklist flags; it is finite quantum mechanics with SU(2) variables.

On asymmetry-first: the philosophical thesis that symmetry groups are stabilizers of a prior oriented structure has one rigorous form, Doplicher–Roberts reconstruction, where the compact group is derived from the category of superselection sectors rather than posited. Your vault already knows the sting: on the physical net of pure Yang–Mills, DR gives a trivial group, and the gauge group lives in Buchholz–Verch's scaling limit. So the one rigorous version of "symmetry emerges" says the gauge group is not a symmetry of the physical theory at all. That is a substantive claim and a publishable one, but it makes step 2 of the proposal ("derive the local symmetry from the oriented primitive") point in an awkward direction: the thing to be derived is a property of the UV limit theory, not of the theory. For the gap itself, directedness is orthogonal. The quotient-clock theorem shows a one-sided semigroup descends to a unitary group, but the gap lives in the reversible quotient, and the discarded part is what the negative control says cannot select it.

On the cosmological branch: your own 09-06 rule is not to import cosmology into the Clay module, and the proposal firewalls it, which is right. Δ_YM/T_c is a fine dimensionless target (lattice gives m_{0++}/T_c ≈ 5–6 for SU(3), from memory), but it needs the theory to exist first.

## What I would do

Three things, in my opinion. First, before revising anything, write the transfer-matrix reading of the sewn overlap into the module as the canonical interpretation, retire the "obstruction" as a UV artifact, and re-derive the round-2 results as the continuum-time limit of the one-link rotor. That converts two rounds of correct work into a correctly labeled positive control instead of a puzzle.

Second, take seriously the positive control the 09-06 plan set and this thread has not met. The sewn-chain machinery is exactly a chain of compact-group variables. Extend it to a two-dimensional lattice of S² or SU(2) variables and you have the O(3) σ-model or principal chiral model: asymptotically free, gapped, exact m/Λ known, gap unproven. If the joint-realization primitive cannot produce that gap, it will not produce the Yang–Mills one; if it can, that is a Clay-independent theorem worth having. Any four-dimensional claim before this is premature by your own standards.

Third, import the precedents that have done what the proposal describes, so the module knows where the wall is. Brothier–Stottmeister (2019, 2020) built operator-algebraic lattice-to-continuum limits with refinement as a directed category, using Jones's Thompson-group technology, and obtained 1+1 Yang–Mills; Stottmeister, Morinelli, Morsella and Tanimoto did the free field by wavelet renormalization; Grundling–Rudolph constructed C*-algebraic QCD on the infinite lattice with dynamics. These are "algebra and state first, geometry afterward" constructions for this exact system, and they stall where everyone stalls. I am citing these from memory; verify before filing, per the library rule.

The deliverable I think you can actually defend, given the profile of the vault and your stated aim, is not a construction but the philosophy-of-physics paper the module keeps circling: what is a gauge group a symmetry of, if DR on the physical net gives a trivial group and the group lives in the scaling limit? That has a real literature to engage (Healey, Weatherall, Rovelli's "Why gauge?", Earman, the Brading–Castellani volume) and the vault's typing discipline is a contribution to it. The Clay problem is the motivating example, not the thesis.
