# Review of the Modular-Axiom Proposal

The [[general-causal-action/research/thread-2026-09-08/commentary/claude-21H20-CST.md|21H20 commentary]] proposes constructing algebra inclusions, faithful regional states, causal access and marked response together beneath the Wightman presentation. This is a useful direction for the joint-realization programme. Its assembled theorem claims are stronger than the cited results, and several intermediate identifications are false. The concrete advance is [[general-causal-action/prepared-sources-and-the-modular-wall|the prepared-source wall test]]: neither the returned commutative source algebra nor its unrestricted dynamical completion supplies the required modular regions.

## What the proposal adds to the current construction

The next algebraic object should carry **proper regional operator algebras and their relative modular positions**, alongside the already returned state, source algebra and clock. The prepared-source theorem proves this is additional information: the faithful equal-time algebra has modular operator one, while adjoining even one positive heat step generates the full operator algebra, on which the pure vacuum is not separating. A regional construction must lie between these operations with its own proof of cyclicity, separatingness and coherent transport.

The [[general-causal-action/access-ports-and-conditional-sewing|access-port construction]] and [[general-causal-action/conditional-access-families-and-the-returned-clock|conditional families]] give actual examples of another part of the proposed object. They distinguish pure presentation, new access, state restriction and temporal processing, retaining the full source amplitudes. Their root-change obstruction is useful evidence: a category of unspecified arrows cannot decide which normalization law is physically retained. A compatible comparison with its response defect is a research target, not a theorem supplied by the word “fibration.”

## Corrections that affect the proposed axiom system

- **Modular reconstruction has a substantial input contract.** Araki–Zsidó requires compatible standard representations and a half-sided inclusion with a common suitable weight or cyclic/separating vector. Its positive translation comes from two modular logarithms, not from the logarithm of one modular operator. The local [[library/extension-of-borchers-structure-theorem/inq|primary-source module]] and [[wall-construction-interface/half-sided-modular-tunnel|transport theorem]] own these hypotheses. In four dimensions, the geometric modular-action result uses declared wedge indexing and further incidence, transitivity, continuity and stability conditions; its spectrum conclusion initially allows either time orientation. An arbitrary poset plus a faithful state is not this data. See [Buchholz–Dreyer–Florig–Summers, Theorem 5.1.2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math-ph/9805026).
- **A single half-sided translation cannot have the proposed positive gap.** Its nontrivial spectrum is dilation invariant down to zero. The existing [[mass-scale-calibration/joint-causal-generators-and-the-mass-casimir|joint-causal-generator theorem]] already gives the viable reversal: construct compatible oppositely scaling translations and a Poincare representation, then prove a full-carrier invariant lower bound. Two generators with the same modular weight do not repair the problem. Non-geometric modular action on a bounded region is not established as an equivalent definition of mass or a uniform mass gap.
- **Global purity, regional faithfulness and modular thermality are distinct.** The [[algebra/faithful-stationary-states-and-the-positive-clock|faithful-state clock theorem]] excludes a nontrivial positive stationary clock preserving an entire algebra on which the vacuum is cyclic and separating. The pure global vacuum can instead be nonfaithful on the global operator algebra and faithful on appropriate regional algebras that time translates. Faithful states are KMS for their own modular dynamics regardless of a purification narrative; the factor \(2\pi\) belongs to the normalization relating modular parameter to geometric boost rapidity. Neither global purity alone nor the basic Wightman list establishes type \(\mathrm{III}_1\) for every proposed local algebra.
- **Commutation is not statistical factorization in the vacuum.** Commuting tensor factors can carry a Bell state with zero separate \(Z\)-means and unit \(Z\otimes Z\) correlation. [[global-local-response-reconstruction/causal-patch-boundary-and-two-times|Causal independence distinctions]] owns the required separation. Likewise, [[conformal-scale-geometry/causal-order-and-metric-scale|causal-order reconstruction]] applies to appropriately regular distinguishing spacetimes; a generic poset has not thereby acquired a manifold and conformal metric.
- **Monotone information metrics are not one Hessian.** The Umegaki relative-entropy Hessian gives BKM, while Bures/SLD has a different coefficient. [[algebra/jordan-covariance-and-the-entropy-weighted-ball|The explicit qubit comparison]] exhibits the difference. Other generalized relative entropies can generate other monotone metrics, as in [Lesniewski–Ruskai](https://arxiv.org/abs/math-ph/9808016). Monotonicity alone does not select the programme's susceptibility, clock or state.
- **Passivity must not be confused with complete passivity.** For \(H=\operatorname{diag}(0,1,2)\), the state \(\rho=\operatorname{diag}(1/2,1/3,1/6)\) is passive by rearrangement of its decreasing populations, but is neither a ground state nor Gibbs: its successive population ratios differ. The equilibrium characterization by ground or KMS states uses complete passivity, requiring passivity for arbitrary tensor powers. See [Pusz–Woronowicz](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01614224). This stability condition still refers to a supplied dynamics and does not imply a gap.
- **Scaling algebra is not the claimed irreversible boundary fibration.** Buchholz–Verch's scaling action on positive-scale functions is automorphic, hence invertible. Scaling limits are obtained from possibly multiple weak-star limit states and their GNS quotient nets, not from a supplied unique fiber at zero or an infrared point fiber. The [[library/scaling-algebras-and-renormalization-group-in-algebraic-quantum-field-theory/inq|canonical source module]] and [primary paper, equation 3.14 and section 4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-th/9501063) establish the distinction. An irreversible comparison law and a categorical lifting law would be extra constructions.
- **The suggested category needs typed arrows.** Normal completely positive maps must be unital to pull normalized states back. A map between inclusions must specify what happens to the included algebra, state and modular data. [[spectral-wall-descent/conditional-expectation-balance|State-preserving conditional expectations]] already require substantive compatibility. A fibration needs cartesian lifts; having scale labels is insufficient. A category fibred in groupoids may have noninvertible arrows over a noninvertible base, so “stack” versus “fibred category” is not a universal reversible/irreversible dichotomy. See the [[basic-concepts/stacks/inq|local account]] and [the precise fibration definition](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/02XJ).
- **Local observables and sharp one-particle position are different tests.** Newton–Wigner restrictions on particle position do not say a gapless local field theory has no local observables or no facts. Buchholz's infraparticle conclusion concerns electric charge detected by Gauss law, not every charged state of an arbitrary gapless theory; see [the primary result](https://doi.org/10.1016/0370-2693(86)91110-X). The proposed equivalences between record storage, mass, bounded modular non-geometricity, a vanishing infrared anomaly coefficient and a spectral gap are not proved by the cited chain. A qualitative terminal limit is insufficient: [[algebra/finite-trace-and-gapless-mixing|gapless mixing]] has a unique limiting vacuum under strong convergence but no uniform decay rate, while [[algebra/characters-factorization-and-the-modular-carrier|factorized response examples]] retain either gapped or gapless normalized spectral measures.
- **Returning a net and returning the required fields require scope checks.** The proposed D,C,T,I list is not an established theorem implying the full Wightman requirements on the intended carrier. For example, [Bostelmann's Theorem 4.3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math-ph/0409070) uses a microscopic phase-space condition to construct pointlike Wightman fields and may initially yield a smaller carrier. The discussion permits nonpointlike components. Field regularity, coverage, four-dimensional covariance and the [[contemporary-puzzles/yang-mills-mass-gap/clay-contract-and-scale-assumptions|Yang–Mills return contract]] remain explicit obligations.

## Research decision

Retain the proposal to build modular regions and their common state from the same primitive comparisons. Add the prepared-source wall theorem as a new construction boundary. Preserve the existing opposite-null and physical susceptibility routes rather than replacing their estimates with a statement that scale descent terminates. Seek a concrete regional algebra or comparison square next; the desired global/local duality must carry its algebra, state, clock, response and normalization through that square.

A useful discriminating test is to append a soft independent sector. Modular behavior retained in one factor cannot certify a gap on the product carrier, whose complete source algebra exposes the new soft direction. This is a test of a proposed certificate, not a pure Yang–Mills candidate. A successful modular comparison must therefore reach the [[general-causal-action/conditional-vacuum-rigidity-and-the-physical-gap|complete physical source bound]] on the same reconstructed clock, including all required sectors, rather than only detect modular behavior in a selected region or factor.
