## The constructive part: calibrate the whole experiment

Gauge-frame change, transport through a physical background, change of units, scale evolution, and restriction to a regional algebra are different operations. An experiment includes its state, observable sources, time calibration and generator. Returning only an unordered spectrum loses most of that information.

At the finite physical benchmark, [[general-causal-action/prepared-readout-algebra-and-physical-source-completeness|prepared sources]] and one actual positive heat step generate the full operator algebra by [[general-causal-action/prepared-sources-and-the-modular-wall|MW6]]. Consequently a closed presentation transport fixing every such source and that heat step is scalar phase; fixing the vacuum vector removes even that phase. This is an exact relational calibration result. It already assumes a complex Hilbert carrier, and does not derive complex numbers, gauge compactness, a mass scale or a gap.

The same specialist owner separates physical holonomy from gauge relabelling with the exact circle example
\[
H_\alpha=(-i\partial_\theta-\alpha)^2,
\qquad E_n=(n-\alpha)^2.
\]
Integer shifts of \(\alpha\) are unitary gauge changes that relabel the same spectrum. Changing \(e^{2\pi i\alpha}\) changes the physical flux and can change the spectrum. Thus phase holonomy is not restricted to an interference effect with no possible spectral consequence. What preserves spectra is the appropriate unitary equivalence of the complete physical problem.

A finite positive gap also forbids a nontrivial positive scale factor around an exact unitary presentation loop, since the lower edge would satisfy \(\Delta=a\Delta\). This is a corollary of the existing [[mass-scale-calibration/internal-yardstick-as-a-generalized-rate-edge|unitary-dilation obstruction]], not its converse. Flat scale transport can accompany a gapless Hamiltonian. Nor must all unitary holonomy vanish merely because energies agree; degenerate spectral subspaces can carry nontrivial rotations.

## Where the spectral argument changes its meaning

- **The clock coupling is essential to Einstein's objection.** History-dependent clock calibration would be a substantive prediction, but Weyl geometry alone is not a complete model of atomic clocks. [Hobson and Lasenby](https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.06407) construct Weyl gauge theories with appropriately scaled matter and clock transport and no second clock effect. This is not a vindication of Weyl's original identification or an acceptance of those gravity models; it defeats the commentary's blanket exclusion of every spacetime scale gauge. Bounds on dimensionless constant variation cannot be assigned to an unspecified scale connection. Identical atoms must also be compared in the same physical environment; external fields, motion and gravitational comparison can alter measured transitions.
- **Exact scale symmetry does not mean no spectrum.** A dilation-covariant nonzero positive Hamiltonian has spectrum reaching zero; that is a spectral statement, not the absence of one. Compact spatial geometry can introduce discrete energy levels without supplying a Minkowski mass gap. The [[contemporary-puzzles/yang-mills-mass-gap/puzzle-as-posed|Clay scale distinction]] is the relevant target.
- **Dimensional transmutation does not select a gap or an absolute member.** With \(\beta(g)=\mu\,dg/d\mu\), a formal renormalization-group invariant has the form \(\Lambda=\mu\exp[-\int^g du/\beta(u)]\), with an integration convention and trajectory datum. Reaching the same ultraviolet fixed point does not erase that freedom. The trace anomaly, its operator normalization and curved-background terms require their own conventions; an anomaly identity alone proves no positive physical lower edge. Statements that every mass ratio is fixed must hold the group, global form, any admitted \(\theta\)-sector and the definition of \(\Lambda\) fixed.
- **Rigidity can address positivity.** The third response correctly distinguishes fixedness from positivity, then asserts that structural rigidity cannot prove the floor. No such impossibility follows. The intended estimate \(\Delta/\Lambda>0\) is dimensionless once a scale convention and trajectory are supplied. [[general-causal-action/conditional-vacuum-rigidity-and-the-physical-gap|Complete-source rigidity]] states a precise sufficient inequality for that positivity. A uniform proof could be structural; dimensional transmutation by itself is insufficient. The programme retains this target rather than treating historical analogy as a no-go theorem.
- **The universal modular factor is not a selected spectral unit.** Under the precise Bisognano--Wichmann hypotheses, \(2\pi\) relates modular parameter to boost rapidity. It occurs for massive free fields of different masses as well as massless examples, so it cannot determine their mass or gap. An atom's proper-time generator is not its modular generator by definition. [[mass-scale-calibration/quantity-lines-and-conformal-scales|Quantity lines]] and [[mass-scale-calibration/hbar-clock-and-the-calibration-firewall|clock calibration]] also distinguish the structural roles of \(c,\hbar\) from their numerical values in chosen units. Neither conversion quantity selects a metric scale section.
- **Regional modular spectra may change when the region changes.** [[general-causal-action/regional-preparation-algebras-and-vacuum-support|The balanced-cut construction]] can change the Schmidt problem while pure subdivision preserves the full physical amplitude. The selected regional modular generator need not be an absolute atomic frequency. [[general-causal-action/modular-recurrence-and-the-regional-limit|Modular recurrence]] proves why the finite faithful type-I factors cannot already realize a proper common-vector half-sided inclusion. A physical modular limit must be constructed, then tied to the tested clock.

The review does not adopt the commentary's numerical varying-constant bounds or historical letter quotations as verified local data. The broad historical motivation is retained without promoting its chronology, attributions or retrospective claims of vindication into theorem premises.

## A mixed comparison square replaces vertical curvature

The proposed phrase “flat horizontally, curved vertically” is not yet a curvature formula. An ordinary connection restricted to a one-dimensional scale interval has no curvature two-form. Moreover, [[scale-score-connection/inq|the scale-score theorem]] proves that a state path alone does not select its connection. A trace anomaly is not automatically the curvature of that unspecified object.

A concrete replacement has two parameters and typed arrows. Let \(\alpha_{xy,s}:\mathcal A_{x,s}\to\mathcal A_{y,s}\) be a declared presentation isomorphism and let \(R_{x,s'\to s}:\mathcal A_{x,s'}\to\mathcal A_{x,s}\) be a declared unital physical readout. Both routes below have domain \(\mathcal A_{x,s'}\) and codomain \(\mathcal A_{y,s}\):
\[
\mathfrak D_{xy;s',s}
=\alpha_{xy,s}R_{x,s'\to s}
-R_{y,s'\to s}\alpha_{xy,s'}.
\]
For pure presentation equivalence, test whether this defect vanishes on the complete prepared source algebra, with compatible states and clock calibration. Retain the full scalar normalization as well as normalized expectations. For a noninvertible readout, this is a comparison defect, not ordinary connection curvature. A real physical extension may change the old processed experiment and need not obey a presentation identity.

The current [[general-causal-action/access-ports-and-conditional-sewing|root-change obstruction]] and [[general-causal-action/conditional-preparation-diagrams-and-ancestral-readout|ancestral readout theorem]] supply actual cases on which to compute this square. ## What survives of the unified-field analogy

The useful demand is that matter, state and motion should share a constructive origin, and that an economical notation must not conceal independently adjustable inputs. The existing [[general-causal-action/research-schema|independent-retuning and disguised-input tests]] already make this demand operational. Several claimed historical realizations need narrower readings:

- [[library/the-spectral-action-principle/inq|The spectral action]] returns an action from specified spectral data. [[symmetry-groups-select/finite-algebra-filters|The represented finite geometry]] still contains a representation and Dirac/Yukawa data. Similarly, Doplicher--Roberts reconstructs a group from a suitable supplied sector category; it does not select that category from no data. These are valuable construction precedents with inputs.
- [[philosophy/principle-of-least-action/einstein-hilbert-action|Metric-action uniqueness]] has a restricted local pure-metric, four-dimensional, second-order scope. It is not uniqueness of every possible gravity theory. In trace-free gravity the general matter term must also be trace free, \(T_{\mu\nu}-g_{\mu\nu}T/4\). With conservation, the omitted trace integrates to a constant; its value is not thereby selected, and the equations are not automatically Weyl invariant. [[library/cosmological-constant-and-general-covariance/inq|The canonical source]] owns this distinction.
- Consistency identities do not supply all dynamics without matter and regularity assumptions. The [Komargodski--Schwimmer argument](https://arxiv.org/abs/1107.3987) uses dilaton scattering and unitarity in addition to anomaly consistency. Its monotonicity is not a theorem of an ontological time arrow or a mass floor. Charge integrality is not a spectral-gap bound; Euclidean instantons are not themselves Lorentzian particle states.
- Internal gauge fibers, renormalization scale and modular parameter must stay distinct. A cylinder condition by itself does not select a compact circle fiber; a principal-bundle gauge theory is not automatically a higher-dimensional Einstein theory. The [[library/scaling-algebras-and-renormalization-group-in-algebraic-quantum-field-theory/inq|scaling-algebra construction]] also shows that scale actions and limiting readouts need not be one irreversible semigroup. A radion, a Weyl compensator and an RG scale require separate identifications.
- Bell-type restrictions do not uniquely select the commentary's proposed realism. Gleason-type results represent suitable probability assignments by a density operator under their Hilbert-space hypotheses; they do not choose that density operator or actual outcomes. Likewise, the claimed stable horizon cosmos is not established by philosophical closure. [[global-local-response-reconstruction/cosmological-balance-and-vacuum-gap-conjecture|The existing instability control]] and [[global-local-response-reconstruction/cosmological-reconvergence-contract|cosmological return]] remain necessary.

The research decision is to retain complete experimental calibration and the two-parameter comparison test. Continue constructing actual frame actions, coherent preparation laws, faithful regional limits and full-source rigidity. Neither a historical lineage nor a universal prohibition on rigidity replaces those constructions.
