# Commutator Word Comparisons and Neutral Soft Escape

A concrete primitive law can act on neutral observables, preserve a positive state, select its comparison clock and vanish for Abelian groups, yet still fail to force a vacuum-complement floor. Symmetric commutator word moves provide such a discriminator. Their first derivative is the identity near a flat configuration, permitting normalized neutral distinctions with arbitrarily small response. The same escape survives in the actual joint-boundary recovery defect at every fixed slab thickness. This rejects the proposed conjunction of rigidity and vacuum-only invariants for this law; it does not assume or determine its full invariant space.

## The elementary law includes observable insertions

Declare a compact connected non-Abelian Lie group \(G\), a faithful finite-dimensional unitary representation \(\rho\), and a finite number \(n\ge3\) of loop variables. The integer \(n\) is an inventory of words, not a spacetime dimension. Use product Haar probability \(\mu\) on \(G^n\) and the complete neutral carrier
\[
\mathcal H=L^2(G^n,\mu)^{\operatorname{Ad}G},\qquad
\Omega=1,\qquad
\mathcal A_0=C(G^n/\operatorname{Ad}G).
\tag{NW1}
\]
For distinct \(i,j,k\), define the actual invertible word move
\[
N_{i;jk}(x)_i=x_i[x_j,x_k],\qquad
[y,z]=yzy^{-1}z^{-1},
\qquad N_{i;jk}(x)_a=x_a\quad(a\ne i).
\tag{NW2}
\]
Its inverse replaces the commutator by its inverse. Because \(j,k\ne i\), the other coordinates are fixed during the move; conditional right-Haar invariance proves preservation of \(\mu\). Every move commutes with simultaneous conjugation. Let \(S\) be the finite symmetric list containing these moves and their inverses, with \(j<k\) to avoid duplicating the same pair.

The comparison algebra is generated by multiplication \(M_f\), \(f\in\mathcal A_0\), and the unitaries
\[
U_sf=f\circ N_s^{-1}.
\]
Its relations include word composition, \(U_s^*=U_{s^{-1}}\), and
\[
U_sM_fU_s^*=M_{f\circ N_s^{-1}}.
\tag{NW3}
\]
Evaluate a marked expression \(A\) by
\[
\mathcal Z(A)=\langle\Omega,A\Omega\rangle
=\int(A1)(x)\,d\mu(x),\qquad
\mathcal Z(A^*A)=\|A1\|_2^2\ge0.
\tag{NW4}
\]
Multiplication sources make \(\Omega\) cyclic on the full carrier (NW1). Comparison words alone all fix \(1\), so their unmarked Gram matrix would be identically one; discarding the source algebra would discard the neutral distinctions being tested. No nontrivial comparison-word-only GNS carrier is claimed.

Equal weights select the dimensionless process
\[
A_S=\frac1{|S|}\sum_{s\in S}U_s,\qquad
L=I-A_S
=\frac1{2|S|}\sum_{s\in S}(I-U_s)^*(I-U_s),
\]
\[
0\le L\le2I,\qquad
T_t=e^{-tL}
=e^{-t}\sum_{m=0}^\infty\frac{t^m}{m!}A_S^m.
\tag{NW5}
\]
This is a symmetric Haar-preserving Markov semigroup. Its jump clock, state norm and source transformations have been fixed by the same declared comparison rule; no Hamiltonian or vacuum projector was used to define \(L\). The unit Poisson rate is a dimensionless convention. Haar evaluation, the move list and that convention remain constitutive inputs, rather than a claimed derivation of four-dimensional Yang–Mills.

The neutral action is nontrivial. For
\(F_i(x)=\operatorname{Re}\operatorname{Tr}\rho(x_i)\),
choose \(x_i=e\) and \(x_j,x_k\) with nonidentity commutator \(c\). Then
\[
F_i(x)=\dim\rho,\qquad F_i(N_{i;jk}x)
=\operatorname{Re}\operatorname{Tr}\rho(c)<\dim\rho.
\tag{NW6}
\]
Faithfulness gives the strict inequality. Continuity makes the change nonzero in \(L^2\), not merely at a measure-zero point. Ordinary gauge transformations could not do this to a neutral \(F_i\).

The ambitious conjecture for this explicit law would be a finite positive certificate for \(L\), together with \(\ker L=\mathbb C\Omega\). The next calculation rejects that conjunction without replacing either condition by an assumed gap.

## The flat first derivative permits neutral soft escape

Fix a bi-invariant metric on \(G\), write \(d=\dim G\), and use exponential coordinates near \((e,\ldots,e)\). For bounded \(X_a\),
\[
[e^{\varepsilon X_j},e^{\varepsilon X_k}]
=I+\varepsilon^2[X_j,X_k]+O(\varepsilon^3).
\tag{NW7}
\]
Thus every move and its inverse changes a tuple within distance \(O(\varepsilon)\) of the flat point by only \(O(\varepsilon^2)\). Its first derivative at that point is the identity.

Choose a nonzero smooth radial bump \(a_\varepsilon\), supported where
\(\sum_a\operatorname{dist}(x_a,e)^2<\varepsilon^2\), by rescaling one fixed bump. Bi-invariance makes it neutral. Smooth positive Haar density gives
\[
\|a_\varepsilon\|_2^2\asymp\varepsilon^{nd},\qquad
\mu a_\varepsilon=O(\varepsilon^{nd}),\qquad
\|\nabla a_\varepsilon\|_\infty\le C/\varepsilon .
\]
Set
\[
f_\varepsilon
=\frac{a_\varepsilon-\mu a_\varepsilon}
{\|a_\varepsilon-\mu a_\varepsilon\|_2},\qquad
\|f_\varepsilon\|_2=1,\quad
\langle1,f_\varepsilon\rangle=0.
\tag{NW8}
\]
The denominator is comparable to \(\varepsilon^{nd/2}\).

The support of \(a_\varepsilon\circ N_s-a_\varepsilon\) lies in a ball of radius \(C\varepsilon\). Indeed, if the image lies in the small ball, the unchanged \(j,k\) variables are small and the inverse commutator changes \(x_i\) by \(O(\varepsilon^2)\). On this support, (NW7) and the gradient estimate give
\[
\|a_\varepsilon\circ N_s-a_\varepsilon\|_2^2
\le C\varepsilon^{nd+2}.
\]
Centering cancels in the difference. Since the move list is finite,
\[
\boxed{\quad
\|(I-U_s)f_\varepsilon\|_2^2\le C_s\varepsilon^2,\qquad
\langle f_\varepsilon,Lf_\varepsilon\rangle
\le C\varepsilon^2\longrightarrow0 .
\quad}
\tag{NW9}
\]
Here \(\varepsilon\) is a configuration-amplitude scale. The group, number of words, move rates and physical slab convention are held fixed.

Consequently no \(\kappa>0\) obeys \(L\ge\kappa(I-P_\Omega)\). In particular the two assertions
\[
L^2-\kappa L=\sum_b B_b^*B_b,\quad \kappa>0,
\qquad
\ker L=\mathbb C\Omega
\tag{NW10}
\]
cannot both hold in this representation. The first assertion alone concerns the complement of the entire kernel. The construction has not proved ergodicity, so (NW9) does not exclude a positive gap above some larger invariant space.

There is a sharper conditional consequence: \(f_\varepsilon\rightharpoonup0\), since its normalized support shrinks and its centering constant tends to zero. If a positive gap above the full kernel did exist, (NW9) would force \(\operatorname{dist}(f_\varepsilon,\ker L)\to0\). A finite-dimensional kernel cannot contain such a weakly vanishing sequence of asymptotic norm one. Thus such a gap would require an infinite-dimensional invariant space, which also fails the vacuum-only target.

The argument extends to any fixed finite family of smooth measure-preserving neutral moves fixing the flat point and having identity first derivative there, with a smooth positive invariant density there. It diagnoses their vanishing displacement relative to the size of a localized distinction. Mere noncommutativity of the move group does not repair it.

## The actual boundary defect fails at fixed slab thickness

Use the stationary reversible process selected by (NW5), on the neutral configuration quotient. For a fixed independently declared slab half-width \(\ell>0\), let \(Jf=f(X_0)\) in its whole path-space \(L^2\) carrier, and let \(P_\partial\) be conditional expectation onto the joint boundary \((X_{-\ell},X_\ell)\). The actual [[global-local-response-reconstruction/quantitative-descent-and-the-shape-of-a-gap|QD2 recovery defect]] is
\[
R_\ell=J^*(I-P_\partial)J .
\tag{NW11}
\]
The joint boundary contains the right boundary alone. Reversibility gives
\(\mathbb E[f(X_0)\mid X_\ell]=T_\ell f(X_\ell)\).
Therefore
\[
0\le\langle f,R_\ell f\rangle
\le\|f\|_2^2-\|T_\ell f\|_2^2
=\langle f,(I-e^{-2\ell L})f\rangle
\le2\ell\langle f,Lf\rangle .
\tag{NW12}
\]
The inequality is an upper bound on this defect, suitable for the present countertest; it must not be reversed into a positive certificate.

Applying (NW9) gives
\[
\boxed{\langle f_\varepsilon,R_\ell f_\varepsilon\rangle
\le C\ell\varepsilon^2\longrightarrow0
\quad\text{at every fixed }\ell>0.}
\tag{NW13}
\]
Thus the proposed vacuum-complement floor fails for the actual selected joint-boundary recovery experiment as well as for the jump generator. Letting the slab grow with \(\varepsilon^{-1}\) would change the fixed-scale question.

For Abelian \(G\), every move is the identity: \(L=0\), the selected process is frozen, and \(R_\ell=0\). This is an exact zero-response control, not a construction of Maxwell dynamics. Non-Abelian \(G\) has nontrivial neutral action by (NW6), but still no vacuum-complement floor by (NW13). The law therefore fails to distinguish the two cases in the way a Yang–Mills rigidity mechanism requires.

## What the failed law rules out

This is a test of a specified elementary rule, not another matched cell operator. It avoids the gauge-fixed-point and formal-Wilson-vacuum failures in [[categorical-gauge-response/categorical-action-on-the-neutral-wilson-carrier|the neutral carrier audit]], yet fails by a different mechanism: weakly visible distinctions concentrate near the flat locus.

Replacing a finite move average by the projection onto its complete orbit-invariant algebra would be a different law. Such infinite-orbit averaging can remain an order-one erasure even when the underlying commutator is arbitrarily small. It needs its own selection and physical-scale comparison; [[trace-dirichlet-descent/two-expectation-angle-process|an engineered projection angle]] cannot serve as evidence that the present word law already has rigidity.

The next primitive proposal must change the vanishing-move mechanism through a specified evaluation, comparison or scale rule. It must then be tested on (NW8), on neutral relative-angle sources, and on the Abelian control at the same declared slab scale. Non-Abelian multiplication alone has not forced the needed exclusion interval.
