# Pointed Comparison and the Yang–Mills Return

The strongest backwards programme makes stable reconstruction a constitutive property of a factual realization, then asks for a source algebra whose local return is pure Yang–Mills. Scale-bearing pointing and invariant comparison would be two presentations of one law. An exact ordered-partner model already has this structure: its homogeneous comparison admits a normalizable pointed return only with a positive threshold. The missing discovery is a complete, source-defined version whose norm and chronology survive the Yang–Mills return.

## The axiom can come first; its realization cannot be omitted

There is a legitimate reversal of the proof programme. Instead of starting with a classical action and asking why its quantum completion has a gap, start with an algebraic requirement on admissible complete comparisons:

**Stable-facticity axiom.** At one relational comparison scale, every centered distinction in a specified factual sector can be reconstructed from its complete comparison response with uniformly bounded cost. The reconstruction is natural under admissible changes of access and extends to newly generated sources.

In a positive realization this asks for
\[
B\delta=Q_\Omega-E,\qquad
\|B\|\le C,\qquad 0<C<\infty,\qquad
\|E\|\le\rho<1.
\tag{PR1}
\]
The complete source algebra, its norm, the comparison \(\delta\) and the allowed reconstruction operations must be specified before a physical spectrum is supplied. “Uniformly” includes all finite linear combinations of sources and the volume and refinement family required by the return.

This strengthens ordinary distinguishability. An injective map can distinguish every input while sending unit inputs arbitrarily close to zero; \(\operatorname{diag}(1,1/2,1/3,\ldots)\) on \(\ell^2\) is an example. Identity of indiscernibles removes an exact kernel. Stable facticity also excludes unbounded reconstruction cost. That is the proposed additional principle, rather than a consequence hidden inside the word “distinct.”

The programme may adopt (PR1) as an axiom while searching for its elementary reason. The resulting existence question is demanding and meaningful: **does a source-defined algebra have a nontrivial positive realization satisfying this axiom whose returned theory is Yang–Mills?** Declaring such a member to exist would assume the result. Constructing the member from finite relations, and proving its return, would establish it.

The earlier [[scale-bearing-descent/minimal-conjectures-and-decisive-returns|constructive conjectures]] seek a finite source calculus that realizes the axiom. The [[scale-bearing-descent/phase-and-distinguishability-from-one-kernel|cyclic Gram relation]] is one seed candidate; an ordered-partner identity is another. The axiom states what the sought calculus must explain. It does not replace that explanation.

The scope is a selected complete neutral branch, not a claim that every observable sector in every theory has a Hamiltonian gap. A stronger ontology of stable records would still need maps explaining which gapless responses can be read from it and why those maps do not transport (PR1). [[scale-bearing-descent/anomaly-lines-and-the-yang-mills-phase-test|The anomaly test]] likewise constrains permitted vacua without making every permitted vacuum massive.

## One comparison, two ordered returns

Let a sourced comparison have a densely defined, closed operator realization \(A\), with adjoint determined by the same positive evaluation. Its two ordered responses are
\[
L_{\mathrm{point}}=A^*A,\qquad
L_{\mathrm{comp}}=AA^*,\qquad
\mathscr D=
\begin{pmatrix}0&A^*\\ A&0\end{pmatrix}.
\tag{PR2}
\]
Then \(\mathscr D^2\) contains both responses. Adjoint factorization is established mathematics. The foundational proposal is to make the sourced comparison primary and derive its two responses together, instead of choosing a state and a local dynamics independently.

The asymmetry can reside in their kernels and boundary conditions even though their nonzero spectral parts are unitarily equivalent. Such a comparison is richer than either squared response alone. It retains which composition was performed, which pointing it supports, and which sources see it. Nonassociativity in the primitive diagrams is a separate possible input; reversing \(A\) and \(A^*\) is not itself a proof of nonassociativity.

There is already an exact model. On the entire logarithmic-scale line, take
\[
A=\partial_N+W(N),\qquad
AA^*=-\partial_N^2+\lambda,\qquad
\psi_0\in L^2(\mathbb R),\quad \|\psi_0\|=1,\quad A\psi_0=0.
\tag{PR3}
\]
For smooth real \(W\) and the operator closures specified in [[logistic-scale-geometry/pointing-coercivity-and-the-flat-partner-law|the flat-partner theorem]], these premises force
\[
\begin{aligned}
\lambda&=\nu^2>0,&
W(N)&=\nu\tanh\!\bigl(\nu(N-N_c)\bigr),\\
|\psi_0(N)|^2&=\frac{\nu}{2}
 \operatorname{sech}^2\!\bigl(\nu(N-N_c)\bigr),&
\sigma(A^*A)&=\{0\}\cup[\nu^2,\infty).
\end{aligned}
\tag{PR4}
\]
The reverse composition is homogeneous. The pointed composition is
\[
A^*A=-\partial_N^2+\nu^2
-2\nu^2\operatorname{sech}^2\!\bigl(\nu(N-N_c)\bigr).
\tag{PR5}
\]
Their difference is \(2W'\). The localized remainder and the normalizable vacuum belong to the same first-order comparison that supplies the homogeneous partner.

The positivity of \(\lambda\) is forced by the compatibility premises. A negative constant is incompatible with positivity of \(AA^*\); the globally regular zero-constant Riccati solution has no normalizable zero mode; and the admissible positive-constant solution selects the kink. The theorem leaves its positive width and address free.

This is a precise small-model version of the proposed synthesis. Homogeneity concerns shifts of the dimensionless variable \(N\); it is not scale invariance of a four-dimensional Hamiltonian. No physical mass has yet been obtained. The scientific leap is the conjecture that **complete source sewing forces an analogous ordered-partner law**, with its pointed response controlling the full returned observable carrier.

Pointing alone is weaker. The same note constructs normalizable heavy-tailed states with zero spectral threshold. Nor does one pointed channel control a whole matrix comparison: \(A_\nu\oplus\partial_N\) has the logistic zero mode in its first channel and arbitrarily soft distinctions in its second. A successful source identity must govern the joint channels, including their mixed combinations.

## The reverse comparison is an exact place to seek rigidity

Let \(A:D(A)\subset\mathcal H\to\mathcal E\) be densely defined and closed, with
\[
\ker A=\mathbb C\Omega,\qquad
\mathcal E_0=\overline{\operatorname{ran}A}
=(\ker A^*)^\perp.
\]
If its reverse response satisfies the closed-form estimate
\[
\|A^*y\|^2\ge\gamma\|y\|^2
\quad(y\in D(A^*)\cap\mathcal E_0),\qquad\gamma>0,
\tag{PR6}
\]
then
\[
\|Ax\|^2\ge\gamma\|Q_\Omega x\|^2
\quad(x\in D(A)).
\tag{PR7}
\]
Polar decomposition proves this: its partial isometry identifies the vacuum complement with \(\mathcal E_0\) and intertwines the positive parts of \(A^*A\) and \(AA^*\). Restricting to \(\mathcal E_0\) allows redundant output channels; demanding a floor on all of \(\mathcal E\) would additionally require surjectivity.

A proposed algebraic discovery could have the particularly short form
\[
\boxed{
\|A^*y\|^2=\gamma\|y\|^2+
\sum_\alpha\|D_\alpha y\|^2.}
\tag{PR8}
\]
The sharp conjecture places the relation before operator evaluation, in the sourced comparison calculus:
\[
aa^*-\sum_\alpha d_\alpha^*d_\alpha
=\gamma\,1_{\mathrm{eff}}.
\tag{PR8a}
\]
Here \(a\) maps a source object to a comparison object, and each \(d_\alpha\) has that comparison object as its domain. The source relations must specify the effective object and its unit independently of a subsequently fitted range projection. Their common calibration must determine the positive coefficient and its uniformity.

Positive evaluation would give (PR8) on a genuine form core, and closure would prove (PR6). Defining the \(D_\alpha\) afterward as a spectral square root of an already-gapped operator would not establish this source relation. The proposed discovery is that the remainder of reversed composition supplies the positive unit term.

**Ordered-source conjecture.** Complete sewing admits a finite relation of the form (PR8a) on an independently specified comparison object. Its positive realization has precisely the distinguished vacuum as the forward kernel, and its bounded comparison factors through the returned chronology with uniformly controlled coefficients. This divides the conjecture into an algebraic identity and a chronological realization.

Homogeneity by itself is insufficient: on an \(n\)-cycle, \(A_n=I-S_n\) has a translation-invariant reverse response and precisely the constant kernel, but its positive edge is \(4\sin^2(\pi/n)\to0\). Likewise, a positive diagonal response in every channel need not bound their mixed response. The conjectured identity must retain a uniform \(\gamma\) on the whole joint carrier through the required family.

There is a domain issue at the physical return. At fixed duration the innovation \(\delta_\ell\) is bounded. An unbounded differential \(A\) therefore cannot equal \(K\delta_\ell\) with bounded \(K\). One possible bounded comparison, when \(A\) is dimensionless as in (PR3), is
\[
C_A=A(I+A^*A)^{-1/2},\qquad
C_A^*C_A\ge\frac{\gamma}{1+\gamma}Q_\Omega.
\tag{PR9}
\]
This follows by functional calculus. The unit in (PR9) belongs to the declared comparison normalization. For dimensionful \(A\), use an independently fixed scalar \(b>0\) with the units of \(A^*A\): \(C_A=A(bI+A^*A)^{-1/2}\), with lower bound \(\gamma/(b+\gamma)\).

The missing physical statement would be an actual source formula
\[
C_A=K\delta_\ell,\qquad \|K\|\le L,
\tag{PR10}
\]
giving \(\delta_\ell^*\delta_\ell\ge\gamma/[(1+\gamma)L^2]\,Q_\Omega\). The bounded transform does not construct this formula, make it a finite word, or show that its constants survive descent. It identifies exactly which bridge a scale comparison needs. A bounded source comparison from the outset, such as the cyclic construction, avoids the unbounded-operator step.

## Scale and invariance are aspects of the same law

The master object should retain a family of evaluated source categories over the positive scale torsor, rather than identify all scale-related presentations and discard their comparison maps. In a returned family,
\[
H_{\lambda s}U_{\lambda,s}
=\lambda^{-1}U_{\lambda,s}H_s,\qquad
\ell_{\lambda s}=\lambda\ell_s,\qquad
\ell_{\lambda s}\Delta_{\lambda s}=\ell_s\Delta_s.
\tag{PR11}
\]
[[mass-scale-calibration/scale-torsor-and-the-global-local-gap-invariant|The torsor theorem]] makes this compatible with a positive threshold and no preferred numerical unit. It relates different realizations. Exact dilation covariance inside one nontrivial vacuum representation would instead preclude a positive gap.

Thus “pure scale” and “pure invariance” name two incomplete views: a pointed response with its comparison calibration, and the reversible or homogeneous relations that survive forgetting that pointing. A scaled theory can still have symmetries, and an invariant theory can still distinguish configurations. The synthesis seeks their common evaluation and the maps between them.

The [[wall-construction-interface/core-spectral-wall|trace-scaling core]] supplies an exact precursor: its homogeneous weight admits no nonzero normal state invariant under the whole scaling action. Normal realization must point. It begins with a supplied operator algebra and proves neither selection of that algebra nor a gap. Combined with the flat-partner model, it suggests a sharper principle: **normal pointing and homogeneous comparison must be mutually realizable by the same source operator.**

The complex positive cone gives a second geometric clue. For \(u\in\mathfrak h_2(\mathbb C)_{++}\),
\[
u=e^s v,\qquad s=\tfrac12\log\det u,\qquad\det v=1.
\]
The same determinant supplies radial scale and a three-dimensional hyperbolic shape space; its Hessian and scale covector combine into the Lorentzian form in [[algebra/positive-cone-processes-and-the-complex-corner|the complex-corner theorem]]. Selecting this complex rank-two corner from the source doctrine, excluding the alternative ranks and real forms, remains the dimension-selection conjecture. Its \(3+1\) conclusion is exact after that selection.

## What would actually return Yang–Mills?

The decisive return is a complete marked theory, not merely a recognizable action. The required constructions have different owners in the workspace.

**Preparation frames must return gauge transport.** Complete equivalent preparations can form a frame fiber; composition-preserving isometries then act on its readouts. A finite-dimensional closed subgroup of the unitary group is compact. Its particular structure still needs to be selected. For Clay, the admissible family must contain every compact simple gauge group; selecting one preferred group for a cosmological theory is a different and stronger question.

There is an exact downstream result. In [[general-causal-action/prepared-readout-algebra-and-physical-source-completeness|prepared readout reconstruction]], cross marks recover the complete faithful transport matrix,
\[
M_\alpha(g)=\mathbb E(Y_1Y_0^\dagger)
=\rho(g)A_\alpha,\qquad
M_\alpha(g)A_\alpha^{-1}=\rho(g).
\tag{PR12}
\]
Products followed by joint vertex averaging generate a dense gauge-invariant algebra on a finite graph. This is why the whole marked preparation matters: neutralizing each boundary separately can discard the very comparisons that reconstruct transport.

**One sewing must return electric and curvature response together.** Smooth infinitesimal loop discrepancy returns curvature. In four dimensions its quadratic pairing
\[
I_{\mathrm{YM}}=\frac1{2g^2}\int\langle F\wedge *_hF\rangle
\tag{PR13}
\]
forgets a conformal scale because the Hodge star on two-forms scales with exponent \(d-4\). The [[directed-analytic-realization/chern-simons-response-and-gauge-action|transgression calculation]] returns the nonlinear curvature term under its declared geometric and kinetic assumptions. [[general-causal-action/local-incidence-preparations-and-the-gauge-transfer|Local incidence preparation]] goes further: it returns a complete finite electric-plus-character Hamiltonian, its physical carrier and joint marks for supplied compact connected simple groups.

These are concrete precedents for the desired return. The stronger conjecture makes their geometry, preparation and relative kinetic normalization consequences of the same source composition. Matching a curvature Hessian is insufficient: [[general-causal-action/equal-character-hessians-and-the-nonlinear-source-discriminator|equal Hessians can have different nonlinear source laws]].

**Auxiliary structure must return pure gauge observables.** [[general-causal-action/cycle-determinants-and-the-pure-gauge-return|The determinant construction]] approaches the complete finite Wilson law as its auxiliary comparisons become numerous and weak. [[general-causal-action/cycle-moments-and-the-pure-gauge-vacuum-return|Its cycle moments]] also return the fixed-graph transfer spectrum, vacuum and bounded marked histories. The [[general-causal-action/cofinal-determinant-and-wilson-source-equivalence|cofinal estimate]] controls the whole-law error even on growing boxes with varying coupling, provided the copy growth dominates the explicit volume and source-moment factors.

This gives an identification bridge: a sufficiently controlled source limit can agree with the corresponding Wilson limit. It supplies neither limit's existence. Nor does it select the microscopic copy count or physical refinement. The new algebra must constrain those choices or prove that its returned theory is independent of them.

**The complete local limit must retain the stability axiom.** Renormalized gauge-invariant sources must converge to nontrivial local fields, with their mixed products, reflection, covariance, regularity and required short-distance Yang–Mills correspondence. Recovering the perturbative \(F^2\) term alone cannot determine that limit. Even non-Gaussian correlations of selected observables are insufficient as a standalone interaction test: composites of a free Gaussian field can have non-Gaussian correlations.

The useful simplification is that the repair need not converge as an operator. [[positive-semigroup-decay/source-pairing-limit-and-the-mass-gap|The source-pairing limit theorem]] transports just
\[
K_r^c(F,F;2\ell_r)
\le(1-\kappa)K_r^c(F,F;0)
\tag{PR14}
\]
for every finite complex linear combination of positive-time sources. Here \(K_r(F,G;t)=S_r(\Theta F\,\tau_tG)\), the superscript subtracts the distinguished vacuum, and \(\ell_r\to\ell>0\) in one inherited length convention, with \(H\) in inverse-length units. Equation (PR1), physically realized on this reflected carrier, gives a regulator-independent \(0<\kappa<1\).

If the complete limiting law meets the full reconstruction hypotheses and these sources span its physical carrier, (PR14) yields
\[
\boxed{
H\ge\frac{-\log(1-\kappa)}{2\ell}\,Q_\Omega.}
\tag{PR15}
\]
It also excludes additional zero-energy vectors in that vacuum representation. A nontrivial strongly continuous return supplies finite-energy spectral support. Positive-energy Poincaré covariance gives the threshold its invariant-mass meaning.

**Conditional Clay theorem.** If a source doctrine constructs, for every compact simple \(G\), a nontrivial Yang–Mills local limit with the stipulated short-distance correspondence and axiomatic strength, and its physically identified comparisons satisfy (PR14) uniformly along that construction, then it supplies Yang–Mills existence and a positive finite mass threshold. No separate numerical mass prediction is required. The new programme is the proposed construction of these hypotheses from common source relations.

## Pointing, Higgs reduction and cosmic response

One can also make compatible pointing a constitutive condition for a realized arena. Its returned local symmetry would then be the stabilizer of what that pointing retains. [[higgs-reduction-as-local-shadow/inq|Higgs reduction]] gives an exact geometric example: a section of \(P\times_GG/H\) corresponds to an \(H\)-reduction of the principal bundle.

This is a structural account of reduction, independent of a proposed early-cosmos thermal history. It does not assume or establish such a history. The pure Yang–Mills branch must obtain its gap without an extra propagating Higgs field. If a master construction uses auxiliary pointing variables, its return theorem must remove their independent observables or prove their decoupling. Otherwise it returns another gauge theory.

For cosmology, the same complete evaluation would have to retain closed weights as well as normalized comparisons. The freedom
\[
\mathcal Z_D\longmapsto e^{-\Lambda V_D}\mathcal Z_D
\tag{PR16}
\]
can preserve sewing and normalized local laws while changing the homogeneous response. [[general-causal-action/closed-normalization-and-cosmic-response|The closed-normalization calculation]] exhibits it explicitly. Neither the mass gap nor pointing fixes this freedom. A common-origin conjecture therefore needs a further closed-diagram relation and a gravitational return before it implies cosmic acceleration.

This is also why the scale family in (PR11) is useful: it does not introduce a physical massless coordinate by declaring exact dilation symmetry spontaneously broken within one vacuum. It organizes comparisons between realizations. A dynamical scalar, if returned, would require its own law.

## The shortest discovery worth attempting

The most promising small paper would construct one new source relation whose **reverse composition is uniformly coercive on the full comparison carrier**, while its forward composition supports normal pointing. It would then derive a bounded comparison formula on an actual finite gauge preparation, including one newly generated loop and its mixed sources. The same boundary rules must carry that formula through two overlapping cuts with controlled coefficients.

Three tests make this more than a reformulation. The relation must exclude the heavy-tailed pointed control; it must detect the soft extra channel missed by a single logistic mode; and it must distinguish actual chronological comparison from freely attached phase or scale auxiliaries. [[scale-bearing-descent/coherent-comparison-lifts-and-bounded-descent|Coherent lifts]] already solve restriction of a fixed law. New-source closure and a uniform physical comparison remain the opening.

The hoped-for conceptual resolution is precise: **Yang–Mills would be the local curvature presentation of a stably pointed comparison law.** Its classical scale invariance would describe what that presentation forgets; its mass threshold would describe a quantitative relation retained by the law. Establishing that one algebra has both returns is the proposed discovery.
