# Scale, Symmetry and Cosmic Response

Scale, symmetry breaking, the mass gap and dark energy could be different returns of one law of relational composition. Scale would compare realizations; symmetry would express their reversible transformations; mass would measure the stability of nonvacuum distinctions; cosmic response would retain the variation of the complete closed weight. This is a proposed common origin, not an identification of those quantities. Exact same-source theorems already constrain part of this picture. The missing selection concerns both the relative excitation law and the normalization that normalized local observations cannot determine.

## Symmetry belongs to a specified object

Four operations need separate names. A **symmetry of the law** preserves its evaluated experiments. A **state symmetry** additionally preserves the chosen state. A **pointing** selects a datum and its stabilizer. A **presentation equivalence** changes descriptions of the same physical experiment.

Spontaneous symmetry breaking concerns a symmetry of the dynamics that is not preserved by the selected equilibrium or vacuum phase; an asymmetric configuration alone does not establish it. Pointing can reduce a stabilizer without changing the law. Gauge fixing instead chooses representatives of redundancy. [[library/impossibility-of-spontaneously-breaking-local-symmetries/inq|Elitzur's result]] and [[library/higgs-phenomenon-without-a-symmetry-breaking-order-parameter/inq|the gauge-invariant Higgs analysis]] explain why a gauge-fixed order parameter must be distinguished from the observable spectral phenomenon.

There is a useful exact constraint. If readouts differ only by state-preserving automorphisms, then
\[
j_\lambda=\alpha_\lambda j_0,
\qquad \omega\alpha_\lambda=\omega
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\omega j_\lambda=\omega j_0.
\tag{SC1}
\]
[[program-core/contextual-descent-from-homogeneity|Contextual descent]] proves that every state response along this orbit vanishes. Differentiation therefore requires some retained relational datum beyond a symmetry-equivalent presentation. This locates the role of asymmetry without making every asymmetry an interaction or every selection a dynamical transition.

[[scale-bearing-descent/anomaly-lines-and-the-yang-mills-phase-test|The theta–center anomaly]] makes a stronger distinction inside pure Yang–Mills. For even \(N\), the \(SU(N)\) law at \(\theta=\pi\) cannot have a trivially gapped vacuum preserving both relevant symmetries; it can have CP-related vacua with a positive excitation gap in each. The obstruction constrains how the law is globally realized. It does not identify broken symmetry with gaplessness, and removing the obstruction does not establish quantitative stability. A common-origin theory should derive the permitted phase structure and the response of each branch from the same evaluation.

Scale has the same distinction. Suppose a nonnegative vacuum-centered generator \(L\) has exact unitary dilation symmetry within one realization,
\[
D_\lambda L D_\lambda^{-1}=\lambda^{-1}L,
\qquad D_\lambda\Omega=\Omega,
\qquad \lambda>0.
\tag{SC2}
\]
Any finite positive lower edge on \(\Omega^\perp\) would then equal \(\lambda^{-1}\) times itself, which is impossible. By contrast, the scale-family covariance in [[scale-bearing-descent/scale-bearing-descent-and-the-yang-mills-return|the Yang–Mills return]] compares different realizations and permits a positive invariant \(\ell\Delta_\ell\). Classical conformal invariance of the four-dimensional curvature action establishes neither (SC2) nor invariance of the quantum vacuum. Selection of a scale-bearing member, quantum scale dependence and spontaneous breaking are separate claims.

\tag{SC3}
\]
The comparison pace, incidence and anchored coefficients remain fixed. Let \(\psi_s\) be the normalized simple ground vector, \(E_s\) its energy, and \(L_s=H_s-E_s\). With
\(J_s=(\partial_uH_s-\partial_uE_s)\psi_s\), the same family gives
\[
\partial_uE_s=\langle sV-s^{-1}K\rangle_{\psi_s},
\qquad
\partial_u^2E_s

\langle J_s,e^{-tL_s}J_s\rangle\,dt.

The isolated finite-carrier vacuum justifies the integral. This is a theorem tying normalization curvature to the original chronology, with its contact term retained. ## The closed weight is an independent selection problem

Let a sourced diagram evaluation admit an additive scalar \(V_D(N)\), independent of its integrated boundary variables and local marks, and vanishing on presentation units. Then
\[
\widetilde{\mathcal Z}_D[N,J]
=e^{-\Lambda V_D(N)}\mathcal Z_D[N,J]
\tag{SC5}
\]
preserves sewing and normalized local source laws, while changing the homogeneous closed response. [[general-causal-action/closed-normalization-and-cosmic-response|The scalar-character construction]] proves this conditional freedom. Even agreement of mixed local derivatives need not fix it.

A positive scalar character also leaves the phase obstruction unchanged. Anomaly matching can therefore select a global compatibility class without selecting its closed normalization. The stronger [[scale-bearing-descent/minimal-conjectures-and-decisive-returns|closed-weight conjecture]] asks for a relation among complete closed diagrams that fixes these real characters, modulo common calibration. Finding that relation is a separate finite target, alongside phase selection and descent division.

Spectrally, \(H\mapsto H+cI\) leaves \(H-E_0I\) unchanged. Geometrically, a term proportional to spacetime volume has a nonzero metric variation. The common-origin question therefore asks what selects both quantities through one evaluation; the gap alone cannot determine a cosmological constant.

[[causal-scale-theory/conjectures/covariant-response-sector|Covariant response]] must return its stress or constitutive system, while [[causal-scale-theory/conjectures/local-global-vacuum-completion|local–global completion]] must select the residual scalar. The decisive test is consequently joint: with the primitive evaluation fixed, can its closed scalar response still be changed while its normalized experiments remain identical? A successful selection principle must answer this alongside the rigidity and repair tests in [[scale-bearing-descent/constructive-descent-division|constructive descent division]].
