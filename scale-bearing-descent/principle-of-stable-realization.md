# The Principle of Stable Realization

Mass is the threshold that stable distinguishability acquires when expressed in a local clock. One law selects the arena of comparisons, keeps their reconstruction controlled as the arena is extended, and preserves that control in its local quantum description. This article adopts that law as an axiom and derives its Yang–Mills consequence. The mathematical task is to realize the axiom; the argument below specifies the theorem that such a realization establishes.

## The law

**Principle of stable realization.** A complete causal comparison law selects its positive realizations together with their admissible extensions. On its rigid branches, complete distinctions have uniformly finite reconstruction cost. The same law returns their local observables, norm and chronology while preserving that control. For every compact simple gauge group, it admits a rigid branch whose exact local realization is four-dimensional pure Yang–Mills.

The fundamental object is the law of comparison. A point is a way its relations become locally accessible. A state is a coherent evaluation of those relations. A clock represents a family of comparisons. Scale is the calibration that belongs to their joint realization.

This reverses the explanatory order:
\[
\boxed{
\text{complete comparison}
\ \longrightarrow\
\text{stable realization}
\ \longrightarrow\
\bigl(\text{local invariance},\text{mass scale}\bigr).}
\tag{SR1}
\]
The word *rigid* names branches selected by the source relations. It does not classify every possible factual process as gapped. The same law can have soft branches, different vacua and obstruction classes. [[scale-bearing-descent/anomaly-lines-and-the-yang-mills-phase-test|Anomaly-compatible branches]] specify one test of that selection.

We now give the principle its mathematical content. Its existence clause is an adopted foundational assertion, not a consequence of the phrase “facts are distinguishable.”

## Complete distinctions remain reconstructible

Let \(\mathfrak U\) be the [[scale-bearing-descent/master-object-and-compatible-realizations|law of sourced composition]]. Its preparations, reversal, gluing, source insertions and changes of access precede its geometric presentations. Its positive evaluation supplies the norm in which comparisons are made.

Fix a compact simple \(G\). On the selected branch, let \(r\) range over the admissible source extensions and, where used, approximations leading to its exact local return. Write \(\mathcal X_{s,r}\) for a centered source Hilbert space. The source law supplies bounded maps
\[
A_r:\mathcal X_{s,r}\longrightarrow\mathcal Y_r,
\qquad
B_r:\mathcal Y_r\longrightarrow\mathcal X_{s,r},
\]
with
\[
\boxed{
B_rA_r=I-E_r,\qquad
\|B_r\|\le C_G<\infty,\qquad
\|E_r\|\le\varepsilon_G<1,\quad\varepsilon_G\ge0.}
\tag{SR2}
\]
Here \(C_G>0\), and the bounds hold through every declared extension at one inherited comparison scale. The formula for \(B_r\) belongs to source composition; it is not chosen by inverting a desired Hamiltonian. All newly generated mixed sources enter the same estimate.

Equation (SR2) says that the discrepancy revealed by comparison contains enough information to reconstruct the distinction with controlled error. It gives immediately
\[
(1-\varepsilon_G)\|z\|
\le\|B_rA_rz\|
\le C_G\|A_rz\|,
\]
and therefore
\[
\boxed{\|A_rz\|^2\ge\gamma_G\|z\|^2,
\qquad
\gamma_G=\frac{(1-\varepsilon_G)^2}{C_G^2}>0.}
\tag{SR3}
\]
This is the algebraic content of stable facticity. No normalized distinction disappears into an indefinitely weak response as the comparison family grows.

[[scale-bearing-descent/constructive-descent-division|Descent division]] and [[scale-bearing-descent/coherent-comparison-lifts-and-bounded-descent|coherent comparison lifts]] are candidate calculi for (SR2). The [[scale-bearing-descent/pointed-comparison-and-the-yang-mills-return|ordered-partner law]] and [[scale-bearing-descent/phase-and-distinguishability-from-one-kernel|cyclic comparison]] supply two routes to its seed. The principle states the common requirement without choosing one implementation in advance.

## The local return carries the whole comparison

The existence clause supplies one nontrivial local theory \(\mathcal W_G\) for every compact simple \(G\). Its complete source evaluation has the positive Hilbert space, vacuum, local tempered fields, common invariant domains, Poincaré covariance and forward-cone spectrum stipulated by the Clay target. Its fields have the required renormalized correspondence with gauge-invariant curvature and covariant-derivative polynomials, including the stress tensor, operator mixing and asymptotically free short-distance structure. These are one field hierarchy and one state.

This clause returns pure Yang–Mills: no extra independent propagating matter and no necessary finite cosmic box. It can be realized directly or through complete correlation limits with the full reconstruction hypotheses. [[scale-bearing-descent/yang-mills-return-signature|The return signature]] gives the detailed types. A leading curvature term alone is not this existence clause.

To state what comparison preservation means, let \(F\) be any finite complex combination of positive-time physical sources. At stage \(r\), its centered reflected norm is \(N_r(F)\), and its translated connected pairing is \(K_r^c(F,F;t)\). Put
\[
D_{r,\ell_r}(F)=N_r(F)-K_r^c(F,F;2\ell_r).
\]
The source realization supplies a linear map \(J_r\) from the centered physical source quotient into \(\mathcal X_{s,r}\), satisfying
\[
\|J_rF\|^2\ge a_G^2N_r(F),\qquad a_G>0.
\tag{SR4}
\]
The same sourced law transports comparison into chronology:
\[
\boxed{
R_r(F,F):=\|A_rJ_rF\|^2
\le L_G^2D_{r,\ell_r}(F)+\eta_GN_r(F),}
\tag{SR5}
\]
where
\[
0<L_G<\infty,\qquad
0\le\eta_G<a_G^2\gamma_G,\qquad
\ell_r\longrightarrow\ell_G\in(0,\infty).
\]
In a direct realization the limiting notation is omitted. The source vectors generate a dense subspace of the physical vacuum complement. Norm, vacuum subtraction, translated pairings and field products all belong to the same return.

For a limiting realization, this includes, for every finite complex source combination,
\[
\begin{aligned}
N_r(F)&\longrightarrow\|Q_{\Omega_G}[F]\|^2,\\
K_r^c(F,F;2\ell_r)&\longrightarrow
\langle Q_{\Omega_G}[F],
e^{-2\ell_GH_G}Q_{\Omega_G}[F]\rangle,
\end{aligned}
\]
with convergent vacuum subtraction. The source maps and repairs themselves need not have operator limits on the physical carrier.

Equation (SR4) makes every physical distinction visible to the stable comparison. Equation (SR5) makes that comparison visible to the returned clock. Their carriers can differ; what is preserved is the quantitative relation between them.

A vanishing microscopic step does not require a vanishing returned duration.

## The Yang–Mills theorem

**Theorem within the adopted principle.** Every compact simple \(G\) has the nontrivial four-dimensional Yang–Mills theory stipulated by Clay, with a finite positive spectral threshold above its vacuum.

**Proof.** The existence clause supplies the theory and its Yang–Mills identity. Equations (SR3)–(SR5) give
\[
(a_G^2\gamma_G-\eta_G)N_r(F)
\le L_G^2\bigl[N_r(F)-K_r^c(F,F;2\ell_r)\bigr].
\tag{SR6}
\]
Choose
\[
0<\kappa_G<
\min\left\{1,\frac{a_G^2\gamma_G-\eta_G}{L_G^2}\right\}.
\]
Then \(K_r^c(F,F;2\ell_r)\le(1-\kappa_G)N_r(F)\). The [[positive-semigroup-decay/source-pairing-limit-and-the-mass-gap|complete source-pairing return]] carries this inequality to every \(x\perp\Omega_G\):
\[
\|e^{-\ell_GH_G}x\|^2
\le(1-\kappa_G)\|x\|^2.
\tag{SR7}
\]
Spectral calculus now gives
\[
\boxed{
H_G\ge m_{0,G}Q_{\Omega_G},
\qquad
m_{0,G}=-\frac{\log(1-\kappa_G)}{2\ell_G}>0.}
\tag{SR8}
\]
The inequality also excludes a second zero-energy vector in the chosen representation. Nontriviality and self-adjointness give nonempty finite-energy spectral support on the vacuum complement, so its lower edge \(m_G\) is finite. The returned Poincaré structure supplies the invariant-mass interpretation. \(\square\)

The proof does not calculate a glueball mass in external units. It proves the required separation from zero. The dimensionless content is
\[
\boxed{\ell_Gm_G\ge-\tfrac12\log(1-\kappa_G)>0.}
\tag{SR9}
\]
Here \(H_G,m_G\) use inverse-length units and duration uses the same length calibration. Conventional mass units multiply \(m_G\) by \(\hbar/c\).

This is the programme's conditional resolution of the Clay problem. The reconstruction and spectral implication are explicit. Constructing a model of the adopted principle—including its exact Yang–Mills existence clause—is the remaining foundational theorem, not a step already established by the argument.

## Why the classical action is scale invariant

The local classical description retains the curvature pairing
\[
I_{\mathrm{YM}}
=\frac1{2g_{\mathrm{YM}}^2}
\int\langle F\wedge *_hF\rangle.
\]
Under \(h\mapsto e^{2\omega}h\), the Hodge star on two-forms transforms by \(e^{(d-4)\omega}\). At \(d=4\), this pairing forgets the conformal calibration. The complete realization retains the pointed state, chronology and reconstruction control.

Thus the homogeneous action and the mass threshold express different information from one parent. A change of common ruler sends
\[
\ell_G\mapsto s\ell_G,\qquad
H_G\mapsto s^{-1}U_sH_GU_s^{-1},
\]
while leaving (SR9) unchanged. This compares members of a scale family; it does not impose dilation symmetry within one fixed vacuum.

**Scale is the residue of realizing an invariant law.** It measures the relation between complete comparison and its local clock. The mass gap is that relation's positive lower edge. ## A quantitative consequence for every source

The law bounds the total connected response to a source over Euclidean duration. For \(x=Q_{\Omega_G}[F]\), define
\[
\chi_G(F)=
\int_0^\infty\langle x,e^{-tH_G}x\rangle\,dt.
\]
Equation (SR8) implies
\[
\chi_G(F)=\langle x,H_{G,Q}^{-1}x\rangle
\le\frac{N_G(F)}{m_{0,G}},
\]
and hence
\[
\boxed{
\frac{\chi_G(F)}{\ell_GN_G(F)}
\le-\frac2{\log(1-\kappa_G)}
\qquad(N_G(F)>0).}
\tag{SR10}
\]
One constant controls every finite source combination, including cross terms between newly accessible observables. This is a consequence of the gap theorem, rather than an independent empirical discovery. It makes the proposed law testable at the level of a complete response family.

## The cosmic completion predicts collective structure

The wider programme applies the same selection idea to the capacity of an arena. Adopt its [[factive-cosmos/boundaries-and-collective-encoding|common-count branch]]: a sphere of radius \(R\) has area-order information capacity and a grain \(\lambda\) satisfying
\[
\lambda^3=\alpha\ell_P^2R,\qquad
\ell_P^2=\frac{\hbar G}{c^3}.
\tag{SR11}
\]
Adopt also its gravitational energy budget and require the capacity to be realized by admissible low-energy states. These are explicit cosmic completion clauses, additional to the pure Yang–Mills theorem.

A picture of fixed finite-dimensional cells with a uniform additive inverse-grain excitation cost fails these clauses. Even allowing every sparse occupation pattern, its available information grows only as
\[
\log\dim\mathcal C_{\mathrm{independent}}
=O\!\left[
(R/\ell_P)^{4/3}\log(R/\ell_P)
\right],
\]
whereas the adopted boundary capacity grows as \((R/\ell_P)^2\). [[factive-cosmos/why-boundary-capacity-requires-collective-modes|The collective-capacity theorem]] proves the mismatch.

**The prediction is that the grain does not count independently chargeable local excitations. Its low-energy distinctions have collective organization.** A positive global mass threshold and a large boundary capacity coexist because the threshold is not charged separately for every independent record label. The theorem forces failure of that additive local energy picture; it does not by itself prove that all admissible states are entangled.

There is a second consequence. If an arena permanently retains \(n\) perfectly recoverable independent binary records through one channel, its recoverable information is at least \(n\log2\). With area cost \(4\ell_P^2\) per nat,
\[
\boxed{A_n\ge4\ell_P^2n\log2.}
\tag{SR12}
\]
An inexhaustibly productive permanent archive has unbounded required area. A bounded patch must eventually lose access to earlier records, cease independent novelty, or enlarge its recording arena. The [[factive-cosmos/why-boundary-capacity-requires-collective-modes#Persistent novelty requires growing capacity|capacity argument]] specifies the common channel and reliability premises.

This is a structural prediction of the cosmic completion. Identifying the growing recording arena with metric expansion requires the [[program-core/record-scale-soldering|record–scale map]]; accelerated expansion additionally requires its clock dynamics. The law already excludes an eternal bounded archive that acquires and retains independent novelty without limit.

The common subject of the two returns is now precise. The Yang–Mills branch bounds how weak a complete distinction can become. The cosmic branch bounds how many distinctions an arena can sustain and retain. Their unification is a law that selects both bounds and their change-of-access maps together.
