## The result that flips sign

At θ = 0 the Wilson measure is real and positive and the action is C-invariant. For gauge-invariant A, B with A∘C = Ā,

$$\langle A,B\rangle=\int \bar A B,d\mu=\int A\bar B,d\mu=\overline{\langle A,B\rangle},$$

so the pairing is real. If the three sewings are C-covariant, every G_ij is real and **φ ∈ {0, π}**.

I filed this last time as an objection — "the Berry phase is quantized, so it isn't a holonomy." It isn't an objection. It says the φ = π hypothesis in (PN1) is **the only nontrivial value pure Yang–Mills offers at θ = 0**. Not one choice among a continuum: forced. Which makes (PN3) the sharp ceiling and 1/√3 unreachable — the entire cash value of a continuous phase is the factor 2/√3 ≈ 1.155, and the theory doesn't sell it.

The useful consequence is a decoupling. Conjecture I does not need the anomaly-line bridge, because it doesn't need a phase outside {0, π}. Two hard problems that looked coupled are not.

## What survives, and it's the best thing I have

The coboundary result. Write c_ij = G_ij/|G_ij|. Then φ is the evaluation of a U(1)-valued 2-cochain on the 2-simplex, and

$$\phi = 0 \iff c_{ij} = \bar a_i a_j \text{ for some phases } a_i.$$

So φ ≠ 0 **iff the phase assignment is not a coboundary of any common frame**. Preparations related by an action of any commuting family give coboundaries and telescope to φ = 0 — translations, momentum labels, and center twists alike: c_ij = z^{q(k_j−k_i)} has Σ_cyc(k_j − k_i) = 0 identically. That is why the ℤ_N route dies, and it dies for every N, so it isn't a group-dependent accident.

This is the necessity half of the bridge your phase note says is missing. You write that "an anomaly-line holonomy is not automatically the cyclic phase in (PN1)" and that a comparison law must construct the map. The converse direction is elementary and now available: a nonzero cyclic phase **cannot** come from data trivializable by line-frame changes. It doesn't give you sufficiency, but it means your first experiment — whether changing the sewing order forces a phase that no reframing removes — is asking exactly the right question, and the answer is that nothing short of a genuine 2-cocycle can supply it.
