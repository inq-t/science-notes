# One Action and Emitted Spacetime

The proposed action of the cosmos acts on complete causal realizations, including their possible records and geometries. Ordinary spacetime action would be one internal representation of this law. Saying that space and duration are emitted by fact formation means that metric extension and clock increments are constructed from the organization of comparisons, rather than supplied as the container in which an independently defined process occurs.

## Composition before a trajectory

Let \(\mathsf P\) be a candidate category of preparations, comparisons, and record extensions, with a distinguished class \(W\) of reversible presentation changes. An object is a context of available comparisons; an arrow changes their availability. A proposed whole realization \(\mathfrak X\) supplies compatible evaluations, readouts, and record extensions over this structure.

This notation is a starting signature, not a completed construction. For compatible marked diagrams \(D,E\), one candidate positive evaluation is

\[
K(D,E)=\mathcal Z(D^\dagger E),
\qquad [K(D_i,D_j)]\ge0.
\]

The null quotient of this kernel can supply a Hilbert realization. The primitive rule must select the kernel and its ordering together; selecting them to reproduce a desired spectrum would reverse the proposed explanation. [[scale-bearing-descent/phase-and-distinguishability-from-one-kernel|Phase and distinguishability from one kernel]] develops the opportunity for composition to constrain both phase and norm.

Non-associative algebra suggests that the manner of assembly can carry information beyond the final factors. But the associator \((ab)c-a(bc)\) measures a bracketing defect, whereas \(ab-ba\) measures an order defect. Ordinary operator composition remains associative. An upstream non-associative product must therefore specify its observable realization, or retain coherent comparison maps between bracketings. Neither non-associativity nor noncommutativity by itself supplies an arrow of recorded history.

## Three meanings of action

A classical spacetime action \(S[q]\) is stationary when \(\delta S=0\) for its declared variations. It need not be a minimum. A stationary state action can instead represent a process already constructed on a Hilbert quotient. Finally, an upstream selector could vary the complete realization itself:

\[
\delta\mathfrak J[\mathfrak X_*]=0,
\qquad
\mathfrak J[\mathfrak X]
=\mathcal C[\mathfrak X]-\eta\mathcal F[\mathfrak X],
\]

subject to independently stated resource and boundary constraints. Here \(\mathcal C\) is a realization cost and \(\mathcal F\) a retained-distinguishability objective. The multiplier \(\eta\) belongs to the constrained problem; it cannot be adjusted separately for each desired particle or cosmic epoch.

**Conjecture.** A finite composition law selects the admissible domain and the two functionals, with a preferred stable class of nontrivial stationary realizations. That class has an internally ordered family of records and a quotient admitting the observed variational dynamics.

This is an explicit constitutive leap. It does not presume that stationarity alone chooses one history or one measurement outcome. Gauge-equivalent representatives should be identified, and any remaining multiple branches need their own selection or interpretation.

The [[algebra/quotient-clock-and-stationary-action|quotient-clock theorem]] shows how a positive complex realization and its process derivative can return

\[
\delta S_I=0\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad i\partial_s\psi=K\psi.
\]

Its parameter \(s\) is not yet calibrated duration. The theorem provides a model of how action can appear downstream, while leaving the proposed whole-realization selector open.

## What a spectral action could select

The established spectral-action strategy uses a represented algebra, Hilbert space, and Dirac-type operator, together with a test function and a scale:

\[
S_\mu(D)=\operatorname{Tr}f(D/\mu).
\]

It organizes geometric and field terms through spectral data; [[library/the-spectral-action-principle/inq|Chamseddine and Connes]] supply the original construction. The proposed extension is to select the comparison geometry and its normalization jointly with a record-compatible realization. A trace of supplied eigenvalues does not itself select those data or explain an invariant mass spectrum. [[spectral-wall-descent/observable-spectral-action|Spectral action and observable response]] owns the precise distinctions, including why a bare gauge Hessian has no automatic infrared floor.

In particular, a functional invariant under \(D\mapsto UDU^{-1}\) is constant on that orbit. It cannot choose a unique factual context from the orbit using eigenvalues alone. The desired selector must include relative placement, boundary access, or records in a way fixed by the source law. This makes “one single action” a relational spectral problem, rather than a scalar function of an isolated spectrum.

## Emission as a mathematical claim

Let a record functor send proper factual extensions to proper inclusions

\[
\mathcal R(x)\hookrightarrow\mathcal R(y),
\qquad
\chi_y|_{\mathcal R(x)}=\chi_x,
\]

where \(\chi\) denotes the realized record values. Suppose the same process supplies a multiplicative scale ratio \(r(p)\), with an additive logarithm

\[
n(q\circ p)=n(q)+n(p),\qquad n(w)=0\quad(w\in W).
\]

The proposed emission law relates proper record extension to positive scale increment. To make this an object-valued cosmic scale, rather than merely an arrow grading, the cocycle must be exact or satisfy the requisite path-independence condition. [[program-core/record-scale-soldering|Record–scale soldering]] proves the conditional order statement and separates it from a clock-rate law.

A metric clock requires another construction, such as a positive duration cocycle with \(H=dN/d\tau\). Spatial extension requires a causal or metric realization of compatible comparisons. A possible algebraic local face is a positive Hermitian \(2\times2\) complex matrix \(x=tI+\boldsymbol v\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma\), whose determinant is \(t^2-|\boldsymbol v|^2\). [[algebra/positive-cone-processes-and-the-complex-corner|The complex-corner construction]] explains this conditional \(3+1\) signature; selecting the corner remains part of the programme.

The heat analogy means that clocks, extension, and entropy can be emergent accounts of one process at different levels of description. It does not identify their units or assert that a lost distinction is literally converted into a cubic meter. The required comparison maps must be derived. A stronger common charge balance would require the [[conservation-of-causal-charge/state-geometry-charge-weld|state–geometry charge weld]], rather than treating information loss as an already conserved substance.
