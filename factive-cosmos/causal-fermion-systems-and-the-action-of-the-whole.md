# Causal Fermion Systems and the Action of the Whole

Causal fermion systems provide a particularly close mathematical comparison for the proposal that one action selects both an arena and the structures within it. Their fundamental variational object is a measure on operators; spacetime is defined from its support, and causal relations are assigned spectrally. The useful research question is whether this construction can also supply the complete comparison and record structures sought here, rather than merely share the vocabulary of emergence.

## A source law that varies spacetime

[[library/causal-fermion-systems-an-elementary-introduction/inq|Finster and Jokel's introduction]] starts with a complex Hilbert space, a prescribed class of finite-rank self-adjoint operators, and a measure \(\rho\) on that class. Its central architecture is

\[
\mathcal S[\rho]=\iint\mathcal L(x,y)\,d\rho(x)\,d\rho(y),
\qquad M=\operatorname{supp}\rho.
\]

The Lagrangian depends on spectra of operator products; volume, trace, and boundedness constraints delimit the variation. Thus the action varies the mathematical realization from which spacetime is read. Hilbert positivity, the operator class, and the constraints remain inputs.

This is an instructive predecessor of [[factive-cosmos/one-action-and-emitted-spacetime|the action of the whole]]. It shows how an action can have a domain prior to a chosen spacetime manifold. It does not yet derive our record objective or show that its optimizing realization is maximally factive.

## A relational interpretation of the selected support

[[library/causal-fermion-systems-spacetime-as-a-web-of-correlations/inq|Fischer and Paganini]] interpret the construction through correlations of a many-body system and discuss a background limit as the number of considered states grows. On a specified subset, they relate action minimization to reduced fluctuations of causal structure.

The connection is to the claim that a stable arena is itself a stable pattern of comparison. A possible common principle would select a relation rigid enough to act as a background for some variations while remaining dynamical for others. The exact comparison to seek is a map from the paper's correlation data to a sourced positive kernel of [[scale-bearing-descent/phase-and-distinguishability-from-one-kernel|complete preparations]]. Such a map would need to preserve the relevant variations and their norm, not only match one background solution.

## Collapse is part of this programme too

[[library/causal-fermion-systems-as-an-effective-collapse-theory/inq|Finster, Kleiner, and Paganini's effective collapse paper]] derives nonlinear stochastic corrections in a nonrelativistic regime and obtains a collapse dynamics compatible with Born weights. The statistical operator has a deterministic Kossakowski–Lindblad-type evolution within that construction.

This offers a concrete comparison for [[conservation-of-causal-charge/factive-descent-and-records|factive descent]]. An effective collapse law and an ensemble evolution are different mathematical data. To reach our target, one must additionally exhibit the records that retain an obtained outcome, their compatible extension, and their relation to the same geometric realization. Agreement with a collapse model cannot be used to infer those maps without constructing them.

## A decisive joint experiment

**Conjectural comparison target.** Restrict to a controlled family of causal-action minimizers and construct, from that family, both its preparation pairing and its admissible record changes. Determine whether the causal-action linearization controls the complete chronological innovation, with a comparison constant that remains bounded through refinement.

This would connect the whole-action architecture to [[scale-bearing-descent/constructive-descent-division|the reconstruction programme]]. A positive pairwise spectral Lagrangian is not already a gap in the physical vacuum Hamiltonian. The missing arrow is the source-complete comparison to the actual chronology. A successful finite example that forces its norm or a forbidden mode would already be more informative than reproducing supplied dynamics under new notation.

The [[factive-cosmos/correlated-boundary-limit-and-yang-mills|correlated boundary limit]] is a further test: identify which regularization parameters in the source construction could realize the proposed limit, and whether its limiting state, observables, and spectral bound become those of pure Yang–Mills. These articles supply a candidate construction language; they do not establish that result.
