# A Correlated Boundary Limit for Yang–Mills

A microscopic scale could survive the coordinated disappearance of gravitational and cosmic boundaries. On the conjectured common-count branch, the product of Planck area and cosmic radius determines a local grain. Holding that product fixed while gravity and curvature vanish is a concrete construction target: the limiting local theory should be pure Yang–Mills, with an invariant spectrum determined by the same comparison law. The scaling identity is exact; the existence, convergence, and positive-gap assertions remain conjectures.

## Preserve the relation while removing its presentations

Write the common-count relation from [[factive-cosmos/boundaries-and-collective-encoding|boundary capacity]] as

\[
\lambda_*^3=\alpha\ell_P^2R_*,\qquad \alpha>0.
\]

For fixed \(c,\hbar\), set \(\ell_P=\epsilon\) and consider

\[
\boxed{
\epsilon\to0,\qquad
R_*(\epsilon)=\frac{L_0^3}{\epsilon^2},\qquad
G(\epsilon)=\frac{c^3\epsilon^2}{\hbar}.}
\]

Then

\[
\lambda_*\to\alpha^{1/3}L_0,
\qquad
E_*:=\frac{\hbar c}{\alpha^{1/3}L_0}>0,
\qquad
R_*^{-2}\to0.
\]

An ordinary box frequency \(c/R_*\) vanishes. The composite microscopic scale stays finite. For a de Sitter interpretation only, \(\Lambda_{\mathrm{cos}}=3/R_*^2\) vanishes as \(\epsilon^4\), while the associated vacuum energy density \(3c^4/(8\pi GR_*^2)\) vanishes as \(\epsilon^2\). Thus this particular scaling can remove gravitational coupling, cosmic curvature, and that vacuum density without removing the candidate local yardstick.

This is not a path-independent limit of an already constructed theory. It defines a proposed family of constructions. [[cosmological-scale-selection/cosmological-selection-of-the-yang-mills-yardstick|The existing decoupling fork]] already explains why taking \(G\to0\) or \(H\to0\) separately fails for the live cubic formula. The present conjecture makes their correlation an explicit object to derive and test.

## The construction conjecture

**Conjecture.** A common source law admits a family of complete positive realizations indexed by \(\epsilon\), with a boundary-selected scale relation and coherent local comparison maps. Along the correlated limit, its local observables, state, and chronology converge to a nontrivial pure Yang–Mills vacuum theory. A positive dimensionless reconstruction bound survives on the complete neutral vacuum complement.

The limiting theory must be autonomous: finite local predictions cannot require the continuing presence of a cosmic horizon or a gravity sector. A boundary relation may select a member of a scale family without becoming an extra propagating field in the resulting pure-gauge theory.

The choice of \(L_0\) can remain a common dimensional calibration for the Clay existence problem. Deriving its realized value is the stronger cosmological question. Neither a value in MeV nor a definition of \(L_0\) from the measured glueball gap may enter the gap proof.

## Where a spectral estimate could enter

Suppose the candidate realizations carry nonnegative generators \(H_\epsilon\), with their energy normalization supplied by the same chronology, and vacuum-complement projections \(Q_\epsilon\). At the fixed dimensionless comparison step define

\[
T_\epsilon=e^{-H_\epsilon/E_*}.
\]

If a source-complete innovation operator obeys

\[
\delta_\epsilon^*\delta_\epsilon
=I-T_\epsilon^2
\ge\kappa Q_\epsilon,
\qquad 0<\kappa<1,
\]

with \(\kappa\) independent of \(\epsilon\), spectral calculus gives

\[
\boxed{
H_\epsilon Q_\epsilon
\ge-\frac{E_*}{2}\log(1-\kappa)\,Q_\epsilon.}
\]

[[scale-bearing-descent/mass-from-the-algebra-of-changing-access|Mass from changing access]] proposes finite source reconstruction as the origin of such a lower bound. [[scale-bearing-descent/constructive-descent-division|Constructive descent division]] and [[scale-bearing-descent/coherent-comparison-lifts-and-bounded-descent|coherent comparison lifts]] give candidate mechanisms for preserving a repair while access changes.

The displayed implication is conditional. The full source space, its norm, the actual transfer law, and the common comparison step must be constructed. The inequality must then pass to the limiting carrier with its vacuum correctly identified. A bound on selected finite matrices or on an unrelated information Hessian does not establish these properties.

## What the classical action forgets

In a four-dimensional metric realization, classical Yang–Mills has the curvature pairing

\[
S_{\mathrm{YM}}
=\frac1{2g_{\mathrm{YM}}^2}
\int\langle F\wedge *_hF\rangle.
\]

Under \(h\mapsto e^{2\omega}h\), the Hodge star on two-forms gains the factor \(e^{(d-4)\omega}\), which is unity in four dimensions. This exact local property does not require that the complete quantum evaluation or its selected vacuum forget its scale.

The conjectural reversal is that the local action is a projection of the scale-bearing composition law. That projection retains the gauge and conformal structure needed for the classical expression while omitting the relational calibration of its realized state. [[scale-bearing-descent/scale-symmetry-and-cosmic-response|Scale symmetry and cosmic response]] formulates this family-level distinction.

## A stable spectrum needs selective hypotheses

Facts do not make every realized theory gapped. Some complete comparison structures have soft modes; an optimality principle can even favor critical behavior. The source law must explain which sectors possess a uniform repair and which do not.

[[scale-bearing-descent/anomaly-lines-and-the-yang-mills-phase-test|The center–CP phase test]] offers a precise exclusion. The even-\(N\) anomaly at \(\theta=\pi\) obstructs a symmetric trivial nondegenerate gapped vacuum, while allowing symmetry-related gapped vacua. Odd \(N\) has the different global-inconsistency pattern described there. Absence of an anomaly does not imply a quantitative gap. Recovering these distinctions from the same composition law would show that the proposed framework constrains its realizations.

The octonionic \(SU(3)\) connection motivates a particular branch, but the [[contemporary-puzzles/yang-mills-mass-gap/clay-contract-and-scale-assumptions|Clay contract]] asks for every compact simple gauge group, a nontrivial four-dimensional continuum theory, the specified Yang–Mills correspondence, and properties at least as strong as the relevant Wightman/Osterwalder–Schrader axioms. A universal construction needs an explicit route to each group; a successful \(SU(3)\) model alone would be significant but narrower.

## An independent first return

The first informative experiment is to hold the primitive composition rules fixed and calculate something that cannot be changed by selecting a new norm or clock afterward: a ratio of two neutral spectral modes, a forbidden quantum-number sector, a nontrivial anomaly relation, or a uniform reconstruction identity through the correlated limit.

The larger conjecture is that this same source law has a cosmological realization satisfying [[factive-cosmos/cosmic-cooling-and-record-extension|the record and capacity response conditions]]. Its vacuum and cosmic returns would explain each other through a common origin. They would remain distinct mathematical realizations with a proved relation, rather than one spectrum interpreted freely as every desired phenomenon.
