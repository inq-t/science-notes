# The Factive Cosmos

A cosmos may be one complete act of causal organization whose internally accessible expressions are space, duration, matter, and records. This module conjectures that the same algebra selects the arena in which distinctions can become facts, the boundaries of their accessibility, and the invariant spectrum of their stable excitations. Cosmic cooling, expansion, acceleration, horizon entropy, quantum interference, and mass would then have a common grounding reason. The proposal is deliberately speculative: its task is to identify a small number of constructive principles that could make these relations necessary, rather than merely compatible.

## The question before the equations

Why should an arena of facts have these particular possibilities and limits? Sharpening a distinction requires resources; concentrating those resources changes accessibility; preserving a distinction requires an ordered history; and extending that history changes which comparisons can be made. These dependencies suggest that energy, geometry, and information should be constructed together. [[factive-cosmos/why-an-arena-of-facts|Why an arena of facts?]] develops this transcendental question and the proposed reversal of explanatory order.

The central conjecture is:

> An admissible cosmos is an extremal complete realization of causal comparison: it sustains the greatest reliably recoverable distinction compatible with the cost of sustaining its own arena. Its stable local spectra and its large-scale history are different variations of the same law.

“Greatest” refers to an independently specified resource constraint and record criterion. It does not mean that every region is a black hole, that entropy is a count of obtained facts, or that an unspecified optimization already proves existence. The phrase “one single fact” means that the local presentations belong to one coherent realization; it does not require an observer outside the cosmos.

## One action, several internal expressions

[[factive-cosmos/one-action-and-emitted-spacetime|One action and emitted spacetime]] proposes a composition law before a spacetime action. It gives precise possible meanings to the intuition that duration and extension are emitted by fact formation, much as heat is generated in a macroscopic description of an underlying process. Ordinary stationary action could return on a reversible quotient, while an upstream variational or spectral principle selects which quotients and records exist.

The exact [[algebra/quotient-clock-and-stationary-action|quotient-clock action theorem]] supplies a limited example of the first arrow. [[spectral-wall-descent/observable-spectral-action|Observable spectral action]] explains what a spectral selector would additionally have to construct. A conventional action and a proposed action of the whole need not have the same domain or the same meaning of stationarity.

[[factive-cosmos/causal-fermion-systems-and-the-action-of-the-whole|Causal fermion systems]] offer a concrete neighboring architecture: an action on a measure can organize the space that supports it, with separate work on correlations and effective collapse. [[factive-cosmos/spectral-action-thermal-time-and-octonionic-selection|Spectral action, thermal time, and octonionic selection]] compares programmes that connect geometry, algebraic clocks, and exceptional structures. The constructive question is which of their starting data a common causal law could itself select.

## The boundaries select a relationship

[[factive-cosmos/boundaries-and-collective-encoding|Boundaries and collective encoding]] treats quantum localization, gravitational collapse, and cosmic inaccessibility as different boundaries of admissible comparison. On the existing common-count branch, matching a volume's effective cell count to an area capacity selects

\[
\lambda_*^3=\alpha\ell_P^2R_*,\qquad \alpha>0.
\]

The microscopic grain belongs to a whole-to-part relationship. Filling every apparent cell with an independent excitation would violate the corresponding energy budget. This makes collective encoding a substantive candidate for the missing structure.

[[factive-cosmos/holographic-grain-and-the-capacity-to-record|Holographic grain and recording capacity]] compares this cube-root scale with Ng and van Dam's measurement argument, Ng's foam cosmology, and Lloyd's computational bounds. Their different meanings of resolution, capacity, and operation count help specify what a collective grain must do.

[[factive-cosmos/cosmic-cooling-and-record-extension|Cooling and record extension]] asks whether the same law can generate the distinct orders of age, scale, entropy, and accessibility. Accelerated expansion would have to follow from its selected clock dynamics, rather than from the word “growth.” A preferred cosmic branch should be selected without using observers' existence as a boundary condition.

[[factive-cosmos/horizon-equilibrium-and-the-selection-of-an-arena|Horizon equilibrium]] compares this question with Padmanabhan's boundary–bulk balance, Jacobson's entropy variations, gravitational information geometry, and the de Sitter observable algebra. [[factive-cosmos/records-and-the-gravitational-arrow|Records and the gravitational arrow]] brings Zurek's redundant environmental records together with Barbour, Koslowski, and Mercati's record-forming gravitational dynamics. These are distinct mechanisms whose compatibility the proposed action must explain.

## An invariant spectrum of possible distinctions

[[factive-cosmos/einstein-weyl-and-invariant-spectra|Einstein, Weyl, and invariant spectra]] explains the historical demand that clocks and atomic spectra retain reproducible calibration despite changes of presentation and transport. The extension proposed here is that stable factual distinctions also have invariant structural types and response thresholds. The extension is our hypothesis, not Einstein's historical assertion.

[[factive-cosmos/harmonics-of-factivity|Harmonics of factivity]] formulates the candidate spectral problem. Its rungs would be admissible collective modes, gluing classes, or rank strata before they acquire a mass value. Octonionic and Jordan structures could organize their compatibility; the logarithmic-scale and triangle-character examples show different ways a domain or comparison law can control a spectral edge.

## The Yang–Mills return

The most concrete new experiment is

\[
\ell_P\to0,\qquad R_*\to\infty,
\qquad \ell_P^2R_*\to L_0^3>0.
\]

[[factive-cosmos/correlated-boundary-limit-and-yang-mills|The correlated boundary limit]] asks whether gravity and cosmic curvature can disappear while a local spectral scale survives. A surviving length is an exact possibility of the scaling law; a surviving nontrivial Yang–Mills theory and uniform gap are conjectural construction targets. [[scale-bearing-descent/inq|Scale-bearing descent]] supplies the companion programme for reconstructing complete distinctions with a bounded comparison rule.

The first decisive result need not explain every feature of nature. It should make one independent choice unnecessary: derive a source relation that fixes a spectral ratio, excludes a phase, selects a record-compatible branch, or preserves the same local comparison law through the correlated limit. That would give the proposed common ground mathematical consequences of its own.
