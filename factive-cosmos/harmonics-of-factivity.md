# Harmonics of Factivity

The proposed mass spectrum is a spectrum of stable collective comparison modes. Its wavefunctional lives on admissible preparations and changes of access before it is represented as a field wavefunctional on spacetime. A mode measures how a distinction and its arena can vary together while preserving the source law. The aim is to recover characteristic masses from that structure, using the same strategy by which a domain, dynamics, and comparison rule explain atomic spectra.

## A record-sensitive variational problem

For a candidate realization \(\mathfrak X\), a finite operational prototype is

\[
\mathcal F_{\varepsilon,d}(\mathfrak X)
=\sup\log M.
\]

The supremum is over sets of \(M\) preparable alternatives with a specified record encoding and decoding tests, whose worst-case decoding error remains at most \(\varepsilon\) through the declared depth \(d\) of admissible record extensions. Resource constraints and the admissible extensions are part of the definition. Without them, the supremum may be infinite, or a demand to survive every conceivable erasure may force it to zero.

Repeated copies of the same record do not multiply the number of alternatives. They can improve reliability. This distinguishes stable retained distinguishability from raw entropy and from an unweighted count of record tokens. It remains a capacity for facts, not an actuality rule.

The proposed whole-state law extremizes \(\mathcal C-\eta\mathcal F\) under its boundary constraints. **The conjectural leap is that composition fixes the cost, the comparison criterion, and the admissible variation together.** A smooth local response calculation may use a justified differentiable relaxation of \(\mathcal F\); the discrete optimization does not automatically have a Hessian.

## The candidate normal-mode equation

Assume such a relaxation exists near a selected stable realization, with positive comparison metric \(\mathscr G\) on physical variations after redundant directions are removed. Define the constrained second variation

\[
\mathscr L
=D^2(\mathcal C-\eta\mathcal F)|_{\mathfrak X_*}.
\]

The candidate generalized spectral problem is

\[
\boxed{\mathscr L\psi_n=\kappa_n^2\mathscr G\psi_n.}
\]

This measures restoring response per unit of distinguishable variation. It becomes a frequency equation only if the same law returns the corresponding kinetic form and evolution. For example, the quadratic action

\[
S^{(2)}[q]=\frac12\int
\left[\langle\dot q,\mathscr G\dot q\rangle
-\langle q,\mathscr Lq\rangle\right]ds
\]

would give those normal frequencies under its domain assumptions. Supplying this action independently would not explain them. Nor does a positive local Hessian prove a nonperturbative quantum gap. The complete law must control variations beyond the quadratic neighborhood.

A complementary capacity formulation would seek two positive forms on the same constructed carrier,

\[
\mathscr R\psi_n=\lambda_n\mathscr B\psi_n,
\qquad
\lambda_n=\frac{\langle\psi_n,\mathscr R\psi_n\rangle}
{\langle\psi_n,\mathscr B\psi_n\rangle},
\]

where \(\mathscr R\) measures complete response and \(\mathscr B\) measures boundary capacity on its non-null domain. A saturated lowest rung might have \(\lambda_1=1\). This is meaningful only if the source independently fixes both forms and their relative normalization; setting the denominator equal to the numerator would make saturation empty. The scalar [[causal-scale-theory/reference-cut-matching-ratio|reference-cut ratio]] motivates this proposed operator extension but does not construct it.

The wavefunctional \(\Psi[D]\) would assign amplitudes to complete marked comparison configurations. A positive evaluation and admissible tests can return Born weights; [[directed-analytic-realization/positive-readout-and-the-born-weight|positive readout]] states one conditional route. The transition from those weights to an obtained record belongs to [[conservation-of-causal-charge/factive-descent-and-records|factive descent]]. These should be two consequences of one law, rather than two meanings silently assigned to the same probability.

## What confines a mode?

The proposed confinement is closure under compatible reconstruction. A complete mode cannot vary one local presentation arbitrarily while leaving all its relations to the whole unchanged. Its domain is constrained by gluing, boundary access, and record compatibility.

An exact model appears in [[triangle-descent-response/triangle-character-cusp-coercivity|triangle-character cusp coercivity]]. For a unitary cusp phase,

\[
f(x+w,y)=e^{2\pi i\alpha}f(x,y),
\]

the Fourier channels are shifted from \(n\) to \(n+\alpha\). A nonintegral \(\alpha\) removes the zero cusp channel. In the note's particular triangle-group construction, every nontrivial character twists its unique cusp, yielding compact resolvent and a positive bottom. Global descent changes the permitted modes of a locally differential operator.

This is a genuine template for structural confinement, on its stated automorphic carrier. It does not yet survive projection to neutral Yang–Mills sources. The complex-\(S^6\) manuscript branch and its chirality interpretation have additional conditional premises; the character theorem does not prove an integrable complex structure on \(S^6\).

## Where octonions could matter

Choosing a unit imaginary octonion \(u\) selects \(\mathbb C_u=\operatorname{span}\{1,u\}\), with stabilizer \(SU(3)\) in \(G_2\). Thus \(G_2/SU(3)=S^6\) organizes compatible complex contexts. The exceptional Jordan algebra \(J=\mathfrak h_3(\mathbb O)\) has a Freudenthal extension

\[
\mathfrak F(J)=\mathbb R\oplus\mathbb R\oplus J\oplus J
\]

of real dimension \(56\), carrying a symplectic form and quartic invariant. [[algebra/octonionic-phase-space-and-the-born-rule-firewall|The exceptional phase-space note]] supplies the precise construction and its limits.

**Exceptional-composition conjecture.** The admissible changes among these contexts constrain the joint phase, positive norm, and reconstruction domain sufficiently to select stable modes. Octonionic rank or orbit strata would classify possibilities; an operator derived from their composition would determine frequencies. The number eight, the existence of \(S^6\), and the compactness of a chosen sphere do not by themselves select the operator.

[[algebra/octonionic-hopf-descent-and-the-complex-purification|Octonionic Hopf descent]] supplies a useful exact readout example: fiber averaging erases some parent harmonics, so the first visible mode can occur higher in the supplied spectrum. It demonstrates spectral selection by access, rather than production of a gap from an arbitrary gapless parent.

## The original sech-squared clue

For \(f(x)=\operatorname{sech}^2x\),

\[
f''=4f-6f^2,
\qquad
x_*=\operatorname{artanh}(1/\sqrt3)>0
\]

is the positive inflection point. This is a dimensionless structural landmark. Replacing \(x\) by \(\nu(s-s_0)\) moves it to \(s_0+x_*/\nu\), exposing the separate width and origin. An inflection point is not a spectral gap.

The stronger existing connection is the weighted gradient operator for \(q(s)\propto\operatorname{sech}^2(\nu s)\). Under multiplication by \(\sqrt q\), it becomes

\[
-\partial_s^2+\nu^2-2\nu^2\operatorname{sech}^2(\nu s),
\qquad
\sigma=\{0\}\cup[\nu^2,\infty).
\]

[[logistic-scale-geometry/pointing-coercivity-and-the-flat-partner-law|The flat-partner law]] proves this after declaring the state and derivation. It gives a normalizable vacuum and a threshold, not an excited bound-state ladder. One bold synthesis would let the scale direction supply localization while compatible internal contexts supply discrete mode types. The two operators and their coupling must be constructed together.

## The decisive spectral test

The programme should predict an invariant mass ratio, allowed quantum-number pattern, or forbidden mode without fitting each rung. Stable mass values may be discrete while momentum and multiparticle spectra remain continuous. The [[factive-cosmos/correlated-boundary-limit-and-yang-mills|Yang–Mills return]] requires only the specified positive threshold above the vacuum, together with the full theory; it does not require the entire spectrum to resemble a harmonic oscillator.
