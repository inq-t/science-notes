# Cosmic Cooling and Record Extension

The conjecture is that cosmic history expresses the continued realization of a common causal law: records accumulate, their metric arena changes, and some comparisons become inaccessible. Cooling, redshift, entropy change, and acceleration would then be connected outputs of that law. Their known equations distinguish the outputs sharply enough to say what a unification must actually derive.

## Cooling already illustrates why the whole matters

For equilibrium radiation with fixed effective species in an adiabatically expanding FLRW description,

\[
T\propto a^{-1},\qquad
u_\gamma\propto a^{-4},\qquad
s_\gamma\propto a^{-3}.
\]

In a fixed comoving volume, \(S_\gamma=s_\gamma a^3V_0\) remains constant while \(E_\gamma=u_\gamma a^3V_0\propto a^{-1}\) decreases. Cooler does not mean lower total entropy: the volume and the thermodynamic comparison class matter. Local heating, collapse, and dissipation can occur within this cooling background. [[cosmological-scale-selection/cosmic-geon-hypothesis-and-horizon-rate-ledger|The cosmic-geon ledger]] owns these identities and the distinction between radiation redshift and horizon supply.

This is the useful philosophical clue. Temperature, volume, and age should not be treated as independent primitive quantities if their changes are jointly constrained. The additional conjecture is that those constraints descend from the mathematics of maintaining and extending comparisons.

## Leakage relative to a presentation

For an adiabatic comoving radiation cell,

\[
dE_\gamma=-p_\gamma\,dV,
\qquad p_\gamma=u_\gamma/3.
\]

This is an energy balance in a specified evolving region. Cosmological redshift does not by itself identify a destination for globally conserved lost photon energy; an expanding FLRW geometry generally lacks the time-translation symmetry required for that global energy account. Crossing a moving horizon is a further boundary process.

The proposed meaning of leakage is therefore initially a change in accessible comparisons. It may be represented by attenuation, restriction, or a boundary flux once the appropriate structures exist. The [[conservation-of-causal-charge/relative-distinguishability-deficit|relative distinguishability deficit]] is one exact channel-level measure, but it is not automatically energy, entropy production, or stored information in an unseen reservoir.

The bold common-origin claim survives this distinction: one source law could return all these balances through different representations. It would have to supply the maps and normalizations relating them. The [[conservation-of-causal-charge/causal-individuation-balance|causal individuation balance]] formulates the additional common-charge construction needed for a literal unified flux equation.

## Age is an order before it is a duration

Compatible record extension gives a directed history. It can preserve earlier obtained values while adding new records. A cosmic scale \(N=\log(a/a_*)\) represents that order only if the source law gives the positive exact scale cocycle described by [[program-core/record-scale-soldering|record–scale soldering]].

Once a proper-time realization exists,

\[
H=\frac{dN}{d\tau},
\qquad
\frac{\ddot a}{a}=\dot H+H^2.
\]

Growing record order does not determine \(H\), and growing \(a\) does not determine the sign of \(\ddot a\). This identifies the missing equation: the common law must select the rate at which new metric extension accompanies new factual organization.

**Capacity-response conjecture.** The preferred cosmic branch balances the cost of preserving established comparisons against the capacity for new compatible distinctions. Its response to changing boundary capacity fixes both a scale history and its proper-time calibration. On an appropriate branch, that relation yields accelerated expansion.

This is stronger than naming dark energy as information. The construction must recover an actual source and dynamics, such as the acceleration condition of [[causal-scale-theory/inq|causal scale theory]], with their constitutive assumptions supplied by the common object.

## Coincidence as synchronization or attraction

An existing candidate compares independently normalized state response and gravitational capacity at a cut:

\[
\mathfrak R_c
=\frac{G^\perp_{NN}(N_c)}{S_c/k_B}.
\]

[[causal-scale-theory/reference-cut-matching-ratio|The reference-cut ratio]] states its evaluation policy. The value \(1\) is presently a proposed matching principle, not an identity obtained by writing both numerator and denominator in the same units.

The new question is whether a variational law forces this matching, or attracts a broad class of histories toward a branch characterized by it. The [[causal-scale-theory/conjectures/event-locus-coincidence|event-locus conjecture]] asks whether balanced susceptibility, density stationarity, gravitational matching, and sector partition are selected together. This would explain a coincidence by a common condition. It would not yet explain why an observer's present lies near that condition unless the history itself supplies that result.

No anthropic weighting is needed to pose either selection or attraction. However, a large family of freely adjustable initial conditions can preserve the original coincidence problem. [[contemporary-puzzles/dark-energy-and-acceleration/coincidence-reframed|The existing coincidence audit]] identifies this remaining burden.

## A lowest structural rung can carry an expanding history

The lowest admissible cosmic rung could be a minimal nontrivial gluing class, capable of retaining records and supporting both local and boundary responses. Its rank or compatibility type could remain fixed while its history and metric realization expand. “Lowest” would then describe irreducible organization, not smallest spatial size or zero information.

An asymptotically de Sitter branch gives the conditional relation

\[
R_\Lambda^2=3/\Lambda_{\mathrm{cos}},
\qquad
S_{\mathrm{dS}}/k_B=3\pi/(\Lambda_{\mathrm{cos}}\ell_P^2).
\]

It relates curvature to capacity but does not select either value. Moreover, the present CST baseline with a zero residual late-time floor cannot simply be identified with a positive de Sitter asymptote. These are candidate histories to be selected or compared, not premises to combine unnoticed.

Finally, maximizing raw horizon entropy can favor an almost empty equilibrium description with little usable record structure. The [[factive-cosmos/harmonics-of-factivity|record-sensitive objective]] is essential if the preferred cosmos is supposed to be factive. Its unified action must explain how records, their accessible lifetime, and the arena's geometry coexist, including the limits beyond which a particular observer loses access.
