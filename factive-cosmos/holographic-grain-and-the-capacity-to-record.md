# Holographic Grain and the Capacity to Record

The proposed cubic grain and action budget have direct antecedents in the literature on spacetime measurement and cosmic computation. Ng and van Dam discuss the same Planck-area–macroscopic-length scaling, while Lloyd bounds information storage and processing by physical resources. These precedents shift the research task from discovering the dimensional combination to constructing its meaning as a relational grain and connecting it to a stable spectrum.

## The cube-root relation is prior art

[[library/comment-on-uncertainty-in-measurements-of-distance/inq|Ng and van Dam]] defend an argued measurement bound of the form

\[
\delta L\gtrsim L^{1/3}\ell_P^{2/3},
\]

and connect it to gravitational and holographic considerations. Its status is a model-dependent argument about resolution, not a universally established measurement theorem.

The expression has the same exponents as

\[
\lambda_*^3=\alpha\ell_P^2R_*.
\]

However, uncertainty in measuring an entire length is not automatically the volume of an independently distinguishable cell. The latter interpretation requires a construction of correlations among measurements. [[cosmological-scale-selection/cosmological-selection-of-the-yang-mills-yardstick|The common-count yardstick]] gives a separate conditional cell-count route and fixes its coefficient only after declaring the counting law.

**Relational-grain conjecture.** A common accessibility law returns the cubic combination through both a resolution limit and an effective bulk–boundary comparison, with a proved map between the two. It would then be possible to determine whether the grain is a width of a source, a correlation scale, or a different geometric quantity.

## The cosmological extension

[[library/holographic-foam-cosmology/inq|Ng's holographic-foam cosmology]] extends the programme toward dark energy and cosmic acceleration, using additional proposals about its microscopic degrees of freedom and statistics. It offers a close comparison for our conjecture that the upper and lower boundaries of accessibility constrain cosmic history together.

Sharing the cubic expression does not import that cosmology's statistics, observational claims, or energy interpretation. Those are choices to inspect separately. The useful question is whether the common source law can remove such independent choices while giving an observable relation of its own.

## A budget is not an actuality rule

[[library/computational-capacity-of-the-universe/inq|Lloyd]] uses quantum speed and storage limits to estimate how much information the universe can register and how many operations its history can support. This gives a concrete operational interpretation to an energy–duration resource budget.

For our purposes, distinguish three quantities: available distinguishable alternatives, transitions among states, and persistent records of obtained outcomes. A bound on operations does not prove that the bound is attained; it does not select the actual outcomes or show that each operation creates a lasting record. [[conservation-of-causal-charge/factive-descent-and-records|Factive descent]] specifies the latter data.

The conditional action–area identity in [[factive-cosmos/boundaries-and-collective-encoding|boundaries and collective encoding]] suggests that these budgets might have one source. The additional factivity principle would constrain how resources are allocated to reliable comparisons and record persistence, rather than merely count a maximum allowed number of elementary changes.

## What would advance the spectral programme

The original local-cell interpretation fails if every cell must simultaneously carry an independent excitation at its inverse-length energy: the total energy exceeds the corresponding collapse budget. That is why collective encoding matters. A source law must specify which comparisons are independent, which are redundant, and what energy is associated with changing the code.

A decisive result would derive a neutral comparison mode and its width from such a code, then show that the same width is selected by the boundary count. That would add an operator and carrier to the dimensional relation. The [[factive-cosmos/correlated-boundary-limit-and-yang-mills|correlated boundary limit]] then asks whether those structures survive removal of gravity and cosmic curvature. Neither a distance-uncertainty argument nor a computational capacity bound establishes that limit, but both constrain what the proposed construction should mean.
