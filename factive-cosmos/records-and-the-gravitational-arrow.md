# Records and the Gravitational Arrow

The existence of an ordered record structure is a separate problem from the existence of time-dependent equations. Quantum Darwinism and the gravitational-arrow programme offer complementary precedents: one studies why some quantum information becomes redundantly accessible, and the other constructs a time-symmetric gravitational model whose solutions generate structure and records. Together they help specify what an optimal factive cosmos would actually have to preserve.

## Accessible facts through redundant records

[[library/quantum-darwinism/inq|Zurek's quantum Darwinism]] studies the proliferation of environmental records of selected states. Multiple observers can obtain compatible information from different parts of the environment without each directly interacting with the original system.

This provides a concrete reason to use retained distinguishability rather than entropy alone in [[factive-cosmos/harmonics-of-factivity|the factivity objective]]. Redundancy can stabilize access to one distinction without creating several independent distinctions. It is therefore natural to count recoverable alternatives and measure the reliability of their records separately.

The additional construction required here is the relation between the record structure and its arena. An environmental encoding model already supplies a system–environment decomposition and dynamics. It does not by itself derive those systems, a cosmic geometry, or the selection of one outcome from an ensemble description. The proposed common source law must specify what replaces those inputs and how its record maps return the familiar regime.

## Records from a dynamically closed gravitational model

[[library/identification-of-a-gravitational-arrow-of-time/inq|Barbour, Koslowski, and Mercati]] analyze a Newtonian many-body model with vanishing total energy and angular momentum. Typical solutions split around a distinguished point into branches with growing shape complexity and structures that can serve as records.

This is a useful counterexample to the expectation that a time-symmetric law can never organize an internal historical arrow without the usual special initial-condition prescription. It does not establish the result for arbitrary cosmologies. Its shape complexity is not automatically thermodynamic entropy, and its two-branch structure differs from a primitive one-sided factual order.

The philosophical connection is direct: a cosmos can develop internally meaningful history because its relational structure produces persistent records. That possibility gives a concrete interpretation to [[factive-cosmos/why-an-arena-of-facts|the arena as one complete fact]] without requiring a clock or observer outside it.

## What the preferred cosmic rung should preserve

**Record-capacity conjecture.** An admissible cosmic branch supports proper extensions of a record algebra while preserving previous obtained values, with a finite resource cost for reliable reconstruction. Its algebraic type may remain fixed while its relational history grows.

An initial test could ask whether the record-forming structures of the gravitational model admit a presentation-invariant scale grading, or whether a controlled environmental encoding admits a cost form compatible with the same grading. The exact conditional criterion is [[program-core/record-scale-soldering|record–scale soldering]]; neither cited source establishes it for the proposed master object.

This also clarifies the mass conjecture. Stability of a record does not force every excitation of every theory to be gapped. The sought invariant is a property of a specified complete comparison sector and its dynamics. Relating that invariant to the cost of sustaining records would connect [[factive-cosmos/harmonics-of-factivity|the harmonic programme]] to fact formation without assuming the mass gap in the definition of a fact.
