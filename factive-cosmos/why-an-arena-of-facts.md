# Why an Arena of Facts?

The foundational question is why distinguishability has an arena, a history, and a cost. The conjecture is that these are mutually supporting aspects of one causal structure: facts need a way to differ, a way to affect subsequent comparisons, and a way to remain recognizable through those changes. A mathematical explanation should construct those conditions together and determine which realizations they permit.

## Begin with the dependence of the categories

A fact cannot be specified by a bare value alone. There must be a comparison under which that value differs from another, a context in which the comparison is available, and a rule relating the result to later comparisons. Calling the value a particle property or the context a location already selects a particular realization of these more elementary relations.

The proposed explanatory order is

\[
\text{composition of comparisons}
\longrightarrow
\text{distinctions and persistent records}
\longrightarrow
\text{causal and metric realizations}
\longrightarrow
\text{energies, masses, and cosmological history}.
\]

This is the meaning of thinking backwards from an axiomatic field theory. Its axioms become properties to construct. [[scale-bearing-descent/master-object-and-compatible-realizations|The master-object signature]] and [[general-causal-action/directed-realization-and-foundational-restart|the foundational restart]] formulate that reversal without making a manifold, a gauge group, or a Hamiltonian the independent starting object.

The identity of indiscernibles acts as a razor: descriptions that no admissible comparison distinguishes should not contribute independent factual degrees of freedom. The comparison family must itself be constructed, since impoverishing it by hand can erase anything. [[conservation-of-causal-charge/indiscernibility-and-the-noether-gap|Indiscernibility and the Noether gap]] explains why the resulting structure may be a torsor, groupoid, or directed process rather than one global group action.

## Why these familiar puzzles belong together

The recurring questions concern the ability to make and preserve a distinction:

| Familiar question | Proposed foundational question |
|---|---|
| Why a Planck length? | When does sharpening a comparison destroy the accessibility it requires? |
| Why black holes? | When does a family of distinctions become inaccessible to an exterior presentation while retaining a measurable boundary response? |
| Why cosmic horizons? | Which records can ever be brought into common comparison? |
| Why wave-like matter? | How do compatible alternatives compose before a particular record is obtained? |
| Why collapse? | What supplies the transition from a law of alternatives to an obtained, persistent value? |
| Why mass? | What invariant resistance characterizes a complete nonvacuum comparison mode? |
| Why cooling, growth, and acceleration? | How does the extension of factual history change capacity, calibration, and the rate of accessible comparison? |

The table proposes a common subject; it does not assert that its rows are equivalent equations. The explanatory prize would be a law whose different representations force the connections, including their signs, normalizations, and exceptions.

## A cosmos as one fact

“One fact” names the coherence of the whole realization. Local facts can be partial presentations of one object without being jointly accessible to any internal observer. Global coherence does not require a global simultaneous measurement or a classical joint probability distribution for incompatible quantum tests.

“One action” names the possibility that its structure and history are selected together. No external clock has to tick while the cosmos chooses a trajectory. A variational problem can be posed on complete mathematical realizations; whether its stationary objects have an internal temporal interpretation is a further result.

The strongest philosophical ambition is that admissibility eventually entails nontrivial realization. Merely excluding the empty object by a normalization constraint does not establish that necessity. A successful deduction would explain why the primitive comparison law has a nontrivial positive realization, why it cannot be replaced by an indiscernible trivial one, and what remains contingent among its realizations. [[factive-cosmos/one-action-and-emitted-spacetime|The proposed action of the whole]] gives a schematic domain on which to begin.

## Optimality without an anthropic selector

An optimal cosmos need not maximize entropy. The maximally mixed product state on two finite registers has maximal joint entropy and zero mutual information. It contains alternatives without a record relation between the registers. Maximizing the recoverability of distinctions is a different problem.

Nor does “lowest rung” mean minimal size or the fewest historical events. It could mean the simplest nontrivial gluing class capable of sustaining an extensible record structure. Enormous histories may be realized within one structural class. The classes may form a partial order under degeneration or loss of access, rather than an evenly spaced ladder.

The non-anthropic conjecture is that algebra restricts admissible classes and dynamics selects stable histories within them. A coincidence would then be explained by a forced intersection of response conditions or by an attracting branch, not by conditioning on the presence of observers. [[causal-scale-theory/conjectures/event-locus-coincidence|Event-locus coincidence]] is an existing precise version of this target.

Chirality and matter–antimatter asymmetry fit the same ambition, with different required returns. A signed index can constrain the difference between two kinds of mode, but it does not by itself determine their populations. Reversing an algebraic orientation is not automatically charge conjugation. [[triangle-descent-response/s6-descent-defect-and-the-chirality-firewall|The descent-defect and chirality analysis]] identifies this gap. The brave question is whether one gluing law selects oriented mode content and the history producing its abundance, rather than assigning both separately.

This programme earns its economy when the same source law removes independent choices. A new name for each old parameter would preserve the old explanatory burden. A relation between independently defined capacities, a forbidden phase, or a fixed spectral ratio would change it.
