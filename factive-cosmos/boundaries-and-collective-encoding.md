# Boundaries and Collective Encoding

Quantum localization, gravitational collapse, and cosmic horizons constrain different aspects of accessibility. Their proposed common meaning is that distinguishability cannot be sharpened, multiplied, or retained independently of the arena supporting it. Matching boundary capacity to a bulk description gives a candidate intermediate grain; comparing that count with the energy of independently occupied cells then points toward collective encoding as the missing structure.

## Three faces of admissibility

In an approximately spherical localization experiment, resolving a length \(L\) with relativistic energy suggests \(E\gtrsim\hbar c/L\). Requiring the associated Schwarzschild radius to remain smaller than \(L\) suggests \(E\lesssim c^4L/(2G)\). Together these motivate a minimum operational extension of Planck order. They are heuristic estimates combining quantum resolution with gravity, not a universal sharp cutoff proved by Heisenberg alone. [Doplicher, Fredenhagen, and Roberts](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0303037) develop gravitational stability of localization as a route to quantum spacetime.

A black-hole horizon supplies a different boundary. The complete exterior algebra can fail to distinguish underlying realizations while retaining measurable area, mass, response, and radiation. “No facts beyond the wall” should mean relative inaccessibility, not the absence of every fact about the wall. [[cosmological-scale-selection/horizon-saturation-and-entropic-distinction|Horizon saturation]] states the Schwarzschild and semiclassical hypotheses of the area benchmark.

A cosmic event horizon limits which events can communicate with an observer. Its existence depends on the future history, and it differs from the Hubble sphere and particle horizon. Recession faster than \(c\) does not itself imply permanent inaccessibility; a finite event-horizon radius does not imply a finite observer lifetime. [Davis and Lineweaver](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0310808) separate these notions.

These boundaries need not be three eigenvalues. They are candidate faces of the domain on which a spectrum of admissible comparisons could be defined.

## The common-count grain

In a recovered three-dimensional spherical description, assume the leading Einstein area capacity

\[
\mathcal I_\partial=\frac{S_\partial}{k_B}
=\frac{\pi R^2}{\ell_P^2}.
\]

Assign \(s_*>0\) nats to each effective bulk cell of volume \(\lambda_*^3\), and posit a wall multiplicity \(\gamma>0\) through

\[
\frac{4\pi R^3}{3\lambda_*^3}s_*
=\frac{\mathcal I_\partial}{\gamma}.
\]

Then, conditionally and exactly,

\[
\boxed{\lambda_*^3=\alpha\ell_P^2R,
\qquad \alpha=\frac{4\gamma s_*}{3}.}
\]

[[cosmological-scale-selection/cosmological-selection-of-the-yang-mills-yardstick|The yardstick construction]] owns this relation and its dimensional conversions. It is a counting postulate with a derived consequence. Neither the effective cells nor \(\gamma,s_*\) are selected by dimensional analysis. The entropy is logarithmic capacity, not the cardinality of a constructed set of microscopic states.

The interpretation proposed here is that a locally meaningful grain expresses compatibility with the capacity of a whole. This is a candidate mathematical reason that a local theory might inherit a scale it cannot determine from its local action alone.

## Why the cells cannot all be independent occupied excitations

Suppressing geometric coefficients, \(R^3/\lambda^3\) independently occupied relativistic cells with energy \(\hbar c/\lambda\) have total energy \(\hbar cR^3/\lambda^4\). Bounding this by \(c^4R/G\) yields

\[
\lambda\gtrsim\sqrt{\ell_PR}.
\]

For \(R\gg\ell_P\), this exceeds the common-count grain \((\ell_P^2R)^{1/3}\). The entropy and occupied-energy constraints therefore select different scales. Cohen, Kaplan, and Nelson formulate this distinction as different ultraviolet–infrared bounds; their stronger energy restriction excludes states whose Schwarzschild radius exceeds the region. [*Effective Field Theory, Black Holes, and the Cosmological Constant*](https://arxiv.org/html/hep-th/9803132v2).

**Collective-encoding conjecture.** The boundary capacity is realized through correlated comparison degrees of freedom whose local presentations do not carry the energy of independently occupying every apparent cell. The microscopic spectrum describes allowed changes of this encoding, rather than an inventory of autonomous cells already filled with energy.

[[factive-cosmos/why-boundary-capacity-requires-collective-modes|The collective-capacity theorem]] gives a stronger conditional obstruction. Fixed-dimensional cells with a uniform additive inverse-grain energy cost admit only \(O((R/\ell_P)^{4/3}\log(R/\ell_P))\) nats below the gravitational budget, even when all sparse occupation patterns are included. Realizing area-order capacity therefore requires a different energetic organization or a change in the declared cell model.

This does not yet construct such a code or identify its information with black-hole microstates. It tells us what a successful construction must avoid. Empty mode capacity, occupied excitations, thermodynamic entropy, and obtained records require separate maps.

## Action capacity over a causal duration

There is a second useful conditional identity. For the spherical Schwarzschild benchmark, let

\[
E_R=\frac{c^4R}{2G},\qquad T_R=\frac Rc.
\]

Then

\[
\boxed{
\frac{E_RT_R}{\hbar}
=\frac{R^2}{2\ell_P^2}
=\frac{1}{2\pi}\frac{S_{\mathrm{BH}}}{k_B}.}
\]

The Margolus–Levitin theorem bounds the orthogonalization time of an isolated quantum system by \(\pi\hbar/(2E)\), with \(E\) measured above its ground energy. It motivates interpreting an action budget as a limit on distinguishable transitions. Applying that interpretation to a causal region requires an appropriate Hamiltonian and energy reference; the identity above does not establish those hypotheses or count persistent records. [*The maximum speed of dynamical evolution*](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9710043).

The speculative target is a construction that joins three capacities: distinguishable alternatives, possible transitions, and retained records. Their common area scaling would become a consequence of their common source. [[factive-cosmos/one-action-and-emitted-spacetime|The action of the whole]] states the proposed variational language.

## Which structures could be rungs?

Promising discrete labels include holonomy classes, indices, ranks, and irreducible representation types. They are not all the same invariant. Black-hole areas and classical masses are generally continuous; an entropy formula alone does not quantize them. [[algebra/octonionic-phase-space-and-the-born-rule-firewall|Jordan and Freudenthal geometry]] supplies rank and orbit structures that might organize a more primitive classification, while [[triangle-descent-response/triangle-character-cusp-coercivity|triangle-character descent]] gives an actual example where a discrete class changes a spectral domain.

A rung would be a stable class of jointly realizable distinctions. Its numerical mass, temperature, or horizon response would be a calibrated consequence of its realization. Determining whether those consequences are tied together is the next spectral question, developed in [[factive-cosmos/harmonics-of-factivity|harmonics of factivity]].
