# Horizon Equilibrium and the Selection of an Arena

Horizon thermodynamics and entanglement equilibrium give several concrete precedents for selecting gravitational behavior through a balance of information and geometry. They make the proposed optimal cosmos a well-shaped question: which state, boundary, and variation are held together by the extremum? Their results support a common-origin investigation while distinguishing equilibrium entropy from persistent factual records.

## Expansion as a departure from boundary–bulk balance

[[library/emergence-and-expansion-of-cosmic-space/inq|Padmanabhan's holographic-equipartition proposal]] writes, in its Planck-unit convention,

\[
\frac{dV}{dt}=N_{\mathrm{sur}}-N_{\mathrm{bulk}}.
\]

The defined difference between boundary and bulk degrees of freedom governs the emergence of Hubble volume; de Sitter supplies the equipartition benchmark. His broader [[library/thermodynamical-aspects-of-gravity-new-insights/inq|thermodynamic-gravity review]] develops entropy extremization and horizon identities as routes to gravitational equations.

This closely resembles [[factive-cosmos/cosmic-cooling-and-record-extension|the capacity-response conjecture]]. Its remaining foundational question is what supplies the degree counts, their temperature conversion, and the time variable together. Replacing their names with “facts” would not answer it. A strong extension would derive the balance and the clock from one record-compatible composition law.

## Geometry and state vary together

[[library/entanglement-equilibrium-and-the-einstein-equation/inq|Jacobson's entanglement-equilibrium paper]] relates fixed-volume stationarity of vacuum entanglement in small balls to the Einstein equation, with precise conformal-field conditions and an additional conjecture for the nonconformal extension. This is a close precedent for the idea that an extremum involving geometry and information can constrain dynamics.

The equilibrium variation does not maximize the number of actual records in the cosmos. The connection to our objective is therefore a construction target: identify whether a retained-distinguishability functional has that entanglement variation as one local limit. [[factive-cosmos/harmonics-of-factivity|The proposed record-sensitive variational problem]] deliberately keeps that distinction visible.

[[library/non-equilibrium-thermodynamics-of-spacetime/inq|Eling, Guedens, and Jacobson]] supply an equally relevant complement:

\[
dS=\frac{\delta Q}{T}+d_iS.
\]

In their treatment of modified gravitational entropy, an internal production term enters the balance. This is a concrete relative of the intuition that a thermodynamic account of geometry may need a resistance or production term. It is not a derivation of mass as friction, nor does it identify record formation with dissipative loss of Lorentzian unitarity.

## Two particularly useful algebraic comparisons

[[library/canonical-energy-is-quantum-fisher-information/inq|Lashkari and Van Raamsdonk]] relate the relative-entropy Fisher metric in a holographic boundary setting to bulk canonical energy. Their correspondence supplies a real example of gravitational response expressed through the geometry of distinguishability. Its specified AdS/CFT carriers matter: the result cannot simply be transferred to a cosmic horizon or a neutral Yang–Mills Hamiltonian.

[[library/de-sitter-observables-algebra/inq|Chandrasekaran, Longo, Penington, and Witten]] construct a de Sitter static-patch observable algebra with a maximum-entropy state associated with empty de Sitter space. Their type-\(\mathrm{II}_1\) structure is finite in the operator-algebraic sense, not a finite matrix algebra or a literal enumeration of finitely many microscopic facts.

Together these results sharpen the proposed “lowest cosmic rung.” A canonical information geometry may define response, and an algebra may admit a preferred entropy state. But a maximum-entropy state need not maximize accessible persistent records. The distinction is part of the selection problem, not a reason to abandon it.

## The proposed return

**Common-extremum conjecture.** One boundary composition law yields a state comparison metric, an independently normalized gravitational capacity, and a record extension rule. Its allowed variations select both a stable local response and a cosmic branch approaching a distinguished capacity relation.

The first quantitative target is the [[causal-scale-theory/reference-cut-matching-ratio|reference-cut response-to-capacity ratio]]. Its value must follow from the shared construction rather than from rescaling its denominator. The stronger cosmological target is [[causal-scale-theory/conjectures/event-locus-coincidence|synchronization of the event loci]] or attraction toward their common branch. These sources suggest mathematical mechanisms for the individual arrows; deriving their compatibility would be the new result.
