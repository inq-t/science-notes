# Program Core

The programme studies a cosmos as a fact-bearing causal arena and asks how distinguishable scale, state, geometry, and record can be representations of one coherent structure without being collapsed into one type. Its present mathematical core is a causal-response realization downstream of [[algebra/entry|an algebraic pre-core]]: after presentations, causal scale, operator algebras, states, transport, and area are supplied, a physical quotient removes declared redundancies, conditional expectation splits retained from erased distinguishability, extra Q-system and fixed-edge-state data can supply a candidate central entropy assignment, and a later record map supplies actual facts. Its gravitational promotion has three separate obligations: retained BKM response must map to unit-normalized gravitational canonical energy; central edge entropy must map to independently normalized spectral area; and, for the canonical scale tangent \(v_N\), a separate theorem must identify the pulled-back canonical energy with the gravitational entropy measure used in the edge/area weld. The Ruble equations assemble a contraction of this response core and then project it into a conjectural homogeneous causal-scale cosmology; they are not an independent replacement for the architecture.

This module is the semantic and logical source of truth for the causal-response realization. It fixes meanings, types, adopted axioms, proposed principles, and the dependency order of the keystone equations. [[algebra/program-core-interface|The algebra interface]] places that realization downstream of algebra selection and generalized probes; [[basic-concepts/descent/entry|strict descent]], [[algebra/local-global-individuation|the presentation/process distinction]], and [[conservation-of-causal-charge/factive-descent-and-records|physical fact and record construction]] retain their separate owners. Detailed proofs, constructions, calculations, historical discussions, and phenomenology remain in their specialist modules. If a downstream note uses a core term differently, the discrepancy is an audit item rather than a second definition.

## A cosmos is an arena of facts

The programme begins with the claim developed in [[program-core/grounding-reasons|the grounding reasons]]: cosmology in the strong sense asks what makes a collection of events one cosmos, not merely how a metric evolves after a cosmic arena has been granted. Formally, a **fact-value** is a determinate value in a declared context. Physically, a **fact-event** is the obtaining of such a value in a concrete history. A **record** is a stable physical encoding through which fact-events participate in one history. A quantum state, probability assignment, symmetry orbit, entropy, fact-value, fact-event, and record are therefore different kinds of object. [[program-core/ontological-registers|The register declaration]] makes these distinctions explicit.

The phrase *pre-observable ground* names a role in the question, not a hidden probability space, a homogeneous object, or a second spacetime. Inaccessibility of a sufficing reason does not make that reason absent or ontically indeterminate. Conversely, determination does not supply an observer with access to the determining structure. The programme consequently prefers state, correlation, distinguishability, and inaccessible reason to an unexplained invocation of ontic chance. Its mathematical realism treats the internal ground and observable spacetime as equally real; a realization map explains how one is presented as the other rather than promoting a model into nonmathematical being. [[algebra/real-forms-and-factive-spacetime|Real forms and factive spacetime]] gives the first exact six-real-to-three-real carrier mechanism and the open categorical target for the temporal direction.

## Indexed indiscernibility yields a quotient after probes are granted

Given presentations \(X\) and a declared observable family \(\mathcal O\), define

$$
x\sim_{\mathcal O}y
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
f(x)=f(y)
\text{ for every }f\in\mathcal O.
$$

Every declared observable factors through \(X/{\sim_{\mathcal O}}\). This is the exact kernel of the programme's identity-of-indiscernibles reasoning after \(X\) and \(\mathcal O\) have been supplied. It is always indexed by \(\mathcal O\): an incomplete family can identify presentations that a richer theory distinguishes. Because geometric, charge, and record consequences are constructed only in later branches, a noncircular implementation must use a staged family \(\mathcal O_0\subseteq\mathcal O_1\subseteq\cdots\), or first bundle the complete consequence family, take its kernel pair, and prove that the induced probes separate the resulting quotient. A difference that is inaccessible directly but leaves such a consequence is not in the kernel of the completed physical family.

A groupoid or stack may be prior to the orbit set because stabilizers, comparison arrows, and gluing data can be physical. [[program-core/physical-quotient|The physical quotient]] states what must be retained and defines the horizontal tangent construction after observationally null, vertical, central-normalization, and gauge directions have been removed.

The quotient does not yet supply dynamics, probability, an outcome, or a conservation law. It says which presented differences do not count as physical differences in the declared register. On the geometric side, [[conformal-scale-geometry/entry|conformal scale geometry]] owns the standard distinction between causal order, conformal structure, and metric calibration.

$$

The proposed new constitutive object is not the scalar \(G^{\perp}_{NN}\) alone. It is a locally additive, measure-valued bilinear form on physical horizontal tangents. ## Gravity requires three independent closure maps

The gravitational side first requires an independently constructed dimensionless entropy measure \(\mu^{S,\mathrm{grav}}\). When it is absolutely continuous with respect to the causal-area measure, its area modulus is

$$
\eta_{\mathrm{grav}}
:=\frac{\mathrm d\mu^{S,\mathrm{grav}}}
{\mathrm d\mu_A}.
$$

For two-derivative Einstein gravity,

$$
\eta_{\mathrm E}
=\frac{c^3}{4\hbar G}
=\frac{1}{4\ell_P^2}.
$$

The finite-index construction refines the gravitational target to a candidate central edge-entropy assignment \(\mu_{\mathrm{edge}}\) built from algebraically selected fixed states of a noncommutative descent. The strong theorem target is

$$
\frac{\mathrm d\mu_{\mathrm{desc}}_{v_N,v_N}}
{\mathrm d\mu_{A,D}}
=
\frac{\mathrm d\mu_{\mathrm{edge}}}
{\mathrm d\mu_{A,D}},
$$

where \(\mu_{A,D}\) is spectral area with a normalization independent of measured gravity. [[deriving-value-of-g/spectral-index-area-route|The spectral index--area route]] states this all-patch operator identity and its anti-circularity test.

The state and gravitational coefficients must be constructed independently. Their dimensionless comparison is the local matching field

$$
\mathfrak r_{\Sigma,N}(p)
:=
\frac{\chi_N(p)}
{\eta_{\mathrm{grav}}(p)}.
$$

The proposed scale-channel equivalence law is \(\mathfrak r_{\Sigma,N}(p)=1\) almost everywhere. A stronger completion requires two compatible results: retained regional BKM response must map to unit-normalized gravitational canonical energy, and the central edge entropy must have the same universal spectral-area density. The scalar law proves neither result by itself. Unity cannot be produced by changing units; it is physical only when neither side was normalized by the other. [[program-core/causal-capacity-equivalence|Causal-capacity equivalence]] separates a weak integrated equality at one cosmological crossing from a strong local law throughout an Einstein universality class.

If a universal \(\chi_*\) is admitted as primitive, it retypes the independent datum \(G\) only after an additional unit Einstein matching principle identifies \(\chi_*=\eta_{\mathrm E}\). Without that principle it is a second independent dimensionful datum. If \(\chi_*\) is calculated without gravitational input and the matching law is then tested, however,

$$
G_{\mathrm{pred}}
=\frac{c^3}{4\hbar\chi_*}
$$

is a genuine cross-register prediction. Dimensionless binary geometry cannot by itself manufacture the required area scale.
