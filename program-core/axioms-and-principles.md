# Axioms and Principles

The programme is a layered research architecture rather than one already closed theory. This ledger fixes which statements are methodological commitments, construction axioms, proposed physical principles, constitutive laws, background assumptions, and conditional outputs; the logical status is part of each statement.

## Status vocabulary

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **[DEFINITION]** | introduces a term or symbol; carries no evidence |
| **[STANDARD]** | imports an established result with its ordinary hypotheses |
| **[EXACT]** | theorem or identity inside a declared mathematical regime |
| **[EXACT — AFTER REDUCTION]** | exact only after a specified reduced model is granted |
| **[CONDITIONAL THEOREM]** | follows from named physical or mathematical hypotheses |
| **[PROGRAMME COMMITMENT]** | methodological or philosophical rule governing construction |
| **[CONSTRUCTION AXIOM]** | structure a completed realization is required to possess; presently may be open |
| **[PRINCIPLE]** | proposed fundamental physical law |
| **[CONSTITUTIVE]** | proposed map from a mathematical response to a physical quantity |
| **[IDENTIFICATION]** | assigns an already-defined object a physical role |
| **[ASSUMPTION]** | grants a premise for one argument without promoting it to a programme-wide law |
| **[BACKGROUND ASSUMPTION]** | imports a dynamical setting or effective theory |
| **[SECTOR]** | chooses boundary, residual, or global solution data |
| **[CONDITIONAL OUTPUT]** | consequence of the assembled stack |
| **[CONJECTURE]** | proposed extension with an upgrade and failure condition |
| **[OPEN CONSTRUCTION]** | required object not yet supplied |
| **[NO-GO]** | proof that one specified route fails |
| **[RECEIPT]** | symbolic or numerical recomputation, not evidence for a physical premise |
| **[REPORTED FIT]** | empirical result with an explicitly limited reproducibility envelope |

Qualifiers and composite tags inherit the force of their base statuses. For example, **[EXACT — AFTER REDUCTION]** is exact only within the named reduction, while **[PRINCIPLE — OPEN CONSTRUCTION]** remains a proposed law whose carrier has not yet been built.

The status of a conclusion cannot exceed its weakest load-bearing edge:

$$
\text{exact algebra}
+\text{constitutive law}
\Longrightarrow
\text{conditional physical output}.
$$

## Programme commitments

**PC0 — Sufficient reason.**

Every free coefficient, identification, quotient, boundary condition, and claimed residue is an explanatory obligation. Inaccessible reason is not absent reason, but the programme does not pretend that the inaccessible carrier has already been constructed.

**PC1 — Ontological type checking.**

Initially different registers remain distinct until an explicit map relates them. Algebraic rearrangeability does not establish ontological identity.

**PC2 — Indexed indiscernibility.**

Physical equivalence is defined relative to a declared family of possible factual consequences. Indirect geometric, charge, or record consequences count as discernment even when they are not directly observable across a wall.

When those consequence functors are themselves constructed downstream, the family and its quotient are staged rather than presupposed as complete.

**PC3 — Relational presentation.**

No preferred representative, frame, origin, or global section is assumed when a torsor, groupoid, stack, or contextual family carries the invariant content.

**PC4 — Local-to-global coherence.**

One cosmos means coherent comparison, gluing, and record compatibility among contexts—not a Kochen--Specker-forbidden global noncontextual valuation.

**PC5 — Independent return values.**

A quantity counts as derived only if its construction does not use that quantity, its measured equivalent, or the target phenomenology as normalization data.

**PC6 — Factive completeness.**

A state or probability law does not complete the ontology. A theory of a cosmos must eventually type facts, records, and their historical compatibility.

**PC7 — Conservative local interface.**

Unless emergence is explicitly claimed, tested GR and QFT may be imported as local fibers. The global programme must preserve their covariance, causal propagation, constraints, conservation laws, positivity, and bounded corrections. Coexistence is not derivation.

## Construction axioms

These are requirements on a completed causal-scale realization, not claims that the current workspace has supplied them. They begin downstream of [[algebra/entry|the algebraic pre-core]], which separately owes the scalar and algebra selection, generalized probes, presentation site, strict descent data, and distinction between reversible presentation arrows and noninvertible physical processes.

**CA1 — Presentation object.**

There exists an ambient category \(\mathcal C\) of admissible causal-scale presentations and processes, with a declared wide subgroupoid \(W\subseteq\mathcal C^{\simeq}\) of reversible presentation changes, observable functors, and local restriction maps. Whenever descent is claimed, the base site and topology are declared, descent for arrows is proved, and effectivity for objects is separately tested. Genuinely causal, lossy, or record-extending arrows live in \(\mathcal C\), not solely in its groupoid core.

**CA2 — Horizontal comparison.**

Scale-indexed algebras and states possess noncircular cross-fiber transport, defining a physical horizontal tangent after vertical, central-normalization, gauge, and observationally null directions are removed.

**CA3 — Finite response geometry.**

The physical horizontal family admits a positive, finite or renormalized coincidence distinguishability form with a declared domain and common prescription.

**CA4 — Localizability.**

The response form restricts to a positive, countably additive measure on causal cuts and is absolutely continuous, or has a controlled alternative relation, with respect to independently normalized area.

**CA5 — Covariant state–geometry map.**

There is a same-tangent, equivariant map from state response to gravitational response. Its rank, kernel, covariance, locality, and preservation properties are specified.

**CA6 — Factive and record map.**

The theory identifies how contextual facts arise and what dynamics makes their records stable and mutually compatible. No global noncontextual outcome section is required.

**CA7 — Variational charge structure.**

If causal conservation is claimed, the combined system carries a continuous group action together with a presymplectic/Hamiltonian or Lagrangian structure, a normalized generator, a moment map or Noether current, and a boundary-flux law.

CA1–CA5 define the constitutive response programme. CA6 is the missing facthood layer. CA7 is the additional conservation programme. Neither CA6 nor CA7 follows from the BKM metric alone.

## Physical principles

**P1 — Observable scale.**

> A realized difference of metric scale has an invariant image as a physical horizontal displacement through state space.

This is the scale-to-state principle. It does not identify Weyl scale with modular time or an RG scale.

**P2 — Unit scale-state rate.**

After a balanced binary channel, its normalized generator, and the logarithmic scale coordinate have been independently selected, an affine soldering law would have the form

$$
x:=N-N_c,
\qquad
\theta=\varrho_\perp x,
\qquad
\nu:=|\varrho_\perp|
=\left|\frac{\mathrm d\theta}{\mathrm dN}\right|.
$$

These are **[DEFINITION — AFTER THE AFFINE SOLDERING IS GRANTED]**. The separate physical principle is

$$
\boxed{\nu=1.}
$$

This fixes the rate of the reduced binary polarization per e-fold. It is a separate principle, not a coordinate convention and not a consequence of the matching law. Since \(\nu\) multiplies \(x\) in the profile, it is an inverse-width parameter; an actual profile width is proportional to \(1/\nu\).

**P3 — Weak Ruble matching.**

At the distinguished reference cut, first define

$$
\mathfrak R_c
:=\frac{k_B}{S_c}G^{\perp}_{NN}(N_c)
$$

when \(S_c/k_B=\mu^{S,\mathrm{grav}}(\Sigma_c)\). The separate weak matching principle is

$$
\boxed{\mathfrak R_c=1.}
$$

It selects the weak-unit homogeneous branch. It is weaker than local gravitational universality.

**P4a — Response-geometry equivalence.**

For physical horizontal tangents carried in one common scheme, the full **[PRINCIPLE — OPEN CONSTRUCTION]** is

$$
\boxed{
\mathrm d\mu^{\mathrm{desc}}_{v,w}
=Z_g\,
\mathrm d\mu^{\mathrm{grav,resp}}_{\mathfrak S_\Sigma v,\mathfrak S_\Sigma w}}
\quad\text{for all }v,w.
$$

**P4b — Einstein scale-channel equivalence.**

Throughout a declared Einstein universality class, after the canonical scale tangent, common carrier, absolute-continuity relation, and horizon/canonical-energy bridge have been constructed in one compatible scheme,

$$
\boxed{
\mathrm d\mu^{\mathrm{desc}}_{v_N,v_N}
=\mathrm d\mu^{S,\mathrm E}
=\eta_{\mathrm E}\,\mathrm dA}
\quad\text{almost everywhere.}
$$

P4b implies P3 only after the reference-cut entropy, area, tangent, and integration hypotheses are supplied. P3 does not imply P4b, and the rank-one P4b does not prove the full bilinear P4a. Identifying this reference cut with a self-dual physical wall is the separate open event-locus conjecture.

## Constitutive law

For the homogeneous specialization, the proposed **[CONSTITUTIVE]** law is

## Background and sector assumptions

The familiar homogeneous Ruble cosmology additionally assumes:

1. spatially flat \(3+1\)-dimensional GR--FLRW;
2. declared matter and radiation sectors;
3. separate conservation of the effective response;
4. a flat-FLRW apparent horizon with a declared horizontal temperature;
5. a residual-vacuum sector;
6. a selected root of the present-flatness equation; and
7. for CST-B2, a constant extensive channel factor \(C_\perp(N)=C_{\perp,c}\).

These premises belong downstream of the constitutive core. A different residual sector, channel-density evolution, or covariant response can change the cosmic history without refuting the quotient, BKM geometry, or binary Casimir.

## Independence rules

The following principles are logically independent unless an additional theorem is proved. In particular,

$$
\nu=1
\not\Longrightarrow \mathfrak R_c=1,
\qquad
\mathfrak R_c=1
\not\Longrightarrow
\mathfrak r_{\Sigma,N}(p)=1,
$$

and none of these unit laws implies CL1. The converse implications are likewise absent.

A reference-cut evaluation is also distinct from three different kinds of constancy:

$$
\mathfrak R_c=1
\not\Longrightarrow
\frac{\mathrm D\mathfrak R_\Sigma}{\mathrm dN}=0,
\qquad
\mathfrak R_c=1
\not\Longrightarrow
\beta_{\mathfrak R}(1)=0,
\qquad
\mathfrak R_c=1
\not\Longrightarrow
\mathfrak R_\Sigma(N)\equiv1.
$$

The first datum is a reference-cut evaluation, followed by pathwise constancy, an RG fixed-point statement after an RG construction, and a global identity. None follows from the others without an additional theorem.
