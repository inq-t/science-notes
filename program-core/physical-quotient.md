# The Physical Quotient

The first exact construction in the causal-response branch, after presentations and probes have been granted, is an observational quotient indexed by a declared family of discernment maps. The physically adequate object may be an effective quotient, groupoid, stack, or derived analogue; retaining arrows, stabilizers, nilpotents, and higher tangent data is essential whenever they carry information that invariant functions or a coarse point set do not.

## Observational equivalence

Let \(X\) be a space or class of presentations and let

$$
\mathcal O=\{f:X\to Y_f\}
$$

be the observable family for one declared physical register. Define

$$
x\sim_{\mathcal O}y
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
f(x)=f(y)
\text{ for every }f\in\mathcal O.
$$

This is an equivalence relation. Every \(f\in\mathcal O\) factors uniquely through

$$
\pi_{\mathcal O}:X\longrightarrow X/{\sim_{\mathcal O}}.
$$

This universal factorization is **[EXACT]**. The assertion that \(\mathcal O\) is physically complete is not. Enlarging the declared family can refine the quotient.

If the family is meant to include later geometric, charge, interference, or record consequences, it cannot be declared complete before those consequence functors exist. Bundle the current family into \(F_i:X\to\prod_{f\in\mathcal O_i}Y_f\) and use its kernel pair. For \(i\leq j\), enlargement gives a refinement map

$$
X/{\sim_{\mathcal O_j}}
\longrightarrow
X/{\sim_{\mathcal O_i}}.
$$

No physically complete final quotient has been justified until the stages stabilize or an appropriate limit and effectivity theorem is supplied. Alternatively, first construct the complete bundled consequence functor, take its kernel pair and effective image, and prove that the induced probes separate the resulting quotient. Requiring the probes to separate the raw presentations would erase the intended observational equivalence. This note owns the quotient theorem and its noncircularity condition; [[algebra/program-core-interface|the algebra interface]] records only where its inputs enter the more primitive construction order.

The index is physical. If a hidden presentation difference changes later geometry, charge, interference, or records, then it is discernible by the full consequence family even when no observer can read it directly across a wall. Direct accessibility and physical discernibility are not synonyms.

## Why the orbit set may be too coarse

When a group \(G\) realizes the equivalence, the action groupoid

$$
G\times X\rightrightarrows X
$$

retains the arrows and stabilizers erased by \(X/G\). More general observer changes, local causal regions, or scale presentations may require a groupoid not generated by one global group. If locally equivalent descriptions must glue across covers, a stack or another descent object may be required.

This is not decorative category theory. A global holonomy can be invisible on every local patch; a stabilizer can control charges or selection rules; two locally equivalent objects can have inequivalent global forms. [[basic-concepts/groupoids/entry|Groupoids]], [[basic-concepts/descent/entry|descent]], and [[basic-concepts/stacks/entry|stacks]] state the relevant mathematical distinctions.

## Presentation data for causal scale

A working presentation at a cut may contain

$$
p=(\Sigma,\sigma,\mathcal A,\omega,\mathcal T,\mathcal O),
$$

where \(\Sigma\) is a causal cut, \(\sigma\) a scale section, \(\mathcal A\) an algebra, \(\omega\) a state, \(\mathcal T\) cross-fiber comparison data, and \(\mathcal O\) the declared discernment family. This tuple is a schema, not an assertion that a global presentation category has been constructed.

Changes of frame, gauge, central normalization, modular presentation, coordinate, and local trivialization may define arrows rather than new physical points. Which changes are redundant must be stated, not inferred from the abstract group alone.

## The physical horizontal tangent

At a regular presentation \(p\), one first-order candidate for the observationally reduced tangent is

$$
\widetilde H_p
:=
\frac{T_p\mathscr P}
{V_p+Z_p^{\mathrm{norm}}+G_p+O_p},
\qquad
O_p:=\bigcap_{f\in\mathcal O}\ker Df_p,
$$

where:

- \(V_p\) contains vertical changes of presentation at fixed physical base point;
- \(Z_p^{\mathrm{norm}}\) contains additive or scalar normalization directions declared invisible to the chosen state comparison, not the full algebraic center;
- \(G_p\) contains declared gauge directions; and
- \(O_p\) contains first-order observationally null directions.

This candidate equals the tangent to the observational kernel only under appropriate regularity or constant-rank hypotheses. The function \(f(x)=x^2\) at \(x=0\) has \(Df_0=0\) although the direction is discernible at second order. A singular or higher-order construction must therefore use the tangent of the kernel pair or a derived tangent complex rather than quotienting by \(O_p\) alone.

If the response form descends to a positive semidefinite form on \(\widetilde H_p\), only then remove its radical:

$$
H_p
:=
\frac{\widetilde H_p}
{\operatorname{Rad}(g^{\mathrm{BKM}})}.
$$

Equality of observational nullity and the BKM radical would itself be a theorem. Central elements may also carry superselection or boundary information, so only the explicitly trivial normalization directions are removed.

The sums and quotients may need to be replaced by a tangent complex when the presentation object is singular or stack-like. Stabilizer dimensions may jump, so a global horizontal vector bundle is a construction target rather than a default.

Cross-scale transport supplies a connection or comparison rule. The physical scale tangent is then

$$
v_N:=D^{\mathrm{hor}}\Phi(\partial_N)\in H_p,
$$

for a scale-to-state map \(\Phi\). Its normalization must be fixed by independently declared scale and state coordinates. A quadratic form can be made arbitrarily large by rescaling its tangent, so no capacity law is meaningful before this step.

## Descent and quotient are not fact selection

The physical quotient removes redundant presentation differences. Mathematical descent glues compatible local data into one object. Neither operation selects a character of a commutative context, actualizes an outcome, or creates a record.

The programme's phrase *descent into facthood* is therefore a project-specific extension and currently conjectural. It would require:

1. the quotient or groupoid of presentations;
2. a response geometry on physical directions;
3. a map to contextual facts; and
4. a persistence law for records.

The first has an exact abstract template. The second has the construction target in [[program-core/localized-areal-response-geometry|localized areal response geometry]]. [[cosmodynamics/fact-record-history|Fact, Record, and History]] owns the meaning and requirements of the later layers; [[conservation-of-causal-charge/factive-descent-and-records|factive descent]] owns their open physical construction target.

One cosmos need not and, in the relevant quantum setting, cannot mean one global noncontextual valuation. It can instead mean coherent translations and records among contexts when they become jointly comparable.

## What the quotient does not imply

The identity of indiscernibles gives no automatic:

- continuous one-parameter group;
- direction of time;
- Hamiltonian or action;
- probability distribution;
- moment map or conserved charge;
- metric scale; or
- spacetime response.

Each requires additional typed structure. In particular,

$$
\text{observational quotient}
\not\Longrightarrow
\text{Noether conservation}.
$$

That boundary is enforced by [[program-core/symmetry-conservation-and-action|the symmetry and action declaration]].
