# Ontological Registers

The core vocabulary assigns different mathematical types to ground, presentation, state, fact, causal order, scale, response, charge, and record. Relations among these registers are encouraged, but every cross-register identification requires an explicit map and a declared logical status.

|---|---|---|

The specialist account of time, state, and fact is [[cosmodynamics/registers-and-type-discipline|the cosmodynamic register discipline]]. The present table is the programme-wide declaration to which other modules should conform.

**Causal individuation** names the whole ordered construction

$$
\text{physical quotient}
\longrightarrow
\text{response geometry}
\dashrightarrow
\text{contextual fact}
\dashrightarrow
\text{stable record and history}.
$$

It is not a synonym for the BKM form, areal modulus, causal compliance, Casimir, or prospective causal charge. Those are differently typed structures that may participate in a completed theory of causal individuation.

## “Information” is not a primitive scalar stock

The word *information* should be qualified by its carrier and operation. Preferred forms include:

- relative distinguishability of two states on an algebra;
- entropy of a specified state and prescription;
- channel capacity under a declared coding problem;
- accessible record content in a context; and
- a representation invariant or charge in a named symmetry structure.

The loose expression

$$
\text{ontological information}
=\text{observable information}+\text{gravity/matter/space}
$$

is not yet an equation because its terms do not share a carrier. Its two disciplined successors are:

$$
\text{fixed representation norm}
=\text{resolved order}+\text{residual susceptibility}
$$

at the quadratic Casimir level, and

$$
\boldsymbol\mu_\Sigma^{\mathrm{causal}}
=\boldsymbol\mu_\Sigma^{\mathrm{state+matter}}
+\boldsymbol\mu_\Sigma^{\mathrm{grav}}
+\boldsymbol\mu_\Sigma^{\mathrm{record}}
\in\mathfrak g_c^*
$$

as a prospective moment-map balance, with matter included in the state contribution. The charge generated by \(\xi\) would be \(Q_\xi[\Sigma]=\langle\boldsymbol\mu_\Sigma^{\mathrm{causal}},\xi\rangle\). The second equation requires a common group action, moment map, embeddings, and flux law.

## Unit and carrier ledger

A quantity is not fully typed by its dimensions. Its carrier, tangent, integration domain, and prescription are part of the object.

| Quantity | Units | Carrier and scope |
|---|---|---|
| \(N=-\ln(\sigma/\sigma_*)\) | \(1\) | logarithmic scale coordinate on a scale-indexed family; equals \(\ln(a/a_*)\) only in the homogeneous FLRW identification |
| \(\theta\) | \(1\) | coordinate on the reduced binary state family after the generator and reference state are normalized |
| \(\zeta(p)\) | \(1\) | inhomogeneous logarithmic scale residue or curvature variable on a spatial or causal cut; not by itself a random variable or outcome |
| \(D(\omega\Vert\varphi)\) | \(1\), in nats for the natural logarithm | ordered pair of states on one algebra or on carriers related by valid transport |
| \(S/k_B\) | \(1\) | entropy of a declared state, algebra or region, and renormalization prescription |
| \(G^{\perp}_{NN}\) | \(1\) when \(N\) is dimensionless | BKM capacity of the normalized physical scale tangent; cut-integrated when an integration domain \(\Sigma\) is declared |
| \(C_\perp(N)\) | \(1\) | extensive response or multiplicity factor of a declared wall sector or cut; not necessarily an integer or a constant |
| \(\mathfrak r_{\Sigma,N}(p)\) | \(1\) | local state--gravity matching field at \(p\in\Sigma_N\), with state, tangent, area, and prescription held explicit |
| \(\mathfrak R_\Sigma(N)\) | \(1\) | integrated cut ratio; the gravitational-entropy-measure-weighted average of \(\mathfrak r_{\Sigma,N}(p)\) when the Radon--Nikodym hypotheses hold |
| \(\mathfrak R_c\) | \(1\) | integrated evaluation on the distinguished cut \(\Sigma_c\) at \(N_c\); not a local field or a pathwise constant |
| \(\chi_N(p)\) | \(L^{-2}\) | contraction of the areal descent modulus on the canonically normalized dimensionless scale tangent at \(p\in\Sigma_N\) |
| \(\eta_{\mathrm{grav}}(p)\), \(\eta_{\mathrm E}\) | \(L^{-2}\) | gravitational entropy--area modulus on a declared cut and in a declared gravitational theory; \(\eta_{\mathrm E}=c^3/(4\hbar G)\) in Einstein gravity |
| \(\mathfrak a_N=\chi_N^{-1}\), \(\mathfrak a_{\mathrm E}=\eta_{\mathrm E}^{-1}\) | \(L^2\) | scalar compliance on a nondegenerate scale direction; the inverse of a full bilinear form instead lives on the dual tangent space |
| \(\mathcal K_\zeta(k)\) | \(L^{-3}\) in the current three-dimensional Fourier normalization | momentum-space precision kernel for the dimensionless field \(\zeta\); a three-dimensional spectral density, not an areal modulus |
| \(\rho_\theta\) | \(1\) as a trace-one operator | density operator on the reduced state algebra; not an energy density |
| \(\rho_X(N)\), \(\rho_{\mathrm{crit}}(N)\) | energy per volume | homogeneous spacetime energy densities along a cosmological history; not quantum density operators |

Fourier coefficients such as \(\zeta_{\mathbf k}\) acquire convention-dependent dimensions. The entry for \(\mathcal K_\zeta\) assumes the convention in which

$$
\frac12\int\frac{\mathrm d^3k}{(2\pi)^3}
\mathcal K_\zeta(k)|\zeta_{\mathbf k}|^2
$$

is dimensionless. Changing the Fourier convention changes the units assigned to the coefficient and modes together, not the underlying quadratic functional.

## Constants and couplings

| Quantity | Type | Structural role |
|---|---|---|
| \(c\) | dimensionful conversion, \(L/T\) | calibrates temporal and spatial components of causal metric descriptions |
| \(\hbar\) | dimensionful conversion, action | relates dimensionless phase to physical action |
| \(k_B\) | dimensionful conversion, entropy | relates dimensionless entropy to thermodynamic units |
| \(G\) | gravitational coupling in mechanical units | relates stress-energy to curvature; \(\mathfrak a_{\mathrm E}=4\hbar G/c^3\) is the corresponding areal compliance |
| \(\alpha\) | dimensionless coupling | depends on field normalization and, in QFT, on a declared RG scale and scheme |
| \(\mathfrak r_{\Sigma,N}(p)\) | dimensionless matching field | compares independently constructed local state and gravitational moduli at a specified cut, scale, and point |
| \(\mathfrak R_c\) | dimensionless crossing number | integrated homogeneous evaluation at one distinguished cut |

Setting \(c=1\) is a unit convention. Deriving \(\mathfrak r_{\Sigma,N}(p)=1\) from independent constructions would be a physical equivalence law. A nonunit value is a new constant only if it is universal and irreducible; otherwise it may be a state-dependent response, calculable number, or scheme artifact.

## Constancy, invariance, and fundamentality

The word *constant* suppresses several logically independent claims.

| Claim | Mathematical test | What it does not imply |
|---|---|---|
| Constant along a path | \(\mathrm D_\lambda q=0\) on one declared history or solution | the same value on other solutions, cuts, or theories |
| Coordinate- or presentation-invariant | \(q\) is unchanged under the declared reparameterizations or presentation arrows | that \(q\) is spatially, temporally, or statewise constant |
| Model parameter | one value is held fixed within a specified model member or fit | universality, irreducibility, or fundamentality |
| Universal coefficient | one value is returned throughout a declared universality class and common prescription | that the value must be admitted as an unexplained input |
| Fundamental constant | an irreducible universal empirical input of the completed theory | a unit-independent decimal when the quantity is dimensionful |

These classifications are not mutually exclusive. A fundamental constant is normally also a universal coefficient and a model parameter, but a solution-specific integration constant is only path-constant. Coordinate invariance lies on a different axis: a scalar field can be presentation-invariant while varying over every cut.

In the present programme, \(\mathfrak R_c\) is defined as a crossing evaluation, not as a path-constant. A hypothetical \(\chi_*\) is a universal coefficient only after universality has been proved or postulated. It becomes a fundamental constant only if it remains primitive and irreducible; on the unit Einstein branch it can instead be a retyping of the datum conventionally carried by \(G\).

## Functionals, horizontal flow, and RG running

Four different kinds of variation must remain separate.

| Structure | Form | Required data |
|---|---|---|
| State functional | \(F[\Sigma,\mathcal A,\omega,v]\) evaluated on one configuration | a carrier, state, tangent, and prescription; no flow is implied |
| Horizontal \(N\)-flow | \(N\mapsto(\Sigma_N,\mathcal A_N,\omega_N)\) and \(\mathrm D_NF\) | cross-fiber transport and a normalized physical horizontal tangent |
| Genuine RG running | \(\mu\,\mathrm dg_i/\mathrm d\mu=\beta_i(g)\) | an action or operator basis, field normalization, regulator, subtraction scheme, thresholds, and probe scale |
| Change of universality class | the response changes type, symmetry, fixed-point basin, or universal data | a comparison of distinct effective classes; it need not be the running of one coefficient within one class |

Thus a state-dependent matching field \(\mathfrak r_{\Sigma,N}(p)\) is first a functional. Its covariant change \(\mathrm D_N\mathfrak r_{\Sigma,N}(p)\) is a horizontal scale flow only after the comparison maps are constructed. Neither expression is an RG beta function. If the areal response ceases to be one universal scalar and becomes tensorial, species-dependent, curvature-dependent, nonlocal, or singular, the return value may indicate a different universality class rather than a “running Ruble constant.”
