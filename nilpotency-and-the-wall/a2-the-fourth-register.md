# $A_2$ and the Rank-Two Motif

The vault's firewall keeps three differently typed occurrences of $A_2$ apart: the Keller cusp discriminant with its $S_3$ sheet monodromy, the toric triangulation in the $S^6$ manuscript's cusp filling, and the root system with its Cartan geometry. Whitney--Thom stability supplies a fourth nearby register, but rank-two transverse data is only a numerical motif shared by them, not a common invariant and not a selector of $A_2$. Ownership of the firewall stays with [[algebra/type-ledger|the type ledger]]; this note records the precise extra hypotheses a genuine bridge would need.

## The fourth register

**[EXACT IN THE DECLARED MAP-GERM SETTING]** Whitney classified the stable singularities of smooth maps between surfaces as folds and cusps (Ann. of Math. **62**, 374 (1955)). For the miniversal deformation of the simple $A_2$ singularity, the discriminant in its two-dimensional control plane $(a,b)$ is $4a^3+27b^2=0$: two smooth fold branches meet at the cusp and are exchanged by $b\mapsto-b$. This does not classify arbitrary two-parameter degenerations of schemes, operator algebras, or physical systems; the source and target dimensions, equivalence relation, and stability topology are part of the theorem.

## The shared numerical motif

Collect the registers' transverse data: the Keller cusp lives in the two-dimensional $(a,b)$ base of the versal deformation ([[algebra/a2-inverse-cover|owner]]); the manuscript's cusp has rank-two vanishing lattice $\Lambda_{\mathrm{tor}}=\langle\hat w,\hat\delta\rangle$ with $N^2=0$ ([[s6-deep-read|deep read]], receipt-verified); the catastrophe control plane is two-dimensional by definition; and the programme's walls are codimension-two cuts whose two null normals span the modular plane ([[the-trichotomy-identification]], [[dimension-drop-as-group-change]]). Proposed common statement, graded and boxed:

> **[PROPOSED COMPARISON]** Several registers carry rank-two transverse data. A common invariant exists only if a functor carries that data and its pairing between the registers; codimension two or rank two alone does not supply the functor.

This is a heuristic prompt, not a prediction. Rank two and codimension two also occur in $A_1\oplus A_1$, $B_2$, $G_2$, arbitrary two-control families, and generic codimension-two cuts. To select $A_2$, a bridge must recover stronger data such as the cubic map-germ $u^3+au+b$, its contact-equivalence class, Milnor or Tjurina number, discriminant cusp, vanishing-cycle pairing, or the relevant Weyl action. Only a functor preserving such structure could turn the numerical recurrence into a common invariant.

## What it does not license

No claim that the Keller monodromy equals the manuscript's triangulation; no derivation of three spatial dimensions; no import of the root system's six into KO-dimension ([[ko-dimension-as-morita-class/inq|separate owner]]). The firewall's discipline stands. [[algebra/a2-positive-completion|The positive-completion theorem]] now supplies one exact use for a compatible real slice: a path constrained to cross a smooth real discriminant meets a binary enveloping-$C^*$ fiber type, while a cusp passage is non-generic and has scalar completion. Neither statement constructs an open binary state family. [[construction-bridges|The bridge ledger]] keeps that result separate from CST's balanced binary member.
