# A2, the Fourth Register

The vault's firewall keeps three differently typed occurrences of $A_2$ apart: the Keller cusp discriminant with its $S_3$ sheet monodromy, the toric triangulation in the $S^6$ manuscript's cusp filling, and the root system with its Cartan geometry. This note nominates a fourth — Whitney–Thom stability — and, more usefully, names the invariant all four share: **rank-two transverse data**. Ownership of the firewall stays with [[algebra/type-ledger|the type ledger]]; this note is an audit item for it, not a second ledger.

## The fourth register

**[EXACT — classical singularity theory]** Whitney: the only stable singularities of smooth maps between surfaces are the fold and the cusp (Ann. of Math. **62**, 374 (1955)); in Thom–Arnold language, $A_1$ and $A_2$ are the only stable catastrophes with at most two control parameters, and the cusp's discriminant in its two-dimensional control plane $(a,b)$ is exactly $4a^3+27b^2=0$ — two fold branches meeting at the cusp point, symmetric under $b\mapsto-b$. So $A_2$ is not merely *a* singularity: it is **the generic stable geometry of two-parameter degeneration**. Anything that degenerates transversally in two parameters, and is stable, is locally a cusp or a pair of folds.

## The shared invariant

Collect the registers' transverse data: the Keller cusp lives in the two-dimensional $(a,b)$ base of the versal deformation ([[algebra/a2-inverse-cover|owner]]); the manuscript's cusp has rank-two vanishing lattice $\Lambda_{\mathrm{tor}}=\langle\hat w,\hat\delta\rangle$ with $N^2=0$ ([[s6-deep-read|deep read]], receipt-verified); the catastrophe control plane is two-dimensional by definition; and the programme's walls are codimension-two cuts whose two null normals span the modular plane ([[the-trichotomy-identification]], [[dimension-drop-as-group-change]]). Proposed common statement, graded and boxed:

> **[PROPOSED INVARIANT]** The registers agree not on the numeral six but on *rank-two transverse degeneration data*. $A_2$ recurs because it is the generic stable algebra of exactly that data — and physics keeps its books on codimension-two loci.

This is a candidate *reason* for the recurrences the firewall polices, which is different from a bridge between any two of them: it predicts $A_2$-shaped structure wherever rank-two transverse degeneration is generic, without identifying the registers.

## What it does not license

No claim that the Keller monodromy equals the manuscript's triangulation; no derivation of three spatial dimensions; no import of the root system's six into KO-dimension ([[ko-dimension-as-morita-class/entry|separate owner]]). The firewall's discipline stands. The constructive use — folds as generic binary events along a scale path, the cusp as the crossing — is made, with its own gates, in [[construction-bridges|Bridge 3]].
