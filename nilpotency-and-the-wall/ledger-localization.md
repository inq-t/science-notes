# Ledger Localization

In the $S^6$ manuscript's family, every smooth fibre is a complex 2-torus with Euler number zero, and the one unipotent-monodromy fibre — the non-normal toric surface built on the $A_2$ triangulation — carries $e(W)=2=e(X)$: the entire Euler characteristic of the six-sphere is stored at the parabolic point. This note records the fact, its standard analogues, and the typed rhyme it supports; it makes no promotion.

## The fact in the source

**[EXACT in the source]** The manuscript computes $e(X)=e(W)=2$, every fibre over the punctured base being a torus or free torus quotient (Euler number zero), and says itself that this is the pattern by which "the twelve nodal fibres carry the 12 of a rational elliptic surface." Localization of characteristic numbers at degenerate fibres is standard geometry; what the source adds is a case where a *single* parabolic degeneration carries the whole invariant of a manifold as featureless as a sphere, with the $A_2$ lattice organizing the collapsed fibre ([[s6-deep-read|deep-read note]] for the mechanics; [[algebra/s6-manuscript-branch|the branch note]] for status).

## The rhyme, typed

**[RHYME — not upgraded]** The pattern — homogeneous bulk carrying nothing, all invariants concentrated on the degeneration locus — is the shape of gravitational bookkeeping: horizon entropy on the codimension-two cut while the bulk state is locally featureless vacuum; the boundary-only gravitational charge of Noether's second theorem ([[philosophy/noether-conservation/inq|owner]]); the black hole encoded in its surface. It is also the shape of [[program-core/contextual-descent-from-homogeneity|contextual descent from homogeneity]]: a homogeneous algebra whose differentiated appearance lives in representation data, not in bulk lumpiness. The rhyme's honest content is a design expectation: **when the wall construction produces its ledger, the ledger should be supported on the parabolic locus and vanish on semisimple fibres.** That expectation is checkable in finite models ([[spectral-wall-descent/inq|spectral wall descent]]) and feeds [[construction-bridges|Bridge 5]]: records, if they are wall-events, inherit exactly this localization, which is what [[program-core/record-scale-soldering|record-scale soldering]] needs the record map to do.

## Boundary

Euler characteristic is not entropy; a complex threefold is not a spacetime; and localization theorems do not construct ledgers. The note asserts a shared *support pattern* across registers and marks it as the thing a correct wall construction would explain rather than assume.
