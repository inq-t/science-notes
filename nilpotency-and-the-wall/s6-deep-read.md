## The construction

The base is $\mathbb P^1$ as the completed orbifold quotient of the upper half-plane by the $(3,4,\infty)$ triangle group $\Delta=\langle g_1,g_2\mid g_1^3=g_2^4=1\rangle$, with two elliptic points and one cusp. A rank-four lattice representation sends $g_1,g_2$ to $T_1,T_2$ of orders 3 and 4, with $T_0=(T_1T_2)^{-1}=\mathbf1+N$, $N^2=0$ — receipt-verified in `receipts/nilpotent_data.py` from the printed matrices, including the stated action $N\gamma=Nu=0$, $Nw=-u$, $N\delta=\gamma$. Three period functions give a family of complex 2-tori over the punctured base; the very general fibre is **non-algebraic** with $\mathrm{NS}(F)=\mathbb Z\eta$ indefinite, and the completed threefold has algebraic dimension $a(X)=1$ — a family of complex presentations without polarization, which is exactly the object [[complex-presentation-without-polarization/entry|that module]] studies in the abstract.

The three special fibres are quotient fillings: Kodaira logarithmic transforms at the elliptic points (bielliptic reduced fibres, multiplicities 3 and 4) and Mumford's toric degeneration at the cusp, whose fan is the cone over the $A_2$ triangulation of the plane; the central fibre $W$ is the degree-six del Pezzo surface — hexagonal moment polygon — glued to itself along opposite sides, non-normal, two triple points. Gluing twists $(\ell_0,\ell_1,\ell_2)$ give $\pi_1(X)=\mathbb Z/|12\ell_0-4\ell_1-3\ell_2|$, with a Seifert reading on the $(3,4)$ fibration of $S^3$; suitable twists give the integral homology of $S^6$, then a diffeomorphism by recognition. The Euler number localizes: $e(X)=e(W)=2$ ([[ledger-localization]]).

## The CDP engagement, located

The manuscript's §10 is unusually disciplined: it grants that $X$ satisfies the hypotheses of CDP's Theorem 2.2 while $c_3(X)=2>0$ contradicts its conclusion, then locates two steps of the *printed* proofs that do not cover $X$. **Place (i), claimed decisive:** the reduction of a vanishing statement to the normalization of the singular fibre fails unconditionally at $W$ — since $H^2(X;\mathbb Z)=0$ forces $\operatorname{Pic}(X)\cong\mathbb C$, every line bundle is topologically trivial, and $R^2f_*(T_X\otimes L)\ne0$ for *every* $L$, so the key hypothesis holds for no twist and the proposition it feeds is vacuous for $X$. **Place (ii), claimed repairable:** a homology count assumes trivial monodromy on $H_1$ of the fibre (printed count $r=6$; with the actual co-invariants, $r=3$); the manuscript shows the input that step was meant to deliver exists for $X$ by another argument, so this gap alone would not save its Main Theorem from CDP. Whether place (i) survives expert scrutiny is precisely the manuscript's open fate; the branch note's verification obligations stand.
