# Dimension Drop as Group Change

"A black hole loses a dimension" is arithmetic; what actually happens, in every register where it can be checked, is a change of acting group. This note assembles four independent legs — one exact in the manuscript, three from the physics literature — and states the invariant they share: the ledger's home is codimension two, and it is reached by contraction, quotient, or symmetry enhancement, never by subtraction.

## Leg 1 — Quotients in the manuscript (exact in the source)

The $(3,4,\infty)$ family's three special fibres are constructed as quotients by three different groups: $\mathbb Z/3$ and $\mathbb Z/4$ rotations at the elliptic points (Kodaira logarithmic transforms), and the unipotent $T_0=\mathbf1+N$ at the cusp (Mumford's toric degeneration on the $A_2$ fan). The "lost" directions of the degenerate fibres are eaten by group actions — rank-two vanishing lattice at the cusp — and the fibre that changes group most violently ([[the-trichotomy-identification|parabolic]]) is the one that carries the topology ([[ledger-localization]]).

## Leg 2 — Carroll contraction at null surfaces (standard)

On any null hypersurface the induced metric degenerates and the local isometry structure contracts from Lorentz to the Carroll group — the $c\to0$ Inönü–Wigner contraction (Lévy-Leblond 1965; horizons as Carrollian geometry: Donnay and Marteau, Class. Quantum Grav. **36**, 165002 (2019)). Read against [[dimensional-ladder/the-saturation-ladder|the ladder]]: *at* the wall the stiffness register degenerates, and what remains is not "one dimension fewer" but a different group with null time. The wall is where the causal speed stops being a conversion and becomes a constraint.

## Leg 3 — The ledger produced by residual symmetry (standard result, imported)

Carlip obtains the Bekenstein–Hawking coefficient by imposing horizon boundary conditions, finding the residual diffeomorphisms form a Virasoro algebra with computable central charge, and letting Cardy's formula count (Phys. Rev. Lett. **82**, 2828 (1999)). Typed: the symmetry change at the wall does not merely accompany the ledger — in this derivation it *generates* it. This is the strongest known precedent for the programme's expectation that the wall's algebra, not its bulk, owns the entropy ([[deriving-g-v2/the-g-free-first-law|the G-free first law]]).

## Leg 4 — Spectral dimension runs to two (numerical/standard)

Causal dynamical triangulations find the spectral dimension of quantum spacetime running from four at large scales to about two in the ultraviolet (Ambjørn, Jurkiewicz, and Loll, Phys. Rev. Lett. **95**, 171301 (2005)), with parallel indications in asymptotic safety; 't Hooft's original dimensional-reduction argument (gr-qc/9310026) is the ancestor. Gravity is two-dimensional at both ends — ultraviolet spectral dimension and infrared ledger — and the two in question is the wedge's modular plane: one boost direction, one null direction, the parabolic-hyperbolic pair of [[the-trichotomy-identification]].

## The invariant, and the categorical form

Codimension two is not "minus one twice": it is the type of a *cut* — the locus whose two null normals span the modular plane. The categorical version of dropping-by-group-change is already in the vault: [[semiorthogonal-decompositions/entry|semiorthogonal decompositions as categorical walls]], where a category loses a "dimension" by acquiring a filtration, not by deleting objects. The design consequence for construction is stated as [[construction-bridges|Bridge 1]]: build the wall's transport so that its non-semisimple part is concentrated on a rank-two block — the group-change, not the subtraction, is the datum.
