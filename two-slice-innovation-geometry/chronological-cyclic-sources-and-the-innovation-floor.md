# Chronological Closure Makes the Innovation Floor a Spectral-Edge Test

The OI quotient on the complete chronological family generated by one source is determined by the lowest energy in that source's spectral support. A finite susceptibility or a positive quotient for the original source does not establish a positive floor on this larger family. Ordinary evolution of the same source supplies explicit filters that approach its spectral edge, with no replacement generator or source algebra.

**Status: exact spectral and chronological identities.** [[two-slice-innovation-geometry/oriented-innovation-and-finite-temporal-repair|OI]] fixes the response \(R=I-A^2\). [[general-causal-action/additive-neutral-sources-and-the-pairing-range-test|AR]] distinguishes a prescribed source span from its chronological closure. This module makes that distinction precise for an arbitrary nonnegative self-adjoint generator.

## The same source generates a reducing chronological carrier

Let \(K\ge0\) be self-adjoint on a Hilbert space. Choose a nonzero vector \(f\perp\ker K\), and define its finite spectral measure by
\[
\nu_f(B)=\langle f,\mathbf1_B(K)f\rangle,\qquad
d_f(s)=\langle f,e^{-sK}f\rangle
=\int_{(0,\infty)}e^{-s\lambda}\,d\nu_f(\lambda).
\tag{CS1}
\]
No normalization of \(f\) is needed. Its chronological carrier is
\[
\mathscr C_f
=\overline{\operatorname{span}\{e^{-sK}f:s\ge0\}}.
\tag{CS2}
\]
The spectral representation identifies \(f\) with the constant function one and \(e^{-sK}f\) with \(e^{-s\lambda}\). These functions span all of \(L^2(\nu_f)\). To verify density, fix any \(s_0>0\) and set \(x=e^{-s_0\lambda}\). The pushforward is a finite measure on \([0,1]\), with no mass at \(x=0\). The functions with \(s=js_0\), \(j\ge0\), are the polynomials in \(x\). Polynomial approximation of continuous functions on \([0,1]\), followed by their density in \(L^2\) of a finite measure, proves the claim.

Thus \(\mathscr C_f\) is precisely the spectral cyclic carrier of \(f\); it reduces \(K\) and its bounded functional calculus. It need not be the complete physical Hilbert space. The statement uses the entire closed family, including arbitrary finite linear combinations and norm limits, rather than a single readout at one duration.

## The cyclic OI floor equals the source's spectral edge

At a fixed duration \(a>0\), put
\[
R_a=I-e^{-2aK},\qquad
r_a(\lambda)=1-e^{-2a\lambda},\qquad
\lambda_f=\operatorname*{ess\,inf}_{\nu_f}\lambda.
\]
There is no vacuum denominator on \(\mathscr C_f\): for nonzero \(u\) in that space,
\(\langle u,R_au\rangle>0\). In spectral coordinates \(u=p(K)f\),
\[
\mathfrak q_a(u)
=\frac{\langle u,R_a^2u\rangle}{\langle u,R_au\rangle}
=\frac{\int r_a(\lambda)^2|p(\lambda)|^2\,d\nu_f}
       {\int r_a(\lambda)|p(\lambda)|^2\,d\nu_f}.
\tag{CS3}
\]
This is an average of \(r_a\) with positive weight \(r_a|p|^2d\nu_f\). Consequently
\[
\boxed{
\inf_{0\ne u\in\mathscr C_f}\mathfrak q_a(u)
=1-e^{-2a\lambda_f}.}
\tag{CS4}
\]
For the upper bound, choose a nonzero spectral restriction to
\([\lambda_f,\lambda_f+\epsilon)\cap(0,\infty)\). Such restrictions belong to \(\mathscr C_f\) by CS2, and their quotient is at most
\(r_a(\lambda_f+\epsilon)\). Let \(\epsilon\downarrow0\).
If \(\lambda_f=0\), arbitrarily small positive-energy support suffices; an atom at zero is neither required nor allowed here.

The same infimum can be approximated by finite linear combinations of the original time-translated source. Both quadratic forms are bounded and continuous in the vector norm. For each selected nonzero spectral restriction its denominator is positive, so sufficiently accurate chronological approximants preserve its quotient. The necessary approximation accuracy may deteriorate as the selected band approaches zero.

## A single inherited heat filter approaches the same edge

An explicit family already attains the infimum in the limit:
\[
u_T=e^{-TK}f,\qquad T\ge0.
\]
It is nonzero because \(e^{-T\lambda}>0\) at every finite spectral value. Its normalization does not change the quotient, and
\[
\boxed{
\mathfrak q_a(u_T)
=\frac{d_f(2T)-2d_f(2T+2a)+d_f(2T+4a)}
       {d_f(2T)-d_f(2T+2a)}.}
\tag{CS5}
\]
This finite-difference expression retains the changing innovation denominator.

Equivalently CS3 uses the exponentially tilted measure
\(e^{-2T\lambda}r_a(\lambda)d\nu_f(\lambda)\). The measure \(r_a\,d\nu_f\) has the same essential lower edge as \(\nu_f\). For every \(\epsilon>0\), compare its nonzero mass below \(\lambda_f+\epsilon/2\) with the mass above \(\lambda_f+\epsilon\). Their tilted ratio is bounded by a fixed constant times \(e^{-T\epsilon}\). The normalized tilted law therefore concentrates at its lower edge. Since \(r_a\) is bounded and continuous,
\[
\boxed{\lim_{T\to\infty}\mathfrak q_a(e^{-TK}f)
=1-e^{-2a\lambda_f}.}
\tag{CS6}
\]
The filter uses only the original chronological evolution. It does not assert that a fixed finite list of durations already attains the infimum, or that the norm of \(u_T\) is uniformly bounded below.

## A power-law spectral onset gives a quantitative filter

Suppose, for some \(p>0\), the spectral measure has a density near zero of the form
\[
\frac{d\nu_f}{d\lambda}
=c\lambda^{p-1}+O(\lambda^{p+1}),\qquad c>0.
\tag{CS7}
\]
The remaining finite measure is supported away from zero. Direct substitution \(z=s\lambda\) in the low-energy integral gives, for \(j=0,1,2\),
\[
(-1)^j d_f^{(j)}(s)
=c\,\Gamma(p+j)s^{-p-j}
+O(s^{-p-j-2}),\qquad s\to\infty.
\tag{CS8}
\]
Differentiation is justified for \(s>0\) by exponential damping; the part bounded away from zero is exponentially small, even if the full spectrum is unbounded.

Put \(d=2a\) and \(s=2T\). The denominator of CS5 is
\(\int_0^d[-d_f'(s+t)]\,dt\); its numerator is
\(\int_0^d\int_0^d d_f''(s+t+u)\,dt\,du\).
Using CS8 in these integrals, for fixed \(a>0\), proves
\[
\boxed{
\mathfrak q_a(e^{-TK}f)
=\frac{a(p+1)}{T}+O_a(T^{-2}).}
\tag{CS9}
\]
For [[general-causal-action/conditional-influence-diagonal-and-bulk-spectral-measure|the bulk conditional influence]], \(p=4\). Its susceptibility is finite, but its chronological filters have quotient
\(5a/T+O_a(T^{-2})\). The positive conditional character response therefore cannot, by itself, serve as a lower bound on this complete chronological carrier.

## What a spectral-measure return can and cannot certify

Even uniform convergence of diagonal time profiles together with convergence in absolute time integral does not guarantee a positive lower edge for approximating cyclic carriers. For example, take
\[
\nu_n=(1-n^{-2})\delta_1+n^{-2}\delta_{1/n},\qquad n\ge2.
\]
These are the spectral measures of normalized vectors for positive two-dimensional diagonal operators. They converge weakly to \(\delta_1\), and their Laplace transforms satisfy
\[
\sup_{s\ge0}|d_n(s)-e^{-s}|\le n^{-2},\qquad
\int_0^\infty|d_n(s)-e^{-s}|\,ds
=n^{-2}(n-1)\longrightarrow0.
\]
Nevertheless,
\[
\boxed{
\inf_{\mathscr C_{f_n}\setminus\{0\}}\mathfrak q_a
=1-e^{-2a/n}\longrightarrow0,}
\tag{CS10}
\]
while the limiting measure's cyclic floor is \(1-e^{-2a}>0\). Small spectral weight still determines the infimum if it is nonzero.

There is a useful converse direction. If an explicit weak limit has positive mass in every open interval \((0,\epsilon)\), weak convergence guarantees eventually positive mass there for the approximating measures. Thus a known gapless limiting measure can force qualitative collapse of their cyclic floors. It does not supply a rate for a moving threshold such as \(C/n\), a lower bound on that band's weight, or controlled normalization of filters whose duration grows with \(n\).

[[general-causal-action/conditional-influence-soft-band-and-chronological-filters|The actual compact soft-band test]] supplies those stronger facts for the original conditional influence. It uses a nonzero harmonic overlap and an actual generator defect before constructing the normalized chronological filter. This separates a returned diagonal measure from a quantified assertion about source-visible spectral support.

All durations in CS1–10 belong to the chosen generator \(K\). Under a supplied physical scale \(E\), a fixed physical duration \(\ell\) uses \(a=E\ell\). A vanishing floor at fixed scaled \(a\) cannot be relabeled as a vanishing floor at fixed physical \(\ell\). Nor does a result on \(\mathscr C_f\) establish the required four-dimensional theory or the composition law that must select its physical limits.
