# The Unit Wall Correspondence Ansatz

The proposed constitutive closure is a factorized correspondence ansatz with separately auditable clauses: it proposes to carry the scale character of the continuous core to cosmic logarithmic scale, the selected BKM tangent to the balanced unit response, a primitive additive channel to one nat, a chosen \(A_2\) positive chamber to two area-writing channels, and the descended response measure to an areal measure whose coefficient is never silently normalized by \(G\). In the reverse protocol that crossing coefficient is solved; in the forward protocol it is supplied. Measured laboratory \(G\) can supply it only through the additional fossil-transport clause. Until the physical target category and carrier-changing natural map are constructed, the clauses are a conjecture package rather than one demonstrated source of grounding economy.

## What is already functorial

[[minimal-cosmodynamic-closure/transport-provider-ledger|The existing transport provider]] separates what the repository has already built from the remaining physical leg. The core spectral member gives exact whole-core and finite-corner scale functors; half-sided modular tunnels give another exact transport constructor; canonical cores are functorial on tempered arrows; the scale-correspondence prestack types the contextual provider; and finite models exercise restriction and expectation naturality. None of these facts may be erased by saying simply that “the functor is missing.”

What remains missing is the **carrier-changing realization**. It must send the spectral context to causal cuts and a local Lorentzian net, preserve the chosen state and readout under scale and context restriction, return physical source and areal measures, and glue effectively. The separate horizontal box-spectrum functor does not act on the modular/weight carrier. What this note calls \(\mathfrak W_c\) is the crossing component of the still-open physical realization, not a replacement for the exact source functors.

## The ansatz

At the selected homogeneous crossing cut \(\Sigma_c\) in a declared Einstein--FLRW wall sector, let

$$
(\mathcal C_c,\tau_c,\beta_s^c,e_N^c,\Omega_c)
$$

be the corresponding continuous-core package of [[wall-construction-interface/core-spectral-wall|the core spectral wall]]. Let \(\mathcal K_A\) denote the proposed module of channels that can write positive areal measure. The **Causal-Wall Correspondence Ansatz** proposes a presentation-equivariant crossing correspondence

$$
\mathfrak W_c:
(\mathcal C_c,\tau_c,\beta_s^c,e_N^c,\Omega_c,\mathcal K_A)
\longrightarrow
(\Sigma_c,[g],v_N,T_X,\mu_A)
$$

for the declared sector, satisfying the following preservation squares. This displayed arrow is a specification, not yet the missing realization functor: the source transport category and its exact core member are supplied above, while the physical response-geometry target, carrier-changing morphisms, and naturality theorem remain construction targets. Extending \(\mathfrak W_c\) compatibly to every local region is the stronger universality construction; the integrated crossing law needed below does not assume that extension has already been proved.

### Scale square

The core trace character

$$
\tau_c\circ\beta_s^c=e^s\tau_c
$$

is carried to logarithmic cosmic scale with unit slope:

$$
s=N-N_c,
\qquad
\frac{\mathrm ds}{\mathrm dN}=1.
$$

This is the proposed origin of the unit-rate principle \(\nu=1\). [[scale-as-modular-observable/inq|Scale as a modular observable]] and [[dimensional-ladder/one-clock-many-dressings|one clock in many dressings]] motivate the map; they do not prove it.

The sign is additional data: the core character fixes an oriented image only after the member's post-crossing arrow has been chosen. The wider unit-rate principle fixes \(|\mathrm ds/\mathrm dN|=1\), not the positive orientation by itself.

### Response square

The physical horizontal quotient is carried to the balanced family

$$
p_\pm(s)=\frac{e^{\pm s}}{2\cosh s},
$$

and its BKM form is carried, in the same tangent and renormalization scheme, to the descended response measure. On the commuting quotient,

$$
g_{ss}^{\mathrm{BKM}}
=I_{\log\omega}
=\operatorname{sech}^2s,
$$

with unit Fisher response at the balanced cut. “Fisher response” means local statistical distinguishability under logarithmic scale displacement; it is not an entropy, charge, or inverse spatial variance. [[the-grain-of-causal-scale/material-realizations|Material realizations and Fisher response]] owns the explicit exponential-family calculation, while [[program-core/response-registers|the response-register ledger]] keeps the ontological types apart.

The homogeneous member additionally uses fixed extensivity in this tangent:

$$
G^\perp_{NN}(N)
=G^\perp_{NN}(N_c)
\frac{g^{\mathrm{BKM}}_{ss}(s)}{g^{\mathrm{BKM}}_{ss}(0)}.
$$

This factorization is part of the response square, not a consequence of the normalized finite-state Hessian.

### Channel-weight square

The correspondence is additionally proposed to be additive under independent primitive channel sums and to obey the channel-normalization relation isolated in [[deriving-g-v2/index-not-entropy|index, not entropy]]:

$$
\mathfrak R_c=\frac{\nu^2}{s_*}.
$$

The frozen unit member has \((\nu,\mathfrak R_c)=(1,1)\), so this square returns

$$
\boxed{s_*=1}.
$$

The relation is an extensive measure-preservation clause. It does **not** follow merely from the intensive equality \(g_{ss}^{\mathrm{BKM}}(0)=1\). Keeping the two units separate prevents a coordinate normalization of the Hessian from silently fixing the bulk ledger weight.

### Positive-support square

The hidden completion is proposed to be the trace-zero \(A_2\) triple, together with a **chosen area-writing chamber** satisfying

$$
\operatorname{rank}\mathcal K_A=\gamma=2.
$$

This rank is a clause of the correspondence, not a theorem from the label \(A_2\) alone. The motivations are distinct: the trace-zero triple has a two-dimensional Cartan plane, while the interior Schwarzschild--de Sitter root triple has two positive radii and one negative balancing root. In that prototype the cut value is understood from the chosen post-crossing chamber,

$$
\mathcal K_A
:=\lim_{\varepsilon\downarrow0}
\operatorname{Ran}\mathbf 1_{(0,\infty)}(D_\varepsilon),
$$

not as the strict-positive spectral rank of the degenerate operator exactly at \(\varepsilon=0\). Neither algebraic fact by itself identifies a wall channel with an areal contribution, and boundary degenerations require this declared multiplicity convention. The clause also does not say that \(A_2\) is a two-outcome sample space: the completion has three slots, whereas the observed response quotient is binary. [[algebra/a2-positive-completion|The \(A_2\) positive completion]], [[a2-ternary-response/inq|the ternary audit]], and [[inbox/the-rank-two-cell/the-rank-two-cell|the rank-two cell]] supply the separate algebraic faces. The physical identification remains conjectural until a trace or cyclic pairing sends \(\mathcal K_A\) to the areal ledger.

[[algebra/s6-manuscript-branch|The conditional \(S^6/A_2\) branch]] motivates seeking a nontrivial global-to-local carrier, but no numerical step in the closure is inferred merely from the existence of a complex structure on \(S^6\). The integer \(\gamma\) stands or falls with the area-writing channel map above.

### Area square

The minimal ansatz equates the **integrated** descended response at the selected cut with an areal measure carrying an initially unknown coefficient:

$$
\mu^{\mathrm{desc}}_{v_N,v_N}(\Sigma_c)
=\eta_*A_c.
$$

Here \(\eta_*\) has units of inverse area. The equality is a symmetric constraint: in the reverse protocol the common-count square below solves \(\eta_*\) without measured \(G\), while in the forward protocol an externally supplied **crossing** coefficient fixes its Einstein presentation. Laboratory \(G\) supplies that coefficient only after fossil transport is assumed. Only in the reverse orientation may one define the prediction

$$
G_{c,\mathrm{pred}}:=\frac{c^3}{4\hbar\eta_*}.
$$

The pointwise law \(\mathrm d\mu^{\mathrm{desc}}=\eta_*\,\mathrm dA\) on every local patch is a stronger universality conjecture, as is a full tensorial response correspondence. [[program-core/causal-capacity-equivalence|Causal-capacity equivalence]] owns those stronger targets.

### Fossil-transport square

Part of the fossil transport is already typed in [[crossing-evaluated-flat-modulus/inq|the crossing-evaluated flat modulus]]. Let

$$
\pi:\mathsf{Cut}\longrightarrow\mathsf{Sol},
\qquad
 c:\mathsf{Sol}\longrightarrow\mathsf{Cut},
\qquad
\pi\circ c=\operatorname{id},
$$

where \(c\) is a presentation-natural crossing selector. With \(\zeta=2/3\), define on solution space

$$
\chi_{\mathrm{foss}}
:=\frac{2}{3}\lambda_*^{-3}c^*R_A.
$$

The common-count result is exactly \(\eta_*=\chi_{\mathrm{foss}}\). Its pullback is automatically flat along every cut fiber:

$$
V\!\left(\pi^*\chi_{\mathrm{foss}}\right)=0
\qquad
\text{for every }V\in\ker(\mathrm d\pi).
$$

This is a proved fiber-flatness lemma after the selector and invariant ingredients are supplied; it is stronger and cleaner than inventing an unspecified connection on a number defined only at the crossing. [[bianchi-protection-of-the-areal-modulus/inq|Bianchi protection]] then proves that the Einstein coefficient \(\eta_{\mathrm E}=c^3/(4\hbar G)\) is constant on a connected nonvacuum Einstein realization. Consequently, the one-cut matching condition

$$
c^*\eta_{\mathrm E}=\chi_{\mathrm{foss}}
$$

extends throughout that connected solution. Ordinary restriction carries the same scalar to every admitted local region in that solution.

What remains open is not ordinary time transport along one supplied smooth Einstein history. It is the presentation-natural construction of \(c\), the state--Einstein equality at the cut without importing measured \(G\), any junction law if the wall is singular or distributional, and universality across distinct solution components or effective Einstein sectors. Only after the relevant conditions may one set \(G_{\mathrm{N,pred}}=G_{c,\mathrm{pred}}\), or insert measured local \(G_{\mathrm N}\) into the forward crossing oracle.

### Source square

On the imported Einstein--FLRW background fiber, the same response is carried to an effective perfect-fluid presentation satisfying

\qquad
\rho_X'+3(\rho_X+p_X)=0,
$$

with \(T_c\) the adopted apparent-horizon temperature scale and prime \(\mathrm d/\mathrm dN\). This is the homogeneous observational realization clause. It is not implied merely by the existence of the core or its state, and it does not claim a covariant perturbation completion.

## The common-count consequence

Suppose the trace-preserving correspondence also constructs an effective wall correlation length \(\lambda_*\), independently of any later material-energy identification, and identifies one bulk cell of volume \(\lambda_*^3\) per realized channel. Then the same dimensionless response count has the two presentations

$$
\eta_*A_c
=\gamma s_*\frac{V_c}{\lambda_*^3},
\qquad
s_*=1,
\qquad
\gamma=2.
$$

For a spherical cell, \(A_c=4\pi R_c^2\) and \(V_c=4\pi R_c^3/3\), so

$$
\boxed{
\eta_* = \frac{2R_c}{3\lambda_*^3},
\qquad
\zeta=\frac{\gamma s_*}{3}=\frac23.
}
$$

In the reverse protocol, defining the Einstein coupling only now gives

$$
\boxed{
G_{c,\mathrm{pred}}
=\frac{3c^3\lambda_*^3}{8\hbar R_c}.
}
$$

After that definition one may equivalently write \(\lambda_*^3=(8/3)\ell_{P,c}^2R_c\), with \(\ell_{P,c}^2:=\hbar G_{c,\mathrm{pred}}/c^3\). In this order the Planck area is a presentation of the solved coefficient rather than an input to it.

The factor \(1/3\) is \(V/(AR)\) for a three-ball, proved in [[bulk-area-cell-normalization/inq|bulk--area cell normalization]]. The factor \(2\) is the selected area-writing multiplicity. The unit weight is the separate channel-additivity clause above, not a consequence of unit Fisher curvature. These origins rhyme, but they must not be collapsed into one unexplained “two-thirds.”

The imported spatially flat Einstein--FLRW fiber supplies the apparent-horizon identity

$$
R_c=\frac{c}{H_c}.
$$

If the independently constructed wall length also obeys the optional reduced-Compton equality \(\lambda_*=\hbar/(m_*c)\), then

$$
\boxed{
G_{c,\mathrm{pred}}=\frac{3\hbar^2H_c}{8cm_*^3}.
}
$$

[[crossing-evaluated-flat-modulus/inq|The crossing-evaluated flat modulus]] explains why this is evaluated once on the solution and does not imply \(G(N)\propto H(N)\). [[bianchi-protection-of-the-areal-modulus/inq|Bianchi protection]] shows why a live varying coupling would be inconsistent with the declared Einstein fiber; it does not select the fossil value.

## Optional chiral-carrier clauses

The strongest economical completion adds two logically independent claims. The first is a normalization-rigid map from the normalized wall tangent to a chosen physical-point chiral kinetic coefficient; the second equates the resulting energy with the independently constructed wall correlation ruler.

Let \(v_N\) be normalized by \(g^{\mathrm{BKM}}(v_N,v_N)=1\) at the balanced cut. Let \(\xi_\chi\) be a declared physical-point chiral tangent with \(\operatorname{Tr}(\xi_\chi^\dagger\xi_\chi)=1\), using generators \(T^a\) normalized by \(\operatorname{Tr}(T^aT^b)=\delta^{ab}/2\). The leading chiral effective Lagrangian uses the chiral-limit constant \(F_0\):

$$
\mathcal L_2
=\frac{F_0^2}{4}
\operatorname{Tr}\!\left(D_\mu U D^\mu U^\dagger+\cdots\right).
$$

The oracle separately introduces the chosen physical-point kinetic form

$$
Q_\chi(\xi)
:=\frac{(F_\pi^\chi)^2}{4}
\operatorname{Tr}(\xi^\dagger\xi),
\qquad
F_\pi^\chi:=\frac{f_{\pi^+}^{\mathrm{PDG}}}{\sqrt2}.
$$

The current conventions are fixed by

$$
\langle0|A_\mu^a|\pi^b(p)\rangle
=i\delta^{ab}F_\pi^\chi p_\mu,
\qquad
\langle0|\bar d\gamma_\mu\gamma_5u|\pi^+(p)\rangle
=if_{\pi^+}^{\mathrm{PDG}}p_\mu,
$$

so the charged-current generator convention gives \(f_{\pi^+}^{\mathrm{PDG}}=\sqrt2F_\pi^\chi\). Thus \(F_\pi^\chi\simeq92\,\mathrm{MeV}\) is the physical-point chiral convention, whereas the Particle Data Group tabulates \(f_{\pi^+}^{\mathrm{PDG}}\simeq130\,\mathrm{MeV}\). Neither is the chiral-limit constant \(F_0\).

**CH1 -- Operator-induced kinetic-coefficient solder.** The wall construction supplies a presentation-equivariant operator map \(\mathfrak S_\chi(v_N)=\xi_\chi\) whose generator, trace, and field normalizations leave no free scalar rescaling, and

$$
Q_\chi(\mathfrak S_\chi v_N)
=E_*^2g^{\mathrm{BKM}}(v_N,v_N),
\qquad
\boxed{E_*^2=\frac{(F_\pi^\chi)^2}{4}}.
$$

This is a dimensionful solder, not an isometry: the source metric is dimensionless and the target quadratic form carries energy squared. Merely observing that two positive one-dimensional quadratic spaces admit a homothety would be tautological. CH1 is falsifiable only because \(\mathfrak S_\chi\) must be independently constructed and normalization-rigid. The kinetic coefficient is not being claimed to equal an axial-current Hessian without a separate second-variation calculation.

**CH2 -- Correlation-ruler identification.** The wall length \(\lambda_*\), already defined by the common-count construction, obeys

$$
\boxed{
\lambda_* =\frac{\hbar c}{E_*},
\qquad
E_*=m_*c^2.
}
$$

CH1 does not imply CH2. In particular, a decay constant is neither a particle pole mass nor automatically a correlation-length gap. If no independently defined wall correlator or spectral inverse length equals \(\hbar c/E_*\), CH2 is false.

The primary EFT source for the chiral quadratic form is Gasser and Leutwyler's [chiral effective action](https://doi.org/10.1016/0003-4916(84)90242-2); the physical charged-pion axial-matrix-element conventions are summarized by the [Particle Data Group](https://pdg.lbl.gov/2024/reviews/rpp2024-rev-pseudoscalar-meson-decay-cons.pdf). The conjecture must select one physical-point prescription before cosmology is inspected.

This is not the statement that the grain is a pion or that \(F_\pi^\chi\) is a particle pole mass. \(F_\pi^\chi\) is a vacuum-current response scale, which makes it a better-typed candidate than an arbitrary mass coincidence. But the clause was discovered by searching near the inverted \(46\)--\(47\,\mathrm{MeV}\) scale. Its numerical agreement is therefore not evidence for itself.

Composing CH1 and CH2 with the common-count law and \(R_c=c/H_c\) first gives a relation for the crossing coefficient \(G_c:=c^3/(4\hbar\eta_*)\):

$$
\boxed{
\Xi_{\chi,c}
:=\frac{G_c(F_\pi^\chi)^3}{\hbar^2c^5H_c}
=3,
\qquad
G_{c,\mathrm{pred}}=\frac{3\hbar^2c^5H_c}{(F_\pi^\chi)^3}.
}
$$

Only after the fossil conditions identify \(G_c=G_{\mathrm N}\) does the local forward oracle follow:

$$
\boxed{
\Xi_\chi
:=\frac{G_{\mathrm N}(F_\pi^\chi)^3}{\hbar^2c^5H_c}
=3,
\qquad
H_c=\frac{G_{\mathrm N}(F_\pi^\chi)^3}{3\hbar^2c^5}.
}
$$

The first orientation conditionally returns the cosmic absolute scale from measured local \(G_{\mathrm N}\) and a local vacuum-response determination. The reverse orientation returns the crossing background coefficient from an independently calibrated crossing rate and a \(G\)-free wall length; it becomes a prediction of Newton's \(G\) only after the same fossil conditions. Only one orientation may be counted in a given test, and neither current numerical protocol is yet the reverse kind.

## One programme, several conjectural clauses

Naturality is the hoped-for unifying mathematical claim: one carrier-changing construction should make all the squares commute. It is reasonable to seek that construction rather than four unrelated calibrations, but until it exists the squares count separately and do not yet establish structural or grounding economy. Failure is localized:

- a nonunit scale image kills the scale square but need not kill the core;
- a nonbinary physical quotient kills CST-B2 but not every response family;
- failure of the trace pairing kills \(\gamma=2\) as an area multiplicity;
- failure of the state--area equality kills the crossing background coefficient;
- failure of the selector, one-cut state--Einstein match, or cross-sector universality blocks its identification with Newton's \(G\), even though fiber-flatness and Bianchi protection may survive;
- failure of conservation kills this homogeneous source realization;
- failure to construct a normalization-rigid kinetic-coefficient solder kills CH1;
- failure of the response scale to define a correlation ruler kills CH2 even if CH1 survives.

[[flux-record-and-top-form-realizations/inq|Flux, records, and top-form realizations]] explains how Connes--Thom, groupoid assembly, and Baum--Connes-type transport could organize the naturality square. Assembly can preserve an already constructed class; it cannot manufacture the metric gap, select the event, or normalize the areal trace.

## The construction target

A completion must supply:

1. the physical response-geometry target category, the carrier-changing realization functor, and naturality under the already typed scale and context restrictions;
2. a physical horizontal quotient with a declared kernel;
3. a common trace or cyclic-cocycle pairing;
4. the additive measure map carrying a primitive normalized channel to one nat;
5. the map from the one-sided \(A_2\) support class to the two area-writing channels;
6. the localized state--geometry equality in one renormalization scheme;
7. the crossing selector and its presentation invariance;
8. the state--Einstein matching at the selected cut and its universality across the claimed solution sectors; the existing solution-space pullback and Bianchi theorem cover only smooth within-solution constancy, not a distributional wall or a cross-solution universality theorem;
9. if CH1 is retained, a presentation-equivariant operator map to the chiral kinetic or axial-current sector, with its second variation, generator, trace, physical-point, and tangent conventions fixed and no free rescaling; and
10. if CH2 is retained, an independently defined wall correlator or spectral inverse length that can be tested against \(\lambda_*=\hbar c/E_*\).

Until then, the ansatz is a legitimate explanatory springboard and a disciplined source of conditional consequences, not a completed theorem or a demonstrated economy theorem.
