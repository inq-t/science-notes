# The Existing Transport Provider

The programme already possesses exact cross-scale transport on its algebraic core, a typed correspondence-prestack scaffold, and finite restriction/naturality benchmarks. The missing keystone is therefore not “some functor” in the abstract: it is a carrier-changing realization from those source systems to causal cuts, a local Lorentzian net, physical state and source tangents, and independently normalized area, compatible with restriction and effective gluing.

## Exact core-scale functors

[[wall-construction-interface/core-spectral-wall|The core spectral wall]] supplies two strict scale systems. For the whole-core response carrier,

$$
\mathfrak F_{\mathrm{resp}}(N)=\mathcal A_N:=\mathcal C^{(N)},
\qquad
\mathfrak F_{\mathrm{resp}}(N_1\to N_2)
=\iota_{N_2:N_1}:=\beta_{N_2-N_1},
$$

and for the finite-capacity carrier,

$$
\mathfrak F_{\mathrm{cap}}(N)=\mathcal K_N:=e_N\mathcal Ce_N,
\qquad
\mathfrak F_{\mathrm{cap}}(N_1\to N_2)
=\kappa_{N_2:N_1}
:=\left.\beta_{N_2-N_1}\right|_{\mathcal K_{N_1}}.
$$

Both obey exact composition and zero holonomy. The response system also carries one transported normal-state orbit and a state-preserving binary expectation whose Fisher--BKM response is the selected logistic pulse. Thus the abstract algebra--state--transport--readout construction is real and uses neither measured \(G\) nor a fitted expansion history.

These functors are global scale systems over a trivial context leg. They do not yet assign local algebras to Lorentzian regions or send the spectral context \(e_N\) to a causal cut \(\Sigma_N\).

## Other exact transport constructors

[[wall-construction-interface/half-sided-modular-tunnel|A half-sided modular tunnel]] constructs a positively oriented nested family and fiber isomorphisms

$$
\iota_{r_2:r_1}
=\left.\operatorname{Ad}U(r_2-r_1)\right|_{\mathcal A_{r_1}},
\qquad
\iota_{r_3:r_2}\iota_{r_2:r_1}=\iota_{r_3:r_1}.
$$

Its invariant reference state is horizontally BKM-null. A nonzero response requires a separately declared faithful state path, so half-sided modularity supplies orientation and transport rather than the physical response law.

[[wall-construction-interface/cross-fiber-transport|Cross-fiber transport]] records a second standard theorem: tempered normal unital \(*\)-homomorphisms induce canonical compatible morphisms of Falcone--Takesaki cores, and composite arrows induce composite core maps. [[library/functoriality-of-connes-takesaki-flow-of-weights/inq|Elliott's theorem]] owns the primary functoriality result. A proposed physical arrow must still be constructed and proved tempered. Half-sided invariance alone does not supply the operator-valued weight required for that conclusion, so the half-sided and Elliott routes cannot be chained without another theorem.

## The typed contextual provider

[[spectral-wall-descent/scale-correspondence-stack|The scale-correspondence prestack]] already gives the correct source scaffold:

$$
\mathfrak X_{\mathrm{corr}}:
\mathsf{Scale}
\longrightarrow
\operatorname{PSt}(\mathsf{Ctx},\mathbf{W^*Corr}),
$$

where scale arrows are von Neumann correspondences and compose by Connes fusion,

$$
X_{32}(U)
\boxtimes_{\mathcal M_{N_2}(U)}
X_{21}(U)
\simeq X_{31}(U).
$$

This is a precise provider specification, not an instantiated physical stack. A bare correspondence does not choose a conditional expectation, CP instrument, edge state, or record. Q-system or inclusion-plus-expectation data are additional, and effective descent on physical covers remains open.

Two finite constructions show that the desired compatibility laws are attainable. [[wall-construction-interface/finite-cellular-markov-wall|The finite cellular Markov wall]] supplies a local net with isotony, commuting disjoint regions, and coherent scale presentation maps. [[spectral-wall-descent/conditional-expectation-balance|Conditional-expectation balance]] supplies nested composition and the equivariance identity

$$
E_{gx}\circ\alpha_g=\alpha_g\circ E_x.
$$

They are continuum test objects, not Lorentzian cosmological realizations.

## Functors that do not close this edge

The raw `inbox/the-box-spectrum-functor` note constructs a canonical horizontal spectral map from Schwarzschild--de Sitter root data into the \(F_4\)-quotient of \(\mathfrak h_3(\mathbb O)\). Its quotient and fiber calculations are relevant to the \(A_2\) carrier, but the note explicitly does not act on weights, modular flow, or the half-sided-modular family. It cannot serve as the core-to-causal realization.

Likewise, [[flux-record-and-top-form-realizations/inq|Baum--Connes and Connes--Thom naturality]] can transport or compare an already constructed equivariant class. Assembly does not create the wall class, select the event, construct a causal net, or normalize area.

## The remaining naturality theorem

The reusable assembly is now narrow enough to state cleanly. Start with the existing scale leg and a declared physical context site. Construct the physical restriction arrows and prove that the algebraic arrows lie in the admissible, where relevant tempered, subcategory. Then construct a carrier-changing realization

$$
\mathcal R:
\text{core/correspondence provider}
\longrightarrow
\text{causal response geometry}
$$

whose components include

$$
e_N\longmapsto\Sigma_N,
\qquad
\mathcal B_N\longmapsto\text{physical quotient/readout},
\qquad
G^{\mathrm{BKM}}_N\longmapsto\mu_N^{\mathrm{desc}},
$$

and which is natural under both scale transport and local restriction. It must additionally return the source presentation and an area measure with independent normalization, then satisfy effective gluing on covers.

| Layer | Present standing |
|---|---|
| abstract core scale transport | exact for the selected core member |
| half-sided modular transport | exact for the reconstructed tunnel; invariant state is response-null |
| functorial cores on tempered arrows | standard theorem after tempering is established |
| contextual correspondence provider | typed prestack specification; physical instantiation and effective descent open |
| finite restriction and expectation naturality | exact benchmarks |
| core-to-causal carrier-changing realization | open keystone |
| area, source, fossil, and factive continuation | separate downstream maps, not consequences of transport alone |

The correct conclusion is therefore neither “the functor is missing” nor “the functor is done.” The source-side functors are done; the physical realization functor and its locality theorem are not.
