## What current data do not yet test

The homogeneous curve does not by itself predict primary CMB anisotropies, structure growth, weak lensing, gravitational slip, neutrino masses, or a dark-matter abundance. Those require [[causal-scale-theory/conjectures/covariant-response-sector|the covariant response sector]] and a declared matter coupling. Preserving the Standard Model on local fibers is compatible with the closure; claiming to derive or modify it would require a different construction.

The next decisive work is therefore not another background curve. It is either a covariant perturbation completion capable of a joint CMB--BAO--supernova--growth likelihood, or the operator correspondence that makes the chiral-grain oracle more than a well-typed post-search conjecture.
