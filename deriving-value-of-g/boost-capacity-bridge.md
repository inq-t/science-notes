# Conjecture: Boost Geometry Determines Horizontal Capacity

A local causal horizon carries a geometrically normalized boost, while modular theory supplies a related \(2\pi\)-normalized flow in suitable wedge and inclusion settings. The conjecture is that these structures can normalize a same-tangent bridge from horizontal state distinguishability to gravitational canonical energy, thereby determining the quadratic capacity \(G^{\perp}_{NN}\). It does not identify that capacity with a boost charge, and it does not permit the horizon-area coefficient to be inserted as an input.

## The typed target

Let a wall construction supply a transported family

$$
N\longmapsto
(\Sigma_N,\mathcal A_N,\omega_N,\mathcal T_{N_2N_1})
$$

and a canonically normalized physical horizontal tangent \(v_N\). Its cut-integrated descent capacity is

$$
G^{\perp}_{NN}[\Sigma_N,N]
:=
\mu^{\mathrm{desc}}_{v_N,v_N}(\Sigma_N)
=g^{\mathrm{BKM}}(v_N,v_N),
$$

when the regulated or renormalized BKM form exists. This is a positive quadratic response. By contrast, a boost charge \(Q_{\xi}\) pairs linearly with a boost generator \(\xi\). A numerical equality between \(Q_{\xi}\) and \(G^{\perp}_{NN}\) would therefore compare different algebraic orders and would not constitute a bridge.

The appropriate gravitational object is instead a quadratic response such as canonical energy. If \(\delta g_N\) is the gravitational image of the same physical tangent, the candidate weld has the form

$$
\boxed{
g^{\mathrm{BKM}}(v_N,v_N)
\stackrel{?}{=}
Z_{\mathrm b}\,
\mathcal E_{\xi_N}(\delta g_N,\delta g_N),}
$$

where \(\xi_N\) is the geometrically normalized local boost and \(Z_{\mathrm b}\) remains symbolic until both sides are independently constructed. The linear Noether charge may determine the generator, boundary term, and first-variation identity from which canonical energy is obtained; it is not itself the capacity.

At a distinguished cut \(\Sigma_c\), let the causal-area measure \(\mu_A\) be fixed independently of the state response, and let the gravitational theory independently supply an entropy measure \(\mu^{S,\mathrm{grav}}\) in the same prescription. Write

$$
\frac{S_c}{k_B}
:=
\mu^{S,\mathrm{grav}}(\Sigma_c),
\qquad
\mathfrak R_c
:=
\frac{k_B}{S_c}G^{\perp}_{NN}(N_c).
$$

The conjectural boost route must calculate this ratio. Setting the Bekenstein--Hawking or Wald coefficient in advance and then recovering it from the same canonical-energy normalization is a consistency check, not a derivation.

## Why the boost structure is suggestive

Local horizon boosts and modular evolution both exhibit a canonical \(2\pi\) normalization. Half-sided modular inclusions sharpen the resemblance: modular dilation acts affinely on a positive null translation. This supplies a promising symmetry skeleton for a causal wall and may remove an otherwise arbitrary rescaling of the relevant generator.

The resemblance does not yet identify the physical deformation. Modular automorphism of a fixed state is vertical motion, whereas \(N\mapsto\omega_N\) is horizontal comparison between transported fibers. [[wall-construction-interface/vertical-and-horizontal-motion|Vertical modular flow and horizontal state change]] states the missing construction precisely. A valid bridge must first produce \(v_N\), then prove that its gravitational image is the perturbation used in \(\mathcal E_{\xi_N}\).

Nor does the normalized two-channel wall profile fill this gap. [[binary-information-geometry/inq|Binary information geometry]] gives an exact reduced susceptibility, but it fixes neither the extensive number of channels nor their density per independently measured area. Its translation tangent is also not automatically an escort-temperature tangent.

The controlled results gathered in [[deriving-value-of-g/noether-capacity-theorem|the Noether--capacity theorem]] show that BKM response, canonical energy, and Noether structure can be linked under explicit hypotheses. [[deriving-value-of-g/obstructions-to-an-unconditional-proof|The obstruction register]] shows why boost symmetry, self-duality, and a first law alone cannot fix the quadratic normalization.

## Upgrade criterion

This conjecture becomes a theorem for a declared wall class only if one construction does all of the following:

1. specifies the local algebra, faithful state family, cross-fiber transport, physical quotient, and normalization of \(N\) without using measured \(G\), the desired cosmological pulse, or the target entropy-area coefficient;
2. constructs the geometric boost and any modular inclusion, and proves how their parameters and generators are related rather than inferring identity from the shared \(2\pi\);
3. maps the transported horizontal tangent \(v_N\) to a definite gravitational perturbation \(\delta g_N\) and proves the same-tangent quadratic identity with \(Z_{\mathrm b}\) left symbolic;
4. controls edge modes, boundary terms, anomalies, gauge degeneracies, trace normalization, and renormalization on both sides;
5. proves local additivity and absolute continuity of the capacity measure with respect to an independently normalized \(\mu_A\); and
6. computes \(G^{\perp}_{NN}(N_c)\), \(S_c/k_B\), and hence \(\mathfrak R_c\) without normalizing either quantity by the desired answer.

A proof only of the integrated equality \(\mathfrak R_c=1\) would establish a weak crossing law. A strong local equivalence additionally requires equality of the corresponding measure densities almost everywhere on the cut.

## Failure criterion

The proposed bridge fails in its present form if the construction supplies only a linear boost charge or first-variation law; if its modular parameter remains purely vertical; if the physical horizontal tangent has an unfixed scale or a component outside the boost sector; or if edge, regulator, state, or region dependence prevents a finite common quadratic form. It also fails as a derivation of gravitational normalization if the area or entropy coefficient is imported from the gravitational action before \(Z_{\mathrm b}\) is solved.

A calculation that fixes a dimensionless \(\mathfrak R_c\) while leaving the number of channels per physical area free does not determine an areal modulus or a value of \(G\). Conversely, a finite and scheme-stable result with \(\mathfrak R_c\ne1\) would refute the unit version of the conjecture without refuting every possible boost--capacity correspondence.
