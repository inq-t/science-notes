# Conjecture: A Horizon Algebra Determines Capacity

A dynamically constructed near-horizon conformal or Virasoro algebra could place the causal-wall sector in an ensemble where entropy and horizontal quadratic capacity are calculable together. The conjecture is viable only if the algebra contains the physical horizontal scale deformation, its extensive normalization is derived, and physical area is fixed independently. A two-dimensional normal plane by itself supplies none of these facts.

## The typed target

The required datum is not the label “CFT,” but a concrete structure such as

$$
(\mathcal A_H,\omega_{H,N},\mathcal T_{N_2N_1},
L_0,c_H,\text{boundary conditions},\text{ensemble}),
$$

with a controlled representation and regime of validity. Cross-fiber transport must define the physical horizontal tangent \(v_N\). Its response is

$$
G^{\perp}_{NN}(N)
=g^{\mathrm{BKM}}_{\omega_{H,N}}(v_N,v_N),
$$

not the expectation value of \(L_0\), a Virasoro charge, or a central charge. Those are linear or representation-theoretic data; capacity is the Hessian of distinguishability along a specified tangent.

For a genuine thermal or escort family generated by a fixed modular Hamiltonian \(K\), one may calculate

$$
C_E
=\operatorname{Var}(K)
=\partial_{\vartheta}^{\,2}\log Z,
$$

where \(\vartheta\) is logarithmic modular temperature. But this equals the wall capacity only after tangent alignment. In general,

$$
v_N=\lambda v_{\vartheta}+v_{\perp},
\qquad
G^{\perp}_{NN}
=\lambda^2C_E+\lVert v_{\perp}\rVert^2_{\mathrm{BKM}}.
$$

Thus a partition function does not by itself turn physical scale change into heat capacity.

At the distinguished cut, the target remains

$$
\boxed{
\mathfrak R_c
:=
\frac{k_B}{S_c}G^{\perp}_{NN}(N_c),}
$$

where \(S_c/k_B=\mu^{S,\mathrm{grav}}(\Sigma_c)\) is the dimensionless gravitational entropy in the common prescription. The area measure \(\mu_A\) must be independently normalized so that both

$$
\frac{G^{\perp}_{NN}(N_c)}{\mu_A(\Sigma_c)}
\quad\text{and}\quad
\frac{S_c/k_B}{\mu_A(\Sigma_c)}
$$

are genuine calculated densities rather than reciprocal definitions.

## The possible mechanism

The hoped-for construction is

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\text{wall modes and boundary conditions}
\longrightarrow
(c_H,L_0,\omega_H,\text{ensemble}),\\
&\text{horizontal transport}
\longrightarrow v_N
\longrightarrow G^{\perp}_{NN},\\
&\text{state counting plus a horizon bridge}
\longrightarrow \frac{S_c}{k_B},\\
&\text{independent causal area}
\longrightarrow \mathfrak R_c
\text{ and the two areal densities}.
\end{aligned}
$$

In a homogeneous \(1+1\)-dimensional conformal thermal sector, the dilation Ward identity gives \(C_E/(S/k_B)=1\). [[deriving-value-of-g/noether-capacity-theorem|The Noether--capacity theorem]] makes the hypotheses explicit: the physical tangent must be the unit escort tangent, the entropy must belong to that same regulated state and equal the horizon entropy, and any reduction must preserve its norm. The central charge cancels from the ratio, but it still controls the extensive entropy and capacity. The theorem therefore does not determine how many channels occupy one square metre.

[[binary-information-geometry/entry|Binary information geometry]] can be a sufficient reduced image of this larger sector only if a sufficiency theorem proves preservation of the physical BKM norm. Its balanced two-level profile is neither a Virasoro algebra nor an extensive area normalization. In particular, its centered translation tangent is not the escort tangent of the same state.

## Why the normal plane is insufficient

The normal bundle of a codimension-two cut is two-dimensional and carries a local boost action. That kinematic fact does not construct a local operator algebra, stress tensor, Virasoro modes, central extension, positive-energy representation, modular-invariant partition function, or Cardy regime. It also does not show that the cosmological horizontal tangent is generated by one of the algebra's charges.

The distinction between vertical modular evolution and horizontal scale change is therefore decisive. [[wall-construction-interface/vertical-and-horizontal-motion|Vertical modular flow and horizontal state change]] explains why even a perfectly defined modular Hamiltonian does not automatically generate the required family \(N\mapsto\omega_{H,N}\). [[deriving-value-of-g/obstructions-to-an-unconditional-proof|The obstruction register]] further excludes arguments from dimensionality, self-duality, replication, or Noether conservation alone.

## Upgrade criterion

This conjecture becomes a theorem for a declared wall class only if one construction:

1. derives the wall modes, their algebra, Hamiltonian, central extension, representation, state or ensemble, and boundary conditions from independently stated wall data;
2. proves the regime in which its thermodynamic or Cardy calculation is valid, including finite-size, curvature, and anomaly corrections;
3. constructs cross-fiber transport and identifies a canonically normalized horizontal tangent represented in the algebra, rather than substituting vertical modular flow;
4. computes the entropy and BKM capacity and proves their same-state and same-tangent relation, with any escort and transverse components displayed;
5. proves that the calculated state entropy is the gravitational entropy entering the horizon law, rather than equating them by name;
6. fixes trace, regulator, edge-mode, and renormalization choices in one common prescription;
7. fixes \(\mu_A\) independently and derives the channel density per area; and
8. obtains a finite, scheme-stable \(\mathfrak R_c\) without inserting measured \(G\), the Bekenstein--Hawking coefficient, or the desired unit value.

If the central charge or zero-mode normalization is first read from a gravitational boundary action proportional to \(1/G\), the calculation may verify compatibility with that action. It derives \(G\) only if the gravitational coefficient is retained symbolically and independently calculated state data determine it afterward.

## Failure criterion

The route fails if no genuine horizon algebra exists, if boundary choices make its central extension noncanonical, if the selected state lies outside the controlled thermodynamic or Cardy regime, or if the horizontal generator is absent from the algebra. It also fails if entropy and capacity belong to different states or tangents, if their common renormalization cannot be fixed, or if the answer changes with regulator or arbitrary trace normalization.

A stable algebraic calculation that leaves the areal channel density free determines at most a dimensionless ratio. A stable result \(\mathfrak R_c\ne1\) would falsify the unit-capacity claim for that wall realization, not the existence of the algebra. A result equal to one is evidential only when tangent alignment, horizon-entropy identification, and independent area normalization were proved before the comparison.
