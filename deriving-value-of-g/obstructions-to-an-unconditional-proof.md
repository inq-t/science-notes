# Obstructions to an Unconditional Capacity Proof

The exact binary, modular, self-dual, and descent structures presently in the workspace do not force \(\chi_{\downarrow}=\eta_{\mathrm E}\). Several explicit countermodels preserve those structures while changing or annihilating the relevant capacity. These are no-go results for insufficient premises, not evidence against every possible causal-wall completion.

## The balanced-binary tangent obstruction

For the normalized binary family

$$
\rho_\theta
=\frac{e^{\theta Q}}{2\cosh\theta},
\qquad
Q^2=\mathbf 1,
$$

the horizontal \(Q\)-tangent has BKM norm

$$
g^{\mathrm{BKM}}_{\theta\theta}
=\operatorname{Var}_{\rho_\theta}(Q)
=\operatorname{sech}^2\theta.
$$

The modular Hamiltonian of the same state is

$$
K_\theta
=-\ln\rho_\theta
=-\theta Q+\ln(2\cosh\theta)\mathbf1.
$$

Its entanglement capacity is therefore

$$
C_E(\rho_\theta)
=\operatorname{Var}_{\rho_\theta}(K_\theta)
=\theta^2\operatorname{sech}^2\theta.
$$

At the self-dual point,

$$
\rho_0=\frac{\mathbf1}{2},
\qquad
g^{\mathrm{BKM}}_{\theta\theta}(0)=1,
\qquad
C_E(\rho_0)=0,
\qquad
\mathcal S(\rho_0)=\ln2.
$$

Thus the binary scale tangent is not the replica-temperature tangent of the same state. The former is maximal at its balanced reference point; the latter vanishes because \(K_0\) is central. The binary profile can consistently modulate an independently supplied extensive wall capacity, but it cannot itself prove that the extensive capacity equals horizon entropy or that the balanced point is a constructed cosmological event.

## The replication obstruction

Take \(m\) independent copies of any admissible reduced channel. Relative entropy, BKM norm, entropy, and modular variance add under tensor products:

$$
G^{(m)}_{NN}=mG^{(1)}_{NN}.
$$

The relations

$$
Q^2=1,
\qquad
JQJ=-Q,
\qquad
\theta\mapsto-\theta,
\qquad
g_{\theta\theta}=\operatorname{sech}^2\theta
$$

remain true in each factor. Causal order, the two null orientations, cocycle composition, self-duality, and the Witten/Darboux profile do not specify \(m\). If the same geometric cut area is assigned to all these models, \(G_{NN}/A\) changes by \(m\).

Therefore no collection of the current dimensionless structural lemmas can determine the number of active channels per square metre. A channel-to-area soldering theorem is logically necessary.

## The tangent-normalization obstruction

The BKM metric is quadratic in the physical tangent. Under

$$
v_N\longmapsto a v_N,
$$

one has

$$
G^{\perp}_{NN}\longmapsto a^2G^{\perp}_{NN},
$$

while the background entropy is unchanged. Ratio dependence of a Connes cocycle proves that a one-generator coordinate is logarithmic,

$$
\theta=\varrho_\perp(N-N_c),
$$

but does not determine \(\varrho_\perp\). A group action, Ward identity, or explicit wall construction must fix which normalized tangent physical scale realizes.

The general decomposition

$$
v_N=\lambda v_{\mathrm{escort}}+v_\perp
$$

gives

$$
G^{\perp}_{NN}
=\lambda^2 C_E+\lVert v_\perp\rVert^2.
$$

Positivity bounds the last term below by zero; it does not set \(\lambda=1\) or \(v_\perp=0\).

## Self-duality does not fix a Hessian

Suppose a logarithmic partition function obeys a reflection or modular-duality symmetry

$$
f(x)=f(-x).
$$

At the fixed point this forces

$$
f'(0)=0,
$$

but leaves \(f''(0)\) arbitrary. The location of a symmetric extremum is first-order information; capacity is its curvature. Self-duality can select the crossing without fixing the BKM norm there.

A maximally mixed finite state makes the point more strongly: it is invariant under every unitary symmetry and has maximal entropy, but its modular variance is zero.

## Noether conservation does not fix normalization by itself

The dilation Ward identity fixes an equation of state and, in one active spatial dimension, the ratio \(C_E/\mathcal S=1\). It leaves the overall central charge or channel density free.

The diffeomorphism Noether identity fixes relations among Hamiltonian charge, symplectic flux, and Wald entropy after a gravitational Lagrangian and its coefficient are supplied. It does not determine that coefficient. Likewise:

- the entanglement first law fixes a first variation;
- relative-entropy monotonicity fixes an inequality;
- canonical energy equals a BKM quadratic form for specified holographic perturbations; and
- generalized-entropy laws fix monotonicity or concavity along nested horizons.

None alone equates an arbitrary second-order horizontal norm with a zeroth-order entropy.

## The continuum and trace-normalization obstruction

Local QFT algebras are generally type III and do not possess density matrices or finite von Neumann entropy. Crossed-product constructions can produce type-II horizon algebras with a semifinite trace and an entropy identified with generalized entropy in controlled gravitational regimes. But a type-\(\mathrm{II}_\infty\) trace has no canonical overall normalization without extra input.

Rescaling the trace changes the additive entropy convention while a modular variance is insensitive to central shifts of \(K\). A comparison of entropy and capacity therefore requires a common, physically fixed trace and renormalization prescription. Matter entanglement alone also carries cutoff and species dependence; the finite gravitational object is renormalized generalized or Wald entropy.

Finite index does not remove this obstruction. For a type-I product state and an auxiliary tracial expectation on the same multiplicity factor, it fixes the edge-entropy ceiling

$$
\frac12\log\operatorname{Ind}_{W}(E_\tau),
$$

while a chosen input edge entropy obeys

$$
S(\chi)
=\frac12\log\operatorname{Ind}_{W}(E_\tau)
-D(\chi\Vert\tau).
$$

This identity neither gives the full subalgebra relative-entropy capacity nor makes \(E_\tau\) select \(\chi\); the code expectation selecting a fixed edge state is generally different. Equating its entropy with half a log index therefore requires independent state selection and edge-entropy saturation. With nontrivial centers, scalar minimal index is not the functorial compositional datum; the matrix dimension or full correspondence must be retained.

## The dimensional obstruction

The binary family, cocycle character, BKM profile, reflection, and Ruble ratio are dimensionless. The desired modulus has dimensions

$$
[\chi_{\downarrow}]=L^{-2}.
$$

No manipulation of dimensionless data, \(c\), \(\hbar\), and \(k_B\) produces an inverse area. A complete theory must construct an independent physical area spectrum, a length relative to another independently fixed scale, or an areal density of channels. Without that datum, even a proof of the unit Ruble ratio does not predict the SI magnitude of \(G\).

## What survives the no-go results

The obstructions leave a sharply defined theorem target:

1. construct a wall algebra and scale-indexed state independently of \(G\) and the fitted expansion history;
2. prove that the physical scale tangent is canonically normalized and identify its escort and transverse components;
3. prove a regulator-independent, locally additive BKM measure on the cut;
4. derive its Radon--Nikodym density with respect to an independently normalized area measure;
5. construct the central edge-entropy operator of the gravitational correspondence and prove its all-patch density with respect to spectral area;
6. prove that the retained regional tangent maps to gravitational canonical energy with the gravitational kinetic coefficient kept symbolic; and
7. show that the resulting edge entropy is the one entering the horizon focusing law.

The [[noether-capacity-theorem|Noether--capacity theorem]] shows one controlled way the unit ratio can emerge. The [[causal-scale-derivation-target|causal-scale derivation target]] states the additional work needed to turn that ratio into a value of \(G\).
