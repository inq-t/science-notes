# Deriving the Value of G

Newton's constant can be read as geometric compliance, proportional to the inverse of a universal entropy modulus per causal area. The construction now has a concrete algebraic route: a finite-index gravitational correspondence together with extra expectation and fixed-edge-state data can supply a candidate central entropy operator, while a cut spectral triple supplies area and a same-tangent theorem must identify that edge density with the horizontal BKM density. In a type-I product cell, a chosen input edge entropy plus its distinction from the tracial edge state equals half the Watatani log index of an auxiliary tracial expectation; the state selection, physical normalization of the Dirac operator, and all-patch density equality remain open. The programme therefore explains the type of object that can determine \(G\) without yet producing its measured magnitude.

## What it would mean to derive a dimensionful constant

The decimal value of \(G\) depends on the chosen units. A claim to derive it can therefore have three different strengths:

| Claim | Required result | Present status |
|---|---|---|
| Structural classification | Identify which independently typed quantities \(G\) relates | Available |
| Conditional determination | Show that a constructed dimensional modulus implies the Einstein coefficient | Available under horizon-equilibrium assumptions |
| First-principles value | Calculate that modulus without using measured gravity, the area law with known \(G\), or a fitted cosmology | Open |

The invariant content is a conversion law, an intrinsic area relative to another physical scale, or a dimensionless ratio. Calling \(G\) a [[cosmodynamics/soldering-constants|soldering structure]] is explanatory only when its source and target structures are independently constructed.

## Two areal coefficients that must remain distinct

Let the dimensionless entropy of an Einstein causal horizon vary locally as

$$
\delta\!\left(\frac{S_{\mathrm{hor}}}{k_B}\right)
=\eta_{\mathrm E}\,\delta A.
$$

The Einstein entropy--area density is

$$
\eta_{\mathrm E}
:=\frac{\mathrm d(S_{\mathrm{hor}}/k_B)}{\mathrm dA}
=\frac{c^3}{4\hbar G}
=\frac{1}{4\ell_P^2}.
$$

Hence

$$
G=\frac{c^3}{4\hbar\eta_{\mathrm E}}.
$$

This is exact given the Bekenstein--Hawking area law, but it merely solves an established relation for \(G\). [[areal-information-modulus]] develops this coefficient and its action, curvature, and Planck-scale readings.

Causal Scale Dynamics suggests a different object. For a scale-indexed family of states on a causal cut \(\Sigma\), let

$$
D(\omega_{N+\delta N}\Vert\omega_N)
=\frac12G^{\perp}_{NN}[\Sigma,N]\,\delta N^2
+o(\delta N^2),
$$

after the central and vertical directions have been removed. Define the proposed **areal descent modulus**

$$
\chi_{\downarrow}[\Sigma,N]
:=\frac{G^{\perp}_{NN}[\Sigma,N]}{A_\Sigma}.
$$

Because \(N\) and relative entropy are dimensionless, \([\chi_{\downarrow}]=L^{-2}\). It measures the second-order distinguishability generated by a Weyl-scale displacement per unit causal area. Its inverse

$$
\mathfrak a_{\downarrow}:=\chi_{\downarrow}^{-1}
$$

is an areal compliance: area per unit natural-log information curvature. It is not thereby a literal area atom or pixel.

The central conjecture is not a definition:

$$
\boxed{\chi_{\downarrow}=\eta_{\mathrm E}.}
$$

The two sides have the same dimensions but different origins. The left side belongs to horizontal quantum-state geometry; the right side belongs to causal-horizon thermodynamics and gravitational focusing. Equating them is the proposed state--geometry weld.

$$

$$

What remains open is upstream. The Noether--capacity theorem fixes the ratio once the physical wall is proved to be a unit thermal-dilation channel, but the wall algebra and state family have not supplied that tangent identification or a finite, regulator-independent channel density per area. The [[causal-scale-derivation-target|causal-scale derivation target]] states the missing construction, anti-circularity test, universality conditions, and failure modes.

This is a conditional determination, not yet a value: rescaling the cut Dirac operator rescales \(a_*\), and a nonmaximal edge state need not saturate the index ceiling.
