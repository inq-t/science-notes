# Research Record: Knotting as Dimensional Presentation

This is the evidence record for the claim that knot formation may select an observable three-dimensional spatial presentation and for its possible connection to mass, time, gravity, and the Yang--Mills mass gap. Research was conducted on 2026-09-02 for the physics knowledge base. The direct finding is conditional: ordinary loop knotting uniquely distinguishes three-space, but topology becomes physical mass only after a carrier, state, scale-bearing energy form, and uniform spectral inequality are constructed.

## Scope and direct answer

The investigation tested five questions:

1. In what exact sense are knots unique to three dimensions?
2. What mathematical object bears the obstruction, and is it directed or irreversible?
3. Can the one-dimensional carrier be motivated by gauge theory rather than assumed ad hoc?
4. Can knot topology force finite positive energy or invariant mass?
5. Does any such result approach the four-dimensional Yang--Mills mass-gap stopping condition?

The resulting claim chain is

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\text{primitive loop carrier plus ambient-isotopy distinction}
&&\Longrightarrow \text{spatial }d=3,\\
&\text{knot sector plus scale-balancing energy}
&&\Longrightarrow \text{positive sector energy in specific models},\\
&\text{positive sector energy}
&&\not\Longrightarrow \text{global quantum mass gap},\\
&\text{three-dimensional Chern--Simons configurations}
&&\Longrightarrow \text{four-dimensional ASD gradient histories},\\
&\text{all of the above}
&&\not\Longrightarrow \text{Lorentzian Yang--Mills existence or a uniform gap}.
\end{aligned}
$$

## Exact topology of a single loop

For unparametrized tame embeddings modulo ambient isotopy,

$$
\mathfrak K_d=\{S^1\hookrightarrow\mathbb R^d\}/\sim,
$$

the planar Jordan--Schoenflies theorem gives one class, ordinary knot theory gives nontrivial classes in \(d=3\), and general-position unknotting gives one class again for \(d\ge4\). Skopenkov's General Position Theorem 2.1(a) states that every \(n\)-manifold unknots in \(\mathbb R^m\) for \(m\ge2n+2\); with \(n=1\), this covers \(m\ge4\).

The claim is sensitive to types. Zeeman constructs higher-dimensional codimension-two knots by spinning, while Haefliger classifies nontrivial smooth embeddings in higher codimension. Consequently, the usable theorem is about a one-dimensional primitive, not topology in general. The dimension filter is rigorous after that primitive and a globally Euclidean or spherical ambient are assumed; the ontological reversal that would derive those assumptions remains conjectural.

For \(S^p\hookrightarrow S^n\), the transverse meridian is \(S^{n-p-1}\), and Alexander duality makes the complement homologically equivalent to that meridian sphere. If the carrier and detector are both one-dimensional, then \(n-p-1=1\) with \(p=1\), hence \(n=3\). In codimension two the abelianized complement group is always \(\mathbb Z\); nontrivial knot type is held by finer nonabelian and peripheral data.

## Operator and carrier audit

The relevant maps are not interchangeable:

$$
\begin{array}{rcl}
\operatorname{PT}_A&:&\gamma\longmapsto\operatorname{Iso}(E_{\gamma(0)},E_{\gamma(1)}),\\
W_R(K)&:&\mathcal A/\mathcal G\longrightarrow\mathbb C,\\
\widehat W_R(K)&:&\operatorname{Dom}(\widehat W_R)\subset\mathcal H_{\mathrm{phys}}\longrightarrow\mathcal H_{\mathrm{phys}},\\
H&:&\operatorname{Dom}(H)\subset\mathcal H_{\mathrm{phys}}\longrightarrow\mathcal H_{\mathrm{phys}}.
\end{array}
$$

Parallel transport operates on paths and returns fiber isomorphisms; a based loop gives a group element only after choosing a frame, and otherwise gives conjugacy data. A Wilson loop is a gauge-invariant function of a connection and may become an observable operator only after a regulator or continuum definition, representation, renormalization, Hilbert completion, and domain are supplied. The Hamiltonian acts on physical states. Its spectral gap is not an eigenvalue or invariant of the knot.

The complement group and character variety,

$$
\pi_1(Y\setminus\nu K),
\qquad
\operatorname{Hom}(\pi_1(Y\setminus\nu K),G)/G,
$$

provide an algebraic bridge from knot topology to flat gauge data. This complement construction is distinct from ordinary Wilson holonomy along the knot itself, because that curve has been removed from the complement. Witten's Chern--Simons path integral provides a quantum bridge from Wilson lines to framed-link invariants, with an additional framing correction or normalization required for the unframed Jones invariant. Classical metric independence, together with quantum framing dependence, still supplies no local propagating energy scale.

The Wilson--'t Hooft algebra gives an even more direct physical distinction: linked electric and magnetic loop supports commute only up to a center phase determined by their charge pairing. It turns spatial linking into an order--disorder relation, but regulator, global gauge-group form, renormalization, and sector determine its Hilbert-space realization. The phase is a charge invariant rather than an energy lower bound.

## Energy and mass audit

An elastic-loop model realizes the proposed units grammar exactly. For

$$
E[\gamma]=T L(\gamma)+B\int_\gamma\kappa^2ds,
$$

the two terms scale oppositely. Cauchy--Schwarz combined with Fenchel's theorem gives \(E\ge4\pi\sqrt{TB}\) for any closed loop, and Fary--Milnor strengthens this to \(E>8\pi\sqrt{TB}\) for a nontrivial knot. The universal nontrivial-knot total-curvature threshold supplies a dimensionless number; it is not itself a knot invariant. The factor \(\sqrt{TB}\) supplies an energy unit. These are classical infimum bounds on smooth embedded curves with fixed \(T,B>0\), not a quantum gap, and reconnection can destroy any putative superselection. Tension alone scales to zero at fixed knot type, so opposing scale behavior is essential.

The Faddeev--Skyrme model is the strongest clean example of topology acquiring energetic cost. The field is a map \(S^3\to S^2\), its Hopf number labels sectors, and the quadratic/quartic derivative terms scale oppositely under dilation. The Vakulenko--Kapitanskii bound prevents the energy of a nonzero sector from approaching zero, and Faddeev--Niemi exhibit numerical knot solitons.

The example also exposes the limitation. A positive infimum in \(Q_H\ne0\) sectors is not a lower bound on every state orthogonal to the vacuum. Without a potential, linearized waves in the topologically trivial sector remain massless. Topological soliton mass and vacuum spectral gap are different predicates.

In flat spacetime, the invariant mass of an isolated finite-energy system is determined by total four-momentum, not by constituent labels. Two nonparallel null momenta have a timelike sum:

$$
M^2c^4=2E_1E_2(1-\cos\theta)>0.
$$

This supports a constrained conjecture: topology could trap non-collinear flux and thereby stabilize a timelike composite sector. It does not show that it does so, and a timelike rest frame is not yet a clock. Radiation stress--energy also curves spacetime, and an isotropic kinetic or coarse-grained radiation gas has a collective fluid frame even though a coherent null beam need not. The defensible ontological concern is that null cones alone determine conformal causal order but not a calibrated metric scale.

## Three-to-four transgression

On an oriented Riemannian three-manifold \(Y\), the Chern--Simons functional has gradient proportional to \(*_YF_A\). On \((\mathbb R\times Y,dt^2+g_Y)\), in temporal gauge and up to normalization, time rescaling, and orientation convention, its negative gradient flow is anti-self-dual Yang--Mills in four Euclidean dimensions. This is an exact Euclidean and Morse-theoretic correspondence, not physical Lorentzian evolution. Instanton knot homology incorporates a three-dimensional knot through prescribed codimension-two singular and meridional data.

The history coordinate is auxiliary or Euclidean at this stage. It becomes physical time only after a positivity and reconstruction theorem or a direct Lorentzian realization. Reversibility of isotopy and gauge flow does not by itself produce actualization or persistent records.

## Yang--Mills stopping condition

Let \(\Omega\) be the unique physical vacuum and \(\mathcal E\) the closed form of \(H-E_0\). The target is

$$
\inf_{\psi\perp\Omega,\ \|\psi\|=1}\mathcal E[\psi]\ge\Delta>0
$$

after infinite volume and continuum removal in fixed physical units. A knot-based proof would have to establish a physical sector decomposition, stability under every allowed quantum process, coercivity on each sector, control of the topologically trivial sector, exhaustivity of the decomposition, and uniformity of the bound. Continuum existence and Lorentzian reconstruction remain additional obligations.

A usable factorization would construct dimensionless closable linear distinction maps \(\delta_j:\mathcal D_0\to\mathcal K_j\) on a centered \(\mathcal E\)-form core and prove, uniformly,

$$
\sum_j\|\delta_j\psi\|^2
\ge\kappa_{\mathrm{top}}\|\psi\|^2,
\qquad
\mathcal E[\psi]
\ge\varepsilon_*\sum_j\|\delta_j\psi\|^2.
$$

Then \(\operatorname{gap}(H)\ge\varepsilon_*\kappa_{\mathrm{top}}\), provided the sum is defined, both constants are regulator- and volume-uniform, the common kernel is \(\operatorname{Ran}P_0\), and the core is an energy-form core. This proves an energy gap for an already constructed Hamiltonian. A Yang--Mills mass gap additionally requires the relativistic continuum theory, translations, spectrum condition, and vacuum representation. The factorization separates the dimensionless algebraic contribution \(\kappa_{\mathrm{top}}\) from the dynamical energy yardstick \(\varepsilon_*\).

The combined dimensional observation is nevertheless notable. A one-dimensional holonomy probe, if separately identified with the primitive physical carrier, conditionally selects spatial \(d=3\), while the Yang--Mills coupling is dimensionless exactly in spacetime \(D=4\). If a separate history theorem supplies \(D=d+1\), there is no classical intrinsic mass scale at the same dimensional junction; a nonzero quantum scale would have to enter through renormalization and dimensional transmutation. This gives a coherent programme, not a proof that the scale exists, a gap proof, or a numerical prediction.
