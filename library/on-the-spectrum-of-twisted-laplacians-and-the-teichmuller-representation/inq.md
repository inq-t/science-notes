# On the Spectrum of Twisted Laplacians and the Teichmüller Representation

Frédéric Naud and Polyxeni Spilioti study flat-bundle Laplacians with nonunitary twists over compact hyperbolic surfaces. Unlike the unitary case, the natural twisted operators are non-self-adjoint; the authors locate their discrete spectra in a parabolic region and prove eigenvalue-counting estimates for Teichmüller-type representations. The paper supplies a clean type firewall between monodromy and a canonically positive spectral operator.

- Authors: Frédéric Naud, Polyxeni Spilioti
- Preprint: [arXiv:2205.09540](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.09540)
- Source: [TeX and ancillary links](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.09540)
