# Generalized Global Symmetries

Davide Gaiotto, Anton Kapustin, Nathan Seiberg, and Brian Willett formulate (q)-form global symmetries, whose charged operators and excitations are extended rather than pointlike. They develop their Ward identities, background-field couplings, gauging, spontaneous breaking, and anomaly structure, providing the standard framework in which the finite center symmetry of pure (SU(N)) Yang--Mills is distinguished from its continuous local gauge redundancy.

- Authors: Davide Gaiotto, Anton Kapustin, Nathan Seiberg, Brian Willett
- Published: 2015
- Journal: *Journal of High Energy Physics* **02** (2015), 172
- DOI: [10.1007/JHEP02(2015)172](https://doi.org/10.1007/JHEP02(2015)172)
- arXiv: [1412.5148](https://arxiv.org/abs/1412.5148)
