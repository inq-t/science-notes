# Poincare and Logarithmic Sobolev Inequalities by Energy-Landscape Decomposition

Menz and Schlichting study low-temperature diffusion in a smooth confining energy landscape. They derive Eyring--Kramers estimates for Poincare and logarithmic Sobolev inequalities by combining estimates inside metastable regions with weighted-transport bounds between them. They distinguish equal-depth and unequal-depth minima.
