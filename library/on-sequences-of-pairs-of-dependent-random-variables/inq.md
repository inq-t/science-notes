# On Sequences of Pairs of Dependent Random Variables

Hans Witsenhausen studies dependence across sequences of independent pairs and proves the tensorization law for maximal correlation: the maximal correlation of a finite product pair is the maximum, rather than the product or sum, of the component coefficients. The article develops the Hilbert-space operator structure behind this result and related asymptotic questions.

- Author: Hans S. Witsenhausen
- Published: *SIAM Journal on Applied Mathematics* **28** (1975), 100--113
- DOI: [10.1137/0128010](https://doi.org/10.1137/0128010)
- Local artifacts: none
