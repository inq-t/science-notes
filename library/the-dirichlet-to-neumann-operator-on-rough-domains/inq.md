# The Dirichlet-to-Neumann Operator on Rough Domains

Wolfgang Arendt and A. F. M. ter Elst define the Dirichlet-to-Neumann operator on \(L^2\) of the boundary of a bounded connected open set whose boundary has finite codimension-one Hausdorff measure. Their form construction yields a self-adjoint operator whose negative generates a contractive \(C_0\)-semigroup, and they relate its long-time behavior to the existence and uniqueness properties of boundary traces of \(H^1\) functions.
