# Witten Laplacian Methods for the Decay of Correlations

Assane Lo studies weighted Witten-Laplacian equations for classical convex unbounded lattice models. The covariance representation through the inverse operator on one-forms yields weighted response estimates and exponential decay of two-point correlations.

- Author: Assane Lo
- Published as: *Witten Laplacian Method for The Decay of Correlations*, Journal of Statistical Physics **132**, 355–396 (2008)
- DOI: [10.1007/s10955-008-9547-6](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10955-008-9547-6)
- Author preprint: [arXiv:math-ph/0611002v9](https://arxiv.org/abs/math-ph/0611002v9), revised 2007-03-24
