# Witten Laplacian Methods for the Decay of Correlations

Assane Lo develops Witten-Laplacian methods for estimating decay of correlations in lattice spin systems. The covariance of two observables is represented through the inverse Witten Laplacian on one-forms, and weighted operator estimates are used to derive exponential spatial decay under the paper's hypotheses.

- Author: Assane Lo
- Published: *Journal of Statistical Physics* **132** (2008), 355--396
- DOI: [10.1007/s10955-008-9547-6](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10955-008-9547-6)
- arXiv and source: [math-ph/0611002](https://arxiv.org/abs/math-ph/0611002)
