# From Euclidean Field Theory to Quantum Field Theory

Dirk Schlingemann presents a \(C^*\)-algebraic version of Osterwalder--Schrader reconstruction that directly reconstructs a Haag--Kastler net of bounded operators from suitable Euclidean data. The framework includes non-pointlike objects such as Wilson loop variables.

- Author: Dirk Schlingemann
- Submitted: 1998-02-06
- Published: *Reviews in Mathematical Physics* **11** (1999), 1151--1178
- DOI: [10.1142/S0129055X99000362](https://doi.org/10.1142/S0129055X99000362)
- arXiv: [hep-th/9802035](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9802035)
