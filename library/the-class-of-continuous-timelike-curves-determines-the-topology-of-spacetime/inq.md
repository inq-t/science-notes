# The Class of Continuous Timelike Curves Determines the Topology of Spacetime

Malament proves that the continuous timelike curves determine the topology of spacetime. As corollaries, the causal structure of every future- and past-distinguishing spacetime determines its topology, and the Hawking--King--McCarthy path topology encodes the spacetime's topological, differential, and conformal structure.
