# Fusion Basis for Lattice Gauge Theory and Loop Quantum Gravity

Delcamp, Dittrich, and Riello construct a fusion basis for lattice gauge theory and loop quantum gravity in 2+1 dimensions, working with finite gauge groups. Representations of the Drinfel’d double organize magnetic and electric excitations, while ribbon operators create and measure them. The resulting hierarchy supports coarse-graining and descriptions of subsystems; the paper also discusses possible extensions to Lie groups.
