# Entropic Order Parameters in Weakly Coupled Gauge Theories

Horacio Casini, Javier M. Magan, and Pedro J. Martinez compute entropic order parameters for smeared nonlocal class operators in weakly coupled gauge theories. Wilson and 't Hooft classes are organized by weight and coweight lattices; finite class groups give finite-index expectations, while continuous class groups lead to divergent index. The paper derives weak-coupling saturation and suppression laws, certainty-relation checks, and perturbative renormalization-group behavior.

- Authors: Horacio Casini, Javier M. Magan, Pedro J. Martinez
- Published: 2022
- Journal: *Journal of High Energy Physics* **01** (2022), 079
- DOI: [10.1007/JHEP01(2022)079](https://doi.org/10.1007/JHEP01(2022)079)
