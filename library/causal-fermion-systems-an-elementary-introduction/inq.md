# Causal Fermion Systems: An Elementary Introduction to Physical Ideas and Mathematical Concepts

Felix Finster and Maximilian Jokel introduce causal fermion systems, emphasizing the physical motivation and the conceptual and mathematical foundations. The presentation explains how a Hilbert space, a space of operators, and a measure provide a framework for describing spacetime and causal relations through the causal action principle.

- Authors: Finster, Felix; Jokel, Maximilian.
- First submitted: 2019-08-22.
- Archived revision: 1908.08451v3.
