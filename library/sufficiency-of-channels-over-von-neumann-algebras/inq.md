# Sufficiency of Channels over von Neumann Algebras

Petz defines sufficiency for a normal faithful 2-positive channel between von Neumann algebras relative to a family of input states and characterizes it through fixed points of the channel followed by its state-dependent adjoint. The result supplies the operator-algebraic recovery criterion underlying equality in relative-entropy data processing.
