# Reflection Positivity and Spectral Theory

Palle Jorgensen and Feng Tian study the change of Hilbert-space geometry induced by Osterwalder--Schrader reflection positivity and its probabilistic Markov counterpart. They formulate reflection positivity using projections and a reflection on one Hilbert space, clarify that the Markov property implies but is stronger than reflection positivity, and give a canonical factorization of the compressed reflection operator.
