# Modular Localization and Wigner Particles

Brunetti, Guido, and Longo construct local real subspaces and observable nets from positive-energy representations of the Poincare group using an abstract Tomita--Takesaki framework. The Poincare representation is an input to the construction, including its Wigner mass and spin data.

## Metadata

- **Authors:** Romeo Brunetti, Daniele Guido, and Roberto Longo.
- **Submitted:** 13 March 2002; revised 18 June 2002.
- **Published:** 2002.
- **Journal:** *Reviews in Mathematical Physics* **14**, 759--786.
- **DOI:** [10.1142/S0129055X02001387](https://doi.org/10.1142/S0129055X02001387).
- **arXiv:** [math-ph/0203021v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/math-ph/0203021).
- **Local artifacts:** none.

The opening paragraph paraphrases the authors' abstract and records the direction of construction.
