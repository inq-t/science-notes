# Inner Product Modules over $B^*$-Algebras

Paschke develops inner-product modules over $B^*$-algebras, proves the
self-duality and adjointability properties available over $W^*$-algebras,
and constructs the Hilbert-module GNS factorization of a completely positive
map through a cyclic vector and a $*$-representation.

## Bibliography

- Author: William L. Paschke
- Published: *Transactions of the American Mathematical Society* **182**
