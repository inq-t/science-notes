# Cosmological Event Horizons, Thermodynamics, and Particle Creation

Gibbons and Hawking extend horizon thermodynamics to cosmological solutions with positive cosmological constant. They associate entropy and surface gravity with an observer's cosmological event horizon and show that a particle detector sees thermal radiation, making the entropy and temperature explicitly dependent on the observer's accessible region and time normalization.
