# A Geometric Approach to the Yang--Mills Mass Gap

Puskar Mondal formulates a conditional geometric route to a Yang--Mills Hamiltonian gap. The reduced space of spatial connections modulo gauge transformations carries the kinetic Riemannian metric; after weighting it by the squared ground-state wave functional, a regularized Bakry--Emery Ricci lower bound yields a lower bound on the Hamiltonian spectrum. The paper assumes that the quantum theory and its normalizable vacuum already exist. In (3+1) dimensions its curvature trace requires a regularization scale whose rigorous origin and normalization remain open.
