# Sufficient Subalgebras and the Relative Entropy of States of a von Neumann Algebra

Denes Petz studies restriction of faithful normal states from a von Neumann algebra to a subalgebra. Equality of Araki relative entropy before and after restriction is characterized by retention of the Connes Radon--Nikodym cocycle and equivalently by generalized conditional-expectation conditions, giving an exact operator-algebraic meaning to a subalgebra retaining all distinction relevant to a state pair.

- Author: Denes Petz
- Published: 1986
- Journal: *Communications in Mathematical Physics* **105**, 123--131
- DOI: [10.1007/BF01212345](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01212345)
