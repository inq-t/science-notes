# Scale Invariance in the Spectral Action

Ali H. Chamseddine and Alain Connes promote the arbitrary mass scale in the spectral action to a dilaton field and compute the resulting low-energy terms for the noncommutative geometry of the Standard Model. In their construction the matter couplings are scale invariant apart from the dilaton kinetic and Einstein--Hilbert terms, making explicit both the geometric usefulness of the spectral action and the separate role of its scale datum.

- Authors: Ali H. Chamseddine, Alain Connes
- Submitted: 2005-12-14
- Published: *Journal of Mathematical Physics* **47** (2006), 063504
- DOI: [10.1063/1.2196748](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.2196748)
- arXiv: [hep-th/0512169](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0512169)
