# The Equation of State in (2+1)-Flavor QCD

The HotQCD Collaboration computes the continuum-extrapolated equation of state and speed of sound of physical-mass $(2+1)$-flavor QCD over $130$--$400\,\mathrm{MeV}$. It gives an analytic pressure parametrization and locates the chiral crossover band at approximately $145$--$163\,\mathrm{MeV}$, with corresponding energy densities $0.18$--$0.5\,\mathrm{GeV/fm^3}$.
