# Shorting, Parallel Addition and Form Sums of Nonnegative Selfadjoint Linear Relations

Arlinskiĭ extends shorting and parallel addition from bounded positive operators to nonnegative self-adjoint linear relations and their closed forms. The extension is relevant when a continuum response is unbounded or has a multivalued part, and it makes domain and strong-resolvent convergence conditions explicit rather than hiding them in a formal Schur complement.

- Author: Yu. M. Arlinskiĭ
- Submitted: 2 December 2019
- arXiv: [1912.00910](https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.00910)
- Published: *Linear Algebra and its Applications* **599** (2020), 156--187
- DOI: [10.1016/j.laa.2020.04.007](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.laa.2020.04.007)
