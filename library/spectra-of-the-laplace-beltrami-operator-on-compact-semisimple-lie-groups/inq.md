# The Spectra of the Laplace--Beltrami Operator on Compact, Semisimple Lie Groups

Beers and Millman study the Laplace--Beltrami spectrum of compact semisimple Lie groups, relating the invariant differential operator to the representation-theoretic decomposition of functions on the group. The paper is a primary source for reading the scalar Laplacian through Casimir eigenvalues after a Riemannian normalization has been fixed.

## Metadata

- **Authors:** Brian Lee Beers and Richard S. Millman.
- **Published:** August 1977.
