# Reflection-Positive Construction of a Four-Dimensional SU(N) Yang--Mills Theory with Mass Gap and Confinement

Mir Faizal and Arshid Shabir present a reflection-positive lattice programme for pure \(SU(N)\) Yang--Mills theory. The article claims that a strong-coupling transfer-operator gap and Wilson-loop area law persist through a finite-range multiscale flow to a universal Osterwalder--Schrader continuum limit compatible with asymptotic freedom.

- Authors: Mir Faizal, Arshid Shabir
- Published: 2026
- Journal: *Fortschritte der Physik* **74** (4), e70097
- DOI: [10.1002/prop.70097](https://doi.org/10.1002/prop.70097)
- arXiv: [2606.19362v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19362v1)
- Local artifacts: none
