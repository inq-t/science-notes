# The Radon--Nikodym Theorem for von Neumann Algebras

Pedersen and Takesaki characterize weights invariant under a modular automorphism group by positive operators affiliated with the centralizer and establish the corresponding noncommutative Radon--Nikodym representation.

## Metadata

- **Authors:** Gert K. Pedersen and Masamichi Takesaki.
- **Published:** 1973.
- **Journal:** *Acta Mathematica* **130**, 53--87.
- **DOI:** [10.1007/BF02392262](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02392262).
- **Local artifacts:** none.
