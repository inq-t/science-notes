# Monotone Metrics on Matrix Spaces

Petz classifies monotone inner products on matrix spaces under stochastic mappings using operator-monotone functions. The result supplies the finite-dimensional family of monotone quantum information metrics and clarifies the relation between statistical geometry, relative entropy, and generalized covariance.
