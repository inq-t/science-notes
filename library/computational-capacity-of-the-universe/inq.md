# Computational capacity of the universe

Seth Lloyd estimates limits on the information that the universe can register and the elementary operations it can perform. The analysis combines bounds from energy, quantum dynamics, entropy, and gravitation to estimate the computational capacity accumulated over cosmic history.

- Authors: Lloyd, Seth.
- First submitted: 2001-10-24.
- Archived revision: quant-ph/0110141v1.
- arXiv: [quant-ph/0110141v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0110141v1).
- DOI: [10.1103/PhysRevLett.88.237901](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.88.237901).
