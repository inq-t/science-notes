# Itô Maps and Analysis on Path Spaces

Elworthy and Li develop Malliavin calculus for diffusion measures on paths in compact manifolds. They study how Itô maps intertwine Sobolev derivatives, conditional expectations, and diffusion operators, using connections induced by the stochastic differential equation. The analysis includes domains and uniqueness questions for derivatives and Dirichlet forms, with complete intertwining when the noise is injective.

## Metadata

- **Authors:** K. D. Elworthy and Xue-Mei Li.
