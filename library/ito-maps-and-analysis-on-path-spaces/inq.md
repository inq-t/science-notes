# Itô Maps and Analysis on Path Spaces

Elworthy and Li develop Sobolev calculus for diffusion measures on compact-manifold path spaces. They establish intertwining results for Itô maps, investigate uniqueness of derivative operators and Dirichlet forms, and analyze conditional expectations and covariant differentiation using connections induced by stochastic differential equations.

K. D. Elworthy and Xue-Mei Li, *Mathematische Zeitschrift* **257** (2007),
643–706. DOI: 10.1007/s00209-007-0145-0.
