# Spinning particles, coadjoint orbits and Hamiltonian formalism

Andrzejewski et al. give an explicit Hamiltonian description of massive relativistic spinning particles using Poincaré coadjoint orbits. They parametrize the orbit by factoring a general Lorentz transformation into a pure boost and a rotation, derive an equivalent constrained dynamics on the Poincaré group, classify its first- and second-class constraints, identify the first-class gauge transformations with the stabilizer of a canonical orbit point, compute the Dirac brackets, and quantize the system to obtain the infinitesimal form of irreducible Poincaré-group representations.
