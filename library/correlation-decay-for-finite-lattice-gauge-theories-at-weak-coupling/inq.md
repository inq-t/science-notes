# Correlation Decay for Finite Lattice Gauge Theories at Weak Coupling

Arka Adhikari and Sky Cao prove exponential decay of correlations at weak coupling for a broad class of gauge-invariant observables in lattice gauge theories with finite, possibly non-Abelian gauge groups. The covered observables include arbitrary functions of Wilson-loop observables.

## Metadata

- **Authors:** Arka Adhikari and Sky Cao.
- **Published:** 2025.
- **Journal:** *The Annals of Probability* **53** (1), 140--174.
- **DOI:** [10.1214/24-AOP1702](https://doi.org/10.1214/24-AOP1702).
- **arXiv and source:** [2202.10375](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.10375).
