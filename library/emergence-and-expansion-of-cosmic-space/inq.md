# Emergence and Expansion of Cosmic Space as due to the Quest for Holographic Equipartition

T. Padmanabhan proposes that the difference between surface and bulk degrees of freedom drives the emergence of cosmic space. In Planck units, the rate of change of Hubble volume equals this difference; with the proposed definitions, the equation reproduces standard cosmological evolution. De Sitter space supplies the equilibrium case in which the boundary and bulk counts coincide.
