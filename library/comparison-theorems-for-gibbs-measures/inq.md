# Comparison Theorems for Gibbs Measures

Rebeschini and van Handel develop comparison bounds for probability measures from their local conditional specifications. Their block-influence framework extends classical Dobrushin comparison beyond its single-site uniqueness regime, with applications to dimension-dependent errors in particle filtering.

## Metadata

- **Authors:** Patrick Rebeschini and Ramon van Handel.
- **Publication:** Journal of Statistical Physics 157, 234--281 (2014).
- **DOI:** [10.1007/s10955-014-1087-7](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10955-014-1087-7).
- **Manuscript:** [author-hosted full text](https://web.math.princeton.edu/~rvan/dobrushin130819.pdf).
- **Local payload:** none stored; linked manuscript consulted September 5, 2026.

The opening paragraph paraphrases the abstract. Corollary 2.6 gives the single-site Wasserstein comparison with the response series \(\sum_{n\ge0}C^n\); Corollary 2.8 lists sufficient alternatives for convergence of the comparison remainder.
