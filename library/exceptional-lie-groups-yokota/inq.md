# Exceptional Lie Groups

Yokota constructs the simply connected compact exceptional Lie groups explicitly, classifies their involutive automorphisms, and determines the corresponding fixed-point subgroups. He also describes the noncompact exceptional groups and maximal subgroups of maximal rank.

- Author: Ichiro Yokota
- Manuscript: [arXiv:0902.0431v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/0902.0431v1), submitted 2009-02-03
- Later book edition: *Lecture Notes in Mathematics* **2369**, Springer (2025)
- Book DOI: [10.1007/978-3-031-88911-0](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-88911-0)
