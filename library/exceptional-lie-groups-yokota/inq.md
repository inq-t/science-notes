# Exceptional Lie Groups

Yokota gives concrete constructions of the simply connected compact exceptional Lie groups, determines fixed-point subgroups of explicit automorphisms, and identifies maximal-rank subgroups. For the Albert algebra, Section 2.12 constructs an order-three automorphism whose fixed algebra is \(\mathfrak h_3(\mathbb C)\) and whose centralizer in \(F_4\) is \((SU(3)\times SU(3))/\mathbb Z_3\).

## Source

Ichiro Yokota, *Exceptional Lie Groups*, arXiv:0902.0431, 2009; later published as Lecture Notes in Mathematics 2369, Springer, 2025.
