# Notes on Black-Hole Evaporation

W. G. Unruh studies black-hole evaporation and accelerated particle detectors. Among the paper's results, a uniformly accelerated detector in flat-spacetime vacuum registers particles, providing the detector-based relation between acceleration and a thermal response and clarifying its comparison with observations near a black-hole horizon.

## Metadata

- **Author:** W. G. Unruh.
- **Published:** *Physical Review D* **14**, 870--892 (1976).
- **DOI:** [10.1103/PhysRevD.14.870](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.14.870).
- **Local artifacts:** none.
