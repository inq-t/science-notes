# Uniqueness Problem for Quantum Lattice Systems with Compact Spins

Albeverio, Kondratiev, Minlos and Shchepan'uk study quantum lattice systems with compact spins and continuous nearest-neighbor interactions. They prove uniqueness of the Euclidean Gibbs state, uniformly in finite inverse temperature, when the kinetic mass parameter is sufficiently small.

## Metadata

- **Authors:** S. Albeverio, Yu. G. Kondratiev, R. A. Minlos and G. V. Shchepan'uk.
- **Manuscript date:** May 8, 1999.
- **Source:** [BiBoS author manuscript](https://bibos.math.uni-bielefeld.de/preprints/bibos99-837.pdf), nine pages.
- **Local payload:** none stored; a machine-readable author source has not been located.

The opening paragraph paraphrases the abstract. Theorem 1 states uniqueness. Section 3 develops the temporal-block cluster expansion; Remark 7 describes the extension to ground-state paths.
