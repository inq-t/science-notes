# SU(N) Gauge Theory Couplings on Asymmetric Lattices

Karsch studies the Euclidean-to-Hamiltonian connection on lattices with unequal spatial and temporal spacings. The asymmetry requires distinct space-like and time-like couplings; their weak-coupling dependence on the anisotropy is computed by the background-field method, including the Hamiltonian limit.
