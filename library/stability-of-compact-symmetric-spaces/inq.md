# Stability of Compact Symmetric Spaces

Semmelmann and Weingart investigate the second variation of the Einstein--Hilbert functional on compact symmetric spaces. Their representation-theoretic analysis completes parts of Koiso's stability classification: the quaternionic and Cayley projective planes are stable, whereas the remaining quaternionic Grassmannians are unstable. The treatment of the Cayley plane uses the trace-free Albert algebra as the irreducible twenty-six-dimensional representation of compact \(F_4\).

## Source

Uwe Semmelmann and Gregor Weingart, *The Journal of Geometric Analysis* **32**, article 137 (2022). Published 8 February 2022.
