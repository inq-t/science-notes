# Stability of Compact Symmetric Spaces

Semmelmann and Weingart study stability of the Einstein–Hilbert functional on compact symmetric spaces. They classify irreducible representations with small Casimir eigenvalues, prove stability of the quaternionic and Cayley projective planes, and prove instability of quaternionic Grassmannians other than projective spaces.

- Authors: Uwe Semmelmann and Gregor Weingart
- Journal: *The Journal of Geometric Analysis* **32**, article 137 (2022)
- Published: 2022-02-08
- DOI: [10.1007/s12220-021-00838-3](https://doi.org/10.1007/s12220-021-00838-3)
- Author preprint: [arXiv:2012.07328v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.07328v1), submitted 2020-12-14
