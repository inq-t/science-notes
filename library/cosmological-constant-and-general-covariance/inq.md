# The Cosmological Constant and General Covariance

Henneaux and Teitelboim give a fully covariant formulation of unimodular gravity in which the cosmological constant appears as an integration constant. Their constrained-Hamiltonian analysis retains the two local gravitational degrees of freedom per point and adds one overall degree of freedom associated with the cosmological constant.

## Source

Marc Henneaux and Claudio Teitelboim.
