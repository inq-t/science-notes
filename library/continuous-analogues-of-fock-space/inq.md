# Continuous Analogues of Fock Space

Arveson develops product systems as continuous tensor-product invariants for
semigroups of endomorphisms, including their index, covariance, divisibility,
and relation to continuous analogues of Fock space.

- Author: William Arveson
- Published: *Memoirs of the American Mathematical Society* **80**, no. 409
  (1989), iv--66
- DOI: [10.1090/memo/0409](https://doi.org/10.1090/memo/0409)
- Publisher record: [American Mathematical Society](https://bookstore.ams.org/MEMO/80/409)
- Local artifacts: none
