# Stochastic Gravity: Theory and Applications

Hu and Verdaguer review stochastic semiclassical gravity, in which the Einstein--Langevin equation augments the expectation-value source of semiclassical gravity with a stochastic source governed by the stress-tensor noise kernel. They develop axiomatic and influence-functional formulations and applications to metric fluctuations, structure formation, and evaporating black holes.
