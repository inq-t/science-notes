# Tensorizing Maximal Correlations

Remi Peyre develops tensorization bounds for Hilbertian maximal correlation under partial independence. Conditional pairwise correlation bounds yield bounds between two collections of variables; applications include decorrelation independent of collection size and shape, spatial central limit theorems, and spectral gaps for Glauber dynamics.

- Author: Remi Peyre
- Publication: arXiv monograph (2010)
- Author manuscript: [arXiv:1004.1602v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1004.1602v2), revised 2010-12-30
