# Tensorizing Maximal Correlations

Remi Peyre develops tensorization bounds for Hilbertian maximal correlation. Conditional pairwise coefficients that remain controlled after arbitrary pinnings are assembled into an operator matrix whose norm bounds the maximal correlation between two arbitrary bunches of variables; applications include exponential bunch-to-bunch decorrelation under complete analyticity.

- Author: Remi Peyre
- Published: arXiv manuscript (2010)
- arXiv and source: [1004.1602](https://arxiv.org/abs/1004.1602)
- Local artifacts: none
