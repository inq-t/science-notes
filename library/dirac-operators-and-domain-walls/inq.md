# Dirac Operators and Domain Walls

Jianfeng Lu, Alexander B. Watson, and Michael I. Weinstein analyze one-dimensional Dirac operators with spatially varying mass. A single kink between nonzero masses of opposite sign has a simple exponentially localized zero mode inside the continuous-spectrum gap; multiple well-separated walls produce exponentially small eigenvalue splittings, with an exact zero mode retained for an odd number of walls.

- Authors: Jianfeng Lu, Alexander B. Watson, Michael I. Weinstein
- Submitted: 2018-08-03
- Published online: 2020-03-12
- Journal: *SIAM Journal on Mathematical Analysis* **52** (2), 1115--1145
- arXiv: [1808.01378](https://arxiv.org/abs/1808.01378)
