# Shorts of Operators and Some Extremal Problems

Pekarev develops shorting for nonnegative bounded operators, gives its projection representation, and proves the variational formula expressing the shorted quadratic form as the infimum of the whole form over compatible hidden extensions. The paper also relates shorting to parallel addition, parallel subtraction, and extremal operator problems.

- Author: E. L. Pekarev
- Published: *Acta Scientiarum Mathematicarum (Szeged)* **56** (1992), 147--163
- Primary PDF: [University of Szeged repository](https://acta.bibl.u-szeged.hu/15339/1/math_056_fasc_001_002_147-163.pdf)
- Local artifacts: none
