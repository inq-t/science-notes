# An Operational Approach to Quantum Probability

Davies and Lewis define an instrument, generalizing observables and operations, to formalize repeated measurements of continuous observables in statistical systems. Their framework develops joint and conditional probabilities without the commutativity conditions of an observable-only treatment and shows how abandoning repeatability assumptions yields a more flexible measurement theory.
