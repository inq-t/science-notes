# A Note on Insufficiency and the Preservation of Fisher Information

David Pollard uses differentiability in quadratic mean to analyze how a statistic transports a statistical experiment. In particular, the pushed-forward experiment remains differentiable in quadratic mean and its score is the conditional expectation of the original score given the statistic; equality of Fisher information need not imply classical sufficiency without further conditions.
