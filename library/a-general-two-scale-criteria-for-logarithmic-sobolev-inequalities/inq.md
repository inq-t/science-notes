# A General Two-Scale Criteria for Logarithmic Sobolev Inequalities

Lelievre extends two-scale logarithmic-Sobolev transfer to nonlinear coarse variables. Under a smooth-submersion condition, uniform logarithmic-Sobolev bounds for conditional level-set measures and the coarse marginal, and quantitative control of the local mean force, the original measure satisfies a logarithmic-Sobolev inequality with an explicit lower bound.
