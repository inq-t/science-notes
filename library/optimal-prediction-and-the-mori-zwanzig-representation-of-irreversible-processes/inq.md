# Optimal Prediction and the Mori--Zwanzig Representation of Irreversible Processes

Chorin, Hald and Kupferman relate prediction of underresolved dynamics to projection methods from nonequilibrium statistical mechanics. Conditional expectation separates resolved evolution from unresolved forcing and a history-dependent memory term, providing a route beyond a first-order autonomous approximation.

## Metadata

- **Authors:** Alexandre J. Chorin, Ole H. Hald and Raz Kupferman.
- **Publication:** PNAS 97(7), 2968--2973, March 28, 2000.
- **Full text:** [PubMed Central article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC16175/).
- **Manuscript:** [author-hosted PDF](https://math.huji.ac.il/~razk/Publications/PDF/CHK00.pdf).
- **Local payload:** none stored; author manuscript consulted September 5, 2026.

The opening paragraph paraphrases the article's scope. Section 2, equations 2.5--2.7, develops orthogonal dynamics and the Dyson identity leading to the generalized memory equation.
