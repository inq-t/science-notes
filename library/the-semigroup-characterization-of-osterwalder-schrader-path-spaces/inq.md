# The Semigroup Characterization of Osterwalder--Schrader Path Spaces and the Construction of Euclidean Fields

Abel Klein gives axioms for relativistic quantum fields whose Schwinger functions are expectation values of Euclidean fields. The main ingredient is a characterization of Osterwalder--Schrader path spaces by their associated semigroup structure.

- Author: Abel Klein
- Published: *Journal of Functional Analysis* **27** (3) (1978), 277--291
- DOI: [10.1016/0022-1236(78)90009-5](https://doi.org/10.1016/0022-1236(78)90009-5)
- Publisher record: [ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/0022123678900095)
