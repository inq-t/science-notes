# Block Factorization of the Relative Entropy via Spatial Mixing

Pietro Caputo and Daniel Parisi prove that strong spatial mixing for a class of lattice spin systems implies a family of volume- and boundary-uniform block factorizations of relative entropy with arbitrary nonnegative block weights. The inequalities extend classical Shearer-type relations and imply modified logarithmic-Sobolev bounds for weighted heat-bath block dynamics.

- Authors: Pietro Caputo, Daniel Parisi
- Published: *Communications in Mathematical Physics* **388** (2021), 793--818
- DOI: [10.1007/s00220-021-04237-1](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00220-021-04237-1)
- arXiv and source: [2004.10574](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.10574)
