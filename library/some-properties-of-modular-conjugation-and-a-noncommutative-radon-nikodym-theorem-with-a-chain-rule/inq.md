# Some Properties of Modular Conjugation Operator of von Neumann Algebras and a Non-Commutative Radon--Nikodym Theorem with a Chain Rule

Araki constructs the canonical positive-cone representative of a normal positive functional in a standard representation and a noncommutative Radon--Nikodym amplitude satisfying a chain rule. In the commutative case this amplitude is exactly the positive square root of the ordinary measure-theoretic Radon--Nikodym derivative.
