# Quadratic Estimates and Functional Calculi of Perturbed Dirac Operators

Axelsson, Keith, and McIntosh prove quadratic estimates and bounded functional calculus for complex perturbations of Dirac-type operators. Their first-order framework recovers several results in the Calderon program, including the Kato square-root theorem and Cauchy-operator bounds on Lipschitz sets.

## Metadata

- **Authors:** Andreas Axelsson, Stephen Keith, and Alan McIntosh.
- **Published:** 2006.
- **Journal:** *Inventiones Mathematicae* **163** (3), 455--497.
