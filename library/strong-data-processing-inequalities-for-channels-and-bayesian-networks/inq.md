# Strong Data-Processing Inequalities for Channels and Bayesian Networks

Yury Polyanskiy and Yihong Wu survey contraction coefficients for individual channels and derive end-to-end strong data-processing inequalities for Bayesian networks. Their comparisons include fixed-input chi-squared, relative-entropy, maximal-correlation, total-variation, and nonlinear coefficients, with general-alphabet extensions and tensorization or percolation interpretations.

- Authors: Yury Polyanskiy, Yihong Wu
- First submitted: 2015-08-25
- Revised: 2016-07-28
- Published in: *Convexity and Concentration*, Springer (2017), 211--249
- arXiv and source: [1508.06025v4](https://arxiv.org/abs/1508.06025v4)
- Local artifacts: none
