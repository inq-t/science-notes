# Conditional Expectations in von Neumann Algebras and a Theorem of Takesaki

Luigi Accardi and Carlo Cecchini construct a state-dependent generalized conditional expectation for inclusions of von Neumann algebras. In standard form the construction is governed by the contraction induced by the inclusion on the cyclic state vectors, and it reduces to an ordinary state-preserving conditional expectation under the corresponding modular-invariance condition.

- Authors: Luigi Accardi, Carlo Cecchini
- Published: 1982
- Journal: *Journal of Functional Analysis* **45** (2), 245--273
- DOI: [10.1016/0022-1236(82)90022-2](https://doi.org/10.1016/0022-1236(82)90022-2)
