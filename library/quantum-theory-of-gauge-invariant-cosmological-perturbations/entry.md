# Quantum Theory of Gauge-Invariant Cosmological Perturbations

Metric perturbations of longitudinal type in an isotropic universe filled with a scalar field are considered. Their action is expressed through a gauge-invariant variable that completely characterizes the perturbations, a consistent quantum theory is constructed, and the spectrum of inhomogeneities in inflationary models is calculated.
