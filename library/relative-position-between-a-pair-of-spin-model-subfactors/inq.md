# Relative Position Between a Pair of Spin Model Subfactors

Keshab Chandra Bakshi and Satyajit Guin compute relative-position invariants for pairs of spin-model subfactors of the hyperfinite type \(II_1\) factor. In the \(2\times2\) family they prove that the two index-two subfactors have an index-four factorial intersection and that their Sano--Watatani angle is \(\{\arccos|\cos(\alpha-\beta)|\}\); they also identify the common inclusion with a diagonal index-four model and describe the intermediates by relative unitary conjugation.

- Authors: Keshab Chandra Bakshi, Satyajit Guin
- Published: 2025
- Journal: *Journal of the Australian Mathematical Society* **119**, 1--38
- DOI: [10.1017/S1446788725000035](https://doi.org/10.1017/S1446788725000035)
- Detailed preprint used here: [arXiv:2212.07197v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.07197v3)
