# Clifford Modules

Michael Atiyah, Raoul Bott, and Arnold Shapiro construct real \(K\)-theory and Thom classes from graded Clifford modules and Clifford multiplication, providing the module-theoretic form of Bott periodicity.

- Authors: Michael Atiyah, Raoul Bott, Arnold Shapiro
- Journal: *Topology* **3**, Supplement 1, 3--38
- Published: 1964
- DOI: [10.1016/0040-9383(64)90003-5](https://doi.org/10.1016/0040-9383(64)90003-5)
- Primary scan: [PDF](https://www.maths.ed.ac.uk/~v1ranick/papers/abs.pdf)
