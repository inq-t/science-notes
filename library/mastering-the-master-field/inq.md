# Mastering the Master Field

Gopakumar and Gross apply noncommutative probability to the large-N limit of matrix models. They construct operator master fields in several examples, including two-dimensional QCD, and develop a Hilbert-space description using creation and annihilation operators for coupled matrix variables.

Rajesh Gopakumar and David J. Gross, *Nuclear Physics B* **451** (1995), 379–415.
