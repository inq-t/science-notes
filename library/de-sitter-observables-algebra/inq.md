# An Algebra of Observables for de Sitter Space

Chandrasekaran, Longo, Penington, and Witten construct an observer-dressed algebra of observables for a de Sitter static patch and identify it as a type-$\mathrm{II}_1$ von Neumann algebra. The finite algebra admits a maximum-entropy state associated with empty de Sitter space, while semiclassical state entropies reproduce generalized entropy up to a state-independent additive constant.
