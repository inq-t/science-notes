# Baryonic Features in the Matter Transfer Function

Eisenstein and Hu derive physically motivated scaling relations and fitting formulae for baryonic effects in the linear matter transfer function, separating acoustic oscillations, Compton drag, velocity overshoot, baryon infall, adiabatic and Silk damping, and cold-dark-matter growth suppression. Their treatment locates the baryonic feature through the accumulated sound horizon and provides an approximate transfer description rather than an exact microscopic derivation.
