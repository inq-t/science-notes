# Undecidability of the Spectral Gap

Toby Cubitt, David Perez-Garcia, and Michael Wolf construct a computably parameterized family of translationally invariant nearest-neighbor quantum spin Hamiltonians on a two-dimensional square lattice, with fixed finite local dimension, for which deciding whether the thermodynamic-limit spectrum is gapped or gapless is algorithmically undecidable. The result holds under a strong promise: in the gapped case the gap has a uniform positive lower bound, whereas in the gapless case low spectral values become dense or continuous above the ground state. The construction encodes a universal Turing-machine halting problem into the many-body spectrum.
