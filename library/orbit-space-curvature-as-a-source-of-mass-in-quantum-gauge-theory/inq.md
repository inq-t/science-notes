# Orbit Space Curvature as a Source of Mass in Quantum Gauge Theory

Vincent Moncrief, Antonella Marini, and Rachel Maitra sharpen Singer's orbit-space proposal by including the Yang--Mills potential through the vacuum weight. In a finite-dimensional Schrödinger model, conjugation by a positive ground state produces a weighted Laplacian whose Bakry--Emery tensor is the ordinary configuration-space Ricci tensor plus the Hessian of the negative logarithm of the vacuum. A uniform positive lower bound gives a spectral-gap estimate. Extending the argument to Yang--Mills requires a constructed vacuum measure and a common regularization of the functional Laplacian and the non-trace-class curvature contraction.
