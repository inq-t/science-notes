# Spectral Shorted Operators

Antezana, Corach, and Stojanoff collect the ordinary Anderson--Trapp short's monotonicity, intersection, and iterated-shorting identities before defining a distinct spectral short. They then study that spectral operation and show, among other continuity subtleties, why increasing finite-dimensional retained subspaces need not approximate an infinite-dimensional short in the naive way.

- Authors: Jorge Antezana, Gustavo Corach, and Demetrio Stojanoff
- Submitted: 28 October 2004
- arXiv: [math/0410573](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0410573)
- Journal version: *Integral Equations and Operator Theory* **55** (2006), 169--188
- DOI: [10.1007/s00020-005-1382-4](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00020-005-1382-4)
- Local artifacts: none
