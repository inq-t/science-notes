# The Spectral Action Principle

Ali H. Chamseddine and Alain Connes propose an action associated with a noncommutative space \((\mathcal A,\mathcal H,D)\), consisting of a fermionic term and the spectral trace \(\operatorname{Tr}\chi(D/\Lambda)\). Applied to the spectral data of the Standard Model, the principle yields the Standard Model action coupled to Einstein and Weyl gravity, with high-scale relations among couplings.

- Authors: Ali H. Chamseddine, Alain Connes
- Submitted: 1996-06-03
- Published: *Communications in Mathematical Physics* **186** (1997), 731--750
- DOI: [10.1007/s002200050126](https://doi.org/10.1007/s002200050126)
- arXiv: [hep-th/9606001](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9606001)
