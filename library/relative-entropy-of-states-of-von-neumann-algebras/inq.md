# Relative Entropy of States of von Neumann Algebras

Relative entropy of two states of a von Neumann algebra is defined in terms of the relative modular operator. Strict positivity, lower semicontinuity, convexity, and monotonicity are proved, together with the Wigner-Yanase-Dyson-Lieb concavity theorem for general von Neumann algebras.
