# Reading between the Lines of Four-Dimensional Gauge Theories

Ofer Aharony, Nathan Seiberg, and Yuji Tachikawa classify consistent choices of Wilson--'t Hooft line operators in four-dimensional gauge theories with a fixed Lie algebra. Electric and magnetic center charges carry a bilinear mutual-locality pairing, and a local theory selects a maximal mutually local set; different choices can define distinct theories despite identical local operator correlation functions on \(\mathbb R^4\).

- Authors: Ofer Aharony, Nathan Seiberg, Yuji Tachikawa
- Submitted: 2013-05-01
- Published: *Journal of High Energy Physics* **08** (2013), 115
- DOI: [10.1007/JHEP08(2013)115](https://doi.org/10.1007/JHEP08(2013)115)
- arXiv: [1305.0318](https://arxiv.org/abs/1305.0318)
