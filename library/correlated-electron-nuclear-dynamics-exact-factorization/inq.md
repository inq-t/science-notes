# Correlated Electron–Nuclear Dynamics: Exact Factorization of the Molecular Wavefunction

Ali Abedi, Neepa T. Maitra and E. K. U. Gross derive coupled equations for an exact factorization of a time-dependent electron–nuclear wavefunction. The construction defines scalar and vector potentials, relates the factorization to the Born–Oppenheimer expansion, and studies a laser-driven molecular-ion example.

Published in *The Journal of Chemical Physics* **137**, 22A530 (2012).
