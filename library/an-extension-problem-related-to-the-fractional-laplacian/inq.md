# An Extension Problem Related to the Fractional Laplacian

Caffarelli and Silvestre realize \((-\Delta)^{1/2}\) as the Dirichlet-to-Neumann map for harmonic extension to a half-space and extend this construction to general fractional powers. Their result is a canonical example of a nonlocal boundary operator arising from a local bulk equation.

## Metadata

- **Authors:** Luis Caffarelli and Luis Silvestre.
- **Published:** 2007.
- **Journal:** *Communications in Partial Differential Equations* **32**, 1245--1260.
- **DOI:** [10.1080/03605300600987306](https://doi.org/10.1080/03605300600987306).
