# Theta, Time Reversal, and Temperature

This article studies how theta dependence and center symmetry constrain Yang–Mills vacua, domain walls and thermal phases. For \(SU(N)\), it derives a mixed CP–center anomaly when \(N\) is even and distinguishes odd \(N\) through incompatible counterterm choices at zero and pi theta.

- Authors: Davide Gaiotto, Anton Kapustin, Zohar Komargodski and Nathan Seiberg.
- Publication: JHEP 05 (2017), 091.
- Primary manuscript: [arXiv:1703.00501v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/1703.00501v3), revised 11 September 2017.
- DOI: [10.1007/JHEP05(2017)091](https://doi.org/10.1007/JHEP05%282017%29091).
