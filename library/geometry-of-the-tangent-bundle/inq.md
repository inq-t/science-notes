# On the Geometry of the Tangent Bundle

For a differentiable manifold with a linear connection, Dombrowski uses the induced horizontal-vertical splitting of the tangent bundle of its tangent bundle to define a natural almost-complex structure. He computes its torsion and proves integrability exactly when the connection has vanishing torsion and curvature. The appendix studies the almost-Hermitian structure induced by a Riemannian metric and its Levi-Civita connection and characterizes connections through invariant horizontal distributions.
