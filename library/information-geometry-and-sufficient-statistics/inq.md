# Information Geometry and Sufficient Statistics

Nihat Ay, Jürgen Jost, Hông Vân Lê, and Lorenz Schwachhöfer develop information geometry for parametrized measure models, including infinite sample spaces. They prove invariance of the Fisher quadratic form under sufficient statistics and monotonicity under statistical reductions and Markov morphisms, and extend Chentsov-type uniqueness results beyond finite probability simplices.
