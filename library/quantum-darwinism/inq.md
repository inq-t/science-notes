# Quantum Darwinism

Wojciech Hubert Zurek describes how the environment acquires many records of selected quantum states, giving those states an effectively classical robustness. The article connects redundant environmental imprints to accessible information and effective wave-packet collapse, and discusses a framework for obtaining Born probabilities from quantum amplitudes.

- Authors: Zurek, Wojciech Hubert.
- First submitted: 2009-03-29.
- Archived revision: 0903.5082v1.
- arXiv: [0903.5082v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/0903.5082v1).
- DOI: [10.1038/nphys1202](https://doi.org/10.1038/nphys1202).
