# Graph Zeta Functions and Wilson Loops in a Kazakov–Migdal Model

So Matsuura and Kazutoshi Ohta relate an extended Kazakov–Migdal model on a graph to a matrix-weighted Bartholdi zeta function. They derive edge and vertex determinant representations and evaluate the partition function on cycle graphs at finite \(N\) and on general graphs in the large-\(N\) limit.

- Authors: So Matsuura and Kazutoshi Ohta.
- Publication: *Progress of Theoretical and Experimental Physics* **2022** (12), 123B03.
- Published: 9 November 2022.
- DOI: [10.1093/ptep/ptac146](https://doi.org/10.1093/ptep/ptac146).
- Primary text: [publisher HTML](https://academic.oup.com/ptep/article/2022/12/123B03/6815789).
