# Solitons with Fermion Number One-Half

R. Jackiw and C. Rebbi study fermions in soliton--monopole backgrounds and show that a nondegenerate isolated zero-energy solution of the Dirac equation makes the soliton sector a degenerate doublet with fermion number \(\pm1/2\). They exhibit such zero modes for Yang--Mills monopoles and dyons, establishing an early physical instance in which global background structure protects a localized zero mode.

- Authors: R. Jackiw and C. Rebbi
- Received: 1975-12-23
- Published: 1976-06-15
- Journal: *Physical Review D* **13**, 3398--3409
- DOI: [10.1103/PhysRevD.13.3398](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.13.3398)
- Publisher page: [American Physical Society](https://journals.aps.org/prd/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevD.13.3398)
