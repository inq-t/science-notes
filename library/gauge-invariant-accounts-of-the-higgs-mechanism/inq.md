# Gauge-Invariant Accounts of the Higgs Mechanism

Ward Struyve explains the classical Abelian Higgs mechanism in gauge-invariant variables and argues that spontaneous breaking of gauge redundancy is not essential to the mass-generating description. The paper distinguishes two notions of gauge and shows how the familiar account can be reformulated without treating gauge-dependent vacuum selection as the physical event.

- Author: Ward Struyve
- Submitted: 2011-02-01
- Published: *Studies in History and Philosophy of Modern Physics* **42** (2011), 226--236
- DOI: [10.1016/j.shpsb.2011.06.003](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.shpsb.2011.06.003)
- arXiv: [1102.0468](https://arxiv.org/abs/1102.0468)
