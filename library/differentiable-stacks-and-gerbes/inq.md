# Differentiable Stacks and Gerbes

Kai Behrend and Ping Xu introduce differentiable stacks through Lie-groupoid presentations and develop principal circle bundles, gerbes, connections, curvings, characteristic classes, and prequantization in that Morita-invariant setting.

- Authors: Kai Behrend, Ping Xu
- Journal: *Journal of Symplectic Geometry* **9** (3), 285--341
- Published: 2011
- arXiv: [math/0605694](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0605694)
- DOI: [10.4310/JSG.2011.v9.n3.a2](https://doi.org/10.4310/JSG.2011.v9.n3.a2)
