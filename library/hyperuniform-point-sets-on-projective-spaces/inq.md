# Hyperuniform Point Sets on Projective Spaces

Brauchart and Grabner extend hyperuniformity to real, complex and quaternionic projective spaces and the octonionic projective plane. They prove implications for uniform distribution and give deterministic and point-process examples, using harmonic analysis on compact doubly homogeneous spaces.

- Authors: Johann S. Brauchart and Peter J. Grabner
- Journal: *Constructive Approximation* **64**, 65–86 (2026)
- Published: 2026-05-29
- DOI and published text: [10.1007/s00365-026-09755-6](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00365-026-09755-6)
- Author preprint: [arXiv:2403.03572v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.03572v1), submitted 2024-03-06
