# Quantum Operations on Conformal Nets

Marcel Bischoff, Simone Del Vecchio, and Luca Giorgetti define quantum operations on a conformal net as compatible, vacuum-preserving, conformally covariant families of unital completely positive maps on its local algebras. They identify fixed-point subnets under such operations and show that the operations fixing a discrete or finite-index conformal subnet carry a compact or finite hypergroup structure, with a corresponding Galois theory for intermediate subnets.
