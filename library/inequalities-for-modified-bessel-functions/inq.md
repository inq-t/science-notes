# Inequalities for Modified Bessel Functions

Näsell proves a sequence of increasingly sharp bounds refining the classical inequality $I_{\nu+1}(x)<I_\nu(x)$ for modified Bessel functions of the first kind. In particular, Proposition 1 states that, for $\nu\geq-1$ and $x>0$,

\[
\left(1+\frac{\nu}{x}\right)I_{\nu+1}(x)<I_\nu(x).
\]
