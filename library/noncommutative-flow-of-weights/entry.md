# The Non-commutative Flow of Weights on a Von Neumann Algebra

Falcone and Takesaki construct a canonical noncommutative flow of weights for a separable factor by relating faithful semifinite normal weights with Connes cocycles. The resulting functor assigns a semifinite von Neumann algebra equipped with a trace-scaling one-parameter automorphism group, supplies a weight-independent construction of the type-III core, and connects this construction with noncommutative $L^p$ spaces and extended modular automorphisms.
