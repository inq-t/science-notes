# Villain Action in Lattice Gauge Theory

Chevyrev and Garban obtain the Villain lattice gauge law as a limit of suitably scaled Wilson and Manton interactions on carpet graphs. Their construction applies to compact connected gauge groups, including non-Abelian examples. For Abelian gauge theory, it extends the Ginibre correlation inequality to Villain interactions.
