# Villain Action in Lattice Gauge Theory

Chevyrev and Garban prove that suitably scaled plaquette interactions on carpet refinements converge to the Villain lattice gauge law for compact connected gauge groups. Their construction includes Wilson and Manton interactions and yields an Abelian correlation inequality as a consequence.

Ilya Chevyrev and Christophe Garban, *Journal of Statistical Physics* **192**, article 38 (2025). Published 10 March 2025.
