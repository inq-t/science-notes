# Quantized Fields and Particle Creation in Expanding Universes. I

Parker canonically quantizes massive scalar fields and massless fields of arbitrary spin on a spacetime with expanding or contracting Euclidean spatial sections. Particle number is adiabatically invariant but not exactly conserved, creation occurs in pairs, and the physical creation and annihilation operators are identified without an S-matrix construction. The paper also relates suppressed creation in radiation- and dust-dominated limits to the corresponding Friedmann expansions.
