# Type III and Spectral Triples

Alain Connes and Henri Moscovici introduce twisted spectral triples in order to include type-III examples such as transverse geometries of codimension-one foliations. They show that the Chern character of a finitely summable twisted spectral triple still lands in ordinary cyclic cohomology, that the local Hochschild character is likewise untwisted, and that pairing with ordinary \(K\)-theory continues to give the index formula.

- Authors: Alain Connes, Henri Moscovici
- Submitted: 2006-09-25
- Revised: 2006-10-24
- Published: 2008, in *Traces in Number Theory, Geometry and Quantum Fields*, Aspects of Mathematics E38, 57--71
- arXiv: [math/0609703](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0609703)
- Author PDF: [alainconnes.org](https://alainconnes.org/wp-content/uploads/typeIIIspectral.pdf)
- Local artifacts: none
