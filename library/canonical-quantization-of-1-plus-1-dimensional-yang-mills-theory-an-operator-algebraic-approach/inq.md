# Canonical Quantization of 1+1-Dimensional Yang–Mills Theory: An Operator-Algebraic Approach

Brothier and Stottmeister construct a canonical quantization of Yang–Mills theory in one space and one time dimension using Hamiltonian lattice gauge theory and inductive limits of operator algebras. Scaling limits of Kogut–Susskind Gibbs states produce spatially localized time-zero von Neumann algebras. The construction includes non-Abelian gauge theory, with its most explicit results in the Abelian case, and relates the resulting dynamics to earlier constructions and multiscale renormalization.

- Authors: Arnaud Brothier; Alexander Stottmeister.
