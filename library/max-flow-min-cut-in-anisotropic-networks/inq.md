# Max-Flow Min-Cut Theorem in an Anisotropic Network

Ryohei Nozawa develops continuous max-flow/min-cut duality for anisotropic capacities, including prescribed distributed sources and boundary flux constraints. Bounded vector fields and bounded-variation potentials provide the primal and dual formulations; cut representations follow from coarea.

Ryohei Nozawa. *Osaka Journal of Mathematics* **27**(4), 805--842 (1990).

[Primary repository record](https://ocu-omu.repo.nii.ac.jp/records/2009227). [Primary full text](https://ocu-omu.repo.nii.ac.jp/record/2009227/files/111F0000002-02704-4.pdf).
