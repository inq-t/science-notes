# The Transition Function of \(G_2\) over \(S^6\)

Ádám Gyenge gives explicit trivializations of the principal \(SU(3)\)-bundle \(G_2\to S^6\) over two affine charts and computes its equatorial transition function, yielding a direct proof that the fibration represents a generator of \(\pi_5(SU(3))\).

- Author: Ádám Gyenge
- Journal: *SIGMA* **15**, 078
- Published: 2019
- arXiv: [1811.03613](https://arxiv.org/abs/1811.03613)
- DOI: [10.3842/SIGMA.2019.078](https://doi.org/10.3842/SIGMA.2019.078)
- Source: [TeX and PDF](https://sigma-journal.com/2019/078/)
