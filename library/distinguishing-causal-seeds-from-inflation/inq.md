# Distinguishing Causal Seeds from Inflation

Hu, Spergel, and White analyze perturbations generated by causal stress seeds and show that their superhorizon behavior generically produces an isocurvature acoustic pattern with shifted compression peaks relative to passive adiabatic inflationary initial conditions. They identify an exceptional compensated-stress case that can mimic part of the inflationary signature, making peak phase, relative height, and coherence concrete tests of an active-source proposal.
