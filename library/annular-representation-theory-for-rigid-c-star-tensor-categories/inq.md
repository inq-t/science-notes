# Annular Representation Theory for Rigid C-Star Tensor Categories

Shamindra Kumar Ghosh and Corey Jones define annular algebras for rigid \(C^*\)-tensor categories, unifying Ocneanu's tube algebra with affine annular categories. The corner indexed by the tensor unit is canonically the fusion algebra, and the Popa--Vaes admissible representations of that fusion algebra are exactly the restrictions of nondegenerate representations of a full annular algebra.
