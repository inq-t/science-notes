# Approximate Tensorization of the Relative Entropy for Noncommuting Conditional Expectations

Ivan Bardet, Angela Capel, and Cambyse Rouze establish approximate tensorization inequalities for relative entropy associated with two intersecting conditional expectations onto arbitrary finite-dimensional von Neumann algebras. Their results generalize strong subadditivity and commuting quasi-factorization, relate Petz and Davies conditional expectations, and estimate constants through correlation clustering in quantum lattice spin systems.

- Authors: Ivan Bardet, Angela Capel, Cambyse Rouze
- Published: *Annales Henri Poincare* (2021)
- DOI: [10.1007/s00023-021-01088-3](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00023-021-01088-3)
- arXiv and source: [2001.07981v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.07981v3)
