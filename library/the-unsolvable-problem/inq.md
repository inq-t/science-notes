# The Unsolvable Problem

Toby Cubitt, David Perez-Garcia, and Michael Wolf give a nontechnical account of their spectral-gap undecidability theorem and its construction from Turing machines, history states, and aperiodic tilings. They emphasize that the words “in general” are essential: the theorem rules out one algorithm deciding the gap for every Hamiltonian in the constructed class, while particular models can still be proved gapped or gapless. They also distinguish the mathematical thermodynamic limit from any finite laboratory sample.
