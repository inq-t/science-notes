# Entropic Order Parameters for the Phases of QFT

Horacio Casini, Marina Huerta, Javier M. Magan, and Diego Pontello formulate entropic order parameters from failures of additivity and Haag duality in topologically nontrivial regions. Nonlocally generated operators encode generalized symmetries; dual conditional expectations obey entropic certainty relations whose constant is the logarithm of the algebraic index. In four spacetime dimensions a ring and its causal complement are both rings, so pure gauge theory admits both Wilson and 't Hooft nonlocal classes in the same regional comparison.

- Authors: Horacio Casini, Marina Huerta, Javier M. Magan, Diego Pontello
- Published: 2021
- Journal: *Journal of High Energy Physics* **04** (2021), 277
- DOI: [10.1007/JHEP04(2021)277](https://doi.org/10.1007/JHEP04(2021)277)
- arXiv: [2008.11748](https://arxiv.org/abs/2008.11748)
