# Derivations and KMS-Symmetric Quantum Markov Semigroups

Matthijs Vernooij and Melchior Wirth show that the \(L^2\)-generator of a KMS-symmetric quantum Markov semigroup can be written as \(\delta^*\delta\) for a closable derivation \(\delta\) taking values in a Hilbert bimodule. Their construction extends square-root factorization beyond the tracial setting.

- Authors: Matthijs Vernooij, Melchior Wirth
- Submitted: 2023-03-28
- Revised: 2023-06-23
- Published online: 2023-07-20
- Journal: *Communications in Mathematical Physics* **403** (1), 381--416 (2023)
- DOI: [10.1007/s00220-023-04795-6](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00220-023-04795-6)
