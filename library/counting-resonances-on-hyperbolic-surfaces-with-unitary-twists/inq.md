# Counting Resonances on Hyperbolic Surfaces with Unitary Twists

Moritz Doll, Ksenia Fedosova, and Anke Pohl construct the Laplace operator associated to a hyperbolic surface and a finite-dimensional unitary representation of its fundamental group, including the cusp model and Fourier decomposition needed to distinguish invariant from non-invariant cusp channels. They prove meromorphic continuation of its resolvent and an optimal upper bound for the resonance-counting function.

- Authors: Moritz Doll, Ksenia Fedosova, Anke Pohl
- Preprint: [arXiv:2109.12923](https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.12923)
- Source: [TeX and ancillary links](https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.12923)
