# Black Holes and Entropy

Bekenstein develops the thermodynamic analogy between horizon area and entropy, interprets black-hole entropy as information about the interior unavailable to an exterior observer, and argues from consistency and dimensional considerations that the entropy is proportional to horizon area in Planck units. He formulates the generalized second law in which ordinary exterior entropy plus black-hole entropy does not decrease.
