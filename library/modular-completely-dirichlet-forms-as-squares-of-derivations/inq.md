# Modular Completely Dirichlet Forms as Squares of Derivations

Melchior Wirth proves that a closable symmetric derivation from a Tomita
algebra into a normal Tomita bimodule has a closed quadratic form that is
modular and completely Dirichlet.  Its semigroup is the GNS implementation of
a GNS-symmetric semigroup of normal contractive completely positive maps on
the generated von Neumann algebra; in the unital case the form is quantum
Dirichlet.

- **Author:** Melchior Wirth.
- **Published:** 14 May 2024.
- **Journal:** *International Mathematics Research Notices* **2024** (14),
  10597--10614.
- **DOI:** [10.1093/imrn/rnae092](https://doi.org/10.1093/imrn/rnae092).
- **arXiv:** [2307.04502](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.04502).
- **Main result:** Theorem 4.4.
- **Standard-form tools:** Section 1.2 and the projection identities in the
  proof of Lemma 4.3.
- **Local artifacts:** none.
