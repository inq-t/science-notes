# Subproduct Systems

Shalit and Solel introduce subproduct systems of $W^*$-correspondences and
relate them to semigroups of normal completely positive maps. For the GNS
construction, the composite fiber embeds isometrically into the tensor product
of the component fibers; self-duality supplies the adjoint coisometries that
form the subproduct multiplication.
