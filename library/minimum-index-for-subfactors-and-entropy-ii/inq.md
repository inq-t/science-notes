# Minimum Index for Subfactors and Entropy II

Fumio Hiai studies inclusion entropy for normal faithful conditional expectations between sigma-finite von Neumann algebras. For an infinite subfactor his ensemble/decomposition entropy agrees with the corresponding index quantity, is bounded by the logarithm of the minimum index, and reaches the log index exactly for the minimal expectation; the paper also relates nonminimal expectations to Connes cocycles on the relative commutant.
