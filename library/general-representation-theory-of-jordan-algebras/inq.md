# General Representation Theory of Jordan Algebras

Jacobson develops representations and bimodules of Jordan algebras, including the regular multiplication representation. This notion of representation is broader than embedding a Jordan algebra into an associative algebra with the symmetrized product and therefore remains available for exceptional Jordan algebras.

## Source

Nathan Jacobson, *Transactions of the American Mathematical Society* **70** (1951), 509--530.
