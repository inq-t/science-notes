# General Representation Theory of Jordan Algebras

Jacobson develops a representation theory of Jordan algebras based on the identities satisfied by regular multiplication operators. He relates representations to Jordan modules, constructs universal associative algebras, and distinguishes these representations from special representations preserving the symmetrized associative product.

- Author: Nathan Jacobson
- Journal: *Transactions of the American Mathematical Society* **70** (3), 509–530 (1951)
- DOI: [10.1090/S0002-9947-1951-0041118-9](https://doi.org/10.1090/S0002-9947-1951-0041118-9)
- Public source copy: [University of California, Irvine](https://webapps.math.uci.edu/~brusso/jacobson1951.pdf)
