# Dualizability and Index of Subfactors

Arthur Bartels, Christopher L. Douglas, and Andre Henriques formulate finite index through dualizable von Neumann bimodules, identify the subfactor index with the square of statistical dimension, prove multiplicativity of dimension under Connes fusion, and compare the categorical definition with Pimsner--Popa inequalities. Their treatment applies to Type I, II, and III von Neumann algebras and retains matrix-valued dimensions for finite-dimensional centers.

- Authors: Arthur Bartels, Christopher L. Douglas, Andre Henriques
- Published: 2014
- Journal: *Quantum Topology* **5** (3), 289--345
- DOI: [10.4171/QT/53](https://doi.org/10.4171/QT/53)
- Open article: [EMS Press](https://ems.press/journals/qt/articles/12498)
