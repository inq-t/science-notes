# Covariance Structure of the Gibbs Sampler with Applications to the Comparisons of Estimators and Augmentation Schemes

Jun S. Liu, Wing Hung Wong, and Augustine Kong study the covariance
structure of Gibbs and data-augmentation chains through forward and backward
conditional-expectation operators. They identify the marginal
data-augmentation convergence rate with the square of maximal correlation,
derive autocovariance comparisons for Rao--Blackwellized estimators, and
show how collapsing or grouping components changes the relevant maximal
correlation.

**Authors:** Jun S. Liu, Wing Hung Wong, and Augustine Kong  
**Journal:** *Biometrika* **81** (1994), 27--40  
**DOI:** [10.1093/biomet/81.1.27](https://doi.org/10.1093/biomet/81.1.27)  
**Article:** [Oxford Academic](https://academic.oup.com/biomet/article-abstract/81/1/27/252043)  
**Accessible manuscript:** [Duke University course archive](https://www2.stat.duke.edu/~scs/Courses/Stat376/Papers/ConvergeRates/GibbsSampling/LiuWongKongBiometrika1994.pdf)
