# Representation Theory for Subfactors, Lambda-Lattices and C-Star Tensor Categories

Sorin Popa and Stefaan Vaes construct the universal \(C^*\)-algebra of a rigid \(C^*\)-tensor category from its admissible fusion-algebra representations and formulate categorical approximation and rigidity properties. In particular, categorical property \((T)\) is equivalent to the existence of a unique Kazhdan projection selecting the trivial representation; for a self-conjugate tensor generator, the trivial dimension character is an isolated endpoint of the generator's universal admissible spectrum. For a finite-index extremal subfactor, these categorical properties agree with those of its standard invariant.
