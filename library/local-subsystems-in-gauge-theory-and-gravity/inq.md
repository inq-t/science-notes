# Local Subsystems in Gauge Theory and Gravity

Donnelly and Freidel construct regional gauge-theory phase spaces by adjoining boundary frames and surface degrees of freedom. Regional states carry boundary gauge representations, and gluing complementary regions imposes the matching constraint by taking singlets under the diagonal boundary action rather than by making each region separately gauge invariant.
