# Local Subsystems in Gauge Theory and Gravity

Donnelly and Freidel introduce boundary degrees of freedom to construct gauge-invariant phase spaces for spatial regions. For Yang–Mills theory these are boundary gauge frames, with transformations generated by the normal electric field; for gravity they include the boundary location and normal frame. The resulting surface symmetries organize gluing and the discussion of entanglement in gauge theory and gravity.
