# Gauge Fixing, the Transfer Matrix, and Confinement on a Lattice

Creutz relates Wilson's Euclidean lattice formulation to the Kogut--Susskind Hamiltonian by transfer-matrix methods. After gauge fixing without changing gauge-singlet Green functions, the Kogut--Susskind Hamiltonian is extracted in the limit in which the timelike lattice spacing tends to zero.
