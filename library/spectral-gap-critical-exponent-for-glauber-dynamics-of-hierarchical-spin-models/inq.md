# Spectral Gap Critical Exponent for Glauber Dynamics of Hierarchical Spin Models

Roland Bauerschmidt and Thierry Bodineau develop a renormalization-group recursion for Brascamp--Lieb and spectral-gap inequalities along a hierarchical covariance decomposition. Their bounds express the fine-scale inverse-gap form through renormalized Hessian deficits and covariance slices; the critical examples also show that multiscale decomposition alone need not create a uniform floor, because the gap can retain free-field finite-size scaling.
