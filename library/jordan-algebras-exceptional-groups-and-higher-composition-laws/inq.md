# Jordan Algebras, Exceptional Groups, and Higher Composition Laws

Sergei Krutelevich develops an integral version of the Freudenthal construction relating cubic Jordan algebras to exceptional algebraic groups, studies the resulting orbit spaces, and connects them with higher composition laws. The construction supplies the symplectic module and quartic form used in the exceptional Freudenthal carrier.
