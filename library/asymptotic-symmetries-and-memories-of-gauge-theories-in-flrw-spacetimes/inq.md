# Asymptotic Symmetries and Memories of Gauge Theories in FLRW Spacetimes

Martin Enriquez-Rojo and Tobias Schroeder study the asymptotic structure of Abelian and non-Abelian gauge theories in decelerating spatially flat FLRW universes, derive the associated memory effects, and formulate the conformal-coordinate and covariant-phase-space structures used to compare FLRW gauge theory with its flat-spacetime counterpart.
