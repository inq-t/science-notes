# Operator-Algebraic Renormalization and Wavelets

This paper constructs the continuum free field from Hamiltonian lattice systems through an operator-algebraic renormalization scheme based on wavelets. A scaling equation identifies lattice observables with continuum fields smeared by compactly supported wavelets. Lieb–Robinson estimates for the harmonic lattice systems establish causality. The construction also connects the renormalization scheme with multiscale entanglement renormalization.
