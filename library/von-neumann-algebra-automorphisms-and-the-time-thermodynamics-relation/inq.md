# Von Neumann Algebra Automorphisms and the Time--Thermodynamics Relation

Alain Connes and Carlo Rovelli formulate the thermal-time hypothesis for generally covariant quantum theories: a faithful thermodynamic state on an observable von Neumann algebra determines a modular one-parameter automorphism group, while Connes' cocycle Radon--Nikodym theorem supplies a state-independent flow in the outer automorphism group.

- Authors: Alain Connes, Carlo Rovelli
- Published: *Classical and Quantum Gravity* **11** (1994), 2899--2918
- DOI: [10.1088/0264-9381/11/12/007](https://doi.org/10.1088/0264-9381/11/12/007)
- arXiv: [gr-qc/9406019](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9406019)
