# Center-Stabilized Yang-Mills Theory: Confinement and Large N Volume Independence

Mithat Unsal and Laurence G. Yaffe construct a double-trace-deformed Yang--Mills theory on \(\mathbb R^3\times S^1\) that preserves center symmetry at small circle size. Center-symmetric holonomy makes the off-diagonal gauge modes heavy, while monopole-instantons generate a potential and mass spectrum for the otherwise perturbatively massless dual photons; the work also delineates the small-circle semiclassical regime and its large-\(N\) volume-independence claims.

- Authors: Mithat Unsal, Laurence G. Yaffe
- Published: *Physical Review D* **78** (2008), 065035
- DOI: [10.1103/PhysRevD.78.065035](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.78.065035)
- Preprint and source: [arXiv:0803.0344](https://arxiv.org/abs/0803.0344)
