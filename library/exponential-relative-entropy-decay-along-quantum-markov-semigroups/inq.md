# Exponential Relative Entropy Decay Along Quantum Markov Semigroups

Wirth proves that exponential decay of Araki relative entropy along a quantum Markov semigroup is equivalent to a modified logarithmic Sobolev inequality in a general von Neumann-algebra setting. For GNS-symmetric semigroups satisfying the paper's regularity assumptions, an intertwining criterion yields complete Fisher-information decay and complete modified logarithmic Sobolev inequalities.
