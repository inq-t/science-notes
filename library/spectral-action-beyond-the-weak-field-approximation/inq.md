# Spectral Action Beyond the Weak-Field Approximation

Bruno Iochum, Cyril Levy, and Dmitri Vassilevich compute the gauge perturbation of a noncompact commutative spectral action through second order in field strength, retaining its momentum dependence. They derive the large-momentum behavior of the curvature form factor in even dimensions.

- Authors: B. Iochum, C. Levy, D. Vassilevich
- Submitted: 2011-08-18; version 2: 2012-06-05
