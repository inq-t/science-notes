# The KMS and GNS Spectral Gap of Quantum Markov Semigroups

Melchior Wirth proves that a quantum Markov semigroup with a faithful normal invariant state on an arbitrary von Neumann algebra has KMS exponential-decay rate at least its GNS rate. More generally, the same lower bound holds for the family of state-induced inner products associated with normalized operator-monotone functions, including the observable BKM inner product; the result also admits a fixed-point-algebra formulation.
