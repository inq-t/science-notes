# The Classically Perfect Fixed Point Action for SU(3) Gauge Theory

Thomas DeGrand, Anna Hasenfratz, Peter Hasenfratz, and Ferenc Niedermayer construct fixed-point actions and operators for pure lattice \(SU(3)\) gauge theory. They study scale-invariant instantons, the quadratic relativistic spectrum, and a tree-level quark--antiquark potential without the usual lattice artifacts, and give a formal renormalization-group argument for one-loop improvement.
