# On the Mathematical Foundations of Theoretical Statistics

Fisher organizes theoretical statistics around data reduction through parametric models and the distinct problems of specification, estimation, and distribution. He develops consistency, efficiency, and sufficiency as criteria for estimators, advances likelihood-based estimation, compares it with the method of moments, and studies continuous and discrete sampling problems.
