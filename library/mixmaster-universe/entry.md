# Mixmaster Universe

The generic nonrotating homogeneous model for a closed Bianchi IX space has a highly complex but describable singularity. Misner argues that particle horizons occur only in exceptional cases, suggesting a possible mechanism by which large-scale homogeneity could have arisen in the early universe.
