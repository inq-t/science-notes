# Scaling Algebras and Renormalization Group in Algebraic Quantum Field Theory

Buchholz and Verch associate a scaling algebra to a net of local observables in Minkowski space so that renormalization-group transformations act canonically. The construction gives an intrinsic framework for short-distance analysis and possibly nonunique scaling limits, classifies limits by classical or quantum behavior, and extends in principle to arbitrary spacetimes.
