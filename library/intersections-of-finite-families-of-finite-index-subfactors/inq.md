# Intersections of Finite Families of Finite Index Subfactors

Vaughan F. R. Jones and Feng Xu prove that, for a finite family of finite-index subalgebras of a finite von Neumann algebra with finite-dimensional center, finite index of their intersection is equivalent to finite-dimensionality of the algebra generated by the corresponding conditional-expectation projections. They also establish a general-factor extension under a common invariant faithful state and a meet condition for the Jones projections.

- Authors: Vaughan F. R. Jones, Feng Xu
- Published: 2004
- Journal: *International Journal of Mathematics* **15** (7), 717--733
- DOI: [10.1142/S0129167X04002521](https://doi.org/10.1142/S0129167X04002521)
- Preprint: [arXiv:math/0406331v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0406331v2)
