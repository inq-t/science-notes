# Trace dynamics, octonions, and unification: An $E_8 \times E_8$ theory of unification

Tejinder P. Singh gives a brief overview of an ongoing octonionic unification programme. The article traces its motivation to quantum foundations and outlines seven ingredients connecting trace dynamics, exceptional algebra, and the proposed emergence of familiar quantum and spacetime descriptions.
