# The Role of Type III Factors in Quantum Field Theory

Yngvason reviews why Type III factors arise naturally as local observable algebras in relativistic quantum field theory and infinite quantum systems, and contrasts their localization and subsystem properties with Type I quantum mechanics. The review emphasizes that local algebras are generically isomorphic to the hyperfinite Type $\mathrm{III}_1$ factor while theories are distinguished by the net structure of their inclusions.
