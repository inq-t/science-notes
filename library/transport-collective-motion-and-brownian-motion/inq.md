# Transport, Collective Motion, and Brownian Motion

Mori develops a statistical-mechanical theory that treats transport, collective motion, and Brownian motion in a unified way. By projecting dynamics onto a chosen set of macroscopic variables, the formalism derives a generalized Langevin equation with memory and fluctuating forces and relates relaxation functions to frequency-dependent transport coefficients.
