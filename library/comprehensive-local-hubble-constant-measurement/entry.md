# A Comprehensive Measurement of the Local Value of the Hubble Constant with 1 km s−1 Mpc−1 Uncertainty from the Hubble Space Telescope and the SH0ES Team

The SH0ES Team reports Hubble Space Telescope observations of Cepheid variables in 42 Type Ia supernova host galaxies and a comprehensive calibration of the local distance ladder. The analysis reaches approximately one kilometer per second per megaparsec precision on the local Hubble constant and compares that result with the value inferred from the early universe under standard cosmology.
