# Q-Balls

Sidney Coleman proves the existence and absolute-stability criterion for a class of spherically symmetric nontopological solitons carrying a conserved charge associated with an unbroken global continuous internal symmetry. For large charge their energy and volume grow linearly with charge, and the paper also derives elementary properties and low-lying excitations.
