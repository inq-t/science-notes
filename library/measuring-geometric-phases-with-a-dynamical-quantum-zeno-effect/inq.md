# Measuring Geometric Phases with a Dynamical Quantum Zeno Effect in a Bose–Einstein Condensate

H. V. Do, M. Gessner, F. S. Cataliotti and A. Smerzi analyze and measure geometric phases using dynamical quantum Zeno evolution in a rubidium Bose–Einstein condensate. Their treatment separates geometric and dynamical contributions and describes the geometric phase acquired through a sequence of projections along a closed trajectory.

Published in *Physical Review Research* **1**, 033028 (2019).
