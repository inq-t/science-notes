# On Measures of Dependence

Alfred Renyi develops axioms for measures of statistical dependence and studies maximal correlation, obtained by optimizing ordinary correlation over square-integrable transformations of the two variables. The resulting coefficient is zero for independence, one when a nonconstant common measurable factor exists, and admits the conditional-expectation operator formulation used in later tensorization and strong data-processing theory.

- Author: Alfred Renyi
- Published: *Acta Mathematica Academiae Scientiarum Hungaricae* **10** (1959), 441--451
- DOI: [10.1007/BF02024507](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02024507)
