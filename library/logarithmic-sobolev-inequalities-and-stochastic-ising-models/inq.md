# Logarithmic Sobolev Inequalities and Stochastic Ising Models

Holley and Stroock develop logarithmic-Sobolev estimates for stochastic Ising dynamics and comparison methods for perturbing reference measures. The bounded-perturbation principle associated with this work controls a new functional-inequality constant by the oscillation of the logarithmic density relative to a reference measure.

## Metadata

- **Authors:** Richard Holley and Daniel Stroock.
- **Published:** 1987.
- **Journal:** *Journal of Statistical Physics* **46** (5--6), 1159--1194.
