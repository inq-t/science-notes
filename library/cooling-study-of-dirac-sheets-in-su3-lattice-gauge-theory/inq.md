# Cooling Study of Dirac Sheets in SU(3) Lattice Gauge Theory below Tc

Ilgenfritz, Martemyanov and Müller-Preussker study constant magnetic configurations obtained by cooling finite-temperature lattice gauge fields. They compare standard and nonstandard Dirac sheets with analytical stability conditions depending on temporal holonomy, finding stable finite-lattice configurations for suitable Polyakov-loop values.
