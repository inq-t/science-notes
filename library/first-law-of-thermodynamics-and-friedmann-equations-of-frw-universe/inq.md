# First Law of Thermodynamics and Friedmann Equations of Friedmann--Robertson--Walker Universe

Rong-Gen Cai and Sang Pyo Kim apply the first law of thermodynamics at the apparent horizon of an FRW universe, together with quarter-area gravitational entropy, to recover the Friedmann equations for arbitrary spatial curvature. They also extend the comparison to Gauss--Bonnet and Lovelock gravity using the corresponding static-horizon entropy formulas.
