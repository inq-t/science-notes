# On Relative Entropy and Global Index

Feng Xu studies the failure of a relative-entropy duality for chiral conformal nets with nontrivial representations and identifies the defect with relative entropy and the global index of multi-interval subfactors for a broad class of conformal nets. The paper proves corresponding holomorphicity and modular-invariance criteria and supplies the duality formula later used in index-capacity and entropic-certainty results.
