# Expansions for One Quasiparticle States in Spin 1/2 Systems

Datta and Kennedy construct convergent expansions for the ground-state and low-energy wavefunctions of quantum transverse Ising systems. They use these expansions to establish a convergent expansion for the dispersion relation of a single quasiparticle.

Authors: Nilanjana Datta and Tom Kennedy.

Source: [arXiv:cond-mat/0104199v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0104199v2),
revised January 17, 2003.
[Author TeX source](https://arxiv.org/src/cond-mat/0104199v2).
Published in *Journal of Statistical Physics* **108** (2002), 373–399.

The opening paragraph paraphrases the author abstract. No manuscript
payload is stored locally.
