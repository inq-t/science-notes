# Classical Higgs Fields

G. Sardanashvily formulates classical gauge theory on a principal bundle whose structure group \(G\) is reduced to a closed subgroup \(H\). Global sections of the quotient bundle \(P/H\to X\) are treated as Higgs fields, and matter fields with exact symmetry group \(H\) are organized through a composite-bundle construction compatible with the original \(G\)-gauge theory.

- Author: G. Sardanashvily
- Published: *Theoretical and Mathematical Physics* **181** (2014), 1599--1611
- DOI: [10.1007/s11232-014-0238-y](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11232-014-0238-y)
- arXiv: [1602.03818](https://arxiv.org/abs/1602.03818) (uploaded 2016)
