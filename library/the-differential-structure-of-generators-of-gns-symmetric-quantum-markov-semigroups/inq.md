# The Differential Structure of Generators of GNS-symmetric Quantum Markov Semigroups

Melchior Wirth shows that the generator of a GNS-symmetric quantum Markov semigroup can be represented as the square of a derivation, extending the tracially symmetric result of Cipriani and Sauvageot. In the nontracial setting the derivation obeys a modularly twisted product rule encoded by a Tomita bimodule; under an additional regularity condition, this bimodule can be realized in the \(L^2\)-space of a larger von Neumann algebra constructed through an operator-valued analogue of free Araki–Woods factors.
