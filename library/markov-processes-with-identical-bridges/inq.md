# Markov Processes with Identical Bridges

Fitzsimmons studies time-homogeneous Markov processes with transition densities and identical pinned path laws. Under the stated hypotheses, their generators differ by an eigenfunction transform and an additive scalar. Additional continuity permits identification from one pinned path law.

P. J. Fitzsimmons, 1998.
