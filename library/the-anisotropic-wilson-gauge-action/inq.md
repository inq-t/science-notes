# The Anisotropic Wilson Gauge Action

Klassen nonperturbatively calibrates the renormalized anisotropy \(\xi=a_s/a_t\) of the quenched anisotropic Wilson gauge action against its bare anisotropy \(\xi_0\) and coupling. By comparing finite-volume heavy-quark potentials oriented along spatial and temporal directions, he obtains percent-level determinations and a parameterization for \(1\leq\xi\leq6\) and \(5.5\leq\beta\leq\infty\) that incorporates the one-loop result and supports fixed-anisotropy continuum limits and thermodynamic calculations.
