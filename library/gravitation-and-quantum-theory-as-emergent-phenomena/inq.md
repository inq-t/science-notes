# Gravitation, and quantum theory, as emergent phenomena

Tejinder P. Singh presents a proposed theory preceding both quantum theory and classical spacetime. Its ingredients include matrix-valued trace dynamics, noncommutative spectral geometry, octonions, and an action with exceptional-group symmetry. The article connects the emergence of classical spacetime to a quantum-to-classical transition and proposes algebraic origins for interactions and coupling parameters through symmetry breaking and exceptional Jordan structures.
