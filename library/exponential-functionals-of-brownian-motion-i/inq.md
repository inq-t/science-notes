# Exponential Functionals of Brownian Motion, I: Probability Laws at Fixed Time

Matsumoto and Yor survey fixed-time distributions of integrated geometric Brownian motion. They develop their connections with Bessel processes and Hartman--Watson distributions, present density and moment formulas, and discuss related identities and limiting laws.

## Metadata

- **Authors:** Hiroyuki Matsumoto and Marc Yor.
- **Published:** *Probability Surveys* **2** (2005), 312--347.
- **DOI:** [10.1214/154957805100000159](https://doi.org/10.1214/154957805100000159).
- **Preprint:** [arXiv:math/0511517](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0511517), submitted November 21, 2005.
- **Author manuscript:** [Full text](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0511517); [source package](https://arxiv.org/src/math/0511517).

The opening paragraph paraphrases the abstract and scope. Section 2, equations (2.2), (2.4), (2.8)--(2.10), presents the Bessel-process change of measure and the Hartman--Watson Laplace transform. Appendix A gives the density-transform proof.
