# The Dark Energy Survey Supernova Program: A Reanalysis of Cosmology Results and Evidence for Evolving Dark Energy with an Updated Type Ia Supernova Calibration

The DES Collaboration reanalyzes the five-year Type Ia supernova sample with an updated photometric calibration and revised treatment of systematic uncertainties. In combination with complementary cosmological probes, the recalibration changes the inferred dark-energy constraints and the reported preference for an evolving equation of state.
