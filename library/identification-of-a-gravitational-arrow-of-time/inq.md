# Identification of a gravitational arrow of time

Julian Barbour, Tim Koslowski, and Flavio Mercati study the Newtonian N-body problem with vanishing total energy and angular momentum as a model of a closed universe. Typical solutions divide into two branches around a distinguished point, with shape complexity growing between rising bounds away from it. The authors relate the formation of structures carrying dynamical records to the time direction inferred by internal observers.

- Authors: Barbour, Julian; Koslowski, Tim; Mercati, Flavio.
- First submitted: 2014-09-02.
