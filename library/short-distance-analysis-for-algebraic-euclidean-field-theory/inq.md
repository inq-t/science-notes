# Short-Distance Analysis for Algebraic Euclidean Field Theory

Schlingemann studies how the short-distance scaling limit of an algebraic Euclidean field theory is reflected in its reconstructed Minkowski theory. Under the paper's hypotheses, reconstruction of the Euclidean scaling limit yields a net equivalent to a scaling-limit net of the reconstructed Minkowski theory.

## Metadata

- **Author:** Dirk Schlingemann.
- **Published:** 1999.
- **arXiv:** [hep-th/9907167](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9907167).
- **Local artifacts:** none.

The opening paragraph paraphrases the article abstract and its reconstruction result.
