# Scaling Algebras and Renormalization Group in Algebraic Quantum Field Theory II: Instructive Examples

Buchholz and Verch apply scaling algebras to massive free fields. In two and three spatial dimensions the short-distance scaling-limit observable net is the corresponding massless free-field net; in one spatial dimension the limit instead acquires a nontrivial center and charged states satisfying Gauss' law.

## Metadata

- **Published:** 1998.
- **Journal:** *Reviews in Mathematical Physics* **10**, 775--800.
