# Properties and Uses of the Wilson Flow in Lattice QCD

Luscher studies the Wilson flow in lattice QCD and argues that gauge fields at positive flow time are smooth renormalized fields whose local gauge-invariant expressions probe length scales of order \(\sqrt t\). The analysis also makes the dynamical separation of topological sectors in the continuum limit transparent.
