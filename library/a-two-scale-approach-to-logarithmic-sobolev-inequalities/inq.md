# A Two-Scale Approach to Logarithmic Sobolev Inequalities and the Hydrodynamic Limit

Grunewald, Otto, Villani, and Westdickenberg establish an abstract criterion that transfers a logarithmic-Sobolev inequality from uniformly controlled conditional microscopic measures and a coarse marginal to the full measure, with an explicit loss determined by the mixed macro--micro Hessian. They apply the method to Kawasaki dynamics and obtain dimension-uniform estimates and a quantitative hydrodynamic limit.
