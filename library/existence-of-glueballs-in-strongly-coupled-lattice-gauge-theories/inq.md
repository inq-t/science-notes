# Existence of Glueballs in Strongly Coupled Lattice Gauge Theories

Schor proves that, at sufficiently large coupling, plaquette--plaquette correlation functions in pure lattice gauge theories exhibit an isolated simple pole in the complex energy variable. This yields isolated one-particle glueball states in the paper's boxiton state space.

## Metadata

- **Author:** Ricardo S. Schor.
- **Published:** 1983-07-18.
- **Journal:** *Nuclear Physics B* **222** (1), 71--82.
- **DOI:** [10.1016/0550-3213(83)90609-0](https://doi.org/10.1016/0550-3213(83)90609-0).
- **Local artifacts:** none.

The opening paragraph paraphrases the publisher abstract. This result is a strong-coupling lattice spectral theorem; it is not a four-dimensional continuum mass-gap construction.
