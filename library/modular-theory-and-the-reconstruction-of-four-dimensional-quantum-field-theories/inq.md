# Modular Theory and the Reconstruction of Four-Dimensional Quantum Field Theories

Ralf Kähler and Hans-Werner Wiesbrock construct a representation of the \(3+1\)-dimensional Poincare group from modular groups of von Neumann algebras in a specified mutual modular position. Combined with the Bisognano--Wichmann result, their construction obtains a local observable net from a finite family of algebras.

- Authors: Ralf Kähler, Hans-Werner Wiesbrock
- Published: 2001
- Journal: *Journal of Mathematical Physics* **42** (1), 74--86
- DOI: [10.1063/1.1327597](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.1327597)
