# Spectral Gaps and Incompressibility in a ν = 1/3 Fractional Quantum Hall System

Nachtergaele, Warzel and Young analyze an effective thin-cylinder Hamiltonian. They describe its ground states using tilings and fragmented matrix-product states, prove a gap for a range of couplings, and derive incompressibility. They also bound correlations for the tiling states while distinguishing more general superpositions.
