# The Equivalence of the Logarithmic Sobolev Inequality and the Dobrushin--Shlosman Mixing Condition

Daniel W. Stroock and Boguslaw Zegarlinski prove, for finite-range lattice gases with compact continuous spin space, that a uniform logarithmic-Sobolev inequality is equivalent to the Dobrushin--Shlosman mixing condition. They also relate these conditions to uniform convergence of the associated Glauber dynamics and to a large-deviation principle for occupation times.
