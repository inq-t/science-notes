# Trotter–Kato Product Formula for Unitary Groups

For two nonnegative self-adjoint operators whose form sum is densely defined, Exner and Neidhardt establish convergence of imaginary-time Trotter products in the Hilbert norm integrated over bounded time intervals. They extend the result to products built from holomorphic Kato functions and obtain a representation of those functions.

- Authors: Pavel Exner; Hagen Neidhardt.
- Version: arXiv:0907.1199v1, submitted 2009-07-07.
- Primary record: [arXiv abstract and version history](https://arxiv.org/abs/0907.1199v1).
- Primary text: [arXiv manuscript](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0907.1199v1).
- Accessed: 2026-09-08; abstract, version metadata, and the introductory source locations below.

Introduction, equations (1.3)–(1.4), records the positive-real-parameter semigroup product theorems for densely defined form sums, including the symmetric product. The paper's principal imaginary-time result is stated separately in Theorem 2.1.
