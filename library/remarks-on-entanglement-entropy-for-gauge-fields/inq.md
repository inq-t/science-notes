# Remarks on Entanglement Entropy for Gauge Fields

Casini, Huerta, and Rosabal analyze regional algebras in lattice gauge theory. Gauge constraints obstruct a canonical tensor-product factorization of the physical Hilbert space and naturally produce boundary centers; extended-lattice and maximal-boundary-tree choices alter the regional presentation, while relative entropy and mutual information retain universal continuum content.
