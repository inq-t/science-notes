# Remarks on Entanglement Entropy for Gauge Fields

Casini, Huerta, and Rosabal study how gauge constraints and boundary centers affect regional operator algebras and entanglement entropy. They compare regional-algebra choices, including choices associated with maximal boundary trees, and discuss relative entropy and mutual information in the continuum limit. Their explicit lattice constructions focus on Abelian gauge fields.

## Metadata

- **Authors:** Horacio Casini, Marina Huerta, and José Alejandro Rosabal.
- **Published:** *Physical Review D* **89**, 085012 (2014).
- **Archived version:** [arXiv:1312.1183v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/1312.1183v1), 4 December 2013; 26 pages.
- **DOI:** [10.1103/PhysRevD.89.085012](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.89.085012).
