# Kazhdan's Property (T) via Semidefinite Optimization

Tim Netzer and Andreas Thom use Ozawa's group-ring criterion to give an exact sum-of-squares certificate for Kazhdan property \((T)\) of \(SL(3,\mathbb Z)\). Their introduction states the representation-uniform spectral characterization: a finitely generated group has property \((T)\) exactly when its unnormalized group Laplacian has a common positive spectral gap above invariant vectors in every unitary representation.

- Authors: Tim Netzer, Andreas Thom
- Published: *Experimental Mathematics* **24** (2015), 371--374
- DOI: [10.1080/10586458.2014.986180](https://doi.org/10.1080/10586458.2014.986180)
- Preprint and source: [arXiv:1411.2488](https://arxiv.org/abs/1411.2488)
