# Derivations as Square Roots of Dirichlet Forms

Cipriani and Sauvageot analyze tracially symmetric Dirichlet forms on $C^*$-algebras in terms of closable derivations valued in Hilbert bimodules together with a killing weight. The generator of the associated Markov semigroup appears as the divergence of a derivation, providing a noncommutative differential calculus and structural decompositions of Dirichlet forms.
