# Orthogonal Pairs of Star-Subalgebras in Finite von Neumann Algebras

Popa studies orthogonality relations between star-subalgebras of a finite von Neumann algebra relative to its trace. The conditional-expectation and Hilbert-projection relations developed there underlie the commuting-square condition used to turn compatible subalgebra intersections into orthogonal joint components.
