# Hamiltonian Formulation of Wilson's Lattice Gauge Theories

John Kogut and Leonard Susskind formulate Wilson lattice gauge theory canonically as coupled rigid rotators. Link electric fields provide the kinetic Laplacians, magnetic plaquette terms provide the potential, and Gauss-law invariance selects the physical carrier. This is the regulated Hamiltonian structure used by the gauge-descent coercivity programme.
