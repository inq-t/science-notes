# Remarks on Unitary Equivalence of Lorentz-Invariant Fields

Reeh and Schlieder prove the vacuum cyclicity result now called the Reeh--Schlieder theorem: under the standard relativistic field-theory assumptions, states obtained by applying observables localized in any nonempty open region to the vacuum are dense in the Hilbert space. Together with locality and cyclicity for a spacelike complement, the vacuum is separating for a local observable algebra.
