# Geometries of Quantum States

Petz and Sudár seek the quantum analogue of the Fisher metric and study Riemannian metrics on positive definite density matrices. They show several examples to be stochastically monotone, characterize every stochastically monotone metric using operator-monotone functions, prove the existence of maximal and minimal members, and identify the Fubini-Study geometry on an extension to pure states.
