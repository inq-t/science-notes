# Asymptotic Behavior of Homogeneous Cosmological Models in the Presence of a Positive Cosmological Constant

Initially expanding Bianchi cosmologies satisfying Einstein's equation with a positive cosmological constant and matter obeying the dominant and strong energy conditions approach de Sitter behavior exponentially for every Bianchi type except IX. Type IX has the same behavior under an additional initial bound comparing the cosmological constant with spatial curvature. The characteristic time is \(\sqrt{3/\Lambda}\). Expansion becomes isotropic and matter density decays; this local asymptotic behavior does not require the global properties of the universe to approach those of de Sitter space.
