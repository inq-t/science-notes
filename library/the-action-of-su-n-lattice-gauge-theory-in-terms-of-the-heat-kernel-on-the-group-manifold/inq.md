# The Action of SU(N) Lattice Gauge Theory in Terms of the Heat Kernel on the Group Manifold

Menotti and Onofri propose the group heat kernel as an alternative lattice gauge action and exhibit its analogy with the Berezinskii--Villain action. They emphasize its close relation to the Hamiltonian formulation, its improved strong-coupling organization, and the absence of the Gross--Witten singularity in two dimensions.
