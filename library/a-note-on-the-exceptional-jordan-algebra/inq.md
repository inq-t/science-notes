# A Note on the Exceptional Jordan Algebra

Albert gives a proof that the exceptional Jordan algebra is not special: it cannot be represented faithfully as a Jordan subalgebra of an associative algebra equipped with the symmetrized product. The argument removes a finite-dimensional restriction from the earlier non-speciality result.

- Author: A. A. Albert
- Journal: *Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences of the United States of America* **36** (1950), 372--374
- DOI: [10.1073/pnas.36.7.372](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.36.7.372)
- Full text: [PubMed Central PMC1063206](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1063206/)
