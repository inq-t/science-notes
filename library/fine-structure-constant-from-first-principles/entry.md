# The Fine-Structure Constant from First Principles: A Geometric Derivation

Paquet proposes a derivation of the fine-structure constant within Relational Physics from a final coalgebra for a ternary branching functor and its realization by the chiral trivalent srs graph. A count of 128 spinorial and 9 gauge degrees of freedom gives the proposed bare inverse coupling 137, while a loop-space variational correction gives the reported value 137.035999187 without fitted parameters.
