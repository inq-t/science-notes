# Adapting the Gibbs Sampler

Chimisov, Latuszynski and Roberts develop an adaptive random-scan Gibbs sampler whose coordinate-selection probabilities are guided by the spectral gap of a Gaussian approximation. They derive the Gaussian gap, discuss adaptation and ergodicity conditions, and test the approach on several statistical models.
