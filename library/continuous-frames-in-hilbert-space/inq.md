# Continuous Frames in Hilbert Space

Ali, Antoine, and Gazeau extend discrete Hilbert-space frame theory to families indexed by discrete, continuous, or mixed measure spaces. They develop the associated analysis, synthesis, and frame operators, characterize equivalence and reproducing-kernel realizations, and show how positive lower and finite upper frame bounds give stable, redundant reconstruction without requiring an orthonormal basis.
