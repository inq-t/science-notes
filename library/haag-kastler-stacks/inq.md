# Haag--Kastler Stacks

Marco Benini, Alastair Grant-Stuart, and Alexander Schenkel formulate algebraic quantum field theory through a two-functor assigning a category of Haag--Kastler theories to each Lorentzian manifold and restriction functors to embeddings. The framework recovers locally covariant theories as points and investigates descent conditions for assembling theories from local data.

- Authors: Marco Benini, Alastair Grant-Stuart, Alexander Schenkel.
