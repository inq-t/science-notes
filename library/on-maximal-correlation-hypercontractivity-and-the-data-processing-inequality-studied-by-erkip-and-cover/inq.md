# On Maximal Correlation, Hypercontractivity, and the Data Processing Inequality Studied by Erkip and Cover

Venkat Anantharam, Amin Gohari, Sudeep Kamath, and Chandra Nair give geometric characterizations of Hirschfeld--Gebelein--Renyi maximal correlation and the hypercontractivity ribbon. They identify maximal-correlation squared with a local Hessian threshold, provide the correct tight coefficient for the associated mutual-information data-processing inequality, and give a counterexample to the previously claimed use of maximal-correlation squared as its universal global coefficient.

- Authors: Venkat Anantharam, Amin Gohari, Sudeep Kamath, Chandra Nair
- First submitted: 2013-04-22
- Conference version: *IEEE International Symposium on Information Theory* (2014)
