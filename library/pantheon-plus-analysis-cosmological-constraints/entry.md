# The Pantheon+ Analysis: Cosmological Constraints

The Pantheon+ analysis derives cosmological constraints from 1701 light curves of 1550 distinct Type Ia supernovae, with improved calibration and systematic-error modeling. It evaluates standard and extended cosmologies, combines the supernova sample with other probes, and quantifies the implications of including the local distance-ladder calibration.
