# Precision Study of the Continuum SU(3) Yang–Mills Theory

S. Borsányi and collaborators use large-scale lattice simulations, parallel tempering, finite-size scaling, and continuum and infinite-volume extrapolations to characterize the thermal transition of pure \(SU(3)\) Yang--Mills theory. They find a first-order transition and report \(w_0T_c=0.25384(23)\) and latent heat \(\Delta E/T_c^4=1.025(21)(27)\). This pure-gauge thermal transition is not the smooth physical-mass \((2+1)\)-flavor QCD crossover.
