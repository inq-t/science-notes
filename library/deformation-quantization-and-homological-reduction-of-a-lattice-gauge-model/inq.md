# Deformation Quantization and Homological Reduction of a Lattice Gauge Model

Pflaum, Rudolph, and Schmidt study deformation quantization of a finite lattice gauge model. Their kinematic reduction first quotients by the freely acting pointed gauge group; maximal-tree gauge identifies the resulting configuration space with a finite product \(G^N\), on which the residual root group acts by simultaneous conjugation. For \(SU(2)\), they then verify the hypotheses needed for homological reduction of the generally singular reduced phase space.
