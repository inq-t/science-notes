# Bargmann Invariants, Null Phase Curves, and a Theory of the Geometric Phase

N. Mukunda, Arvind, E. Ercolessi, G. Marmo, G. Morandi and R. Simon formulate geometric phase using Bargmann invariants and null-phase curves. The third-order invariant defines the relevant curves, while the symplectic form on ray space supplies the surface-integral formulation. Geodesics provide special null-phase curves; the broader construction separates the phase geometry from reliance on a particular metric path.

- **Authors:** N. Mukunda, Arvind, E. Ercolessi, G. Marmo, G. Morandi, R. Simon.
- **Primary text:** [Indian Academy of Sciences repository](https://repository.ias.ac.in/25284/1/332.pdf).
