# Some Remarks on the Gribov Ambiguity

Singer proves topological obstructions to choosing a continuous global gauge representative for nonabelian gauge connections in the compact-base settings studied in the article, placing the Gribov ambiguity beyond a peculiarity of one local gauge condition.
