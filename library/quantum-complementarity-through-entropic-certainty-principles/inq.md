# Quantum Complementarity through Entropic Certainty Principles

Javier M. Magan and Diego Pontello formulate an entropic certainty principle for complementary observable algebras: two relative entropies associated with dual conditional expectations sum to the logarithm of the algebraic index. Applications include charge intertwiners and twists for global symmetries and Wilson and 't Hooft loops for local symmetries, with uncertainty relations obtained by monotonicity from the underlying certainty identity.
