# Relative Hamiltonian for Faithful Normal States of a von Neumann Algebra

Araki constructs the relative Hamiltonian between faithful normal states of a von Neumann algebra and proves its uniqueness under the stated order bounds. The associated cocycle intertwines the two modular automorphism groups; the paper also relates their modular operators and establishes a chain rule for relative Hamiltonians.
