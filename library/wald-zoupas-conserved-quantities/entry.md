# A General Definition of “Conserved Quantities” in General Relativity and Other Theories of Gravity

Wald and Zoupas extend Hamiltonian charge methods to radiative settings in which symplectic current escapes through a boundary and a Hamiltonian generator need not exist. They propose a modified prescription for asymptotic charges in diffeomorphism-covariant Lagrangian theories and recover the standard null-infinity result for general relativity, while leaving the most general existence and uniqueness questions open.
