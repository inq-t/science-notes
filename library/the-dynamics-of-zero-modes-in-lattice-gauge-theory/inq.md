# The Dynamics of Zero Modes in Lattice Gauge Theory

Yuhma Asano and Jun Nishimura study the nonperturbative zero-mode sector of
four-dimensional pure \(SU(2)\) and \(SU(3)\) lattice Yang--Mills theory at
large \(\beta\). They test perturbative predictions around the trivial
vacuum in the presence of degenerate flat toron vacua and find logarithmic
subleading decay for \(SU(2)\), versus power-law decay for \(SU(3)\); their
analysis identifies the four-dimensional \(SU(2)\) case as marginal under
the finite-box zero-mode criterion.

- **Authors:** Yuhma Asano and Jun Nishimura.
- **Published:** 2023.
- **Journal:** *Journal of High Energy Physics* **11** (2023), 204.
- **DOI:** [10.1007/JHEP11(2023)204](https://doi.org/10.1007/JHEP11(2023)204).
- **arXiv:** [2303.01008](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.01008).
