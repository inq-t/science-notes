# Relative Quantum Field Theory

Freed and Teleman formulate quantum field theories whose evaluations take values assigned by a theory in one higher dimension. Their examples include anomalous theories and gauge theories specified by a compact Lie algebra, with applications to six-dimensional superconformal theory.

- Authors: Daniel S. Freed and Constantin Teleman.
- First submitted: 7 December 2012.
- Primary manuscript: [arXiv:1212.1692v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/1212.1692v3), revised 14 January 2014.
- Machine-readable article: [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/1212.1692v3).
- Accessed: 9 September 2026. The primary manuscript was inspected; no article payload archived locally.
