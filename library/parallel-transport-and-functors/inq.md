# Parallel Transport and Functors

Urs Schreiber and Konrad Waldorf characterize the smooth functors that arise as parallel transport for connections on fibre bundles by local trivializations and smooth descent data, giving an equivalence between connection geometry and appropriately smooth path-groupoid functors.

- Authors: Urs Schreiber, Konrad Waldorf
- Journal: *Journal of Homotopy and Related Structures* **4**, 187--244
- Published: 2009
- arXiv: [0705.0452](https://arxiv.org/abs/0705.0452)
- TeX source: [arXiv e-print](https://arxiv.org/e-print/0705.0452)
