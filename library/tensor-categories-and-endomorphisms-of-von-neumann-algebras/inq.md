# Tensor Categories and Endomorphisms of von Neumann Algebras

Marcel Bischoff, Roberto Longo, Yasuyuki Kawahigashi, and Karl-Henning Rehren review the correspondence between Q-systems in endomorphism categories and finite-index extensions of infinite von Neumann factors, including modules, bimodules, decompositions, centers, and applications to algebraic quantum field theory. Their normalization distinguishes the categorical dimension of the dual canonical endomorphism \(\theta=\bar\iota\iota\), equal to the Jones index, from the Q-system dimension \(d_A=\dim(\iota)=\sqrt{\dim(\theta)}\).
