# Calderon Reproducing Formulas and Applications to Hardy Spaces

Auscher, McIntosh, and Morris establish Calderon reproducing formulas for self-adjoint operators with finite-propagation groups, combining holomorphic and Fourier functional calculi. They apply the formulas to Hardy-space embeddings and atomic decompositions for Hodge--Dirac and related nonnegative self-adjoint operators.

## Metadata

- **Authors:** Pascal Auscher, Alan McIntosh, and Andrew J. Morris.
- **Published:** 2015.
