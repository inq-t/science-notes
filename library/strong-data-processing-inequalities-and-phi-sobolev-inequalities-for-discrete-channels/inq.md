# Strong Data Processing Inequalities and Phi-Sobolev Inequalities for Discrete Channels

Maxim Raginsky develops variational characterizations, bounds, tensorization
properties, and functional-inequality interpretations of strong data
processing coefficients for discrete channels. For a fixed input law, the
chi-squared contraction coefficient is squared maximal correlation, and the
associated channel Poincare energy is the conditional variance remaining
after the channel output is known.

**Author:** Maxim Raginsky  
**Journal:** *IEEE Transactions on Information Theory* **62** (2016),
3355--3389  
**DOI:** [10.1109/TIT.2016.2549542](https://doi.org/10.1109/TIT.2016.2549542)  
**arXiv and source:** [1411.3575](https://arxiv.org/abs/1411.3575)
