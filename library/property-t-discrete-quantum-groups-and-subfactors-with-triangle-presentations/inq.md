# Property (T) Discrete Quantum Groups and Subfactors with Triangle Presentations

Stefaan Vaes and Matthias Valvekens formulate a tube-algebra averaging operator for a finite symmetric generating set of a rigid \(C^*\)-tensor category and prove that categorical property \((T)\) is equivalent to isolation of its trivial eigenvalue in the universal nondegenerate module. They also give a finite-dimensional link-Laplacian criterion that is sufficient for this property and use triangle presentations to construct property-\((T)\) quantum groups and subfactor standard invariants.
