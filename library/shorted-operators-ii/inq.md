# Shorted Operators II

Anderson and Trapp prove that, for a bounded positive operator on a Hilbert space and a closed subspace, there is a largest positive operator below the original operator whose range lies in that subspace. They develop the infinite-dimensional shorting operation and relate it to parallel addition; the construction is the operator-theoretic form of eliminating hidden ports in a passive network.

- Authors: W. N. Anderson, Jr. and G. E. Trapp
- Published: *SIAM Journal on Applied Mathematics* **28** (1) (1975), 60--71
- DOI: [10.1137/0128007](https://doi.org/10.1137/0128007)
- Publisher record: [SIAM](https://epubs.siam.org/doi/10.1137/0128007)
