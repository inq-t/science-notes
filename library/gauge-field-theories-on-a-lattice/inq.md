# Gauge Field Theories on a Lattice

Osterwalder and Seiler give a rigorous Euclidean treatment of lattice gauge and fermion fields, verify physical positivity of the regulated Schwinger functions, and thereby obtain a positive self-adjoint transfer matrix. They also prove strong-coupling infinite-volume analyticity and a Wilson confinement bound within the scope stated in the paper.

## Metadata

- **Authors:** Konrad Osterwalder and Erhard Seiler.
- **Published:** February 1978.
- **Journal:** *Annals of Physics* **110** (2), 440--471.
- **DOI:** [10.1016/0003-4916(78)90039-8](https://doi.org/10.1016/0003-4916(78)90039-8).
