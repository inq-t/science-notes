# A Probabilistic Mechanism for Quark Confinement

Sourav Chatterjee gives a rigorous lattice-gauge formulation of center symmetry and proves that unbroken center symmetry implies confinement. When the gauge-group center is nontrivial, exponential correlation decay under arbitrary boundary conditions is a sufficient condition for the center symmetry to remain unbroken.
