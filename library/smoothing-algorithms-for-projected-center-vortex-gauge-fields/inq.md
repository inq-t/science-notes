# Smoothing Algorithms for Projected Center-Vortex Gauge Fields

Virgili, Kamleh and Leinweber examine smoothing methods for center-projected \(SU(3)\) lattice gauge fields. They identify limitations of standard procedures and develop preconditioning and vortex-preserving annealing methods.

Adam Virgili, Waseem Kamleh, Derek Leinweber. *Physical Review D* **106**, 014505, published July 11, 2022.

[Publisher](https://journals.aps.org/prd/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevD.106.014505). [arXiv record and source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.09764). [Open-access published text](https://scoap3-prod-backend.s3.cern.ch/media/files/71281/10.1103/PhysRevD.106.014505.pdf).
