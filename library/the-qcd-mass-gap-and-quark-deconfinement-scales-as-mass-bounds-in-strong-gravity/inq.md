# The QCD Mass Gap and Quark Deconfinement Scales as Mass Bounds in Strong Gravity

Piyabut Burikham, Tiberiu Harko, and Matthew J. Lake study Salam's speculative strong-gravity model for gauge-singlet hadrons. Using an effective strong cosmological constant, static spherical field equations, generalized Buchdahl bounds, and quantum minimum-length considerations, they derive lower and upper mass/radius scales associated respectively with a proposed gap and deconfinement. They distinguish the ordinary de Sitter cell \((\ell_P^2\ell_{\mathrm{dS}})^{1/3}\) from its strong-gravity analogue \((\ell_{\mathrm{sP}}^2\ell_{\mathrm{sd}})^{1/3}\). The model is explicitly not a theory of \(SU(3)\) color interactions, and its proposed mass-gap mechanism assumes the glueball mass entering the mixing term a priori.
