# Ramanujan Graphs

Alexander Lubotzky, Ralph Phillips, and Peter Sarnak construct explicit
families of regular graphs whose nontrivial adjacency eigenvalues satisfy the
optimal Ramanujan bound. The construction also supplies large-girth families,
combining strong one-step spectral expansion with locally tree-like graph
geometry.

**Authors:** Alexander Lubotzky, Ralph Phillips, Peter Sarnak  
**Journal:** *Combinatorica* **8** (1988), 261--277  
**DOI:** [10.1007/BF02126799](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02126799)  
**Manuscript:** [Hebrew University](https://math1.math.huji.ac.il/~alexlub/PAPERS/ramanujan%20graphs/ramanujanGraphs.pdf)
