# Beurling–Fourier Algebras on Compact Groups: Spectral Theory

Ludwig, Spronk and Turowska define weighted Fourier algebras on compact groups using positive weights on the irreducible representations. They study their spectra inside a complexification of the group, identify cases where the spectrum is the original group, and investigate symmetry, regularity and spectral synthesis.

Authors: Jean Ludwig, Nico Spronk and Lyudmila Turowska.

Source: [arXiv:1103.4063v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/1103.4063v1),
submitted March 21, 2011.
