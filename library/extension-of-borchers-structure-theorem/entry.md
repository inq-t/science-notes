# Extension of the Structure Theorem of Borchers and Its Application to Half-Sided Modular Inclusions

Araki and Zsidó extend Wiesbrock's half-sided modular-inclusion theorem from a common cyclic-separating vector to a common normal faithful semifinite weight, while repairing a gap in the earlier proof. Under their half-sided modular hypotheses, a unitary one-parameter group with positive generator induces an endomorphism semigroup on the appropriate half-line and participates in an affine-group representation.
