# Quantum Cosmology. I

Misner applies Hamiltonian general relativity to homogeneous cosmological models, explicitly solves their constraints, and recasts their evolution as a time-dependent mechanical system. A natural factor ordering supports canonical quantization while preserving the metric signature. For the closed Bianchi IX model, the analysis finds that a high-quantum-number classical state remains classical when evolved toward the initial singularity.
