# Primordial Gravitational Waves, Precisely: The Role of Thermodynamics in the Standard Model

Saikawa and Shirai reconstruct the Standard Model equation of state across a wide temperature range from perturbative and nonperturbative results, tabulating the distinct effective degrees of freedom for energy density and entropy density. They propagate these thermodynamic corrections through the evolution of primordial gravitational waves and find smooth QCD-crossover and free-streaming features together with percent-level changes in the predicted spectrum.
