# Cosmological Strangeness Abundance

Yang and Rafelski calculate thermal and chemical strangeness abundances through the primordial hadronic epoch and compare weak, electromagnetic, and strong production rates with Hubble expansion. Their model finds distinct reaction-dependent loss-of-detailed-balance temperatures near (33.8\), (23)--(25), and (19.8\,\mathrm{MeV}), illustrating that there is no single universal temperature at which a generic “massive sector” freezes out.
