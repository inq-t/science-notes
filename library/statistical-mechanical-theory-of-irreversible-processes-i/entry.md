# Statistical-Mechanical Theory of Irreversible Processes. I. General Theory and Simple Applications to Magnetic and Conduction Problems

Kubo develops a general fluctuation-dissipation theorem relating linear response coefficients and complex susceptibilities to equilibrium time-correlation functions. The formulation treats magnetic and electric polarization and transport phenomena, and gives simple applications to magnetic resonance and electrical conduction.
