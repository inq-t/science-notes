# Structure of Uniformly Continuous Quantum Markov Semigroups

Deschamps, Fagnola, Sasso, and Umanita analyze uniformly continuous quantum Markov semigroups whose decoherence-free subalgebra is atomic. They decompose the dynamics into noiseless and irreducible components and use that structure to characterize invariant states and decoherence-free subsystems.

## Metadata

- **Authors:** Julien Deschamps, Franco Fagnola, Emanuela Sasso, and Veronica Umanita.
- **Published:** 2016.
- **Journal:** *Reviews in Mathematical Physics* **28** (1), 1650003.
- **DOI:** [10.1142/S0129055X16500033](https://doi.org/10.1142/S0129055X16500033).
