# Construction of a Selfadjoint, Strictly Positive Transfer Matrix for Euclidean Lattice Gauge Theories

Luscher constructs the transfer matrix for Wilson's Euclidean lattice gauge theory and proves that it is self-adjoint and strictly positive. The result supplies a positive Hamiltonian interpretation at finite lattice regulator and is a canonical bridge between Euclidean lattice amplitudes and physical time evolution.
