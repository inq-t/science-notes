# Quantum G2 Categories Have Property (T)

Corey Jones proves that the rigid \(C^*\)-tensor categories of finite-dimensional type-1 unitary representations of \(U_q(\mathfrak g_2)\) have categorical property \((T)\) for every positive \(q\neq1\). The proof uses the trivalent planar presentation of the quantum \(G_2\) category: its commutative fusion algebra is generated by the fundamental objects, admissibility is constrained by tube-algebra positivity, and the trivial dimension character is shown to be isolated. The classical \(q=1\) certificate degenerates, consistently with the amenability of \(\operatorname{Rep}(G_2)\).
