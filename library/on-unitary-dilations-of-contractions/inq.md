# On Unitary Dilations of Contractions

Schäffer gives an explicit bilateral matrix construction of a unitary
dilation for an arbitrary Hilbert-space contraction (A), with compression
of every positive power recovering the corresponding power of (A).

- Author: J. J. Schäffer
- Published: *Proceedings of the American Mathematical Society* **6** (2)
  (1955), 322
- DOI: [10.1090/S0002-9939-1955-0068740-7](https://doi.org/10.1090/S0002-9939-1955-0068740-7)
- Primary PDF: [American Mathematical Society](https://www.ams.org/proc/1955-006-02/S0002-9939-1955-0068740-7/S0002-9939-1955-0068740-7.pdf)
