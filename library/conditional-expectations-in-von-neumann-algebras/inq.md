# Conditional Expectations in von Neumann Algebras

For an inclusion \(N\subseteq M\) of von Neumann algebras and a faithful semifinite normal weight whose restriction to \(N\) is semifinite, Takesaki proves that \(N\) is invariant under the weight's modular automorphism group exactly when there exists a faithful sigma-weakly continuous norm-one projection from \(M\) onto \(N\) that preserves the weight. He also proves that a von Neumann algebra is finite exactly when each maximal abelian self-adjoint subalgebra is the range of a sigma-weakly continuous norm-one projection.
