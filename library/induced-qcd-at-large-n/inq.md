# Induced QCD at Large N

V. A. Kazakov and A. A. Migdal study a lattice model in which an adjoint scalar field induces gauge interactions. Integrating the gauge variables gives an effective description in terms of scalar eigenvalues. At large \(N\), they identify two phases and derive nonlinear integral equations for the vacuum average and scalar excitation spectrum in the weak-coupling phase. They associate asymptotic freedom with the strong-coupling phase, subject to a stated phase-transition qualification, and leave that phase unsolved.

- Authors: V. A. Kazakov and A. A. Migdal.
- Preprint: [arXiv:hep-th/9206015v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9206015v1), submitted 3 June 1992.
- Publication: *Nuclear Physics B* **397** (1993), 214–238.
