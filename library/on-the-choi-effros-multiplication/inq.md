# On the Choi–Effros Multiplication

Bebe Prunaru gives a short proof of the Choi–Effros theorem describing the algebraic structure of the range of a completely positive projection.

Author: Bebe Prunaru.

Source: [arXiv:1304.6664v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1304.6664v2),
submitted April 24, 2013; revised April 26, 2013.
[Author TeX source](https://arxiv.org/src/1304.6664v2).

The opening paragraph paraphrases the author abstract. No manuscript
payload is stored locally.
