# The Geometry of the Orbit Space for Non-Abelian Gauge Theories

I. M. Singer studies the geometry and topology of the space of non-Abelian
connections modulo gauge transformations. The article develops the natural
connection and curvature of the gauge-orbit bundle and discusses
regularization of the functional Laplacian and Sobolev-metric control. It is
a geometric source for horizontal gauge response, not by itself a
construction of a quantum vacuum measure or a mass-gap estimate.

- **Author:** I. M. Singer.
- **Published:** 1981.
- **Journal:** *Physica Scripta* **24** (5), 817--820.
- **DOI:** [10.1088/0031-8949/24/5/002](https://doi.org/10.1088/0031-8949/24/5/002).
- **Local artifacts:** none.
