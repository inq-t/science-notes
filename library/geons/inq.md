# Geons

John Archibald Wheeler studies self-consistent classical configurations in which the gravitational attraction associated with electromagnetic radiation confines that radiation for times long compared with its characteristic periods. The paper analyzes toroidal and idealized spherical models, their mass--radius scaling, leakage and instability, interactions, and the regime in which quantum effects would become unavoidable.
