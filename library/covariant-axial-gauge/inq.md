# Covariant Axial Gauge

Dimock constructs an axial gauge for Abelian lattice gauge theories that respects lattice symmetries and is compatible with block-averaging renormalization. The paper develops constrained Gaussian integrals, minimizing fields, and uniform fluctuation estimates.
