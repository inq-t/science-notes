# Causal Fermion Systems as an Effective Collapse Theory

Felix Finster, Johannes Kleiner, and Claudio F. Paganini derive an effective collapse model from the causal action principle in a nonrelativistic limit. Nonlinear stochastic corrections modify the Schrödinger equation, while the statistical operator obeys an equation of Kossakowski–Lindblad form. They obtain collapse compatible with the Born rule and describe differences from continuous spontaneous localization, including microscopic nonlocality in time.
