# A Simple Proof of the Poincare Inequality

Bakry, Barthe, Cattiaux and Guillin give a direct proof of Poincare inequalities from a Lyapunov drift condition and a local inequality. They apply the method to a broad class of probability measures, including log-concave measures on Euclidean space, and discuss a stronger first-order inequality and extensions.
