# Dynamics for QCD on an Infinite Lattice

Grundling and Rudolph construct Hamiltonian QCD dynamics on an infinite fixed lattice in three-dimensional space. Finite-sublattice evolutions converge in pointwise norm to a global automorphism group. The orbits of local algebras generate a field algebra on which this evolution is strongly continuous. The construction implements gauge transformations and the Gauss constraint, descends to physical observables, and establishes the existence of gauge-invariant ground states.
