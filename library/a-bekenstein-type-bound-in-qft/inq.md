# A Bekenstein-Type Bound in QFT

Roberto Longo proves a model-independent upper bound on the vacuum relative entropy of a vector state localized in a spacetime region of width (2R): for a wedge-dual, translation-covariant local net with positive Hamiltonian (H), the regional relative entropy is at most (2\pi R) times the state's energy in natural units. The proof uses modular theory, locality, and positive energy and does not require full Poincare covariance.

- Author: Roberto Longo
- Published: *Communications in Mathematical Physics* **406** (2025), article 95
- DOI: [10.1007/s00220-025-05261-1](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00220-025-05261-1)
- Preprint and source: [arXiv:2409.14408](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.14408)
