# The Glueball Spectrum of SU(3) Gauge Theory in 3+1 Dimension

Athenodorou and Teper calculate low-lying glueball energies in pure lattice \(SU(3)\) gauge theory and extrapolate dimensionless mass ratios to the continuum using the string tension. They identify continuum spins for several states and examine topological charge, its renormalization and the topological susceptibility.

- Authors: Andreas Athenodorou and Michael Teper.
- Publication: JHEP 11 (2020), 172.
- Primary manuscript: [arXiv:2007.06422v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.06422v2), revised 20 October 2020.
- DOI: [10.1007/JHEP11(2020)172](https://doi.org/10.1007/JHEP11%282020%29172).
