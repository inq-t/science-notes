# Confinement of Quarks

Wilson formulates gauge theory on a Euclidean lattice with group-valued link variables, exact gauge invariance, and plaquette interactions, then uses its computable strong-coupling expansion to obtain a confinement mechanism. This paper introduced the regulator whose normalized fundamental (SU(3)) plaquette action is used in the exceptional-normal recovery theorem.
