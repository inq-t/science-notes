# Quantum reference frames, measurement schemes and the type of local algebras in quantum field theory

Fewster, Janssen, Loveridge, Rejzner, and Waldron combine relativistic measurement theory with quantum reference frames to construct invariant joint algebras of fields and reference frames. Under stated thermal hypotheses on the field, modular theory gives a semifinite trace; additional matched KMS-weight conditions on the reference frame make the trace finite and yield precise conditions for a type-$\mathrm{II}_1$ factor.
