# Witten Laplacian on a Lattice Spin System

Ichiro Shigekawa studies the Witten Laplacian for finite regions of an unbounded lattice spin system. Under the stated assumptions, the paper derives estimates for the bottom of the one-form Hodge--Kodaira operator that are uniform in the finite region and boundary condition, and uses them to control correlation decay and the infinite-volume limit.

- Author: Ichiro Shigekawa
- Published: *Asterisque* **327** (2009), 115--129
- DOI and source: [10.24033/ast.860](https://doi.org/10.24033/ast.860)
- Local artifacts: none
