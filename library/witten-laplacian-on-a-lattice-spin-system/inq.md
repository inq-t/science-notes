# Witten Laplacian on a Lattice Spin System

Ichiro Shigekawa studies the Hodge–Kodaira operator on differential forms for an unbounded lattice spin system with a Gibbs measure. Sufficient conditions give positive lower bounds in every positive form degree, independent of the finite region and boundary condition, together with Hodge decomposition.

- Author: Ichiro Shigekawa
- Published: *Astérisque* **327**, 115–129 (2009)
- DOI: [10.24033/ast.860](https://doi.org/10.24033/ast.860)
- Publisher archive record: [Numdam](https://www.numdam.org/item/AST_2009__327__115_0/)
- Author publication list: [Kyoto University](https://www.math.kyoto-u.ac.jp/~ichiro/)
