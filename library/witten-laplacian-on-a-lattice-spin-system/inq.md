# Witten Laplacian on a Lattice Spin System

Ichiro Shigekawa studies the weighted Hodge--Kodaira operator on differential forms for finite regions of an unbounded lattice spin system. Under the stated assumptions, the paper derives lower bounds in positive form degrees that are uniform in the finite region and boundary condition, and proves Hodge decomposition results. The estimates also apply to an infinite-volume Gibbs state if such a state exists.

- Author: Ichiro Shigekawa
- Published: *Asterisque* **327** (2009), 115--129
- DOI and source: [10.24033/ast.860](https://doi.org/10.24033/ast.860)
- Local artifacts: none
