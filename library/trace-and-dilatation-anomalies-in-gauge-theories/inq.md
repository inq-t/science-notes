# Trace and Dilatation Anomalies in Gauge Theories

Collins, Duncan, and Joglekar derive the renormalized energy-momentum trace for theories containing fermions and non-Abelian gauge fields, including the gauge-dependent operators that enter through renormalization. They connect the on-shell trace to the beta function and examine the electromagnetic-current dilatation anomaly, distinguishing finite perturbative orders from summation of the strong interaction before taking the four-dimensional limit.
