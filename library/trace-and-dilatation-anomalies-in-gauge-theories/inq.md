# Trace and Dilatation Anomalies in Gauge Theories

John C. Collins, Anthony Duncan, and Satish D. Joglekar derive the renormalized trace anomaly for general interacting theories of fermions and non-Abelian gauge bosons and relate its on-shell softness to zeros of the Callan--Symanzik beta function. The result identifies the beta-function-weighted gauge operator in the stress-tensor trace, subject to operator-mixing and convention qualifications.
