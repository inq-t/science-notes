# On Information and Sufficiency

Kullback and Leibler introduce directed mean information for discriminating between statistical hypotheses and its symmetrized divergence. They establish nonnegativity and behavior under statistical reduction, and relate preservation of information under a reduction to sufficiency, giving the quantity later called Kullback-Leibler divergence its foundational role.
