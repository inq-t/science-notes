# The Full Laplace--Beltrami Operator on \(U(N)\) and \(SU(N)\)

Ole L. Trinhammer and Gestur Olafsson derive the Laplace--Beltrami operator on \(U(N)\) and \(SU(N)\), including its radial part in eigenangle variables and the off-diagonal part. The radial operator is the compact-group operator relevant to conjugation-invariant wavefunctions in lattice gauge theory. Its Hilbert measure is the squared Weyl denominator, while its spectrum is governed by representation-theoretic Casimirs rather than by the polynomial degree of the Vandermonde.
