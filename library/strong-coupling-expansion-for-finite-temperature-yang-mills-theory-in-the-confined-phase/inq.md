# Strong Coupling Expansion for Finite Temperature Yang–Mills Theory in the Confined Phase

Jens Langelage, Gernot Münster and Owe Philipsen develop Euclidean strong-coupling expansions for finite-temperature lattice Yang–Mills theory. They formulate the expansion for general \(SU(N)\), calculate low-order free-energy and screening-mass terms for \(SU(2)\), and compare the confined-phase expressions with a glueball-gas description and Monte Carlo results.

- **Authors:** Jens Langelage, Gernot Münster, Owe Philipsen.
- **Publication:** *JHEP* **07** (2008), 036.
