# Particle Creation by Black Holes

Hawking derives quantum particle creation by gravitational collapse and finds an asymptotically thermal flux whose temperature is fixed by the black hole's surface gravity. The emission decreases the black-hole mass and, together with the area term, leads to the generalized entropy law. The calculation fixes the Bekenstein--Hawking entropy coefficient at one quarter of the horizon area in Planck units.
