# A New Basis for Hamiltonian SU(2) Simulations

Bauer, D'Andrea, Freytsis, and Grabowska construct a mixed basis for Hamiltonian \(SU(2)\) lattice gauge theory intended to remain useful from strong to weak bare coupling. Their maximal-tree treatment associates every off-tree link with a loop based at one origin, leaves residual conjugation at that origin, and makes explicit the nonlocal electric couplings introduced by this gauge-fixed presentation.
