# A New Basis for Hamiltonian SU(2) Simulations

Bauer, D’Andrea, Freytsis, and Grabowska develop a basis for Hamiltonian SU(2) lattice gauge theory in maximal-tree gauge. They study truncations intended to retain both electric and magnetic spectral information across bare-coupling regimes. The construction exposes loop variables, the residual global gauge action, and the couplings introduced by gauge fixing.
