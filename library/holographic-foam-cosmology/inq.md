# Holographic Foam Cosmology: From the Late to the Early Universe

Y. Jack Ng develops a cosmological model based on holographic spacetime foam. The proposal associates late-time dark energy with a critical energy density and quanta obeying infinite statistics, and compares an early strongly fluctuating spacetime regime with turbulence. It seeks a common spacetime-foam origin for late acceleration and early inflation.

- Authors: Ng, Y. Jack.
- First submitted: 2021-02-03.
- Archived revision: 2102.01836v2.
- arXiv: [2102.01836v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.01836v2).
