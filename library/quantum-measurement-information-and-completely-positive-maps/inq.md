# Quantum Measurement, Information, and Completely Positive Maps

Ozawa develops an axiomatic theory of quantum measurement that characterizes the statistical behavior of apparatuses measuring observables with nondegenerate spectrum. The paper relates affine laws for successive outcomes, positive and completely positive instruments, indirect unitary measurement models, and outcome-indexed Borel families of posterior density operators. It shows that the projection postulate describes only a restricted class of the state changes compatible with an observable's measurement statistics.
