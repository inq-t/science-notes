# Spin Network States in Gauge Theory

Baez constructs the gauge-invariant Hilbert space associated with a compact connected gauge group and shows that, on any fixed finite graph, it has an orthonormal basis of spin-network states. Edges carry irreducible unitary representations and vertices carry invariant intertwiners between the incident representation spaces.
