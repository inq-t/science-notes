# Operator Valued Weights Without Structure Theory

Falcone and Takesaki give a proof, avoiding structure theory, of Haagerup's criterion for the existence of a faithful normal semifinite operator-valued weight for an inclusion of von Neumann algebras. The criterion is expressed through faithful normal semifinite weights whose modular automorphism groups agree on the subalgebra; the operator-valued weight may then be chosen so that the weight on the larger algebra factors through it.
