# Generalizing Dirichlet-to-Neumann Operators

Liping Li studies Dirichlet-to-Neumann operators in the framework of Dirichlet forms and identifies, for irreducible forms, the corresponding operators with trace Dirichlet forms associated to time-changed boundary processes. Perturbations are also considered; in typical cases the resulting operator corresponds to a quasi-regular positivity-preserving symmetric coercive form and related Markov processes obtained through Doob \(h\)-transforms.
