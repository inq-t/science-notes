# Comment on "Uncertainty in measurements of distance"

Y. Jack Ng and H. van Dam defend the proposed distance-measurement uncertainty bound \(\delta l\gtrsim l^{1/3}l_P^{2/3}\). Responding to a measuring-device construction by Baez and Olson, they dispute its claimed evasion of the bound and discuss the relationship of their measurement argument to black-hole physics and holography.

- Authors: Ng, Y. Jack; van Dam, H..
- First submitted: 2002-09-06.
- Archived revision: gr-qc/0209021v1.
- arXiv: [gr-qc/0209021v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/0209021v1).
- DOI: [10.1088/0264-9381/20/2/311](https://doi.org/10.1088/0264-9381/20/2/311).
