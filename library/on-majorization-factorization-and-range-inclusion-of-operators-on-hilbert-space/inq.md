# On Majorization, Factorization, and Range Inclusion of Operators on Hilbert Space

Douglas proves the equivalence between range inclusion, bounded operator factorization, and a positive-operator majorization inequality. In the form used by the mass-gap programme, a closed subspace \(L\) lies in \(\operatorname{Ran}A^{1/2}\) exactly when its projection is bounded above by a scalar multiple of \(A\).
