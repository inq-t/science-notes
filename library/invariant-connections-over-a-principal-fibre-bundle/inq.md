# On Invariant Connections over a Principal Fibre Bundle

Hsien-Chung Wang classifies invariant connections on homogeneous principal bundles by equivariant linear data and relates their curvature and holonomy to the corresponding Lie-algebra decomposition.

- Author: Hsien-Chung Wang
- Journal: *Nagoya Mathematical Journal* **13**, 1--19
- Published: 1958
- DOI: [10.1017/S0027763000023461](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0027763000023461)
