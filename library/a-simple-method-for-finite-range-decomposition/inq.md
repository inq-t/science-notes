# A Simple Method for Finite Range Decomposition of Quadratic Forms and Gaussian Fields

Roland Bauerschmidt decomposes Green forms associated with a class of symmetric Dirichlet forms into positive semidefinite finite-range forms with improved regularity. The construction yields independent multiscale Gaussian fields with spatially localized correlations, using finite propagation speed for the wave equation and Chebyshev polynomials.

- Author: Roland Bauerschmidt.
- Published: *Probability Theory and Related Fields* **157** (2013), 817--845.
- DOI: [10.1007/s00440-012-0471-y](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00440-012-0471-y).
- arXiv: [1206.2212](https://arxiv.org/abs/1206.2212), submitted 2012; version 3 revised 2019.
