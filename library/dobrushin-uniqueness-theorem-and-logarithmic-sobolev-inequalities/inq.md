# Dobrushin Uniqueness Theorem and Logarithmic Sobolev Inequalities

Zegarlinski formulates a weak-dependence condition for local specifications on countable products whose single-site space is a Riemannian manifold or a two-point set, and proves uniqueness of the corresponding Gibbs measure together with a logarithmic Sobolev inequality. The result supplies a route from local conditional control and quantitative decay of interdependence to a functional inequality that remains meaningful in the infinite-volume limit.
