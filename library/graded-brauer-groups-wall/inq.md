# Graded Brauer Groups

C. T. C. Wall develops the Brauer group of central simple \(\mathbb Z/2\)-graded algebras and the graded Morita framework in which real Clifford-algebra classes exhibit eightfold periodicity.

- Author: C. T. C. Wall
- Journal: *Journal für die reine und angewandte Mathematik* **213**, 187--199
- Published: 1964
- DOI: [10.1515/crll.1964.213.187](https://doi.org/10.1515/crll.1964.213.187)
- Primary scan: [PDF](https://webhomes.maths.ed.ac.uk/~v1ranick/papers/wallbrauer.pdf)
