# A Remark on the Functoriality of the Connes--Takesaki Flow of Weights

Elliott extends the functoriality of the Falcone--Takesaki noncommutative flow of weights from isomorphisms to tempered normal unital homomorphisms, characterized by the existence of a normal faithful semifinite operator-valued weight onto the image. He also describes the more restricted covariant and contravariant functoriality available for the center-valued Connes--Takesaki flow.
