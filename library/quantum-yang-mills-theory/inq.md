# Quantum Yang--Mills Theory

Jaffe and Witten formulate the Clay Millennium problem of constructing a nontrivial four-dimensional quantum Yang--Mills theory for every compact simple gauge group and proving that its Hamiltonian has a strictly positive spectral gap above the vacuum. The required theory must satisfy axiomatic quantum-field-theoretic conditions and have the expected short-distance correspondence with asymptotically free Yang--Mills theory. For regulator-based constructive approaches, the article discusses the continuum and infinite-volume limits, emphasizes the possible role of a gap uniform in finite-volume approximations, and surveys lattice, renormalization, and duality-based approaches.
