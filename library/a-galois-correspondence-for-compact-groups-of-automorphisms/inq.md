# A Galois Correspondence for Compact Groups of Automorphisms of von Neumann Algebras

Masaki Izumi, Roberto Longo, and Sorin Popa prove a Galois correspondence for a minimal action of a compact group \(G\) on a factor \(M\): every intermediate von Neumann algebra between \(M^G\) and \(M\) is the fixed-point algebra of a closed subgroup. Their spectral analysis identifies the charged Hilbert spaces and finite-statistics endomorphisms associated with the irreducible representations of \(G\), including the infinite-index setting.

- Authors: Masaki Izumi, Roberto Longo, Sorin Popa
- Published: 1998
- Journal: *Journal of Functional Analysis* **155** (1), 25--63
- DOI: [10.1006/jfan.1997.3228](https://doi.org/10.1006/jfan.1997.3228)
- arXiv: [funct-an/9604004](https://arxiv.org/abs/funct-an/9604004)
