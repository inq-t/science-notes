# Tensor Product Systems of Hilbert Modules and Dilations of Completely Positive Semigroups

B. V. Rajarama Bhat and Michael Skeide develop a representation-free Hilbert-module dilation theory for unital completely positive semigroups. They construct tensor product systems of Hilbert modules with distinguished units, use them to dilate CP semigroups first to weak Markov flows and then to endomorphism semigroups, and identify the adjointable-operator algebra on a Hilbert module as the natural carrier of the resulting $E_0$-semigroup. The construction extends product-system methods beyond $B(H)$ and supplies CP-semigroup analogues of Paschke's results for individual CP maps.

**Authors:** B. V. Rajarama Bhat and Michael Skeide  
**Journal:** *Infinite Dimensional Analysis, Quantum Probability and Related Topics* **3** (2000), 519--575  
**DOI:** [10.1142/S0219025700000261](https://doi.org/10.1142/S0219025700000261)
