# Gravity and the Crossed Product

Witten studies $1/N$ corrections to an emergent type-$\mathrm{III}_1$ algebra in large-$N$ gauge theory and obtains a type-$\mathrm{II}_\infty$ algebra as its crossed product by the modular automorphism group. In this crossed-product setting, black-hole state entropy is defined up to a state-independent additive constant.
