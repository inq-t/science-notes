# Reciprocal Processes: A Measure-Theoretical Point of View

This survey presents Markov and reciprocal processes through a common measure-theoretic framework covering diffusion and jump processes. It emphasizes time symmetries and explains why mixtures of Markov bridges remain reciprocal but generally need not be Markov, with examples and counterexamples.

Christian Léonard, Sylvie Roelly, and Jean-Claude Zambrini, *Probability Surveys* **11** (2014), 237–269.
