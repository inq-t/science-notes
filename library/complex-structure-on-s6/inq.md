# A Compact Complex Threefold Fibred by Tori over the Projective Line, and the Six-Sphere

The manuscript completes the $(3,4,\infty)$ modular family of complex two-tori at its three special points to construct a compact connected complex threefold $X$ with a surjective holomorphic map $f:X\to\mathbb P^1$. Its cusp fibre is a non-normal degeneration with normalization the degree-six del Pezzo surface $dP_6$, and its two elliptic fibres are multiple with smooth bielliptic reductions. The manuscript computes that $X$ is simply connected with the integral homology of $S^6$, proves $X\cong S^6$ smoothly, and thereby transports its integrable complex structure to the standard six-sphere. Its final section addresses the incompatibility of this construction with the published Campana--Demailly--Peternell obstruction.
