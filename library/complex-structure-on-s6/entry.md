# The (3, 4, ∞) Modular Family of 2-Tori as a Complex Structure on $S^6$

The (3, 4, ∞) modular family of 2-tori, completed at its three special points, is a complex structure on $S^6$. The manuscript's Corollary 1.1 states the consequence: "The six-sphere $S^6$ carries an integrable complex structure, namely the one obtained by transporting the complex structure of $X$ along a diffeomorphism $X \to S^6$." Here $X$ is a compact connected complex threefold with a surjective holomorphic map $f : X \to B = \mathbb{P}^1$ whose fibres over the punctured base are complex 2-tori, whose fibre over the cusp is a non-normal degeneration with normalisation the degree-six del Pezzo surface $dP_6$, and whose fibres over the two elliptic points are multiple with smooth bielliptic reduction. Section 10 addresses the incompatibility of the Main Theorem with the published result of Campana, Demailly and Peternell.
