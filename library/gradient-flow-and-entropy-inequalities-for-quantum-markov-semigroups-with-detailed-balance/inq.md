# Gradient Flow and Entropy Inequalities for Quantum Markov Semigroups with Detailed Balance

Carlen and Maas study ergodic quantum Markov semigroups on finite-dimensional unital $C^*$-algebras satisfying detailed balance with respect to a faithful invariant state. They construct a noncommutative transport metric for which the dual semigroup is the gradient flow of relative entropy and derive entropy-decay and related functional inequalities from suitable convexity estimates.
