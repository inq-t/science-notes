# Instantons on the Six-Sphere and Twistors

Olaf Lechtenfeld and Alexander D. Popov study the nearly Kähler presentation \(S^6=G_2/SU(3)\), its reduced twistor space \(G_2/U(2)\), and the equivalence between Hermitian Yang--Mills connections on \(S^6\) and flat partial connections on the twistor space. Their explicit homogeneous formulas include the torsionful canonical \(SU(3)\) connection.

- Authors: Olaf Lechtenfeld, Alexander D. Popov
- Journal: *Journal of Mathematical Physics* **53**, 123506
- Published: 2012
- arXiv: [1206.4128](https://arxiv.org/abs/1206.4128)
- DOI: [10.1063/1.4765065](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.4765065)
