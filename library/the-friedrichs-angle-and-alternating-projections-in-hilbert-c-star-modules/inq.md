# The Friedrichs Angle and Alternating Projections in Hilbert \(C^*\)-Modules

Bram Mesland and Adam Rennie extend the alternating-projections theorem and the Friedrichs angle to complemented Hilbert-\(C^*\)-submodules. They prove, under the stated complementability conditions, that a Friedrichs cosine strictly below one is equivalent to closedness of the sum and controls convergence of alternating projections to the intersection.

- Authors: Bram Mesland, Adam Rennie
- Submitted: 2021-12-07
- Revised: 2021-12-15
