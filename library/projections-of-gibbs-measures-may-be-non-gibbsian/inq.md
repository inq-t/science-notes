# Projections of Gibbs Measures May Be Non-Gibbsian

Schonmann proves that projecting a low-temperature two-dimensional Ising Gibbs measure onto a one-dimensional layer can produce a non-Gibbsian measure. The result is a sharp warning that locality or Gibbsianness of a bulk measure does not automatically survive marginalization onto a boundary or retained subsystem.

## Metadata

- **Author:** Roberto H. Schonmann.
- **Published:** 1989.
- **Journal:** *Communications in Mathematical Physics* **124** (1), 1--7.
- **DOI:** [10.1007/BF01218465](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01218465).
- **Local artifacts:** none.

The opening paragraph states the paper's counterexample at the level used by the consuming module.
