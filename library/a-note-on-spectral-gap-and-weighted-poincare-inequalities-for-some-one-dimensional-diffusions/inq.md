# A Note on Spectral Gap and Weighted Poincaré Inequalities for Some One-Dimensional Diffusions

Michel Bonnefont, Aldéric Joulin, and Yutao Ma identify the optimal constant in a weighted Poincaré inequality with the spectral gap of the associated one-dimensional Markov diffusion and develop estimates and exact examples. Their generalized-Cauchy analysis distinguishes the gapless ordinary gradient form from weighted forms that regain a positive lower bound.

- Authors: Michel Bonnefont, Aldéric Joulin, Yutao Ma
- Submitted: 2014-11-21
- Published: 2016
- Journal: *ESAIM: Probability and Statistics* **20**, 18--29
- DOI: [10.1051/ps/2015019](https://doi.org/10.1051/ps/2015019)
- arXiv: [1411.5982](https://arxiv.org/abs/1411.5982)
- Source: [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/1411.5982v1)
