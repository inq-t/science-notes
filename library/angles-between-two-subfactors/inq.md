# Angles Between Two Subfactors

Takashi Sano and Yasuo Watatani introduce the angle and opposite-angle operators for pairs of subfactors of a type \(II_1\) factor using their Jones projections. They relate trivial angle spectrum to commuting and co-commuting squares, calculate several families, and show finiteness of the angle set under finite common-index hypotheses.

- Authors: Takashi Sano, Yasuo Watatani
- Published: 1994
- Journal: *Journal of Operator Theory* **32** (2), 209--241
- Article and full text: [Journal of Operator Theory](https://jot.theta.ro/jot/archive/1994-032-002/1994-032-002-001.html)
- Local artifacts: none
