# Compact Hypergroups from Discrete Subfactors

Marcel Bischoff, Simone Del Vecchio, and Luca Giorgetti associate a canonical compact hypergroup of bimodular quantum operations with every irreducible local discrete Type III subfactor and recover the smaller factor as its fixed points. The work treats infinite-index inclusions, shows that the dual canonical endomorphism is a finite direct sum exactly in the finite-index case, and identifies compact groups in the depth-two case.

- Authors: Marcel Bischoff, Simone Del Vecchio, Luca Giorgetti
- Published: 2021
- Journal: *Journal of Functional Analysis* **281** (1), 109004
- DOI: [10.1016/j.jfa.2021.109004](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfa.2021.109004)
- arXiv: [2007.12384](https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.12384)
- Local artifacts: none
