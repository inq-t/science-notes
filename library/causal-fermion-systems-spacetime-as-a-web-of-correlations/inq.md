# Causal Fermion Systems: Spacetime as the web of correlations of a many-body quantum system

Patrick Fischer and Claudio F. Paganini interpret spacetime in causal fermion systems as a network of correlations in a many-body quantum system. They explain the appearance of a spacetime background through a limit involving increasingly many states. For a specified class of systems containing the Minkowski-vacuum continuum limit, they relate causal-action minimization to the minimization of fluctuations in causal structure.
