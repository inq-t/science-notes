# Universally Valid Reformulation of the Heisenberg Uncertainty Principle on Noise and Disturbance in Measurement

Masanao Ozawa distinguishes measurement noise and disturbance from state uncertainties. He derives a more general trade-off and constructs a measurement model violating the familiar noise-times-disturbance lower bound, while preserving the broader relation. The paper proposes an optical-quadrature implementation using linear optical devices.

- Author: Masanao Ozawa.
- Published: *Physical Review A* **67**, 042105 (2003).
- DOI: [10.1103/PhysRevA.67.042105](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.67.042105).
- arXiv: [quant-ph/0207121](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0207121), submitted 19 July 2002.
