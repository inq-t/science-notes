# The Uncertainty Principle

Robertson gives a general formulation of the uncertainty principle for two Hermitian quantum-mechanical variables. Defining each uncertainty as the root-mean-square deviation from its mean, he uses the Schwarz inequality to obtain \(\Delta A\,\Delta B\geq \tfrac12\lvert\langle AB-BA\rangle\rvert\), recovers Heisenberg's result for conjugate variables, and applies the bound to angular momentum, where an example with \(m=l\) attains equality.
