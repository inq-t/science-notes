# Exact Scheme Independence

Jose I. Latorre and Tim R. Morris formulate exact renormalization-group scheme changes as field redefinitions. In this formulation the kernel controlling mode integration acts as a field connection along the flow, so covariance under a scalar reparameterization is weaker than covariance under the full family of admissible scale-dependent carrier identifications.
