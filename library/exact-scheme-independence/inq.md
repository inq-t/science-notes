# Exact Scheme Independence

Jose I. Latorre and Tim R. Morris formulate scheme independence of exact renormalization-group equations through field redefinitions, including changes of cutoff function. Their formulation makes the equations covariant under these transformations, with the integration kernel acting as a field connection along the flow.

## Metadata

- **Authors:** Jose I. Latorre, Tim R. Morris.
- **Published:** 2000.
- **Journal:** *Journal of High Energy Physics* **11** (2000), 004.
- **arXiv:** [hep-th/0008123v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0008123v2).
- **DOI:** [10.1088/1126-6708/2000/11/004](https://doi.org/10.1088/1126-6708/2000/11/004).
