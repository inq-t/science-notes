# Visual Horizons in World-Models

This paper unifies and generalizes results on visual horizons by defining a horizon as a frontier between observable and unobservable things. It distinguishes event horizons from particle horizons, illustrates models possessing either, both, or neither type, and formulates the analysis using proper distance, cosmic time, and the Robertson-Walker line element.
