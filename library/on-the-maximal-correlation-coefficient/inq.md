# On the Maximal Correlation Coefficient

Yaming Yu gives a short self-contained proof of the
Gebelein--Lancaster theorem that the Hirschfeld--Gebelein--Renyi maximal
correlation of a bivariate normal pair equals the absolute value of its
ordinary correlation. The article also proves general properties of maximal
correlation and evaluates it for overlapping partial sums of independent
random variables.

**Author:** Yaming Yu  
**Journal:** *Statistics & Probability Letters* **78** (2008), 1072--1075  
**DOI:** [10.1016/j.spl.2007.10.006](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.spl.2007.10.006)  
**Article:** [ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0167715207003689)
