# Complete Monotonicity for Inverse Powers of Some Combinatorially Defined Polynomials

Scott and Sokal study complete monotonicity of inverse polynomial powers, including determinant polynomials, and applications to graphs and matroids. They relate positivity to Laplace transforms and Riesz distributions on Euclidean Jordan algebras. The paper also classifies determinant powers that define positive kernels for addition on a symmetric cone.

Alexander D. Scott and Alan D. Sokal, *Acta Mathematica* **213** (2014), 323–392.
