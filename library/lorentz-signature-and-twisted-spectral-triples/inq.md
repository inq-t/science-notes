# Lorentz Signature and Twisted Spectral Triples

Agostino Devastato, Shane Farnsworth, Fedele Lizzi, and Pierre Martinetti study a twist of the Standard-Model spectral triple, show how it yields a Krein-space structure associated with Lorentzian signature, and relate it to Wick rotation. They also analyze the corresponding fermionic and bosonic actions.

- Authors: Agostino Devastato, Shane Farnsworth, Fedele Lizzi, Pierre Martinetti
- Submitted: 2017-10-13
- Revised: 2018-03-18
- Published: *Journal of High Energy Physics* **03** (2018), 089
- DOI: [10.1007/JHEP03(2018)089](https://doi.org/10.1007/JHEP03(2018)089)
- arXiv: [1710.04965](https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.04965)
- Local artifacts: none
