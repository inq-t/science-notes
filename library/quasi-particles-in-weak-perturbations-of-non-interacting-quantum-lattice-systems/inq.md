# Quasi-Particles in Weak Perturbations of Non-Interacting Quantum Lattice Systems

Yarotsky studies weak perturbations of quantum lattice systems with a nondegenerate gapped product ground state. Under additional assumptions on isolated excited levels, the perturbed system has quasiparticle subspaces; the paper also constructs asymptotically free scattering states.

## Metadata

- **Author:** D. A. Yarotsky.
- **Preprint:** [arXiv:math-ph/0411042](https://arxiv.org/abs/math-ph/0411042), submitted November 11, 2004.
- **Author manuscript:** [HTML full text](https://arxiv.org/html/math-ph/0411042); [TeX source](https://arxiv.org/src/math-ph/0411042).
- **Local artifacts:** no manuscript payload stored.

The opening paragraph paraphrases the abstract. Section 1, Theorems 1--3, states the finite-volume stability and thermodynamic-limit results; Section 2 reviews their perturbative proofs. Theorems 4--5 concern quasiparticles and scattering.
