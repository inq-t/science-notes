# Ergodic States on Type III\(_1\) Factors and Ergodic Actions

Amine Marrakchi and Stefaan Vaes prove that a nontrivial von Neumann algebra with separable predual admits a faithful normal state with trivial centralizer exactly when it is a type III\(_1\) factor. They further prove that these ergodic states form a dense \(G_\delta\) set and relate the result, through Connes' Radon--Nikodym cocycle theorem, to ergodic cocycle perturbations of outer actions.
