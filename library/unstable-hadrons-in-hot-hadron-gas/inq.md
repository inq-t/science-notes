# Unstable Hadrons in Hot Hadron Gas: In the Laboratory and in the Early Universe

Kuznetsova and Rafelski derive kinetic master equations for unstable-particle decay and inverse formation in a thermal bath. They calculate thermal reaction and relaxation rates for processes including ππ↔ρ and π⁰↔γγ and apply the framework to pion and muon freeze-out in the early universe, showing why a short vacuum lifetime does not by itself imply disappearance from an equilibrated bath.
