# Impossibility of Spontaneously Breaking Local Symmetries

Elitzur argues that a symmetric gauge theory without gauge fixing cannot spontaneously break a local symmetry, and demonstrates the argument in a simple lattice system of Abelian gauge fields.

## Metadata

- **Author:** S. Elitzur.
- **Received:** 29 July 1975.
- **Published:** 15 December 1975.
- **Journal:** *Physical Review D* **12** (12), 3978--3982.
