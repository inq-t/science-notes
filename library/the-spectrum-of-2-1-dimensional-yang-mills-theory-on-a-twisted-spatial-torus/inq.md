# The Spectrum of 2+1 Dimensional Yang-Mills Theory on a Twisted Spatial Torus

Margarita Garcia Perez, Antonio Gonzalez-Arroyo, Masanori Koren, and Masanori Okawa use perturbation theory and lattice calculations to study the twisted-torus spectrum. They distinguish nonzero-electric-flux torelons and their neutral pairs from the zero-flux glueball that emerges toward large volume, clarifying why a twist-induced finite-volume threshold is not by itself an infinite-volume glueball mass gap.

- Authors: Margarita Garcia Perez, Antonio Gonzalez-Arroyo, Masanori Koren, Masanori Okawa
- Published: *Journal of High Energy Physics* **07** (2018), 169
- DOI: [10.1007/JHEP07(2018)169](https://doi.org/10.1007/JHEP07(2018)169)
- Preprint and source: [arXiv:1807.03481](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.03481)
