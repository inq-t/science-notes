# Approximate Tensorization of Entropy at High Temperature

Pietro Caputo, Georg Menz, and Prasad Tetali prove approximate tensorization of entropy for weakly dependent variables under explicit high-temperature influence conditions. The resulting volume-independent entropy inequality yields logarithmic-Sobolev and, after quadratic linearization, Poincare estimates for the associated heat-bath dynamics.

- Authors: Pietro Caputo, Georg Menz, Prasad Tetali
- Published: *Annales de la Faculte des sciences de Toulouse* **24** (2015), 691--716
- DOI: [10.5802/afst.1460](https://doi.org/10.5802/afst.1460)
- arXiv and source: [1405.0608](https://arxiv.org/abs/1405.0608)
- Local artifacts: none
