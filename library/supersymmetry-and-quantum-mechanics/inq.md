# Supersymmetry and Quantum Mechanics

Fred Cooper, Avinash Khare, and Uday Sukhatme review supersymmetric quantum mechanics, including Hamiltonian factorization, partner potentials, shape invariance, exact spectra, scattering amplitudes, isospectral transformations, reflectionless systems, and subtleties of Witten-index prescriptions with continuum spectrum. The review explicitly treats the superpotential \(W=n\tanh x\), whose partners are shifted Pöschl--Teller potentials and whose hierarchy terminates in a free reflectionless operator.

- Authors: Fred Cooper, Avinash Khare, Uday Sukhatme
- Submitted: 1994-05-04; revised 1994-05-05
- Journal: *Physics Reports* **251** (1995), 267--385
- arXiv: [hep-th/9405029](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9405029)
