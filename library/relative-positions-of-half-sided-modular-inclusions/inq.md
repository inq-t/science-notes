# Relative Positions of Half-Sided Modular Inclusions

Ian Koot characterizes inclusions among half-sided modular inclusions inside a common standard subspace by passing to their associated standard pairs, spectral subspaces, and operator-valued inner functions. The paper shows that a half-sided relative inclusion makes the associated positive one-parameter groups commute and gives examples in which an ordinary relative inclusion is not itself half-sided modular.

- Submitted: 2025-03-23
- Published online: 2025-10-29
