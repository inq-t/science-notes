# Complex Structures on $S^6$

Philip Engel gives a self-contained geometric proof that a one-parameter family of compact complex threefolds is diffeomorphic to the standard six-sphere. The construction begins with a rational elliptic surface having singular fibers of types $IV^*$, $III$, and $I_1$, lifts translation by a Mordell--Weil section to a punctured line bundle, takes a properly discontinuous quotient whose smooth fibers are complex two-tori, performs logarithmic modifications at the order-three and order-four fibers, and uses a Mumford fan at the cusp. Suitable torsion choices make the resulting threefold simply connected with the integral homology of $S^6$; Smale recognition and $\Theta_6=0$ then identify it diffeomorphically with the standard sphere.
