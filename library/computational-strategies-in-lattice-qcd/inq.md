# Computational Strategies in Lattice QCD

Luscher introduces computational methods for lattice QCD with emphasis on their theoretical foundations. The lectures cover quark-propagator solvers, gauge-field simulation algorithms, variance reduction and statistical error analysis.

## Metadata

- **Author:** Martin Luscher.
