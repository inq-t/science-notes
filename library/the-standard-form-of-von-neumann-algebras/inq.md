# The Standard Form of von Neumann Algebras

Haagerup constructs the standard form of an arbitrary von Neumann algebra and proves its uniqueness: every normal isomorphism between standard forms has a unique unitary implementation that intertwines the algebras and modular conjugations and maps one self-dual cone onto the other. In particular, automorphisms of an algebra in standard form have canonical unitary implementations.
