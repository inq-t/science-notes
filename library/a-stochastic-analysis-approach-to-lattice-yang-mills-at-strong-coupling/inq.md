# A Stochastic Analysis Approach to Lattice Yang--Mills at Strong Coupling

Shen, Zhu, and Zhu study Langevin dynamics for Wilson lattice Yang--Mills measures with gauge groups $SO(N)$ and $SU(N)$. Under explicit strong-coupling conditions they prove uniqueness and exponential ergodicity, volume-uniform logarithmic-Sobolev and Poincare inequalities, an infinite-volume measure, large-$N$ factorization statements, and exponential decay of correlations for a broad class of observables.
