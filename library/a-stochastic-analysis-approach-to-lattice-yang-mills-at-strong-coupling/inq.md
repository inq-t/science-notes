# A Stochastic Analysis Approach to Lattice Yang–Mills at Strong Coupling

Shen, Zhu, and Zhu study Langevin dynamics for Wilson lattice Yang–Mills measures with gauge groups SO(N) and SU(N). Under explicit strong-coupling conditions they prove uniqueness and exponential ergodicity, logarithmic-Sobolev and Poincare inequalities uniform in volume, large-N factorization for Wilson loops, and exponential decay of correlations for smooth local observables.

- Authors: Hao Shen, Rongchan Zhu, and Xiangchan Zhu
- Published: *Communications in Mathematical Physics* **400**, 805–851 (2023); online 2022-12-23
- DOI: [10.1007/s00220-022-04609-1](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00220-022-04609-1)
- Author preprint: [arXiv:2204.12737v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.12737v1), submitted 2022-04-27
