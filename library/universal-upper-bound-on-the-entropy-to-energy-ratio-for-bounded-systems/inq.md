# Universal Upper Bound on the Entropy-to-Energy Ratio for Bounded Systems

Bekenstein presents evidence for an upper bound \(S/E\leq2\pi k_BR/(\hbar c)\) for a system of effective radius \(R\), motivated by the generalized second law and supplemented by statistical arguments. He argues that black holes attain the bound and hence have the maximum entropy compatible with a given mass-energy and size within the proposed comparison class.
