# On Unitary Representations of the Inhomogeneous Lorentz Group

Wigner classifies the continuous unitary representations up to phase of the inhomogeneous Lorentz group acting on quantum-mechanical state rays. He reduces the phase ambiguity to two-valued representations, decomposes the problem by Lorentz-invariant classes of translation momentum, and characterizes each class through representations of the subgroup fixing a representative momentum. In the positive timelike subclass, the irreducible representations are labelled by \(P>0\) and \(j\in\{0,\tfrac12,1,\ldots\}\); for the null subclass, the paper obtains discrete-label representations and additional infinite-dimensional cases, including a continuous-spin representation.
