# Asymptotic Properties of Fields and Space-Times

Penrose outlines a conformal technique for treating infinity as a regular boundary of an auxiliary spacetime. The construction gives geometric definitions of asymptotic flatness and radiation, yields peeling behavior for curvature, supports energy-momentum and radiation-loss laws, relates finite and asymptotic characteristic initial-value problems, and recovers the Bondi-Metzner-Sachs group.
