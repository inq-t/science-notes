# The International System of Units (SI)

The BIPM's SI Brochure defines the International System of Units through seven defining constants and sets out its base and derived units, dimensions, prefixes, and notation. It explains how a quantity value combines a numerical value with a unit and records the historical decisions and practical conventions underlying the system.
