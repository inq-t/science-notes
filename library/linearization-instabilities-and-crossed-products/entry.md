# Linearization (in)stabilities and crossed products

De Vuyst, Eccles, Höhn, and Kirklin analyze the second-order boost constraints used in modular crossed-product constructions through linearization stability and covariant phase space. Their treatment gives an essentially unambiguous justification in spatially closed settings, while showing that boundaries and the absence of isometries make the justification depend on which second-order observables are retained.
