# Axioms for Euclidean Green's Functions II

Osterwalder and Schrader give new conditions for Euclidean Green functions to admit analytic continuation to a relativistic field theory. The reconstruction results extend and correct their earlier paper.

- Authors: Konrad Osterwalder and Robert Schrader
- Published: *Communications in Mathematical Physics* **42**, 281–305 (1975)
- DOI: [10.1007/BF01608978](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01608978)
- Appendix: Stephen Summers
- Preceding article: [[library/axioms-for-euclidean-greens-functions/inq|Axioms for Euclidean Green's Functions]] (1973)
