# Spatial Volume Dependence for 2+1 Dimensional SU(N) Yang-Mills Theory

Margarita Garcia Perez, Antonio Gonzalez-Arroyo, and Masanori Okawa analyze \(SU(N)\) Yang--Mills theory on a spatial two-torus with irreducible 't Hooft twist. The twist combines ordinary momentum and color momentum in units of \(2\pi/(NL)\), organizes electric-flux sectors, and permits perturbative and nonperturbative comparison across volume and rank.

- Authors: Margarita Garcia Perez, Antonio Gonzalez-Arroyo, Masanori Okawa
- Published: *Journal of High Energy Physics* **09** (2013), 003
- DOI: [10.1007/JHEP09(2013)003](https://doi.org/10.1007/JHEP09(2013)003)
- Preprint and source: [arXiv:1307.5254](https://arxiv.org/abs/1307.5254)
