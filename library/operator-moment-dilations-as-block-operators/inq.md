# Operator Moment Dilations as Block Operators

Bhat, Ghatak and Pamula study when a sequence of bounded operators is the compression of powers of a single operator on a larger Hilbert space. They discuss existence and uniqueness of moment dilations and give block-operator representations for self-adjoint, isometric and unitary cases, including generalized contraction dilations.

B. V. Rajarama Bhat, Anindya Ghatak and Santhosh Kumar Pamula.
arXiv:2302.13873, version 2, submitted 3 February 2025.
The version-2 rendered manuscript uses the title *Block decompositions of
operator moment dilations*.
