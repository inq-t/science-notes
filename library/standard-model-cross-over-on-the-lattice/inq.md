# Standard Model Cross-over on the Lattice

Michela D'Onofrio and Kari Rummukainen study the electroweak thermal crossover at the physical Higgs mass using lattice simulations of an effective \(SU(2)\times U(1)\) gauge--Higgs theory. They determine the gauge-invariant Higgs condensate, its susceptibility, thermodynamic quantities, and screening masses, with continuum extrapolation. The transition is a smooth crossover, with a pseudocritical temperature near \(159.5\) GeV.
