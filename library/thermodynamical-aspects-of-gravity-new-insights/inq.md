# Thermodynamical Aspects of Gravity: New Insights

Padmanabhan reviews horizon temperature and entropy, their relation to Noether charge in diffeomorphism-invariant theories, thermodynamic readings of gravitational field equations, and variational routes which obtain those field equations from entropy extremization.
