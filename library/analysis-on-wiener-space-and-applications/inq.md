# Analysis on Wiener Space and Applications

Üstünel gives a graduate introduction to analysis on Wiener space and the stochastic calculus of variations. The text develops stochastic integration, Malliavin derivatives, Ornstein–Uhlenbeck operators, functional inequalities, and transformations of measure, with applications to optimal transport and stochastic analysis on Lie-group path spaces.

- **Author:** A. S. Üstünel.
