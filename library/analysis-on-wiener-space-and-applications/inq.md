# Analysis on Wiener Space and Applications

Üstünel presents analysis on Wiener space through stochastic integration, Malliavin derivatives, Ornstein–Uhlenbeck operators, functional inequalities, and measure transformations. The text includes applications to optimal transport and stochastic analysis on paths in compact Lie groups.

A. S. Üstünel, *Analysis on Wiener Space and Applications*, arXiv:1003.1649v2
(2015), a revised book-length manuscript.
