# Approximate Recoverability and Relative Entropy II

Thomas Faulkner and Stefan Hollands prove a strengthened data-processing inequality for 2-positive channels between general von Neumann algebras. The lower bound is expressed through fidelity to a recovery channel, extending approximate-recoverability results beyond finite-dimensional and semifinite settings. For a state-preserving conditional expectation, their specialization gives an exact relative-entropy chain rule between restriction loss and recovery through the expectation.

- Authors: Thomas Faulkner, Stefan Hollands
- Published: 2022
- Journal: *Letters in Mathematical Physics* **112**, 26
- DOI: [10.1007/s11005-022-01510-9](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11005-022-01510-9)
- arXiv: [2010.05513](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.05513)
