# Evolution of Antibaryon Abundances in the Early Universe and in Heavy-Ion Collisions

Satarov, Mishustin, and Greiner study baryon--antibaryon production and annihilation with kinetic rate equations in expanding homogeneous matter. For the observed primordial baryon asymmetry, their model keeps baryons and antibaryons nearly symmetric above roughly (50\,\mathrm{MeV}), then exposes the conserved baryon excess as the equilibrium pair density falls; chemical freeze-out of the residual antibaryon population occurs only at substantially lower temperature. The calculation distinguishes the disappearance of cancelable pairs from the persistence of net baryon number.
