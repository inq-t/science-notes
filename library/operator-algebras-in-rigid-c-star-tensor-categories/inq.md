# Operator Algebras in Rigid C-Star Tensor Categories

Corey Jones and David Penneys define (C^*)- and (W^*)-algebra objects internal to a rigid (C^*)-tensor category. They establish categorical analogues of the Gelfand--Naimark, bicommutant, GNS, completely-positive-map, and Stinespring theorems and formulate approximation and rigidity properties for connected (W^*)-algebra objects.
