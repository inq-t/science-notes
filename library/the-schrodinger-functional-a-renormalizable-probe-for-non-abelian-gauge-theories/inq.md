# The Schrodinger Functional: A Renormalizable Probe for Non-Abelian Gauge Theories

Luscher, Narayanan, Weisz, and Wolff formulate the Schrodinger functional for lattice gauge theories without matter and argue that gauge invariance prevents extra boundary counterterms, giving it a well-defined continuum limit in their perturbative analysis. They develop it as a finite-volume boundary amplitude and a probe of the scale dependence of the renormalized gauge coupling.
