# Poincare and Transportation Inequalities for Gibbs Measures under the Dobrushin Uniqueness Condition

Wu proves explicit sharp spectral-gap and transportation inequalities for Gibbs measures under a Dobrushin uniqueness condition. In particular, the spectral radius of a Wasserstein interdependence matrix controls approximate tensorization of variance and turns uniform conditional Poincare bounds into a global Poincare inequality.
