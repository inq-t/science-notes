# A Note on Continuous Entropy

Roberto Longo and Edward Witten relate Segal entropy for semifinite von Neumann algebras to Araki relative entropy and bound entropy increase across a finite-index inclusion by the logarithm of the index. For inclusions of infinite-dimensional factors the bound is optimal, and the paper records the dual relative-entropy identity in which the losses for an expectation and its commutant-dual sum to the log index.
