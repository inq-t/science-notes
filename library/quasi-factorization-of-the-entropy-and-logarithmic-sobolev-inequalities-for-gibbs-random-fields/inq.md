# Quasi-Factorization of the Entropy and Logarithmic Sobolev Inequalities for Gibbs Random Fields

Filippo Cesi proves that entropy approximately factorizes across two weakly dependent sigma-algebras and applies the result to finite-range summable Gibbs interactions. Dobrushin--Shlosman complete analyticity then implies uniform logarithmic-Sobolev inequalities, without requiring a topology on the single-spin space, so both discrete and continuous spins are included.
