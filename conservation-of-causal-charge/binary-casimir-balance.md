# The Binary Casimir Balance

A self-adjoint involution carries an exact quadratic invariant: its squared expectation and its variance always sum to one. In the balanced binary exponential family, these two terms become \(\tanh^2\theta\) and \(\operatorname{sech}^2\theta\). This is the rigorous algebraic seed of causal-charge conservation, but it is a Casimir decomposition inside a reduced channel—not yet a Noether law, a theory of facts, or a derivation of gravity.

## The involution identity

Let \(\mathcal A\) be a unital C\(^*\)-algebra, let \(\omega\) be a state on \(\mathcal A\), and let \(Q=Q^*\in\mathcal A\) satisfy

$$
Q^2=\mathbf 1.
$$

Define the polarization and variance

$$
\eta_\omega:=\omega(Q),
\qquad
\operatorname{Var}_\omega(Q)
:=\omega(Q^2)-\omega(Q)^2.
$$

Then

$$
\boxed{
1=\eta_\omega^2+\operatorname{Var}_\omega(Q).
}
$$

This follows immediately from \(\omega(Q^2)=\omega(\mathbf1)=1\). Positivity of the state also gives

$$
-1\leq\eta_\omega\leq1,
\qquad
0\leq\operatorname{Var}_\omega(Q)\leq1.
$$

The equation is a decomposition of a fixed second moment. Calling it a conservation law means only that the representation invariant \(Q^2=1\) is unchanged while its mean and fluctuation contributions vary with the state.

## The balanced exponential family

Now suppose the physical reduction is the two-dimensional commutative quotient \(\operatorname{span}\{P_+,P_-\}\cong\mathbb C^2\) generated by the spectral projectors

$$
P_\pm=\frac{\mathbf1\pm Q}{2}
$$

with equal reference weights. On this quotient, with its canonical trace, the exponential family is

$$
\rho_\theta
=\frac{e^{\theta Q}}{2\cosh\theta},
\qquad
p_\pm(\theta)
=\frac{e^{\pm\theta}}{2\cosh\theta}.
$$

Therefore

$$
\eta(\theta)
=\operatorname{tr}(\rho_\theta Q)
=p_+-p_-
=\tanh\theta,
$$

and

$$
\operatorname{Var}_{\rho_\theta}(Q)
=1-\eta^2
=\operatorname{sech}^2\theta.
$$

Because this is a commuting exponential family, the score is \(Q-\eta\mathbf1\), and its BKM metric component is the same variance:

$$
G^{\mathrm{BKM}}_{\theta\theta}
=\operatorname{Var}_{\rho_\theta}(Q)
=\operatorname{sech}^2\theta.
$$

Hence

$$
\boxed{
1=\eta^2+G^{\mathrm{BKM}}_{\theta\theta}.
}
$$

The balance assumption matters. The relation \(Q^2=1\) alone gives the mean--variance identity, but unequal reference weights shift the canonical center and change the displayed \(2\cosh\theta\) family. The physical existence of a balanced normal quotient is an identification in [[causal-scale-master-v8/entry|Causal Scale Dynamics v8]], not a consequence of there being two null-normal rays.

## Claim boundary

- \(\eta\) is an actual fact rather than an expectation value;
- variance is Shannon entropy, von Neumann entropy, or ontological information;
- the binary quotient is the full causal-wall state;
- BKM susceptibility is gravitational area or curvature; or
- a spacetime current is conserved.
