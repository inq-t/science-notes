# The Causal-Charge Theorem Programme

Conservation of causal charge becomes a physical law only when its presentation symmetry, fact-forming maps, common moment map, state--geometry soldering, and cosmological reductions are independently constructed. The programme below assigns a success condition and a failure condition to each bridge so that an attractive remainder cannot be called conserved merely by definition.

## CC--T1: construct the presentation groupoid

Specify the objects—causal cuts, observer contexts, algebra--state fibers, scale presentations—and the admissible reversible arrows among them. Define the accessible observable functor and prove that the proposed arrows coincide with its operational indiscernibilities to the claimed accuracy.

**Success:** a groupoid or stack with explicit composition, stabilizers, transport, and descent conditions.

**Failure:** the equivalence depends on undeclared observables, changes physical predictions, or cannot compare states on different cuts.

## CC--T2: derive the causal representation and binary reduction

Construct the physical symmetry representation, its invariant form, and any reduced normal generator \(Q\). Derive rather than name the balanced quotient, its channel multiplicity, and the reflection action. [[binary-information-geometry/entry|Binary information geometry]] supplies the exact reduced results once these inputs exist; it does not supply the reduction itself.

**Success:** the full wall theory has a controlled infrared sector with \(Q^2=1\), balanced weights, a finite BKM tangent, and quantified errors from discarded modes.

**Failure:** extra light modes survive, balance is not selected, the invariant form is indefinite, or the BKM profile is not \(\operatorname{sech}^2\).

## CC--T3: construct the diagonal causal symmetry

Define one normalized boost or dilation generator acting on the nongravitational state, gravitational, and record sectors. Construct their moment maps or covariant charges in one common target. A finer wall-state/bulk-matter split is optional and must be gauge compatible.

**Success:** a Ward identity or covariant phase-space theorem yields

$$
Q_\xi[\Sigma_2]-Q_\xi[\Sigma_1]+
\mathcal F_\xi[W]=0.
$$

**Failure:** the sectors require unrelated generators, arbitrary relative normalization, anomalous nonconservation, or gauge-dependent charges.

## CC--T4: construct factive descent

Supply the context rule, instrument, actual outcome slot, conditional state, record algebra, and one-sided record-preserving evolution. Show how local pointings glue into one common history.

**Success:** facts and records are physical objects in the model rather than informal readings of expectation values, and their charge contribution closes the balance.

**Failure:** restriction is mistaken for outcome selection, records are unstable, or observer histories admit no coherent orientation.

## CC--T5: prove the state--geometry weld

Construct the measure-valued bilinear form \(\mu^{\mathrm{desc}}_{v,w}\) required by [[program-core/descent-response-geometry|descent-response geometry]]; prove a same-tangent relation to gravitational canonical energy; and derive its Radon--Nikodym density \(\boldsymbol\chi(v,w;p)\) with respect to independently normalized area.

**Success:** the localized response and the gravitational form satisfy the covariant homothety

$$
\mathrm d\mu^{\mathrm{desc}}_{v,w}
=Z_g\,
\mathrm d\mu^{\mathrm{grav,resp}}_{\mathfrak S_\Sigma v,
\mathfrak S_\Sigma w}
$$

for all declared physical tangents in one common scheme. On a universal Einstein scale channel, an independently returned \(\chi_*\) additionally satisfies

$$
\mathfrak r_{\Sigma,N}(p)=1,
\qquad
\chi_N(p)=\chi_*=\eta_{\mathrm E},
\qquad
G_{\mathrm{pred}}=\frac{c^3}{4\hbar\chi_*},
$$

without importing the measured \(G\).

**Failure:** the density is nonlocal, scheme-, species-, state-, or observer-dependent without a controlled law, or the compared tangents differ.

## CC--T6: derive the cosmological shadows

Obtain the scale cocycle, unit or nonunit slope, binary transfer current, [[conformal-scale-geometry/horizon-allocation|horizon connection split]], and a covariantly conserved spacetime response from the constructed charge system rather than from the target expansion history.

**Success:** the binary Casimir, horizon allocation, and source pulse arise as reductions of one off-shell or coupled construction.

**Failure:** the target \(H(N)\) is used to define the state path; the horizon identity remains only a rearrangement; or no stable hyperbolic perturbation system realizes the background source.

## CC--T7: test universality and the cosmos as a whole

Compare the same areal modulus and balance law across local focusing, laboratory gravity, FLRW background expansion, growth and lensing, gravitational waves, horizon entropy, and record-bearing perturbations. Test the pulse's width, oriented endpoint index, mixed homogeneous--inhomogeneous blocks, and higher correlations.

**Success:** several dimensionless relations are fixed independently and agree across registers with bounded corrections.

**Failure:** each sector requires a separately fitted effective \(G\), the pulse shape changes arbitrarily, local conservation or causal propagation fails, or the model cannot preserve the imported GR/QFT fiber described in [[compatible-with-existing-physics/local-physics-interface|the local physics interface]].

## Promotion rule

The current hierarchy is:

| Status | Content |
|---|---|
| Exact under stated finite or geometric hypotheses | observational quotient; involutive Casimir; balanced binary identities after reduction; two-channel conversion; compact Casimir decomposition; data processing; product moment-map theorem; [[conformal-scale-geometry/horizon-allocation|flat-FLRW horizon allocation]] |
| Conditional established routes | dilation-current capacity ratio; horizon thermodynamic derivation of Einstein focusing; controlled BKM--canonical-energy examples |
| Physical conjectures | one causal charge; factive descent; state--geometry weld; common origin of the cosmological shadows |
| Interpretive | causal individuation, the price of facthood, Being/Nothing/Becoming language |

No claim advances merely because its dimensions match, its formula is aesthetically rigid, or a background fit is favorable. Promotion requires the objects, maps, symmetry, normalization, and failure tests appropriate to its mathematical type.
