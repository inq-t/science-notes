# Being, Nothing, and Becoming

The ancient formula “Being is balanced between Nothing and Becoming” can be given a disciplined mathematical reading. Being is the invariant relational structure; Nothing is the kernel of accessible distinction or the absence of a preferred pointing; Becoming is the oriented conversion or record-forming process. None of these identifications should be mistaken for a literal definition of metaphysical being, nonbeing, or time.

## Being as invariant structure

The most conservative mathematical image of Being is not a static substance. It is what remains invariant across admissible transformations and presentations. In the binary sector,

$$
Q^2=\mathbf1
$$

is such an invariant. For the balanced commuting exponential family of [[binary-casimir-balance]],

$$
1=\eta^2+G^{\mathrm{BKM}}_{\theta\theta}
$$

shows how its statewise representation is divided between resolved polarization and residual susceptibility. For a general state of the involution, the exact second term is \(\operatorname{Var}(Q)\); identifying it with this BKM component requires the stated family and tangent.

In a fuller theory, Being would be represented not merely by the number one but by the causal groupoid, its Casimir data, and the total moment map proposed in [[causal-individuation-balance]]. It is structural identity through change.

## Nothing as indiscernibility, not emptiness

The pre-observable ground should not be called “nothing” because it has no classical point. A noncommutative algebra, a probability measure, an unpointed torsor, and an empty set are radically different objects.

The defensible mathematical analogue is narrower:

$$
\text{Nothing}
\rightsquigarrow
\ker(\text{accessible distinction})
$$

or the absence of a privileged section. Transformations in this kernel change a presentation without changing any declared readout. [[indiscernibility-and-the-noether-gap]] explains why this creates a quotient or groupoid rather than literal nonbeing.

There may nevertheless be rich stabilizer, cocycle, and correlation data in what is inaccessible. The causal wall is therefore not the border of mathematical existence; it is the boundary of a particular mode of fact-making access.

## Becoming as conversion current

In [[two-channel-conversion-law|the two-channel theorem]], a fixed total allocation obeys

$$
1=m^2+\frac1\nu\frac{\mathrm dm}{\mathrm dN}.
$$

On the unit branch,

$$
\boxed{
1=m^2+\frac{\mathrm dm}{\mathrm dN}.
}
$$

The term \(m^2\) represents settled imbalance; the term \(\mathrm dm/\mathrm dN=\operatorname{sech}^2(N-N_c)\) is the rate at which the allocation changes. In that restricted sense, the pulse is the **energy of becoming**: not an energy in joules, but the normalized current of transition inside a conserved binary capacity.

A genuine temporal Becoming requires more. The coordinate \(N\) is logarithmic scale, not by itself an arrow of time. Records must accumulate or remain stable under a one-sided action, as described in [[cosmodynamics/fact-record-history|Fact, Record, and History]] and [[sufficient-reason/algebraic-arrow-of-time|the algebraic arrow programme]].

## Cosmos as the history-bearing balance

The cosmos in this programme is not the sum of all objects. It is the closure structure that lets facts belong to one causal, metric, and record-bearing history. The conservation conjecture therefore concerns the whole as whole:

$$
\text{invariant causal structure}
\longrightarrow
\text{local fact}
\longrightarrow
\text{record}
\longrightarrow
\text{common history}.
$$

The poetic formulation becomes rigorous only if the arrows are constructed and the same causal charge can be followed across them. Until then, the poetry identifies the intended direction of explanation; it does not count as evidence for the proposed law.
