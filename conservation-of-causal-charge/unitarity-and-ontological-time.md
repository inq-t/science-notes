# Why Unitarity Is Not the Wall Symmetry

The pre-observable-to-observable wall should not be governed by a Unitarity Principle. Unitarity preserves an inner product under reversible transport, whereas the wall is meant to discard distinctions, produce an observable law, and orient irreversible factual history. The strongest present replacement has three different parts: an algebraic class can persist through changing representatives, a conditional expectation can split response exactly into retained and lost blocks, and observable physics can later possess ordinary Noether charges and actions. None is a conservation of information across the wall.

## The wall is deliberately noninvertible

Let a state-preserving conditional expectation represent a controlled wall,

$$
E:\mathcal M\longrightarrow\mathcal N,
\qquad
E^2=E.
$$

It is generally neither an automorphism nor a unitary channel. In the finite tracial representation, the trace-self-adjoint expectation acts on density matrices and represents restriction to \(\mathcal N\); setting \(\bar\rho=E(\rho)\) gives the exact identity

$$
\boxed{
S(\bar\rho)-S(\rho)
=D(\rho\Vert\bar\rho)
\geq0.}
$$

The observable state gains entropy by precisely the relative distinction removed by the wall. With the tracial reference \(\tau\),

$$
\boxed{
D(\rho\Vert\tau)
=D(\bar\rho\Vert\tau)
+D(\rho\Vert\bar\rho).}
$$

At a faithful \(E\)-fixed reference, for fixed \(E\) and self-adjoint trace-zero density tangents, the coincidence Hessian supplies the corresponding response balance

$$
\boxed{
\mathbb G^{\mathrm{pre}}
=\mathbb G^{\mathrm{obs}}
+\mathbb G^{\mathrm{wall}}.}
$$

[[spectral-wall-descent/conditional-expectation-balance|The conditional-expectation theorem]] proves these formulas and states the modular existence gate. They are non-unitary Pythagorean identities, not conservation of a substance. Something is lost as accessible distinction and gained as entropy of the unconditioned observable law.

## A dilation does not restore the ontology

Every unital completely positive map may be represented in a larger Hilbert space by an isometry,

$$
\Phi(a)=V^*\pi(a)V.
$$

This is a representation theorem. It does not prove that the added carrier is physically real, that the pre-observable register evolves unitarily, or that lost distinction remains as observable information in an environment. Treating the dilation as the hidden ontology would erase the very noninvertibility the wall is meant to explain.

Unitary transport remains useful for comparing two presentations inside one register. It is not the arrow that crosses registers.

## What can persist through symmetry breaking

The natural upstream invariant is structural rather than quantitative. For a norm-continuous bounded perturbation of a spectral triple,

$$
D_t=D_0+B_t.
$$

the bounded transforms form a Fredholm homotopy under the standard hypotheses. The \(K\)-homology or \(KK\)-class and its index pairings remain fixed even while inner fluctuations alter gauge fields, Higgs data, and local metric representatives. For a smooth, suitably summable path and a specified JLO or periodic Chern cocycle, the character changes by an exact transgression,

$$
\operatorname{Ch}(D_1)-\operatorname{Ch}(D_0)
=(b+B_{\mathrm{cyc}})\operatorname{CS}(D_t).
$$

This is the plausible sense in which symmetry is maintained during observable symmetry breaking:

$$
\boxed{
\text{representative and stabilizer change;
pairing class persists}.}
$$

It is not a conserved energy, entropy, bit count, or Hilbert norm. [[spectral-wall-descent/index-and-curvature-transgression|Index and curvature transgression]] develops this claim and its failure conditions.

## Gravity is the proposed image of the wall defect

The exact algebra stops at \(\mathbb G^{\mathrm{wall}}\). Let \(\mathfrak S\) be a proposed map from wall tangents to geometric tangents. The well-typed metric conjecture is a pullback equality

$$
\boxed{
\mathbb G^{\mathrm{wall}}
\stackrel{?}{=}
Z_g\,\mathfrak S^*\mathbb G^{\mathrm{grav}},}
$$

meaning \(G^{\mathrm{wall}}(X,Y)=Z_gG^{\mathrm{grav}}(\mathrm d\mathfrak S X,\mathrm d\mathfrak S Y)\) if the conjecture holds. The map must produce a connection or metric tangent, localization on a causal wall, curvature, area normalization, and the relevant gauge and boundary identities; \(Z_g\) remains independent until a unit matching is proved. A quotient-metric formulation would require an independently declared minimization over lifts. In this sense gravity would balance lost observable distinction by representing the same response geometrically.

Entropy is close to anti-information only after a reference and algebra are declared. In a finite \(n\)-level tracial register,

$$
D(\rho\Vert\mathbf1/n)=\ln n-S(\rho).
$$

This does not make entropy identical to curvature. [[spectral-wall-descent/heat-entropy-no-go|The heat-entropy calculation]] shows that normalized spectral entropy cancels the term linear in the \(a_2\) coefficient containing the Einstein--Hilbert action. A genuine gravity map must therefore use relative wall data, a curvature transgression, canonical energy, or another independently justified construction.

## Least action belongs to the observable register

Once an observable spectral geometry has descended, its spectral action can generate ordinary gravitational, gauge, Higgs, and mass terms. A continuous symmetry of that action may then yield a standard Noether or boundary balance. Those statements are downstream:

$$
\text{wall descent}
\longrightarrow
\text{observable spectral action}
\longrightarrow
\text{observable equations and charges}.
$$

There is presently no reason to impose stationary action on the homogeneous pre-observable algebra itself. [[spectral-wall-descent/observable-spectral-action|The observable spectral-action note]] proves that a bulk spectral action is constant on conjugacy orbits even when a fixed-context wall entropy varies. It therefore cannot itself be the wall-loss functional.

The proposed diagonal causal charge in [[conservation-of-causal-charge/causal-individuation-balance|causal-individuation balance]] remains a possible observable or boundary law, but it is no longer the candidate invariant of the wall crossing. It must be derived from a continuous action and moment map on the descended carrier.

## Ontological time is the orientation of records

The wall expectation yields an unconditioned observable state, not a realized fact. A fact-bearing history additionally requires compatible characters on a directed family of commutative unital \(C^*\)-record algebras, or atomic von Neumann record algebras if normal operational states are required,

$$
\mathcal R_{\Sigma_1}
\xhookrightarrow{}\mathcal R_{\Sigma_2}
\xhookrightarrow{}\cdots,
\qquad
\chi_{\Sigma_2}|_{\mathcal R_{\Sigma_1}}
=\chi_{\Sigma_1}.
$$

This one-sided inclusion order is a candidate for ontological time. It is neither a fourth information reservoir nor the parameter of a reversible equation. The irreversibility lies in persistent factual extension, not in a fundamental lossless evolution seen from a larger Hilbert space. [[conservation-of-causal-charge/factive-descent-and-records|Factive descent]] owns the still-open selection and stability construction.

## Failure conditions

- If the wall map is unitary or invertible, it cannot account for genuine loss. A \(KK\)-equivalent inclusion may still have metric defects, but its relative \(K\)-groups cannot carry the proposed topological wall class.
- If no state-preserving expectation or substitute instrument exists, the exact entropy and BKM split is unavailable.
- If an index class is the only invariant, it is too coarse to fix a continuous gravitational coupling or local curvature.
- If \(\mathbb G^{\mathrm{wall}}\) is simply renamed gravity, the carrier-changing weld has not been constructed.
- If the spectral action is applied upstream merely because it works downstream, observable variational structure has been projected into the wrong register.
- If there is no actual character and persistent record, increased observable entropy describes possibilities rather than one cosmic history.

The strongest present principle is therefore not unitarity but **structural exactness under noninvertible descent**: an algebraic pairing class may persist, the wall may exactly divide response into retained and lost distinction, gravity may be the geometric representation of the lost block, and ontological time may be the directed stabilization of one resulting factual history.
