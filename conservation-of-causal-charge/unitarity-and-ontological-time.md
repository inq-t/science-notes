# Why Unitarity Is Not the Wall Symmetry

The pre-observable-to-observable wall should not be governed by a Unitarity Principle. Unitarity preserves an inner product under reversible transport, whereas a nonfaithful observational wall discards distinctions and produces an observable law; Euclidean transfer attenuates distinctions without generally discarding them; and a separate fact-and-record arrow orients irreversible factual history. [[algebra/nonfaithful-realization|The categorical obstruction]] proves that genuine forgetting cannot be an equivalence. The strongest present replacement is compositional rather than dynamical: a correspondence class fuses coherently across scale, a conditional expectation divides retained from erased distinction, and in a type-I product cell the erased edge factor's log-dimension partitions into a chosen input edge entropy and its tracial defect. Observable Noether charges and least action arise only later. None is conservation of an information substance across the wall.

## The wall is deliberately noninvertible

Let a state-preserving conditional expectation represent a controlled wall,

$$
E:\mathcal M\longrightarrow\mathcal N,
\qquad
E^2=E.
$$

It is generally neither an automorphism nor a unitary channel. In the finite tracial representation, the trace-self-adjoint expectation acts on density matrices and represents restriction to \(\mathcal N\); setting \(\bar\rho=E(\rho)\) gives the exact identity

$$
\boxed{
S(\bar\rho)-S(\rho)
=D(\rho\Vert\bar\rho)
\geq0.}
$$

The observable state gains entropy by precisely the relative distinction removed by the wall. With the tracial reference \(\tau\),

$$
\boxed{
D(\rho\Vert\tau)
=D(\bar\rho\Vert\tau)
+D(\rho\Vert\bar\rho).}
$$

At a faithful \(E\)-fixed reference, for fixed \(E\) and self-adjoint trace-zero density tangents, the coincidence Hessian supplies the corresponding response balance

$$
\boxed{
\mathbb G^{\mathrm{pre}}
=\mathbb G^{\mathrm{obs}}
+\mathbb G^{\mathrm{wall}}.}
$$

[[spectral-wall-descent/conditional-expectation-balance|The conditional-expectation theorem]] proves these formulas and states the modular existence gate. They are non-unitary Pythagorean identities, not conservation of a substance. Something is lost as accessible distinction and gained as entropy of the unconditioned observable law.

## A dilation does not restore the ontology

After choosing a faithful normal representation of the codomain, every
normal unital completely positive map may be represented on a larger Hilbert
space by an isometry,

$$
\Phi(a)=V^*\pi(a)V.
$$

This is a representation theorem. It does not prove that the added carrier is
physically real, that the pre-observable register evolves unitarily, or that
lost distinction remains as observable information in an environment.
Treating the dilation as the hidden ontology merely relocates reduced
noninvertibility to compression or restricted access; it does not remove that
noninvertibility from the reduced arrow or establish the proposed ontology.

Unitary transport remains useful for comparing two presentations inside one register. It is not the arrow that crosses registers.

## Local unitarity can be a quotient phenomenon

There is also a positive theorem behind the Copernican claim. Unitarity is a
predicate of a represented transformation on an inner-product carrier; it is
not a predicate of “the whole” before a carrier and transformation have been
declared. An upstream register with only a semidefinite response may therefore
be neither unitary nor nonunitary in that sense.

Let

\[
q:\mathcal A\twoheadrightarrow\mathcal A/I
\]

be a nontrivial \(C^*\)-algebra quotient. An upstream automorphism
\(\alpha_t\) induces an observable automorphism

\[
\bar\alpha_t(q(a))=q(\alpha_t(a))
\]

exactly when it preserves the forgotten ideal, \(\alpha_t(I)=I\). An invariant
quotient state implements every \(\bar\alpha_t\) by a GNS unitary. GNS strong
continuity is the additional hypothesis needed for a Stone generator and,
only after the parameter is calibrated as clock time, a Hamiltonian. Thus

\[
\boxed{
\text{noninvertible formation map}
\quad\text{and}\quad
\text{reversible local clock flow}
\text{ are different arrows}.}
\]

The same structure appears before a \(C^*\)-algebra has been chosen. If a
positive semidefinite response is

\[
g(\xi,\eta)=\langle J\xi,J\eta\rangle,
\qquad
\operatorname{rad}g=\ker J,
\]

then every invertible transformation preserving \(g\) becomes unitary on the
completion of \(\mathcal K/\ker J\). The lifted observable symmetry group is
the image of the response stabilizer modulo transformations that differ from
the identity only in invisible directions. In this exact sense, symmetry can
be the grammar of a retained presentation rather than the primitive datum.
The upstream transformation need not itself be invertible: form preservation
together with \(T(\mathcal K)+\ker J=\mathcal K\) already makes its quotient
map unitary, while any kernel or failure of surjectivity may remain confined
to the invisible radical.
[[algebra/quotient-unitarity-and-kernel-stabilization|Quotient unitarity and
kernel stabilization]] proves both versions.

The split-idempotent version makes the change of identity explicit. If

\[
W\mathop{\longrightarrow}^{q}L
\mathop{\longrightarrow}^{h}W,
\qquad qh=I_L,
\qquad e=hq,
\]

then [[algebra/retract-corners-and-local-unitarity|the retract-corner
theorem]] gives

\[
\operatorname{End}(L)\cong e\operatorname{End}(W)e.
\]

A local unitary \(U\) is represented on the whole carrier by \(hUq\). Its
inverse multiplies back to \(e\), the identity of the retained corner, not to
\(I_W\); if \(\ker q\neq0\), the representative is noninvertible on \(W\).
Thus the prior asymmetry is not “symmetry breaking.” It is the idempotent
selection of the carrier on which symmetry is subsequently a meaningful
predicate.

If \(h\) is the harmonic section of a whole positive form, the same
idempotent removes the vertical response. [[trace-dirichlet-descent/inq|The
Dirichlet descent audit]] distinguishes two ways to induce a local process.
Pulling actual local observables into the whole through a multiplicative
inclusion safely restricts a Dirichlet form; infimizing over every forgotten
fibre gives the sharper least-cost response but need not remain Markovian.
[[trace-dirichlet-descent/standard-form-pullback-and-reducing-wall|The
standard-form theorem]] proves the safe pullback completely Dirichlet for an
expected Type-III inclusion, subject to domain density. It agrees with the
least-cost quotient only when the retained subspace reduces the whole
semigroup. In every case the local Markov process and local clock unitary
remain separate endomorphisms.

This establishes compatibility, not the poetic causal “so that.” Strict
descent can merely glue compatible local objects and forget nothing. A
physical wall must additionally construct the quotient, restriction,
expectation, instrument, or other nonfaithful realization; show why its kernel
is preserved by the recovered clock flow; and supply the invariant state and
continuity. Nor does positivity on discarded directions give a mass gap on the
retained carrier. That requires a transfer to a physical quadratic form and a
uniform lower bound relative to an independently declared physical norm.

[[vacuum-aligned-innovation-completion/inq|Vacuum-aligned innovation]]
supplies a useful stress test of this grammar. Boundary Gauss gluing is an
exact invariant-carrier construction, multiplication by a faithful
correlation half-density is a unitary change of Hilbert presentation, and
splitting the vacuum complement into internal and balance blocks is an
orthogonal analysis. None of those three operations forgets a state. A
nonfaithful wall, a positive Euclidean transfer, and a later Lorentzian
unitary therefore remain three additional arrows. Calling their composite
“nonunitary descent” would erase precisely the order of explanation the
Copernican proposal needs.

## Euclidean attenuation is not the forgetting wall

There is a second distinction that prevents the word *nonunitary* from doing
too much work. Let \(H\geq0\) be self-adjoint on a physical Hilbert carrier and
define, using Euclidean length \(\ell\geq0\) and Lorentzian duration
\(t\in\mathbb R\),

\[
C_\ell:=e^{-\ell H/(\hbar c)},
\qquad
U_t:=e^{-itH/\hbar}.
\tag{UO1}
\]

The spectral theorem gives a strongly continuous self-adjoint contraction
semigroup \(C_\ell\) and a strongly continuous unitary group \(U_t\). For every
\(\ell>0\), the function \(e^{-\ell\lambda/(\hbar c)}\) is strictly positive at
every \(\lambda\in\sigma(H)\), so

\[
\ker C_\ell=0,
\qquad
\overline{\operatorname{Ran}C_\ell}=\mathcal H.
\tag{UO2}
\]

If \(H\) is unbounded, \(C_\ell\) is generally not surjective because its
inverse is unbounded. If \(H\) is bounded, it is even invertible as a bounded
linear map, although its inverse is not a contraction and normally is not a
positive or Markov operation. In neither case is Euclidean attenuation by
itself an information quotient. The exact forgetting in an
Osterwalder--Schrader construction belongs instead to the reflection null
quotient or to the conditional-expectation cuts used to represent past and
future information.

Let \(P_0=\mathbf1_{\{0\}}(H)\) and put

\[
\Delta_E
:=
\inf\sigma\!\left(H\big|_{(I-P_0)\mathcal H}\right).
\tag{UO3}
\]

Functional calculus then gives the exact uniform-rate identity

\[
\boxed{
\left\|C_\ell(I-P_0)\right\|
=
e^{-\ell\Delta_E/(\hbar c)}.}
\tag{UO4}
\]

Thus a mass or energy gap is not a kernel appearing at finite Euclidean depth.
It is uniform exponential attenuation of every nonvacuum distinction. If the
nonvacuum spectrum accumulates at zero, each fixed vector can still attenuate
while the operator norm in (UO4) remains one. Pointwise fading is therefore
not the mass-gap predicate.

After Osterwalder--Schrader reconstruction, \(C_\ell\) and \(U_t\) are two
functional-calculus presentations of the same positive generator. Their
relation does not prove that forgetting causes unitary time. The exact typed
ledger is

\[
\boxed{
q\ \text{or}\ E
\;\neq\;
C_\ell
\;\neq\;
U_t:}
\qquad
\begin{array}{c}
\text{nonfaithful formation}\quad
\text{Euclidean attenuation}\quad
\text{Lorentzian clock transport}.
\end{array}
\tag{UO5}
\]

The Copernican point survives in a stricter form: unitarity is meaningful only
after the physical carrier and clock action have been formed. It is not a law
that the formation arrow itself must obey.

[[contemporary-puzzles/yang-mills-mass-gap/past-future-angle-and-the-transfer-gap#The whole shift, the slice compression, and the clock are different arrows|The stationary path dilation]] gives a useful exact warning against
overcorrecting. A two-sided stationary history has a unitary Koopman shift
\(\mathscr U_{\mathrm{hist}}\), while its one-slice Euclidean transfer is the
compression \(T^n=J_0^*\mathscr U_{\mathrm{hist}}^nJ_0\). The later
Lorentzian \(U_{\mathrm{clock}}(t)=e^{-itH/\hbar}\) is a different unitary on
its native OS-reconstructed carrier; in a Markov realization this may be
identified with the slice \(L^2\) carrier but not canonically with the whole
history shift. Hence neither “the whole is unitary” nor “the whole is
nonunitary” is a well-typed claim until an action and an inner-product
carrier have been named. The noninvertibility belongs to the specified
expectation, quotient, or compression.

The discarded part is nevertheless exact rather than rhetorical. With
\(e_0=J_0J_0^*\), the map
\((I-e_0)\mathscr U_{\mathrm{hist}}^nJ_0\) has Gramian
\(I-T^{2n}\) in the reversible positive-transfer case. It is the distinction
not recoverable from the initial slice after depth \(n\). A lower frame for
that Gramian is a transfer-gap certificate; it is not yet an entropy law,
measurement outcome, or proof of fundamental irreversibility.

[[algebra/os-descent-naturality-and-clock-no-go|The idempotent-clock no-go]]
makes the separation algebraic. If a surjective factor intertwines an
idempotent wall projection with a unitary, the unitary is necessarily the
identity. A nontrivial clock may instead descend from a separate
kernel-preserving action, or arise through OS functional calculus from the
positive transfer generator. An OS-bounded translation-covariant
whole-to-local map then intertwines both the Euclidean semigroups and their
Lorentzian clock groups without becoming either one.

If that OS-natural map is surjective, it also gives a precise whole/local
spectral statement: a Borel band is absent from the local generator exactly
when the corresponding whole spectral subspace lies in the map's kernel.
Thus a gapless whole carrier may have a gapped presentation by spectral
deletion, but neither the kernel nor nonfaithfulness alone selects the band.

When the Euclidean transfer and Lorentzian clock have both been reconstructed
from the same positive generator, compatibility is an intertwiner. With
$W_\ell x=C_\ell x\oplus\mathcal R_\ell x$ the minimal residue completion
and with $U_t^{\mathrm{res}}$ acting pointwise on its residue-output
functions, functional calculus gives

\[
\boxed{
W_\ell U_t
=
(U_t\oplus U_t^{\mathrm{res}})W_\ell.}
\tag{UO5a}
\]

Thus a proper Euclidean residue completion and a unitary clock can be exactly
equivariant. This is coexistence of distinct arrows, not a theorem that
nonunitarity or forgetting produces unitarity.

[[two-slice-innovation-geometry/inq|The two-slice theorem]] now gives this
ledger a concrete whole/part model. The whole datum is a joint relation
\(\mathsf J\), not an evolution operator. Its endpoint conditional
expectations are noninvertible projections on \(L^2(\mathsf J)\); their
conditional variances are the distinctions each local presentation forgets.
Their reduced Friedrichs angle is the norm of supported positive transfer,
and only the logarithm of that norm per slab thickness becomes a Hamiltonian
after OS identification. The later Lorentzian unitary is therefore neither
the restriction nor the inverse of either expectation. It is a
different-carrier functional-calculus presentation of the reconstructed
generator.

[[gauge-boundary-frame-gluing/inq|Gauge Boundary Frames and Gauss Gluing]]
give a complementary spatial theorem. A gauge-invariant whole is generally
not the tensor product of gauge-invariant parts: regional carriers must first
retain boundary charges, and the physical whole pairs each charge with its
dual before taking the diagonal invariant. Closing both parts first erases
every nontrivial paired sector. This is a genuine whole/part asymmetry, but
it is neither forgetting by strict descent nor time evolution. It identifies
the carrier on which the later transfer and clock questions must be asked.

[[vacuum-aligned-transfer-cocycle/inq|The carrier-changing block theorem]]
makes the same distinction through composition. A pointed chain
$P_k:\mathcal H_k\to\mathcal H_{k+1}$ has an exact centered operator cocycle,
but replacing every operator block by its norm gives only the lax law
$\mathscr B(RP)\leq\mathscr B(R)\mathscr B(P)$. The inequality records phases,
directions, and cancellations discarded by scalarization; it is not
ontological indeterminacy. If an intermediate visible subspace is compressed
away, the exact composite differs from the product of visible compressions by
a leave-and-return term through its orthogonal complement. This is a
deterministic source of apparent memory. It does not create an exactly unitary
local clock: for a contractive whole map, a unitary compression is already a
reducing noiseless sector. Quotient formation or OS reconstruction remains a
different arrow.

For contractive stages, write $B_0=I$ and
$B_k=A_{k-1}\cdots A_0$. There is then an exact conservation-shaped ledger:

\[
\|x\|^2
=
\|B_nx\|^2
+
\sum_{k=0}^{n-1}
\left\|
(I-A_k^*A_k)^{1/2}B_kx
\right\|^2.
\]

What is conserved here is the initial Hilbert norm split between surviving
amplitude and transported stage defects. It is not yet causal charge or
entropy. Its value is conceptual: a uniform gap in the product is equivalent
to a lower-frame inequality for the whole family of stage defects, even when
each single defect has a nontrivial kernel.

[[transported-response-observability-solder/inq|The transported-response
theorem]] separates the geometrically interpreted part of this ledger from
the rest. If an independently derived response satisfies
$I-A_k^*A_k\geq L_k^*L_k$, then

\[
\|x\|^2
=\|B_nx\|^2
+\sum_k\|L_kB_kx\|^2
+\sum_k\|(I-A_k^*A_k-L_k^*L_k)^{1/2}B_kx\|^2.
\]

Thus neither “forgetting” nor “conservation” needs to be metaphorical: the
first response sum is the certified geometric portion of attenuation and the
last sum is the still-unsoldered residue. What remains poetic is any claim
that either sum is entropy, causal charge, or factification. Those
interpretations require their own carrier maps.

[[directed-isometric-residue-completion/inq|The directed-completion theorem]]
now makes the transfer's minimal norm completion exact. For one contraction
$A$, the minimal column

\[
x\longmapsto Ax\oplus(I-A^*A)^{1/2}x
\]

is isometric and is unitary exactly when $A$ is a coisometry. A nonidentity
positive transfer therefore sits as the compression of a proper isometry,
not as the restriction of a hidden reversible clock. Its cascade preserves
the initial Hilbert norm by retaining all transported defect coordinates;
Douglas factorization types any proved response $L^*L\leq I-A^*A$ as a
contractive readout of the one-stage minimal defect carrier. The full
chronological archive may contain unreachable stage coordinates and need not
be the endpoint-minimal completion. This is a sharp valid model behind the
Copernican phrase only if its arrows are independently realized: the
formation diagram may be nonautomorphic, the declared Euclidean transfer may
have a proper residue completion, and a later Lorentzian clock is a separate
unitary arrow on a reconstructed carrier.
Non-surjectivity is not by itself absolute irreversibility, because the
adjoint recovers compatible histories on the range; and the defect carrier
is not a fact archive until a physical record algebra and write map have been
supplied.

[[pointed-cp-fusion-residue/inq|Pointed CP Fusion Residue]] gives a second,
genuinely correspondence-valued realization of this type distinction. For
two normal UCP arrows, the minimal correspondence of their composite embeds
as the cyclic subcorrespondence of the fused two-stage carrier. Its adjoint is
a coisometry whose kernel is the orthogonal family of intermediate fusion
directions omitted by the endpoint composite. It is noninvertible exactly
when that fusion residue is nonzero; in either case it is not a global time
evolution.

It also sharpens the relation to local unitarity. If $v:F\to E$ is the cyclic
isometry, $U=v^*$ its coisometric descent, and
$r=I-vv^*$ its residue projection, then every adjointable unitary $q$ on the

\[
\widetilde q=vqv^*+r,
\qquad
U\widetilde q=qU.
\]

Thus, when the residue is nonzero, a proper endpoint descent and a
retained-carrier unitary can coexist by an exact
intertwiner. The descent does not create the unitary, and unitarity need not
fail on every operation available on the fused carrier. Moreover,
$rv=0$: the raw fusion residue has zero pullback along the canonical endpoint
lift. [[contemporary-puzzles/yang-mills-mass-gap/fusion-residue-is-not-transfer-defect|The
fusion/transfer firewall]] shows why a separate history-sensitive carrier map
is required before the residue can be interpreted as forgetting or compared
with a physical transfer defect.

[[bridge-score-fusion-geometry/inq|Bridge-Score Fusion Geometry]] constructs

=

The bridge construction also supplies an exact conservation-shaped ledger.
Let \(K_n=\mathsf E_{0,2n}J_n\) be the endpoint predictor and
\(L_n^{\mathrm{br}}=(I-\mathsf E_{0,2n})J_n\) the orthogonal residue. Then

\[
J_nf
=
K_nf+L_n^{\mathrm{br}}f,
\qquad
\boxed{
\|f\|^2
=
\|K_nf\|^2
+
\|L_n^{\mathrm{br}}f\|^2.}

Equivalently, the defect column

\[
f\longmapsto
K_nf\oplus L_n^{\mathrm{br}}f

is isometric. The first coordinate is the distinction presentable from the
two boundaries; the second is what that presentation omits. Conditional
descent is nonunitary only after the residue coordinate is discarded. On the
enlarged predictor-plus-residue carrier the norm is exactly retained. This is
a precise Pythagorean meaning for a conservation law between accessible and
forgotten distinction, but it is not conservation of an information

## The ambient process category has a reversible core

There is now a precise candidate for the larger algebra in which these two
arrows coexist:

\[
\operatorname{Aut}(M)
\subset
\operatorname{UCP}_{\mathrm n}(M,M).
\]

The automorphisms form the reversible core. Normal UCP maps add composable
arrows that need not be invertible within the positive-process category,
without denying that clock evolution is an automorphism represented
unitarily. A state-preserving symmetric average of automorphisms gives a
GNS-self-adjoint contraction. If its Dirichlet defect has a positive edge on
the vacuum complement, its Poissonization is strictly contractive there and
is a one-sided quantum Markov semigroup in that category, not an
automorphism group.

This does not collapse three different arrows into one:

\[
\mathcal C_{\mathrm{whole}}\xrightarrow{\Pi}\mathcal C_{\mathrm{local}},
\qquad
\mathsf P_t:\mathcal A(O)\to\mathcal A(O),
\qquad
\alpha_s\in\operatorname{Aut}(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{ql}}),
\quad
\alpha_s(\mathcal A(O))=\mathcal A(O+s).
\]

The first is realization across registers, the second is Markov relaxation
inside a declared observable algebra, and the third is reversible global
clock evolution covariant on the local net. It need not preserve one fixed
regional algebra. A construction must relate them; a shared diagram does not.

[[contemporary-puzzles/yang-mills-mass-gap/kazhdan-markov-process-carrier|The
Kazhdan--Markov theorem]] proves more. If the automorphisms carry a
property-\((T)\) composition law and have only the vacuum as a common GNS
invariant, their normalized symmetric average has a positive dimensionless
Dirichlet edge on the same type-III carrier. This is a rigorous candidate for
a uniform Markov distinction-decay rate. It is not yet nonfaithful
forgetting, ontological time, clock time, or mass: its semigroup parameter is
freely rescalable, and the realization wall, operation, their intertwining,
net covariance, record interpretation, and energy comparison must be
constructed independently. Indeed, at finite parameter the bounded Poisson
semigroup is invertible as a linear map, although its inverse is generally
not UCP or contractive.

The symmetric average does not make symmetry ontologically prior. It is the
positive part used to measure coercivity inside a potentially oriented
process category. Chirality or causal directedness cannot be inferred from
that average; it requires additional oriented data such as a grading, ordered
correspondence, antisymmetric component, instrument, or record map, and that
data must survive the later quotient in a declared form.

## What can persist through symmetry breaking

The natural upstream invariant is structural rather than quantitative. For a norm-continuous bounded perturbation of a spectral triple,

$$
D_t=D_0+B_t.
$$

the bounded transforms form a Fredholm homotopy under the standard hypotheses. The \(K\)-homology or \(KK\)-class and its index pairings remain fixed even while inner fluctuations alter gauge fields, Higgs data, and local metric representatives. For a smooth, suitably summable path and a specified JLO or periodic Chern cocycle, the character changes by an exact transgression,

$$
\operatorname{Ch}(D_1)-\operatorname{Ch}(D_0)
=(b+B_{\mathrm{cyc}})\operatorname{CS}(D_t).
$$

This is the plausible sense in which symmetry is maintained during observable symmetry breaking:

$$
\boxed{
\text{representative and stabilizer change;
pairing class persists}.}
$$

It is not a conserved energy, entropy, bit count, or Hilbert norm. [[spectral-wall-descent/index-and-curvature-transgression|Index and curvature transgression]] develops this claim and its failure conditions.

## Central edge entropy is not the lost block

For physical perturbations with classical asymptotically AdS duals, to second order about a vacuum CFT ball and in its AdS Rindler wedge, regional Fisher information retained by the reconstructable algebra pulls back to gravitational canonical energy,

$$
G^{\mathrm{ret}}
\stackrel{\mathrm{AdS\ calibration}}{=}
\mathfrak S^*\mathcal E_{\mathrm{can}}^{\mathrm{grav}},
$$

while in exact complementary-recovery code models the fixed edge-area term is central and cancels from relative entropy. Identifying that edge operator with gravitational area is the independent conjectural weld

$$
\mathcal L_\chi(U)
\stackrel{?}{=}
\eta_*\mathcal A_D^Z(U).
$$

In the type-I factor cell of [[spectral-wall-descent/finite-index-area-weld|the finite-index area weld]], take \(E_\tau\) to be the auxiliary tracial expectation and \(\rho=\rho_a\otimes\chi\) a product state on the same multiplicity space. Then

$$
\boxed{
S(\chi)
+D(\chi\Vert\tau)
=\frac12\log\operatorname{Ind}_{W}(E_\tau).}
$$

The chosen input edge entropy \(S(\chi)\) and distinction from the tracial state \(D(\chi\Vert\tau)\) are complementary portions of the erased factor's log-dimension, not the full subalgebra-relative-entropy capacity. The code expectation determined by \(\chi\) need not equal \(E_\tau\); proving that its fixed edge entropy is gravitational area is the separate conjectural weld.

Entropy is close to anti-information only after a reference and algebra are declared. In a finite \(n\)-level tracial register,

$$
D(\rho\Vert\mathbf1/n)=\ln n-S(\rho).
$$

This does not make entropy identical to curvature. [[spectral-wall-descent/heat-entropy-no-go|The heat-entropy calculation]] shows that normalized spectral entropy cancels the term linear in the \(a_2\) coefficient containing the Einstein--Hilbert action. A genuine gravity map must therefore use relative wall data, a curvature transgression, canonical energy, or another independently justified construction.

The compositional candidate for persistence is a dualizable correspondence. In a factor sector,

$$
d(X_{32}\boxtimes X_{21})
=d(X_{32})d(X_{21}),
$$

so \(\log d\) is additive under fusion. This is a categorical valuation, not a Noether charge. With centers, the full matrix dimension or bimodule must be retained. ## Least action belongs to the observable register

Once an observable spectral geometry has descended, its spectral action can generate ordinary gravitational, gauge, Higgs, and mass terms. A continuous symmetry of that action may then yield a standard Noether or boundary balance. Those statements are downstream:

$$
\text{wall descent}
\longrightarrow
\text{observable spectral action}
\longrightarrow
\text{observable equations and charges}.
$$

There is presently no reason to impose stationary action on the homogeneous pre-observable algebra itself. [[spectral-wall-descent/observable-spectral-action|The observable spectral-action note]] proves that a bulk spectral action is constant on conjugacy orbits even when a fixed-context wall entropy varies. It therefore cannot itself be the wall-loss functional.

The proposed diagonal causal charge in [[conservation-of-causal-charge/causal-individuation-balance|causal-individuation balance]] remains a possible observable or boundary law, but it is no longer the candidate invariant of the wall crossing. It must be derived from a continuous action and moment map on the descended carrier.
