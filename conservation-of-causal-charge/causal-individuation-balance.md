# Causal-Individuation Balance

The central conjecture is that nongravitational state charge, gravitational boundary charge, and the charge deposited in records or carried through a causal wall are representations of one causal symmetry charge. The state term includes bulk matter unless a finer gauge-compatible factorization is constructed. Conservation is therefore a flux-inclusive balance on causal cuts, not conservation of entropy, an absolute amount of information, or a scalar substance called space.

## Conjectured carrier

Let \(\Sigma\) be an admissible causal cut. Suppose the theory constructs a combined state--geometry--record phase space

$$
\mathcal P_\Sigma
=\mathcal P_\Sigma^{\mathrm{state}}
\times\mathcal P_\Sigma^{\mathrm{grav}}
\times\mathcal P_\Sigma^{\mathrm{rec}},
$$

or the corresponding covariant presymplectic object. Suppose further that one causal boost or dilation symmetry \(G_{\mathrm c}\), with Lie algebra \(\mathfrak g_{\mathrm c}\), acts diagonally and admits a moment map

$$
\mu_\Sigma:
\mathcal P_\Sigma\longrightarrow\mathfrak g_{\mathrm c}^*.
$$

After canonical embeddings into this common charge space, require

$$
\boxed{
\mu_\Sigma
=\mu_\Sigma^{\mathrm{state}}
+\mu_\Sigma^{\mathrm{grav}}
+\mu_\Sigma^{\mathrm{rec}}.
}
$$

The plus signs are meaningful only because all three terms represent the same normalized generator. In this minimal partition, the state sector includes bulk matter together with the relevant wall or observer-state degrees of freedom. The note does not assume that these admit independent phase-space factors. A finer split must be constructed compatibly with gravitational constraints and gauge reduction. Equality obtained by independently rescaling the sectors would not establish one charge.

## Balance law

For a causal slab \(W:\Sigma_1\to\Sigma_2\) and \(\xi\in\mathfrak g_{\mathrm c}\), define

$$
Q_\xi[\Sigma]
:=\langle\mu_\Sigma,\xi\rangle.
$$

The conjectured law is

$$
\boxed{
Q_\xi[\Sigma_2]-Q_\xi[\Sigma_1]
+\Phi_\xi[W]=0,
}
$$

where \(\Phi_\xi[W]\) includes physical flux through the remaining boundary. Each \(\mu^i\) counts the charge assigned to sector \(i\) on a cut. Charge deposited in a stable record is therefore counted by the change of \(\mu^{\mathrm{rec}}\); it belongs in \(\Phi_\xi\) only when that record lies outside the chosen subsystem. Matter contained on a cut belongs to \(\mu^{\mathrm{state}}\); matter crossing out of the chosen subsystem belongs to the flux. For a closed enlarged system,

$$
\frac{\mathrm d}{\mathrm dN}Q_\xi^{\mathrm{total}}=0
$$

after \(N\) has been justified as a parameter along the physical solution. [[diagonal-charge-balance]] gives the exact Hamiltonian theorem whose causal and gravitational generalization is being proposed.

## Sectoral representations

At an equilibrium causal cut, the state contribution is expected to be a modular boost charge generated by an appropriately normalized \(K_\Sigma\), with bulk matter included in the corresponding nongravitational algebra or charge. The geometric contribution is expected to be a diffeomorphism Noether, area, edge, or corner charge for the same boost. Observer degrees of freedom must likewise be assigned once, usually to the state or record sector, rather than introduced as an unrelated charge.

The proposal named “one causal boost charge” in [[causal-scale-master/latest/Causal_Scale_Dynamics_Master_v7_0|Causal Scale Dynamics v7]] is the immediate antecedent. Controlled gravitational and holographic settings show that modular charge, area charge, canonical energy, and symplectic structure can cohere. They do not prove that a dynamical FLRW wall realizes this structure or that its horizontal scale tangent is the relevant boost tangent.

## Charge is not capacity

The moment map is linear in the generator. BKM capacity is a positive quadratic response:

$$
G^{\perp}_{NN}
=g^{\mathrm{BKM}}(v_N,v_N).
$$

The conjecture does not equate these objects directly. Their proposed relationship passes through a first variation, Hessian, or canonical-energy construction, as stated in [[state-geometry-charge-weld]]. This distinction is essential: a signed charge can reverse orientation, whereas a squared norm cannot.

## Descent across presentations

If admissible causal cuts and contexts form a groupoid, causal charge must be invariant under presentation arrows and compatible on overlaps. Schematically,

$$
x\simeq y
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
Q_\xi(x)=Q_\xi(y),
$$

after transporting both sides to a common charge fiber. A locally selected fact may fail to be symmetric while the charge assignment and descent data remain equivariant. [[facthood-and-symmetry-breaking]] explains this retyping.

## Status

This is the module's central physical conjecture. Its ingredients are individually motivated, but the combined action, common generator, moment map, boundary flux, fact-forming record sector, and descent law have not been constructed. [[conservation-of-causal-charge/theorem-programme]] states the upgrade and failure conditions that prevent the conjecture from becoming a definition by remainder.
