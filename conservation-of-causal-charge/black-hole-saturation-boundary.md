# Black Holes as a Saturation Boundary

Black-hole mechanics gives the strongest established illustration of energy, causal accessibility, entropy, area, and Noether charge meeting at one boundary. It supports treating horizon area as a geometric carrier of charge or capacity. It does not prove that spacetime is made from discarded information, that a black hole has no interior, or that generalized entropy is exactly conserved.

## The controlled relations

For a Schwarzschild black hole,

$$
r_s=\frac{2GM}{c^2},
\qquad
A=4\pi r_s^2,
$$

and

$$
S_{\mathrm{BH}}
=\frac{k_BA}{4\ell_P^2},
\qquad
k_BT_H
=\frac{\hbar c^3}{8\pi GM}.
$$

These imply the Smarr relation

$$
Mc^2=2T_HS_{\mathrm{BH}}
$$

and the first law for the nonrotating, uncharged family,

$$
\mathrm d(Mc^2)=T_H\,\mathrm dS_{\mathrm{BH}}.
$$

For stationary black holes with an appropriate bifurcate Killing horizon and a diffeomorphism-covariant Lagrangian, the Iyer--Wald framework identifies horizon entropy with a diffeomorphism Noether charge. Dynamical horizons require additional prescriptions. [[deriving-value-of-g/noether-capacity-theorem|The Noether--capacity theorem]] explains why the stationary result is a serious precedent for a common state--geometry charge, while also recording its normalization limits.

[[deriving-value-of-g/capacity-identities|The capacity identities]] give a related saturation statement: the ratio between a Bekenstein information load assigned to energy \(E\) in radius \(R\) and the corresponding area capacity equals the compactness ratio \(r_s(E)/R\). Equality at \(R=r_s\) is exact algebra after the standard bounds are adopted.

## The charitable causal-charge reading

The useful interpretation is:

> At gravitational saturation, energy, causal accessibility, and geometric boundary charge can no longer be treated as independent weak perturbations of one another.

The horizon is then a calibration boundary for the areal modulus in [[state-geometry-charge-weld]]. More mass corresponds to more area and entropy, while the Hawking temperature decreases. This is consistent with geometry acting as a stiff, extensive carrier for causal capacity.

The surface need not be imagined as a material membrane storing classical bits. A boundary charge can encode the contribution of a constrained gauge system without being a substance painted on the boundary.

## Claims not supplied by the area law

The standard formulas do not establish that

- the classical black-hole interior is absent;
- holographic encoding eliminates a bulk description;
- time stops invariantly at the horizon;
- the horizon area is operationally meaningless;
- \(c\), \(G\), \(\hbar\), or \(k_B\) attain maximum values; or
- a black hole maximizes entropy without specifying the conserved quantities and admissible comparison class.

An event horizon is globally defined, but its area can be inferred and quasi-local horizons can have measurable geometry. An infalling observer crosses a regular classical horizon in finite proper time. A theory that denies an interior must be presented as a new global ontology and must still preserve the exterior, horizon-mechanics, infall, lensing, ringdown, and evaporation regimes it claims to surround.

## Monotonicity is not conservation

Generalized entropy ordinarily obeys a monotonicity or generalized-second-law statement. A Noether balance instead relates charges and fluxes. These must not be collapsed:

$$
\text{entropy increase}
\neq
\text{charge conservation}.
$$

The causal-charge proposal may explain why an area term participates in the accounting, but the conserved object must include the appropriate modular, matter, edge, record, and flux sectors. Black-hole thermodynamics is a controlled exemplar of the grammar, not proof of the universal conjecture.
