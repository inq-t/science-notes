# Cosmological Shadows of Causal Charge

The binary wall pulse and the horizon-clock allocation may be two reductions of one causal-charge law. The first describes conversion within a fixed normal Casimir; the second allocates scale displacement between signed horizon rapidity and areal growth. Their formal resemblance is real, but no map presently identifies their variables or derives both from one symmetry object.

## Binary conversion shadow

The exact binary identities are

$$
\eta=\tanh\theta,
\qquad
G^{\mathrm{BKM}}_{\theta\theta}
=\operatorname{sech}^2\theta,
\qquad
1=\eta^2+G^{\mathrm{BKM}}_{\theta\theta}.
$$

If a fixed extensive capacity \(C_{\mathrm{tot}}\) is allocated by

$$
C_\pm
=\frac{C_{\mathrm{tot}}}{2}(1\pm\eta)
$$

and \(\theta=\nu(N-N_c)\), [[two-channel-conversion-law]] gives

$$
J_N
:=\frac{\mathrm dC_+}{\mathrm dN}
=-\frac{\mathrm dC_-}{\mathrm dN}
=\frac{\nu C_{\mathrm{tot}}}{2}
\operatorname{sech}^2\!\bigl(\nu(N-N_c)\bigr).
$$

If the extensive BKM norm uses the same conserved multiplicity,

$$
G^{\perp}_{NN}
=C_{\mathrm{tot}}\nu^2
\operatorname{sech}^2\!\bigl(\nu(N-N_c)\bigr),
$$

then

$$
G^{\perp}_{NN}=2\nu J_N.
$$

On the unit branch, the anchored source law can be written

$$
\rho_X
=\frac{k_BT_c}{V_c}J_N.
$$

This gives a precise conjectural reading of the cosmological pulse: the effective density is the fixed wall energy scale per crossing volume multiplied by a causal-capacity conversion current. It remains conditional on the physical channel, its conserved multiplicity, affine soldering, unit width, and constitutive source law.

## Horizon allocation shadow

For the flat expanding FLRW apparent horizon, [[causal-scale-theory/horizon-clock|the horizon-clock identity]] is

$$
\boxed{
\mathrm dN
=\mathrm d\widehat\eta_A
+\frac14\,\mathrm d\ln\mathcal S_A.
}
$$

It can be read as an equality of additive one-cocycles,

$$
c_{\mathrm{scale}}
=c_{\mathrm{boost}}
+c_{\mathrm{area\ capacity}}.
$$

As written, this follows from FLRW definitions. The conjecture is that scale, boost, and capacity are line bundles or charge fibers with compatible connections whose homogeneous pullback gives the displayed equation. Such a construction would explain why growing horizon area participates in the history of the causal arena rather than merely redescribing \(H(N)\).

The vertical rapidity \(\widehat\eta_A\), horizontal state coordinate \(\theta\), scale coordinate \(N\), and any record arrow remain distinct. Unit slope does not identify them.

## Proposed common origin

The strongest programme is that

$$
\begin{aligned}
1&=\eta^2+G^{\mathrm{BKM}}_{\theta\theta},\\
\mathrm dN&=\mathrm d\widehat\eta_A
+\frac14\mathrm d\ln\mathcal S_A,\\
Q_\xi[\Sigma_2]-Q_\xi[\Sigma_1]
+\Phi_\xi[W]&=0
\end{aligned}
$$

are respectively

- a Casimir allocation in the reduced normal channel;
- a connection allocation in homogeneous horizon geometry; and
- the full flux-inclusive Noether balance

of one causal-individuation structure.

The first two equations do not prove the third. The programme “snaps into place” only if explicit reduction maps derive them from the common carrier in [[causal-individuation-balance]].

## Empirical and structural risk

The conjecture fails in its minimal form if the wall has several unsuppressed normal modes, if channel multiplicity varies, if the state path is non-affine or has holonomy, if \(\nu\neq1\), if the \(\operatorname{sech}^2\) density admits no conserved covariant stress tensor, or if the horizon split has no invariant extension beyond the selected FLRW branch. A failure can preserve the broader causal-charge idea while falsifying this binary cosmological representation.
