# Sources for Sufficing and Necessitating Reason

The argument depends on several established literatures that should remain distinguishable from the proposed synthesis: commutative and operator algebra, quantum no-go theorems, universal coalgebra, irreversible endomorphism theory, and philosophical work on temporal asymmetry. These are the principal source landmarks inherited from the working document.

## Points, states, and quantum constraints

- Gelfand and Naimark (1943), commutative C\*-algebras and spectral representation.
- Gleason, “Measures on the Closed Subspaces of a Hilbert Space,” *Journal of Mathematics and Mechanics* 6 (1957), 885--893.
- Bell, “On the Einstein Podolsky Rosen Paradox” (1964).
- Kochen and Specker, “The Problem of Hidden Variables in Quantum Mechanics” (1967).
- Isham and Butterfield, “A Topos Perspective on the Kochen--Specker Theorem,” parts I--IV (1998--2002).
- Bong et al., “A Strong No-Go Theorem on the Wigner's Friend Paradox,” *Nature Physics* 16 (2020), 1199--1205.

## Algebra, coalgebra, and behavior

- Lambek, “A Fixpoint Theorem for Complete Categories,” *Mathematische Zeitschrift* 103 (1968).
- Adámek, work on terminal sequences and fixed points (1974).
- Aczel, *Non-Well-Founded Sets* (1988).
- Larsen and Skou, “Bisimulation through Probabilistic Testing,” *Information and Computation* 94 (1991).
- Rutten, “Universal Coalgebra: A Theory of Systems,” *Theoretical Computer Science* 249 (2000).
- Sokolova, “Probabilistic Systems Coalgebraically,” *Theoretical Computer Science* 412 (2011).
- Abramsky, “Big Toy Models” (2012), and “Coalgebras, Chu Spaces, and Representations of Physical Systems” (2013).
- Jacobs, *Introduction to Coalgebra* (2016).

## Modular theory and one-sided dynamics

- Bisognano and Wichmann, modular structure for wedge algebras, *Journal of Mathematical Physics* 16 (1975).
- Connes, cocycle Radon--Nikodym theory and the classification of type III factors.
- Wiesbrock, half-sided modular inclusions, *Communications in Mathematical Physics* 157 (1993), together with Borchers' related results.
- Connes and Rovelli, “Von Neumann Algebra Automorphisms and Time-Thermodynamics Relation,” *Classical and Quantum Gravity* 11 (1994).
- Arveson, *Noncommutative Dynamics and E-Semigroups* (2003).

The local [[causal-wall-spectral-theory/sources/entry|operator-algebra and QFT source library]] contains additional primary material on relative entropy, local covariance, observer algebras, horizons, and controlled half-sided modular structures.

## Statistical time and interpretation

- Price, *Time's Arrow and Archimedes' Point* (1996).
- The Everettian probability programmes of Deutsch and Wallace, and the self-locating uncertainty programme associated with Sebens and Carroll.
- The decoherence and einselection literature for dynamical selection of stable pointer observables.

These sources support particular mathematical results or interpretive alternatives. Their coexistence does not itself establish the two-reason synthesis or the algebraic arrow.
