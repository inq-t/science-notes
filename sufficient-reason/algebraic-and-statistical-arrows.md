# Algebraic and Statistical Arrows

A Boltzmannian arrow is a typicality claim about states relative to a reference measure and a low-entropy boundary condition. The proposed algebraic arrow instead locates asymmetry in a one-sided inclusion or endomorphism structure. The absence of a trace on a type III algebra removes a canonical Liouville-like measure, but it does not by itself produce an arrow, records, or a thermodynamic gradient.

## The contrast

| | Boltzmannian account | Proposed algebraic account |
|---|---|---|
| Ambient dynamics | reversible Hamiltonian group | reversible unitary or modular group |
| Asymmetric datum | low-entropy boundary condition and coarse-graining | half-sided inclusion or non-surjective endomorphism action |
| Measure | Liouville measure supplies typicality | no canonical tracial state on a type III factor |
| Locus of asymmetry | state and boundary condition | relation among algebras or accessible regions |
| Reversed behavior | atypical but possible | absent from the one-sided action on the chosen algebra |

This comparison is meaningful only after the algebraic column has been physically instantiated. A generic many-to-one restriction of states is ordinary loss of information and can be used in a coarse-graining account; it is not yet a new kind of temporal asymmetry.

## What type III structure establishes

A type III factor has no faithful normal trace. Consequently, there is no canonical tracial analogue of finite-dimensional uniform state-counting on the local algebra. Ordinary von Neumann entropy of a density matrix is also unavailable for a strictly local type III algebra.

These absences do not eliminate every statistical or entropic construction. Relative entropy, modular Hamiltonians in controlled cases, KMS structure, and generalized entropy can still be defined. The correct conclusion is narrower: a state-independent tracial reference measure is unavailable, so a Boltzmannian argument must say what replaces it.

For faithful normal states $\omega$ and $\varphi$, Connes' cocycle relation makes their modular automorphism groups equivalent modulo inner automorphisms. The induced outer modular flow is therefore attached to the algebra more canonically than either state-specific implementation. But outer modular flow is still a group. Direction enters only through additional one-sided structure such as a half-sided inclusion.

The continuum state mathematics relevant to the surrounding physics programme is kept distinct in [[basic-concepts/hessians/symmetrized-relative-entropy-hessian|the relative-entropy Hessian theorem]] and [[causal-wall-spectral-theory/conjectures/bkm-to-spatial-precision|the open state-to-spatial-precision conjecture]].

## Records and the past

The interpretive proposal is:

> The past is the direction in which stable records are available, and records are residues of a physically one-sided restriction or inclusion.

This resembles Kant's claim that objective succession requires a rule connecting appearances, but algebra alone has not yet supplied the record theory. Three further results are needed:

1. a construction of stable record subalgebras;
2. a proof that the proposed semigroup generates or preserves records in only one direction; and
3. a compatibility theorem giving the same orientation to interacting observers.

Thus the proposal may replace a boundary-condition question with an algebraic-compatibility question, but it has not yet derived memory, entropy increase, or a shared past.
