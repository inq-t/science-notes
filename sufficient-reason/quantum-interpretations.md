# Quantum Interpretation and the Type Change

Restricting a quantum state to a commutative context changes the kind of object used to describe it: the state becomes a classical probability measure on that context's spectrum. This clarifies what decoherence and the standard interpretations must supply, but it neither selects an outcome nor defines a physical collapse process.

## The exact type change

Given $i:\mathcal D\hookrightarrow\mathcal M$ and $\omega\in S(\mathcal M)$,

$$
\omega
\xmapsto{\ i^*\ }
\omega|_{\mathcal D}
\longleftrightarrow
\mu_{\omega,\mathcal D}\in\operatorname{Prob}(\operatorname{Spec}\mathcal D).
$$

This is a mathematically exact change of representation from a state on a noncommutative algebra to a measure on a commutative spectrum. It supplies neither

$$
\mu_{\omega,\mathcal D}\longrightarrow\chi
$$

nor a post-measurement state on $\mathcal M$. Those require, respectively, an outcome rule and a quantum instrument or other state-update law.

Consequently, “collapse is a type change” can mean one of two things:

- as a diagnosis of descriptions, it is exact;
- as a mechanism of fact formation, it is incomplete.

The question “when does collapse happen?” is malformed only for an interpretation that denies collapse is a temporal physical process. It remains well-formed for objective-collapse theories.

## Decoherence

Environmental decoherence can dynamically stabilize an approximate pointer algebra and suppress interference between selected sectors. In the present vocabulary it helps answer which $\mathcal D$ becomes a robust readout context. It does not, without further interpretive or dynamical structure, select one character of $\mathcal D$.

Thus two debts remain distinct:

$$
\text{context selection}
\ne
\text{outcome selection}.
$$

## Interpretive diagnostics

| Position | Treatment in this vocabulary | Outstanding burden |
|---|---|---|
| Copenhagen families | retain unitary dynamics and a distinct measurement rule | state the cut or event conditions precisely |
| Everettian families | retain only universal unitary evolution | justify probability and experienced branch-relative outcomes without circularly assuming the Born measure |
| Bohmian mechanics | add determinate configuration variables | accept and control the nonlocal structure required by Bell correlations |
| GRW/CSL | make fact formation a stochastic physical dynamics | meet experimental bounds and give a relativistically coherent mechanism |
| Relational/QBist families | treat facts or probabilities as relative to agents or systems | specify cross-perspective consistency and which no-go assumptions are rejected |

This table is diagnostic rather than exhaustive. The internal diversity of each family matters, and the no-go theorems constrain packages of assumptions rather than labels.

Extended Wigner's-friend and Local Friendliness results are especially relevant to relative-fact accounts: consistency requires naming the assumption abandoned, not merely asserting that facts are relative.

## Relation to causal-scale interpretation

The neighboring [[causal-wall-spectral-theory/interpretation|non-stochastic and cosmokinetic interpretation]] explicitly leaves probability, measurement, irreversible fact formation, and the Born rule open. A horizontal family of causal states can therefore provide a setting for this programme, but it cannot be cited as its solution.
