# Consequences of the $A_2$ Ternary Test

The ternary calculation changes the larger programme by turning balanced binarity from a convenient exact reduction into a falsifiable construction target. It supplies a natural three-sheet competitor, identifies the neutral mode that must be controlled, and separates algebraic monodromy, state selection, information response, wall forgetting, and spatial realization into distinct maps. It does not yet select a cosmology or replace the open BKM-to-spatial weld.

## The conceptual correction

The earlier binary branch begins after one has granted a balanced two-outcome generator. Within that branch,

$$
m^2+g_{\mathrm{bin}}=1
$$

and the $\operatorname{sech}^2$ response are exact. The three-sheet model shows that neither statement follows merely from the presence of an $A_2$ root direction. The corresponding natural identity is instead

$$
m^2+g_{A_2}+p_0=1.
$$

Thus the foundational question is no longer “why does the binary formula work so well internally?” It is:

$$
\boxed{
\text{What physical or mathematical construction makes }p_0
\text{ vanish or become observationally redundant?}}
$$

If no such construction exists, the ternary response is not an error term. It is the more natural finite response associated with the selected three-sheet model.

## A construction programme

### Build the state bundle

Over the semisimple locus, construct a finite $C^*$-algebra or finite von Neumann bundle whose local fibers are $\mathbb C^3$, together with a canonical faithful reference state and monodromy-equivariant transition maps. The holomorphic pushforward algebra alone does not provide the required positive involution.

Pure sheet relabeling preserves the uniform state and produces no physical horizontal response. Nonzero response therefore requires additional spectral data, for example a self-adjoint family $D_u$ whose Gibbs states vary while transforming equivariantly under monodromy.

### Select or transport the root tangent

The local generator $Q=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1,0)$ lies in one root direction. Full $S_3$ monodromy permutes such directions. A realization must either select one by additional structure, retain the full two-dimensional trace-zero response plane, or formulate the response equivariantly over the Weyl orbit. This is part of [[wall-construction-interface/cross-fiber-transport|cross-fiber transport]], not a choice of notation.

### Derive binarity or accept ternarity

Near a smooth discriminant component, two sheets form the local colliding pair and the third is spectrally separate. This makes a controlled two-level approximation plausible. The approximation becomes a theorem only after a gap estimate, adiabatic bound, conditional instrument, or sufficient-statistic result shows that the spectator contribution is absent below a declared error.

If the third branch remains active, retain $\Psi_{A_2}$ as the finite audit object and propagate its quadratic and higher response rather than forcing a $\operatorname{sech}^2$ fit. Calling it a physical member still requires the missing construction data listed above.

### Test the two shapes only after soldering

Suppose an independently derived affine law gives

$$
\theta=\nu(N-N_c).
$$

Then, at fixed nonzero $\nu$, the asymptotic shapes differ:

$$
g_{\mathrm{bin}}\sim4e^{-2\nu|N-N_c|},
\qquad
g_{A_2}\sim e^{-\nu|N-N_c|}.
$$

This is a possible signature, not yet a prediction. The raw ternary response satisfies $g_{A_2}(0)=2/3$; dividing by $2/3$ permits a shape comparison but does not produce a unit-response wall or a cosmology. [[causal-scale-theory/response-family-interface#The $A_2$ audit seed|The family interface]] owns that normalization warning. The soldering law, $\nu$, physical normalization, background source, and observable map must be constructed without fitting the same history used to choose between the profiles.

### Preserve the W2 firewall

Neither a finite Fisher coefficient nor its Hessian potential is a spatial inverse covariance. The carrier-changing map remains

$$
G^{\mathrm{BKM}}_{\zeta\zeta}
\xrightarrow{\ \mathcal W_{\mathrm{BKM}\to\mathrm{spatial}}\ }
\Gamma^{(2)}_{\zeta_{\mathrm{wall}}}.
$$

The ternary test changes the input that W2 may receive; it does not construct W2.

## What has become a matter of construction

The following choices cannot be called measured constants because they determine which mathematical object exists:

- whether the wall response has rank one or retains the two-dimensional $A_2$ trace-zero plane;
- whether the neutral sheet is absent, gapped, conditioned away, merged, or observable;
- which state and transport turn reversible monodromy into a nonzero state tangent;
- whether a proposed reduction is deterministic, postselected, state preserving, or only approximate; and
- whether the resulting BKM form lives on the same carrier as the CRF blocks consumed downstream.

Once those constructions are fixed independently, peak location, normalized shape, tail, higher response, and correlations become quantities that can be calculated and compared with measurement. This is the proper distinction between constructing the theory and measuring its signature.

## Failure and payoff

The binary member fails in this route if no canonical or controlled removal of the third mode exists, or if the pushed-forward family does not have balanced $\operatorname{sech}^2$ response. The $A_2$ route itself fails physically if its cover cannot be equipped with the required positive state geometry and realization maps.

The payoff is substantial even before either outcome. The programme now has two exact finite response calculations with different profiles and a clean construction gate between finite algebra and physical membership. Algebraic geometry is doing more than decorating a chosen response: it is exposing a hidden assumption and specifying the theorem required to justify it.
