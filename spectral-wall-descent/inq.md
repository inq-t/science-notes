# Spectral Wall Descent

Spectral wall descent asks how relative noncommutative spectral data could yield observable geometry. A conditional expectation specifies retained and erased distinctions; a finite-index correspondence with selected edge states can supply a central entropy operator; a separately constructed spectral area would give that operator a geometric interpretation. These stages require different data. Exact finite calculations constrain their possible relation, while the patchwise gravitational weld, de Sitter realization and observable dynamics remain open.

## A comparison must construct its observable carrier

The upstream datum is a candidate represented spectral package \(\Sigma_-=(\mathcal A_-,\mathcal H_-,D_-,J_-,\gamma_-,\omega_-)\). No information-conserving evolution or variational principle is imposed at this register. A proposed gravitational comparison must supply its observable algebra, positive state and admissible expectation; the later passage to a character and persistent record is a separate construction, generally outside finite-index descent.

[[spectral-wall-descent/scale-correspondence-stack|The scale-correspondence prestack]] specifies the composable algebraic data. A bare correspondence supplies neither a preferred CP map nor an edge state. Effective gluing, compatible centers, and the required state and geometric decorations must be constructed. [[spectral-wall-descent/observable-spectral-action|Observable spectral action]] acquires its ordinary variational meaning once the downstream represented geometry exists. This does not exclude an abstract internal spectral selector upstream; it prevents a chosen downstream action from silently supplying its own carrier.

Homogeneity of an algebra or context family is also distinct from an invariant physical state. [[higgs-reduction-as-local-shadow/symmetry-without-a-random-trigger|The invariant-state theorem]] separates those assumptions. An asymmetry-first construction still owes its effective observable symmetries.

## Lost response and retained stiffness have different directions

For a trace-preserving expectation on a finite matrix algebra and faithful density matrices \(\rho,\sigma\) with \(E\sigma=\sigma\), [[spectral-wall-descent/conditional-expectation-balance|conditional-expectation balance]] gives

\[
D(\rho\Vert\sigma)=D(E\rho\Vert\sigma)+D(\rho\Vert E\rho),
\qquad \sigma\in\operatorname{Ran}E,
\]

and \(D(\rho\Vert E\rho)=S(E\rho)-S(\rho)\), with densities and entropy computed in the declared ambient trace. At an adapted faithful reference, its Hessian splits retained and erased BKM tangents orthogonally. General state preservation does not imply an increase of absolute entropy: the note's nontracial qubit expectation has a positive relative-entropy loss even when its Schrödinger action lowers entropy.

This lost form vanishes on recovered output tangents. [[measured-response-carriers/descent-loss-cocycle-and-recovery-fork#The preserving-expectation Hessian is vertical|The recovery fork]] therefore blocks interpreting one expectation's defect as stiffness of its retained carrier. A family of descents may detect more directions, but its common kernel and quantitative relative position remain additional inputs. [[trace-dirichlet-descent/subfactor-angle-coercivity-and-the-index-firewall|The fixed-index angle counterexample]] shows why scalar inclusion sizes cannot replace that information.

Even the availability of an expectation is substantive. The modular invariance condition is required for a preserving expectation; under the usual cyclicity and separation hypotheses, proper nested vacuum local algebras cannot support the proposed vacuum-preserving shells. [[physical-response-coercivity/causal-frame-coercivity#Conditional-expectation shells|The shell obstruction]] requires a regulator, comparison carrier or weaker inequality. A final type-III-to-commutative expectation, when it exists, cannot have finite Pimsner--Popa index. [[conservation-of-causal-charge/factive-descent-and-records|Factive descent]] then distinguishes a probability law over characters from one obtained character and its record.

## Edge entropy, index capacity and geometric area require separate comparisons

[[finite-index-distinction/inq|Finite-index distinction]] separates conjugate normalization, maximum relative-entropy loss and minimum response. In the matched type-I product cell, [[spectral-wall-descent/finite-index-area-weld|the edge-entropy calculation]] gives

\[
S(\chi)+D(\chi\Vert\tau_d)
=\log d
=\tfrac12\log\operatorname{Ind}_{W}(E_\tau).
\]

This is a product-state budget for an auxiliary tracial expectation. It is not the unrestricted index capacity, and the code expectation selecting \(\chi\) need not equal \(E_\tau\). Finite-dimensional centers require sector and matrix-dimension data; more general centers require the full correspondence and further decomposition hypotheses.

[[spectral-wall-descent/ads-calibration-and-ds-carrier|AdS calibration]] fixes the direction of the controlled gravitational comparison: for the stated vacuum-ball perturbations with classical AdS duals, retained regional quantum Fisher information corresponds to gravitational canonical energy. In an exact complementary-recovery code, the fixed central edge term cancels from relative entropy. Identifying its operator \(\mathcal L_\chi\) with independently normalized spectral area is the separate proposed law

\[
\mathcal L_\chi(U)\stackrel{?}{=}\eta_*\mathcal A_D^Z(U)
\qquad\text{for every admissible patch }U.
\]

Local type-III algebras supply no ordinary absolute entropy stock. The construction needs appropriate edge-sector data, center-compatible transport, spectral area, and an independent normalization of \(\eta_*\). Neither the exact code identity nor AdS calibration supplies a de Sitter carrier or this all-patch weld.

## Relative geometry can change while an index class persists

[[spectral-wall-descent/finite-spectral-wall|The finite spectral wall]] holds the bulk Dirac spectrum fixed while its response relative to a chosen context varies, including a vanishing centered floor. Bulk eigenvalues alone therefore do not determine the context-relative response. [[spectral-wall-descent/index-and-curvature-transgression|Index and curvature transgression]] instead describes the persistence of a K-homology pairing under its bounded-perturbation hypotheses, while local curvature representatives and stabilizers change. Relating that class to physical curvature still requires a connection and soldering to tangent geometry.

The [[spectral-wall-descent/mixed-response-jet|mixed-response jet]] realizes another distinction: a vanishing quadratic cross block can have a nonzero first scale variation. The [[spectral-wall-descent/twist-fixed-point-wall|twisted fixed-point construction]] supplies an expectation onto a fixed algebra and a binary response after its additional state choice. Neither finite identity selects the physical state or proves an energy floor.

## Downstream elimination tests the proposed geometric return

[[spectral-wall-descent/heat-entropy-no-go|The heat-entropy calculation]] shows that normalized heat entropy cancels the term linear in the coefficient containing the Einstein--Hilbert action. [[spectral-wall-descent/response-determinant|The response--determinant comparison]] gives a different finite construction: one Gaussian hidden operator supplies a Schur correction and a determinant, with opposite signs for the positive Fisher Hessian and the bosonic effective action. Their relation depends on that operator and measure, rather than on calling entropy an action.

[[spectral-wall-descent/hidden-resolvent-and-seesaw|Hidden-resolvent elimination]] compares homogeneous response, nonconstant kernels and internal seesaw masses. The common operation is a Schur complement; the positive response block and complex symmetric Majorana mass matrix remain differently typed objects. In observable spectral geometry, the [[spectral-wall-descent/majorana-response-jacobian|Majorana response Jacobian]] relates changes in Einstein, cosmological, Higgs and seesaw data. The [[spectral-wall-descent/majorana-square-and-cosmic-pulse|Majorana square]] distinguishes the source's anisotropic stationary ray from the project's chosen hyperbolic orbit and retains its unresolved amplitude hierarchy. [[program-core/singlet-response-completion|Singlet response completion]] asks whether one positive scalar can repair a specified mismatch, using a rank and positivity test.

[[vendor/postquantum-gravity/inq|Postquantum gravity]] supplies a different downstream precedent: a positive history cost can describe stochastic classical--quantum dynamics without being a deterministic higher-derivative action. Its assumed classical carrier and conditioned-purity statements do not construct this wall or establish its unitarity. The proposed return still needs carrier realization, state selection, physical dynamics, and geometric normalization together.
