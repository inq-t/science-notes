# The Dimensional Ladder

Played strictly, dimensional analysis reorganizes gravity's constants into one picture: selected causal-horizon temperatures, accelerations, and expansion rates share one frequency type after their geometric solderings are declared; $G$ converts density into squared frequency and mass into acceleration-flux; the combinations $c^n/G$ organize characteristic scales and proposed extremal bounds; and nature's own measurement record exposes $GM$ much more directly than $G$. The split into $G\times M$ costs five digits of precision because it solders the gravitational register to the $\hbar$-referenced mass register. This module owns those rearrangements. They are standard physics, retyped, except where a bound or common-clock interpretation is explicitly marked conditional; the programme-facing consequences feed [[deriving-g-v2/entry|the G-free second pass]] and the construction bridges of [[nilpotency-and-the-wall/construction-bridges|the wall module]]. Claim labels follow [[program-core/axioms-and-principles#Status vocabulary|the status vocabulary]]; receipts for every number are in `receipts/`.

## The rearrangements in order

**1. Mass is acceleration-flux; gravity is volume response.** **[STANDARD]** Gauss's law reads $\oint \mathbf g\cdot\mathrm d\mathbf A=-4\pi GM$: the source register of gravity is a flux of acceleration, with units $\mathrm{m^3\,s^{-2}}$ — the cubed meters live here. The Einstein equation, restricted to a small ball of initially comoving test particles, is $\ddot V/V=-4\pi G(\rho+3p/c^2)$: fractional volume acceleration per density. [[mass-is-acceleration-flux]] owns both, with the typed reading of "gradient of space."

**2. Several horizon quantities have one frequency type; G converts density to frequency squared.** **[STANDARD DIMENSIONS + CONDITIONAL SOLDERING]** $a/c$, $H$, $\kappa/c$, and $k_BT/\hbar$ all have units of inverse time. In the corresponding Unruh, de Sitter, and black-hole settings they obey the same KMS conversion pattern, with the geometric relations needed to identify two rows belonging to the same horizon ([[deriving-g-v2/the-modular-register-is-g-free|the G-free modular audit]]). They are not one universal numerical frequency across unrelated systems. Separately, $G\rho=1/t^2$: the surface-orbit period of a spherical body depends only on its mean density (84 minutes at Earth's density, 198 at water's). Divide $G$ by specific volume and only $1/t^2$ remains. [[one-clock-many-dressings]] owns the typed clock reading.

**3. The ladder $c^n/G$ is a stack of characteristic scales and candidate bounds.** **[STANDARD DIMENSIONS + CONDITIONAL BOUNDS + INTERPRETATION]** $c^2/G$ is the compactness scale in mass per length; $c^3/G$ is a characteristic mass-throughput scale; $c^4/G$ and $c^5/G$ are the force and power scales entering proposed maximum-tension and maximum-luminosity principles. Black holes realize the compactness scale up to conventional factors and motivate comparing the other rungs, but no theorem says every black hole saturates every proposed bound. [[the-saturation-ladder]] owns the table and its use as a universality test set.

**4. Nature measures GM, never G.** **[EMPIRICAL RECEIPT]** Planetary ephemerides fix the Sun's $GM$ to about ten significant figures; CODATA fixes $G$ to five. The missing digits are the cost of the solder to the SI mass register — since 2019 itself defined by fixing $\hbar$ — so the badly measured object is precisely the exchange rate $\ell_P^2=\hbar G/c^3$, exactly as [[deriving-g-v2/rulers-are-matter|the ruler reading]] requires. [[gm-not-g]] owns this.

**5. Black holes sit at the Heisenberg edge.** **[STANDARD + interpretation]** The Compton radius falls with mass, the gravitational radius grows; they cross near $m_P/\sqrt2\approx8.6\times10^{18}$ GeV, and the generalized uncertainty relation $\Delta x\gtrsim\hbar/\Delta p+(G/c^3)\Delta p$ has its minimum $2\ell_P$ there. Past the crossover, resolution manufactures object: the black hole is the self-censoring particle. [[heisenberg-edge]] owns the statement and its boundary with [[conservation-of-causal-charge/black-hole-saturation-boundary|the saturation-boundary note]].
