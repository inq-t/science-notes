# The Dimensional Ladder

Played strictly, dimensional analysis reorganizes gravity's constants into one picture: every thermal-gravitational quantity is the rate of a single clock in different unit dressing; $G$ converts density into squared frequency and mass into acceleration-flux; the combinations $c^n/G$ are the saturation bounds of the registers, with black holes as the saturating objects; and nature's own measurement record exposes only $GM$, never $G$ — the split into $G\times M$ costs five digits of precision because it solders the gravitational register to the $\hbar$-referenced mass register. This module owns those rearrangements. They are standard physics, retyped; the programme-facing consequences feed [[deriving-g-v2/entry|the G-free second pass]] and the construction bridges of [[nilpotency-and-the-wall/construction-bridges|the wall module]]. ## The rearrangements in order

**1. Mass is acceleration-flux; gravity is volume response.** **[STANDARD]** Gauss's law reads $\oint \mathbf g\cdot\mathrm d\mathbf A=-4\pi GM$: the source register of gravity is a flux of acceleration, with units $\mathrm{m^3\,s^{-2}}$ — the cubed meters live here. The Einstein equation, restricted to a small ball of initially comoving test particles, is $\ddot V/V=-4\pi G(\rho+3p/c^2)$: fractional volume acceleration per density. [[mass-is-acceleration-flux]] owns both, with the typed reading of "gradient of space."

**2. Everything is a frequency; G converts density to frequency squared.** **[STANDARD, retyped]** $a/c$, $cH$, $\kappa/c$, and $k_BT/\hbar$ are all rates of the one modular clock ([[deriving-g-v2/the-modular-register-is-g-free|whose G-freeness is the second pass's anchor]]); and $G\rho=1/t^2$ — the surface-orbit period of any body depends only on its density (84 minutes at Earth's density, 198 at water's). Divide $G$ by specific volume and only $1/t^2$ remains. [[one-clock-many-dressings]] owns the clock reading; "time lurking in the denominator" is this, made exact.

**3. The ladder $c^n/G$ is a stack of saturation bounds.** **[STANDARD + interpretation]** $c^2/G$ (mass per length), $c^3/G$ (mass rate), $c^4/G$ (force — the stiffness whose inverse is Einstein's coupling), $c^5/G$ (power — the bandwidth at which [[deriving-g-v2/the-leak-register|the cosmos leaks]]). Black holes saturate every rung simultaneously; that is the typed reason future world-lines can terminate on them. [[the-saturation-ladder]] owns the table and its use as a universality test set.

**4. Nature measures GM, never G.** **[EMPIRICAL RECEIPT]** Planetary ephemerides fix the Sun's $GM$ to about ten significant figures; CODATA fixes $G$ to five. The missing digits are the cost of the solder to the SI mass register — since 2019 itself defined by fixing $\hbar$ — so the badly measured object is precisely the exchange rate $\ell_P^2=\hbar G/c^3$, exactly as [[deriving-g-v2/rulers-are-matter|the ruler reading]] requires. [[gm-not-g]] owns this.

**5. Black holes sit at the Heisenberg edge.** **[STANDARD + interpretation]** The Compton radius falls with mass, the gravitational radius grows; they cross near $m_P/\sqrt2\approx8.6\times10^{18}$ GeV, and the generalized uncertainty relation $\Delta x\gtrsim\hbar/\Delta p+(G/c^3)\Delta p$ has its minimum $2\ell_P$ there. Past the crossover, resolution manufactures object: the black hole is the self-censoring particle. [[heisenberg-edge]] owns the statement and its boundary with [[conservation-of-causal-charge/black-hole-saturation-boundary|the saturation-boundary note]].
