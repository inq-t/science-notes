# The Heisenberg Edge

A black hole can be typed as the fixed point of observation itself: the object for which resolving and enlarging are the same act. This note states the standard two-radius argument and the generalized uncertainty relation that glue the quantum and gravitational branches, and fixes what the slogan "observing it would take infinite energy" does and does not mean.

## Two radii

The Compton radius $\lambda_C=\hbar/mc$ falls with mass; the gravitational radius $r_s=2Gm/c^2$ grows. They cross at

$$
m_\times=\sqrt{\frac{\hbar c}{2G}}=\frac{m_P}{\sqrt2}
\approx8.6\times10^{18}\ \mathrm{GeV}.
$$

Below the crossover, an object's quantum extent exceeds its horizon scale and "particle" is the right type; above it, the horizon exceeds the quantum extent and the object is geometry. The generalized uncertainty relation interpolates the branches:

$$
\Delta x\;\gtrsim\;\frac{\hbar}{\Delta p}+\frac{G}{c^3}\,\Delta p ,
$$

with minimum $\Delta x_{\min}=2\ell_P$ at $\Delta p=m_Pc$ (receipts; the heuristic is standard — Maggiore, Phys. Lett. B **304**, 65 (1993); Kempf–Mangano–Mann, Phys. Rev. D **52**, 1108 (1995); Adler–Santiago, Mod. Phys. Lett. A **14**, 1371 (1999)). Note the gravitational term's coefficient: $G/c^3$ has units *time per mass* — one more rung of [[the-saturation-ladder|the ladder]], and one more appearance of time in the denominator per [[one-clock-many-dressings]].

## The UV/IR inversion

Past the minimum, $\mathrm d\Delta x/\mathrm d\Delta p>0$: adding resolution energy *grows* the object, because the probe's own $r_s$ dominates. Observation manufactures more horizon than it resolves — the black hole is the self-censoring particle, and "infinite energy to observe" is this inversion plus the horizon's infinite redshift, not a literal divergent budget. In programme terms the horizon is the wall at which the probe register saturates ([[conservation-of-causal-charge/black-hole-saturation-boundary|the saturation boundary]]), and what remains exchangeable across it is ledger bookkeeping, not microstate resolution — the reading [[deriving-g-v2/the-g-free-first-law|the G-free first law]] gives exactly.

## Boundary

The GUP is a heuristic family, not a theorem of quantum gravity; the crossover mass shifts by order-one conventions; and nothing here derives horizon thermodynamics — it locates the black hole in the type system: **the object pinned at the edge of the uncertainty principle, where the quantum register hands the bookkeeping to the causal one.** Any use beyond that typing must import its own construction.
