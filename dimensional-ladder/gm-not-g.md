# GM, Not G

Nature's own measurement record splits gravity's constant into a superbly known product and a badly known factor, and the split falls exactly where the ruler reading says it must. This note records the asymmetry and types it.

## The asymmetry

Orbits couple to the flux $GM$ ([[mass-is-acceleration-flux]]), so celestial mechanics determines $GM$ directly: the Sun's gravitational parameter is fixed by planetary ephemerides to about **ten significant figures** ($GM_\odot\simeq1.327\,124\,400\times10^{20}\,\mathrm{m^3\,s^{-2}}$). Meanwhile CODATA's $G$ carries a relative uncertainty of about $2.2\times10^{-5}$ — five digits, the worst-measured fundamental constant, with famously discrepant laboratory determinations. The missing five digits are not experimental embarrassment; they are the *cost of a register conversion*. To split $GM$ into $G\times M$ one must express $M$ in the SI mass register, and that register is anchored by laboratory matter (torsion-balance test masses) against the 2019 SI definition, which fixes the kilogram through $\hbar$.

## The typed reading

Since 2019, then, the split is precisely a solder between the gravitational flux register and the $\hbar$ register — and the composite whose precision suffers is exactly

$$
\ell_P^2=\frac{\hbar G}{c^3},
$$

the area of one ledger unit in [[deriving-g-v2/the-g-free-first-law|the presentation reading]]. Gravity's internal bookkeeping ($GM$, orbits, the ladder ratios) is precise; the exchange rate to the quantum register is what carries all the uncertainty. That is an empirical receipt for [[deriving-g-v2/rulers-are-matter|rulers are matter]] in its post-2019 form: the ruler register is $\hbar$-referenced, and $G$'s five digits are the current state of the solder — the very object the wall programme is trying to *derive* as an index-per-area ([[deriving-g-v2/index-not-entropy|the index note]]).

## Boundary

This note asserts a reading of measurement structure, not a mechanism. It would be embarrassed only by a measurement path that fixed $G$ alone, to ephemeris precision, without passing through any $\hbar$-referenced mass standard — no such path is known, and the reading predicts none exists.
