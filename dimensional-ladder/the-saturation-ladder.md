# The Saturation Ladder

In many gravitational formulas $G$ appears through $G/c^n$, and the inverse combinations $c^n/G$ organize characteristic scales in several registers. The compactness rung is realized by black-hole geometry up to conventional factors; interpreting the force and power rungs as universal maxima requires additional maximum-tension or maximum-luminosity principles. The ladder is therefore a cross-register test set for a derived gravitational coefficient, not a proof that every black hole saturates every rung. Numbers are receipt-asserted.

## The ladder

| n | c^n/G | Value | Register | Reading |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | c^2/G | 1.35e27 kg/m | mass per length | compactness scale (Schwarzschild relation; hoop criterion heuristic) |
| 3 | c^3/G | 4.04e35 kg/s | mass rate | characteristic gravitational mass-throughput scale; no universal bound asserted here |
| 4 | c^4/G | 1.21e44 N | force | proposed maximum-tension scale; Einstein's coupling $8\pi G/c^4$ is proportional to its inverse |
| 5 | c^5/G | 3.63e52 W | power | proposed maximum-luminosity scale; the imported FLRW flux is of this order |

The $n=4$ and $n=5$ bound readings belong to Gibbons' maximum-tension proposal (Found. Phys. **32**, 1891 (2002)) and related maximum-luminosity arguments; the $n=2$ hoop statement is likewise conjectural beyond controlled settings. The combinations themselves and their dimensions are exact. The user-facing slogans "$c$ is a stiffness" and "$c$ is a bandwidth" are interpretations of the $n=4$ and $n=5$ rows, with the caveat that the relevant composites are $c^4/G$ and $c^5/G$, not $c$ alone.

## What black holes establish

For a Schwarzschild black hole, $M/r_s=c^2/(2G)$ realizes the compactness scale exactly. Converting this relation with a light-crossing time produces quantities of order $c^3/G$, $c^4/G$, and $c^5/G$, which explains why the same dimensional ladder recurs in horizon physics. It does not prove universal maximum-force or maximum-power theorems, nor that every black hole attains those proposed maxima. Future-directed world-lines fail to escape because of the horizon's causal geometry; the ladder organizes associated scales but is not the cause of that causal fact. [[conservation-of-causal-charge/black-hole-saturation-boundary|The saturation-boundary note]] owns the programme interpretation, and [[deriving-g-v2/the-leak-register|the leak register]] owns the imported FLRW flux rearrangement.

## The ladder as a test set

Any candidate ledger density $\eta$ from the wall programme implies values for *all* rungs at once ($G_{\mathrm{pred}}=c^3/4\hbar\eta$ inserted into each row). The universality requirement of [[deriving-value-of-g/causal-scale-derivation-target|the derivation target]] — one coefficient across Newtonian, Friedmann, wave, and horizon sectors — is therefore checkable rung by rung: a derived $\eta$ that reproduced the entropy-area rung but violated the maximum-tension rung would be typing a non-Einstein constitutive sector, per the failure table there. The ladder adds no new physics; it arranges the checks.
