# The Saturation Ladder

In dynamics $G$ never stands alone; it appears as $G/c^n$, and each value of $n$ names a register whose extremal scale is $c^n/G$. Black holes saturate every rung at once, which is the typed reason future world-lines can terminate on them: the horizon is the state of maximal load in *all* registers simultaneously. The ladder also supplies the cross-register test set any derived ledger density must pass. Numbers are receipt-asserted.

## The ladder

| n | c^n/G | Value | Register | Reading |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | c^2/G | 1.35e27 kg/m | mass per length | compactness bound (hoop scale: r_s = 2Gm/c^2) |
| 3 | c^3/G | 4.04e35 kg/s | mass rate | the accretion/evaporation extremal scale |
| 4 | c^4/G | 1.21e44 N | force | maximum-tension scale; Einstein's coupling 8 pi G/c^4 is its inverse — the compliance |
| 5 | c^5/G | 3.63e52 W | power | maximum-luminosity scale; the cosmic leak runs at order half of it |

The $n=4$ and $n=5$ readings are Gibbons' maximum-tension principle (Found. Phys. **32**, 1891 (2002)) and its luminosity companion; the $n=2$ rung is the hoop-conjecture scale. The user-facing slogans "$c$ is a stiffness" and "$c$ is a bandwidth" are the $n=4$ and $n=5$ rows made literal — with the caveat that the *stiff* thing is the composite $c^4/G$: the arena's rigidity is a joint property of the causal speed and the ledger price, per [[deriving-g-v2/the-g-free-first-law|the presentation reading]].

## Why world-lines terminate

A horizon forms exactly when a register is driven to its rung (mass per length beyond $\sim c^2/G$ inside a hoop). Because the rungs are one constant in different dressings, saturating one drags the others: a maximal-compactness object is automatically the maximal-tension and maximal-power boundary object, and [[conservation-of-causal-charge/black-hole-saturation-boundary|the saturation-boundary note]] owns the charge-register version. Termination of future-directed world-lines on the horizon is the causal-order face of sitting on every extremal rung at once — nothing further can be loaded, so nothing further can be exported except through [[deriving-g-v2/the-leak-register|the leak register]].

## The ladder as a test set

Any candidate ledger density $\eta$ from the wall programme implies values for *all* rungs at once ($G_{\mathrm{pred}}=c^3/4\hbar\eta$ inserted into each row). The universality requirement of [[deriving-value-of-g/causal-scale-derivation-target|the derivation target]] — one coefficient across Newtonian, Friedmann, wave, and horizon sectors — is therefore checkable rung by rung: a derived $\eta$ that reproduced the entropy-area rung but violated the maximum-tension rung would be typing a non-Einstein constitutive sector, per the failure table there. The ladder adds no new physics; it arranges the checks.
