# One Clock, Many Dressings

Every thermal-gravitational quantity in the programme's registers is a frequency: $a/c$, $\kappa/c$, $H$, and $k_BT/\hbar$ all carry units $\mathrm s^{-1}$, and the theorems of [[deriving-g-v2/the-modular-register-is-g-free|the modular audit]] say they are the *same* frequency — the rate of the modular clock — read through different soldering constants. Meanwhile $G$ enters time bookkeeping exactly once: it converts density to squared frequency. This note fixes both statements and their programme consequences.

## The clock table

| Quantity | Expression | Dressing |
|---|---|---|
| proper acceleration | a = c times rate | c |
| expansion | H = rate | none |
| surface gravity | kappa = c times rate | c |
| temperature | k_B T = hbar times rate / 2 pi | hbar, k_B |
| scale age | N = log of accumulated scale | dimensionless |

The $2\pi$ between temperature and the rest is the KMS period — a theorem, not a choice. That one clock underlies all rows is exact for the horizon cases (Unruh, Gibbons–Hawking, Hawking-as-$\kappa$); treating *physical time itself* as modular flow is the Connes–Rovelli thermal-time reading, imported at [[cosmodynamics/soldering-constants|the soldering note's]] grade. The scale coordinate $N=\ln a_{\mathrm{scale}}$ of [[cosmodynamics/scale-age|scale age]] and [[misner-log-time/entry|Misner log time]] is the dimensionless log of this clock's accumulation — which is why the normalization of its rate against modular time is a construction gate and not a convention ([[nilpotency-and-the-wall/construction-bridges|Bridge 2]]).

## G converts density to frequency squared

$$
[G\rho]=\mathrm s^{-2}:
\qquad
t_{\mathrm{dyn}}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{G\rho}} .
$$

Divide $G$ by specific volume ($V/M$) and what remains is $1/T^2$ — the clean content of "$G$ has cubed meters in it." Receipts: a circular orbit at the surface of *any* body of Earth's mean density takes 84.3 minutes — of water, 198 minutes — independent of size; Friedmann ($H^2=\tfrac{8\pi}{3}G\rho$) and Jeans are the same statement at other carriers. Gravity has no scale of its own in the time register: it inherits one from whatever density it is fed. This is the dynamical face of the dimensional lemma in [[deriving-g-v2/rulers-are-matter|rulers are matter]] — as the value of $G$ is relational to a matter ruler, the *tempo* of gravity is relational to a matter density.

## Programme consequence

When [[causal-scale-theory/horizontal-temperature|the horizontal-temperature identification]] sets the anchored temperature to the apparent-horizon temperature, it is asserting a row-identification in the clock table: the wall's thermal rate is the expansion rate. In clock language the open CST principles read: $\nu=1$ says the scale clock and the modular clock tick together at the wall; $\mathfrak R_c=1$ says one ledger unit per tick per channel ([[deriving-g-v2/index-not-entropy|the index note]]). Neither is proved here; the point is that both are statements about one clock's dressings, which is what makes [[nilpotency-and-the-wall/construction-bridges|Bridge 2's]] group-theoretic normalization a live route to the first.
