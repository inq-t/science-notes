# Leibniz

A Leibnizian physics programme treats identity, discernibility, possibility, actuality, and sufficient reason as constraints on what counts as an explanation: a fitted parameter or successful representation is not yet the ground of the structure represented. This module is a philosophical synthesis; [[sufficient-reason/inq|sufficient reason]] and [[philosophy/indiscernibility-of-identicals/inq|indiscernibility]] remain the exact owners of those two doctrines in the working graph.
