# A Cosmos as the Structure of Facts

A cosmos is the intrinsic organization by which facts can be definite, mutually related, locally accessible, and parts of one history. It is neither the aggregate of all factual content nor a spectator's representation of that aggregate. “The whole as whole” names closure and internal coherence, not exhaustive knowledge from outside.

## Four different objects

| Term | Object |
|---|---|
| Universe as inventory | all physical contents taken collectively |
| Cosmological model | a mathematical representation of large-scale spacetime, matter, and observations |
| Observer's world | the facts and records accessible from a situated causal standpoint |
| Cosmos | the structure making facts, standpoints, accessibility, metric relations, and common history jointly possible |

Cosmodynamics concerns the fourth object. It can constrain cosmological models, but it is not identical with observational cosmology.

## Arena-forming rather than an arena

Calling a cosmos an *arena* is useful only if the arena is not imagined as a completed stage prior to events. The intended dependence is constitutive:

$$
\text{cosmic structure}
\longrightarrow
\begin{cases}
\text{event and causal relation},\\
\text{observer and accessibility},\\
\text{metric comparison},\\
\text{fact and record},\\
\text{history and orientation}.
\end{cases}
$$

The arrow expresses a grounding proposal. It is not a temporal production process, because time is among the structures to be grounded.

## Global structure without an external observer

A property can concern a whole without being known from outside it. Connectedness, orientability, and consistency of local charts are intrinsic global properties. Their definitions quantify over relations internal to the object.

The analogous cosmodynamic task is local-to-global: observer-relative facts and algebras must agree on overlaps, records must be transportable across causal channels, and local temporal orientations must compose into a common history. A “whole as whole” is achieved by compatibility and closure conditions, not by summing every fact.

This suggests a sheaf-like or categorical form, but no such construction is yet supplied. The distinction is philosophical until the local objects, overlap maps, and gluing conditions are defined.

## Fact is not yet primitive enough

The [[sufficient-reason/entry|sufficing and necessitating reason]] module proposes a contextual fact as a character of a commutative readout algebra and separates a probability measure from a selected point. Cosmodynamics needs at least that distinction, but it also needs a physical account of realizability, persistence, causal availability, and agreement among observers.
