# Cosmodynamics

Cosmodynamics is the study of the constitutive structure by which facts can be causally ordered, metrically related, locally accessible, recorded, and composed into one irreversible history. A cosmos in this sense is not an inventory of everything that exists or a view from outside the universe; it is the internally articulated form that makes fact, locality, scale, duration, possibility, and history jointly meaningful. Causal Scale Dynamics is one proposed scale--information sector of this larger programme, not yet a complete cosmodynamics.

## Object of inquiry

Cosmology ordinarily studies models of the observed universe: expansion history, matter content, perturbations, and their data. Cosmodynamics asks a prior question:

> What structure must exist for there to be locally accessible facts with metric relations and a common history at all?

The word *cosmos* therefore names neither the set of all objects nor a container in which facts occur. It names the organization in virtue of which there can be

$$
\text{possibilities}
\longrightarrow
\text{facts}
\longrightarrow
\text{records}
\longrightarrow
\text{history},
$$

together with causal order, metric scale, quantum state, accessibility, and entropy. The arrows are an analysis of required distinctions, not yet a derived dynamics.
