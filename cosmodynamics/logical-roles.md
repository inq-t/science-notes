# Logical Roles of Physical Theories

Newtonian mechanics, QFT, GR, Causal Scale Dynamics, and cosmodynamics differ here by explanatory role rather than by a hierarchy of length scales. The distinction permits successful local theories to remain intact while asking for additional compatibility and constitutive laws; it does not make cosmodynamics automatically deeper or exempt it from recovering their tested regimes.

## Division by object of inquiry

| Framework | Primary role in this programme |
|---|---|
| Newtonian mechanics | trajectories and forces in a largely given spatial and temporal arena |
| QFT | local quantum observables, states, amplitudes, and correlations |
| GR | dynamical metric-causal geometry constrained by stress-energy and boundary data |
| Causal Scale Dynamics | proposed constitutive relation among metric scale, state distinguishability, horizon capacity, and homogeneous response |
| Cosmodynamics | compatibility laws by which local possibilities, geometry, facts, records, and orientation form one cosmos |

These roles overlap. QFT applies at macroscopic distances, GR applies locally, and neither theory is confined to a size range.

## Representation is not constitutive closure

Einstein's equation can be written schematically as

$$
G_{ab}+\Lambda g_{ab}
=\frac{8\pi G}{c^4}T_{ab}.
$$

Once the matter model, $\Lambda$, initial data, and boundary conditions are specified, GR determines the compatible metric evolution. It does not by itself select the matter sector, explain the numerical value of $\Lambda$, or solve the radiative vacuum-energy problem.

Thus GR represents accelerated FLRW histories perfectly well after an accelerating source or cosmological constant is supplied. The further question is why that source law and scale are physically selected. Causal Scale Theory proposes one answer, whose assumptions are exposed in [[causal-scale-theory/closure-stack|the closure stack]].

## “Leaving QFT alone”

A surrounding framework may take local QFT as input rather than derive the Standard Model. Schematically,

$$
O\longmapsto(\mathcal A_O,\omega_O)
$$

can be the local fiber, while new structure compares fibers across regions, horizons, or physical scale.

This division of labor is legitimate only if the new theory constructs the comparison maps and preserves locality, unitarity, conservation, renormalized observables, and the observed Standard Model sector in its appropriate limit. Coexistence is not recovery. [[compatible-with-existing-physics/local-physics-interface|The local-physics interface]] and [[causal-wall-spectral-theory/causal-scale-interface|the causal-scale interface]] state those burdens.

## Retyping quantum gravity

The conjectural strategic shift is that metric, quantum state, entropy, and fact formation may be representations of one typed structure rather than independent objects to be combined only after quantizing $g_{ab}$. This can change the problem formulation, but it is not a solution to quantum gravity until it provides a mathematical structure that recovers controlled GR and QFT regimes and yields new consistency conditions or predictions.

Dissatisfaction with vacuum energy, measurement, singularities, or ultraviolet completion motivates the search. It is not evidence for a particular replacement.
