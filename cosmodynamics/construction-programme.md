# A Construction Programme for Cosmodynamics

A full cosmodynamics needs one mathematical package containing facts, causal order, local observables and states, metric scale, accessibility, records, and orientation, together with maps that recover GR and QFT in their tested regimes. The tuple below is a typing scaffold, not an existing theory.

## Candidate signature

Write schematically

$$
\mathfrak C=
\bigl(
\mathsf F,
\mathsf{Reg},
\preceq,
\mathcal A,
\mathcal S,
[g],
\sigma,
\mathsf{Acc},
\mathsf{Rec},
\Phi
\bigr),
$$

where:

- $\mathsf F$ is the object or event type of facts;
- $\mathsf{Reg}$ is a category or ordered family of causal regions;
- $\preceq$ is causal or objective precedence;
- $\mathcal A$ assigns observable algebras to regions;
- $\mathcal S$ assigns admissible states and their comparison structure;
- $([g],\sigma)$ supplies conformal geometry and metric calibration;
- $\mathsf{Acc}$ states observer-relative accessibility;
- $\mathsf{Rec}$ is a stable record relation; and
- $\Phi$ transports or compares state, scale, and record data.

Every symbol requires a category, variance, domain, and compatibility law. Merely placing them in one tuple does not unify them.

## Construction obligations

**C1 — Facts and outcomes.** Define a physical fact and construct the map from quantum probability to realized contextual value, or state precisely why no such absolute map is required. The [[sufficient-reason/theorem-programme|theorem programme for sufficient reason]] gives the narrower coalgebraic version.

**C2 — Local-to-global composition.** Specify restriction and overlap maps among observer regions and prove a gluing or descent theorem for mutually accessible facts and records.

**C3 — Geometry.** Recover causal order, conformal structure, metric scale, and proper time with the correct local covariance and gravitational dynamics.

**C4 — State and scale transport.** Construct the scale-indexed algebra/state family and its horizontal comparison independently of the cosmological response it is intended to explain. [[causal-scale-master-v8/wall-state-construction|Wall-state construction]] is the current focused obligation.

**C5 — Records and orientation.** Define stable records, produce a one-sided physical evolution or inclusion, and prove that local arrows combine into a shared orientation.

**C6 — Soldering laws.** Derive or explicitly postulate the maps by which causal, metric, quantum, thermodynamic, and informational quantities become mutually calibrated. Unit dependence and dimensionless invariants must remain explicit.

**C7 — Recovery.** Exhibit regimes in which local QFT, the Standard Model, and GR emerge with their tested locality, unitarity, gauge, conservation, and perturbative properties. “Surrounding” existing physics is acceptable only with this interface theorem.

**C8 — Empirical closure.** Derive background and perturbation observables from the independent structure, then test them with documented likelihoods. [[causal-wall-spectral-theory/open-problems|The causal-wall theorem programme]] contains the specialized perturbation obligations.

## Failure conditions

The programme fails in its strong form if any of the following persists in principle rather than temporarily:

- the proposed package only redescribes GR and QFT without a new consistency law;
- fact, probability, and record cannot be distinguished mathematically;
- local observer descriptions admit no coherent gluing into a common history;
- the proposed arrow is merely a reversible flow with an orientation chosen by hand;
- the scale--state law must be reconstructed from the observations it claims to explain;
- the new horizontal structure violates or cannot recover established local physics; or
- no calculable observable distinguishes the physical sector from an unconstrained effective source.

The programme may also succeed only partially. Causal Scale Dynamics could furnish a viable scale--information constitutive sector even if the larger theory of fact formation remains open; that would be a sector result, not full cosmodynamics.
