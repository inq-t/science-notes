# Fact, Record, and History

Full cosmodynamics requires more than a quantum state and a metric: it needs a physical distinction between possibility and fact, a criterion for stable records, a compatibility law among observers, and an irreversible composition of records into history. None of these is yet supplied by Causal Scale Dynamics or Causal-Wall Spectral Theory.

## Fact

The [[sufficient-reason/facticity-and-pointing|facticity and pointing]] note models a contextual fact by a character

$$
\chi:\mathcal D\to\mathbb C
$$

of a commutative readout algebra. A quantum state restricted to $\mathcal D$ gives a probability measure on such characters, not a selected character.

Cosmodynamics must add physical conditions. At minimum, an account of fact must state:

1. the readout context in which the value is definite;
2. the event or process by which one value obtains;
3. the state update, if any;
4. the causal region in which the fact is available; and
5. the compatibility rule for other observers' descriptions.

## Record

A record is not merely another correlated variable. A candidate record relation

$$
f\rightsquigarrow r
$$

should require that $r$ is reliably correlated with fact $f$, persists under an explicitly defined class of later dynamics, and can enter subsequent causal or inferential processes. Redundant encoding may strengthen objectivity, but redundancy alone does not define truth or actuality.

Stability is necessarily relative to dynamics and tolerance. Without a specified evolution, subsystem structure, and error criterion, “stable record” remains an informal phrase.

## History

A history should be more than a parameterized curve of states. It is a compatible ordered structure of facts and records,

$$
(\mathsf F,\preceq,\mathsf R),
$$

where $\preceq$ records causal or objective precedence and $\mathsf R$ records which later structures preserve evidence of earlier facts. The physical arrow would be the orientation in which record structure accumulates or is preserved.

A one-sided endomorphism semigroup is a candidate algebraic carrier for such an orientation, but [[sufficient-reason/algebraic-arrow-of-time|the algebraic arrow of time]] explains why the bridge to records is still missing.

## Present and common history

A present boundary might be modeled as a maximal antichain, an observer-dependent causal cut, or a frontier of settled records. These are different proposals and need not coincide.

Likewise, observer-relative facts form one cosmos only if their overlap data glue consistently. Agreement cannot mean that all observers access the same facts; it must mean that when records become jointly comparable, their translations satisfy an explicit consistency law. This is the local-to-global burden hidden inside the phrase “one common history.”

Horizontal change through a family of states may eventually participate in fact formation. At present it compares scale-indexed states and does not supply an outcome rule, a record dynamics, or a present frontier.
