# Constants as Soldering Structures

The constants $c$, $\hbar$, $G$, and $k_B$ relate quantities belonging to different physical registers: causal-metric calibration, quantum phase and action, curvature and stress, dimensionless information and thermodynamic entropy. Calling them soldering structures describes this relational role; it does not derive their values or show that they arise from one underlying mechanism.

## Four conversion roles

| Constant | Dimensional relation | Structural role |
|---|---|---|
| $c$ | length / time | calibrates temporal and spatial components of Lorentzian metric descriptions |
| $\hbar$ | energy $\times$ time | converts dimensionless quantum phase into physical action |
| $G$ | curvature / stress coupling through $8\pi G/c^4$ | relates energy-momentum response to metric curvature and horizon area capacity |
| $k_B$ | entropy / dimensionless information | converts dimensionless statistical entropy into thermodynamic units |

Examples include

$$
\text{phase}=\frac{S}{\hbar},
\qquad
S_{\mathrm{therm}}=k_B\,s,
\qquad
\frac{S_A}{k_B}=\frac{Ac^3}{4G\hbar}.
$$

These equations display bridges among registers. They do not establish that the constants are ontologically prior to the structures they relate.

## Numerical values and invariant content

The numerical value of a dimensionful constant changes with the unit system. A first-principles derivation must therefore produce either

- a unit-independent structural law;
- a dimensionless ratio;
- or a scale fixed operationally by other defined quantities.

Setting $c=\hbar=k_B=1$ removes conversion factors from notation but does not erase the distinct types they connect. Conversely, interpreting a unit-dependent decimal as a pure number confuses convention with physics.

The [[deriving-value-of-g/inq|attempt to classify or derive $G$]] asks whether gravitational coupling can be re-expressed through causal information capacity. The [[deriving-fine-structure-constant/inq|fine-structure programme]] concerns a dimensionless running coupling and must additionally specify gauge algebra, representations, normalization, thresholds, and renormalization scale. Neither problem is solved by calling a constant a soldering map.

## The cosmodynamic question

The productive question is not merely “what number is the constant?” but:

> Which independently typed structures does the constant make mutually measurable, and what compatibility law fixes that map?

An answer becomes explanatory only if the source and target structures are constructed independently and the conversion law is derived or falsifiably postulated rather than read backward from the measured value.
