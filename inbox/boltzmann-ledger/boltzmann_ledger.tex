\begin{abstract}
Boltzmann's constant, like $c$ and $\hbar$, is an exact convention; what it
marks is not. It marks that entropy is a dimensionless count and that
temperature is a derived rate --- in the algebraic setting of the program,
the \emph{modular} rate: $\kB T=\hbar/2\pi$ per unit of modular flow in
proper time. Taking that seriously turns out to reorganize the framework.
The master equation $Z=\pi/C$ is exactly the fused
Bekenstein--Hawking--Gibbons--Hawking law $4\pi S\hat T^2=1$, with
$E=TS$ as the wall's Euler identity; Branch~A becomes one sentence
(``the squared temperature of the world is completely monotone in
e-folds''), and the branch fork becomes the third-law question
(temperature floor $\hat T_\infty=\sqrt{\mu_0}/2\pi$ with finite entropy,
or $T\to0$ with $S\to\infty$). The Branch-B valuation $p(N)$ is
\emph{exactly} a Fermi--Dirac occupation --- forced, not chosen, because
facts are projections ($P^2=P$) --- so dark energy is the thermal
broadening of a fermionic level crossing: the Schottky heat capacity of
the $\{0,g\}$ pair, two e-folds wide because the KMS temperature is one.
Under peak--equality the magnitude problem contracts to a theorem:
$\hat\kappa\,C_c=\tfrac34$ exactly, $\hat\rho_*C_c=\tfrac{3}{16}$,
$\hat E_X(\mathrm{peak})=\sqrt{C_c}/4\sqrt\pi$ --- the entire dark sector
is pure numbers times one dimensionless count, the capacity at the
crossing, $C_c\simeq1.49\times10^{122}$. ``Why so small'' and ``why now''
fuse into a single question about a single count, whose scale matches the
modular-fluctuation law $\langle\Delta K^2\rangle=\langle K\rangle$
(established for CFT vacua on balls, conjectured gravitationally) and the
Poisson scaling of Everpresent $\Lambda$. Two numerological temptations
are declined on the record; one ($3/4$) turned out to be a theorem.
\end{abstract}

\section{What the constant marks}\label{sec:marks}

Since 2019, $\kB$ is exactly $1.380649\times10^{-23}$ J/K by definition:
like $c$ and $h$, it is a translation coefficient, not a fact about
nature. Its content is what it translates. $S/\kB$ is a pure count
(nats); $\kB T$ is an energy, and hence a rate: temperature is not a new
dimension of the world but the price of energy in entropy,
$1/T=\partial S/\partial E$. In the algebraic setting the program already
inhabits, this becomes precise in a stronger way: a state's modular flow
$\sigma_t=\mathrm{Ad}\,\Delta^{it}$ satisfies KMS at $\beta=1$ in modular
time, so \emph{temperature is the exchange rate between modular time and
proper time},
\begin{equation}\label{eq:modularT}
\kB T \;=\; \frac{\hbar}{2\pi}\times
\bigl(\text{modular flow rate per unit proper time}\bigr),
\end{equation}
the Connes--Rovelli thermal-time reading \cite{ConnesRovelli}. Every
famous temperature is an instance: Unruh's $\hbar a/2\pi c\kB$
(Bisognano--Wichmann: boosts are modular flow \cite{BW1976,Unruh1976}),
Hawking's $\hbar\kappa/2\pi c\kB$ \cite{Hawking1975}, Gibbons--Hawking's
$\hbar H/2\pi\kB$ \cite{GH1977}.

Hawking's formula is the Rosetta stone of the constants question:
\[
T=\frac{\hbar\,\kappa}{2\pi\,c\,\kB},\qquad
S=\frac{\kB c^3 A}{4G\hbar}.
\]
Four dimensionful constants each perform one translation ($\hbar$:
modular rate $\to$ energy; $c$, $G$: geometry $\to$ rate and count;
$\kB$: energy $\to$ temperature, count $\to$ entropy) --- and the physics
resides entirely in two pure numbers: the $2\pi$ (KMS periodicity of
modular flow) and the $\tfrac14$ (the capacity normalization). The
program already \emph{owns} the $2\pi$: it is the Borchers commutation
relation $\Delta^{it}U(s)\Delta^{-it}=U(e^{-2\pi t}s)$ of the arrow
postulate \cite{Borchers1992,Ruble2026a}, the same $2\pi$ as in R2's
$N=2\pi t_{\rm mod}$. The $\tfrac14$ is imported with
$C=A/4\lP^2$ \slab{established import}; Question~O7 below records the one
place the framework generates its own quarter.

\smallskip
The master equation of \cite{Ruble2026a} is therefore not merely
\emph{analogous} to horizon thermodynamics: it \emph{is} the
Bekenstein--Hawking entropy and the Gibbons--Hawking temperature fused
into one pure-number law, $4\pi S\hat T^2=1$, holding at every epoch on
the FRW wall and at the de Sitter fixed point
($\mu_0C_\infty=\pi\Leftrightarrow4\pi S_\infty\hat T_\infty^2=1$). The
first-law route from $\delta Q=T\,dS$ on the apparent horizon to the
Friedmann equation is Cai--Kim \cite{CaiKim2005}, in Jacobson's lineage
\cite{Jacobson1995}; the dictionary above is its integrated,
dimensionless form.

\begin{remark}[Black holes as the other wall type]
For Schwarzschild: $S=4\pi\hat M^2$, $\hat T=1/8\pi\hat M$, Smarr
$M=2TS$, and $4\pi S\hat T^2=\tfrac14$. The pure-number invariant
$4\pi S\hat T^2$ takes the value $1$ on the cosmological wall and
$\tfrac14$ on the black-hole wall --- a clean, dimensionless label
distinguishing the two ways causality terminates. What the fact algebra
assigns to this invariant is open. \slab{derivation; open reading}
\end{remark}

\section{The descent is a cooling curve}\label{sec:cooling}

The flowed weights $z_i(N)=\mu_ie^{-\lambda_iN}$ are a Gibbs structure
with the e-fold number as inverse temperature: $\beta=N$, energies
$\lambda_i$, partition function $Z(N)=\sum_iz_i$. Then, exactly:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:calorimetry}
\langle\lambda\rangle=-\D\ln Z\;\;(\text{internal energy}),\qquad
\Var(\lambda)=\D^2\ln Z\;\;(\text{heat capacity}),\qquad
\D q=\tfrac12\bigl(\langle\dot\lambda\rangle-\Var(\lambda)\bigr).
\end{equation}
The checkpoint's no-rebound identity is calorimetry: \emph{the universe's
deceleration changes at half the rate its grade spectrum stores heat}.
Two conjugate readings of the same kernel coexist and should not be
conflated: the \emph{cooling} reading ($\beta=N$ grows, fixed levels; the
descent cools the wall toward the $L$-ground state, so $\Lambda$CDM's
$\Lambda$ is ground-state occupation and matter is a still-populated
excited grade) and the \emph{KMS} reading ($\beta=1$ fixed at the modular
value, levels swept linearly in modular time by the Borchers rescaling).
The KMS reading is the modular-covariant one; the cooling reading is the
bookkeeping that makes \eqref{eq:calorimetry} literal.

In this language Branch~A is one sentence:
\begin{center}
\emph{The squared temperature of the world, $(2\pi\hat T)^2=Z(N)$, is a
completely monotone function of e-folds.}
\end{center}
Complete monotonicity --- the Bernstein condition of \cite{Ruble2026c}
--- is the statement that the cooling curve is monotone with all
derivatives alternating: cooling without rebound, reheating, or ripple.
And the branch fork is the \textbf{third-law question}: Branch~A halts at
the Gibbons--Hawking floor $\hat T_\infty=\sqrt{\mu_0}/2\pi>0$ with
finite entropy $S_\infty=\pi/\mu_0$ (type $\mathrm{II}_1$); Branch~B
cools to absolute zero, $\hat T\propto1/a\to0$, with entropy unbounded,
$S\propto a^2\to\infty$ (type $\mathrm{II}_\infty$). DESI, read
calorimetrically, is asking whether the universe has a temperature floor.

\section{The defect is fermionic}\label{sec:fermionic}

\begin{theorem}[Fermi--Dirac occupation]\label{thm:FD}
\slab{derivation} Let $P$ be the Branch-B projection with two-block
flowed weights of grades $\{\lambda_1,\lambda_2\}$, gap
$g=\lambda_2-\lambda_1$. Then the valuation is exactly a Fermi--Dirac
function of e-folds,
\begin{equation}
p(N)=\frac{1}{1+e^{-g(N-N_c)}},
\end{equation}
with $N_c$ the equipoise. The two-valuedness is forced: $P^2=P$ means the
fact has spectrum $\{0,1\}$ --- occupation at most one --- so the
logistic law of B2 is not an ansatz among sigmoids but the unique
statistics of a projection-valued fact. A Bose--Einstein form
$1/(e^{x}-1)$ is excluded by idempotency (and would diverge at the
crossing).
\end{theorem}

\begin{corollary}[Dark energy as thermal broadening / Schottky
anomaly]\label{cor:schottky}
\slab{derivation} $\rho_X\propto p(1-p)=\tfrac1g\D p$ is the thermal
broadening function $-\partial n/\partial\varepsilon$ of the level
crossing, equivalently the Schottky heat capacity of the two-level pair:
$\Var_{\rm pair}(\lambda)=g^2p(1-p)$, maximal at equipoise. The observed
dark energy is the Schottky bump of the wall's two-level system as the
descent sweeps the level through zero; the present epoch sits at the
peak (companion fits: $\D q|_0=0.00\pm0.03$). Widths are fixed by KMS
normalization ($\beta=1$): the density bump has full width at half
maximum $\tfrac4g\operatorname{arccosh}\sqrt2=1.76$ e-folds and the
occupation swings 10\%--90\% in $2\ln9/g=2.20$ e-folds ($g=2$).
\emph{Dark energy lasts about two e-folds because the modular temperature
is one.}
\end{corollary}

This answers, at derivation level, a ``why'' the fits alone could not:
why $w_0\approx-0.8$ rather than $-0.99$ --- because the transition
width is a KMS unit, not a tunable.

\section{The crossing-capacity theorem}\label{sec:crossing}

\begin{theorem}[Magnitude, made exact]\label{thm:crossing}
\slab{derivation, given B4$'$} Under peak--equality
($\rho_X(N_c)=\rho_m(N_c)=\rho_*$), let $C_c\equiv C(N_c)$ be the wall
capacity at the crossing. Then, exactly (radiation neglected),
\begin{equation}\label{eq:crossing}
\hat\kappa\,C_c=\frac34,\qquad
\hat\rho_*\,C_c=\frac{3}{16},\qquad
\hat E_X(\mathrm{peak})=\frac{\sqrt{C_c}}{4\sqrt{\pi}},\qquad
\hat T(N_c)=\frac{1}{2\sqrt{\pi C_c}} .
\end{equation}
\emph{Proof.} At the peak $\rho_{\rm tot}=2\rho_*$, so
$Z(N_c)=\tfrac{8\pi}{3}\cdot2\hat\rho_*$ and
$C_c=\pi/Z(N_c)=3/16\hat\rho_*$; the rest is substitution
($\hat\kappa=4\hat\rho_*$;
$\hat E_X=\hat\rho_*\cdot\tfrac{4\pi}{3}Z^{-3/2}$). $\square$
\end{theorem}

The entire dark sector is now \emph{pure numbers times one dimensionless
count}: $C_c\simeq1.49\times10^{122}$, the capacity of the wall at the
epoch the fermionic level crosses. The coupling is exactly
three-quarters of one inverse capacity; the peak density is exactly
$3/16$ of an inverse capacity; the defect's total energy inside the
horizon at peak is exactly $1/4\sqrt\pi\approx0.14$ of $\sqrt{C_c}$.
Consequences for the honest ledger:

\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\roman*)},leftmargin=2.2em]
\item \emph{``Why so small'' and ``why now'' fuse.} Previously two
mysteries ($\hat\kappa\sim10^{-123}$ and the epoch $N_c$), they are now
one: the value of a single count, $C_c$. Under B4$'$ that count is fixed
by $(\Omega_m,h)$; nothing else about the dark sector is free.
\item \emph{The scale is the fluctuation scale.} $\sqrt{C_c}$ is one
modular standard deviation: for CFT vacua on causal balls the modular
Hamiltonian satisfies $\langle\Delta K^2\rangle=\langle K\rangle=S$
\slab{established, leading order}
\cite{Perlmutter2014,deBoer2018}, conjectured to persist gravitationally
(Verlinde--Zurek \cite{VZ2019}). A source worth an $O(10^{-1})$ fraction
of one modular $\sigma$ is exactly critical-density scale --- the same
Poisson scaling, $Z=1/\sqrt{\hat V_4}$, by which Everpresent $\Lambda$
predicted the magnitude before 1998 \cite{Zwane2018}. O2 is hereby
upgraded: from ``smallness relocated'' to ``smallness $=$ one modular
fluctuation; open residue $=$ the mechanism selecting the crossing count
and the fermionic pair.''
\item \emph{One temptation declined, one promoted.} The fitted
$\hat\kappa C_0=1.120$ is \emph{not} $9/8$: it equals
$\tfrac32\Omega_m(1+z_c)^3$, an $\Omega_m$-dependent quantity
\slab{declined}. The fitted $\hat\kappa C_c=0.750$, first flagged as a
suspicious rational, is Theorem~\ref{thm:crossing}
\slab{promoted to theorem}.
\end{enumerate}

\section{New formal objects}\label{sec:formal}

\begin{conjecture}[C8: KMS strip]\label{conj:C8}
With $N=2\pi t_{\rm mod}$ (R2), the KMS analyticity of the descent state
should appear as analytic continuation of $Z(N)$ to the strip
$|\operatorname{Im}N|<\pi$, complete monotonicity being the
boundary shadow of strip analyticity, and
$\operatorname{Im}N$ playing the role of modular phase (the Fourier dual
recovering the grade spectrum). \slab{conjecture}
\end{conjecture}

\begin{observation}[Defect poles at half depth]
The defect $\sech^2(N-N_c)$ continues to complex $N$ with poles at
$\operatorname{Im}(N-N_c)=\pi/2+\pi\mathbb Z$ --- inside the conjectured
KMS strip, at exactly half its depth. Whether this is the analytic
signature of the crossing or an accident of the sech-squared form is
open. \slab{observation}
\end{observation}

\begin{question}[O7: the two quarters]\label{q:O7}
The framework contains two quarters: the imported capacity normalization
$C=(A/\lP^2)\cdot\tfrac14$, and the derived variance bound
$\max_p p(1-p)=\tfrac14$ of a binary fact. If each Planck cell of the
wall carries one $\mathbb Z/2$ fact --- the sign character the $A_2$
register already supplies \cite{Ruble2026b} --- and capacity counts
\emph{maximal binary variance per cell} (equivalently, linear entropy /
purity deficit, the quantity the two-block proposition already met),
then the Bekenstein--Hawking quarter and the Bernoulli quarter are the
same quarter. Flagged hard: the standard it-from-bit counting gives
$\ln2$ per cell, not $\tfrac14$, so this reading reinterprets what
capacity counts; it is a conjecture with numerology risk, recorded
because it is the framework's only self-generated $\tfrac14$.
\slab{conjecture; numerology risk}
\end{question}

\begin{remark}[The three-temperature program: R2 sharpened]
Hawking's derivation succeeded by forcing three separately established
structures --- field theory, geometry, thermodynamics --- to agree, and
the agreement fixed the pure numbers. The program's analog: demand that
the wall's \emph{modular} temperature (from the HSMI/Borchers structure),
its \emph{geometric} temperature ($\hbar H/2\pi\kB$), and its
\emph{calorimetric} temperature (the KMS $\beta=1$ of the descent Gibbs
kernel) coincide. The triple match is the sharpened form of the
identification conjecture R2, and it is the kind of consistency that
would fix the framework's remaining normalizations the way Hawking's
fixed the quarter. \slab{program}
\end{remark}
