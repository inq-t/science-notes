## 4. What must be corrected — the stress test's teeth

**C1. **C2. **C3. Conservation was never at stake in "being everywhere."** Energy conservation lives in the generator (unitarity, Noether), orthogonal to the support of amplitudes. The genuine lesson of all-paths is C-adjacent but different: **"where" is an algebra-relative predicate** — location is a question about which local algebra an observable belongs to, not about where the state is.
