## 1. The demand

The program's grammar requires of a fact-breaking obstruction that its fiber-ambiguity be **transportable and braided**:
(i) *connected transport* — nominations at different regular points must be comparable along paths (facts are invariants; invariance presupposes re-description transport; O2/O3);
(ii) *nontrivial monodromy* — by the descent-necessity logic, trivial monodromy plus transitivity (irreducible source) forces generic degree one, i.e., no obstruction exists at all.
So the regular locus must be connected (π₀ trivial) and its wall-encircling loops nontrivial (π₁ sees the wall).

## 2. The pincer

One polynomial condition carves a wall whose real codimension equals the real dimension of the base field. The connectivity arithmetic:

**Theorem (conditional; assembly of classical parts).** *Among connected locally compact division rings [Pontryagin], the demand that fiber-ambiguity admit connected, nontrivially braided transport selects ℂ uniquely: codimension two is the only codimension at which a wall can be encircled but not crossed, and codimension-two-from-one-condition is the defining property of the complex numbers.* The i is the license for one algebraic condition to consume exactly two real dimensions.

**Witness on the ℝ side.** Pinchuk (1994) [pin]: a real polynomial map ℝ² → ℝ² with everywhere nonvanishing Jacobian that is not injective — over ℝ, local invertibility fails to globalize already dyadically; the triad floor, Campbell rigidity, and the entire monodromy grammar are complex phenomena. The real category does not merely lack the theorems; it exhibits the opposite behavior.

The *value* of orientation therefore remains an input — in exact agreement with the coupling theorem's grace note (existence of handedness derivable; the hand itself not). The theorem and its predecessor dovetail unprompted.

## 3. Phase: the central charge of braided ambiguity

Adopting the user's term: a **pre-observable** is a datum that is real in the model, determining of downstream structure, and provably unrecoverable from the observables. The obstruction already contains pre-observables at theorem grade: the branch nomination and the orientation class — locally meaningful, globally invisible (singlet theorem), yet fully determining. Consequence, stated as the program's reading of quantum randomness: *the sufficient reason for an outcome exists (the fiber datum) and is structurally non-invertible from the record* — determinism without predictability; the principle of sufficient reason preserved at the pre-observable level; Born-type randomness as the shadow of a quotient map. (Leibniz, vindicated by Galois descent.)

Where phase specifically lives: the ambiguity's true transport group is not the symmetric shadow S₃ but the braid group **B₃** of the wall complement (Brieskorn), and the difference is the pure-braid kernel with its **canonical central ℤ** (the full twist; B₃/center ≅ PSL₂(ℤ)). Central extensions are precisely where quantum phases originate (Bargmann: projective representations ↔ central extensions; the Heisenberg group as the central extension of translations). The transport of nominations therefore carries a canonical central charge — the algebraic slot of phase — and already pays discrete phases on the nose: the μ₃ characters of the residual monodromy on the double cover, and the μ₆ eigenvalues of the Milnor monodromy. What is not yet supplied is the continuous U(1): the exponentiation of the central ℤ into a connected phase group is Bridge C (quantum completion), as ever. The necessity of i (field side) and the necessity of phase-type pre-observables (transport side) are one theorem-shape: **braided ambiguity forces both.**

## 4. Honesty panel

(i) The theorem is *conditional*: its clauses — local compactness, continuity of transport, the braiding demand — are traced to the facts-grammar (O2/O3) but are not themselves theorems; contest the model there. (ii) Scope: uniqueness is proved within Pontryagin's classification; exotic non-locally-compact or non-metrizable candidates are not addressed. (iii) Kill conditions: a meaningful braided-obstruction theory over a disconnected field, or a real-Keller counterexample with det ≡ const (the real Keller problem in low dimension is open and would complexify into JC₂'s territory — exposure noted). (iv) The continuous phase remains undischarged: μ₃ and μ₆ are supplied; U(1) is Bridge C. (v) Adoption note: Class V of the prediction charter is renamed **pre-observables** per this document.
