# Radical-Copernican Correction Ledger

This historical ledger records claims in the raw notes or the [first audit](../commentary/audit-findings.md) that must not migrate into canonical modules in their present form. Its dispositions are audit markers, not additions to the house claim-status vocabulary: **REVOKE** marks an invalid inference, **FALSE** a contradiction with the stated mathematics, **OVERREACH** a conclusion stronger than its premises, **UNTYPED** a statement whose carriers or operations have been conflated, and **OPEN** a potentially valid construction not yet supplied. The linked explanatory note owns the reasoning.
