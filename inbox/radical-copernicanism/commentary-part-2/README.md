# Radical Copernicanism: Second Commentary

The durable lesson of the radical-Copernican notes is not that global and local descriptions are secretly identical. It is that no presentation of reality is ontologically privileged, while genuine mathematical differences between presentations must be preserved and related by explicit arrows. This folder records that stronger principle, the corrections required by it, and the constructions that would let it improve the canonical programme.

**Status.** These are audit and proposal notes. They do not redefine terms owned by canonical modules, and they do not supersede the raw research in `..` or the first commentary in `../commentary`. Their purpose is to prevent the strongest philosophical insight from being weakened either by conventional physical intuition or by premature operator-algebraic identifications.

The governing sentence is:

> No presentation is privileged; covariance preserves, rather than erases, typed differences between presentations.
