# The Time-Parameter Type Audit

Modular time, dual trace-scaling parameter, scale age, proper time, conformal time, Misner time, four-volume time, and record order are different mathematical objects. Their analogies can guide construction, but identifying any pair requires an explicit map with units, normalization, covariance, and physical recovery conditions.

## The parameter register

| Parameter | Native structure |
|---|---|
| modular parameter $t_{\mathrm{mod}}$ | the real parameter of $\sigma^\omega:\mathbb R\to\operatorname{Aut}(M)$ |
| dual/core parameter $s$ | the real parameter of $\theta:\mathbb R\to\operatorname{Aut}(\widetilde M)$, whose action scales the canonical semifinite trace |
| scale age $N$ | the dimensionless logarithmic ratio $\ln(a/a_*)$ in the homogeneous cosmological branch |
| proper time $\tau$ | Lorentzian length along a timelike worldline |
| conformal time $\eta$ | a coordinate satisfying $d\eta=d\tau_{\mathrm{cos}}/a(\tau_{\mathrm{cos}})$ for comoving cosmic proper time $\tau_{\mathrm{cos}}$ in a chosen FLRW geometry |
| Misner-type logarithmic time | a variable formed from spatial volume or scale in canonical cosmology |
| four-volume time $T_4$ | a global volume variable appearing in unimodular formulations |
| record order | a preorder or proper extension relation among persistent records |

Unit conversion does not identify their categories. Even two dimensionless parameters need not represent the same action.

## What modular theory does establish

Every faithful normal state is KMS with respect to its own modular automorphism group in the standard modular sense. This does not say that every physical state is in equilibrium with respect to one externally specified dynamics, nor that modular parameters are operational clock readings.

As established in [the canonical-core firewall](canonical-core-and-type-firewall.md), the dual trace-scaling action is structural whether or not an associated physical spacetime expands. Consequently, “static world means trivial dual action” is false as a statement about the canonical core. Here $\theta$ is the dual action; its restriction to $Z(\widetilde M)$ is the flow of weights, while $\operatorname{mod}(\alpha)$ denotes the Connes--Takesaki module of an automorphism. A nontrivial dual action cannot itself be interpreted as production of time.

A nonzero deformation or response parameter $\epsilon$ can show that a particular ansatz fails to be invariant under a chosen dynamics. It cannot by itself prove that no exact KMS state or no dominant weight exists: KMS is relative to a declared automorphism group, while dominance is a separate property of the weight and algebra.

**[STANDARD — IN THE HENNEAUX--TEITELBOIM FORMULATION]** A global “cosmic time” mode is canonically conjugate, up to conventions, to the cosmological constant $\Lambda$; differences of this mode track four-volume. This is the specific precedent supplied by [[library/cosmological-constant-and-general-covariance|Henneaux and Teitelboim]], not a universal statement about every unimodular formulation. It does not identify $T_4$ with $s$, $N$, or record order.

## Required soldering receipts

A proposed identification $f:s\mapsto N$ or $f:t_{\mathrm{mod}}\mapsto\tau$ must specify:

1. domain and codomain;
2. units and normalization;
3. the state or weight dependence;
4. covariance under admissible presentation changes;
5. how generators and spectra are transported;
6. the geometric or operational clock recovered; and
7. the failure condition.

The same applies to the proposed relation among produced past, increasing horizon capacity, expansion, and entropy. Those may ultimately be faces of one structure, but at present the vertical modular flow, horizontal scale transport, and factive record order remain separately typed by [[wall-construction-interface/vertical-and-horizontal-motion|Vertical and Horizontal Motion]], [[misner-log-time/inq|Misner Log-Time]], [[conformal-time/inq|Conformal Time]], and [[program-core/record-scale-soldering|Record--Scale Soldering]].

## Energy at the cosmic register

Before asking what the cosmos emits as radiation redshifts, one must establish that the whole spacetime admits the relevant global energy charge. In a general expanding FLRW spacetime there is no timelike Killing symmetry supplying an ordinary conserved total energy. The local continuity equation is covariant stress-energy conservation, not evidence that a compensating object has been emitted at the boundary.

The imaginative question remains valuable after retyping: what global balance or record structure corresponds to the local presentation of redshift and acceleration? That question should be answered by a constructed balance law, not by presupposing a missing energy substance.

If promoted, the table should become a programme-wide time-parameter register. The existing vertical/horizontal and record-scale modules should continue to own the actual soldering problems.
