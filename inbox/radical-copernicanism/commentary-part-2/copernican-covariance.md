# Copernican Covariance

Radical Copernicanism should be adopted as a programme commitment against privileged mathematical presentation, not as an assertion that whole-cosmos, local-observer, compact, asymptotic, algebraic, and spatiotemporal regimes are equivalent. The stronger law requires every such regime to be treated as real and every claimed relation between them to be carried by a typed comparison, realization, or recovery map.

## The philosophical principle

The ordinary Copernican move removes a privileged location. Relativity removes a privileged inertial presentation while retaining invariant structure. The next move is to remove the tacit privilege accorded to the form in which nature becomes locally observable.

This does not make the local presentation illusory. A local fact is as mathematically real as the algebraic structure from which it is realized. Nor does it install the whole as a superior observer. The whole as whole is not a view from nowhere; it is the structure in virtue of which local views, comparisons, facts, and records belong to one cosmos.

The proposed **[PROGRAMME COMMITMENT — PROPOSED]** is:

> No admissible presentation of the cosmos is ontologically privileged merely because it is local, global, geometrical, algebraic, observable, or pre-observable. Objective content is structure preserved or coherently transformed under declared comparison maps. Non-isomorphic carriers may remain genuinely different while belonging to one law.

This principle is not a theorem or transformation law. It governs what a successful construction must return.

## Covariance is weaker than equivalence

Several relations must remain distinct:

- **equivalence:** an invertible arrow preserving the declared structure;
- **covariance:** a law is natural or equivariant under a declared class of changes;
- **comparison:** two objects are related by a span, correspondence, profunctor, or other non-identity datum;
- **realization:** structure on one carrier produces structure on another carrier;
- **descent:** compatible local data glue to an object relative to a specified cover or site;
- **recovery:** a target regime reproduces a declared source theory or observable sector under controlled hypotheses.

Copernican non-privilege requires none of these relations to collapse into equality. A higher law may assign different factor types, boundary structures, or causal presentations to different regimes. Indeed, refusing to erase such asymmetries is part of fidelity to the mathematics.

The principle should eventually strengthen PC3, relational presentation, and PC4, local-to-global coherence, in [[program-core/axioms-and-principles|Axioms and Principles]] rather than become a duplicate axiom. Every appeal to a whole/local, timeless/temporal, inner/Lorentzian, or pre-observable/factual divide must name the carriers and the arrows between them, following [[program-core/operation-registers|Operations Between Registers]] and [the Copernican comparison receipt](copernican-comparison-receipt.md) proposed here. For the $S^6/A_2$ programme, this means that the six-dimensional complex or spectral carrier need not be demoted to a model of a more-real $3+1$ spacetime. Conversely, complex integrability does not by itself realize Lorentzian signature, particles, time, or facts. The programme requires a chain of actual constructions,

$$
(S^6,A_2,\text{spectral data})
\longrightarrow
(\text{Lorentzian causal carrier})
\longrightarrow
(\text{local fields and particles})
\longrightarrow
(\text{readouts and records}),
$$

with an invariant or explicitly typed loss or remainder, together with a recovery statement, at every arrow. This is the rigorous form of philosophical bravery: permit the ontology demanded by the source mathematics, then accept the full burden of constructing how it presents itself.
