# Balance Before Conservation

Balance is a wider philosophical category than conservation of a scalar substance. The radical-Copernican programme should compare stationarity, coherence, allocation, constraint, flux, monotonicity, scaling, and record compatibility without calling them one conservation law until maps place them on a common carrier.

## Different mathematical shapes

| Structure | Mathematical statement | What it does not yet imply |
|---|---|---|
| stationary action | first variation vanishes on admissible variations | a conserved charge without a symmetry theorem |
| Noether-I current | finite-dimensional continuous variational symmetry yields an on-shell divergence-free current, modulo hypotheses and improvements | a charge without a hypersurface integral and boundary or integrability conditions |
| Noether-II identity | local gauge symmetry yields differential identities and, after Hamiltonian analysis, constraints | that every physical charge vanishes or lives at a boundary |
| flux balance | charge change equals flux plus source and corner terms | conservation in the absence of boundary data |
| Connes chain rule | cocycle comparisons compose coherently | conservation of physical information or energy |
| Casimir allocation | an invariant is decomposed among state-dependent terms | a time-dependent continuity equation |
| relative-entropy monotonicity | distinguishability cannot increase under a CPTP channel in Schrödinger form, the corresponding normal unital CP map in Heisenberg form, or a genuine subalgebra restriction | equality, cosmic entropy growth, or a clock |
| trace scaling | a dual action rescales a semifinite trace | cosmological expansion or production of time |
| categorical dimension | dimension composes multiplicatively under stated categorical hypotheses | a temporally conserved substance |
| record compatibility | later records preserve declared earlier values | unitarity of the underlying presentation |

The common word *balance* can name the demand that the relevant diagram commute or that every change have a typed account. It cannot erase the different domains and codomains of the entries above.

## Retyping the programme's attractive identities

The binary identity

$$
1=\langle Q\rangle^2+\operatorname{Var}(Q)
$$

is an exact allocation identity for an involution. In the balanced commuting exponential family, the variance can coincide with the declared BKM component. Neither fact by itself establishes a Noether current, information conservation, or a universe-wide actuality/potentiality ledger.

Likewise, a BKM Hessian is a static response geometry until dynamics, a KMS setting, retarded response, and a source-response identification are supplied. Calling the anchored CST source law a fluctuation--dissipation theorem would promote a constitutive bridge into a derivation.

For a compatible chain of restrictions, relative-entropy decrements may be nonnegative and their sum may telescope arithmetically. Equality in data processing under a sufficiency or recovery condition makes the corresponding information loss vanish. It does not identify a nonzero decrement with physical heat or flux. A Clausius-type law requires a separately defined $\mathcal F_k$ and a theorem

$$
D_k-D_{k+1}=\mathcal F_k.
$$

Gravitational memory is likewise not “radiation without energy flux.” At null infinity it enters a boundary charge-and-flux balance and may be sourced by radiative or matter flux, together with changes in Coulombic data; the precise statement depends on the memory sector and boundary conditions.

## A better conservation question

Instead of asking “what substance compensates the lost local information or redshifted energy?”, ask:

1. Which carrier supports the proposed balance?
2. What symmetry, variation, channel, or composition defines it?
3. Is the result an equality, inequality, identity, constraint, or commuting diagram?
4. What boundary, corner, state, and integrability data are required?
5. Which map transports this balance to geometry, facts, or records?
