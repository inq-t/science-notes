# Two Axes of Descent

The directed scale axis and the site of overlapping causal contexts solve different problems. Trivial first cohomology on a nested scale chain can eliminate an ordinary base cocycle there without eliminating contextual gluing, correspondence transport, observational descent, or fact formation inside each scale fibre.

## The two bases

The existing architecture is best expressed schematically as

$$
\mathsf{Scale}
\longrightarrow
\operatorname{PSt}(\mathsf{Ctx},\mathbf{W^*Corr}).
$$

Here:

- $\mathsf{Scale}$ indexes wall or scale stages and may be a directed category or preorder;
- $\mathsf{Ctx}$ is a site of causal or observational contexts with declared covers;
- $\mathbf{W^*Corr}$ supplies von Neumann algebras and correspondences, rather than pretending every inclusion induces a homomorphism of canonical cores.

For a directed poset, every flat net-cohomological $1$-cocycle with the constant group coefficients and Roberts--Ruzzi hypotheses considered in the source discussion is a $1$-coboundary, so its cohomology class is trivial. This kills that proposed *scale-chain base-loop obstruction*. It does not annihilate arbitrary sheaf cohomology, local-coefficient cohomology, contextual descent failure, or noninvertible transport between scale fibres.

## What the correction leaves open

The correction removes one bad obstruction; it does not construct the desired stack. A viable architecture must still distinguish:

- scale transport between different fibres;
- restriction or correspondence data inside a context site;
- compatibility between scale transport and contextual gluing; and
- the later physical map from a descended object to geometry, observable law, fact, or record.

The site, covers, morphism class, overlap coherence, effectivity test, and physical-consequence map are completion obligations already owned by [[spectral-wall-descent/scale-correspondence-stack|the Scale-Correspondence Prestack]]. They should not be redefined in this commentary.

One load-bearing instance remains worth flagging. For an inclusion $N\subset M$, the assignment $M\mapsto\widetilde M$ is not automatically functorial for the inclusion. Any proposed lift must declare its morphism class—possibly an inclusion equipped with a normal faithful semifinite operator-valued weight or a $W^*$-correspondence—and prove compatibility with the dual actions and composition. That problem belongs to the canonical prestack and [[spectral-wall-descent/conditional-expectation-balance|Conditional-Expectation Balance]], not to the scale-chain cohomology claim.
