# The Copernican Comparison Receipt

A claim that two regimes present one reality is a claim about a comparison arrow, not merely a resemblance between the objects at its ends. This receipt makes radical Copernicanism operational by requiring the carrier, arrow, invariant, one-sidedness, remainder, recovery, and status of every cross-presentation identification.

## Receipt fields

For a proposed relation $X\rightsquigarrow Y$, record:

1. **Source carrier:** the category and object containing $X$.
2. **Target carrier:** the category and object containing $Y$.
3. **Arrow type:** isomorphism, functor, natural transformation, correspondence, inclusion, quotient, expectation, instrument, realization, or other declared operation.
4. **Required data:** state, weight, topology, cover, involution, measure, representation, boundary condition, or observer.
5. **Invariant:** the structure proved to survive the comparison.
6. **Direction:** whether the arrow is invertible, adjointable, recoverable, idempotent, or genuinely one-sided.
7. **Remainder:** kernel, fibre, cokernel, obstruction, entropy defect, stabilizer data, or record increment—if one is actually defined.
8. **Recovery:** the theorem or limit in which the appropriate local physics is reproduced.
9. **Status and failure condition:** exact, conditional, constitutive, conjectural, or open, together with what would refute the claim.

These fields specialize [[program-core/operation-registers|Operations Between Registers]] to the whole/local question. They do not create a new operation vocabulary. If promoted, the receipt fields should be folded into [[program-core/claim-and-failure-contract|the Claim-and-Failure Contract]], while the operation register continues to own arrow definitions.

## Three sample receipts

### Modular presentations on fixed $M$

- source and target: two modular presentations of the same von Neumann algebra $M$;
- arrow: inner cocycle perturbation of the modular flows, implemented by the time-dependent Connes cocycle derivative;
- invariant: the outer modular class and canonical core;
- direction: invertible;
- status: **[STANDARD]**;
- boundary: this does not establish physical equivalence of arbitrary states or different gravitational algebras.

### Inner $S^6/A_2$ geometry to Lorentzian presentation

- source: the declared complex, spectral, or categorical inner carrier;
- target: a Lorentzian causal and metric carrier;
- proposed arrow: realization or soldering;
- invariant: not yet fixed;
- direction and remainder: not yet known;
- recovery: Poincaré representation content, local QFT/SM observables, and gravitational geometry;
- status: **[OPEN CONSTRUCTION]**.

Complex integrability is therefore source structure, not yet the receipt for its physical presentation.

### Wall algebra to factual record

- source: a wall or accessible von Neumann algebra with state/weight data;
- target: an outcome-labelled record object;
- arrows: contextual readout, factual instrument, then proper record extension;
- invariant: declared earlier record values;
- direction: one-sided at the record stage;
- recovery: agreement with the ordinary observational law;
- status: **[OPEN CONSTRUCTION]**.

Restriction or conditional expectation alone does not complete this receipt because neither operation selects an obtained value and preserves it as history.

## Promotion rule

A cross-presentation slogan may guide research before its receipt is complete. It should not enter a canonical derivation as an identity until the missing fields have owners. This is especially important for

$$
\text{modular parameter}\leftrightarrow\text{cosmic time},
\qquad
\text{BKM response}\leftrightarrow\text{gravity},
\qquad
\text{inner geometry}\leftrightarrow 3+1\text{ spacetime}.
$$

The receipt is successful when it makes a bold identification more precise. It is also successful when it shows that two attractive terms belong to different categories and the proposed identity must be abandoned.
