# A Copernican Revolution for the Global/Local Distinction: A Sourced Assessment of Candidate Formalisms

## TL;DR
- No existing framework fully delivers the requested revolution — a covariant transformation law relating "X-as-whole" to "X-as-part" with familiar physics as fixed points — but a small cluster comes close: the **Connes cocycle / Connes–Takesaki flow of weights** (a rigorous 1-cocycle relating observer descriptions), **SymTFT / relative QFT** (observable physics as a boundary of a topological bulk), and **topos "no global sections" theorems** (Kochen–Specker, Isham–Butterfield) are the three most load-bearing, theorem-grade pieces.
- The strongest single realization of "global is derivable, not primitive" is the **Doplicher–Roberts reconstruction theorem**: the global compact gauge group and field algebra are *reconstructed* from the purely local observable net — a genuine THEOREM, but with sharp kill conditions (dimension > 2, Haag duality, mass gap; it fails for braided sectors and infraparticles).
- The descent-with-remainder primitive the requester wants is best seeded by **framing a family of observer/modular algebras as a stack over a base of states/regions, with the flow of weights as the 1-cocycle and the descent obstruction as the "remainder."** This synthesis does not yet exist in the literature; the closest rigorous ingredients (Connes 1973; Connes–Takesaki 1977; Roberts' net cohomology) are established but have never been combined into a cosmological "descent" theorem.

## Key Findings

The requester's test is exacting: a framework passes only if it provides a **precise transformation law** relating the description of X-as-whole to X-as-part, with a well-defined "frame change" and familiar physics recovered as fixed points or limits. Judged by this test, the candidate literatures sort into three tiers.

**Tier 1 (theorem-grade, transformation-law-bearing):** (i) modular theory's Connes cocycle and the Connes–Takesaki flow of weights; (ii) Doplicher–Roberts reconstruction; (iii) topos "no global sections" results. These have genuine mathematical content that bears directly on the whole/part relation.

**Tier 2 (structurally on-target, physics still developing):** SymTFT / Freed–Teleman relative QFT (observable sector = boundary of a pre-observable topological bulk); Harlow–Ooguri "no global symmetries in quantum gravity" (a proof-in-AdS/CFT that the global/local symmetry distinction collapses at the level of the whole); the Coleman–Mandula → Haag–Łopuszański–Sohnius grading precedent (a template for "evade a no-go by going graded/higher").

**Tier 3 (conceptually essential, mostly programmatic or verbal):** cohesive ∞-topos "meta-geometry" (Schreiber); F₁ / absolute geometry (Connes–Consani); Manchak's underdetermination theorems (the epistemic case *for* treating globality as non-absolute); Smolin's cosmological-fallacy critique and Rovelli's relational QM (the philosophy of laws-for-a-unique-whole); torsor formulations of energy/charge; Crane and Markopoulou's categorical/internal cosmology.

I now assess each named cluster.

## Details

### (1) Descent, stacks, torsors, gerbes, nonabelian cohomology as physics

**What it is.** Grothendieck's *relative point of view* replaces the study of an object X by the study of morphisms X → S over an arbitrary base S, demanding stability under base change; the "absolute" statement is recovered when S is the terminal object (a point). The passage from local data to global data, and the obstructions to it, is **descent theory**; when naive gluing fails, one repairs it with a **stack**, and the failure/remainder is measured by **(nonabelian) cohomology** (Giraud). A **torsor** is a set with free transitive group action — "a group that has forgotten its identity element" — the local model of a principal bundle; **gerbes** are the next categorical level (torsors of torsors), classified by degree-2 nonabelian cohomology. The Wikipedia formulation of Grothendieck's heuristic is exactly the requester's slogan: "the various aspects of an object can be made manifest once it is put in relation with other objects," and Grothendieck "proved theorems in a 'relative' context and showed how the usual 'absolute' version becomes a simple consequence."

**Torsors as "no absolute zero."** The cleanest physics instance is John Baez's exposition (math.ucr.edu/home/baez/torsors.html): energy *differences* live in the group ℝ, but energies themselves live in an ℝ-**torsor** — "there is no absolute zero, only differences" is literally the torsor axiom. Voltage, phase, and (before absolute zero was found) temperature are the standard examples. This is a real, if elementary, instance of "being-absolute is a frame choice": choosing a zero is choosing a trivialization of the torsor. It is, however, only abelian and kinematic; it carries no dynamics and no cosmology.

**Higher gauge theory / cohesive ∞-topos.** Urs Schreiber's *Differential cohomology in a cohesive ∞-topos* (arXiv:1310.7930) axiomatizes differential cohomology and Chern–Weil theory inside a class of ∞-toposes ("cohesive"), generating gauge fields, higher principal bundles and connections on gerbes from *adjoint modalities* (shape/flat/sharp, reduction/infinitesimal). This is the best existing candidate for a "meta-geometry" in which locality, spacetime and gauge structure are *derived* from adjunctions rather than presupposed — precisely the requester's aspiration. Sati–Schreiber's *Hypothesis H* (proper orbifold cohomology; the conjecture that M-theory C-field flux is quantized in twisted equivariant cohomotopy) is the most concrete physics deployment.

**Honest grade: POSTULATE / OPEN.** Schreiber's programme is mathematically rigorous *as mathematics* (much is theorem), but as physics it is, so far, a synthetic reformulation of gauge theory and string/M-theory backgrounds. It does **not** address cosmology or the whole-vs-part question directly; there is no theorem here of the form "global description of the universe = base change of a local one." The resonance with descent-with-remainder is strong and real, but currently structural, not a delivered result. **Kill condition:** if no cohesive-topos statement can be made whose "de-globalization" limit reproduces a standard cosmological observable, the connection remains verbal.

### (2) Topos-theoretic / internal-logic physics

**What it is.** Isham–Butterfield and Döring–Isham build quantum theory inside the topos of presheaves over the poset V(N) of *abelian* (commutative) von Neumann subalgebras ("contexts") of the algebra N of observables. The central object is the **spectral presheaf** Σ, whose value on each context is that context's Gelfand spectrum. The signature theorem: **the Kochen–Specker theorem is exactly equivalent to the statement that Σ has NO global sections** (no global elements). This is a genuine mathematical realization of the requester's target: a *God's-eye global valuation does not exist*; only local sections over contexts do. "Daseinisation" maps projections to subobjects of Σ; states become truth objects because there are no microstates in the classical sense.

**Bohrification** (Heunen–Landsman–Spitters) is the covariant sibling: to a noncommutative C*-algebra one associates the *internal* commutative algebra living in the topos of its commutative subalgebras, and reasons with it using the topos's intuitionistic internal logic. Isham's "quantising on a category" generalizes the base.

**Markopoulou and Crane (internal/categorical cosmology).** Markopoulou's *The internal description of a causal set: what the universe looks like from the inside* (Comm. Math. Phys. 211 (2000) 559) puts a presheaf of Hilbert spaces / evolving sets over a causal set: each event carries the Hilbert space / algebra of its own causal past, and the propositional logic available to an internal observer is a multivalued Heyting algebra, not Boolean — because each observer can only assign truth to propositions about their own past. Louis Crane's *Clock and Category: Is Quantum Gravity Algebraic?* (J. Math. Phys. 36 (1995) 6180) makes the explicit claim the requester quotes: **there is no Hilbert space of the whole universe**; instead there is a system of observable algebras/Hilbert spaces, one for every splitting of the universe into system + observer, organized categorically (functors from cobordisms to Hilbert spaces). Smolin (hep-th/0003056) reads Markopoulou as the "stronger version" of Crane.

**Test.** Topos physics passes the "no global section" half of the test cleanly — the pre-observable *is* precisely "the presheaf that has no global section," and the observable here-and-now *is* a section over a context. What it does **not** yet deliver is a *transformation law* between contexts that plays the role of a frame change with dynamics; the poset V(N) has inclusions (refinement) but no group of "changes of global viewpoint," and the programme is famously light on dynamics and empirical output. **Standard criticisms:** it has produced re-derivations (spectral theorem, a topos notion of state and of entropy) more than new predictions; the intuitionistic logic is a feature to some and a liability to others; and it is unclear it improves on ordinary algebraic QM operationally.

**Grade: THEOREM** for the Kochen–Specker/"no global sections" equivalence (Isham–Butterfield, Int. J. Theor. Phys. 1998–2002; Döring–Isham JMP 49 (2008) 053516); **POSTULATE/OPEN** for its cosmological ambitions. Crane's "no Hilbert space of the universe" is a POSTULATE (physically motivated, partially realized in TQFT-constrained gravity); Markopoulou's Heyting-algebra observation is a DEDUCTION within classical GR for closed universes.

### (3) The observable sector as boundary/interface of a pre-observable bulk

**Relative QFT (Freed–Teleman, arXiv:1212.1692, Comm. Math. Phys. 326 (2014) 459).** A *relative* quantum field theory is not absolute: its partition function is **not a number but a vector** in the state space of a field theory in one dimension higher. Anomalous theories are exactly the relative ones: they live on the boundary of an invertible ("anomaly") theory in dimension d+1. This is a precise, theorem-grade instantiation of "the here-and-now theory is an interface; its would-be globality is only defined relative to a higher-dimensional structure."

**SymTFT / symmetry-topological-field-theory "sandwich."** A d-dimensional QFT is presented as a *dynamical boundary condition* of a (d+1)-dimensional **topological** bulk (the SymTFT), with a second, *topological* boundary carrying the symmetry data (a Lagrangian algebra / module category). Shrinking the interval recovers the absolute d-dimensional theory; **changing the topological boundary condition changes the global form / gauging / symmetry of the boundary theory** while leaving the local dynamics fixed. This is close to a literal transformation law: the "frame change" is the choice of topological boundary condition, and the pre-observable = the topological bulk (all symmetry/superselection data, no local dynamics). The Freed–Teleman "relative theory = vector, not number" statement is precisely the SymTFT slogan (arXiv:2605.06653 states it verbatim: "its partition function is not a number, but a vector in the Hilbert space of the SymTFT ... to obtain an ordinary absolute QFT, one must choose a topological boundary condition"). SymTFTs for **spacetime** symmetries and even **general relativity** (MacDowell–Mansouri formulation) now exist (arXiv:2509.08819, 2509.07965), and SymTFT has been embedded in holography (arXiv:2509.14783) and applied to baby-universe/ensemble averaging (arXiv:2605.06653). Application to *cosmology* proper is still nascent; de Sitter and braneworld cosmology holography exist but do not yet have a fully worked "universe = boundary" SymTFT.

**Doplicher–Roberts reconstruction (the strongest "global from local" theorem).** Given only the *local observable net* in the vacuum sector, obeying the DHR axioms, the DR theorem (Doplicher–Roberts, *Why there is a field algebra with a compact gauge group…*, Comm. Math. Phys. 131 (1990) 51; and the duality theorem Invent. Math. 98 (1989) 157) proves that the **superselection structure determines a unique compact global gauge group G and a field algebra F** on which G acts with the observables as the fixed points — the intrinsic statement being that *the pre-observable global structure (gauge group, charged fields, statistics) is completely derivable from purely local observable data*. The abstract engine is: every symmetric tensor C*-category with conjugates and End(𝟙)=ℂ is the representation category of a unique compact group (Müger gave a streamlined proof, 2006). This is exactly "global is a theorem about the local," the requester's target in miniature.

**Kill conditions (sharp).** DR requires **spacetime dimension > 2** (so the permutation/symmetric structure holds); **Haag duality** and **property B** in the vacuum sector; and effectively a **mass gap** (the DHR/BF localizability of charges). It **fails** where: (a) low dimension (d ≤ 2), where statistics is *braided* not symmetric and one gets the circle group / non-integer statistical dimensions (Fredenhagen–Rehren–Schroer, subfactor indices à la Jones); (b) **topological/cone-localized charges** need spacelike-cone (BF) localization, not bounded regions; (c) **long-range forces / infraparticles** (electric charge in QED), where the charged sectors are not DHR and Gauss-law/superselected total charge obstructs the reconstruction. These are precisely the "remainders" a descent-with-remainder programme would want to classify.

**Grade: THEOREM** (DR reconstruction; Freed–Teleman relative QFT). **DEDUCTION/POSTULATE** for the SymTFT-in-cosmology extension.

### (4) Super-algebras and graded evasion of no-go theorems as a precedent

**The precedent (theorem).** Coleman–Mandula (Phys. Rev. 159 (1967) 1251) proves that, under reasonable assumptions (mass gap, analyticity, finitely many particle types below any mass), the only symmetries of the S-matrix are the direct product Poincaré × internal — spacetime and internal symmetries **cannot mix nontrivially**; internal charges must be Lorentz scalars. **Haag–Łopuszański–Sohnius** (*All Possible Generators of Supersymmetries of the S Matrix*, Nucl. Phys. B88 (1975) 257) shows the *unique* way to evade this is to **drop the assumption that the symmetry algebra is an ordinary (bosonic) Lie algebra** and allow a ℤ₂-**graded** Lie (super)algebra: the fermionic supercharges Q anticommute, {Q,Q̄} ∼ P, and this is the only consistent nontrivial mixing. This is the paradigmatic "the no-go is evaded by going graded/higher" pattern.

**The analog for global vs. local.** The modern statement that **global and gauge symmetries sit inside one higher structure** is realized by **2-group global symmetries** (Córdova–Dumitrescu–Intriligator, *Exploring 2-Group Global Symmetries*, JHEP 02 (2019) 184; Benini–Córdova–Hsin, JHEP 03 (2019) 118): a 0-form and a 1-form symmetry combine, via a mixed anomaly, into a single 2-group in which the currents fuse nontrivially — a genuine higher-categorical merger of symmetry structures that "ordinary" reasoning keeps separate. In holography, "global symmetries of the bulk = gauge symmetries of the boundary" is the operative slogan.

**Harlow–Ooguri.** Using AdS/CFT they *prove* — as stated verbatim in *Constraints on Symmetries from Holography*, Phys. Rev. Lett. 122, 191601 (2019) [arXiv:1810.05337] — "a set of old conjectures about symmetries in quantum gravity hold within the Anti-de Sitter/Conformal Field Theory (AdS/CFT) correspondence. These conjectures are that no global symmetries are possible, that internal gauge symmetries must come with dynamical objects that transform in all irreducible representations, and that internal gauge groups must be compact." (Long version: *Symmetries in quantum field theory and quantum gravity*, Commun. Math. Phys. 383 (2021) 1669–1804 [arXiv:1810.05338].) The proof leans on the emergence of bulk geometry as a **quantum error-correcting code**: a global symmetry would have to act on the code subspace in a way incompatible with the erasure-correction property.

**Assessment.** This *is* a partial realization of the requested revolution: at the level of the whole cosmos, the global/local distinction *for symmetry* collapses — everything global is either gauged or broken. The pattern "mixing forbidden structures requires a graded/higher extension" (Coleman–Mandula → HLS) is a strong **precedent** for the conjecture that mixing global-as-whole with local-as-part requires a graded/higher/categorical extension. But note the grade: Harlow–Ooguri is **THEOREM-within-AdS/CFT** (a DEDUCTION contingent on assuming a holographic dual), not a model-independent theorem; the "global/local collapses" reading is an interpretation, sound but not proven in general quantum gravity.

### (5) Absolute geometry / the field with one element

**What it is.** The F₁ programme seeks a "base below Spec ℤ" — an absolute point over which even the integers are relative — so that Grothendieck's relative viewpoint is pushed to its limit: nothing is absolute, everything is relative to an absolute point that is itself contentless. Connes–Consani develop this via characteristic-one algebra, semirings, Segal's Γ-sets, and the "arithmetic site" and "scaling site," with the ultimate target being a Riemann-hypothesis strategy (an analog of the Weil proof over the adele class space). Recent work continues actively (Connes–Consani, *On the Absolute Geometry of Spec ℤ*, arXiv:2606.06604, 2026; *On the metaphysics of F₁*, Atti Accad. Naz. Lincei Rend. Lincei Mat. Appl. 35 (2024) 121).

**Physical interpretation.** The Connes–Consani programme connects to physics mainly through the **Bost–Connes system** (a quantum statistical-mechanical system whose partition function is the Riemann zeta function, with a phase transition and symmetry breaking) and the adele class space / trace-formula approach. There is a small literature on "Galois field quantum mechanics" and the q → 1 limit (arXiv:1312.4191). But there is **no** established connection of F₁ to cosmology or to the removal of absolute structure in *physical* spacetime.

**Honest grade: mostly PROGRAMME.** The relative-viewpoint philosophy is exactly on-target conceptually — F₁ is "the relativity of the arithmetic base" — but the theorems are in number theory, not physics, and the RH strategy remains open. For the requester's purpose F₁ is an inspiring structural analogy (remove even the arithmetic absolute), not a usable physical formalism. **Flag: verbal/structural resonance, not a physics derivation.**

### (6) Observational underdetermination of global structure (the epistemic case)

**What it is.** Manchak's *Can we know the global structure of spacetime?* (Stud. Hist. Phil. Mod. Phys. 40(1) (2009) 53–56), building on Glymour (1972,1977) and Malament (1977), proves a **theorem**: barring one pathology, **every spacetime that is "not causally bizarre" (no point p with I⁻(p) = M) has an observationally indistinguishable counterpart with different global structure** — one that agrees on all local conditions (including the Einstein equation) yet differs globally. The upshot, verbatim: "There seems to be a robust sense in which the global structure of every cosmological model is underdetermined" by all possible observational data (the past light cone of any observer). Follow-on results extend this to inextendibility, hole-freeness, singularity-theorem hypotheses, and the very notion of a cosmic "beginning" (Manchak; Doboszewski; arXiv:2603.04159).

**Test and role.** This is the rigorous **epistemic argument FOR** treating globality as non-absolute: no local/observational data can force a unique global whole, so "which whole am I a patch of?" is empirically undetermined — globality behaves like an indexical/frame choice, not an observable fact. It does not itself supply a transformation law, but it is the cleanest theorem justifying the *need* for one. **Responses:** Norton (2011) argues the underdetermination challenges *any* inductivist; some (Beisbart, Butterfield 2012/2014) accept knowledge of global properties is beyond the epistemic horizon; others (the "Careful with those scissors, Eugene!" paper, Stud. Hist. Phil. Mod. Phys. 2021) argue Manchak's counterpart spacetimes are "not physically reasonable" because their features are not the product of any physical process, and propose an extra condition to exclude them.

**Grade: THEOREM** (Manchak/Malament), with a live methodological debate about whether inductive/physical-reasonableness principles defeat its skeptical force.

### (7) Philosophy of cosmology: laws for a unique whole

**Smolin's cosmological fallacy.** Smolin (*Time Reborn*, 2013; Unger–Smolin, *The Singular Universe and the Reality of Time*, 2014) argues that the "Newtonian paradigm" — a fixed configuration space + external time + freely specifiable initial conditions — is a method for **subsystems ("physics in a box")** and its extension to the universe as a whole is the **cosmological fallacy**: "The universe is an entity different in kind from any of its parts"; and since there is only one universe, one case "does not yield sufficient evidence to justify the claim that a particular law of nature applies" (the *cosmological dilemma*). His positive proposal: a new *kind* of law (evolving laws; energetic causal sets with Cortês) that is intrinsically cosmological. This is a direct philosophical articulation of the requester's motivation, but it is a critique + programme, not a formalism with a whole/part transformation law.

**Rovelli's relational QM.** States are always relative to a system; there is **no absolute state of the universe** (Rovelli, Int. J. Theor. Phys. 35 (1996) 1637). Recent developments: Di Biagio–Rovelli "Stable facts, relative facts" (Found. Phys. 51 (2021) 30); Adlam–Rovelli "Information is physical: cross-perspective links" (Philosophy of Physics 1 (2023) 4) adds cross-perspective links and (in RQM+) treats quantum events as absolute while values remain relative. **Critiques:** Pienaar's "Quintet of Quandaries" (Found. Phys. 51 (2021)); Brukner; and the Lawrence–Markiewicz–Żukowski / Adlam-response exchange arguing relative facts are formally incompatible with QM (Quantum 7 (2023) 1015). Rovelli is the closest to "global state is an indexical," but the framework is about observers/systems, not the whole/part *globality* frame change.

**Ismael.** *The Situated Self* (2007) supplies the philosophy of indexicality and the "view from nowhere": physics has no built-in perspective, yet all experience is from a standpoint; she builds a theory of self-locating knowledge that reconciles the objective description with the indexical "here-and-now." This is the natural philosophical home for "global becomes an indexical like here," though Ismael does not formalize it for cosmology.

**Grade: POSTULATE/critique.** These are the philosophical warrant for the programme (and identify the desiderata) rather than deliverers of the transformation law. Smolin explicitly frames it as needing a Copernican-style change of law-kind; Rovelli and Ismael supply the indexical/relational ontology.

### (8) Spacetime points as derived / here-and-now as composite

Brief, and mostly verbal for the present purpose. **Twistor theory** treats spacetime points as secondary (an event is a holomorphic 2-sphere in twistor space); **noncommutative geometry** replaces points by pure states of an algebra; **pregeometry** traditions (Wheeler) deny fundamental spacetime. These relativize *locality* (what a point is) but none of them, on their own, relativizes the *global/local distinction itself* into a frame choice with a covariant law. **Flag: relevant only as raw material; no direct delivery of the target.**

### (9) Wildcard

The explicit phrase-hunts mostly surface the items already covered (relational cosmology → Rovelli/Smolin; "universe from inside" → Markopoulou; "no Hilbert space of the universe" → Crane). Two genuine finds: (a) causal-set theorist **Benjamin Dribus** (*On the Axioms of Causal Set Theory*, arXiv:1311.2148) explicitly imports Grothendieck's **relative viewpoint** and "principle of hidden structure" as foundational for discrete causal theory, and defines "co-relative histories" as formalized spacetime evolution — the clearest existing attempt to make the relative point of view do causal-cosmological work. (b) Grothendieck himself (per the memorial arXiv:1605.08112) regarded relativity as mathematically minor ("a change from one French dialect to another") while quantum theory was "like a transition to Chinese," and connected his intuition about the nature of a *point* to topos theory — suggesting he did *not* himself draw the relativity-principle analogy the requester seeks. I found **no** essay (Cartier's "A mad day's work," Zalamea, Caramello's "bridges") that explicitly equates Grothendieck's relative point of view with the physical relativity principle; the analogy appears to be, at present, unclaimed territory.

### The decisive technical spine: modular theory as a cocycle

The single most load-bearing discovery for a descent-with-remainder programme is that **modular theory already contains an exact, theorem-grade "change-of-observer" cocycle.**

- **Connes cocycle derivative** (Connes, *Une classification des facteurs de type III*, Ann. Sci. ÉNS (4) 6 (1973) 133–252). For two faithful normal states/weights φ, ψ on a von Neumann algebra M, there is a σ-strongly continuous family of unitaries u(t) = (Dφ:Dψ)_t ∈ M satisfying the **1-cocycle identity** u(t+s) = u(t)·σ^ψ_t(u(s)) and the **intertwining** σ^φ_t(x) = u(t) σ^ψ_t(x) u(t)*. Consequence (standard): the modular automorphism group is canonical **modulo inner automorphisms** — its class in Out(M) is state-independent. This is *precisely* "the description depends on a choice (the state/weight), and two choices are related by a cocycle valued in the algebra," i.e., a transformation law with the physics-independent part as the fixed point.
- **Connes–Takesaki flow of weights** (Connes–Takesaki, *The flow of weights on factors of type III*, Tôhoku Math. J. (2) 29 (1977) 473–575; Takesaki duality, *Duality for crossed products…*, Acta Math. 131 (1973) 249–310). The crossed product N = M ⋊_{σ^φ} ℝ (the "core," type II_∞) carries a canonical trace-scaling dual ℝ-action θ; the restriction of θ to the center Z(N) is a **functorial invariant flow**, independent of φ up to isomorphism (the cocycle implements the conjugacy of cores for different weights). Takesaki duality: (M ⋊ G) ⋊ Ĝ ≅ M ⊗ B(L²(G)). The flow classifies type III factors (III₁ ⇔ trivial flow; III_λ ⇔ periodic; III₀ ⇔ properly ergodic).
- **This is now live in gravity.** Witten (*Gravity and the crossed product*, JHEP 10 (2022) 008) shows 1/N corrections turn the emergent type III₁ algebra into a type II crossed product by the modular flow; CLPW (Chandrasekaran–Longo–Penington–Witten, *An algebra of observables for de Sitter space*, JHEP 02 (2023) 082) adjoin an **observer** to get a type II₁ algebra with a maximum-entropy state. Most on-point: **Antony J. Speranza, *An intrinsic cosmological observer* (arXiv:2504.07630, 2025; Class. Quantum Grav. 42 (2025) no. 21, 215023)** explicitly "relate[s] this procedure to the **Connes–Takesaki theory of the flow of weights** for type III von Neumann algebras," yielding "a decomposition of the gravitational algebra into quantum field and observer degrees of freedom, with different choices of observer being related to changes in a quantum reference frame for the algebra" — and shows the Bunch–Davies weight "defin[es] a dominant weight, in the terminology of the Connes–Takesaki classification." Klinger–Leigh's "G-framed algebra" (arXiv:2405.13884) even flags a "classical Gribov problem" analog — a gauge/descent-flavored obstruction to globally choosing an observer frame. De Vuyst–Eccles–Höhn–Kirklin (arXiv:2405.00114; 2412.15502, 2025) prove gravitational entropy is observer-dependent via the crossed product.
- **Net cohomology already exists** (Roberts, *Local cohomology and superselection structure*, Comm. Math. Phys. 51 (1976) 107; Roberts–Ruzzi–Vasselli, *A theory of bundles over posets*, Adv. Math. 220 (2009) 125): DHR charge transporters are literally 1-cocycles of the poset of regions valued in the observable net's unitaries, and net cohomology classifies superselection sectors — nonabelian cohomology over the base of regions.

**Crucial honesty flag:** the flow-of-weights facts are **established theorems** (Connes 1973; Takesaki 1973; Connes–Takesaki 1977). The *reading* of the family {choice of observer/weight} as a genuine **descent object over a base of regions/states, with a cohomological "remainder,"** is **not yet a theorem** — the "observer/QRF/G-framed" language is used suggestively, and no paper formally proves a groupoid-cohomology/descent statement identifying observer-assignment with a cohomology class. Roberts' net cohomology (rigorous) concerns superselection charge transport, not modular/observer data. **Bridging the two would be a genuinely new synthesis, not a relabeling.**

## Recommendations

**Ranked shortlist of the 3–5 most load-bearing items** for a "cosmos as a cosmos" formalism built on descent-with-remainder:

1. **Connes cocycle + Connes–Takesaki flow of weights, as deployed in the crossed-product gravity programme (Witten; CLPW; Speranza 2025).** This is the *only* place where a rigorous, theorem-grade **1-cocycle transformation law** relating observer-descriptions already exists *and* is already attached to cosmology (de Sitter; the Bunch–Davies state as a Connes–Takesaki *dominant weight*). It is the natural home for the requester's exact proposal: **a family of observer/modular algebras as a stack over a base of states/regions, with the flow of weights as the 1-cocycle and the "remainder" as a descent obstruction class.**
2. **Doplicher–Roberts reconstruction + Roberts net cohomology.** DR is the cleanest existing *theorem* that "global structure (gauge group, fields) is derivable from local observable data," and net cohomology already casts the local→global transport as nonabelian 1-cocycles over the poset of regions. Its **kill conditions** (d > 2, Haag duality, mass gap; failure for braided sectors, cone-localized/topological charges, and infraparticles) are exactly the *remainders* a descent theory should classify.
3. **SymTFT / Freed–Teleman relative QFT.** The rigorous statement "a physical theory is not a number but a boundary vector of a higher topological bulk; changing the topological boundary condition changes the global form" is the best worked "observable = interface, pre-observable = topological bulk" formalism, now reaching spacetime symmetries and gravity.
4. **Topos "no global sections" (Kochen–Specker / Isham–Butterfield; Bohrification).** The theorem that the spectral presheaf has no global section is the sharpest existing formalization of "no God's-eye global valuation; only local sections over contexts." Use it as the logical skeleton (the base = contexts; the obstruction = the missing global section).
5. **Manchak's underdetermination theorems** as the epistemic warrant, and **Harlow–Ooguri** as the proof-of-concept that the global/local distinction genuinely collapses at the level of the whole.

**The single sharpest theorem-shaped first target.** Formalize the assignment `region/state ↦ (observer algebra, modular data)` as a **stack (fibered category) over a site of causal regions**, with the Connes cocycle (Dφ:Dψ) as the descent 1-cocycle, and **prove that the obstruction to a global section (a single, state-independent "algebra of the whole") is a non-vanishing class in the appropriate nonabelian/net cohomology — and identify that class with the Connes–Takesaki flow of weights / the type III₁ obstruction.** Concretely: *show that "the universe has no global observer algebra" is the operator-algebraic analog of "the spectral presheaf has no global section," with the flow of weights as the precise cohomological remainder.* This is theorem-shaped (all ingredients are rigorous), genuinely novel (the DR/net-cohomology and modular/flow-of-weights literatures have never been fused this way), and directly instantiates descent-with-remainder.

**Benchmarks that would change the recommendation.** (a) If a cohesive-∞-topos statement is produced whose "de-globalization" limit reproduces a standard cosmological observable, promote Schreiber's programme from Tier 3 to Tier 1. (b) If SymTFT-for-cosmology yields a concrete "universe = boundary" model with a computable observable, elevate item 3 to the lead. (c) If the Lawrence–Żukowski incompatibility critiques of relational QM stand unrebutted, further discount Rovelli-style relational framings as a foundation.

## Caveats

- **Verbal resonance vs. derivation.** The F₁/absolute-geometry connection to physics, the cohesive-topos "meta-geometry" claim, and the "descent object" reading of the observer/weight family are, at present, **structural analogies, not theorems**. I have graded each explicitly; the reader's allergy to "relabeling presented as derivation" is warranted precisely at these junctions.
- **Contingent theorems.** Harlow–Ooguri is proven *within AdS/CFT* (a DEDUCTION assuming a holographic dual), not model-independently. DR reconstruction is a theorem only under its stated hypotheses and *fails* in the physically important cases of low dimension, braided statistics, and long-range/infraparticle charges.
- **Topos physics' empirical thinness.** The "no global sections" theorem is solid, but the broader Döring–Isham programme has delivered re-derivations more than predictions; treat it as logical scaffolding, not as a predictive cosmology.
- **Relational QM is contested.** The relative-facts framework faces live formal no-go challenges (Lawrence–Markiewicz–Żukowski; Pienaar) that are not fully resolved.
- **Grothendieck-analogy is unclaimed.** I found no published essay equating the relative point of view with the physical relativity principle; Grothendieck's own recorded view downplayed relativity. The requester's core analogy is therefore, encouragingly, *original* — but that also means there is no authority to lean on, only the mathematics itself.
- **Search limitations.** Some cluster-1 items (Bohrification primary papers, Sati–Schreiber Hypothesis H, Baez–Schreiber higher gauge theory) were confirmed via secondary/citation sources rather than fully fetched primaries, owing to search-budget exhaustion; the load-bearing Tier-1 claims (Connes, Takesaki, Connes–Takesaki, DR, Freed–Teleman, Manchak, Harlow–Ooguri, Speranza) are sourced to primary literature.
