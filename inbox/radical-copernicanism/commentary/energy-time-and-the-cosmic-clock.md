# Energy, Time, and the Cosmic Clock

The README's demand: connect energy back to time, with fidelity to the math. Locally the connection is Noether 1 — energy is the symmetry of local time. The question is what the pairing becomes at the cosmos register, where there is no timelike Killing vector and hence no referent for (t, E).

## Every being conserves its own time

The modular form of the local statement is worth having on record: every faithful normal state is KMS with respect to its own modular flow. Every being carries its own time and is in equilibrium with it — self-conservation is built into the state/algebra pair. The Connes cocycle (Dφ:Dψ)_t is then the *exchange rate between two beings' times*, and temperature is the ratio (the KMS strip width). Conservation of energy, read algebraically, is the self-consistency of a being with its own becoming. GRADE: THEOREM (Tomita–Takesaki, KMS, cocycle); GLOSS (the phrasing).

## The global conjugate pair: (Λ, four-volume time)

The covariance survey (§7) already contains the replacement pairing, filed under unimodular gravity: in the Henneaux–Teitelboim formulation, **the cosmological constant is canonically conjugate to four-volume cosmic time**. That is the theorem-adjacent anchor for the README's intuition:

- Locally, energy generates time translation.
- Globally, Λ generates the only clock the cosmos-as-whole possesses — accumulated four-volume.

Dark energy is not an energy that failed to be conserved; it is **the momentum conjugate to the cosmic clock**. GRADE: THEOREM (the canonical pair, classically); OPEN (its quantum status, and whether the identification survives beyond the unimodular gauge trade).

## The chain, mapped link by link

The README's chain — *not conserving time = having more past = being bigger in space = causal inaccessibility = dark energy* — is not a slide of metaphors once each link is typed:

1. **"Not conserving time"** = the module is nontrivial. On the Falcone–Takesaki core the dual flow scales the trace, τ∘θ_s = e^{−s}τ. In a static (globally KMS, Killing) world this module is trivial and time is group-like; in an expanding world time itself has a nonunit Radon–Nikodym derivative. Time is being *produced*, and the production has a rate.
2. **"Having more past"** = the cosmic clock's reading = accumulated four-volume (the Λ-conjugate), equivalently the accumulated trace-scaling parameter.
3. **"Bigger in space"** = the growth of capacity: CST's dC/dN = 2εC, which the inventory's dictionary maps to the trace-scaling action, with ε as the rate. (This dictionary entry is itself an ungraded cross-type solder — see the audit item in `next-targets.md` — but the direction of the map is right: capacity growth is module nontriviality.)
4. **"Causal inaccessibility"** = the growth of the kernel of restriction — the many-to-one fibres of i*, the commutant beyond the horizon — with horizon area as its measure.
5. **"Dark energy"** = the conjugate of the clock (link 2) and the work term of the ledger (d(ρ_Λ a³) = +ρ_Λ d(a³); see `emitting-nothing.md`).

Each link is a typed map; the composite is the claim that these maps commute over one wall family. That composite is exactly the wall-construction obligation, not a new one.
