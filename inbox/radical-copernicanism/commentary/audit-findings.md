# Audit Findings: The Five Notes Against the Three Modules

Audit of 2026-08-25, comparing the five commissioned research notes in this folder against [[program-core/entry|program core]], [[sufficient-reason/entry|sufficient reason]], and [[causal-scale-theory/entry|causal scale theory]].

## Overall verdict

The notes are unusually good commissioned research. They internalized the programme's claim-and-failure discipline — everything graded, kill conditions attached, repackaging risk policed — and they converge rather than sprawl: four of the five land on the same technical spine (Connes cocycle, flow of weights, crossed products). They also contain internal supersessions that must not be lost, because this vault's documented failure mode is revoked inferences resurfacing.

## What each note establishes

**copernican-global-local.md** surveys formalisms for "global is derivable or frame-dependent." Its durable content: the three theorem-grade anchors (Connes cocycle / flow of weights; Doplicher–Roberts reconstruction with its sharp kill conditions; the topos no-global-sections theorems), plus the finding that the Grothendieck-relative-viewpoint ↔ relativity-principle analogy is unclaimed territory. Its "sharpest first target" is partially superseded — see below.

**stack-of-weight-groupoids.md** is the prior-art attack on that target. Verdict: roughly 80% assembled from Falcone–Takesaki (fibre direction) and Roberts–Ruzzi–Vasselli (base machinery, wrong coefficients), with one genuinely open ligament — the costack/descent lemma for O ↦ core(M(O)) over the causal site with modular coefficients. Its two kill conditions bite: a directed or totally ordered base has trivial 1-cohomology, and the type III₁ centralizer is generically trivial, so the bundle rigidifies to the abelian trace-scaling datum.

**covariance-across-the-divide.md** delivers the deepest tension: linearization instability (Taub constraints exist only in the spatially compact case; De Vuyst–Eccles–Höhn–Kirklin tie them to the crossed product) shows the globaled and un-globaled cases are genuinely asymmetric, and the II₁/II∞ dichotomy is a discrete toggle on charge-spectrum boundedness, not a continuous frame transformation. The one place the target is fully realized: conservation and flux-balance are two cases of one Wald–Zoupas statement — but only at asymptotic boundaries.

**modular-theory-inventory.md** verifies the theorem inventory and states the reduced covariance principle worth adopting: weights are coordinates related by the Connes cocycle (THEOREM); invariants are the algebra, the outer class of modular flow, and the Falcone–Takesaki core with its trace-scaling action (THEOREM); the II₁/II∞ selection by the charge spectrum (DEDUCTION). "Weight = frame" outside gauged-modular-flow settings stays GLOSS. Its employment manual is the practical payoff: operator-valued weights along inclusions generically fail to exist except for half-sided modular inclusions; the ε ≠ 0 case has no exact dominant weight (GAP); relative entropy is the safe workhorse.

**algebra-of-causality.md** answers "causality as algebra": Euler element + invariant cone + compression semigroup (Neeb–Ólafsson–Morinelli), provably containing Bisognano–Wichmann and the type III₁ wedge results. For a totally ordered wall chain, the lattice/Haag-duality face trivializes and the surviving causal content is exactly the positive semigroup plus modular positivity — a chain of half-sided modular inclusions.

## Internal supersessions — do not re-import

1. **"The obstruction class = the flow of weights" is dead as stated.** The stack note shows the III₁ flow of weights is ergodic — a point — and the correct nonvanishing datum is the trace-scaling module on the core. Any draft using the slogan will be shot down.
2. **No base cohomological obstruction exists for the cosmological chain.** Directed base ⟹ π₁ = 1 ⟹ every 1-cocycle is a coboundary. The copernican note's stack-over-site framing only pays off when the base widens beyond the chain to genuine covers with spacelike overlaps — which is also when Casini's lattice and Haag duality reactivate.

- The stack note's ligament is the concrete instantiation of program-core's demand that strict descent have a site, cocycle data, and effectivity.
- The inventory's employment manual addresses the decisive open problem of the wall-construction interface — non-circular cross-fiber transport — and its failure diagnostics point at the same structural question as the causality note's chain modeling and sufficient-reason's arrow target C4. See `next-targets.md` for the single gating question.
- The topos no-global-sections theorem is the exact formal statement of "no global necessitating reason"; the weight groupoid is a theorem-level relativity of sufficing reasons. Their fusion is unclaimed. See `the-structure-of-nothing.md`.
- The inventory's dictionary maps CST's capacity law dC/dN = 2εC to the trace-scaling module. This is the one ungraded cross-type identification in an otherwise fastidious document — flagged as an audit item in `next-targets.md`.
