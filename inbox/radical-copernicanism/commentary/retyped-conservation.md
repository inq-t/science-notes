# The Retyped Conservation Law

In 1905 the retyped concept was simultaneity: the mathematics was kept and one classical notion was demoted from absolute to indexical, whereupon the equations balanced. The material in this folder points at the analogous demotion here, and it is not time or space. It is **conservation itself**.

The classical type of a conservation law — a quantity constant along a group of time translations — has no referent for the cosmos as a whole. What survives is a three-clause law, and every clause already lives in the vault.

## Clause 1 — the relativity clause (groupoid)

$$

The Michelson–Morley null result of this principle: no local experiment detects the absolute weight of being. GRADE: THEOREM (Connes 1973; the groupoid reading is the inventory note's clause (b)).

## Clause 3 — the arrow clause (monoid)

What is *produced*, not conserved, is past: the trace-scaling module τ∘θ_s = e^{−s}τ, the growth of capacity, the accumulating kernel of restriction. If an *equality* is wanted for the whole, the candidate is the Hamiltonian constraint plus flux-balance (see `emitting-nothing.md`), not the GSL. ## The law in one breath

**What is conserved: the unit and the books. What flows: the allocation between actuality and potentiality. What is produced: past.**

Clause 1 is the Copernican half (democracy of weights). Clause 3 is the arrow half (the monoid). Clause 2 is the hinge — the exact identity through which a fixed unit is spent differently along the history. The three clauses use only objects already constructed in the vault; the new content is the assertion that *this triple*, and not a group-valued charge, is the correct type of "what the cosmos conserves." That assertion is the retyping the README calls for, and it is falsifiable clause by clause: clause 1 fails if a physical weight is distinguished (a preferred absolute accounting); clause 2 fails wherever the balanced binary reduction fails (the escort-tangent no-go polices this boundary); clause 3 fails if the module trivializes while expansion persists, or if a monotone violation survives the GSL's stated hypotheses.
