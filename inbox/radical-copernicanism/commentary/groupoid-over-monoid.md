# Radical Copernicanism, Corrected: A Groupoid Fibered Over a Monoid

The commissioned research went looking for a higher relativity principle that would make "globaled" and "un-globaled" a frame choice, and reported that the symmetry fails: linearization instability makes the compact and noncompact cases mathematically different in kind, the II₁/II∞ dichotomy is a discrete toggle, and operator-valued weights along inclusions generically do not exist. Read naively, that kills the equivalence-principle analogy.

But the README asked for something subtler than a symmetry. It says the interface's time-category is "not reversible in the same way that locality equations are reversible" — an *ordered* change. The research found exactly that, and the vault already owns its formal shape.

## The two clauses

**Within a level, Copernican democracy is a theorem.** The weight groupoid under the Connes cocycle is connected; no weight is privileged; the invariants are the algebra, the outer class of its modular flow, and the Falcone–Takesaki core with its trace-scaling action. This is the reduced covariance principle of `../modular-theory-inventory.md`, clauses (b)+(c)+(e). GRADE: THEOREM.

**Across levels, the structure is one-sided.** The inclusion M_N ⊂ M_{N'} is endomorphic, not automorphic; restriction of states is many-to-one with no canonical inverse; the Taub constraints exist on one side of the compact/noncompact divide only. This is not the failure of the higher law — it is its second clause. GRADE: THEOREM (each ingredient), DEDUCTION (the reading).

The honest form of radical Copernicanism is therefore not a bigger group. It is **a relativity groupoid fibered over a monoid**: democracy of descriptions at each level, one-way structure across levels. This matches the vault's existing spine — [[sufficient-reason/algebraic-arrow-of-time|the arrow criterion]] (a physically instantiated monoid action that does not extend to a group), the programme's noninvertible wall, and the causality note's own verdict that direction *is* the endomorphic character of the inclusion.

## The boggle, dissolved the 1905 way

"The local frame appears as non-invertible — and that boggles the mind." The theorem that plays the role Lorentz invariance played for the missing ether wind is **Wiesbrock's bijection** (with the Araki–Zsidó correction): a standard half-sided modular inclusion — the one-sided, arrow-bearing object — is in one-to-one correspondence with a positive-energy representation of the ax+b group — a fully reversible group. The monoid and the group are *the same datum in two registers*.

So the arrow is not a dynamical destruction anywhere. It is **spectral positivity seen from a corner**: the ambient flow is invertible, but it preserves the accessible algebra on only one half-line. [[sufficient-reason/algebraic-arrow-of-time|The arrow note]] already states the mechanism ("a group at the ambient level can induce one-sided action on an observable region"); Wiesbrock upgrades the mechanism to an equivalence.

And note what "positivity of energy" means at this register: energy bounded below = being has a floor = there exists a vacuum, a nothing. **The existence of the nothing is what makes local time one-sided.** No floor, no arrow. This is the precise sense in which the sober verdict (asymmetry is structural) and the brave demand (a higher symmetry exists) are simultaneously theorems: the higher symmetry is ambient — the reversible group, the constraint-balanced whole — and irreversibility is its indexical shadow on a part. Exactly as simultaneity turned out to be a projection of an invariant interval, the arrow turns out to be a restriction of an invariant group.

## What this changes in practice

Rewrite the README's target in the two-clause form before distilling; otherwise the "frame choice" slogan will keep pulling the work toward a symmetry the research already excluded. The retyping the situation demands is not of time or space but of *conservation itself* — see `retyped-conservation.md`.
