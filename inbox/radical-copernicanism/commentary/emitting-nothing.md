# Emitting Some Nothing

The README asks: when an electron drops a level it emits a photon — what is the cosmos emitting when it loses energy as a whole? The first sober answer, "nothing is emitted," turns out to be correct when taken literally: the emission is *of nothing*, and "nothing" has enough structure to be emitted.

## The physics instance: memory and soft charges

There is a mainstream, theorem-adjacent instance of emitting some nothing: **gravitational memory**. A wave passes; locally it deposits no energy — the residual disturbance is locally pure gauge, a coordinate change, literally nothing — and yet it leaves a permanent displacement, because it has shifted *which vacuum* the system occupies. The vacuum is a torsor under supertranslations; radiation re-points it. The emission is a coboundary locally and a class globally, and only the boundary ledger (BMS flux-balance; the Λ-BMS de Sitter analogue catalogued in `../covariance-across-the-divide.md` §8) records the transaction.

So the completed analogy: the electron drops a level and emits a something (a photon); the cosmos redshifts and emits a re-pointing of the torsor of nothing — memory without local energy. Noether's second theorem is not the denial of the emission; it is its accounting scheme. GRADE: THEOREM (memory, soft charges, flux-balance, at asymptotic and dS boundaries); OPEN (the fully compact case, where there is no codimension-1 ledger surface — the covariance note's declared gap).

## The FRW bookkeeping form

At the homogeneous level the ledger already balances, in the oldest possible notation: d(ρa³) = −p d(a³). For the vacuum sector p = −ρ, so d(ρ_Λ a³) = +ρ_Λ d(a³): **dark energy gains exactly in proportion to new volume**. What the cosmos emits, in this register, is *more vacuum* — more nothing, and the nothing carries an account (horizon entropy) and does work (negative pressure). GRADE: THEOREM (the continuity equation); GLOSS (the "emits more nothing" phrasing).

## The sign flip, vindicated

The README gropes toward "an E = mc² with a sign flip" and immediately flags the grain of salt. The precise object underneath is the first law of the de Sitter horizon, with its famous minus sign: dE = −T dS. Add matter-being inside the horizon and the horizon-nothing's account *shrinks*. Being and nothing sit on opposite sides of the ledger with a relative minus sign. GRADE: THEOREM (Gibbons–Hawking). The speculative "energy converts to mass at the boundary" phrasing should be quarantined in favor of this.

## The balance is mandatory, and imposing it creates the nothing

The being/nothing balance is not a poetic aspiration; it is the Hamiltonian constraint of a closed cosmos:

$$
H_{\text{matter}} + H_{\text{grav}} = 0.
$$

Total energy zero as a law: every something canceled by spacetime's own negative account. The De Vuyst–Eccles–Höhn–Kirklin result (`../covariance-across-the-divide.md` §3) proves that in the spatially compact case this constraint *must* be imposed at second order — it is the Taub/linearization-stability consistency condition, not an option. And imposing it is precisely what builds the crossed product, which is precisely what creates the trace.

Read as a chain: **the demand of the Whole (the constraint) is what brings the nothing into being** — and time, entropy, and the observer arrive in the same act. Being, forced to balance against itself, precipitates the state of no distinctions. GRADE: THEOREM (each link: Taub ↔ crossed product ↔ trace, within the semiclassical framework); GLOSS (the ontological reading).
