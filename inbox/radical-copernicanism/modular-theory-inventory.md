# A Relativity Principle for "Globality": Verified Modular-Theory Inventory, a Covariance Principle by Deduction, and an Employment Manual for Nested Cosmological Horizon Algebras

## TL;DR
- The verified theorem inventory supports a **smaller-than-advertised but genuine** covariance principle: the algebra M, the outer class of its modular flow, and the Falcone–Takesaki core with its trace-scaling ℝ-action (equivalently the Connes–Takesaki flow of weights) are the frame-independent invariants; a choice of faithful normal semifinite (f.n.s.) weight is a "coordinate frame," and any two frames are inner-related by the Connes cocycle (Dφ:Dψ)_t obeying an exact chain rule. This is THEOREM-backed.
- The globaled/unglobaled dichotomy (II_∞ with semifinite trace scaled by the dual action vs. II_1 with finite trace and a maximum-entropy state) is correctly read as **two realized weight classes of one core-level structure**, selected by whether the added constraint/charge (ADM/observer Hamiltonian) has spectrum bounded below; this is DEDUCTION resting on Witten 2022, CLPW 2023, Chen–Penington 2024, Kudler-Flam–Leutheusser–Satishchandran 2024, and Speranza 2025 — all verified for their type assignments.
- The principle's Achilles heel for the nested wall family is EMPLOYMENT RISK 1.5: a normal faithful semifinite operator-valued weight between nested type III_1 horizon algebras exists **iff** the modular flow of some weight preserves the subalgebra (Takesaki/Haagerup), which generically FAILS for local QFT double cones; the manual must route around it via Connes' spatial derivative, relative modular operators, and half-sided modular inclusion technology. The non-stationary (ε≠0) case has **no exact KMS/dominant weight** and only heuristic "approximately dominant" analogues — a genuine GAP.

## Key Findings
1. **Every load-bearing modular-theory theorem checks out against primary sources**, with two subtleties that matter for employment: (i) the converse to the Connes cocycle theorem requires an analyticity/KMS boundedness hypothesis, not just the algebraic cocycle identity; (ii) dominant weights in Connes–Takesaki are not required faithful, and their uniqueness is only up to unitary conjugacy in M.
2. **The Copernican clause is a theorem, not a metaphor, at the level of the weight groupoid**: Connes' cocycle Radon–Nikodym theorem makes the set of f.n.s. weights a connected groupoid under (Dφ:Dψ)_t, with the chain rule as the groupoid composition law. No weight is privileged.
3. **For type III_1 factors the state centralizer is generically trivial.** This is Marrakchi–Vaes, "Ergodic states on type III₁ factors and ergodic actions," J. Reine Angew. Math. 809 (2024) 247–260 (arXiv:2305.14217): states with trivial centralizer "form a dense G_δ set among all faithful normal states on any III₁ factor with separable predual." The companion structural fact is Marrakchi, "Full factors, bicentralizer flow and approximately inner automorphisms," Invent. Math. 222 (2020) 375–398 (arXiv:1811.10253): "the bicentralizer flow of a type III₁ factor is always ergodic." Consequently the nonabelian/holonomy content collapses and the surviving invariant is the abelian trace-scaling module — exactly as the reader's prior work assumed.
4. **The weight↔reference-frame identification is established as more than analogy only in the crossed-product/QRF constructions** (Ali Ahmad–Chemissany–Klinger–Leigh; De Vuyst–Eccles–Höhn–Kirklin; Speranza), and there only for weights whose modular flow is being gauged. The general "weight = frame" gloss remains INTERPRETIVE.

## Details

### PART 1 — Verified theorem inventory

**(1.1) Connes cocycle Radon–Nikodym theorem.** Source: A. Connes, "Une classification des facteurs de type III," Ann. Sci. ÉNS (4) 6 (1973) 133–252; direct cocycle result Théorème 1.2.1, converse ≈ Théorème 1.2.3/1.2.4. For f.n.s. weights φ, ψ on a von Neumann algebra M there is a strongly continuous family of unitaries (Dφ:Dψ)_t ∈ M satisfying:
- Cocycle identity: (Dφ:Dψ)_{s+t} = (Dφ:Dψ)_s · σ^ψ_s((Dφ:Dψ)_t).
- Chain rule: (Dφ:Dψ)_t (Dψ:Dχ)_t = (Dφ:Dχ)_t.
- Intertwining: σ^φ_t(x) = (Dφ:Dψ)_t σ^ψ_t(x) (Dφ:Dψ)_t^*.
- **Converse (load-bearing):** given φ_0 and a strongly continuous σ^{φ_0}-cocycle {u_t} satisfying an analyticity/KMS boundedness condition (u_t extends to a bounded weak-* continuous function on the strip {0 ≤ Im z ≤ 1}, analytic inside, with boundary values F(t)=φ(u_t), F(t+i)=φ_0(u_t)), there exists a **unique** f.n.s. weight φ with u_t = (Dφ:Dφ_0)_t; explicitly φ(x)=φ_0(u_{-i/2}^* x u_{-i/2}). Restated cleanly by T. Masuda, "A Note on a Theorem of A. Connes on Radon–Nikodym Cocycles," Publ. RIMS Kyoto 20 (1984) 131–136 — abstract verbatim: "every unitary cocycle (relative to a modular automorphism of a weight φ₀) is a Radon-Nikodym unitary cocycle (Dφ:Dφ₀)_t for some faithful normal semifinite weight φ" — and by Longo (gr-qc/9605073). **SUBTLETY / EMPLOYMENT FLAG:** the algebraic cocycle identity alone is NOT sufficient for the converse; the analytic boundedness ("positive type") hypothesis is exactly what must be added. Standard restatements: Takesaki, Theory of Operator Algebras II, Thm VIII.3.3; Ştrătilă, Modular Theory. Grade: THEOREM.

**(1.2) Falcone–Takesaki noncommutative flow of weights.** Source: A. J. Falcone & M. Takesaki, "The non-commutative flow of weights on a von Neumann algebra," J. Funct. Anal. 182 (2001) 170–206. From the paper's abstract: it is "a canonical functor from the category of separable factors to the category of covariant systems of semi-finite von Neumann algebras equipped with trace scaling one parameter automorphism groups with conjugations as morphisms," obtained "by relating an arbitrary pair of faithful semi-finite normal weights by the Connes cocycle." Concretely the core Ñ = (N × W_0(N))/~_t is built choice-free by gluing all f.n.s. weights via the Connes-cocycle equivalence (x,ω) ~_t (x',ω') iff x' = x (Dω/Dω')_t. It carries a canonical trace τ̃ with **τ̃ ∘ σ̃_s = e^{−s} τ̃** (the trace-scaling/dual flow), and the system (Ñ, ℝ, σ̃, τ̃) is isomorphic to the crossed product N ⋊_{σ^ω} ℝ by any chosen weight's modular flow. Morphisms are normal *-homomorphisms/conjugations; the payoff is a weight-independent (choice-free) core, which "put[s] a period to the search for a canonical construction of the core of a factor of type III." Grade: THEOREM.

**(1.3) Connes–Takesaki dominant weights & the flow of weights.** Source: A. Connes & M. Takesaki, "The flow of weights on factors of type III," Tôhoku Math. J. 29 (1977) 473–575, Chapter II (Definition II.1.2, Theorem II.1.3; relative commutant theorem p. 513; dual weight of trace is dominant p. 497).
- **Definition:** an f.n.s. weight is dominant if it is integrable, lacunary (1 isolated in Spec Δ), and of infinite multiplicity (φ ≅ φ⊗Tr). Connes–Takesaki do not require faithfulness in general (Haagerup, Acta Math. 158 (1987) 96: "The weights considered in [13] are not necessarily faithful").
- **Existence + uniqueness:** dominant weights exist on any type III factor with separable predual and are unique up to unitary conjugacy by a unitary u ∈ M (ψ = φ∘Ad(u^*), i.e. φ ≡ ψ mod Inn(M)).
- **Structure:** on a type III_1 factor the centralizer M_φ of a dominant weight is a type II_∞ factor (Haagerup, Acta Math. 158 (1987) 96, quoting CT: "Since dominant weights are unitarily equivalent, it follows that M_φ is a II_∞-factor for every dominant weight φ on a factor M of type III₁"), and {M, σ^φ} ≅ {M_φ ⋊_θ ℝ, θ̂} reproduces the continuous decomposition/core with trace-scaling θ.
- **mod homomorphism:** mod: Aut(M) → Aut(flow of weights) sends α to the restriction of the extended α̃ to the center Z(M̃) of the core (equivalently to the flow of weights). It is Connes–Takesaki's "fundamental homomorphism." For a **full** factor Inn(M) is closed in Aut(M), and for type III_1 the flow of weights is trivial; approximately inner automorphisms lie in ker(mod), and for injective/AFD factors ker(mod) = closure of Inn(M) = Int̄(M) (Kawahigashi–Sutherland–Takesaki, Acta Math. 169 (1992) 105). Grade: THEOREM; the exact ker(mod) statement is type/injectivity-dependent, so flagged.

**(1.4) Takesaki duality & Landstad.** Source: M. Takesaki, "Duality for crossed products and the structure of von Neumann algebras of type III," Acta Math. 131 (1973) 249–310. For a W*-dynamical system (M, ℝ, α) with dual action α̂: (M ⋊_α ℝ) ⋊_{α̂} ℝ ≅ M ⊗ B(L^2(ℝ)). Thus the original M is recovered (up to the stabilization ⊗B(L^2ℝ), harmless for properly infinite M) from the core **plus the dual action** — the dual action is the extra datum. Landstad's theorem characterizes which algebras arise as crossed products (existence of a suitable dual coaction/normal representation of ℝ and equivariant structure) and recovers (M, α) up to isomorphism, whereas Takesaki–Takai alone recovers it only up to Morita equivalence/stabilization. Grade: THEOREM.

**(1.5) Operator-valued weights — the critical employment question.** Sources: U. Haagerup, "Operator valued weights in von Neumann algebras I, II," J. Funct. Anal. 32 (1979) 175–206 and 33 (1979) 339–361; Takesaki's conditional-expectation theorem (Theory of Operator Algebras II, Thm IX.4.2 and IX.4.18).
- **Takesaki:** a normal faithful φ-preserving conditional expectation E: M → N exists iff N is invariant under σ^φ (σ^φ_t(N)=N) and φ|_N is semifinite.
- **Haagerup existence criterion:** a normal faithful semifinite operator-valued weight T: M → N with ψ = φ∘T exists iff there are f.n.s. weights ψ on M, φ on N with σ^ψ_t|_N = σ^φ_t for all t, and then T is unique with that property. Equivalently P(M,N) ≠ ∅ iff P(N',M') ≠ ∅, with a canonical order-reversing bijection E ↦ E^{-1}. Operator-valued weights are strictly more flexible than conditional expectations because they are the "improper" (unbounded, not defined on 1) generalization — like weights vs. states.
- **CRITICAL VERDICT for type III_1 inclusions M(O) ⊂ M(O'):** existence of a normal f.n.s. operator-valued weight requires a weight on the larger algebra whose modular flow preserves the subalgebra. For generic local QFT double cones / nested horizon algebras this modular-invariance condition FAILS (the vacuum modular flow of M(O') does not preserve M(O) except in special geometric coincidences), so a normal f.n.s. operator-valued weight does **not** exist in general. It DOES exist in the special half-sided modular inclusion (h.s.m.i.) case, where by construction the modular flow of the larger algebra acts as a compression semigroup on the smaller (Wiesbrock, Comm. Math. Phys. 157 (1993) 83; gap corrected by Araki–Zsidó, Rev. Math. Phys. 17 (2005) 491). Grade: THEOREM (existence criterion) + EMPLOYMENT RISK (generic nonexistence).

**(1.6) Haagerup–Størmer.** Sources: "Pointwise inner automorphisms of von Neumann algebras," J. Funct. Anal. 92 (1990) 177–201 (with appendix by Sutherland); "Equivalence of normal states on von Neumann algebras and the flow of weights," Adv. Math. 83 (1990) 180–262. An automorphism α is pointwise inner if for every φ ∈ M_*^+ there is u ∈ U(M) with φ∘α = uφu^*. Modular automorphisms σ^φ_t are pointwise inner; the Haagerup–Størmer conjecture (any pointwise inner automorphism of a type III_1 factor is Ad(u)∘σ^φ_t) is now known for trivial-bicentralizer III_1 factors (Isono, arXiv:2309.05279) and holds if Connes' bicentralizer problem is affirmative. The Adv. Math. paper characterizes when two normal states are equivalent (in the u-orbit sense) via the flow of weights. Bearing on the principle: "any two observer descriptions are inner-related" is exactly right at the *state/weight* level (the Connes-cocycle groupoid) and is refined by these results; it does NOT say the implementing automorphism is inner as an automorphism of M. Grade: THEOREM, with careful reading.

**(1.7) Gravitational deployments (type assignments verified).**
- **Witten, "Gravity and the crossed product," JHEP 10 (2022) 008 (arXiv:2112.12828).** Abstract verbatim: "Here we describe some 1/N corrections to this picture and show that the emergent Type III₁ algebra becomes an algebra of Type II∞. The Type II∞ algebra is the crossed product of the Type III₁ algebra by its modular automorphism group. In the context of the emergent Type II∞ algebra, the entropy of a black hole state is well-defined up to an additive constant, independent of the state." The crossing generator is the boundary/ADM Hamiltonian, with spectrum unbounded below and above; this is what forces a *semifinite* (not finite) trace and hence II_∞. Grade: THEOREM (within its semiclassical framework).
- **Chandrasekaran–Longo–Penington–Witten (CLPW), "An algebra of observables for de Sitter space," JHEP 02 (2023) 082 (arXiv:2206.10780).** Abstract verbatim: "The algebra is a von Neumann algebra of Type II₁… There is a maximum entropy state, which corresponds to empty de Sitter space, and the entropy of any semiclassical state of the Type II₁ algebras agrees, up to an additive constant independent of the state, with the expected generalized entropy S_gen = (A/4G_N) + S_out." Mechanism: the observer Hamiltonian spectrum is bounded below (positive energy), which produces a **finite** trace (II_1) and a max-entropy tracial state. Grade: THEOREM (within framework).
- **Speranza, "An intrinsic cosmological observer," arXiv:2504.07630 (Class. Quantum Grav. 42 (2025) 215023).** In the Chen–Penington slow-roll model the Bunch–Davies weight is argued to be a **dominant weight** (Connes–Takesaki integrable-weight theory); the gravitational algebra is a modular crossed product, the observer arising from a preferred trace-scaling automorphism from the scalar's shift symmetry. Different observer choices = different quantum reference frames. Speranza's own key conclusion is verbatim: "The existence of a trace and renormalized entropies is not directly related to the inclusion of an observer; rather, it is the result of gauging modular flow." He explicitly cautions that neighboring works "contain some erroneous claims about the Connes–Takesaki classification theorem and properties of centralizers." Grade: THEOREM/DEDUCTION (the "dominant weight" claim is argued, not proven with full CT rigor).
- **Chen–Penington, "A clock is just a way to tell the time," arXiv:2406.02116; Kudler-Flam–Leutheusser–Satishchandran (KFLS), "Algebraic Observational Cosmology," arXiv:2406.01669 (Phys. Rev. D 111, 025013 (2025)).** FRW/inflaton-clock case gives type **II_∞**, with **no maximum-entropy state**. KFLS abstract verbatim: "An essential quantized degree of freedom is the zero-mode of the inflaton, which leads to fluctuations in the effective cosmological constant during inflation and prevents the existence of a maximum entropy state in the semiclassical limit… we demonstrate that all states are mixed with well-defined von Neumann entropy (up to a state-independent constant)." Chen–Penington's abstract confirms: "The resulting algebras are both Type II∞ factors, but neither is manifestly a crossed product algebra," and stresses "the crucial role of out-of-equilibrium dynamics for defining gauge-invariant observables." Mechanism: the effective-Λ fluctuation makes the relevant charge spectrum unbounded below, precluding a finite trace. Grade: THEOREM (within framework).

### PART 2 — The covariance principle as a deduction

I now state the principle and grade every clause.

**COVARIANCE PRINCIPLE (as supported).**
- **(a) Frame = weight.** A choice of f.n.s. weight φ on M fixes: its modular Hamiltonian/"energy" (generator of σ^φ), its KMS temperature normalization (β=1 in the modular convention), its entropy assignments (via the associated density matrix relative to a trace once one is available), and after crossing, its trace normalization. Grade: DEDUCTION for the mathematical objects (each is literally a function of φ); INTERPRETIVE GLOSS for the word "frame" — see (a') below.
- **(a') "Weight = reference frame."** This identification is established as MORE than analogy only where the weight's modular flow is the symmetry being gauged into a crossed product: Ali Ahmad–Chemissany–Klinger–Leigh (arXiv:2405.13884, Phys. Rev. D 110 (2024) 065003) show the crossed product realizes a QRF from the bottom up and, top-down, that one "cannot obtain inequivalent quantum reference frames using this approach"; De Vuyst–Eccles–Höhn–Kirklin (arXiv:2412.15502, JHEP 07 (2025) 063) show different QRFs give different algebras and different entropies, so "gravitational entropy is observer-dependent"; Speranza ties observer-choice to weight-choice. Outside these gauged-flow settings the identification is INTERPRETIVE GLOSS.
- **(b) Transformation law / Copernican clause.** Any two frames are related by u_t = (Dφ:Dψ)_t, an inner cocycle, with composition = chain rule; the weight groupoid is connected (Connes' converse, 1.1), so all frames are equivalent and none privileged. Grade: THEOREM (1.1).
- **(c) Frame-independent content.** Exactly: the algebra M; the class of its modular flow in Out(M) (well-defined by 1.1 since two modular flows differ by an inner cocycle); the Falcone–Takesaki core with its trace-scaling ℝ-action = the flow of weights (1.2, 1.3); and functions thereof — Connes' S(M), T(M) invariants and the type. Grade: THEOREM (1.1–1.3).
- **(d) Admissibility.** Statements of physical law are admissible only if formulated on the core / cocycle-covariant; weight-dependent statements are "coordinate expressions." Grade: DEDUCTION (a methodological corollary of (b)+(c); it is a stipulation licensed by the theorems, not itself a theorem).
- **(e) Globaled/unglobaled dichotomy.** The physical constraint structure selects which weight classes are realizable: a bounded-below charge/observer-Hamiltonian spectrum yields a **finite** trace (II_1, unglobaled, max-entropy state exists — CLPW); an unbounded spectrum yields a **semifinite** trace scaled by the dual action (II_∞, globaled, no max-entropy state — Witten, KFLS, Chen–Penington). Both are descriptions of the SAME core-level (type III_1 fibre) structure with different realized weight classes. Grade: DEDUCTION, well-supported by 1.7; the "same core" identification across the II_1/II_∞ divide is the interpretive load — II_1 and II_∞ are non-isomorphic, so "same core" must be read as "same underlying type III_1 fibre before crossing," which IS correct.
- **Dominant-weight clause.** Dominant weights are unique only up to inner (unitary) conjugacy (1.3), so even the "canonical/maximally spread" frame has no privileged representative — the Copernican clause survives at the canonical level. Speranza's Bunch-Davies-as-dominant-weight is then the algebraic version of "the CMB rest frame is convenient but not fundamental." Grade of analogy: FAIR but imperfect — a dominant weight is genuinely distinguished as a *class* (it is integrable/lacunary/infinite-multiplicity, unlike a generic weight), so it is more like "the CMB frame is a geometrically preferred *class* of frames (comoving) though no individual comoving observer is privileged" than a fully democratic choice. INTERPRETIVE GLOSS with this caveat.

**Honest overall grade:** the principle is TRUE in the reduced form (b)+(c)+(e); clauses (a),(d) and the QRF identification are DEDUCTION/GLOSS; the grand "weight = frame, always" reading is unsupported and should be weakened to "weight = frame within a gauged-modular-flow crossed-product construction."

### PART 3 — Employment manual for the wall family

Setup (taken as given from prior work): one-parameter nested family M_N of horizon algebras indexed by e-fold time N, type III_1 fibres; transition maps scale horizon capacity C = Area/4G by dC/dN = 2εC with ε = −dlnH/dN; NEC ⇔ positivity of this modulus; exact de Sitter (ε=0) is the trace-preserving II_1 case.

**(3.1) Dictionary.**
- Modular clock (generator of σ^φ on the fibre) ↔ fibre/modular direction (1.1); its outer class is the invariant.
- Capacity flow C(N), dC/dN=2εC ↔ the module / trace-scaling ℝ-action σ̃_s with τ̃∘σ̃_s=e^{−s}τ̃ (1.2). The e-fold "clock" is the log of the trace-scaling parameter; ε sets the rate.
- Bunch–Davies-type state ↔ dominant weight (1.3, Speranza).
- ε=0 exact de Sitter equilibrium ↔ II_1 trace-preserving limit, CLPW max-entropy tracial state (1.7).
- Nesting M_N ⊂ M_{N'} along a totally ordered base ↔ the inclusion direction, where base-cohomology is trivial (reader's prior result), so all invariant content lives in the fibre/module direction.

**(3.2) Three covariant statements one can make today.**
1. **Relative entropy between wall states is frame-independent.** Araki–Uhlmann relative entropy S(φ||ψ) = −⟨Φ|log Δ_{Ψ|Φ}|Φ⟩ is defined purely from relative modular operators, is representation-independent, positive, monotone under restriction (data-processing), and does not depend on any choice of trace or weight-frame. Citations: Araki, "Relative entropy of states of von Neumann algebras," Publ. RIMS 11 (1976) 809, and II, Publ. RIMS 13 (1977) 173; Uhlmann monotonicity. Grade: THEOREM.
2. **The ordering of the modular clock is frame-independent up to inner cocycles.** By (1.1), two modular flows σ^φ, σ^ψ differ by the inner cocycle (Dφ:Dψ)_t; their common image in Out(M) is a single one-parameter group, canonical to M. So "which way the modular clock runs" and its outer-conjugacy class are frame-invariant. Grade: THEOREM.
3. **The II_1 vs II_∞ distinction is weight-class-independent, hence an observable of the family.** The type of the crossed product (equivalently whether the flow of weights admits a finite invariant measure / whether the trace is finite) is an isomorphism invariant, independent of which weight is used to build the core (1.2, 1.3). Physically it is fixed by whether the charge spectrum is bounded below (1.7). Grade: THEOREM (type is invariant) + DEDUCTION (identification with the physical bounded-below criterion).

**(3.3) Two places employment can FAIL, with diagnostics and fallbacks.**
- **(a) Nonexistence of operator-valued weights along the inclusions (from 1.5).** Diagnostic: check whether there is a weight on M_{N'} whose modular flow preserves M_N (σ^ψ_t(M_N)=M_N). If not — the generic case for type III_1 local algebras — no normal f.n.s. operator-valued weight M_{N'} → M_N exists, and naive core-functoriality along the inclusion is blocked. Fallback: (i) work with Connes' **spatial derivative** dφ/dψ' (Connes, J. Funct. Anal. 35 (1980) 153) and **relative modular operators** Δ_{φ|ψ}, which need no conditional expectation; (ii) if the inclusion is (or can be modeled as) a **half-sided modular inclusion**, use Wiesbrock's theorem (Comm. Math. Phys. 157 (1993) 83), Borchers' theorem, and the corrected Araki–Zsidó version (Rev. Math. Phys. 17 (2005) 491) to get a canonical compression semigroup and a genuine operator-valued weight; (iii) use relative entropy (3.2.1) as the primary invariant, since it never needs the operator-valued weight.
- **(b) Non-stationary (ε≠0): no exact KMS/dominant weight.** Diagnostic: if ε≠0 the modulus flow is genuinely time-dependent and there is no σ^φ fixing a state, so no exact KMS state and no exact dominant weight exist. What IS rigorously available: essentially nothing exact — "adiabatic/approximately dominant/slowly varying weights" are at present heuristic, with no theorem providing them. Chen–Penington and KFLS handle ε≠0 not by finding an equilibrium weight but by using the out-of-equilibrium dynamics itself (the rolling inflaton) as a physical clock, obtaining a II_∞ algebra directly (Chen–Penington: "the crucial role of out-of-equilibrium dynamics for defining gauge-invariant observables"); this is the correct model to imitate. Grade: GAP.

**(3.4) Worked toy check: the exact de Sitter limit ε→0.** In the limit ε=0: (i) dC/dN=0, the trace-scaling flow σ̃_s becomes trivial on the relevant sector, the module degenerates, and the finite trace is preserved — the algebra is II_1. (ii) The dominant weight (Bunch–Davies) has a nontrivial centralizer only after adjoining the observer; without it the static-patch modular flow is ergodic (centralizer = ℂ1 — consistent with Marrakchi–Vaes generic triviality for III_1), reproducing CLPW's observation that an observer must be added by hand. (iii) The maximum-entropy state = the tracial state = empty de Sitter (CLPW). (iv) Relative entropies (3.2.1) reduce to the CLPW/Segal-entropy differences, and the II_1 normalization τ(1)=1 makes the max entropy 0 with all physical states at entropy ≤ 0. Every clause of the covariance principle reduces to a CLPW II_1 statement, and no clause requires the operator-valued weight (since at ε=0 the observer-dressed inclusion admits the trace-preserving conditional expectation). This is the consistency check the reader should run: verify that setting ε=0 collapses the module direction and returns exactly CLPW.

## Recommendations
1. **Adopt the reduced principle (b)+(c)+(e) as the operative "relativity principle for globality," and explicitly demote (a),(d) and the QRF identification to interpretive scaffolding.** The reader's stated preference for a smaller true principle over a grander unsupported one is exactly right: the theorems deliver "M + Out(σ) + core/flow-of-weights are invariant; weights are coordinates related by the Connes cocycle; type is fixed by the charge spectrum," and nothing stronger. Threshold that would upgrade this: a theorem establishing the weight↔frame identification outside gauged-flow settings would let (a) be promoted from GLOSS to DEDUCTION.
2. **Make relative entropy the workhorse invariant.** Because Araki relative entropy needs neither a trace nor an operator-valued weight, it is the safest frame-independent quantity across the whole wall family, including ε≠0. Build the manual's quantitative claims on it first; add trace-based (Segal) entropies only once a trace is established.
3. **Before invoking core-functoriality along any inclusion M_N ⊂ M_{N'}, run the σ-invariance diagnostic (1.5).** If it fails, switch to spatial derivatives / relative modular operators, or establish a half-sided modular inclusion and use Wiesbrock/Borchers/Araki–Zsidó. Do not assume a conditional expectation or operator-valued weight exists.
4. **Treat ε≠0 by the Chen–Penington/KFLS physical-clock route, not by equilibrium modular theory.** Flag any use of "adiabatic" or "approximately dominant" weights as a conjecture requiring its own proof.
5. **Benchmarks that would change the recommendations:** (i) a theorem establishing existence of "approximately dominant" weights with error bounds would upgrade the ε≠0 GAP to a DEDUCTION and let the module direction be treated perturbatively; (ii) a proof that the nested horizon inclusions are half-sided modular (or admit modular-invariant weights) would restore operator-valued weights and full core-functoriality; (iii) a resolution of Connes' bicentralizer problem would sharpen the Haagerup–Størmer "observer descriptions are inner-related" clause to full generality for III_1.

## Caveats
- **Framework-level, not theorem-level, for the gravity papers.** Witten, CLPW, Chen–Penington, KFLS, and Speranza operate in semiclassical (G→0, large-N) perturbation theory; the type assignments are rigorous *given* their algebraic idealizations (emergent III_1 large-N algebra, crossed product by a chosen flow), not theorems about full quantum gravity. Speranza himself notes some neighboring literature made erroneous claims about the Connes–Takesaki classification, so "Bunch-Davies = dominant weight" should be treated as a well-motivated argument, not a completed proof.
- **"Same core" across the II_1/II_∞ divide.** II_1 and II_∞ are non-isomorphic; the correct invariant statement is that they share the same type III_1 fibre before crossing and the same flow-of-weights data, not that the crossed products are the same algebra.
- **mod-homomorphism kernel** is type- and injectivity-dependent; the clean "ker(mod) = closure of Inn(M)" holds for injective/AFD factors (Kawahigashi–Sutherland–Takesaki), and for type III_1 the flow of weights is trivial so mod carries no information there — the invariant content in the III_1 fibre is entirely the (trivial-flow) trace-scaling module, consistent with the reader's collapse-to-abelian-module claim.
- **The converse to the Connes cocycle theorem needs the analyticity hypothesis** (1.1); an employment that constructs a cocycle by hand must verify the KMS/positive-type boundedness before concluding a weight exists.
- **Primary-source access.** The original Tôhoku (Connes–Takesaki 1977) and Ann. ÉNS (Connes 1973) PDFs are behind publisher robots blocks; their theorem numbers and statements here are corroborated through Haagerup (Acta Math. 158, 1987, which quotes them verbatim), Masuda (Publ.
