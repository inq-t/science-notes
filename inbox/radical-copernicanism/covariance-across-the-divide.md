# Covariance Across the Boundary/No-Boundary Divide: A Survey of Frameworks

## TL;DR
- No existing framework fully achieves the "radically Copernican" goal of making the presence or absence of a boundary a genuine *frame choice* under a single higher law; the closest thing to a unifying principle is the **crossed-product / modular-theory mechanism**, in which "global energy exists as a boundary charge" (type II∞) and "no global energy, only a maximally-mixed constraint" (type II₁) appear as two outcomes of ONE construction — but the interpolation is a *discrete* criterion (bounded vs. unbounded modular-charge spectrum, finite vs. infinite trace), not a smooth parameter, and the general boundary↔no-boundary dichotomy is explicitly labeled a *suggestion*, not a theorem.
- The single sharpest, most rigorous statement of the actual distinction is the theory of **linearization instabilities** (Moncrief, Fischer–Marsden, Arms): in spatially compact spacetimes with a Killing symmetry, physical linearized perturbations must satisfy quadratic "Taub charge" constraints that have NO analogue in the asymptotically flat case — and 2024–2025 work (De Vuyst–Eccles–Höhn–Kirklin) shows these are exactly the second-order constraints that generate the gravitational crossed product, tying the compact/noncompact split directly to the II₁/II∞ split.
- To work on this seriously you would combine four ingredients — locally covariant QFT (functorial "same physics"), the universal corner symmetry algebra, gravitational crossed products with quantum reference frames, and linearization-instability theory — but you should treat most cross-framework "unifications" as relabelings or conjectures, not derivations; the honest state of the art is that the pieces rhyme but no one has written down the single covariant law the conjecture asks for.

## Key Findings

**The conjecture is well-posed and partially vindicated, but the "higher law" does not yet exist as a theorem.** The user's intuition — that "globaled vs. unglobaled" resembles "inertial vs. accelerated" — maps onto a real and active research frontier. In several frameworks the two cases genuinely appear as instances of one structure. But in every case either (a) the unification is conceptual/postulated rather than derived, (b) the interpolation is discrete rather than a continuous frame transformation, or (c) the framework covers matter fields on a fixed background and does not touch the gravitational-energy question the conjecture is really about.

A recurring and important warning sign: the theory of **linearization instabilities** provides positive evidence that the two cases are genuinely *asymmetric* — mathematically different in kind, not merely different in description. That is the opposite of what the inertial/accelerated analogy would predict, and it is the deepest tension in the whole picture.

**Grading key used throughout:** THEOREM (proved), DEDUCTION (follows rigorously from stated assumptions, but assumptions are physics-level), POSTULATE (assumed as a principle), CONJECTURE (explicitly hedged by its authors), OPEN (not addressed).

## Details

### (1) Locally Covariant QFT (Brunetti–Fredenhagen–Verch; Fewster)

**What it is.** LCQFT formulates a QFT as a *functor* 𝒜 from the category **Loc** — whose objects are globally hyperbolic spacetimes and whose morphisms are isometric, causality- and orientation-preserving embeddings — to a category **Alg** of algebras. "The theory" is the functor; "the same physics in all spacetimes" becomes a naturality/covariance condition on it. Brunetti–Fredenhagen–Verch introduced this as the "generally covariant locality principle" (*Commun. Math. Phys.* 237, 31–68, 2003), describing locally covariant theories "in terms of covariant functors between the categories... of globally hyperbolic spacetimes with isometric embeddings as morphisms and... of \*-algebras with unital injective \*-monomorphisms." The **relative Cauchy evolution** — comparing the algebra map you get by perturbing the metric in a compact region against the identity — is the functorial analogue of the stress-energy tensor: in their words, "the functional derivative of a relative Cauchy-evolution with respect to the spacetime metric... has the significance of an energy-momentum tensor." Fewster and Verch's **dynamical locality** (arXiv:1106.4785, 1109.6732; *Ann. Henri Poincaré* 13, 2012) is the criterion that kinematic and dynamical descriptions of local physics coincide; Fewster's "same physics in all spacetimes" (SPASs) program (arXiv:1105.6202; arXiv:1502.04642, *Phil. Trans. R. Soc. A* 373, 2015) uses it to prove, e.g., that no covariant choice of preferred (vacuum) state exists.

**How far it goes.** LCQFT treats compact and noncompact Cauchy surfaces *uniformly and by construction*: **Loc** contains both, and the functor assigns algebras to both without privileging either. This IS a Copernican move — but for **matter fields on a fixed or perturbatively-treated background**, not for gravitational energy as a boundary charge. LCQFT deliberately brackets the dynamical-gravity question. It gives you covariance of the *field theory* across the boundary/no-boundary distinction, and its "no natural state" theorem is a genuine model-independent result about why there is no global vacuum in general. But it does not, and does not try to, make the ADM/Bondi charge vs. Wheeler–DeWitt constraint dichotomy a frame choice. **Grade: THEOREM (for matter-field covariance across spacetimes); OPEN (for gravitational energy / boundary charges).** Kill condition: LCQFT would address the conjecture only if the relative Cauchy evolution / stress tensor could be promoted to carry the full diffeomorphism charge structure including boundary terms — which is not part of the framework.

**The crucial 2024 bridge.** Fewster–Janssen–Loveridge–Rejzner–Waldron, "Quantum reference frames, measurement schemes and the type of local algebras in QFT" (arXiv:2403.11973; *Commun. Math. Phys.* 406:19, 2025) connects LCQFT-style rigor to the crossed-product story of §5. They prove: combining relativistic measurement theory with a quantum reference frame yields a joint algebra invariant under the isometry group, parameterized as a crossed product; if the field has a KMS state at temperature β the invariant algebra is **semifinite (type II)**; if *additionally* the QRF has good thermal behavior (a KMS weight finite on a dense subalgebra at the same β), the trace is **finite (type II₁)**. This is the most mathematically careful version of "boundary structure ⟺ type II∞, no boundary ⟺ type II₁." **Grade: THEOREM (precise sufficient conditions given).**

### (2) Corner Symmetry and Edge Modes

**What it is.** Conserved charges in gauge/gravity theories live on codimension-2 surfaces ("corners"). Donnelly–Freidel (arXiv:1601.04744) introduced **edge modes** as the degrees of freedom restoring factorization of the Hilbert space across an entangling surface in gauge theory. Freidel–Geiller–Pranzetti and Ciambelli–Leigh–Freidel developed the **corner symmetry algebra**. Ciambelli–Leigh ("Isolated Surfaces and Symmetries of Gravity," arXiv:2104.07643; "Universal Corner Symmetry and the Orbit Method," arXiv:2207.06441, *Nucl. Phys. B*) identified a **Universal Corner Symmetry (UCS)** group, UCS = (Diff(S) ⋉ GL(2,ℝ)^S) ⋉ (ℝ²)^S — surface diffeomorphisms, a general linear group acting on the two normal directions at each corner point, and normal translations. Finite-distance corners realize the **Extended Corner Symmetry (ECS)** subalgebra, whose maximal factor is sl(2,ℝ) ⋉ ℝ² (also written GL(2,ℝ)⋉ℝ²); asymptotic corners realize a *complementary* subalgebra, and BMS/BMSW appears as a subgroup of the universal one.

**How far it goes.** This is the framework most explicitly *designed* to unify boundary-at-infinity charges with quasi-local ones: the papers state the algebra "applies to both finite distance and asymptotic corners," and via the coadjoint-orbit method the generic UCS orbit "split[s] into sub-orbits spanned by finite distance and asymptotic corner symmetries, such that the full universal symmetry algebra gives rise to a unified treatment of corners in a manifold." So a compact universe with no boundary at infinity and an asymptotically flat one carry the *same* universal corner algebra at any corner; asymptotics only select which subalgebra is realized. This directly supports the conjecture's spirit: the fundamental symmetry structure is corner-local and asymptotics-independent. **However:** (i) the claim that UCS is "the fundamental symmetry structure of gravity" and that a quantum theory would "unitarily glue" corner structures (with "spacetime geometries appearing as semi-classical configurations") is a research program, not a theorem — the gluing/quantization is conjectural. (ii) Corner charges are quasi-local; the framework does not by itself tell you whether a *compact universe has zero total energy* — it relocates the question to how corner charges compose globally. (iii) In a closed universe you can still draw corners and get the same algebra, but the *global* charge (sum over a closed slice) still vanishes by the same Noether-second-theorem logic; the corner proposal does not dissolve that, it reframes it. **Grade: DEDUCTION (universality of the corner algebra, off-shell); POSTULATE/CONJECTURE (that this is THE fundamental structure and that global gravity emerges by gluing corners).** Kill condition: the program fails the conjecture's test if gluing corner Hilbert spaces cannot reproduce the compact-universe constraint (H|Ψ⟩=0) as a limit — currently unknown.

### (3) Linearization Instabilities (Moncrief; Fischer–Marsden; Arms; Taub charges)

**What it is — and why it is the sharpest statement of the problem.** In a nonlinear theory, a solution of the *linearized* Einstein equations is a necessary but not sufficient condition to be tangent to a curve of *exact* solutions. Fischer–Marsden and Moncrief ("Spacetime symmetries and linearization stability of the Einstein equations. I," *J. Math. Phys.* 16, 493, 1975) proved: for vacuum spacetimes with **compact Cauchy surfaces**, the space of solutions is linearization-**stable if and only if the spacetime admits no global Killing vector field**. In Moncrief's own words, "We showed that a space–time of this class is linearization stable if and only if it admits no global Killing vector fields... we derive the general nonlinear constraints upon the perturbations which are necessary, whenever Killing symmetries occur, to exclude spurious perturbation solutions." That quadratic (second-order) constraint is the vanishing of the **Taub conserved quantity**. Arms–Marsden–Moncrief showed the solution space has a **conical singularity** at symmetric backgrounds: linearized solutions violating the Taub constraint are spurious tangent vectors that do not integrate to real solutions.

Crucially, **this phenomenon is absent in the asymptotically flat / noncompact case**. Arms–Marsden ("The absence of Killing fields is necessary for linearization stability of Einstein's equations," *Indiana Univ. Math. J.* 28, 119, 1979) established the necessity of no-Killing-fields; concretely, as later work summarizes (Phys. Rev. D 97, 024028), "Minkowski space, having a non-compact Cauchy surface, is linearization stable." This is a rigorous, theorem-level statement of exactly the compact/noncompact asymmetry the conjecture is about. Altas–Tekin (*Phys. Rev. D* 99, 104078, 2019; arXiv:1705.10234) extended Taub-charge/ADT-charge analysis to generic gravity and (A)dS; Losic–Unruh and later work (arXiv:2003.14108) worked out the closed-FRW/Einstein-static case explicitly.

**The 2024–2025 unification with crossed products.** De Vuyst–Eccles–Höhn–Kirklin, "Linearization (in)stabilities and crossed products" (arXiv:2411.19931; *JHEP* 05(2025)211), is the paper that most directly serves the conjecture. Their results:
- The CLPW crossed-product-generating boost constraints (§5) are **second-order** constraints, and in spatially closed spacetimes with Killing symmetries they are **exactly the Taub-charge constraints**: a linearized classical solution can be integrated to an exact one if and only if all the second-order Killing symmetry gauge generators (the Taub charges) are imposed to vanish. This is a THEOREM (classical).
- Therefore, in the closed case, imposing the constraint C = H_QFT + ΣᵢHᵢ = 0 that produces the type II algebra is **unambiguously justified** by linearization stability — you *must* impose it or the perturbation theory is inconsistent.
- In the case *with an asymptotic boundary or no isometries*, **no instability arises** (Fischer–Marsden, Arms). Verbatim from their abstract: "While for spatially closed spacetimes there is an essentially unambiguous justification, in the presence of boundaries or the absence of isometries this depends on whether one is also interested in second-order observables." They add that this boundary case "stands on arguably weaker grounds as far as the G_N→0 limit is concerned."
- They recast the whole subject from ADM canonical language into **covariant phase space** language, exhibiting "the universal structure behind (in)stabilities" for arbitrary generally covariant theories, and connect the Taub charges to generators of modular flow.

This is the single most honest and precise bridge in the literature between the compact/noncompact geometric distinction and the II₁/II∞ algebraic distinction. It does NOT, however, present them as two frames of one law with a symmetry relating them; it presents them as two *regimes* with genuinely different mathematical status (one has a rigorous justification, the other does not). **Grade: THEOREM (the Taub↔crossed-product identification in the closed case); explicitly asymmetric — the framework says the two cases are NOT symmetric, which is evidence AGAINST a naive "frame choice" reading.** Moncrief already suggested in the 1970s including "internalized observers" for de Sitter thermodynamics — the modern crossed product realizes exactly this idea.

### (4) Quantum Reference Frames and the Perspective-Neutral Framework (Höhn, Vanrietvelde, Giacomini, Castro-Ruiz, Brukner, Smith)

**What it is.** In the perspective-neutral program (Vanrietvelde–Höhn–Giacomini–Castro-Ruiz, "A change of perspective," *Quantum* 4, 225, 2020), there is a **perspective-neutral structure** — the gauge-invariant "clock-neutral" physical Hilbert space of Dirac quantization — from which each frame's description is obtained by a *quantum symmetry reduction* (a gauge-fixing). Switching frames always proceeds *through* the neutral structure, exactly analogous to changing coordinate charts through a manifold. Höhn–Smith–Lock's "Trinity of Relational Quantum Dynamics" (arXiv:1912.00033) proves the equivalence of (i) Dirac relational observables in the clock-neutral picture, (ii) the Page–Wootters formalism, and (iii) symmetry-reduced relational Heisenberg dynamics — three faces of one dynamics. **Relational Dirac observables** ("the value of A when clock C reads τ") are the gauge-invariant content; in a spatially compact universe with H|Ψ⟩=0, a QRF (a physical clock/rod subsystem) supplies the reference that a boundary would otherwise supply.

**How far it goes.** This framework directly generalizes the relativity principle to *quantum* frames and gives a clean, theorem-level account of how a compact universe with no external time still has relational dynamics — the "problem of time" is dissolved by making the clock internal. It supplies precisely the "reference that a boundary would otherwise supply." And it connects to the algebraic story: the CLPW observer is a QRF, and the crossed product is generated by relational observables (made explicit in arXiv:2412.15502 and 2411.19931). **But:** (i) the "absence of global relational perspectives" result (Vanrietvelde–Höhn–Giacomini, arXiv:1809.05093) shows that in nontrivial cases (Gribov obstructions, no global gauge-fixing) there is NO globally valid frame perspective — a genuine obstruction to the "single law in all frames" picture. (ii) The explicit formulation of "changing between an internal-observer frame and an external/asymptotic frame *as a QRF transformation*" is only partially developed — the pieces exist (asymptotic boundary as a clock; observer as a clock) but a clean frame-transformation dictionary between them is not established. **Grade: THEOREM (trinity equivalence, perspective-neutral reduction); OPEN (internal↔asymptotic frame transformation as a single QRF change); the no-global-perspective result is a partial KILL condition against naive universal covariance.**

### (5) Observer-Dressed Algebras and Crossed Products (Witten; CLPW; Leutheusser–Liu; Jensen–Sorce–Speranza; Kudler-Flam–Leutheusser–Satishchandran; Chen–Penington)

**What it is.** The local algebra of QFT observables in a subregion is a **type III₁** von Neumann factor — no trace, no density matrices, no well-defined entropy. Leutheusser–Liu (arXiv:2110.05497, 2112.12156) found an emergent type III₁ algebra in large-N holography. Witten, "Gravity and the crossed product" (arXiv:2112.12828, *JHEP* 10(2022)008), showed that including the fluctuating boundary Hamiltonian (the ADM mass) via the **crossed product** of the type III₁ algebra by its modular automorphism group converts it to **type II∞**. In Witten's words: "the emergent Type III₁ algebra becomes an algebra of Type II∞. The Type II∞ algebra is the crossed product of the Type III₁ algebra by its modular automorphism group. In the context of the emergent Type II∞ algebra, the entropy of a black hole state is well-defined up to an additive constant, independent of the state." CLPW (Chandrasekaran–Longo–Penington–Witten, "An algebra of observables for de Sitter space," arXiv:2206.10780, *JHEP* 02(2023)082) did the de Sitter static patch: with no asymptotic boundary to dress to, they dress to an **observer's worldline** (a clock), and get **type II₁**. In their words: "The algebra is a von Neumann algebra of Type II₁... There is a maximum entropy state, which corresponds to empty de Sitter space, and the entropy of any semiclassical state... agrees, up to an additive constant independent of the state, with the expected generalized entropy S_gen=(A/4G_N)+S_out." Jensen–Sorce–Speranza (arXiv:2306.01837) generalized to arbitrary subregions.

**Is there a UNIFIED construction interpolating II∞ ↔ II₁?** This is the crux for the conjecture, and my targeted investigation found:
- **Kudler-Flam–Leutheusser–Satishchandran, "Generalized Black Hole Entropy is von Neumann Entropy" (arXiv:2309.15897; *Phys. Rev. D* 111, 025013, 2025)** state the cleanest dichotomy, verbatim: *"Our results suggest that in all cases where there exists another 'boundary structure' (e.g., an asymptotic boundary or another Killing horizon) the algebra of observables is Type II∞ and in the absence of such structures (e.g. de Sitter spacetime) the algebra is Type II₁."* Their proven core is a **structure theorem** (Theorem 1): the exterior of a bifurcate Killing horizon carries a type II algebra. The further II∞/II₁ split is fixed *discretely* by the spectrum of the gravitational charge X = δ²Q/4G_Nβ: spectrum = ℝ (unbounded) ⟹ II∞; spectrum bounded above (e.g. by the observer energy in de Sitter) ⟹ II₁. **The general boundary↔no-boundary dichotomy is explicitly a "suggestion," i.e. CONJECTURE-level, not a theorem.**
- **The "centaur-algebra" (Aguilar-Gutierrez–Bahiru–Espíndola, arXiv:2307.04233, *JHEP* 03(2024)008)** is the one paper explicitly building a *geometric interpolation* — flow geometries between AdS₂ and dS₂. But on close reading, the *undeformed* centaur stays **type II∞ throughout** the AdS₂→dS₂ interpolation ("since the trace of the identity matrix Tr 𝟙 → ∞ then the algebra is type II∞"); the reduction to II₁ comes from *separately added* structure (a TT̄ deformation that bounds the Brown–York energy spectrum, or an infalling-observer dressing), which the authors call "a priori independent discussions." So the geometric boundary→no-boundary interpolation does NOT itself flip the type.
- **Fewster et al. (arXiv:2403.11973)** give the most genuinely unified *abstract* criterion (see §1): one crossed-product construction, with II vs II₁ toggled by a single trace-finiteness condition on the QRF (a KMS weight finite on a dense subalgebra at the same temperature β), interpretable as a growth/spectral-multiplicity condition analogous to nuclearity.

**Verdict on the unified statement.** The common thread is real and important: II∞ and II₁ are two faces of ONE crossed-product/modular mechanism, and which one you get is controlled by whether the modular-charge spectrum yields an infinite or finite trace. This is a genuine *conceptual* unification and is the strongest single piece of support for the user's conjecture. **But it is a discrete criterion (bounded vs. unbounded spectrum), not a continuous frame transformation; and the overarching "boundary ⟺ II∞ / no-boundary ⟺ II₁" law is a stated conjecture, not a theorem.** Chen–Penington ("A clock is just a way to tell the time," arXiv:2406.02116) and Kudler-Flam–Leutheusser–Satishchandran ("Algebraic Observational Cosmology," arXiv:2406.01669) further show that in FRW/inflation a rolling inflaton can act as the clock, giving type II∞ with no external clock or asymptotic charge, and that the inflaton zero-mode fluctuation prevents a maximum-entropy state. **Grade: DEDUCTION/CONJECTURE — one mechanism, two discrete outcomes; the interpolation is not yet a covariant frame change.**

### (6) Holographic Screens Without Asymptotics (Bousso–Engelhardt)

**What it is.** Holographic screens (Bousso, "Holography in general spacetimes," hep-th/9906022) and future/past **holographic screens** (Bousso–Engelhardt, "New Area Law," *Phys. Rev. Lett.* 115, 081301, 2015; "Proof," *Phys. Rev. D* 92, 044031, 2015) are hypersurfaces foliated by marginally (anti-)trapped surfaces — quasi-locally defined, requiring no event horizon or boundary at infinity. They obey a **classical area theorem** (area monotonically increases along the foliation). **Q-screens** (Bousso–Engelhardt, "Generalized Second Law for Cosmology," *Phys. Rev. D* 93, 024025, 2016) are the semiclassical generalization obeying a **generalized second law** (generalized entropy = area/4G + S_out increases), proved assuming the Quantum Focusing Conjecture. Applied to cosmology this gives a **holographic no-hair theorem** ("Cosmic Equilibration," arXiv:1703.09241).

**How far it goes.** This is holography for spacetimes with no boundary at infinity, and it is a genuine, quasi-local, boundary-independent formulation of an *area/entropy law*. It is the best candidate for "holography without asymptotics." **But** it is holography in the weak sense of an entropy bound / area law, NOT a full boundary-independent *duality* (no dual theory living on the screen with a complete dictionary). The GSL is proven only modulo the Quantum Focusing Conjecture. It does not address gravitational energy/charge covariance. **Grade: THEOREM (classical area law); DEDUCTION-modulo-QFC (cosmological GSL); OPEN (a genuine boundary-free holographic *duality*).** Kill condition: screens give a duality only if a dynamical theory can be defined intrinsically on them — not achieved.

### (7) Unimodular Gravity and Shape Dynamics

**Unimodular gravity.** Fixing det(g) (or the trace-free Einstein equations) demotes full diffeomorphism invariance to **transverse (volume-preserving) diffeomorphisms**; the cosmological constant Λ reappears as an **integration constant** rather than a Lagrangian parameter (Henneaux–Teitelboim gave the covariant Hamiltonian formulation). Its conjugate variable is a **global cosmic time** (four-volume time), so unimodular gravity restores a notion of global time and a nonzero global Hamiltonian even in the compact case — directly relevant to the conjecture. **How far it goes:** it makes a *global* quantity (Λ, and its conjugate time) frame-dependent in a controlled way, trading one gauge symmetry for another. But it does not make the *presence of a boundary* frame-dependent; it changes which symmetries are gauge, not whether the universe is bounded. It is classically equivalent to GR-with-Λ; its distinctive claims (cosmological-constant-problem resolution) rely on a global four-volume constraint and remain contested. **Grade: POSTULATE (reshuffling of gauge); a real but limited example of "global becomes frame-dependent"; does NOT address boundary covariance.**

**Shape dynamics** (Barbour–Koslowski–Mercati; Gomes–Gryb–Koslowski). Trades **refoliation (many-fingered-time) invariance** for **local spatial conformal (Weyl) invariance**: scale becomes relational rather than absolute. GR and shape dynamics are two gauge-fixings of one **linking theory** — a concrete, worked example of "reshuffling which symmetries are gauge," and the closest existing analogue in spirit to the conjecture's "frame choice." Barbour–Koslowski–Mercati claim it solves the classical problem of time. **How far it goes:** it is a genuine dualization (symmetry trading via a linking theory) and demonstrates the *mechanism* the conjecture wants — but for the foliation/scale symmetry, not for the boundary/no-boundary distinction. It is a minority program; its quantization is undeveloped and it is defined on compact slices essentially by construction. **Grade: DEDUCTION (GR↔SD equivalence via linking theory, classical); the linking-theory idea is the best template for "two frames of one law," but it has NOT been applied to the boundary question.**

### (8) Asymptotic Symmetries and Flux-Balance Laws (BMS; Barnich–Troessaert; Wald–Zoupas; de Sitter)

**What it is.** The BMS group (supertranslations + Lorentz/superrotations) is the asymptotic symmetry group at null infinity of asymptotically flat spacetime. In the presence of radiation the associated charges are **non-integrable** and **not conserved**; the correct statement is a **flux-balance law** (Bondi mass loss). The **Wald–Zoupas prescription** (*Phys. Rev. D* 61, 084027, 2000) singles out a preferred symplectic flux / integrable charge by requiring conservation in the absence of news; the **Barnich–Troessaert bracket** (*JHEP* 12(2011)105) gives a consistent algebra for non-integrable charges. Strominger's infrared triangle links these to soft theorems and memory.

**Is conservation a special case of flux-balance?** Yes — this is the one place where the conjecture's "two cases of one statement" is essentially *achieved and standard*. A flux-balance law dQ/du = F (charge change = flux through the boundary) reduces to conservation dQ/du = 0 when the flux (news) vanishes. Wald–Zoupas is precisely the prescription that makes "conserved when non-radiative, flux-balanced when radiative" one uniform statement. Chandrasekaran–Flanagan–Prabhu and Freidel with collaborators have derived the Barnich–Troessaert bracket from a subregion variational principle (rendered "unambiguous by appealing to the variational principle for the subregion, which selects a preferred form of the symplectic flux"), giving conservation and flux-balance a common origin. **Grade: THEOREM/DEDUCTION — conservation and flux-balance ARE two cases of one statement; this is the conjecture's target, realized, but only for asymptotic boundaries.**

**BMS-like structures without a boundary at infinity.** These exist: (i) **Λ-BMS** at the conformal boundary of asymptotically de Sitter space, reducing to BMS as Λ→0, with an SO(1,4) flux-balance law for gravitational radiation at quadrupolar order (arXiv:2411.16215); (ii) **BMS-like symmetries in FRW cosmology** (*Phys. Rev. D* 102, 104043, 2020) — a semidirect sum of supertranslations and the Lorentz algebra, but with *no preferred translation subgroup*, so not isomorphic to BMS; (iii) **near-horizon / cosmological-horizon** symmetry algebras with their own flux-balance laws (Barnich–Troessaert bracket at horizons, e.g. arXiv:2511.11136). So the *machinery* (flux-balance, non-integrable charges) transplants to horizons and cosmological boundaries. But these still rely on *some* codimension-1 surface (a horizon or conformal boundary) to define fluxes; they do not reach the fully compact, boundaryless case, where the charges become the Wheeler–DeWitt constraints. **Grade: THEOREM (Λ-BMS, cosmological BMS exist); OPEN (a flux-balance law for a genuinely closed universe with no horizon).**

### (9) Explicit Work on the Global/Local Covariance Question and Philosophy of Boundaries

**Physics.** No paper poses the exact question "is the presence of a boundary frame-dependent?" as a generalized equivalence principle. The closest are: the corner-symmetry program (§2, "universal, asymptotics-independent"), the crossed-product dichotomy (§5), and the linking-theory idea of shape dynamics (§7). Speranza's "An intrinsic cosmological observer" (arXiv:2504.07630) and "An algebra for covariant observers in de Sitter space" (arXiv:2511.00622) push toward observer-intrinsic, boundary-free formulations.

**Philosophy of physics.** There is a substantial literature on whether boundary/corner charges are physical or conventional and on the status of Noether's second theorem:
- **Gomes–Roberts–Butterfield, "The Gauge Argument: A Noether Reason"** (in *The Philosophy and Physics of Noether's Theorems*, CUP 2022; arXiv:2105.11154) reconstruct the gauge principle via Noether's *second* theorem — directly relevant to "what does it mean that all charges are demoted to constraints in the compact case."
- **Gomes, "Noether charges, gauge-invariance, and non-separability"** (same volume) and **Gomes–Hopfmüller–Riello, "A unified geometric framework for boundary charges and dressings"** (*Nucl. Phys. B* 941, 2019; arXiv:1808.02074) argue that with a field-space connection ϖ one gets a *background/boundary-condition-independent* notion of charge — "the horizontal gauge charges always vanish, while global charges still arise for reducible configurations characterized by global symmetries." This is a rigorous statement that boundary charges depend on a *choice of dressing*, close to the conjecture's "conventional vs. physical" question.
- **David Wallace, "Isolated systems and their symmetries," Parts I & II** (*Stud. Hist. Phil. Sci.* 92, 2022) analyzes when boundary/edge structure is physical vs. an artifact of treating a subsystem as isolated — the philosophical core of the conjecture.
- **Teh; Wallace–Greaves; Murgueitio Ramírez–Teh** debate the "empirical significance" of gauge symmetry (the "Galileo's ship" question), the philosophical form of "is a global charge a real degree of freedom?"

The consensus emerging from this literature leans *against* a naive reading of boundary charges as straightforwardly physical: they are dressing/reference-dependent, and whether they carry physical significance depends on the availability of an external reference — precisely the point the QRF/crossed-product physics makes technically. **Grade: this literature POSES the conjecture's question sharply and leans toward "boundary charges are reference-dependent, not absolute," which SUPPORTS the frame-dependence intuition — but no one has written down the covariant higher law.**

## Recommendations

**Which framework comes closest.** The **gravitational crossed product + quantum reference frames + linearization instabilities** cluster (§§3,4,5, tied together by De Vuyst–Eccles–Höhn–Kirklin arXiv:2411.19931 and Fewster et al. arXiv:2403.11973) comes closest to the goal. It genuinely exhibits "boundary energy exists" (type II∞) and "no global energy, maximally mixed" (type II₁) as two outcomes of ONE crossed-product construction, with the modular Hamiltonian / Taub charge playing the role of the interpolating object, and it ties this rigorously to the compact/noncompact geometric distinction. This is the strongest realization of the conjecture that exists. Behind it, in second place for *spirit*, sits the corner-symmetry program (a single universal algebra for all corners) and, for the *mechanism* of symmetry-trading, shape dynamics' linking theory.

**The sharpest remaining obstruction.** The unification is *discrete and asymmetric*, not a covariant frame transformation. Linearization-instability theory shows the two cases are genuinely mathematically *different*: the compact case has a rigorous consistency requirement — the Taub constraint — that the noncompact case simply lacks (Minkowski is linearization-stable *because* its Cauchy surface is noncompact). A true "generalized equivalence principle" would require a symmetry transformation continuously connecting the II∞ and II₁ constructions and mapping the ADM/Bondi charge to the observer clock — and De Vuyst et al. essentially prove the two cases stand on *different* footings. **This is the crux: the analogy to GR's equivalence principle may fail precisely because linearization stability breaks the symmetry between the cases.** Whether that asymmetry is fundamental or an artifact of the perturbative (G_N→0) treatment is the central open problem, and settling it is where a serious research contribution lies.

**Staged learning path** (given your operator-algebra/modular-theory maturity):
1. **Covariant phase space + charges** (Wald–Zoupas; Iyer–Wald; Harlow–Wu). Learn how boundary terms produce charges and how flux-balance generalizes conservation. *Benchmark:* derive Bondi mass-loss and see Wald–Zoupas unify it with conservation.
2. **Linearization instabilities** (Fischer–Marsden; Moncrief 1975; Arms–Marsden–Moncrief; then De Vuyst–Eccles–Höhn–Kirklin arXiv:2411.19931 in covariant-phase-space language). *Benchmark:* state the Taub constraint and see why it vanishes identically in the noncompact case.
3. **Gravitational crossed products** (Witten arXiv:2112.12828 → CLPW arXiv:2206.10780 → Fewster et al. arXiv:2403.11973). *Benchmark:* reproduce the II∞ (black hole) and II₁ (de Sitter) results and identify the exact spectral condition that flips the type.
4. **Quantum reference frames / perspective-neutral** (Vanrietvelde–Höhn–Giacomini–Castro-Ruiz *Quantum* 4:225; Höhn–Smith–Lock "trinity" arXiv:1912.00033). *Benchmark:* express the crossed-product observer as a QRF and the ADM boundary as a QRF, and attempt the internal↔asymptotic frame transformation — this is where the open problem lives.
5. **In parallel:** corner symmetry (Ciambelli–Leigh arXiv:2207.06441; Freidel–Geiller–Pranzetti) and the philosophy (Gomes–Roberts–Butterfield; Wallace's isolated-systems papers) to keep the conceptual target — "is a boundary physical or a frame choice?" — sharp.

**Concrete thresholds that would change the assessment.** (a) If someone constructs a *single* algebra with a continuous, intrinsic geometric parameter (not TT̄ of the boundary) whose type continuously reduces II∞→II₁ as a horizon is pushed to infinity or a slice is compactified, the conjecture would be substantially vindicated. (b) If the internal-observer↔asymptotic-boundary QRF transformation is written down explicitly with a group law, "globaled vs. unglobaled" becomes literally a frame change. (c) Conversely, if the linearization-instability asymmetry is shown to survive non-perturbatively, that is a strong *kill condition* on the equivalence-principle analogy.

## Caveats

- **Relabeling vs. deriving.** Several frameworks (corner symmetry as "the fundamental structure"; the II∞/II₁ dichotomy as a universal law) *state* universality but *derive* it only in worked examples or off-shell. Treat "universal" and "fundamental" in these titles as research programs, not theorems, unless a specific theorem is cited.
- **The crossed-product dichotomy is a "suggestion."** The clean "boundary ⟺ II∞, no boundary ⟺ II₁" statement is, in the authors' own word (Kudler-Flam–Leutheusser–Satishchandran), a *suggestion* extrapolated from cases; the proven content is narrower (a structure theorem giving type II, plus case-by-case spectral analysis).
- **Perturbative limitation.** Almost all the algebraic results (CLPW, JSS, Kudler-Flam et al., De Vuyst et al.) are semiclassical / G_N→0. Whether the type classification and the compact/noncompact asymmetry survive at higher order or non-perturbatively is largely OPEN; Kudler-Flam–Leutheusser–Satishchandran expect the FRW algebra to remain II∞ but lose its center at higher order.
- **Closed-universe triviality.** A separate line (Harlow–Usatyuk–Zhao; Abdalla et al.; Chen–Penington; Maldacena) finds that the gravitational path integral suggests a *closed* universe has a one-dimensional Hilbert space unless observers are added by special rules — a live controversy bearing directly on whether "no global energy" in the compact case is a feature or a pathology.
- **Scope mismatch.** LCQFT (§1) rigorously handles matter-field covariance across compact/noncompact spacetimes but deliberately does not address gravitational energy; do not over-read it as solving the gravitational version.
- **Contested programs.** Unimodular gravity's cosmological-constant claims and shape dynamics' quantization are minority positions; cited here for the *mechanism* (symmetry trading), not as established physics.
- **Some journal/volume identifiers** (page numbers, exact years for a few older papers such as the Moncrief sequel and Arms–Marsden) are given from secondary citations; verify against the primary source before formal citation.
