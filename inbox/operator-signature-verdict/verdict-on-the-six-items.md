# Verdict on the Six Items

**TL;DR.** Agree on substance: the list is a faithful compression of [[wall-construction-interface/core-spectral-wall|the core spectral wall's]] own eight-item ledger, correctly ordered from carrier to interpretation. Four amendments change how it should be worked, and a re-partition into four layers changes what "done" means for each item. Under that re-partition, two items are theorems-to-prove, one is a gauge theorem plus an *empirical* slot that should never be closed by derivation, and one contains the layer no construction will ever close — which is not a defect.

## Amendment 1 — Item 2 conflates three types of choice **[TYPE AUDIT]**

$\varphi$ selects the *pointing* of the spectral filtration: the core note itself records that rescaling a weight translates $X$ by a constant while a genuinely different state moves the cuts by a noncommuting cocycle. That is a **gauge-covariance theorem target**: prove the physical content of $\{e_N\}$ is cocycle-invariant, or characterize the invariance class. $d$ is the *physical member* — the analogue of a Hamiltonian, not of a law (see [[inbox/operator-signature-verdict/the-cosmodynamical-signature|the signature note]]). $\nu$ is a parameter *of* $d$, not a third selection. "A principled selection of $\varphi,d,\nu$" therefore names one theorem and one empirical slot, and demanding a derivation of the empirical slot is a category error of the same shape as demanding that quantum mechanics derive the Coulomb potential.

**Dependency correction.** $d$ should ultimately come from *coupling to item 1's local net* — from matter content — the way potentials come from electromagnetism. Item 2's member half is downstream of item 1, not parallel to it. A concrete candidate already exists in the vault: [[spectral-wall-descent/twist-fixed-point-wall|the twisted fixed-point wall]], where the averaging expectation $E_\rho=(1+\rho)/2$ and the Standard-Model fixed algebra supply exactly the kind of matter-side datum that could select $d$.

## Amendment 2 — Item 3 gates item 1 **[ORDERING]**

Which carrier realizes the physical wall algebra — whole-core response, finite corner, or a controlled relation — determines what $e_N\mapsto\Sigma_N$ is a functor *of*. The carrier firewall in the core note ($\mathcal B_N\subset\mathcal A_N$, $\mathcal B_N\not\subset\mathcal K_N$) is the reason this cannot be deferred: a functor built for the wrong carrier realizes the wrong package. Decide 3 first or build 1 twice.

## Amendment 3 — Two cross-cutting obligations are absent **[COMPLETENESS]**

First, the naturality square of [[wall-construction-interface/scale-character-solder|the scale-character solder]]: item 1 as stated produces $\Sigma_N$, but the realization must commute with the HSMI and $A_2$ registers *simultaneously* — the solder note itself insists that matching three formulas is not the theorem. Second, [[wall-construction-interface/elimination-test|the elimination tests]] are not a seventh item; they are a standing discipline every item must re-pass, and the region trap named there (the apparent-horizon cut is located by the solved background) applies with full force to item 1.

## Amendment 4 — Item 6 bundles two different fates **[SPLIT]**

Cosmic-scale and entropy realization are correspondence-layer work: constructible, falsifiable, owed. Records and Lorentzian history are the interpretation layer — [[program-core/record-scale-soldering|record–scale soldering]] and [[algebra/local-global-individuation|the record functor]] — and a century of quantum mechanics says that layer survives *any* posited law. Bundling them lets the hardest philosophical item hide inside an engineering list.

## The re-partition

| Layer | Items | What "done" means |
|---|---|---|
| Signature (posit) | none of the six — see the signature note | adopted, rigid, published with its failure contract |
| Correspondence | 1, 3, the solder square, elimination tests | constructed functors and theorems; the posit touches nature here or not at all |
| Member | 2 split: gauge theorem (phi) + empirical selection (d, nu) | the theorem proved; the member measured, forever case-by-case |
| Theorems in the posit | 4, 5 | proved inside the signature, like unitarity inside Schrodinger's |
| Interpretation | 6's record half | owned by the philosophy spine; open under every posit; progress measured differently |

Nothing in the six is wrong. What the list lacks is the declaration of *which layer each item lives in* — and that declaration is what decides whether "call it a day" is legitimate at each line.
