# The Cosmodynamical Signature

The proposal under review: at some point, posit a new kind of operator for this branch of mathematics and let "solving" be case-by-case, as Schrödinger did — with the suspicion that the cosmos's operator signature depends on state or frame. My verdict: the proposal is methodologically sound, the mathematics of state-dependent operators already exists and the programme is standing on it, the frame question has an exact resolution, and after [[wall-construction-interface/core-spectral-wall|the core spectral wall]] the posit can be written in three axioms. What follows writes it.

## The state-dependence hunch has a precise landing **[STANDARD]**

"An operator whose signature depends on state or frame" is not a mathematics waiting to be invented. Tomita–Takesaki theory *is* state-dependent operator theory: the pair (algebra, state) generates its own flow $\Delta_\varphi^{it}$; the Connes cocycle is the frame-change law between the flows of different states; and the Falcone–Takesaki core ([[library/noncommutative-flow-of-weights/entry|owner]]) is precisely the construction that extracts the *weight-independent* shadow of all that state-dependence. So the resolution of "depends on state or frame — or perhaps not" is: **both, exactly. The signature is frame-independent; its presentation is state-dependent.** The core is canonical; the character $e^s$ is absolute; $\tau(e_N)=e^N$ is forced. What a state contributes is the pointing — where $N=0$ sits, which cocycle dresses the cuts. Invariant law, relative coordinates: the Copernican structure of [[inbox/radical-copernicanism/README|the copernicanism folder]], now in operator form.

## The posit, in three axioms **[PROPOSED CONSTITUTIVE LAW]**

> **(S1) Locality.** Local observables form a net of type-$\mathrm{III}_1$ von Neumann algebras (imported, standard AQFT).
> **(S2) Scale.** Physical scale is the trace-scaling flow of the canonical core, $\tau\circ\beta_s=e^s\tau$; observation-at-scale is spectral cutting, $\beta_s(e_N)=e_{N+s}$, $\tau(e_N)=e^N$.
> **(S3) State.** The cosmos is one faithful normal state $\Omega_d$ on the core.

Signature relations: the trace-scaling covariance, the cut covariance, the forced unit slope, and the KMS period $2\pi$. Everything else — which $d$, hence which response profile, which $\nu$ — is member data, selected by coupling to matter and by measurement, case-by-case, exactly as Hamiltonians are.

## What the posit already *does* **[THE SPOTLIGHT]**

The most important sentence in the new material is the core note's theorem: **the scale-covariant whole is a weight, while every normal $L^1$ state points.** Normalizability forces symmetry breaking — being a state at all costs scale invariance, by a three-line layer-cake argument. Inside the posited signature this upgrades the [[sufficient-reason/entry|two-species distinction]] to a theorem with dynamical content: the cosmos does not point because something selected a direction; it points because existence-as-a-state and scale-symmetry are incompatible. Typed carefully: the theorem forces *a* pointing, not an outcome, not a record — actuality remains open, as the interpretation layer of [[inbox/operator-signature-verdict/verdict-on-the-six-items|the verdict]] says it must. But as an answer-shape for the README question "why is there a fact to observe at all," this is the closest the programme has come: within the signature, *that* the cosmos points is necessary; *where* it points is the member; *what obtains* is still the open third layer.

## What the posit buys, item by item

Items 4 and 5 of the referee's list become theorems-inside-the-posit — the analogues of proving unitarity and Ehrenfest's theorem from Schrödinger's signature. Items 1 and 3 become the correspondence conditions — the analogue of the Hamilton–Jacobi limit — and they are where the posit touches nature or fails to: without $e_N\mapsto\Sigma_N$ the signature is a beautiful spectral theory with no hydrogen atom. Item 2 splits as the verdict says. Item 6's record half is untouched, by design and by precedent.
