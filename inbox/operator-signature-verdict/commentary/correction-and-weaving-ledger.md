# Correction and Weaving Ledger

The operator-signature packet should be preserved as strong referee advice and woven selectively rather than promoted wholesale. Its four-layer explanatory partition and normal-state insight are durable; its claims of a new operator, completed frame independence, automatic source and gravity theorems, permanently empirical member data, and permanently unconstructible factive history require correction.

## Durable contributions

The following claims are worth canonical adoption at their stated grades:

- **[PROGRAMME ARCHITECTURE]** Separate constitutive signature, cosmic member, observational realization, and factive history.
- **[EXACT, DECLARED CORE]** No nonzero normal state is invariant under the full trace-scaling action.
- **[PROPOSED INTERPRETATION]** Normal-state non-invariance is a mathematical model of unavoidable situated presentation.
- **[OPEN THEOREM]** Prove cocycle naturality of the physical filtration across faithful weights and local regions.
- **[METHODOLOGICAL RULE]** A posit must remain rigid, pass correspondence, return something independent, and expose failure conditions.
- **[TYPE FIREWALL]** Nilpotent or unipotent monodromy in one representation must not be identified with a unitary Hilbert-space implementer or positive generator in another.
- **[BENCHMARK]** A causal commutator front and noncompact state correlations illustrate that locality belongs to the observable net while correlations belong to the state, without proving the project's vertical--horizontal identification.

## Corrections to the source packet

**The cosmodynamical-signature note.** Replace "new kind of operator" by **core-scale constitutive signature** unless a single operator with a declared domain, codomain, composition law, and equivalence notion is introduced. Replace "the cosmos is one faithful normal state" by "a candidate cosmic member contains a compatible faithful normal core-state family." Downgrade "the frame question has an exact resolution" to an exact algebraic template plus an open cocycle-naturality theorem.

**The verdict on six items.** Preserve the layer partition but rename *interpretation* as **factive history**. Records and Lorentzian history are legitimate construction targets. Split the final metaphysical question "why this fact?" from the mathematical questions of instruments, persistence, gluing, and orientation.

Do not classify items 4 and 5 as theorems of S1--S3. The proposed signature contains none of the quotient, scalar/TT source, area, edge-state, same-tangent, canonical-energy, or gravitational data required to prove them.

**The Schrödinger-discipline note.** Correct the four-paper chronology, distinguish a Hamiltonian from a state density, and treat the logistic pulse as retrodiction. Preserve rigidity, correspondence, the independent-return requirement, and the failure contract.

**The sweep findings.** Preserve the nilpotent/unitary register firewall. Keep the codimension-two convergence with [[inbox/supplying-complex-numbers/the-necessity-of-i|The Necessity of i]] as a conditional comparison rather than "one fact." Keep [[spectral-wall-descent/twist-fixed-point-wall|The Twisted Fixed-Point Wall]] as a matter-side selector candidate: its fixed algebra and expectation do not yet construct the scale-indexed density $d$.

## Adoption gates

Before the signature is canonicalized, the programme should require:

1. a formal definition of the signature's objects, morphisms, equivalences, and admissible members;
2. a net-indexed core construction compatible with restriction, locality, and covariance;
3. a faithful-weight groupoid and cocycle-naturality statement for pointed filtrations;
4. an explicit two-carrier wall object relating capacity and response without identification;
5. a declared provenance and pre-observation freeze for each selected $d$ and $\nu$;
6. separate correspondence maps to causal cuts, Lorentzian geometry, sources, area, and gravity; and
7. a factive-history programme for instruments, contextual outcomes, persistent records, and compatible orientation.

These gates do not demand that the signature derive every Standard Model parameter or every factual outcome. They ensure that each claimed explanatory layer supplies its own mathematics.

The packet's lasting conclusion should therefore be:

> A rigid law may be posited before every member and fact is derived. What may not be posited away is the distinction between law, member, observational realization, and actuality.
