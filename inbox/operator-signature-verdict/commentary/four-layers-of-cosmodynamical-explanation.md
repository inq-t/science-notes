# Four Layers of Cosmodynamical Explanation

Cosmodynamics should separate the universal law of admissible cosmic presentations, the particular member realized, the map by which that member appears as local observable physics, and the factive process by which possibilities become records. Most apparent demands to "derive everything" conflate these four layers; most premature declarations of completion allow one layer to answer for another.

## The four questions

Let $\mathfrak S$ denote a proposed cosmodynamical signature and $\operatorname{Mod}(\mathfrak S)$ its class of members. The explanatory structure has the schematic form

$$
\mathfrak S
\rightsquigarrow
m\in\operatorname{Mod}(\mathfrak S)
\xrightarrow{\ \mathfrak R\ }
P_m
\xrightarrow{\ \mathfrak F\ }
H_m.
$$

Here $m$ is a cosmic member, $P_m$ is an observational presentation, and $H_m$ is a fact-bearing history. The hooked first arrow is deliberately not written as a function: a signature may admit many members without canonically selecting one.

| Layer | Governing question | Candidate data | What closure requires |
|---|---|---|---|
| Constitutive signature | Why this universal form of possible cosmos? | object types, covariance, scale character, composition laws | a rigid axiom schema, naturality, failure conditions, and possibly a later necessity theorem |
| Cosmic member | Why this state, matter content, coupling, branch, or boundary datum? | $d$, $\nu$, matter representation, initial or boundary data | independent specification, measurement, or an acknowledged form of contingency |
| Observational realization | Why does this member present itself as local $3+1$ physics? | local nets, causal cuts, Lorentzian geometry, stress response, readout | constructed functors or correspondences preserving tested local physics |
| Factive history | Why is there this contextual fact and one compatible history? | instruments, characters, records, comparison maps, historical order | a factive process and persistent record structure, not necessarily a deterministic selector |

The final layer should not be called merely *interpretation*. [[cosmodynamics/fact-record-history|Fact, Record, and History]] makes stable records and common-history gluing part of the mathematical object sought by cosmodynamics. Only the further metaphysical question of why this particular fact obtains may remain underdetermined by the preceding law.

## Contingency is not arbitrary fitting

A member can be contingent without being freely adjustable after an observation is known. If $d$ or $\nu$ is member data, the programme may learn it from an independently declared matter coupling, preparation, or measurement. It may not choose a new $d$ for each expansion history and then cite the recovered history as evidence for the signature.

The distinction is

$$
\text{not necessitated by the signature}
\ne
\text{available for retrospective tuning}.
$$

This is the member-level form of the independence test in [[wall-construction-interface/elimination-test]]. A contingent parameter is fixed for a test once its provenance is declared.

## Sufficient reason without Laplacian closure

[[sufficient-reason/two-species-of-reason|Two Species of Reason]] distinguishes a reason that fixes a probability law from one that fixes the value realized. This permits a layered principle of sufficient reason:

1. the signature grounds the types and invariant relations that make a cosmos possible;
2. a member grounds one lawful probability structure or physical presentation;
3. a factive event supplies actuality and a record without having to be the value of a hidden deterministic selector.

The programme may eventually argue that the signature is necessary while its members are genuinely contingent. It may also discover global compatibility conditions that restrict or select members. The four-layer distinction does not decide that metaphysical question in advance; it prevents the question from being mis-typed.

## Two kinds of closure

A theory layer may be **internally closed** when its axioms, objects, maps, and theorems are sufficiently definite to publish and investigate. It is **explanatorily closed** only when the programme has also answered why those axioms, rather than alternatives, are privileged.

Positing a core-scale signature can achieve the first without achieving the second. For a programme governed by [[program-core/grounding-reasons|Grounding Reasons]], this is a legitimate research stopping point, not permission to declare the signature its own ultimate sufficient reason.

The explanatory discipline is therefore:

> Close each layer when its own obligations are met. Do not delay a precise constitutive theory until every member is derived, and do not promote a constitutive posit into a fact, geometry, or observation without the maps belonging to those later layers.
