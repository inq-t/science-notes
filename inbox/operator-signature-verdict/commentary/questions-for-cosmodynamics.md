# Questions for Cosmodynamics

Cosmodynamics is suited to questions about the constitution and compatibility of presentations, not to recalculating every contingent quantity already described inside a local physical fiber. Its distinctive explanatory task is to show how scale-relative states, local quantum laws, contextual facts, persistent records, and Lorentzian histories can be presentations of one cosmos without making any one local frame the ontology of the whole.

## The characteristic question

The programme can be summarized by one demand:

> What mathematical whole makes every normalized presentation situated, leaves no presentation globally privileged, and nevertheless permits contextual facts to compose into one irreversible common history?

This is prior in explanatory order to an equation of motion inside spacetime. It is not temporally earlier, because time is one of the structures to be realized.

## Questions the theory may dissolve

Several familiar questions presuppose the local presentation they are trying to explain.

**What evolves before spacetime time exists?** A pre-spatiotemporal object need not evolve in a hidden copy of proper time. The upstream relation may be modular, categorical, spectral, or factive, with Lorentzian duration appearing only after realization.

**Where is the wavefunction really located?** A wavefunction is a coordinate representative in a chosen complex Hilbert-space realization. The more invariant state may itself remain fiber-relative. Location is a predicate of a local observable algebra, not necessarily a property of the upstream state.

**What fluid or field is dark energy made of?** If acceleration is the Lorentzian presentation of a global response, the substance question may be mis-typed. This possibility becomes an explanation only after the response-to-stress map is constructed.

**Where did the information go?** Information capacity, BKM response, entropy, accessible distinctions, facts, and records are different objects. A nonfaithful realization can remove recoverable distinctions without violating unitary evolution inside the source fiber. No conservation balance with area or gravity follows until an explicit same-tangent law is supplied.

## Questions not answered by the signature

The current core-scale signature does not explain:

- why space has three real dimensions or why history has Lorentzian signature;
- why the Standard Model algebra, representations, generations, masses, or couplings have their observed values;
- why one particular quantum outcome occurs;
- why one density $d$ or width $\nu$ is the cosmic member;
- why trace capacity is gravitational area;
- why the measured universe accelerates; or
- why records possess the stability and compatibility required for one history.

Some of these may be contingent member questions rather than targets of unique derivation. Others are indispensable realization or factive obligations. ## The intended explanatory achievement

Cosmodynamics need not tell us why Earth has its measured mass or why one electron produced one detector click. Its stronger target is:

> Explain why there can be lawful probabilities, definite contextual facts, mutually comparable records, stable local $3+1$ presentations, and an oriented history at all--and why no local presentation needs to be installed as the absolute frame of the whole.

That is not a smaller ambition than deriving another local field equation. It is a different object of inquiry.
