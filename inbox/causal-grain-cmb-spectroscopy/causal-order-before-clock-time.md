# The Grain of Causal Scale Before Clock Time

The grain of causal scale belongs first to an algebraic order between whole and local registers, not to a clock, length, particle, or already formed spacetime. A reversible ambient group or presentation groupoid can induce a one-sided compression semigroup on a locally accessible algebra; a primitive positive index in that semigroup can carry an additive scale valuation. Only after contextual, Lorentzian, metric, and factual realization does the same unit acquire the local presentations \(\lambda_g\), \(\tau_g=\lambda_g/c\), \(E_g=\hbar/\tau_g\), or \(H\tau_g\). On this reading the gravity wave/particle duality is the global-to-local descent itself: global coherence and causal possibility on one side, a localized pole, fact, and record on the other, with the causal grain naming the oriented passage.

## The meaning of the three words

The phrase **grain of causal scale** has a strict internal grammar.

- **Grain** means a primitive positive process or index after a wall has made an integral unit available. It does not mean a minimal projection of a type-\(\mathrm{III}\) algebra or a smallest spacetime voxel.
- **Causal** means that the process belongs to a presentation-independent positive order: an ambient reversible symmetry preserves the locally accessible carrier only in one direction.
- **Scale** means that the process admits a multiplicative character, equivalently an additive logarithmic valuation. It does not initially mean centimetres or seconds.

The native object is therefore a morphism with a valuation, not a small substance:

$$
\boxed{
\text{causal grain}
=
\text{primitive positive factive process}
\quad\text{carrying one unit of scale valuation}.
}
$$

This places the proposal upstream of the usual choices between corpuscle and field, space and time, or mass and curvature.

## Whole and local do not yet mean spatially large and small

The **whole register** is the carrier on which cosmos-wide compatibility, symmetry, state, or closure is posed. The **local fact register** is the carrier on which a context, obtained value, causal address, and record are represented. Before Lorentzian geometry is realized, these are ontological and categorical roles, not spatial sizes.

This distinction is already owned by [[inbox/radical-copernicanism/commentary-part-2/globality-register|the register of globality]]. A global object need not admit a global valuation or preferred section. Conversely, the absence of a global noncontextual valuation does not imply the absence of a global algebra, state, or cosmos. The whole need not be a God's-eye observer.

The intended grounding order is

$$
\text{whole compatibility}
\longrightarrow
\text{local context}
\longrightarrow
\text{local fact}
\longrightarrow
\text{persistent record}
\longrightarrow
\text{common history}.
$$

This arrow is not a process occurring in an earlier clock time. Time is among its eventual local return values. [[cosmodynamics/cosmos-as-structure-of-facts|A cosmos as the structure of facts]] makes precisely this constitutive distinction.

## The algebraic causal skeleton

Let \(G\) be an ambient reversible symmetry group, or more generally let \(\mathcal C^\simeq\) be the subgroupoid of reversible presentation changes in a process category \(\mathcal C\). Let \(W\) denote one accessible context, wedge, wall, or algebraic region. Define its compression semigroup schematically by

$$
S_W
:=
\{g\in G:gW\preceq W\}.
$$

Its reversible part is

$$
K_W:=S_W\cap S_W^{-1}.
$$

The semigroup determines an order on contexts or their orbits:

$$
g_1W\preceq g_2W
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
g_2^{-1}g_1\in S_W.
$$

When the relevant cone is pointed, the quotient by the reversible stabilizer has no nontrivial two-way cycles. This is causality as a Lie-semigroup or compression order. It requires no metric duration.

The operator-algebraic realization is a half-sided modular inclusion. Under its standard hypotheses,

$$
P\geq0,
\qquad
U(r)=e^{irP},
\qquad
\alpha_r(\mathcal M)\subseteq\mathcal M
\quad(r\geq0),
$$

although \(U(r)\) is a reversible group on the ambient Hilbert space. The same datum is therefore

$$
\boxed{
\text{ambient reversible group}
\quad\longleftrightarrow\quad
\text{local one-sided semigroup}.
}
$$

This is the repository's sharpest form of the global/local division: **spectral positivity seen from a corner**. [[wall-construction-interface/half-sided-modular-tunnel|The half-sided modular tunnel]] owns the theorem-level construction, while [[sufficient-reason/algebraic-arrow-of-time|the algebraic arrow]] explains why the semigroup is not yet experienced or metric time.

## The scale character

Let

$$
v:S_W\longrightarrow\Gamma_+
$$

take values in a positive additive ordered monoid and satisfy

$$
v(st)=v(s)+v(t),
\qquad
v(k)=0\quad(k\in K_W).
$$

The exponential

$$
\chi_{\mathrm{sc}}(s):=e^{v(s)}
$$

is the corresponding multiplicative scale character:

$$
\chi_{\mathrm{sc}}(st)
=
\chi_{\mathrm{sc}}(s)\chi_{\mathrm{sc}}(t).
$$

This is scale before a ruler. It is a comparison law on causal processes.

If the positive image is discrete,

$$
v(S_W)=s_g\mathbb N_0,
$$

then

$$
s_g=\min\{v(s):v(s)>0\}
$$

is a candidate scale grain. Equivalently, one may normalize a primitive positive order class \(q_{\mathrm{ord}}\in S_W/K_W\) by

$$
\nu(q_{\mathrm{ord}})=+1,
\qquad
v(q_{\mathrm{ord}})=s_g,
$$

where \(\nu\) is an integer valuation to be constructed from the wall index. The whole semigroup need not be commutative or generated by \(q_{\mathrm{ord}}\); only its declared scale valuation has a primitive positive image.

The discreteness clause is essential. The unbroken half-sided modular translation generator obeys dilation covariance and has spectrum

$$
\sigma(P)=\{0\}
\quad\text{or}\quad
\sigma(P)=[0,\infty).
$$

It has no isolated positive eigenvalue. [[the-grain-of-causal-scale/causal-spectrum|A quantum of causal realization]] therefore places the finite grain at the wall: a corner, support jump, boundary condition, record, or material correlation compresses the scale-free orbit and returns an integer positive-support change.

## A grain is an act of individuation

The carrier-changing realization is a second object,

$$
q_{\mathrm{wall}}:
X_{\mathrm{whole}}
\dashrightarrow
X_{\mathrm{local}},
$$

with four linked but non-identical properties:

1. \(q_{\mathrm{wall}}\) is outside the reversible presentation subgroupoid;
2. \(q_{\mathrm{wall}}\) lies in the positive causal process class;
3. \(q_{\mathrm{wall}}\) carries a positive-support or spectral-flow index \(+1\);
4. its local realization properly extends a compatible record.

The identification

$$
q_{\mathrm{ord}}
\longmapsto
q_{\mathrm{wall}}
$$

is part of the proposed causal-grain weld, not a consequence of notation. If constructed, it makes the wall event an atom of **causal individuation**: one distinction becomes causally situated, metrically presentable, contextually definite, and available to later records. The grain is not the global possibility and not the resulting local object. It is the irreducible oriented realization relating them.

That grammatical point is philosophically decisive:

$$
\text{potential whole}
\xrightarrow{\ q_{\mathrm{wall}}\ }
\text{actual local fact}.
$$

The source is not temporally earlier than the target. “Earlier” and “later” become physical only when the order is represented in a record-bearing Lorentzian history.

## The welded triple: direction, realization, scale

The deepest candidate is not one object forced to play three incompatible roles. It is a welded triple:

$$
\boxed{
\mathfrak g_{\mathrm{causal}}
=
\bigl(
\text{primitive cone ray},
\text{primitive positive-support transition},
\text{scale character}
\bigr).
}
$$

1. A primitive extreme ray of a positive cone supplies a **causal direction**.
2. A boundary index or spectral-flow event \(\nu=+1\) supplies one **causal realization**.
3. An additive valuation \(v\), or multiplicative character \(e^v\), supplies **causal scale**.

The weld among the three is a theorem target. None follows merely from sharing the word *positive*.

The finite Jordan carrier makes the first component unusually concrete. The Koecher--Vinberg correspondence identifies Euclidean Jordan algebras with homogeneous self-dual cones, and

$$
\mathfrak h_2(\mathbb C)_+
\cong
\overline{C^+_{1,3}}
$$

as ordered cones. For

$$
X=
\begin{pmatrix}
t+z&w\\
\bar w&t-z
\end{pmatrix}
\succeq0,
$$

the determinant is the Lorentz norm:

$$
\det X=t^2-z^2-|w|^2.
$$

Rank-one positive elements lie on \(\det X=0\), the future null boundary; rank-two positive elements lie at \(\det X>0\), the timelike interior. Primitive Jordan idempotents generate extreme rays of the finite cone and possess a canonical algebraic normalization:

$$
e^2=e,
\qquad
(\lambda e)^2=\lambda e
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\lambda\in\{0,1\}.
$$

Thus the cone direction remains continuous, but the idempotent section of each primitive ray has fixed magnitude and trace \(1\). A global automorphism orbit contains no privileged primitive direction; a local context selects one. This supplies exactly the sort of discrete algebraic support unit absent from continuous positive-character theory. It does not yet set a wavelength or energy coefficient on that ray.

These idempotents are not minimal projections of the upstream type-\(\mathrm{III}\) algebra; they become available only on the descended finite Jordan carrier.

This suggests a genuinely algebraic photon--baryon geometry:

$$
\boxed{
\text{photon propagation}
\leftrightarrow
\text{null boundary of the positive cone},
\qquad
\text{massive baryonic dwell}
\leftrightarrow
\text{timelike interior}.
}
$$

The tightly coupled acoustic cone is then a characteristic cone inside the light cone. The enthalpy ratio \(R=h_b/h_\gamma\) measures how much timelike interior loading narrows null propagation:

$$
C_s(R)\subset C_c,
\qquad
\frac{c_s}{c}
=
\frac{1}{\sqrt{3(1+R)}}.
$$

This is an explanatory candidate from algebraic first principles, not yet a derivation of the fluid equation. Its missing bridge must map the cone's rank/convex data to photon and baryon enthalpies and prove that the resulting characteristic speed is the standard \(c_s\).

## Connes covariance within, realization across

For faithful weights \(\varphi\) and \(\psi\) on one von Neumann algebra,

$$
\sigma_t^\varphi(x)
=
(D\varphi:D\psi)_t\,
\sigma_t^\psi(x)\,
(D\varphi:D\psi)_t^*.
$$

The Connes cocycle compares modular presentations without selecting an absolute weight. The Falcone--Takesaki core packages these presentations into a weight-independent carrier with a trace-scaling dual action. This supplies a theorem-grade covariance **within** one algebraic level.

The carrier-changing arrow to a local fact is different. It may be a proper inclusion, corner, expectation, instrument, correspondence, or record extension. It generally lies in

$$
\mathcal C\setminus\mathcal C^\simeq.
$$

The architecture is thus

$$
\boxed{
\text{groupoid covariance within a register}
\quad+\quad
\text{one-sided realization across registers}.
}
$$

This is more precise than saying that global and local are equivalent frames. They need not be isomorphic. The higher law is the coherence of their comparison, not the erasure of their difference.

## Gravity's global/local wave--particle duality

In this programme the deepest wave/particle distinction is not initially a local superposition of two appearances of one small object.

| Whole or wave register | Local or particle register |
|---|---|
| state, correlation law, amplitude, or section over contexts | obtained contextual value |
| reversible presentation symmetry | one-sided accessible semigroup |
| nonlocal or globally constrained coherence | localized pole, support, or excitation |
| possibility without one global valuation | one here-and-now record |
| nonassociative or higher carrier may be admissible | associative complex operator presentation is required |
| causal compatibility without calibrated duration | Lorentzian metric, clock, ruler, mass, and detector event |

The **grain** is the vertical operation that turns one unit of whole-register causal possibility into a locally stable fact while preserving the invariant causal accounting.

This can be stated without pretending that an ordinary position-space wavefunction is fundamental. Let \(\mathfrak A_{\mathrm{whole}}\) carry a compatible state \(\omega\), let \(\mathcal A(W)\) be the algebra available in context \(W\), and write a local instrument in the Heisenberg direction as normal completely positive operations.

$$
\mathcal I_x^W:
\mathcal A(W)
\longrightarrow
\mathfrak A_{\mathrm{whole}}.
$$

Then the local outcome weight and conditioned state take the familiar schematic form

$$
p(x\mid W)
=
\omega\!\left(\mathcal I_x^W(1_W)\right),
\qquad
\omega_{x,W}(A)
=
\frac{\omega\!\left(\mathcal I_x^W(A)\right)}
{\omega\!\left(\mathcal I_x^W(1_W)\right)},
\qquad
A\in\mathcal A(W).
$$

The **wave** side is the whole compatible state and its correlations over possible contexts. The **particle** side is the selected local sector together with an obtained value and a record address. The instrument is a mathematical local presentation of the vertical passage; it is not yet a derivation of that passage. In particular, Born weighting may be granted as part of the descended local quantum interface without being mistaken for the algebraic origin of causal order.

What is ordinarily compressed into the word *collapse* therefore separates into three operations:

1. **contextual restriction**, which selects an associative accessible algebra;
2. **factive selection**, which returns one value or sector rather than the whole possibility family; and
3. **record stabilization**, which makes the selected fact available to a later common history.

Only the third supplies a physical “now” in the historical sense. None is an evolution in a pre-existing universal clock. The proposed causal grain is the primitive positive unit common to this vertical composite, not the numerical probability of an outcome and not the outcome object itself.

The gravity claim can now be stated conditionally:

> The wave aspect of gravity is the global relational organization of causal compatibility; the particle aspect is a localized, associative, fact-bearing pole or boundary excitation. A causal grain is one primitive unit of the descent between them.

This does not identify the BKM form, information loss, curvature, and a graviton. A gravitational realization must still map the common process to a covariant metric response, recover the massless spin-two sector where appropriate, and explain any massive or finite pole separately. The point is an ontology and a construction order, not a completed propagator.

## Octonions and the associative local context

The exceptional carrier supplies an unusually literal version of the same pattern:

$$
\text{globally nonassociative possibility}
\longrightarrow
\text{locally associative complex presentation}.
$$

An octonion-organized Jordan or Freudenthal carrier can preserve global exceptional structure without being an octonion-valued Hilbert space. Choosing a rank-two corner and an associative complex slice gives

$$
\mathfrak h_3(\mathbb O)
\longrightarrow
\mathfrak h_2(\mathbb O)
\longrightarrow
\mathfrak h_2(\mathbb C_u)
\cong
\mathbb R^{1,3}.
$$

This returns a local Lorentz cone after the choices. It does not yet return a fact. The complete descent has separate stages:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{whole exceptional carrier}
&\longrightarrow
\text{associative context}\\
&\longrightarrow
\text{Lorentzian tangent carrier}\\
&\longrightarrow
\text{metric scale and local dynamics}\\
&\longrightarrow
\text{obtained value and stable record}.
\end{aligned}
$$

The octonionic associator can be read as an obstruction to gluing all local complex presentations into one global associative chart. That makes it relevant to the whole/local problem without pretending that nonassociativity itself performs quantum collapse.

There are then two distinct \(3+1\) constructions in the repository.

1. The Hermitian complex corner \(\mathfrak h_2(\mathbb C_u)\cong\mathbb R^{1,3}\) supplies a Lorentz quadratic vector space in one algebraic object.
2. [[algebra/real-forms-and-factive-spacetime|Real forms and factive spacetime]] proposes that a three-dimensional real form is an **object**, while the temporal “one” is the oriented **history arrow** by which factive spatial stages compose.

The second is closer to the present philosophical claim:

$$
\boxed{
\text{three-space is locally presented as an object},
\qquad
\text{spacetime is globally composed as a causal process}.
}
$$

Metric proper time is still downstream. The temporal dimension here is first the order and composition of histories, not a fourth internal coordinate or a universal clock parameter. The two constructions may eventually meet through a Lorentzian-history realization functor, but dimensional agreement does not identify them.

The full atemporal-to-historical ladder is therefore

$$
\text{atemporal complex presentation}
\longrightarrow
\text{positive state geometry}
\longrightarrow
\text{selected real form}
\longrightarrow
\text{factive records}
\longrightarrow
\text{Lorentzian history}.

## Clock time is a local representation

Under the standard reconstruction hypotheses, causal order determines only a conformal class:

$$
\text{causal order}\Longrightarrow[g],
$$

not a calibrated metric. A positive scale section \(\sigma\) then selects

$$
g_{\mathrm{phys}}=\sigma^{-2}\boldsymbol g.
$$

A timelike worldline \(\gamma\) and this metric define proper duration. The constant \(c\) calibrates the temporal and spatial components of the local causal metric; it does not create the prior order. Symbolically,

$$
\boxed{
\text{causal order}
\longrightarrow
[g]
\xrightarrow{\ \sigma\ }
g_{\mathrm{phys}}
\xrightarrow{\ \gamma,c\ }
\tau[\gamma].
}
$$

Only now may a local metric image of the grain be written as

$$
\tau_g=\frac{\lambda_g}{c},
\qquad
E_g=\frac{\hbar}{\tau_g},
\qquad
\delta N_g=H\tau_g
=\frac{\lambda_g}{R_A}.
$$

These identities are useful and exact in their realized domain. They are **clock, energy, and horizon readings of the grain**, not its definition. Proper time is relative to a worldline and local frame; the causal order is the prior relation that makes such local clocks mutually situatable.

## The CMB as a whole-to-local instrument

The CMB is unusually well suited to this ontology. It is not a view of the cosmos from outside. It is one observer's here-and-now record on a celestial two-sphere, produced by radiation arriving along the observer's past light cone. Yet the correlations in that record retain information about one coherent primordial state and one shared hot history.

The observational chain is

$$
\text{whole-state covariance}
\longrightarrow
\text{primordial modes}
\longrightarrow
\text{photon--baryon transfer}
\longrightarrow
\text{past-light-cone sky}
\longrightarrow
\text{local detector records}.
$$

The sky coefficients \(a_{\ell m}^X\) are local-record variables. Their covariance,

$$
\left\langle
a_{\ell m}^X
a_{\ell'm'}^{Y*}
\right\rangle
=
\delta_{\ell\ell'}\delta_{mm'}C_\ell^{XY}
$$

under statistical isotropy, is a local statistical presentation of global coherence. The spectra

$$
C_\ell^{XY}
=
4\pi
\int\mathrm d\ln k\,
\mathcal P_\zeta(k)
\Delta_\ell^X(k)
\Delta_\ell^Y(k)
$$

separate the whole-state covariance \(\mathcal P_\zeta\) from the local Lorentzian and plasma transfer \(\Delta_\ell^X\).

This separation gives the causal grain two distinct empirical jobs.

### Primary job: derive coherent descent

The strong programme should derive why one whole-state phase descends consistently through many local modes and readout channels. Its targets are:

- the passive coherent initial condition rather than an actively re-randomized source;
- one relative phase across TT, TE, and EE;
- statistically compatible local frames across the sky;
- the same causal orientation in temperature, polarization, lensing, and matter records; and
- one common scale character behind the background history and primordial covariance.

The acoustic peaks are then local resonances of the photon--baryon medium, while their remarkable phase coherence is evidence about the global-to-local boundary condition.

### Secondary job: leave a discrete-scale fossil

If wall realization makes the positive scale valuation discrete, a residual can survive as periodicity in the logarithmic scale coordinate:

$$
v\sim v+s_g
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\delta\ln\mathcal P_\zeta(k)
\sim
A_g\cos\!\left(
\frac{2\pi}{s_g}\ln\frac{k}{k_g}+\phi_g
\right).
$$

The period, phase, and symmetry selection rule must be shared by TT, TE, EE, lensing, and matter transfer. Independent phases per observable would abandon the whole-register origin.

## What the ordinary acoustic phase does and does not show

Writing the acoustic phase in grain units gives

$$
\theta_k
=
\int
\frac{k\lambda_g}{a}
\frac{R_A}{\lambda_g}
\frac{c_s}{c}
\,\mathrm dN.
$$

The grain cancels:

$$
\theta_k
=
\int
\frac{k c_s}{aH}
\,\mathrm dN
=
\int k c_s\,\mathrm d\eta.
$$

That cancellation is conceptually correct. A unit of comparison must disappear from a dimensionless phase. It also proves that the factorization alone predicts no new peak position.

The genuine explanatory burden is upstream. The causal-order model must derive something that does not cancel:

- the global coherent state and its phase;
- the baryon/photon dwell allocation \(R\);
- the opacity and visibility history;
- the background \(H(N)\);
- the scalar or tensor source covariance; or
- a residual fixed by the primitive scale valuation.

Calling \(H\tau_g\) the grain itself would reverse this order and confuse a local clock reading with the prior causal operation.

## Economical empirical candidates

The most valuable candidates are linked predictions rather than isolated numerology.

1. **One phase, three spectra.** TT, TE, and EE must descend from one complex associative phase with no spectrum-specific phase parameters.
2. **Whole-state isotropy.** Any off-diagonal covariance must follow a frozen representation or gluing defect, not an arbitrary sky template.
3. **Count-cumulant hierarchy.** If local facts are finite grain realizations of one whole state, the same effective count should control scalar amplitude, tilt, running, and higher cumulants.
4. **Parity and context holonomy.** A nontrivial obstruction to gluing local complex contexts may produce correlated TB, EB, birefringence, or higher-point phase closure. The representation must fix which of these is allowed.
5. **Discrete scale.** A primitive positive scale valuation predicts one log-periodic phase across every descended observable.
6. **Scalar/tensor covariance.** Scalar and tensor realization maps remain separately constructed, but any shared whole-state symmetry should impose a testable relation without identifying their carriers.
7. **Common-history consistency.** Background distances, lensing, late ISW, acoustic phase, and primordial statistics must be returned by one oriented realization rather than separately tuned local models.

## Failure conditions

This interpretation fails if

- no pointed or presentation-invariant causal process order exists;
- the proposed scale valuation changes under reversible presentation changes;
- the positive image is continuous but a primitive grain is asserted without a wall or index;
- the same object is called a global state, local fact, and record without the intervening operations;
- the local Lorentzian realization does not recover causal cones, proper-time relativity, and tested QFT/GR behavior;
- the supposed particle pole has no associative operator, domain, residue, or observable coupling;
- the CMB construction merely rewrites the standard acoustic phase in grain units and derives none of its surviving inputs; or
- each empirical channel requires an independently retuned descent law.
