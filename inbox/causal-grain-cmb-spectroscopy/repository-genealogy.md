# Repository Genealogy of the Causal-Scale Grain

The repository already contains nearly every typed ingredient needed for the present synthesis, but they are owned by different modules and have different proof status. The controlling inheritance is now: **the grain belongs to the global-to-local causal arrow, not to the local clock readings at either endpoint**. Algebraic order and a primitive positive index come first; a scale character grades that process; conformal, metric, clock, energy, mass, entropy, and CMB presentations arise only after explicit realization maps.

## The genealogy of the present synthesis

### Algebra before clock

The deepest repository line begins with reversible equivalence and one-sided accessibility, not with an external time coordinate:

$$
\text{presentation groupoid}
\supset
\text{local compression semigroup}.
$$

The Connes cocycle and canonical core organize covariance among weights and presentations on one algebraic level. A half-sided modular inclusion then shows how a reversible ambient group can preserve a local algebra on only one half-line. This gives an oriented order without identifying its parameter with proper time.

### Order before measure

Causal reconstruction supplies the next separation:

$$
\text{causal order}
\longrightarrow
[g]
\xrightarrow{\ \sigma\ }
g_{\mathrm{phys}}.
$$

The first arrow recovers conformal causal geometry under its hypotheses; the positive section \(\sigma\) supplies metric calibration. The constant \(c\) then converts temporal and spatial readings in a local Lorentzian frame. It is a yardstick internal to the realized causal geometry, not the ground of causal precedence.

### Index before length

The continuous modular generator has no isolated positive gap. The finite grain therefore cannot be read off from continuous modular flow alone. The repository’s exact discrete prototype is instead a wall-induced support change

$$
\Delta\operatorname{rank}=+1.
$$

The new synthesis treats this index as the candidate unit of causal realization and asks a scale valuation to assign it one primitive amount \(s_g\). The metric common-count construction may then return \(\lambda_g\), but it does not retroactively turn the prior process into a small spatial cube.

### Scale character before logarithmic clocks

The logarithm is shared because it turns multiplicative comparison into additive composition:

$$
v(st)=v(s)+v(t),
\qquad
\ln\frac{a_3}{a_1}
=
\ln\frac{a_3}{a_2}
+
\ln\frac{a_2}{a_1}.
$$

This common algebraic grammar explains why scale characters, Misner time, e-folds, trace scaling, resolution depth, and nats repeatedly appear together. It does not make them identical observables. The bridge

$$
\mathrm dN=H\,\mathrm dt
$$

is a local cosmological realization: \(H\) has type \(T^{-1}\), so it converts local duration into logarithmic scale increment.

### Whole wave before local particle

The current philosophical reading makes gravity’s deepest wave/particle duality a vertical distinction:

$$
\text{global coherence and causal possibility}
\xrightarrow{\ q_{\mathrm{wall}}\ }
\text{local associative pole, fact, and record}.
$$

The octonionic or exceptional carrier is a candidate whole-register organization; a chosen associative complex corner is a candidate local Lorentzian presentation. Nonassociativity may obstruct one global associative chart, but it does not itself select an outcome. The factive, metric, and dynamical legs remain separate construction obligations.

### Global CMB coherence before local acoustic readout

The ordinary CMB peak comb is produced by local photon--baryon transfer. Its phase can be written in grain units,

$$

=
\int
\frac{k\lambda_g}{a}

but \(\lambda_g\) cancels. That exact cancellation moves the explanatory target upstream: the causal-grain theory must derive the coherent whole-state boundary condition, the photon--baryon dwell allocation, the common TT/TE/EE phase, the background history, or a non-cancelling residual. It cannot count a change of units as a peak prediction.
