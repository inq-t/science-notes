## The Planck analogy, corrected

Max Planck inferred a quantum law from the thermal blackbody continuum, not from atomic emission lines. The Planck satellite measured CMB anisotropy power as a function of angular multipole \(\ell\), not photon intensity as a function of microwave frequency. The analogy survives at the level of method:

> Treat the standard acoustic plasma as a known response function, then infer whether a smaller universal unit is needed to explain a repeatable residual pattern.

That is exactly how this programme should “read” the CMB. It should not rename the standard acoustic ruler as the causal grain. Planck’s TT, TE, and EE data and the independent ACT DR6 spectra are well fit by ordinary flat \(\Lambda\)CDM, and Planck’s dedicated primordial-feature searches found no significant feature. The null residual baseline is therefore \(A_g=0\), while the stronger grain explanation may still operate through the ordinary background, composition, opacity, and coherent phase rather than through an extra wiggle.
