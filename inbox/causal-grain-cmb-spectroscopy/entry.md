A selected associative \(3+1\)-dimensional complex context then presents temperature and polarization as complementary quadratures of one coherent state. A log-periodic primordial residual is a sharper secondary prediction if discrete scale survives coarse graining.

## Two resonators, two kinds of harmonic spacing

In the tight-coupling approximation, the acoustic phase at last scattering is

$$
\theta_\gamma(k)=k r_s(\eta_*),
$$

so neighboring ideal acoustic extrema obey

$$
\Delta k\simeq\frac{\pi}{r_s}.
$$

This is an approximately **linear** comb in wavenumber. Projection gives the familiar near-regular spacing in \(\ell\), with baryon loading, gravitational driving, neutrino phase shifts, finite recombination width, and lensing producing real departures from an integer series. [[inbox/causal-grain-cmb-spectroscopy/cmb-acoustic-resonator|The acoustic-resonator note]] owns this established physics.

A unit of scale has a different natural coordinate. If

$$
\sigma:=\ln\frac{k}{k_0},
$$

then equal causal-scale intervals have

$$
\sigma_{n+1}-\sigma_n=\delta\sigma_g,
\qquad
\frac{k_{n+1}}{k_n}=e^{\delta\sigma_g}.
$$

Their spacing is **geometric** in \(k\), or periodic in \(\ln k\). This does not compete with the acoustic comb. The linear comb describes collective standing waves in the emergent photon--baryon geometry; the logarithmic comb would be a residual memory of the underlying scale unit. A sharp event at one conformal time could instead make oscillations linear in \(k\), but that is a different causal mechanism and must be selected before a fit.

## The acoustic dwell reading

For an ideal oscillator,

$$
Q_k(\eta_*)\sim\cos(k r_s),
\qquad
P_k(\eta_*)\sim\sin(k r_s).
$$

The plasma moves slowly near compression and rarefaction turning points and fastest near equilibrium. In that restricted classical sense, the temperature peaks select modes caught near displacement dwell maxima, while E-polarization preferentially reads the velocity quadrature and therefore lies approximately between them. TE records their relative orientation and alternates in sign.

This is a useful interpretation, not a replacement for the Einstein--Boltzmann calculation. The CMB is a snapshot across a continuum of \(k\)-modes, not a time histogram of one oscillator. Nor is this classical turning-point dwell automatically the Born dwell in [[inbox/the-box-spectrum-functor/the-box-spectrum-functor|the cosmic box model]], where under a declared dust clock and WKB ordering

$$
|\psi(a)|^2\,\mathrm da\propto\mathrm d\eta.
$$

The two dwell notions can be connected only through a state, clock, hot-history matching law, and curvature-mode solder. The resemblance identifies the right missing map; it does not supply it.

## The Planck analogy, corrected

Max Planck inferred a quantum law from the thermal blackbody continuum, not from atomic emission lines. The Planck satellite measured CMB anisotropy power as a function of angular multipole \(\ell\), not photon intensity as a function of microwave frequency. The analogy survives at the level of method:

> Treat the standard acoustic plasma as a known response function, then infer whether a smaller universal unit is needed to explain a repeatable residual pattern.

That is exactly how this programme should “read” the CMB. It should not rename the standard acoustic ruler as the causal grain. Planck’s TT, TE, and EE data and the independent ACT DR6 spectra are well fit by ordinary flat \(\Lambda\)CDM, and Planck’s dedicated primordial-feature searches found no significant feature. The null residual baseline is therefore \(A_g=0\), while the stronger grain explanation may still operate through the ordinary background, composition, opacity, and coherent phase rather than through an extra wiggle.

## Anti-circularity

The canonical \(46.27\,\mathrm{MeV}\) grain value uses a crossing rate obtained on a CMB-conditioned homogeneous branch. That value cannot be fed back into the same CMB spectra and advertised as an independent explanation of them. A noncircular test needs one of the following:

1. derive \(\lambda_*\), \(\delta\sigma_g\), the branch weights, and the phase without using the tested CMB modes;
2. use the separately frozen material/chiral oracle and predict a CMB feature before looking at it; or
3. fit one declared subset and predict a genuinely held-out TT, TE, EE, lensing, or higher-point observable.

This is the same discipline already imposed by [[minimal-cosmodynamic-closure/prediction-contract|the minimal-closure prediction contract]].
