# Causal-Individuation Geometry

Causal-individuation geometry is the proposed positive geometry of physical scale--state differences after observationally empty, vertical modular, central-normalization, and gauge directions have been removed. It is a unifying construction target for the homogeneous capacity of Causal Scale Theory, the inhomogeneous precision of Causal-Wall Spectral Theory, and the state-side modulus sought in the derivation of $G$; no common global object of this kind has yet been constructed.

## Meaning

A difference becomes physically individuated only relative to a structure in which it can be distinguished, causally placed, metrically compared, and made available as a record. The relevant primitive is therefore not an absolute number of bits. It is the geometry of admissible differences in a fact-bearing causal presentation.

The proposed order is

$$
\text{presentations}
\longrightarrow
\text{physical quotient or groupoid}
\longrightarrow
\text{distinguishability geometry}
\longrightarrow
\text{facts and records}.
$$

The first two arrows concern equivalence and comparison. The metric concerns quadratic response. The last arrow requires a fact-forming instrument and record dynamics and does not follow from the metric alone.

## Presentation data and quotient

Let $\mathfrak P$ schematically contain tuples

$$
p=(\Sigma,\sigma,\mathcal A,\omega,\mathcal T),
$$

where $\Sigma$ is a causal cut, $\sigma$ is scale data, $\mathcal A$ is an observable algebra, $\omega$ is a state, and $\mathcal T$ transports data between cuts or fibers.

For a declared family of accessible observables $\mathcal O$, define observational equivalence by

$$
p\sim_{\mathcal O}q
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
F(p)=F(q)
\text{ for every }F\in\mathcal O.
$$

This produces a set quotient only after $\mathcal O$ is specified. If stabilizers, local observer changes, or comparison arrows matter, a groupoid or stack may be required instead. [[conservation-of-causal-charge/indiscernibility-and-the-noether-gap|Indiscernibility and the Noether Gap]] owns the strict quotient statement.

Further reductions may be necessary before a nondegenerate metric exists. Candidate null or redundant directions include

- central shifts that do not change normalized states;
- vertical modular-frame transformations;
- internal reparameterizations of the state coordinate;
- the homogeneous constant mode when defining the inhomogeneous residue; and
- gauge directions in the eventual spacetime descent.

These reductions are not interchangeable and need not be generated by one group.

## Pullback state geometry

Suppose an independently constructed causal-scale law gives

$$
\Phi:\mathfrak P_{\mathrm{phys}}\longrightarrow\mathfrak S_{\mathrm{adm}},
$$

where $\mathfrak S_{\mathrm{adm}}$ is a suitable space or bundle of faithful states with controlled cross-fiber transport. When the BKM form is finite or consistently renormalized, define

$$
\boxed{
\mathcal G_{\mathrm{CI}}
:=\Phi^*G_{\mathrm{BKM}}.}
$$

For a physical tangent $v$,

$$
\mathcal G_{\mathrm{CI}}(v,v)\ge0.
$$

This is a susceptibility or squared speed. It is not a linear charge, an actual outcome, an entropy, or an action.

The construction is explanatory only if $\Phi$ and the state family are obtained without reconstructing them from the cosmological response or spectrum they are intended to explain. [[causal-wall-spectral-theory/causal-scale-interface|The causal-scale interface]] states the common-algebra and state-selection obligations.

## Homogeneous and inhomogeneous sectors

Write the infinitesimal scale presentation as

$$
-\delta\ln\sigma(x)=\delta N+\zeta(x),
$$

with the constant mode removed from $\zeta$. At a homogeneous reference the tangent space is schematically

$$
T\mathfrak P_{\mathrm{phys}}
\simeq
\mathbb R\,\partial_N
\oplus
C^\infty(\Sigma)/\mathbb R.
$$

The proposed block form is

$$
\mathcal G_{\mathrm{CI}}
\sim
\begin{pmatrix}
G^\perp_{NN} & G_{N\zeta}\\
G_{\zeta N} & \mathcal K_\zeta
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

The entries have different present standings:

- $G^\perp_{NN}$ is modeled by the homogeneous binary response after several identifications and normalizations;
- $\mathcal K_\zeta$ is the target cosmological precision kernel, with its BKM-to-spectral identification still open;
- $G_{N\zeta}$ is generally unconstructed and need not vanish away from a symmetric homogeneous reference.

The three-dimensional spectral representation and the codimension-two causal cut have different carriers. Their quadratic densities consequently have different length dimensions. A common metric must construct the maps between them rather than identify their coefficients by name.

## Localization on causal cuts

For measurable patches $U\subseteq\Sigma$, the homogeneous norm should define a positive countably additive measure

$$
\mu^\perp_{\mathrm{BKM}}(U)
:=G^\perp_{NN,\mathrm{ren}}[U].
$$

Absolute continuity with respect to independently normalized area would give

$$
\frac{\mathrm d\mu^\perp_{\mathrm{BKM}}}
{\mathrm d\mu_A}
=\chi_{\downarrow}.
$$

Constancy of this density throughout an Einstein universality class is stronger than extensivity on one cosmological horizon. [[deriving-value-of-g/causal-scale-derivation-target|The Causal-Scale Derivation Target]] owns the regulator, locality, additivity, and universality tests.

## Relation to facthood

The metric measures how distinguishability changes under a tangent deformation. It does not select an actual fact. A state restricted to a commutative context yields a probability measure, while a fact requires a selected character or outcome. A record further requires dynamical stability and inter-observer compatibility.

Causal individuation therefore has at least three layers:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{kinematic individuation}&:\ \text{physical distinctions survive the quotient},\\
\text{metric individuation}&:\ \mathcal G_{\mathrm{CI}}\text{ measures their response},\\
\text{factive individuation}&:\ \text{one distinction becomes a stable record}.
\end{aligned}
$$

Only the first two are partially represented by the present state geometry. [[conservation-of-causal-charge/factive-descent-and-records|Factive Descent and Records]] states the additional conjecture.

## Claim boundary

This note proposes an architecture. It does not establish that

- one quotient or groupoid captures every redundancy;
- a global smooth state manifold exists for the relevant type-III algebras;
- the BKM form is finite after continuum renormalization;
- the homogeneous and spectral kernels are blocks of one Hessian;
- the localized measure is proportional to area; or
- preserving this geometry yields a causal Noether charge.

Each failure would refine or restrict the concept rather than retroactively invalidate the exact finite-dimensional Hessian and quotient results from which it was abstracted.
