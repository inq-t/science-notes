## Least action or conservation?

The theory is currently much closer to a prospective **conservation-through-symmetry principle** than to a principle of least action.

There is presently no functional whose stationary points generate the wall construction, scale transport, factual descent, and gravitational response. A relative-entropy Hessian is not an action, and a BKM squared norm is not automatically something nature extremizes.

But neither is the full conservation law established. The exact seed,

\[ 1=\eta^2+G^{\mathrm{BKM}}_{\theta\theta}, \]

is a quadratic Casimir decomposition arising from \(Q^2=\mathbf1\). It is genuine mathematics, but it is not a Noether charge. The reflection symmetry is discrete, while an ordinary Noether current requires a continuous action and invariant dynamics. \[ \text{Casimir allocation} \longrightarrow \text{common causal boost/dilation symmetry} \longrightarrow \text{diagonal moment-map balance} \longrightarrow \text{state--geometry--record conservation}. \]

Only the first item is presently exact in the proposed binary sector. The remaining arrows form a serious theorem programme, not yet a discovered law.

## The deeper fundamental concept

I think the concept worth preserving is **causal individuation**, not a scalar substance called causal charge or determination.

Its meaning is relational:

> the capacity for a distinction to become causally placed, metrically comparable, accessible as a fact, and persistent as a record within one history.

That genuinely belongs at the cosmodynamic level. It is broader than information, entropy, energy, or metric geometry, and it explains why those registers might require common compatibility laws. The corresponding candidate principle would be:

> A fact-bearing cosmos preserves its invariant structure of causal individuation while redistributing its representations among resolved order, residual susceptibility, geometry, and records.

That is a respectable fundamental hypothesis. It does not yet imply that there is literally a conserved fluid or scalar stock called “causality.”

## Are we hallucinating?

Not in the mathematical core. The Casimir identity, binary conversion law, BKM geometry, and FLRW allocation are real results under their stated hypotheses.

The hallucination risk begins when their formal resemblance is treated as evidence that they arise from one physical carrier. In particular:
