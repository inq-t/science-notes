# Casimir, Capacity, Charge, and Action

The programme contains a genuine binary Casimir identity, a proposed positive causal capacity, a conjectural conserved causal charge, and no completed least-action principle. These objects occupy different mathematical orders. Their resemblance suggests a research architecture, but none should be named as another without the maps that relate representation theory, information geometry, symplectic dynamics, and gravitational response.

## Four mathematical orders

| Object | Order and type | Present example |
|---|---|---|
| Casimir | invariant polynomial of a representation | $Q^2=\mathbf1$ and $1=\eta^2+\operatorname{Var}(Q)$ |
| Capacity | positive quadratic form or susceptibility | $G^{\mathrm{BKM}}(v,v)$ |
| Charge | linear moment-map, Noether, or boundary value | prospective $Q_\xi=\langle\mu,\xi\rangle$ |
| Action | functional whose stationary variations give dynamics | no common causal-scale action has been constructed |

The difference between linear and quadratic objects is load-bearing. A signed charge can reverse under an orientation change; a squared norm cannot.

## Exact binary seed

For a state $\omega$ and a self-adjoint involution $Q^2=\mathbf1$,

$$
1=\omega(Q)^2+\operatorname{Var}_\omega(Q).
$$

In the balanced exponential family,

$$
\eta=\tanh\theta,
\qquad
G^{\mathrm{BKM}}_{\theta\theta}=\operatorname{sech}^2\theta,
\qquad
1=\eta^2+G^{\mathrm{BKM}}_{\theta\theta}.
$$

This is a fixed representation norm allocated between resolved polarization and residual susceptibility. It is exact after the binary reduction and balanced-reference premise. It is not a spacetime continuity equation and does not identify either term with a fact or with gravity. [[conservation-of-causal-charge/binary-casimir-balance|The Binary Casimir Balance]] owns the theorem.

The integrated transition identity

$$
\frac12\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}
\frac{\mathrm d\eta}{\mathrm dN}\,\mathrm dN=1
$$

is an endpoint normalization when the path reaches both saturated sectors. It is not a Noether charge derived from an action.

## The Noether gap

An observational quotient or a discrete reflection supplies no ordinary infinitesimal current. A genuine conservation law additionally requires

- a continuous physical symmetry group;
- a phase space, presymplectic form, or variational structure;
- a normalized common generator;
- an invariant Hamiltonian or action;
- well-defined moment maps or covariant charges; and
- boundary and flux terms.

If state, gravitational, and record sectors are Hamiltonian representations of the same group, a diagonal action could yield

$$
\mu_{\mathrm{causal}}
=\mu_{\mathrm{state}}
+\mu_{\mathrm{grav}}
+\mu_{\mathrm{record}}
$$

in one common dual Lie algebra, together with

$$
Q_\xi[\Sigma_2]-Q_\xi[\Sigma_1]
+\Phi_\xi[W]=0.
$$

This is the central conjecture of [[conservation-of-causal-charge/causal-individuation-balance|Causal-Individuation Balance]]. The common generator, action, moment maps, gauge reduction, edge terms, and record sector remain open.

## Capacity is not charge

For a physical horizontal tangent $v_N$,

$$
G^\perp_{NN}=g^{\mathrm{BKM}}(v_N,v_N)
$$

is second order. A proposed same-tangent theorem may relate it to gravitational canonical energy,

$$
G^{\mathrm{BKM}}(v_N,v_N)
\stackrel{?}{=}
Z_g\,\mathcal E^{(1)}_{\mathrm{can}}(h[v_N],h[v_N]),
$$

while covariant phase space relates canonical energy to variations of a Noether charge. This is a plausible bridge among the correct mathematical types:

$$
\text{BKM Hessian}
\longleftrightarrow
\text{canonical energy}
\longleftrightarrow
\text{Noether structure}.
$$

Controlled examples motivate the architecture, but the causal wall has not supplied the tangent map or normalization.

## Why this is not yet least action

The relative-entropy Hessian gives a local quadratic response. It does not imply that nature minimizes relative entropy, BKM length, or causal capacity. A least-action formulation would need a functional

$$
\mathcal S_{\mathrm{CI}}[\text{state},\text{geometry},\text{records}]
$$

whose admissible variations yield the wall state law, gravitational response, constraints, and factive descent. No such functional is presently defined.

Einstein--Hilbert gravity can be rewritten using the areal modulus,

$$
\frac{S_{\mathrm{EH}}}{\hbar}
=\frac{\eta_{\mathrm E}}{4\pi}
\int R\,\mathrm dV_g,
$$

but this is an exact reparameterization after Einstein gravity is assumed. It does not make the wall BKM metric an action or derive $\eta_{\mathrm E}$.

$$
\text{representation Casimir}
\longrightarrow
