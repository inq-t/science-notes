# Units and the Planck-Like Rebasing

The proposed Ruble structure introduces no demonstrated new base dimension. Its natural quantitative object is a dimensionless distinguishability quadratic form; localization on different geometric carriers produces inverse-area or inverse-volume densities, and the Einstein state--geometry weld re-expresses $G$ through an inverse-area modulus. This is a change of dimensional basis and interpretation, not yet a calculation of a new constant.

## Capacity-generated Planck units

For any positive inverse-area modulus $\chi$, define the conventionally normalized characteristic length

$$
\ell_\chi:=\frac1{2\sqrt\chi}.
$$

Then define

$$
t_\chi:=\frac{\ell_\chi}{c},
\qquad
m_\chi:=\frac{\hbar}{c\ell_\chi},
\qquad
E_\chi:=\frac{\hbar c}{\ell_\chi},
\qquad
T_\chi:=\frac{\hbar c}{k_B\ell_\chi}.

$$

E_\chi=E_P,

## The dimensional obstruction

The unit-width law, unit Ruble law, binary Casimir, and spectral coefficient are dimensionless. No combination of dimensionless mathematics with $c$, $\hbar$, and $k_B$ produces an area:

$$
G=\frac{c^3}{\hbar}\times(\text{area})
$$

up to a dimensionless coefficient. A first-principles determination of $G$ therefore requires at least one of

- an independently normalized physical area spectrum;
- a finite density of causal channels per area;
- a derived length relative to another independently fixed physical scale; or
- an areal soldering modulus admitted as primitive data.
