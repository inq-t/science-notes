# Theorem and Failure Ledger for the Ruble Programme

The Ruble programme becomes physical only if independently constructed state, area, symmetry, and record structures produce one universal causal-capacity modulus and a noncircular state--geometry matching law. This ledger separates the shortest decisive construction path from outcomes that would modify or falsify the proposed synthesis.

## T1 — construct the physical presentation object

Specify causal cuts, scale sections, observable algebras, states, accessible observables, and admissible presentation arrows. Decide whether the physical carrier is a quotient, groupoid, stack, or another local-to-global object.

**Success:** comparison, stabilizers, and descent are explicit, and operationally indiscernible presentations have one physical image.

**Failure:** the proposed quotient removes observable differences, depends on undeclared observables, or cannot compare states on different cuts.

## T2 — construct horizontal state transport

Construct

$$
N\longmapsto
(\Sigma_N,\mathcal A_N,\omega_N,\mathcal T_{N_2N_1})
$$

independently of the background response and primordial spectrum it is intended to explain.

**Success:** transported neighboring states admit a finite or consistently renormalized relative-entropy Hessian.

**Failure:** the state path is reconstructed from $H(N)$ or $P_\zeta(k)$, or the comparison depends on arbitrary fiber identifications.

## T3 — define the causal-individuation metric

Remove vertical modular, central, coordinate, and gauge directions and construct

$$
\mathcal G_{\mathrm{CI}}=\Phi^*G_{\mathrm{BKM}}
$$

on the physical quotient.

**Success:** the form is positive on physical directions, covariant under presentation changes, and its domain and null space are controlled.

**Failure:** every proposed horizontal tangent is gauge, the form is divergent without a physical renormalization, or distinct comparison choices produce inequivalent answers.

## T4 — localize the homogeneous capacity

For cut patches $U\subseteq\Sigma$, prove that

$$
\mu^\perp_{\mathrm{BKM}}(U)
=G^\perp_{NN,\mathrm{ren}}[U]
$$

is positive, countably additive, and absolutely continuous with respect to independently normalized area.

**Success:** throughout a declared Einstein class,

$$
\frac{\mathrm d\mu^\perp_{\mathrm{BKM}}}
{\mathrm d\mu_A}
=\chi_*>0
$$

with controlled corrections.

**Failure:** the norm is nonlocal, nonextensive, scheme dependent, or lacks a finite area density.

## T5 — derive the state--geometry weld

Show independently that the same coefficient controls horizon entropy variation and local focusing:

$$
\delta\!\left(\frac{S_{\mathrm{hor}}}{k_B}\right)
=\chi_*\,\delta A.
$$

**Success:**

$$
\chi_*=\eta_{\mathrm E},
\qquad
\mathfrak R_\Sigma=1,
\qquad
G_{\mathrm{pred}}=\frac{c^3}{4\hbar\chi_*}
$$

without importing measured $G$, the Bekenstein--Hawking normalization, or a fitted cosmology.

**Failure:** the compared entropy and BKM norm belong to different states, tangents, cuts, or renormalization prescriptions.

## T6 — unify homogeneous and spectral response

Construct the inhomogeneous block of the same state metric and prove or refute

$$
\operatorname{Hess}\mathscr J_{\mathrm{wall,spec}}
\stackrel{?}{=}
\mathcal K_\zeta
$$

after the BKM, Euclidean, wavefunctional, probability, and spacetime-descent maps are supplied.

**Success:** $G_{NN}$, $\mathcal K_\zeta$, and any mixed blocks arise from one positive physical state geometry, while local GR/QFT are preserved.

**Failure:** the three-dimensional spectral wall and codimension-two causal cut have no common carrier, or the continued BKM kernel is not the cosmological precision.

## T7 — construct the causal symmetry and charge

Identify one normalized continuous generator acting on the state, gravitational, and record sectors. Construct its moment maps, gauge reduction, boundary terms, and flux law.

**Success:** a Ward identity or covariant phase-space theorem yields

$$
Q_\xi[\Sigma_2]-Q_\xi[\Sigma_1]+\Phi_\xi[W]=0.
$$

**Failure:** the sectors use unrelated generators, arbitrary relative normalizations, anomalous nonconservation, or gauge-dependent charges.

## T8 — construct factive descent

Supply the outcome slot, context rule, instrument, conditional state, stable record algebra, and compatibility of observer histories.

**Success:** factual pointing and record formation have a physical role in the charge and descent structure rather than being readings of expectation values.

**Failure:** restriction is mistaken for outcome selection, records are unstable, or local histories cannot glue.

## Interpretation of nonunit or variable results

| Result | Physical interpretation |
|---|---|
| no finite $\chi_{\downarrow}$ | the BKM route does not define a gravitational modulus |
| finite $\chi_{\downarrow}$ but $\mathfrak R_c\ne1$ | unit Ruble matching is false or incomplete |
| $\chi_{\downarrow}=\chi_{\downarrow}(x)$ | varying-$G$ or scalar--tensor response rather than Einstein gravity |
| curvature-dependent modulus | higher-curvature constitutive response |
| state- or species-dependent modulus | universality or equivalence-principle violation |
| nonlocal cut dependence | nonlocal gravitational response |
| cosmological agreement but local or wave disagreement | no universal gravitational coefficient has been derived |
| correct unit ratio but undetermined $\chi_*$ | symmetry fixes matching but not the dimensional magnitude of $G$ |

These are empirical alternatives, not merely failures of notation.

## The shortest decisive calculation

The most informative next result is not another rearrangement of a Planck or horizon identity and not a background fit of $\mathfrak R_c$. It is the independent construction of

$$
\boxed{
\chi_{\downarrow}
=\frac1{A_\Sigma}
\Phi^*G^\perp_{\mathrm{BKM}}(\partial_N,\partial_N)
}
$$

with

- a physical scale-indexed state;
- a canonical horizontal tangent;
- regulator-independent normalization;
- independently calibrated area;
- locality and additivity; and
- no use of the target $G$ or fitted cosmology.

Only after that calculation can $\mathfrak R$ distinguish a new physical equivalence from a chosen normalization.
