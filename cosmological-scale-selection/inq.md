# Cosmological Scale Selection

A whole-state law might select the dimensional scale realized by a local theory without making cosmological expansion responsible for the existence of its mass gap. This module separates that proposed selection from three related questions: what horizon saturation actually fixes, how a selected scale remains constant downstream, and whether a carrier-changing wall leaves an observable fossil. The horizon ledgers are exact under their stated hypotheses, and the fossil proposal applies the shared transport-intertwining theorem; a common parent for the cosmological and local spectral returns remains unconstructed.

## A whole-scale benchmark is not a local spectral floor

[[cosmological-scale-selection/horizon-saturation-and-entropic-distinction|Horizon saturation]] fixes the comparison class before speaking of a maximum: Schwarzschild compactness and area entropy refer to a fixed boundary radius and a specified semiclassical theory. They do not give a universal maximum mass or prove a positive lower energy cost for every local excitation. The proposed exterior-equivalence-class description likewise does not prove that a classical interior is absent; it must recover the complete exterior observable dynamics.

[[cosmological-scale-selection/cosmic-geon-hypothesis-and-horizon-rate-ledger|The cosmic-geon ledger]] supplies a different, cosmological benchmark. In flat Einstein--FLRW, the apparent-horizon energy, canonical temperature and area capacity satisfy

\[
E_A=\Theta_A\iota_A=\frac{c^5}{2GH},
\qquad
\frac{P_A}{E_A}=\frac{d}{dt}\log\iota_A=2H(1+q).
\]

Here \(\iota_A=S_A/k_B\), \(\Theta_A=\hbar H/(2\pi)\), \(q=-1-\dot H/H^2\), and the signed supply \(P_A\) uses the declared horizon orientation. Radiation redshift in a fixed comoving cell is another ledger, not leakage into an exterior of the cosmos. Neither identity alone supplies entropy production, a persistent record, or a Wheeler geon. [[conformal-scale-geometry/inq|Conformal scale geometry]] owns the underlying metric and horizon conversions.

## Select the member without inserting its gap

[[cosmological-scale-selection/cosmological-selection-of-the-yang-mills-yardstick|The yardstick proposal]] distinguishes a positive dimensionless Yang--Mills coercivity coefficient from the choice of its dimensional member. With fixed gauge data and scale convention, the former must be proved independently of the cosmological calibration. On the declared common-count branch, the proposed inverse-length energy has the conditional presentation

\[
E_*=
\left(\frac{6\pi^2}{\gamma s_*}\right)^{1/3}
E_{A,c}\,\iota_c^{-2/3}.
\]

Here \(\gamma>0\) is the wall-to-area multiplicity, \(s_*>0\) the information per effective bulk cell, and the subscript \(c\) denotes the selected cut. The coefficient follows from the assumed bulk-cell and boundary-capacity matching, not from dimensional analysis alone. The resulting \(E_*=\hbar c/\lambda_*\) is not yet a particle mass, localization width, or spectral threshold. [[mass-scale-calibration/inq|Mass-scale calibration]] supplies the separate clock, action, carrier and Poincare comparisons; the yardstick note's localized energy implication still requires its positive Hermitian response form, full lower bound and width map.

The selector itself has two jobs. A dilation-invariant condition can select an event in a cosmological history, but not the history's absolute normalization. An independently normalized capacity or equivalent datum must fix the latter without consulting the target mass. Evaluating the common-count formula at a continuously changing \(H\) would give \(d\log E_*/dN=-\epsilon/3\), with \(N=\log(a/a_*)\) and \(\epsilon=-\dot H/H^2\), while the other inputs stay fixed. A stable local scale therefore needs an actual transport law from the selected cut, not repeated recalibration against the live Hubble rate. A pure-Yang--Mills claim must also retain its positive dimensionless floor when gravity is decoupled.

## A fossil must survive the carrier change and the readout

[[cosmological-scale-selection/wall-crossing-defect-and-the-fossil-of-mass-engagement|The wall-engagement application]] imports [[transport-intertwining-defect/propagator-intertwining-and-placement|the transport-intertwining theorem]] for a physically selected realization \(J_\sigma\). A zero defect erases wall placement from the declared terminal-state composite, even when the map is noninvertible. A nonzero defect is presentation covariant but still needs a physical selection principle; [[transport-intertwining-defect/observation-of-a-transport-defect|the observation theorem]] determines whether it survives transfer and nuisance projection. An independently selected post-wall metric can turn the defect into a positive response form, whose physical lower bound and Casimir comparison remain separate requirements.

This separates engagement, gap and fossil into different tests of the same proposed parent. [[causal-grain-cosmology/inq|Causal-grain cosmology]] owns the downstream acoustic and material-history tests. In the declared charge-even survivor-loading branch, the readout loses baryon-sign information and cannot recover an erased annihilation history; a proposed wall signature must survive beyond what the final loading already fixes. Numerical agreement of a length, energy or acoustic scale cannot replace the missing comparison maps.

[[global-local-response-reconstruction/cosmological-reconvergence-contract|The reconvergence contract]] states the common target: construct one upstream law with distinct vacuum and cosmological returns, then prove their compatibility. The vacuum return must carry the required local theory and uniform spectral floor; the cosmological return must select its normalization and make a prospectively testable prediction. This module's ledgers constrain those arrows without identifying their different carriers.
