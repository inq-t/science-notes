# The Schrödinger Stopping Rule

The Schrödinger analogy licenses a precise constitutive posit before every member and interpretation has been derived, provided the posit is rigid, respects established correspondence, and returns an independent result. It does not license arbitrary member fitting, the promotion of absent interface maps into theorems, or the declaration that factive history is permanently outside mathematical construction.

## The historical sequence

The source packet compresses several 1926 papers into one founding act. Schrödinger's [first communication](https://doi.org/10.1002/andp.19263840404) formulated the stationary eigenvalue problem and treated the hydrogen atom. The general time-dependent equation appeared later in the [fourth communication](https://doi.org/10.1002/andp.19263861802).

The corrected history strengthens the methodological analogy. A spectral law and a worked member can arrive before the mature temporal formulation and before the interpretation of the state is settled. A new programme need not solve every downstream question in the same paper that introduces its constitutive signature.

The analogy must remain type-correct. Planck's constant, a Hamiltonian, a state density $d$, a response width $\nu$, the trace-scaling character $e^s$, and a modular $2\pi$ normalization are not parallel mathematical objects. In particular, $d$ is a state density rather than a generator of evolution.

## What the analogy licenses

It licenses the programme to:

- state S1--S3 as a proposed core-scale axiom schema;
- calculate exact consequences within that schema;
- admit that different matter systems or cosmic members may supply different state data;
- publish before deriving every member from a more primitive uniqueness theorem; and
- leave the metaphysics of why one fact obtains distinct from the law governing its probabilities.

This is **internal closure of a theory layer**. It allows work to proceed without an infinite regress of preliminary derivations.

## What the analogy requires

**Rigidity.** Freedom must be localized in declared members. If an arbitrary function, state, carrier, quotient, or normalization is changed for each desired observation, the signature has no invariant empirical content. [[program-core/explanatory-economy|Explanatory Economy]] owns this audit.

**Correspondence.** The signature must be shown to preserve the tested local physics it imports. For cosmodynamics this means a net-compatible core construction, $e_N\mapsto\Sigma_N$, a carrier decision, and conservative local restriction. Without those maps, the posit is an operator-algebraic theory of spectral scale, not yet a theory of the scale measured in nature.

**An independent return value.** The first worked member may reproduce a known result, but the programme eventually needs a relation or number not used to select the member. A retrodiction validates consistency and calculational fertility; it does not establish discovery.

**A failure contract.** A returned nonunit rate, a nonbinary quotient, failure of local conservation, or an unacceptable cosmological likelihood must be allowed to reject the relevant principle or member. A discrepancy cannot always be absorbed into a new state choice.

## The Balmer requirement

The logistic density was selected with the hyperbolic pulse already in view. The resulting $\operatorname{sech}^2$ response is therefore an exact retrodictive member, not yet the analogue of an unanticipated spectral line.

The proposed DESI tangent, one-nat counting pattern, and fossil $\dot G/G$ condition are promising test targets, but they are conditional on additional branch, normalization, and realization assumptions. None is presently a theorem of S1--S3 alone. A genuine Balmer result should be frozen before confronting the observation and should survive the independence and restriction tests in [[wall-construction-interface/elimination-test|The Elimination Tests]].

## What cannot be dismissed by precedent

The historical persistence of the quantum measurement problem is not a theorem that records or actuality are unconstructible. Cosmodynamics may coherently decline to derive which individual outcome occurs. It still owes the mathematical distinction among an instrument, a fact, a persistent record, and a compatible history if it claims to explain what makes a cosmos a cosmos.

The correct stopping rule is therefore:

> Posit the rigid signature when it is exact enough to generate a research class. Treat member data as contingent or empirical when appropriate. Do not stop at a missing correspondence needed for an observational claim, and do not rename an unconstructed factive layer "mere interpretation."

For a sufficient-reason programme, the posit remains a provisional explanatory terminus. It can be scientifically foundational before it is shown to be metaphysically necessary.
