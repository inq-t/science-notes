# Wall-Crossing Defect and the Fossil of Mass Engagement

A proposed mass-engagement wall must do three different jobs: construct a physically selected carrier change, support a post-wall mass gap, and leave an observable image after cosmological transfer. The generic transport defect tests sensitivity to wall placement; it supplies neither a coercive physical energy form nor a surviving fossil by itself. This application states the additional Casimir, metric, material-loading, and acoustic conditions needed to connect those three claims.

## Engagement, gap, and fossil are three proof obligations

A physical translation representation can possess a genuine mass gap even when the wall leaves no measurable cosmological fossil. Conversely, a wall can leave a large transfer signature without constructing a Yang--Mills mass gap. A bare carrier has no mass gap until a Hamiltonian or joint translation representation acts on it.

First comes the **engagement construction**: fix \(X_-\), \(X_+\), \(J_\sigma\), their naturality/locality/gauge conditions, and a defect \(\mathfrak D_\sigma\) that is nonzero on a physically supplied incoming family. The defect of an arbitrary admissible comparison still transforms covariantly; what an arbitrary choice lacks is established physical selection and an identified mechanism. If \(X_+\) is initially a response carrier, a separate realization

$$
\mathfrak R_+:X_+\longrightarrow\mathcal H_{\mathrm{phys}}
\tag{WD10a}
$$

must identify its physical image.

Second comes the **gap theorem**. After constructing a positive-energy Poincare representation on \(\mathcal H_{\mathrm{phys}}\), with strongly commuting translations \((H_+,\mathbf P_+)\), Lorentz-invariant joint spectrum, and unique-vacuum projection \(P_0\), define the nonnegative energy-squared translation Casimir

$$
\mathcal C_+
:=
H_+^2-c^2|\mathbf P_+|^2.
\tag{WD10b}
$$

The domain-safe Casimir floor is

$$
\|\mathcal C_+^{1/2}\psi\|^2
\geq
\Delta_E^2\|\psi\|^2,
\qquad
\psi\in
D(\mathcal C_+^{1/2})
\cap(1-P_0)\mathcal H_{\mathrm{phys}},
\qquad
\Delta_E>0.
\tag{WD11}
$$

[[mass-scale-calibration/joint-causal-generators-and-the-mass-casimir|The joint-Casimir theorem]] owns the equivalence with the Hamiltonian gap under these reconstruction hypotheses. In \(3+1\) dimensions a directional null-pair product includes transverse momentum; its lower bound suffices only when proved on the full vacuum complement and combined with the Lorentz-reconstruction theorem. Neither a single null generator nor an untyped response carrier supplies (WD11).

Third comes the **fossil theorem**: prove that the same wall construction has a nonzero, prospectively declared image under (WD9), or a specified higher-order replacement. A common-origin explanation must show that the response defect and the Casimir floor descend from one carrier construction through \(\mathfrak R_+\). Numerical proximity between their dimensional presentations does not provide that theorem.

## The defect is a residue, but not yet an energy cost

The defect is a rate of the realization map per unit scale order, generally rectangular and non-self-adjoint. [[transport-intertwining-defect/metric-response-of-an-intertwining-defect|The metric-response construction (WD11a)]] requires a selected positive post-wall metric \(G_+\) before it defines a nonnegative pullback form. Its closure, kernel, normalization, and physical lower bound remain additional obligations. A dimensionless positive form becomes a mass statement only through an independent energy scale and a same-carrier Casimir comparison.

[[trace-dirichlet-descent/oriented-descent-angle-and-emergent-symmetry#Exact two-wall angle theorem|The two-wall angle theorem]] supplies a structured metric candidate once its physical descents have been selected. If the post-wall carrier admits two \(\varphi\)-preserving opposed descents with standard-form projections \(e_\pm\) and common-core projection \(P_0\), then

$$
G_+
=G_{\mathrm{pair}}
=2I-e_+-e_-,
\qquad
G_{\mathrm{pair}}
\geq
(1-c_F)(1-P_0).
\tag{WD11b}
$$

Here \(P_0\) denotes the common retained projection on \(X_+\); identifying it with the physical vacuum through \(\mathfrak R_+\) is a separate condition. For the subsequent incoming-norm comparison, require \(X_-\) to be Hilbert and choose a densely defined closed realization \(T=(1-P_0)\mathfrak D_\sigma:D(T)\subset X_-\to X_+\), or a bounded extension, with its domain and source norm fixed. The post-wall inequality alone does not supply those hypotheses. With the canonical two-wall analysis map \(\mathfrak D_{\mathrm{pair}}f=((I-e_+)f,(I-e_-)f)\), the resulting pullback satisfies

$$
\left\|
\mathfrak D_{\mathrm{pair}}(1-P_0)\mathfrak D_\sigma x
\right\|^2
\geq
(1-c_F)
\left\|(1-P_0)\mathfrak D_\sigma x\right\|^2.
\tag{WD11c}
$$

This controls the *post-wall image* when \(c_F<1\), not the incoming norm. Under the declared realization hypotheses, [[transport-intertwining-defect/metric-response-of-an-intertwining-defect|the closed-range criterion]] equates closed range with a positive lower bound on \(D(T)\cap(\ker T)^\perp\); a merely core-defined Banach-space defect is insufficient. Nor does the angle identify the form with energy: [[physical-response-coercivity/retained-output-casimir-bound#The finite-lattice Hamiltonian comparison|finite-regulator comparison]] uses the OS/transfer Hamiltonian, while the Poincare Casimir requires continuum reconstruction.

[[wall-construction-interface/half-sided-modular-tunnel#The differentiable modular wall is defect-flat|The half-sided modular tunnel]] gives an exact negative test. Dropping the endpoint transporter creates the apparent defect \(iPe^{-r_\sigma P}\), but that is a typing mismatch and its squared form has infimum zero. Modular orientation and transport therefore do not select a nonzero wall residue for free.

This prevents an equation of units from becoming an equation of concepts. A factor such as \(\hbar c/\lambda_g\) presents a length as an energy. It does not show that the wall defect acts as a Hamiltonian, that \(\lambda_g\) is a Compton wavelength, or that its reciprocal is a spectral floor.

## A harmonic sweet spot requires a spectral statement

A sudden generator change is not by itself a resonance. For an observed mode \(k\) and frozen response functional \(\ell_{\alpha,k}\), with realification when complex Fourier modes are used, the wall-address derivative has the form

$$
\partial_\sigma O_\alpha(k)
=
\ell_{\alpha,k}\,
U_+(N_f,\sigma)\,
\mathfrak D_\sigma\,
U_-(\sigma,N_i)x_i.
\tag{WD15}
$$

A **harmonic sweet spot** requires more than a preferred dimensional number. The theory must derive at least one of:

- a pole or narrow enhancement in a physical resolvent;
- a discrete eigenvalue or band edge with nonzero overlap in (WD15);
- a phase-coherent characteristic shared across independently transferred observables; or
- a selection rule that suppresses generic modes and leaves a declared harmonic family.

An instantaneous wall generally excites a broad response. The familiar acoustic comb is generated later by photon--baryon oscillation and projection. A mass-engagement wall could constrain its state, loading history, source, or phase law, but it does not turn one early length into the drag ruler by passive stretching. ## Promotion and kill conditions

The operator-switch proposal advances only if it supplies:

1. declared pre- and post-wall carriers and a natural realization map \(J_\sigma\);
2. a nonzero intertwining defect on physically occupied states;
3. an independently normalized post-wall form with a uniform continuum floor, or a transfer contraction with a positive logarithmic rate per fixed physical thickness;
4. a same-carrier solder of that form to the full Poincare Casimir;
5. a predeclared fossil signature with positive post-nuisance response; and
6. a common-origin theorem joining the metric, Casimir, and observable branches.

The route is killed or downgraded if:

- \(\mathfrak D_\sigma=0\), so the wall address is invisible within the declared terminal-state composite;
- the only nonzero-defect witness comes from a comparison \(J_\sigma\) with no independently established physical selection;
- every declared observation kills the defect after nuisance projection;
- the claimed harmonic is obtained only by equating unrelated dimensional readings;
- the post-wall coercive operator acts on a different carrier from the physical Casimir without a pullback theorem; or
- BAO is asked to reconstruct a sign or history already erased by the material-loading map.

For adjacent Euclidean regulator slices, a raw angle floor must vanish at finite physical gap; [[two-slice-innovation-geometry/past-future-angle-and-the-transfer-gap]] identifies the fixed-thickness contraction and logarithmic rate that remain meaningful.
