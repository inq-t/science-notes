# The Closure Stack

The homogeneous response is not the output of one theorem. It is a dependency stack whose exact algebra sits between proposed identifications, two independent unit principles, one constitutive source law, standard horizon relations, and a global sector choice. Keeping the stack visible prevents an attractive final formula from laundering its premises.

## The load-bearing sequence

1. **[STANDARD]** Causal order fixes conformal structure under the usual hypotheses; a scale section fixes a metric representative.
2. **[EXACT REFORMULATION]** The trace-free Einstein equation can be written as a scale-tractor transport equation.
3. **[OPEN — WALL STRUCTURE]** A scale-indexed family $(\mathcal A_N,\omega_N)$ and a valid cross-fiber comparison must exist.
4. **[IDENTIFICATION]** The homogeneous reflection-odd response factors through a balanced binary normal channel generated by $Q$.
5. **[ALGEBRA]** That channel has $\eta=\tanh\theta$ and $G^{\rm BKM}_{\theta\theta}=\operatorname{sech}^2\theta$.
6. **[CONDITIONAL THEOREM]** Ratio dependence, rank-one generation, cocycle composition, negligible holonomy, and measurability imply $\theta=\varrho_\perp x$.
7. **[PRINCIPLE — WIDTH]** The physical width $\nu=|\varrho_\perp|$ is one.
8. **[PRINCIPLE — SCALE–CAPACITY]** The peak ratio $\mathfrak R_c=(k_B/S_c)G^\perp_{NN}(N_c)$ is one.
9. **[CONSTITUTIVE LAW]** $\rho_X=(k_BT_c/2V_c)G^\perp_{NN}$ along the full path, with crossing data held fixed.
10. **[IDENTIFICATION — HORIZONTAL TEMPERATURE] [ALGEBRA]** The horizontal temperature uses the canonical $2\pi$ normalization and the wall uses the flat-FLRW apparent-horizon entropy and volume.
11. **[ASSUMPTION — BACKGROUND]** GR, flat FLRW, the declared matter/radiation content, and separate conservation apply at background level.
12. **[SECTOR]** $\Lambda_{\rm res}=0$ selects the transient-acceleration/coasting branch.

Only after steps 1–10 does the generalized profile follow:

$$
\rho_X(N)=\frac{\mathfrak R_c}{2}\rho_{\rm crit,c}
\operatorname{sech}^2(\nu x).
$$

Separate conservation then supplies $w_X$. It is not part of the information-geometric derivation.

## Two unit laws, not two total assumptions

The proposed-v8 phrasing “two unit laws close the homogeneous theory” is useful only as parameter accounting. It does not mean that the rest of the stack is theorem-free.

The width law

$$
\nu=1
$$

selects the rate of horizontal polarization per Weyl e-fold. It does not fix the extensive BKM norm.

The amplitude law

$$
\mathfrak R_c=1
$$

fixes the entropy-normalized peak norm. It does not create the $\operatorname{sech}^2$ shape, choose the source prescription, or determine the residual vacuum sector.

The actual homogeneous rigidity comes from a larger conjunction:

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\text{binary channel}
+\text{ affine soldering}
+\nu=1\\
&+\mathfrak R_c=1
+\text{ anchored source law}
+\text{ horizon bridge}\\
&+\text{ GR background assumptions}
+\Lambda_{\rm res}=0.
\end{aligned}
$$

This is still economical: there are no free functions and the new dimensionless numbers are fixed. But honest economy counts identifications, constitutive choices, and sectors as well as numerical postulates.

## What is invariant under reparameterizing the internal coordinate

If $\tilde\theta=f(\theta)$, then the metric component and the slope transform oppositely:

$$
G_{NN}=G_{\theta\theta}\left(\frac{\mathrm d\theta}{\mathrm dN}\right)^2
=G_{\tilde\theta\tilde\theta}
\left(\frac{\mathrm d\tilde\theta}{\mathrm dN}\right)^2.
$$

Thus

$$
\mathfrak R_c=\frac{k_B}{S_c}G_{NN}(N_c)
$$

is coordinate invariant. The separate numerical meaning of $\nu$ requires the canonical binary normalization $Q^2=1$ and the exponential coordinate conjugate to $Q$. This is why replacing the earlier capacity symbol by the Ruble number is a genuine improvement: it packages the measurable amplitude without tying it to an arbitrary internal coordinate.

## Minimal ways the stack can fail

- If the wall state has no dominant balanced binary channel, the $\operatorname{sech}^2$ profile need not survive.
- If the cocycle is not rank one or has nontrivial holonomy, a single affine $\theta(N)$ is insufficient.
- If direct modular data give $\nu\ne1$ or $\mathfrak R_c\ne1$, the corresponding unit principle is falsified rather than “renormalized away.”
- If the source is instantaneous rather than anchored at the crossing, the density history changes even with the same BKM metric.
- If no conserved covariant $T^X_{ab}$ realizes the background, the homogeneous fluid is not a physical sector.
- If a positive residual is selected, transient acceleration and the coasting future disappear even if the pulse remains.

The stack is valuable because its failure modes are localized. A failed amplitude principle need not invalidate tractor geometry; a failed perturbation lift need not invalidate the binary algebra; a failed binary wall construction would, however, reduce the cosmology to a phenomenological ansatz with suggestive notation.
