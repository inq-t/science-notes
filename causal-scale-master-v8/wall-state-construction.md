# Constructing the Causal-Wall State

The binary profile becomes physics only if it descends from a scale-indexed family of causal-region states constructed independently of the target cosmology. This note states the minimum operator-algebraic data, distinguishes the full type-III wall theory from its proposed binary channel, and turns the central conjecture into a sequence of calculations that can fail cleanly.

## The object that is presently missing

Choose a family of causal or marginal regions $\mathcal O_N$ associated with a flat-FLRW apparent-horizon cut $\Sigma_N$. A candidate construction needs at least:

$$
N\longmapsto
(\mathcal O_N,\mathcal A_N,\omega_N,J_N^{\rm TT},\Delta_N).
$$

Here $\mathcal A_N$ is a local von Neumann algebra, $\omega_N$ is faithful in the regime where modular theory is used, and $(J_N^{\rm TT},\Delta_N)$ are its Tomita modular conjugation and modular operator in a specified standard form.

Different $N$ generally means different regions and possibly different algebras. A derivative $\partial_N\omega_N$ is meaningless until the fibers are compared. One must provide one of the following, or an equivalent construction:

- embeddings $\iota_{N_2N_1}:\mathcal A_{N_1}\to\mathcal A_{N_2}$ with a composition law;
- a common ambient algebra into which all $\mathcal A_N$ embed;
- a crossed-product or core construction on which the relevant states become comparable;
- a modular Berry connection or parallel transport with controlled gauge and holonomy.

Only then is relative entropy across scale, a horizontal BKM norm, or a Connes cocycle well typed.

## Vertical and horizontal generators

At fixed $N$,

$$
\sigma_s^{\omega_N}(A)
=\Delta_N^{is}A\Delta_N^{-is}
$$

is vertical modular flow. A horizontal tangent compares $\omega_N$ with $\omega_{N+\delta N}$ after transport to a common algebra. Schematically, the logarithmic change of modular data may contain

$$
\delta K
=\delta K_{\rm vertical\ gauge}
+\delta K_{\rm horizontal\ noncentral}
+\delta\alpha\,\mathbf 1.
$$

The central term changes normalization or a scalar lift but has zero BKM length. The vertical term is modular-frame gauge. The programme assigns cosmological response to the noncentral horizontal term. Demonstrating this decomposition for an FLRW wall is part of the theory, not a matter of notation.

## The proposed binary channel

The cut has two null-normal rays. Let $P_\pm$ denote the spectral projections of a proposed orientation observable,

$$
Q=P_+-P_-,
\qquad Q^2=1,
\qquad
J_{\rm refl}QJ_{\rm refl}^{-1}=-Q.
$$

The last equation uses a geometric or internal normal-reflection operator $J_{\rm refl}$ and says that reflection exchanges the two orientations. It does not say that the full wall Hilbert space has dimension two.

This reflection must not be silently identified with $J^{\rm TT}$. Tomita conjugation maps $\mathcal A$ to its commutant $\mathcal A'$. If $Q\in\mathcal A$ is noncentral, the equation $J^{\rm TT}QJ^{\rm TT}=-Q$ with the **same** $Q\in\mathcal A$ is incompatible with factoriality. A doubled realization could instead use

$$
Q_L\in\mathcal A,
\qquad
Q_R\in\mathcal A',
\qquad
J^{\rm TT}Q_LJ^{\rm TT}=-Q_R,
$$

together with an explicit geometric intertwiner relating left and right orientation data. Which realization is intended is part of the missing wall construction.

**[IDENTIFICATION]** There should exist a physically specified channel

$$
\mathcal E_N:\mathcal A_N\longrightarrow\mathcal B_N
$$

whose reflection-odd homogeneous part is generated by $Q$. Depending on the construction, $\mathcal B_N$ could be a subalgebra, quotient, sufficient statistic, conditional expectation image, or effective code subspace. These possibilities are not interchangeable and should not be hidden behind the word “reduction.”

The familiar formulas require a **balanced** reference representation: at $\theta=0$, the two outcomes have equal reference weight. On the genuine two-outcome commutative algebra, write

$$
p_\pm(\theta)=\frac{e^{\pm\theta}}{2\cosh\theta},
\qquad
\Psi(\theta)=\ln(2\cosh\theta),
$$

and $\omega_\theta(Q)=p_+-p_-$. On a larger code subspace with degeneracies, the normalized state must instead be defined relative to a declared balanced reference $\rho_0$:

$$
\rho_\theta
=\frac{e^{\theta Q/2}\rho_0e^{\theta Q/2}}
{\operatorname{tr}(\rho_0e^{\theta Q})},
\qquad
\operatorname{tr}(\rho_0P_+)
=\operatorname{tr}(\rho_0P_-)=\frac12.
$$

For a reflection-invariant $\rho_0$ commuting with $Q$, the denominator is $\cosh\theta$ and the induced outcome law is the binary law above. Then

$$
\eta=\Psi'=\tanh\theta,
\qquad
G^{\rm BKM}_{\theta\theta}=\Psi''
=\operatorname{sech}^2\theta.
$$

The relation $Q^2=1$ alone does not imply equal degeneracy or equal reference weights. An imbalance $p_+\ne p_-$ shifts the intrinsic center and changes the log-partition function. Reflection symmetry must enforce balance, or balance must be admitted as an additional premise.

For the balanced family,

$$
\eta^2+G^{\rm BKM}_{\theta\theta}=1
$$

and the reflected-state comparison is

$$
\mathfrak S_{\rm refl}(\theta)
:=S(\omega_\theta\Vert\omega_{-\theta})
+S(\omega_{-\theta}\Vert\omega_\theta)
=4\theta\tanh\theta.
$$

This has a unique minimum at $\theta=0$. If the full construction does not preserve this balanced reflection, the claimed self-dual crossing must be reformulated.

## What the cocycle must actually show

After transporting to a common algebra, let

$$
u_{21}(s):=(D\omega_{N_2}:D\omega_{N_1})_s
$$

be the relative modular cocycle. The desired rank-one result is that its noncentral binary component takes the form

$$
u_{21}^{\perp}(s)
\sim
\exp\!\left[is\bigl(\theta(N_2)-\theta(N_1)\bigr)Q\right],
$$

up to a controlled central phase and vertical gauge. If it depends only on the oriented inverse-scale ratio, composition gives

$$
f(r_1r_2)=f(r_1)+f(r_2),
\qquad
r=\frac{\sigma_1}{\sigma_2}
=\frac{a_2}{a_1}>0.
$$

Measurability yields

$$
f(r)=\varrho_\perp\ln r,
$$

and therefore, with the chosen orientation,

$$
\theta=\varrho_\perp x.
$$

This is a **conditional theorem**. It derives logarithmic affinity, not the value $|\varrho_\perp|=1$.

Possible failures are informative:

- several noncommuting generators produce a higher-rank path rather than one scalar $\theta$;
- nontrivial holonomy makes the result path dependent;
- a state-dependent generator or non-affine transport makes $\theta(N)$ nonlinear;
- a scale-dependent channel changes the binary normalization;
- failure of geometric modular flow removes the proposed horizon interpretation without invalidating abstract relative modular theory.

## The two calculations the wall should return

Once the family is constructed, compute

$$
\nu=\left|\frac{\mathrm d\theta}{\mathrm dN}\right|
$$

in the canonical $Q^2=1$ coordinate, and

$$
\mathfrak R(N)
=\frac{k_B}{S(N)}G^\perp_{NN}(N).
$$

The principles predict, at the self-dual cut,

$$
\nu=1,
\qquad
\mathfrak R(N_c)=1.
$$

A result different from one is not a small technical discrepancy: it directly falsifies or modifies the corresponding principle. A non-$\operatorname{sech}^2$ BKM profile falsifies the balanced binary reduction. No cosmological fit should be used to force these outputs.

There is a stronger anti-circularity requirement because the apparent-horizon region itself depends on $H(N)$. Evaluating modular data only on the already fitted benchmark history would not derive that history. A genuine construction must provide either

- an off-shell functional $G^\perp_{NN}[g,\omega]$ for arbitrary FLRW histories, followed by a coupled Einstein–wall solution; or
- a state family fixed by independently specified initial and matter data, from which the resulting $H(N)$ is predicted.

Using the target $H(N)$ to define both the wall path and its supposed source would fail this test even if the modular calculation were otherwise exact.

## A controlled construction ladder

The most credible route is incremental. The general observable-map requirements are also developed in [[scale-as-modular-observable/observable-map|the scale observable map]]:

1. reproduce the calculation in a Rindler wedge, where geometric modular flow is known;
2. repeat it for a CFT ball or causal diamond;
3. add controlled gravitational or crossed-product degrees of freedom;
4. treat slowly evolving or perturbative horizons;
5. finally address the dynamical flat-FLRW apparent-horizon cut.

At each rung, define the region, algebra, state, transport, binary channel, and entropy normalization before calculating the two ratios. Agreement at earlier rungs would establish a universality pattern; disagreement would identify exactly which structural ingredient fails.

## Upgrade criteria

The programme may promote the binary wall from **[IDENTIFICATION]** to a derived effective sector only when:

- $Q$ and its reflection are constructed from the wall algebra;
- the balanced channel is canonical or dynamically selected;
- the cross-scale transport is defined and its gauge dependence controlled;
- the cocycle gives $\theta(N)$ inside an off-shell or coupled construction rather than by evaluating only the target $H(z)$;
- the BKM norm is finite or properly renormalized and its normalization is unambiguous;
- the resulting source can be lifted to a covariantly conserved spacetime response.

Until then, the binary theory is a sharply specified ansatz with unusually rich internal geometry—not yet a derivation from QFT in curved spacetime.
