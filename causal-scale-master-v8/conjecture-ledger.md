# Conjectures and Brave Routes

The programme is most interesting where several mathematical structures almost lock together but do not yet do so. Each entry below states the bold idea, why it is tempting, what calculation would upgrade it, and what result would kill it; analogies without such an upgrade path are retained only as rhymes.

## Scale induces horizontal modular transport

> **[CONJECTURE — PROGRAMME]** A change of physical scale induces horizontal transport of a causal-region state through a bundle of algebras and states, and the noncentral BKM squared tangent—or free-energy Hessian—along that path sources gravity.

This is stronger than saying that modular theory can be discussed on each background. It claims that scale itself is encoded by relative state comparison.

**Upgrade:** construct the fibers, transport, connection, and cocycle for a dynamical FLRW wall and recover a state path without fitting $H(z)$.

**Kill:** no canonical comparison exists, the horizontal tangent is pure modular-frame gauge or central, or the result depends arbitrarily on the chosen embeddings.

## Normal-binary universality

> **[CONJECTURE]** Homogeneity and normal reflection make the infrared, reflection-odd wall response a balanced two-outcome sufficient statistic even though the microscopic algebra is type III.

The two null-normal orientations make the binary channel geometrically natural, and the relative-entropy/BKM metric of the balanced commuting binary family yields the rigid $\operatorname{sech}^2$ pulse without selecting a potential by hand.

**Upgrade:** derive a canonical conditional expectation or sufficient channel onto $Q$, show reflection enforces equal reference weights, and bound corrections from discarded modes.

**Kill:** additional reflection-odd modes remain unsuppressed, the channel is unbalanced, or the reduced metric is not binary BKM geometry.

## A single isometry may underlie the two unit laws

> **[CONJECTURE — UNIFICATION]** At the self-dual wall, the pullback BKM metric of the physical scale translation is isometric to the horizon entropy metric,
>
> $$
> \Phi^*G^{\rm BKM}\big|_c
> =\frac{S_c}{k_B}\,\mathrm dN^2.
> $$

This equation directly gives $\mathfrak R_c=1$. In a canonical balanced binary coordinate it may also explain why the fundamental scale character couples with unit slope, but that second conclusion does not follow without fixing the normalization and representation of $Q$.

**Upgrade:** derive the isometry from modular data and show that the scale group acts in the fundamental $Q^2=1$ representation.

**Kill:** either the peak norm or canonical slope differs from one in the explicit wall construction.

## Euclidean periodicity might quantize a character

> **[CONJECTURAL ROUTE]** Euclidean continuation of the normal boost may turn it into a $2\pi$ rotation whose single-valued representations discretize a horizontal character.

This is the serious remnant of the rejected claim that real conformal weights are automatically integral. The obstruction is substantial: angular periodicity naturally constrains weight per vertical rotation, whereas the proposed width is $\mathrm d\theta/\mathrm dN$. Along FLRW evolution,

$$
\frac{\mathrm d\widehat\eta_A}{\mathrm dN}
=\widehat\mu_A(N)
$$

is neither constant nor generally one.

**Upgrade:** construct the Euclidean wall geometry, identify the horizontal character under the angular action, and derive a map from angular integrality to $\mathrm d\theta/\mathrm dN=1$.

**Kill:** the horizontal transport does not extend around the Euclidean circle, or quantization applies only to the vertical generator with no fixed relation to scale.

## Einstein-capacity universality at a dynamical wall

> **[CONJECTURE]** A self-dual marginal FLRW cut belongs to the same capacity universality class in which modular variance equals horizon entropy.

Controlled holographic examples make this plausible, but an apparent-horizon cut in a dynamical cosmology is not automatically one of those examples.

**Upgrade:** compute $G^\perp_{NN}$ and $S$ in a regulated wall algebra and obtain $(k_B/S)G^\perp_{NN}=1$ without imposing it.

**Kill:** the ratio is state dependent, regulator dependent, nonlocal in scale history, or robustly different from one.

## A near-horizon Virasoro algebra could explain capacity

> **[CONJECTURAL ROUTE]** An emergent $1+1$ conformal algebra at the self-dual wall may place the horizontal sector in a Cardy regime and derive the capacity law.

A two-dimensional normal vector space is not a CFT. A serious version requires an actual algebra of modes, Hamiltonian, central charge, boundary conditions, and thermodynamic regime.

**Upgrade:** construct those data and derive both entropy and capacity from the same partition function.

**Kill:** no such algebra exists, the Cardy regime is inapplicable, or it predicts the wrong entropy-normalized capacity.

## Anchored free-energy curvature is a real stress response

> **[CONJECTURE]** The relative-entropy Hessian at the self-dual event defines an anchored susceptibility whose continuation along the state path is realized by a conserved spacetime stress tensor.

This is the indispensable bridge from information geometry to cosmology. Its fixed crossing temperature and volume may be a feature—an event-indexed material law—rather than an approximation, but that must be derived.

**Upgrade:** obtain the constitutive density and pressure from an action, influence functional, Ward identity, or algebraic response theorem and derive $\nabla^aT^X_{ab}=0$.

**Kill:** every covariant completion requires an instantaneous normalization, violates conservation, becomes singular at $w=-1$, or introduces uncontrolled extra functions.

## Self-duality dynamically synchronizes equality

> **[CONJECTURE]** The intrinsic minimum of reflected-state distinguishability is a physical marginal event at which the response capacity saturates and the ordinary and collective sectors divide the critical density equally.

This would turn a numerical coincidence into a state-defined event. The current theory obtains equality from $\mathfrak R_c=1$ plus flatness; it does not yet derive why the actual wall reaches capacity saturation at the self-dual point.

**Upgrade:** show from wall dynamics that the minimum of $\mathfrak S_{\rm refl}$ is also the capacity-saturation event and that the gravitational constraint enforces equal partition.

**Kill:** self-duality, peak capacity, and gravitational equality occur at distinct cuts.

## Local central quotient and global scalar dynamics are complementary

> **[CONJECTURE]** Vacuum energy becomes tractable only after separating local noncentral response from a global scalar calibration sector; neither register can solve the other's problem.

The tractor trace split and modular central blindness point in the same direction. A top-form, flux, unimodular, or sequestering-like completion could provide the missing global equation.

**Upgrade:** write the combined dynamics, include loop corrections, and derive a stable residual sector.

**Kill:** the scalar datum remains arbitrary and radiatively sensitive, or the global mechanism spoils the local wall response.

## The Witten pair has a covariant descendant

> **[CONJECTURAL ROUTE]** The internal Pöschl–Teller pair is the symmetry-reduced normal operator of a covariant causal-response complex, and its one protected zero mode corresponds to one physical collective response.

The equality between the zero-mode density and the BKM metric is too structured to ignore, but spectral beauty is not a spacetime map.

**Upgrade:** derive a hyperbolic, conserved, gauge-consistent Lorentzian system whose reduction gives the pair and whose physical inner product reproduces the internal norm.

**Kill:** no natural lift exists, the putative mode is gauge, or every lift is unstable or acausal.

## QFT may be reconstructed from modular relations—but is not yet

> **[CONJECTURAL ROUTE]** A sufficiently rich net of causal-wall algebras, inclusions, modular conjugations, and superselection sectors may reconstruct spacetime symmetries and gauge sectors, while the scale connection supplies an additional gravitational register.

Algebraic reconstruction and finite-spectral-geometry ideas make this a legitimate research direction. The specific Standard Model gauge group, chiral representations, Yukawas, and anomaly cancellation must emerge from the construction rather than be recognized after the fact. [[symmetry-groups-select/reconstruction-versus-selection|Reconstruction and selection]] records this distinction.

**Upgrade:** prove recovery of local nets and their low-energy correlators, or derive the full Standard Model representation data and renormalized effective action.

**Kill:** the construction supplies only compatible terminology, cannot reproduce chiral matter, or fails locality, unitarity, anomalies, or decoupling.

## Horizon-clock complementarity

> **[CONJECTURE]** The identity
>
> $$
> \mathrm dN=\mathrm d\widehat\eta_A+\frac14\mathrm d\ln\mathcal S_A
> $$
>
> is the geometric shadow of a deeper connection splitting scale transport into reversible modular motion and irreversible capacity growth.

As written, the identity follows from FLRW kinematics and definitions. Its thermodynamic interpretation is additional.

**Upgrade:** derive the split from a modular connection with a positive entropy-production theorem and identify which term controls the horizontal BKM path.

**Kill:** the decomposition is gauge dependent or has no state-theoretic realization beyond algebraic rewriting.

## Rhymes kept at zero evidential weight

**[RHYME — causal-set residual]** A volume-suppressed fluctuating cosmological residual may land near the same numerical scale as a horizon-sized response. One predicts a stochastic residual; the zero-residual unit branch predicts deterministic coasting. Same magnitude is not same mechanism, and their late-time modalities differ.

**[RHYME — Keller correspondence]** Reflectionless potentials can be encoded by special spectral curves, while Keller-map obstructions involve branched-cover discriminants. Both exhibit “trivial local invariant, nontrivial global remainder.” A correspondence would require an explicit map between the $\ell=1$ Pöschl–Teller spectral data and the relevant Keller degeneration. Until that calculation exists, the resemblance carries no inferential weight.

Boldness is useful here only when it concentrates risk. Each conjecture should make a future calculation more decisive, not make the current evidence sound larger.
