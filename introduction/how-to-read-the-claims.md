# How to Read the Claims

The wiki deliberately places established mathematics, philosophical interpretation, proposed physical principles, conditional deductions, reproducibility receipts, empirical comparisons, and open constructions in one graph. A careful reader should ask what kind of claim is being made before asking whether it is persuasive. This note supplies a reading discipline; [[program-core/axioms-and-principles#Status vocabulary|the canonical status vocabulary]] and [[program-core/claim-and-failure-contract|claim and failure contract]] remain the authorities.

## Exact in which structure?

An identity can be exact inside a declared finite algebra, exponential family, background spacetime, or symmetry reduction while its application to nature remains conditional. “Exact” answers whether the conclusion follows from the stated hypotheses; it does not establish that the hypotheses describe the cosmos. A theorem about a selected theory member is not automatically a theorem about its wider response family.

## What are the types and carriers?

State, fact, probability, entropy, causal order, metric scale, response, precision, charge, action, and record are not synonyms. Nor is a scalar response on one family automatically a spatial kernel or gravitational modulus on another carrier. [[program-core/ontological-registers|Ontological registers]], [[program-core/operation-registers|operation registers]], and [[program-core/response-registers|response registers]] state the reusable distinctions.

When two quantities resemble one another, look for a map with a declared domain, codomain, normalization, tangent, and locality. Shared notation, dimension, symmetry, or functional form is evidence that a bridge may be worth constructing; it is not the bridge.

## Is the connecting map constructed or proposed?

The programme is strongest where it turns evocative identifications into named interfaces with failure conditions. [[algebra/program-core-interface|The algebra-to-core interface]], [[wall-construction-interface/entry|the wall-construction interface]], and the CST and CWST realization maps separate providers from consumers. An arrow labeled *open*, *postulated*, or *conditional* must not be mentally upgraded because the mathematics on either side is exact.

## Is familiar physics imported, preserved, recovered, or derived?

These relations carry different burdens. A proposal may coexist with GR or QFT, restrict them conservatively in a domain, add a constitutive closure, recover them as a limit, or derive them from a deeper object. [[compatible-with-existing-physics/relations-among-theories|Relations among theories]] owns this vocabulary. The present programme often imports tested local physics while asking whether a deeper realization can eventually explain the global arena in which it applies.

## What kind of evidence is present?

A symbolic proof verifies an implication. A computational receipt verifies a stated calculation. A background fit tests a phenomenological member. A prediction requires parameters and mappings fixed independently of the data being predicted. None of these by itself constructs a microscopic wall, derives a carrier, or actualizes a fact. For changing empirical detail, consult [[causal-scale-theory/empirical-status|CST empirical status]] and [[causal-wall-spectral-theory/empirical-targets|CWST empirical targets]] rather than this introduction.

The most useful general rule is asymmetric: failure of a downstream physical weld need not invalidate exact upstream mathematics, while success of a downstream fit cannot retroactively prove the missing upstream construction. Links in this wiki show intended dependence and conceptual relation; they do not by themselves certify derivation.
