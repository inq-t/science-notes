# Map of the Programme

The programme attempts to construct a fact-bearing cosmos from increasingly concrete mathematical structures. Its organizing spine runs from algebraic distinction and local presentation, through physical quotient and state-response geometry, to spacetime, gravity, facts, records, and observable cosmology. This is a dependency architecture and theorem programme—not a claim that every arrow has already been built.

## The governing question

[[cosmodynamics/entry|Cosmodynamics]] asks what makes possibilities, facts, records, causal relations, metric scale, and one common history belong to a single cosmos. [[sufficient-reason/entry|Sufficing and necessitating reason]] asks what kind of mathematical ground determines a law of possibilities and what further ground determines an obtained fact.

The programme adopts mathematical realism. An internal algebra is not less real than three-dimensional space, and observable spacetime is not promoted into reality by ceasing to be mathematics. The burden is instead to construct typed realization maps between mathematical regimes. This commitment does not imply that every consistent structure is physically instantiated, or that the present corpus has uniquely selected the structure of our universe. [[program-core/grounding-reasons|Grounding reasons]] states that stronger explanatory demand.

## The dependency spine

The intended construction order is:

> algebraic doctrine and generalized probes  
> → local presentation, gluing, and global individuation  
> → physical quotient and transported state-response geometry  
> → causal, Lorentzian, gravitational, and factive realization  
> → cosmological members, observables, and empirical tests

[[algebra/algebra-before-geometry|Algebra before geometry]] explains why the starting point must specify operations, morphisms, probes, covers, and equivalences rather than simply naming a preferred algebra. [[algebra/entry|The algebraic pre-core]] develops presentation, obstruction, pointing, and record as the proposed foundational pattern. [[algebra/theorem-programme|The algebra-to-cosmos theorem programme]] turns the attractive identifications into separate construction and recovery gates.

[[program-core/entry|The program core]] begins downstream, once enough algebraic, causal, state, transport, and area data have been supplied. It types the physical quotient, common response geometry, localization, gravitational comparison, and factive completion without allowing one object to silently stand for another. [[wall-construction-interface/core-spectral-wall|The core spectral wall]] now supplies an exact algebraic pre-wall: finite corners carry trace-capacity, while labelled whole-core fibers carry state, transport, binary readout, and response. [[wall-construction-interface/entry|The wall-construction interface]] owns the remaining realization of those distinct carriers as one physical causal and local wall, together with source readiness; area, gravity, and records remain later welds. [[spectral-wall-descent/entry|Spectral wall descent]] supplies further finite tests and candidate mechanisms without promoting them into a continuum cosmology.

## The principal physical branches

[[causal-scale-theory/entry|Causal Scale Theory]] studies homogeneous scale response. Its developed binary member has rigid conditional consequences once its wall reduction, scale-state soldering, source law, matching principles, and cosmological background are granted. The wider response family and the selection of a physical member remain distinct questions.

[[causal-wall-spectral-theory/entry|Causal-Wall Spectral Theory]] studies nonconstant observational response and its possible presentation as primordial correlations. It shares the upstream response architecture with the homogeneous branch, but its spatial-precision, Lorentzian, curvature, tensor, and likelihood maps are separate constructions. Neither branch currently derives the other.

[[causal-grain-cosmology/entry|Causal-grain cosmology]] reconstructs where these branches later converge: unit response and a proposed rank-two wall give a common-count scale, whose logarithmic cosmic depth supplies a freezeable candidate for the CMB acoustic ruler. It is the canonical synthesis of that discovery path, not a replacement for the specialist grain, transfer, or likelihood modules.

## Why the programme matters

The programme tests whether several familiar puzzles are symptoms of importing the arena too early. [[contemporary-puzzles/dark-energy-and-acceleration/entry|Cosmic acceleration]] is explored as a possible constitutive scale response rather than automatically as another substance inside spacetime. [[deriving-value-of-g/entry|The value of (G)]] is treated as a possible conversion between independently constructed state-response and gravitational area measures rather than merely an unexplained constant. [[program-core/record-scale-soldering|Record--scale soldering]] asks what would be required for expansion, temporal orientation, entropy, and factual history to be representations of one process without declaring them identical by analogy.

These are research targets, not completed derivations. [[compatible-with-existing-physics/entry|Compatibility with existing physics]] states how the programme can surround imported GR and QFT while preserving their tested local content. The current proof obligations and failure conditions live in [[program-core/claim-and-failure-contract|the claim and failure contract]] and the realization maps of the specialist branches.
