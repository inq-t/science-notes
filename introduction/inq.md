# Introduction

This wiki develops a first-principles research programme in which physics is mathematics: algebraic structure, internal geometry, observable spacetime, states, facts, and records are differently typed mathematical regimes of one reality. Its governing question is not merely which equations describe the universe, but what makes a cosmos—a coherent arena of distinguishable facts, causal relations, scale, and history—possible and necessary. The corpus contains exact mathematics, philosophical commitments, conditional physical models, limited empirical comparisons, and open realization problems; it should not be read as one completed theory.

The shortest conceptual route is [[cosmodynamics/inq|Cosmodynamics]] for the question, [[algebra/inq|the algebraic pre-core]] for the proposed foundation, and [[program-core/inq|the program core]] for the shared causal-response architecture. [[introduction/programme-map|The map of the programme]] explains how those layers are meant to depend on one another without pretending that every connecting map has been constructed.

Different readers will want different entrances. [[introduction/reading-paths|Ways into the programme]] gives short paths through the philosophical, algebraic-geometric, cosmological, observational, and measurement-centered material. [[introduction/how-to-read-the-claims|How to read the claims]] explains the distinctions needed to tell an exact theorem from a proposed interpretation, a conditional consequence, a reproducibility receipt, or an open physical weld.

Definitions, proofs, sources, and current status belong to the linked modules rather than to this introduction. When an introductory sentence and a canonical module diverge, the canonical owner governs.
