# Ways Into the Programme

This corpus is easier to enter by following a question than by reading folders alphabetically. Each path below is a short traversal through canonical modules; it is not a table of contents, and none of the paths needs to be completed before another can begin.

## If the question is “what makes a cosmos?”

Begin with [[cosmodynamics/cosmos-as-structure-of-facts|a cosmos as the structure of facts]], then read [[sufficient-reason/entry|sufficing and necessitating reason]]. [[program-core/grounding-reasons|Grounding reasons]] gives the programme's demand that constants, identifications, losses, and boundary choices receive typed explanations. [[cosmodynamics/fact-record-history|Fact, record, and history]] identifies the part still missing from a merely geometric or probabilistic universe.

## If the attraction is algebraic geometry

Start with [[algebra/entry|the algebraic pre-core]] and [[algebra/algebra-before-geometry|algebra before geometry]]. The conditional [[algebra/a2-inverse-cover|(A_2) inverse cover]] is the clean local--global test case; [[a2-ternary-response/entry|the ternary-response audit]] shows why that geometry does not automatically justify a binary physical ontology. [[complex-presentation-without-polarization/entry|Complex presentation without polarization]], [[algebra/real-forms-and-factive-spacetime|real forms and factive spacetime]], and [[semiorthogonal-decompositions/entry|semiorthogonal decompositions as categorical walls]] develop three different bridges that must not be collapsed into one.

## If the concern is cosmic acceleration or gravity

Read [[contemporary-puzzles/dark-energy-and-acceleration/puzzle-as-posed|the dark-energy puzzle as contemporary physics poses it]] before the programme's reinterpretation in [[contemporary-puzzles/dark-energy-and-acceleration/entry|Dark Energy and Cosmic Acceleration]]. [[causal-scale-theory/entry|Causal Scale Theory]] gives the conditional homogeneous response model, while [[causal-scale-theory/observables|observables and discriminating tests]] and [[causal-scale-theory/empirical-status|its empirical status]] prevent the background calculation from outrunning its evidence. [[deriving-value-of-g/entry|Deriving the value of (G)]] then exposes the deeper normalization and anti-circularity problem.

## If the concern is quantum measurement, information, or time

[[basic-concepts/conditioning/entry|Conditioning, instruments, and postselection]] separates outcome-relative updates from deterministic channels and algebraic expectations. [[program-core/contextual-descent-from-homogeneity|Contextual descent from homogeneity]] explains how differentiated observation need not imply a lumpy microscopic substrate. [[conservation-of-causal-charge/factive-descent-and-records|Factive descent and records]] and [[program-core/record-scale-soldering|record--scale soldering]] state what must still be added before information loss, outcome actuality, temporal orientation, and cosmic expansion can be treated as aspects of one structure.

## If the interest is response geometry and primordial structure

Begin with [[basic-concepts/hessians/entry|Hessians]] and [[hessian-response-geometry/entry|the Hessian common-response geometry]]. [[program-core/response-registers|The response register]] distinguishes Fisher--BKM response from real-time susceptibility, spatial precision, and gravitational response. Then follow [[wall-construction-interface/entry|the wall-construction interface]], [[spectral-wall-descent/entry|spectral wall descent]], and [[causal-wall-spectral-theory/entry|Causal-Wall Spectral Theory]]. Its live construction obligations are kept in [[causal-wall-spectral-theory/open-problems|the CWST open-problem ledger]] and [[causal-wall-spectral-theory/empirical-targets|empirical targets]].

## If the first instinct is to audit

Read [[program-core/ontological-registers|the ontological registers]], [[program-core/operation-registers|operation registers]], and [[program-core/claim-and-failure-contract|the claim and failure contract]]. Then compare [[compatible-with-existing-physics/entry|the local-physics interface]], [[algebra/cst-cwst-closure-audit|the CST/CWST closure audit]], and the empirical-status notes above. This route shows most quickly which results are exact, which are conditional, and which physical realization maps remain unconstructed.
