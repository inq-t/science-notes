## Central judgment

The mathematically solid core is the exact chain

\[ \Delta_\zeta^2(k) = \frac{k^3}{16\pi^2\,\rho_B(k)} = \frac{4}{\pi^4c^{(0)}(k)}, \qquad \rho_B(k)=\operatorname{Im}B(-k^2-i0), \]

and therefore

\[ \mathcal K_\zeta(k) = \frac{k^3}{2\pi^2\Delta_\zeta^2(k)} = 8\rho_B(k) = \frac{\pi^2}{8}c^{(0)}(k)k^3. \]

That holographic statement is supported by McFadden’s spectral-density construction: the cosmological scalar spectrum is inversely proportional to the spectral density of the stress-tensor trace two-point function. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1308.0331?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

What remains unproved is the specifically **causal-wall** statement:

\[ \text{relative-entropy geometry of the actual wall state} \quad\stackrel{?}{=}\quad \text{continued stress-trace spectral precision}. \]

The earlier document occasionally spoke as though this equality had already been established. It has not. The revised document makes it the load-bearing theorem target, CW–T1.

## The most important new correction: a factor of two

The referee correctly noticed that the transition from the relative-entropy Hessian to the stress-trace correlator needed a proof. But it did not notice a normalization mismatch inside that transition.

Version 2.0 defined the one-sided relative entropy

\[ \mathscr S[\zeta] = D(\omega_\zeta\Vert\omega_0) \]

and identified its Hessian directly with

\[ \mathcal K_\zeta=8\,\operatorname{Im}B. \]

For a regular exponential family generated by \(T\), however,

\[ \operatorname{Hess}_0D(\omega_\zeta\Vert\omega_0) = G^{\mathrm{BKM}}_{TT}. \]

Since

\[ \langle TT\rangle=4B, \]

the spectral part of the ordinary BKM Hessian corresponds to \(4\rho_B\), whereas the cosmological probability precision is \(8\rho_B\). The latter factor is also what one expects when a wavefunctional is converted into the probability weight \(|\Psi|^2\). The general relationship between relative entropy and its associated monotone information metric is well established. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/math-ph/9808016?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

Version 3.0 repairs this without inserting an arbitrary normalization into the wall map. It uses the symmetrized relative entropy

\[ \mathscr J[\zeta] = D(\omega_\zeta\Vert\omega_0) + D(\omega_0\Vert\omega_\zeta), \]

for which

\[ \operatorname{Hess}_0\mathscr J = 2G^{\mathrm{BKM}}. \]

Thus, provided the causal-wall source-family identification is proved,

\[ \operatorname{Hess}\mathscr J_{\mathrm{spec}} = 2(4\rho_B) = 8\rho_B = \mathcal K_\zeta. \]

Equivalently, one could retain one-sided relative entropy and define cosmological precision as twice its Hessian. The revision makes the convention explicit.

## Referee claims that were weakened or rejected

Several proposed improvements would have created new overclaims.

### The exponential-family structure is a theorem target, not a consequence of first variation

The fact that a Weyl variation inserts \(T\) does not prove that the actual causal-wall state family has the form

\[ \rho_\zeta = \exp\!\bigl(\log\rho_0+X[\zeta]-\psi[\zeta]\bigr). \]

That regular exponential or Araki cocycle structure must be constructed. The v3.0 proposition states what follows conditionally, while CW–T1 records the missing continuum theorem.

### Abelianization does not automatically identify every correlator

The completion memo claimed that BKM, Euclidean, symmetrized, and other correlators all coincide because the observable sector abelianizes. That is too fast. The wall stress tensor is not presently shown to lie in such a commutative sector, and Chentsov uniqueness does not remove the KMS or analytic-continuation kernels of a generic quantum field theory. Version 3.0 therefore states the exact correlator identification as part of the weld to be proved.

### The Planck–ACT difference is not itself a running measurement

The difference between the quoted Planck and P–ACT–LB values is about \(1.74\sigma\) under a naive combination of errors, but the two estimates come from different, overlapping likelihood combinations. Assigning them an ad hoc \(\Delta\ln k\) and converting the difference into

\[ \alpha_s\sim 3\text{--}6\times10^{-3} \]

is not statistically legitimate.

The appropriate comparison is the extended-model likelihood. ACT DR6 reports

\[ \alpha_s = \frac{dn_s}{d\ln k} = 0.0062\pm0.0052 \]

for P–ACT–LB and explicitly describes the result as consistent with vanishing running. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.14454v1)

Consequently, v3.0 says:

- the constant-exponent member predicts \(\alpha_s=0\) exactly because that is its definition;
- a robust nonzero running would reject that member;
- the general spectral formulation has not derived a universal bound such as \(|\alpha_s|\lesssim\delta^2\).

### \(c^{(0)}\) is not automatically a central charge or an effective \(N^2\)

The completion interpreted

\[ c^{(0)}(k_*)\simeq1.96\times10^7 \]

as a large central capacity and inferred \(N\sim\sqrt{c^{(0)}}\). That inference is not justified. Here \(c^{(0)}\) is the beta-weighted spin-zero spectral density of the stress trace, and it vanishes at a conformal fixed point. It is not automatically the fixed-point \(c_T\) or a microscopic rank.

The revision therefore uses:

- **scalar response:** \(c^{(0)}\);
- **spin-two capacity:** \(c^{(2)}\), or \(c_T\) where independently justified;
- **discernibility:** \(\mathcal I_\zeta\);
- **precision:** \(\mathcal K_\zeta\).

### Small non-Gaussianity has not yet been derived

The referee’s proposed chain

\[ c^{(0)}\gg1 \Longrightarrow \kappa_n\sim[c^{(0)}]^{1-n/2} \Longrightarrow |f_{\rm NL}|\ll1 \]

does not follow without a genuine microscopic large-\(N\) factorization theorem. Nor does the present framework justify a universal kill threshold \(|f_{\rm NL}|\gtrsim1\).

Version 3.0 keeps the conditional single-clock squeezed relation

\[ f_{\rm NL}^{\rm sq} = \frac5{12}(1-n_s), \]

but labels all of its assumptions. The general result must come from the actual continued \(\langle TTT\rangle\).

## A second major correction: higher derivatives are not cumulants

The v2.0 shorthand

\[ K_3\leftrightarrow\text{bispectrum}, \qquad K_4\leftrightarrow\text{trispectrum} \]

has been removed.

The revised treatment distinguishes:

\[ \text{source cumulants}, \qquad \text{relative-entropy derivatives}, \qquad \text{probability vertices}, \qquad \text{connected cosmological correlators}. \]

If

\[ \Gamma[\zeta] = \frac12\zeta\mathcal K_\zeta\zeta + \frac1{3!}\Gamma_3[\zeta^3] +\cdots, \]

then at leading order

\[ \langle\zeta_1\zeta_2\zeta_3\rangle_c = -\mathcal C_1\mathcal C_2\mathcal C_3 \,\Gamma_3(1,2,3)+\cdots. \]

Holographically, \(\Gamma_3\) is determined by the continued stress-tensor three-point function together with semilocal terms. It is not simply the third derivative of relative entropy.
