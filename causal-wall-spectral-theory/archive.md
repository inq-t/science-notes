# CWST Source Archive and Provenance

The active CWST graph supersedes the former v3 master and the earlier calculation module without erasing their provenance. The monographs, revision dispositions, conversations, original receipts, and rejected microscopic-member attempt are retained as immutable source history under [[causal-wall-spectral-theory/sources/legacy/README|the legacy-source ledger]]; their claims are not alternative canonical definitions.

## Monolithic sources

[[causal-wall-spectral-theory/sources/legacy/v3-master/Causal_Wall_Spectral_Theory_v3.tex|The v3 TeX source]] and [[causal-wall-spectral-theory/sources/legacy/v3-master/Causal_Wall_Spectral_Theory_v3.pdf|rendered PDF]] are the last monolithic formulation. [[causal-wall-spectral-theory/sources/legacy/v3-master/Causal_Wall_Spectral_Theory_v3_referee_disposition|The v3 referee disposition]] records revisions from v2; it is a revision ledger, not independent validation.

The files under [[causal-wall-spectral-theory/sources/legacy/v2-and-v2_1/causal-wall-spectral-completion-v2_1|the v2.1 completion archive]] retain the earlier report, completion memo, and JSON receipt. Their strongest normalization corrections have been rechecked by the current [[causal-wall-spectral-theory/receipts/README|scoped receipts]]. Claims later revoked by v3 are not restored merely because an old script recomputes their arithmetic.

## Conversations

The files under `sources/legacy/conversations/` preserve the discussions from v2 through the first wall, category, definition, and gravity audits. They are research history rather than active theorem owners. In particular, [[causal-wall-spectral-theory/sources/legacy/conversations/05-attempt-at-vacuum-gravity|the capacity-normalized gravity exploration]] belongs to the broader state--gravity programme now typed by [[program-core/causal-capacity-equivalence|causal-capacity equivalence]]; it supplies no CWST result.

The retired calculation module's unique chat material is preserved under `sources/legacy/calc-chats/`. Its first chat preserves the prompt and response around the original v2.1 receipt; that script and its three outputs are stored once in the adjacent v2.1 archive. Its second chat is a frozen, rejected attempt to select a microscopic \(A_2\) member. [[causal-wall-spectral-theory/no-gos/singularity-invariants-do-not-select-a-wall-member|The extracted no-go]] records why classical singularity invariants, monodromy, and a post-hoc running coincidence did not construct a wall algebra, state, transport, or response.

## Canonical ownership after refactor

[[causal-wall-spectral-theory/realization-map|The realization map]] is the single active owner ledger; this archive does not repeat it. Git preserves the deleted active notes and the retired `causal-wall-spectral-calc` tree. The local archive preserves the irreplaceable source artifacts needed for human review.
