# Calculate the Higher Response

After W3 has constructed the cosmological field, CWST must calculate a consistent hierarchy of cubic and higher probability-1PI vertices before it can predict non-Gaussianity. Higher derivatives of relative entropy and textbook singularity polynomials are not substitutes for connected source responses, analytic continuation, semilocal terms, and Lorentzian in-in correlators.

## The required hierarchy

Write the physical probability or Legendre-1PI functional as

$$
\Gamma[\zeta_{\mathrm{cos}}]
=\frac12\zeta_{\mathrm{cos}}\mathcal K_{\zeta_{\mathrm{cos}}}\zeta_{\mathrm{cos}}
+\frac1{3!}\Gamma_3[\zeta_{\mathrm{cos}}^3]
+\frac1{4!}\Gamma_4[\zeta_{\mathrm{cos}}^4]+\cdots.
$$

On the nondegenerate sector, the leading connected three-point function is

$$
\langle\zeta_{\mathrm{cos},1}\zeta_{\mathrm{cos},2}\zeta_{\mathrm{cos},3}\rangle_c
=-\mathcal C_1\mathcal C_2\mathcal C_3
\Gamma_3(1,2,3)+\cdots,
\qquad
\mathcal C=\mathcal K^{-1}.
$$

The four-point function includes both a dressed \(\Gamma_4\) contribution and exchange terms built from two \(\Gamma_3\) vertices. [[basic-concepts/hessians/higher-relative-entropy-is-not-cumulants|The higher-Hessian no-go]] explains why derivatives of a symmetrized divergence do not supply these vertices.

In a holographic member, the calculation must include the continued \(\langle TTT\rangle\) and higher stress responses with their semilocal and contact structures; [[vendor/holographic-cosmology/higher-point-dictionary|the vendor dictionary]] states that framework. A Lorentzian state and in-in prescription remain necessary.

## No universal floor

The large target value of \(c^{(0)}\) does not prove large-rank factorization: the spin-zero trace response can contain beta-function factors and is not automatically the central charge governing all normalized correlators. No universal \(1/\sqrt{c^{(0)}}\) non-Gaussianity floor follows.

## Completion and failure

W4 completion requires independently calculated vertices, the relevant Ward identities, and a Lorentzian state and contour on the W3 field. A likelihood for the resulting shapes belongs to W5. The W4 hierarchy fails if it violates conservation or soft identities in its claimed regime, or if semilocal ambiguities enter the physical shapes uncontrollably; empirical exclusion rejects the downstream member. Failure of one vertex ansatz does not refute the two-point kernel theorem.
