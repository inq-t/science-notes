# Construct Lorentzian Evolution and Matching

A spatial wall precision becomes cosmological physics only after it is embedded in a covariant constrained system, assigned a Lorentzian state, and matched into passive growing modes. This note owns that Lorentzian evolution-and-matching problem, not late transfer, data products, or likelihoods. CWST presently specifies the slots but has not constructed the realization. Actual outcomes and persistent records belong to the later [[conservation-of-causal-charge/factive-descent-and-records|factive-record construction]].

## Construction target

The required output is the Lorentzian portion of [[compatible-with-existing-physics/primordial-observable-interface|the primordial observable interface]]: reduced scalar and tensor phase spaces, symplectic normalization, Lorentzian equations and constraints, a state, and matching into declared passive growing modes. Late transfer to observables and likelihood evaluation remain W5.

W2 and the first W3 gate return static or equal-time closed forms on configuration carriers. [[measured-response-carriers/inq|Measured response carriers]] controls their domain-correct transport, but that theorem supplies no time parameter, transfer step, conjugate momentum, or energy normalization. A physical clock must enter through additional Lorentzian or Euclidean-history structure; [[lorentzian-spectral-envelope/inq|the Lorentzian spectral envelope]] owns that general boundary.

For the imported-transfer route, CWST must additionally prove the hypotheses of [[compatible-with-existing-physics/passive-adiabatic-transfer|passive adiabatic transfer]]. A common clock can remove relative density isocurvature, but intrinsic entropy, anisotropic stress, interactions, decaying modes, and transition data still require control.

The construction should return a complete linear evolution first. Only then can stability, sound speeds, growth, lensing, neutrino response, and nonlinear corrections be calculated without assuming the answer through standard transfer functions.

## Independence and preservation

The wall-to-spacetime map must be derived independently of the CMB spectra it is meant to explain. If GR plus local QFT is imported after matching, [[compatible-with-existing-physics/local-physics-interface|the local-physics interface]] requires conservative restriction: the new horizontal or global structure may select initial or boundary data without contradicting tested local covariance, conservation, gauge identities, positivity or unitary evolution in the recovered Lorentzian physical sector, or causal propagation. A Euclidean member must establish reflection positivity for a full time-dependent history or Schwinger hierarchy, or provide an explicitly controlled alternative continuation. Positivity of a single three-dimensional equal-time precision is insufficient for Osterwalder--Schrader reconstruction. None of these requirements makes the nonfaithful wall map itself unitary.

## Completion and failure

This construction is complete when a wall member has a gauge-reduced Lorentzian phase space, state, stable evolution, and coherent matching prescription derived independently of the target spectra. It fails if the scalar cannot be made gauge invariant, if constraints or matching are inconsistent, if the Lorentzian equations are unstable or ill posed, if a continuing incoherent source destroys acoustic coherence, or if local restrictions disagree with established physics in the declared overlap regime. A later likelihood failure belongs to W5 and does not by itself identify which Lorentzian arrow failed.
