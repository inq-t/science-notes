# Calculate the Scalar Response

CWST becomes a scalar-spectrum theory only when a microscopic wall member independently calculates its positive spin-zero or equivalent response function, including amplitude and scale dependence. Inferring that function from the measured \(A_s\), \(n_s\), or \(\alpha_s\) is calibration, not completion.

## Required calculation

A completed W2 member should return

$$
\mathcal K_{\mathrm{wall}}^{\zeta,\mathrm{calc}}(k)
$$

on its physical spatial quotient. If the optional holographic adapter independently calculates \(c_{\mathrm{calc}}^{(0)}(k)\), the cosmological formula begins only after [[causal-wall-spectral-theory/conjectures/wall-scalar-to-cosmological-curvature|the scalar W3 map]] has supplied an isomorphism on the reduced physical image:

$$
c_{\mathrm{calc}}^{(0)}(k),
\qquad
\mathcal K^{\mathrm{calc}}_{\zeta_{\mathrm{cos}}}(k)
=R_{\mathrm s}^{-*}
\mathcal K_{\mathrm{wall}}^{\zeta,\mathrm{calc}}(k)
R_{\mathrm s}^{-1}
=\frac{\pi^2}{8}c_{\mathrm{calc}}^{(0)}(k)k^3.
$$

The member must calculate the normalization, not merely the degree-three factor. Near-critical language must be backed by a flow equation, operator mixing, and a controlled distance from a fixed point. The strict trace-fixed-point obstruction in [[critical-scale-kernels/trace-fixed-point-null-no-go|the null no-go]] must be addressed by a deformation, double scaling, or different operator rather than by inverting a zero response.

## Upgrade test

The microscopic calculation is upgraded when a declared algebra and state, renormalized scalar source, physical quotient, and W2 transform independently return \(\mathcal K_{\mathrm{wall}}^{\zeta,\mathrm{calc}}\) with its amplitude and scale dependence. W3 continuation and a prediction for an observable not used to select the member are stronger downstream accomplishments, not part of this calculation's definition. A derived relation among the wall amplitude, its scale variation, and another calculated response channel would be materially stronger than a fitted power law.

## Failure

This W2 calculation fails if its wall precision is negative on a required physical mode, singular without a justified quotient, or dependent on arbitrary counterterm data on its declared physical image. Failure of W3 continuation or of the scalar likelihood rejects the downstream realization or empirical member, not this microscopic-response calculation. An unrestricted positive-function interface survives a member failure but remains nonpredictive by [[critical-scale-kernels/unrestricted-response-no-go|the unrestricted-response no-go]].
