The cleaner distinction is by **logical role**:

\[ \begin{aligned} \text{QFT} &: \quad \text{what local quantum observables exist and how they correlate},\\[3pt] \text{GR} &: \quad \text{how local stress-energy and metric geometry co-determine one another},\\[3pt] \text{CSD} &: \quad \text{which causal-scale state or cosmic history is realized as a whole}. \end{aligned} \]

Newtonian mechanics models trajectories in a largely fixed arena.

> GR can represent the expansion of the arena. CSD seeks to explain why the arena follows this particular scale-state trajectory and why geometry, horizon entropy, state distinguishability, and cosmic acceleration obey one common law.

That is a legitimate distinction.
