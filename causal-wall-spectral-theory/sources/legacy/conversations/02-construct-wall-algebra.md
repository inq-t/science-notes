## What “construct the wall algebra” actually means

The phrase is currently too compressed. It does **not** mean “invent an abstract algebra that has the desired answer.” It means replacing the placeholders

\[ \mathcal A_\Sigma,\qquad \omega_\Sigma,\qquad \Phi \]

with an actual quantum theory of the observables accessible to an observer-bounded causal region.

More precisely, the task is:

\[ \boxed{ \text{construct a scale-indexed wall net, its state family, and the action of scale on that family.} } \]

The master document already states the task in nearly these terms: build the scale-indexed local algebras and states for a dynamical FLRW apparent horizon, derive the horizontal cocycle directly, and calculate the BKM capacity from modular data rather than postulating it. The v3 spectral memorandum sharpens the same issue. Its finite-dimensional or regularized exponential-family lemma is exact, but it explicitly leaves open whether the actual continuum wall state realizes the required Araki/Connes source family generated by the stress trace. ## 1. What an algebra means here

In algebraic quantum field theory, an algebra is, roughly, the complete collection of measurements that can be performed in a specified causal region, together with the rules for:

- adding and multiplying observables;
- taking adjoints;
- deciding which observables commute;
- embedding the observables of a smaller region into those of a larger region.

The **algebra** says what can be observed and how observables relate.

The **state** says what expectation values and correlations those observables possess.

The **state family** says how those answers change when physical scale changes.

That last part is essential. The same algebra can support many inequivalent states. Consequently, the CMB spectrum is not an output of an algebra alone. It is an output of something like

\[ \boxed{ (\text{observable net}) + (\text{state}) + (\text{scale deformation law}). } \]

The natural mathematical object is therefore not one isolated algebra \(\mathcal A_\Sigma\), but a structured family such as

\[ \mathfrak W= \left( \{D_N,\mathcal H_N,\Sigma_N\}, \{\mathcal A_N(O)\}, \{\omega_N\}, \{\iota_{N_1N_2}\}, \{u_{N_2:N_1}(s)\}, \Phi, T \right). \]

Here:

- \(D_N\) is an observer-accessible causal diamond or causally complete region;
- \(\mathcal H_N\) is the relevant horizon or causal boundary;
- \(\Sigma_N\) is a codimension-two cut;
- \(\mathcal A_N(O)\) is the algebra of observables in a subregion \(O\);
- \(\omega_N\) is the state at Weyl scale \(N\);
- \(\iota_{N_1N_2}\) describes how nested region algebras embed;
- \(u_{N_2:N_1}(s)\) is the Connes cocycle comparing the two states;
- \(\Phi\) maps scale changes into state changes;
- \(T\) is the renormalized stress trace that is supposed to generate the local Weyl-source direction.

That whole package is what “the wall algebra” should mean.

## 2. There are presently three different objects hiding under the word “wall”

This needs to be resolved before a construction can be called complete.

### The observer-region algebra

This is the algebra

\[ \mathcal A(D_N) \]

of measurements accessible within an observer’s causal diamond or observable domain.

This is the cleanest primary object because local quantum field theory naturally assigns algebras to open, causally complete spacetime regions—not directly to a bare two-dimensional surface.

### The horizon or cut algebra

The master document places the binary normal pair at a spacelike codimension-two cut:

\[ N(\Sigma)=L_+\oplus L_-, \qquad Q=P_+-P_-. \]

But it carefully says that this is an **infrared projection** of the full state deformation. The full modular generator of a type-III algebra is not a two-level operator, and the entire field theory is not a binary Hilbert space.

Thus the desired relation is schematically

\[ (\mathcal A(D_N),\omega_N) \longrightarrow \text{horizon/corner sector} \longrightarrow \operatorname{span}\{1,Q\}, \]

where the final binary algebra is a quotient or reduced sector of a much larger algebra.

At present, the documents specify the right-hand side. Constructing the wall algebra means deriving the arrows from the left.

### The three-dimensional spectral QFT

The spectral memorandum uses a three-dimensional QFT stress tensor and a three-dimensional conformal operator \(P_3\). Its holographic dictionary is naturally expressed in terms of a Euclidean three-dimensional generating functional,

\[ W[g],\qquad \langle T_{ij}T_{kl}\rangle, \]

with momentum \(k\) interpreted as the cosmological comoving wavenumber and as a wall resolution scale.

That is not automatically the same object as a finite FLRW apparent-horizon cut. The former is a three-dimensional spectral or holographic theory; the latter is a codimension-two geometric surface, or a three-dimensional Lorentzian horizon worldvolume when evolved.

A completed construction must therefore do one of two things:

\[ \boxed{ \text{derive the 3D spectral theory from the observer/horizon algebra,} } \]

or

\[ \boxed{ \text{prove a duality or analytic-continuation map between them.} } \]

Without that bridge, the holographic spectral dictionary is a powerful imported laboratory, but it is not yet the derived spectral representation of the actual causal wall.

## 3. What must concretely be constructed

### 3.1 The region and its net of observables

One should begin with a comoving observer \(\gamma\) and a family of causally complete regions \(D_N\), probably with the FLRW apparent horizon selecting distinguished cuts \(\Sigma_N\).

Then one constructs a net

\[ O\longmapsto\mathcal A_N(O) \]

satisfying the standard structural requirements:

\[ O_1\subset O_2 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \mathcal A_N(O_1)\subset\mathcal A_N(O_2), \]

together with locality, covariance, and appropriate causal propagation or time-slice properties.

Locally covariant QFT already supplies a general formalism of exactly this kind: it assigns algebras functorially to globally hyperbolic curved spacetimes, and its relative Cauchy evolution describes how the theory responds to metric variations. The derivative of that response is the stress-energy tensor. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/math-ph/0112041)

This gives a particularly natural candidate for the scale map. For a Weyl perturbation

\[ g_{ab}\mapsto e^{2\zeta}g_{ab}, \]

one may define schematically

\[ \omega_\zeta = \omega_0\circ \operatorname{rce}_{g}[2\zeta g]^{-1}, \]

where \(\operatorname{rce}\) is the relative Cauchy evolution.

Then the claim that the scale tangent is generated by the stress trace is no longer merely inferred from a formal path integral. It is built into the covariant response of the algebra.

### 3.2 A physically selected state

For every scale \(N\), one needs a faithful normal state

\[ \omega_N:\mathcal A_N\to\mathbb C. \]

“Faithful” is important because modular theory requires that the state not annihilate a nonzero positive observable. In ordinary local QFT this is normally expressed through a cyclic and separating vector in the relevant representation.

The state must also satisfy the appropriate ultraviolet regularity condition—normally a Hadamard-type condition in curved-spacetime QFT—and whatever horizon equilibrium or near-equilibrium condition replaces exact stationarity.

There is precedent. For a class of expanding FRW spacetimes with cosmological horizons, Dappiaggi, Moretti, and Pinamonti constructed a horizon observable algebra, embedded the bulk Klein–Gordon algebra into it, and identified a preferred pure quasifree boundary state satisfying an energy-positivity condition. That state induces a physically meaningful bulk state and reproduces the Bunch–Davies state in de Sitter. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/0712.1770)

That does not solve the present theory: it concerns a free scalar and a restricted class of cosmologies. But it demonstrates that “construct a cosmological horizon algebra and preferred state” is an actual mathematical operation, not a metaphor.

### 3.3 The modular structure

Given \((\mathcal A_N,\omega_N)\), Tomita–Takesaki theory supplies

\[ \Delta_N,\qquad J_N,\qquad \sigma_s^{\omega_N}(A) = \Delta_N^{is}A\Delta_N^{-is}. \]

This is the **vertical** flow at fixed \(N\).

For two scales \(N_1,N_2\), the relative state comparison is encoded by a Connes cocycle

\[ u_{N_2:N_1}(s) = [D\omega_{N_2}:D\omega_{N_1}]_s. \]

This is the proper object from which the horizontal scale coordinate should be derived.

The master currently assumes that after reduction to the normal sector, this cocycle depends only on the scale ratio and has one noncentral generator \(Q\). Connes’ chain rule then yields

\[ \theta(r_1r_2)=\theta(r_1)+\theta(r_2), \]

and hence

\[ \theta(r)=-\varrho_\perp\ln r. \]

That deduction is valid **after** the rank-one ratio hypothesis is established. Constructing the wall algebra means proving that the actual relative cocycle really has that reduction, rather than beginning with the reduced answer.

### 3.4 The scale-to-state source family

The spectral weld requires more than the statement

\[ \frac{\delta W}{\delta\zeta} = \langle T\rangle. \]

It requires that the scale-deformed states form the appropriate noncommutative exponential or Araki-perturbed family, so that

\[ \operatorname{Hess}\mathscr J = 2G^{\mathrm{BKM}}_{TT}. \]

In a finite-dimensional regularization this can be written

\[ \rho_\zeta = \exp\!\left( \log\rho_0+ \int_\Sigma\sqrt g\,\zeta T -\psi[\zeta] \right). \]

But local QFT algebras are generally type III, so there need not be a density matrix \(\rho_\zeta\) or an ordinary trace. The continuum construction must instead be stated using Araki relative entropy, cocycle perturbations, and modular operators.

This is exactly why “constructing the algebra” is not decorative. Without the actual algebraic state family, the central identity

\[ \boxed{ \operatorname{Hess}\mathscr J_{\mathrm{spec}} = 8\rho_B } \]

is still conditional.

### 3.5 The normal binary quotient

The full theory must establish a projection such as

\[ \Pi_{\perp}^{\mathrm{IR}} \bigl((\mathcal D_NK_N)_\perp\bigr) = \dot\theta(N)\,Q, \]

and show that the other possible state directions either:

- are absent;
- are constrained;
- become massive or irrelevant;
- decouple from cosmological scale descent;
- or represent isocurvature sectors that observations tightly limit.

Only then does

\[ Q^2=1 \]

produce the exact binary information geometry

\[ \eta=\tanh\theta, \qquad G_{\theta\theta}^{\mathrm{BKM}} = \operatorname{sech}^2\theta. \]

At present, the binary family is an economical and mathematically exact reduced model. The wall construction must show why nature selects precisely that quotient.

### 3.6 Gravitational dressing and edge modes

Ordinary matter QFT in a bounded region generally gives a type-III algebra. Relative entropy is well defined there, but ordinary density matrices, traces, and finite von Neumann entropies are not.

The master theory also wants a genuine horizon entropy and a capacity normalized by area. This suggests that one probably needs two related algebras:

\[ \mathcal A_{\mathrm{QFT}}(D_N) \subset \widehat{\mathcal A}_{\mathrm{grav}}(D_N), \]

where the second includes gravitational dressing, boost/area charges, corner modes, and possibly an observer degree of freedom.

Recent semiclassical work constructs type-II gravitational subregion algebras whose entropy agrees, up to a state-independent constant, with generalized entropy. That construction relies on gravitational constraints and on the existence of suitable locally geometric modular states. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2306.01837)

More recent work has constructed horizon-cut algebras with gravitational corner edge modes, crossed-product structure, Connes cocycles, and area operators; in the linearized regime around horizons, each cut carries a type-II\(_\infty\) algebra with a trace and generalized entropy. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.07915)

There is also now an explicit construction of gravitationally dressed observables accessible to a comoving observer in a class of FLRW cosmologies. Its algebra has finite entropy identified with the generalized entropy of the observer’s causal diamond. The assumptions and inflationary background differ from Causal Scale Dynamics, but it is direct evidence that an “observer-dressed FLRW algebra” is mathematically constructible. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.01669v1)

This literature suggests a likely architecture:

\[ \boxed{ \text{type-III matter/graviton algebra} \quad\longrightarrow\quad \text{gravitational crossed product} \quad\longrightarrow\quad \text{type-II observer-wall algebra}. } \]

The exact crossed product and algebra type will depend on the global sector and zero modes. It should not be assumed in advance.

## 4. Why the construction is required

### It gives the information geometry an actual subject

Relative entropy is not a free-floating scalar function. It is relative entropy **between two states on the same algebra**.

Likewise,

\[ G^{\mathrm{BKM}}_{NN} \]

is not defined by drawing a \(\operatorname{sech}^2\) curve. It is the norm of a particular state-space tangent in a particular algebraic state family.

Without \((\mathcal A_N,\omega_N,\Phi)\), the phrase “modular free-energy curvature of causal-state change under scale” has a clear mathematical type, but no instantiated object.

### It distinguishes derivation from curve fitting

The master document’s own novelty criterion is essentially:

\[ \theta \text{ independently defined by causal-state structure} \quad\Rightarrow\quad \text{new physics}, \]

whereas

\[ \theta \text{ defined only by rewriting }H(z) \quad\Rightarrow\quad \text{an effective fluid in new notation}. \]

Constructing the wall algebra is how the theory passes that test.

### It must derive the binary reduction

The binary family is elegant, but elegance does not prove that an interacting quantum field theory on a cosmological causal region has only one relevant horizontal mode.

The algebra must tell us whether:

\[ \dim T_{\mathrm{physical}}=1 \]

in the cosmological scalar sector, or whether other noncentral tangents survive.

This is what underwrites the claims of:

- one adiabatic scalar;
- no independent isocurvature;
- one self-dual crossing;
- no arbitrary slip or sound-speed function.

### It is needed to calculate the spectral functions

The chain

\[ \text{algebra and state} \longrightarrow c^{(0)}(k),\,c^{(2)}(k),\,\langle TTT\rangle,\ldots \]

is the analogue of

\[ \text{Lagrangian and vacuum} \longrightarrow \text{particle spectrum and scattering amplitudes} \]

in ordinary QFT.

Until that first entry is supplied, the measured

\[ c^{(0)}(k_*)\simeq1.956\times10^7 \]

is a target placed into the theory from observation, not something the theory has computed. The original memorandum itself correctly says that once a microscopic algebra and state are specified, the spectral data become calculable QFT outputs. Causal_Wall_Spectral_Theory_v2.pdfPDF

### It tests the Scale–Capacity Equivalence Principle

The homogeneous theory can legitimately adopt

\[ \left. \frac{k_B}{S_c}G^\perp_{NN} \right|_{N_c}=1 \]

as a fundamental equivalence principle.

But an underlying wall construction could do more: calculate the ratio

\[ \frac{G^\perp_{NN}}{S/k_B} \]

and determine whether it is actually one.

That is not required to use the homogeneous background law as a phenomenological theory. It **is** required to claim that the law follows from the microscopic modular structure of the causal wall. The master explicitly identifies this calculation as the principal research target and a falsification condition. Causal_Scale_Dynamics_Master_v7_0.pdfPDF

## 5. Can it be accomplished in principle?

There are three different questions.

### Can one construct some wall or observer algebra?

**Yes.**

This is already established in substantial controlled settings:

- locally covariant QFT on curved globally hyperbolic spacetimes;
- bulk-to-cosmological-horizon algebras for free fields on classes of FRW backgrounds;
- null-plane and null-cone modular algebras;
- gravitationally dressed subregion algebras;
- observer algebras in semiclassical FLRW cosmology. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/math-ph/0112041)

There is no known categorical obstruction saying that a causal region, its quantum fields, its state, and its modular data cannot be constructed.

### Can one construct a useful Causal Scale Dynamics prototype?

**Very plausibly, yes.**

A controlled first construction does not require a nonperturbative theory of all quantum gravity. One could begin with:

1. a fixed spatially flat FLRW background;
2. a free conformally coupled scalar, or a controlled CFT/deformed CFT;
3. the algebra of a comoving observer’s causal diamond;
4. a Hadamard state;
5. Weyl deformation implemented through relative Cauchy evolution;
6. the Connes cocycle between nearby scale states;
7. projection onto the normal \(J\)-odd sector;
8. direct calculation of the BKM Hessian.

Null-surface modular Hamiltonians are explicitly known in important CFT settings and are local stress-tensor integrals along null generators, giving a concrete starting point for the normal/horizon sector. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1703.10656)

Such a model could answer several decisive questions without pretending to be the final universe:

\[ \begin{aligned} &\text{Does the horizontal kernel have the }|k|^3\text{ or }P_3\text{ form?}\\ &\text{Does the normal sector reduce to one }Q\text{ mode?}\\ &\text{Does the BKM norm produce }\operatorname{sech}^2\theta?\\ &\text{Does the cocycle depend only on the scale ratio?}\\ &\text{Does the capacity/entropy ratio approach unity?} \end{aligned} \]

A negative answer would be scientifically useful: it would tell us exactly which identification fails.

### Can one guarantee the construction will reproduce this theory?

**No guarantee follows from constructibility.**

There is an enormous difference between:

\[ \text{a consistent wall algebra exists} \]

and

\[ \text{its modular data produce the Ruble equations and the observed CMB spectrum}. \]

The actual construction might reveal:

- more than one physical horizontal mode;
- a nonbinary normal response;
- a BKM profile different from \(\operatorname{sech}^2\);
- a capacity ratio different from one;
- a scalar kernel outside the \(P_3\) universality class;
- a tensor response incompatible with observations;
- no natural state-selection principle;
- or no clean Euclidean continuation to the three-dimensional spectral QFT.

Those are not technical annoyances to be patched. They are genuine ways for the proposed microscopic realization to fail.

## 6. The most economical construction strategy

I would not begin by assigning an algebra directly to the apparent-horizon two-sphere.

The cleaner route is:

\[ \boxed{ \text{observer causal diamond first;} \quad \text{horizon and cut algebras derived second.} } \]

### Stage A — Fixed-background observer net

Construct

\[ D_N\longmapsto (\mathcal A_{\mathrm{QFT}}(D_N),\omega_N) \]

for a controlled field theory on FLRW.

Use locally covariant relative Cauchy evolution to define the Weyl scale action and identify the stress-trace tangent.

### Stage B — Horizon representation

Construct an injective bulk-to-horizon map or boundary representation,

\[ \mathcal A_{\mathrm{QFT}}(D_N) \hookrightarrow \mathcal A_{\mathcal H_N}, \]

and organize the nested horizon cuts as modular inclusions.

This is where the normal boost directions and the Connes cocycle become geometrically visible.

### Stage C — Derive the binary quotient

Show that the homogeneous \(J\)-odd part of the horizontal tangent has a single infrared generator:

\[ \Pi^{\mathrm{IR}}_\perp(\delta K_N) = \delta\theta\,Q. \]

Then derive, rather than assume,

\[ \theta=N-N_c, \qquad G_{\theta\theta}^{\mathrm{BKM}} = \operatorname{sech}^2\theta. \]

### Stage D — Add gravitational dressing

Include the area/boost charge, corner modes, observer degrees of freedom, and constraints. Determine the relation between the type-III matter algebra and the gravitationally dressed type-II algebra.

Then calculate

\[ \frac{k_B}{S_c}G^\perp_{NN}(N_c) \]

from the state rather than setting it equal to one.

### Stage E — Establish the spectral representation

Construct the three-dimensional Euclidean or holographic representation required for

\[ \langle T_{ij}T_{kl}\rangle = A\Pi_{ijkl}+B\pi_{ij}\pi_{kl}, \]

and prove the continuation

\[ \operatorname{Hess}\mathscr J_{\rm spec} = 8\,\operatorname{Im}B(-k^2-i0). \]

Then calculate

\[ c^{(0)}(k),\qquad c^{(2)}(k),\qquad \langle TTT\rangle. \]

This separates the problem into testable mathematical joints instead of asking for a miraculous final QFT all at once.

## 7. A better formulation of CW–T1

I would now split “construct the wall algebra” into five explicit obligations:

\[ \boxed{ \begin{aligned} \text{CW–T1a:}\;& \text{construct the scale-indexed observer-region net;}\\ \text{CW–T1b:}\;& \text{select the faithful Hadamard/KMS-like state family;}\\ \text{CW–T1c:}\;& \text{construct the Weyl/Connes cocycle and stress-trace tangent;}\\ \text{CW–T1d:}\;& \text{derive the rank-one normal binary quotient;}\\ \text{CW–T1e:}\;& \text{relate the Lorentzian wall algebra to the 3D spectral QFT.} \end{aligned} } \]

Gravitational dressing and the capacity/area relation can either be included in CW–T1e or given their own theorem target.

## Bottom line

“Construct the wall algebra” means:

> **Specify the observer-accessible quantum observables, their localization and inclusion relations, the physical state on them, and the precise manner in which Weyl scale changes that state; then derive the binary normal quotient and the stress spectral functions from this structure.**

It is required because otherwise \(\mathcal A_\Sigma\), \(\omega_\Sigma\), and \(\Phi\) are placeholders, and the modular interpretation remains a mathematically elegant typing of an effective cosmology rather than its microscopic derivation.

It can be accomplished **in principle** in the important sense that every major ingredient has controlled precedents, and an informative fixed-background prototype is already within the reach of existing algebraic-QFT and modular methods.

What is not assured is the scientifically decisive part:

\[ \boxed{ \text{that the algebra nature gives us actually reduces to } Q,\ \operatorname{sech}^2,\ P_3,\ \mathfrak R_c=1, \text{ and the observed spectral data.} } \]

That is not a reason for pessimism. It is precisely why CW–T1 is a genuine theorem target rather than unfinished notation.
