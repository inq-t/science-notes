# Why the $A_2$ Wall Attempt Was Not Promoted

The calculation chat verified several classical facts about the $A_2$ singularity and two elementary flow-law identities, but it did not construct a wall algebra, state family, horizontal scale tangent, KMS flow, or spectral response. Its proposed physical completion therefore fails by category mismatch before its numerical candidate selection is considered.

The classical return value is modest and reusable: the depressed cubic and its discriminant, the $A_2$ Jacobi algebra and Milnor number, and—after declaring the appropriate plane or elliptic suspension—the corresponding monodromy or elliptic invariant. Those facts do not establish the identifications made by the memo.

The load-bearing errors are:

- finite-order Milnor monodromy was treated as modular/KMS flow and then as horizontal scale or RG flow, although [[program-core/ontological-registers#Functionals, horizontal flow, and RG running|these are different mathematical types]];
- a representation of monodromy on homology was treated as a constructed wall observable algebra and state;
- the complex two-dimensional miniversal base was described as two real parameters without selecting a real slice, while the once-punctured-torus and $j$-invariant statements silently introduced different suspensions;
- the Einstein-member relation between $c^{(0)}$ and $epsilon$ was promoted to an identity of general CWST;
- the distance $1/6$ from a shifted spectral number to its center was treated as a derived anomalous dimension;
- a cubic singularity normal form was treated as proof of a single cosmological 1PI vertex and Suyama--Yamaguchi saturation, contrary to the distinct derivative hierarchies in [[causal-wall-spectral-theory/cosmological-descent|cosmological descent]].

The arithmetic observation

$$
\alpha_s=\frac{\delta}{6}=0.00585
$$

lies about $0.07\sigma$ from the locally archived P--ACT--LB constraint $0.0062\pm0.0052$. It is not a prediction or retrodiction: $1/6$ was selected after inspecting a curated candidate grid and ACT-related tilt information, and no independent wall calculation produced that rate. At most it is a post-hoc candidate coincidence with an explicit upgrade condition: construct the wall and derive the rate before comparing it with data not used in its selection.

The historical script's passing labels certify mostly hardcoded classical algebra and arithmetic. They do not certify any arrow from $A_2$ singularity data to wall physics. In particular, its parameter-count weld is literally a check that an assigned value `2` equals `2`, its marginal-rate grid omits other integers inside its own band, and its all-sector “battery” consists chiefly of hardcoded verdict strings.
