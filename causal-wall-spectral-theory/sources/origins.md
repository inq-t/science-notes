## Registered but unmirrored upstream products

- [BK18 full chains](https://lambda.gsfc.nasa.gov/product/bicepkeck/bicep2_prod_table.html): 66.72 GB, or 2.71 GB without CosmoMC `.data` files.
- [Planck PR3 full parameter grid](https://irsa.ipac.caltech.edu/data/Planck/release_3/ancillary-data/cosmoparams/COM_CosmoParams_fullGrid_R3.01.zip): 11 GB.
- [Planck PR3 SMICA I/Q/U map](https://irsa.ipac.caltech.edu/data/Planck/release_3/all-sky-maps/maps/component-maps/cmb/COM_CMB_IQU-smica_2048_R3.00_full.fits): about 1.9 GB; the associated FFP10 simulation ensemble is not a compact public likelihood artifact.
- [WMAP7 full likelihood 4.1](https://lambda.gsfc.nasa.gov/data/map/dr4/dcp/wmap_likelihood_full_v4p1.tar.gz): about 988 MB compressed.
- [Planck PR2 baseline likelihood](https://irsa.ipac.caltech.edu/data/Planck/release_2/software/COM_Likelihood_Data-baseline_R2.00.tar.gz): about 300 MB.
