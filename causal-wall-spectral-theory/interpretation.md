# Non-Stochastic and Cosmokinetic Interpretation

The interpretive proposal is that primordial correlations may characterize one observer-relative causal-scale state as a whole rather than arise from literal random kicks at an earlier proper time. This is compatible with the observable correlation hierarchy, but it changes ontology rather than the measured two-point function and does not by itself explain probability, measurement, or irreversible fact formation.

## What the data require

CMB observations require a theory to supply:

- scalar and tensor correlation functions with the observed amplitudes and shapes;
- mode content, including stringent limits on isocurvature;
- coherent initial phases and a transfer law;
- higher-point functions compatible with non-Gaussianity bounds; and
- a consistent spacetime evolution into the observed hot universe.

Those data do not determine whether a classical random field is ontologically fundamental. A classical Gaussian field can represent a commuting late-time two-point function even when the underlying description is a quantum state, a wavefunctional, or another noncommutative structure.

The phrase “non-stochastic” should therefore mean only:

> The theory does not posit a primitive classical noise source or a temporally localized random seeding event.

It must not mean that operational probabilities disappear. The calculations still use expectation values, a covariance, and sometimes a probability functional. The interpretation makes no new two-point prediction merely by changing the story attached to those objects.

## Whole-state reading

The inhomogeneous scale residue in

$$
-\delta\ln\sigma(x)=\delta N+\zeta(x)
$$

can be read as the inhomogeneous part of one scale presentation of the causal state. Its correlation functional is then structural data of that state,

$$
\Gamma[\zeta]
=\frac12\zeta\mathcal K_\zeta\zeta
+\frac1{3!}\Gamma_3[\zeta^3]+\cdots,
$$

rather than a substance deposited by random impulses. In an FLRW reconstruction, the same data may be represented as primordial initial conditions and then evolved by ordinary transfer. Calling them “initial perturbations” describes that reconstruction; it need not settle the ontology of their origin.

This interpretation is viable only if [[cosmological-descent|the descent map]] recovers the gauge-invariant curvature mode, its quantum or statistical state, and the standard observed correlation hierarchy. A re-description of measured $\mathcal K_\zeta$ without an independent state law has no additional explanatory content.

## Cosmokinetic scope

The later conversations use “cosmos” in a specialized sense: not the inventory of every object, but the structure by which facts can have causal order, metric scale, accessibility, records, and a common history. On that reading, causal-scale dynamics studies constitutive relations among

$$
[g],\quad \sigma,\quad
\text{state distinguishability},\quad
\text{horizon entropy},\quad
\text{scale history}.
$$

The aim is closer to a thermodynamics of the causal arena than to a replacement for every local field theory. It asks how an observer-relative history-bearing state is selected and organized while accepting successful local physics as input.

This philosophical scope is coherent but exceeds the current mathematics. The theory does not yet define a physical fact, stable record, objective outcome, present boundary, or irreversible fact-formation law. “Grammar of facts” is therefore a research orientation, not a derived structure in v3.

## Dependence on GR and QFT

[[causal-scale-interface|The interface note]] owns the technical division of labor and the conservative local-restriction test. The interpretive point is only that a horizontal state-selection law could surround established QFT without replacing its local observables or amplitudes. That scope choice does not itself prove preservation of covariance, conservation, causal propagation, anomalies, or tested QFT/GR phenomenology.

Similarly, GR already represents cosmic acceleration when a source is supplied. The broader Causal Scale Dynamics programme seeks a constitutive law selecting the source and history; it should not claim that GR is unable to describe acceleration. The spectral module addresses primordial correlations, not the independent validity of the late-time source law.

## Holographic representation is not forced ontology

The clean conceptual order is

$$
\text{causal-scale state}
\longrightarrow
\text{positive physical correlation functional}
\longrightarrow
\begin{cases}
\text{holographic QFT representation},\\
\text{geometric filling representation},\\
\text{another microscopic realization}.
\end{cases}
$$

The three-dimensional QFT is valuable because it offers calculable scalar, tensor, and higher-point slots. It need not be declared what the wall *is*. Conversely, if the programme uses the McFadden--Skenderis formulas as more than a representational member, it must provide the duality and continuation that make them applicable.

## Claims not supplied by this interpretation

- It does not derive the Born rule or eliminate measurement probabilities.
- It does not explain decoherence, classicalization, definite outcomes, or wavefunction collapse.
- It does not establish that horizontal scale deformation is the dynamics of fact formation.
- It does not show that the pre-observable structure lacks Lorentzian time; it merely leaves that ontology open.
- It does not turn a fitted positive spectrum into an explanation.
- It does not derive local QFT or the Standard Model.
