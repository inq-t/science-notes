# Tensor State Response Is Spatial Precision

Any CWST member that predicts primordial tensors must construct an independent transverse-traceless state-response block and transport it into a positive three-dimensional tensor precision. This W2\(_{\mathrm t}\) conjecture is not a corollary of the scalar response, a tensor-to-scalar ratio, or the existence of one scalar wall generator.

## Statement

Let a realization of [[wall-construction-interface/entry|the wall interface]] supply physical transverse-traceless source directions and a regular response block

$$
G^{\mathrm{BKM}}_{\mathrm{TT}}.
$$

The proposed tensor transfer is

$$
\boxed{
\mathcal K^{\gamma}_{\mathrm{wall}}
=\mathcal W^{\mathrm{TT}}_{\mathrm{BKM}\to\mathrm{spatial}}
[G^{\mathrm{BKM}}_{\mathrm{TT}}]}
$$

on a declared physical TT subspace. This is **[CONJECTURE — OPEN CONSTRUCTION]**. The construction must specify the tensor source, quotient, carrier change, state-dependent response transform, two-polarization convention, contact terms, and probability-1PI normalization.

## Independence from the scalar branch

Positivity or critical scaling of \(\mathcal K^{\zeta}_{\mathrm{wall}}\) does not determine \(\mathcal K^{\gamma}_{\mathrm{wall}}\). A relation between them requires additional microscopic structure. [[vendor/holographic-cosmology/scalar-and-tensor-spectra|The holographic scalar-and-tensor dictionary]] supplies a conditional representation after its own hypotheses are met, but it does not derive the TT wall block from the scalar block.

The later map

$$
\mathcal K_{\gamma_{\mathrm{cos}}}
=R_{\mathrm t}^{-*}
\mathcal K^{\gamma}_{\mathrm{wall}}
R_{\mathrm t}^{-1}
$$

belongs to the physical tensor realization in [[causal-wall-spectral-theory/open-questions/calculate-tensor-response|the tensor-response problem]]. W2\(_{\mathrm t}\) therefore ends before a primordial graviton claim.

## Upgrade and failure conditions

The conjecture is upgraded when one wall realization calculates the TT block and every transform above independently of tensor data, with positivity and normalization checked on the physical two-polarization sector.

It fails for that realization if no physical TT source exists, if the block is null or indefinite where a tensor mode is claimed, if its carrier cannot be changed consistently, or if the state-response transform is incompatible with the required spatial precision. Failure leaves a scalar-only CWST member possible.
