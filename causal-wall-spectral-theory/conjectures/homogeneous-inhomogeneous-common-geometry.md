# Homogeneous and Inhomogeneous Response Share One Geometry

The programme conjectures that the homogeneous scale capacity used by CST and the inhomogeneous precision sought by CWST arise as blocks of one localized causal-state response geometry. This is stronger than the independent consistency of either branch and requires one carrier, transport, quotient, and renormalization prescription together with an explicit areal-to-spectral soldering map.

## Block conjecture

Around a homogeneous reference, a candidate physical tangent chart is

$$
H^{\mathrm{phys}}_\Sigma
\simeq
\mathbb R v_N
\oplus
H^{\mathrm{phys}}_{\Sigma,\mathrm{inh}}.
$$

The response would then have the schematic form

$$
g^{\mathrm{BKM}}
\simeq
\begin{pmatrix}
G^{\perp}_{NN}&G_{N\zeta}\\
G_{\zeta N}&G_{\zeta\zeta}
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

At a homogeneous and isotropic reference, representation symmetry may force the mixed block to vanish at quadratic order. The quotient decomposition alone does not. Beyond that reference or beyond quadratic order, mixing may return.

The conjecture is that the full matrix is produced by one realization of [[wall-construction-interface/entry|the wall interface]] and one localization measure, and that its two diagonal sectors return the homogeneous and spectral consumers after declared contractions and transforms. This is **[CONJECTURE — OPEN CONSTRUCTION]**.

## The required soldering

The homogeneous branch uses a cut-integrated norm and, after localization, an areal response density \(\chi_N(p)\) with units \(L^{-2}\). The spectral branch uses a three-dimensional Fourier precision \(\mathcal K_\zeta(k)\) with units \(L^{-3}\) in the current convention. Consequently, no block identity of the form

$$
\chi=\mathcal K_\zeta
$$

is well typed.

A successful common geometry must construct a map such as

$$
\mathfrak B:
\mu^{\mathrm{desc}}_{\zeta,\zeta}
\longmapsto
\mathcal K_\zeta,
$$

where \(\mathfrak B\) includes the boundary, history, normal-direction, measure, and spectral transformations needed to change carriers and dimensions. [[basic-concepts/soldering/entry|Soldering]] names the general kind of operation; it does not supply this one.

## Independence test

The common-geometry claim is explanatory only if the wall state family and \(\mathfrak B\) are constructed independently of both the CST background history and the observed primordial spectrum. Supplying \(G_{NN}\) from a fitted pulse and \(G_{\zeta\zeta}\) from an independently fitted power spectrum does not show that they are blocks of one geometry.

## Upgrade and failure conditions

The conjecture is upgraded by one continuum construction that returns the full block form, its mixed terms, localization, and both consumer maps with compatible symmetries and normalization.

It fails in its strong form if the two sectors require inequivalent carriers, states, transports, or prescriptions; if no covariant areal-to-spectral map exists; or if a nonzero mixed block contradicts the separations used by either phenomenology. Either branch could remain as an independent interface theory after that failure, but the claimed unification and any derivation of their relative normalization would be lost.
