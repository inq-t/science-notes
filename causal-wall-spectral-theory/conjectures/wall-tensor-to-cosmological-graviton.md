# The Wall-Tensor-to-Cosmological-Graviton Map

CWST conjectures that a transverse-traceless wall field whose W2 response has become a positive spatial tensor precision can first be represented as a gauge-reduced equal-time metric configuration and then lifted to the Lorentzian graviton whose correlations are observed. This W3\(_{\mathrm t}\) construction is independent of the scalar curvature map and of the microscopic calculation of a spin-two response.

## Candidate wall tensor

W2\(_{\mathrm t}\) returns a precision

$$
\mathcal K^\gamma_{\mathrm{wall}}
$$

on a declared physical two-polarization spatial wall carrier. Positivity, transverse-traceless labeling, and critical scaling do not yet make its field a spacetime metric perturbation. The candidate \(\gamma_{\mathrm{wall}}\) may still be a response coordinate, boundary variable, or effective source.

## Conjectured realization

There should exist a map

$$
R_{\mathrm t}^{\mathrm{eq}}:
H^{\mathrm{TT}}_{\mathrm{wall}}
\longrightarrow
\mathcal Q^{\mathrm{tensor}}_{\mathrm{cos}}
$$

into the reduced equal-time two-polarization configuration carrier of an imported or independently recovered \(3+1\)-dimensional Lorentzian theory such that

$$
\gamma_{\mathrm{cos}}
=R_{\mathrm t}^{\mathrm{eq}}\gamma_{\mathrm{wall}}.
$$

This is **[IDENTIFICATION — OPEN CONSTRUCTION]**. It must specify the spatial gauge quotient, constraint reduction, canonical normalization, two-polarization convention, and relation to the perturbed physical metric. A separate lift from \(\mathcal Q^{\mathrm{tensor}}_{\mathrm{cos}}\) to the Lorentzian phase space \(\mathcal P^{\mathrm{tensor}}_{\mathrm{cos}}\) must supply the conjugate momentum, symplectic form, state, clock evolution, and matching into the observable growing tensor mode.

The equal-time map is not supplied by calling the W2 source transverse traceless. Nor is either W3 gate supplied by the optional holographic spectrum dictionary: that dictionary converts a qualified spin-two response into correlations after its own hypotheses, while \(R_{\mathrm t}^{\mathrm{eq}}\) identifies only the physical equal-time configuration and the later lift identifies the Lorentzian field.

## Precision under the map

Let \(q^{\gamma}_{\mathrm{wall}}\) be the closed positive form associated with \(\mathcal K^\gamma_{\mathrm{wall}}\). If \(R_{\mathrm t}^{\mathrm{eq}}\) is a bounded Hilbert-space isomorphism with bounded inverse, define

$$
D(q^{\gamma}_{\mathrm{cos}})
=R_{\mathrm t}^{\mathrm{eq}}D(q^{\gamma}_{\mathrm{wall}}),
\qquad
q^{\gamma}_{\mathrm{cos}}[R_{\mathrm t}^{\mathrm{eq}}\xi,R_{\mathrm t}^{\mathrm{eq}}\eta]
=q^{\gamma}_{\mathrm{wall}}[\xi,\eta].
$$

Then the associated equal-time precision is

$$
\boxed{
\mathcal K^{\mathrm{eq}}_{\gamma_{\mathrm{cos}}}
=\bigl(R_{\mathrm t}^{\mathrm{eq}}\bigr)^{-*}
\mathcal K^\gamma_{\mathrm{wall}}
\bigl(R_{\mathrm t}^{\mathrm{eq}}\bigr)^{-1}.}
$$

[[measured-response-carriers/inq|Measured response carriers]] owns the generic form-transport theorem. If \(R_{\mathrm t}^{\mathrm{eq}}\) has a kernel, the inverse formula is valid only after quotienting the null or gauge sector and proving an isomorphism onto a closed physical image. A genuinely projective realization instead requires a projected-covariance, constrained, or shorted-form construction.

## Independence and failure

The scalar W3 map does not determine \(R_{\mathrm t}^{\mathrm{eq}}\), and a scalar-only member is consistent. [[causal-wall-spectral-theory/open-questions/calculate-tensor-response|The tensor-response calculation]] separately asks whether the wall theory produces a spin-two response at all; [[compatible-with-existing-physics/primordial-observable-interface|the primordial observable interface]] owns its later transfer to gravitational-wave and CMB observables.

The equal-time gate is upgraded by a covariant derivation of \(R_{\mathrm t}^{\mathrm{eq}}\), its form domain, and normalization. Full W3\(_{\mathrm t}\) requires a reduced action or equivalent constrained Hamiltonian construction that also derives the Lorentzian lift and physical tensor state. [[lorentzian-spectral-envelope/inq|The Lorentzian spectral envelope]] records the separate clock requirement. The conjecture fails for a member if the wall tensor is pure gauge, constrained away, has the wrong number of polarizations, cannot be assigned a stable causal Lorentzian evolution, or cannot be matched into the observed tensor phase space.
