# The Wall-Tensor-to-Graviton Map

CWST conjectures that a transverse-traceless wall field whose W2 response has become a positive spatial tensor precision can be represented as the gauge-reduced Lorentzian graviton whose correlations are observed. This W3\(_{\mathrm t}\) map is independent of the scalar curvature map and of the microscopic calculation of a spin-two response.

## Candidate wall tensor

W2\(_{\mathrm t}\) returns a precision

$$
\mathcal K^\gamma_{\mathrm{wall}}
$$

on a declared physical two-polarization spatial wall carrier. Positivity, transverse-traceless labeling, and critical scaling do not yet make its field a spacetime metric perturbation. The candidate \(\gamma_{\mathrm{wall}}\) may still be a response coordinate, boundary variable, or effective source.

## Conjectured realization

There should exist a map

$$
R_{\mathrm t}:
H^{\mathrm{TT}}_{\mathrm{wall}}
\longrightarrow
\mathcal P^{\mathrm{tensor}}_{\mathrm{cos}}
$$

into the reduced tensor phase space of an imported or independently recovered \(3+1\)-dimensional Lorentzian theory such that

$$
\gamma_{\mathrm{cos}}
=R_{\mathrm t}\gamma_{\mathrm{wall}}.
$$

This is **[IDENTIFICATION — OPEN CONSTRUCTION]**. It must specify the Lorentzian carrier and state, spacetime gauge quotient, constraint reduction, symplectic and canonical normalization, two-polarization convention, relation to the perturbed physical metric, and matching into the observable growing tensor mode.

The map is not supplied by calling the W2 source transverse traceless. Nor is it supplied by the optional holographic spectrum dictionary: that dictionary converts a qualified spin-two response into correlations after its own hypotheses, while \(R_{\mathrm t}\) identifies the physical Lorentzian field.

## Precision under the map

If \(R_{\mathrm t}\) is an isomorphism on the reduced physical image, then

$$
\boxed{
\mathcal K_{\gamma_{\mathrm{cos}}}
=R_{\mathrm t}^{-*}
\mathcal K^\gamma_{\mathrm{wall}}
R_{\mathrm t}^{-1}.}
$$

If \(R_{\mathrm t}\) has a kernel, the inverse formula is valid only after quotienting the null or gauge sector and proving an isomorphism onto the physical image. A genuinely projective realization instead requires a projected-covariance, constrained, or Schur-complement construction.

## Independence and failure

The scalar W3 map does not determine \(R_{\mathrm t}\), and a scalar-only member is consistent. [[causal-wall-spectral-theory/open-questions/calculate-tensor-response|The tensor-response calculation]] separately asks whether the wall theory produces a spin-two response at all; [[compatible-with-existing-physics/primordial-observable-interface|the primordial observable interface]] owns its later transfer to gravitational-wave and CMB observables.

The conjecture is upgraded by a covariant reduced action or equivalent constrained Hamiltonian construction that derives \(R_{\mathrm t}\), its normalization, and the physical tensor state. It fails for a member if the wall tensor is pure gauge, constrained away, has the wrong number of polarizations, cannot be assigned a stable causal Lorentzian evolution, or cannot be matched into the observed tensor phase space.
