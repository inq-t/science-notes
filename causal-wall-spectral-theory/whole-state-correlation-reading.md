# The Whole-State Correlation Reading

CWST reads primordial correlations as relational structure of one cosmic state rather than as evidence that miniature local events were produced by an ontically indeterminate noise source. This is a positive claim about what the spectral data may represent, not a denial of measured probability rules; determined fact, accessible determining reason, state, outcome, and record remain distinct.

## What the interpretation affirms

Cosmological observations give correlation functions of local records: temperature and polarization maps, lensing reconstructions, matter distributions, and other late observables. A theory may represent those correlations through one global or boundary state whose restrictions determine compatible local expectations. The covariance then describes relations among possible records in that state. It need not be narrated as a sequence of independent classical draws that occurred before the records existed.

This fits the programme's object of inquiry. A cosmos is not merely a collection of local mechanisms; it is an arena in which local facts belong to one causally and historically coherent whole. CWST asks whether the inhomogeneous part of scale response helps constitute that whole-state correlation geometry.

[[program-core/contextual-descent-from-homogeneity|Contextual descent]] sharpens this reading. The sub-observable algebra and correlation law may remain homogeneous while an actual character of the commutative record algebra is nonconstant. The lumpy sky is then a property of the pointed factual history, not direct evidence for a lumpy pre-observable substrate. [[program-core/common-response-matrix|The common response matrix]] describes the susceptibility of the law of distinctions before that pointing is selected.

## Determination and access

[[program-core/grounding-reasons|The grounding-reason discipline]] distinguishes

$$
\text{a fact is determined}
\qquad\text{from}\qquad
\text{its determining reason is accessible to an observer}.
$$

Failure of access does not establish absence of a sufficing reason. The words *random* and *stochastic* are therefore avoided as ontological verdicts unless a precise probability object and interpretation have been declared. They may still name useful mathematical tools: a measure, a sampling algorithm, an effective open-system description, or a likelihood model.

The whole-state reading is compatible with several formal uses of probability. Born probabilities, conditional records, decoherent histories, Bayesian uncertainty, or a classical effective ensemble can all be calculationally meaningful. CWST does not yet derive one of them or explain outcome selection. [[sufficient-reason/quantum-interpretations|The interpretation ledger]] owns those wider alternatives.

## Correlation is not facthood

A positive state and its covariance specify expectations. They do not select a realized character of a context, create an event, or make a durable record. In the core vocabulary,

$$
\text{state}
\ne
\text{formal fact-value}
\ne
\text{physical fact-event}
\ne
\text{record}.
$$

Nor does a BKM susceptibility prove that inaccessible distinctions have literally become geometry. [[program-core/descent-response-geometry|Response geometry]] ends before fact formation; [[conservation-of-causal-charge/entry|causal charge]] asks whether a future symmetry law can relate state, geometry, matter, and records on one carrier.

## Why the reading does not weaken empirical obligations

Retyping the origin question changes the explanatory target but not the observable target. CWST must still return

- the gauge-invariant scalar and tensor variables;
- their normalization, scale dependence, and higher correlations;
- a Lorentzian state and consistent constraints;
- passive coherent transfer or an end-to-end alternative; and
- the measured late-time records.

The theory is not rescued by saying that conventional perturbations ask the wrong ontological question. It succeeds only if the whole-state account explains at least the same measurable structure while making fewer primitive or category-confused assumptions.

## Scope

The interpretation does not assert a literal wavefunction of the universe, a many-worlds ontology, hidden classical variables, objective collapse, or a probability-free replacement of quantum theory. Holographic cosmology can be used as a representation without treating the three-dimensional QFT as literal microscopic furniture. Conversely, failure of that representation would not by itself refute the whole-state reading.

The interpretive claim becomes physically substantial only when [[conjectures/state-response-is-spatial-precision|the state response]], [[conjectures/wall-residue-is-curvature|the wall residue]], and [[compatible-with-existing-physics/primordial-observable-interface|the observable interface]] are constructed on one compatible chain.
