# CWST Realization Map

Causal-Wall Spectral Theory consumes the programme core's physical quotient and inhomogeneous response block, then adds a conjectural chain from causal-wall state response to primordial observables. It does not consume the homogeneous CST pulse equations, and it does not promote an exact Hessian or an optional holographic representation into the missing physical weld.

## The CWST-specific chain

Let \(H^{\mathrm{phys}}_{\Sigma,\mathrm{inh}}\) denote the inhomogeneous part of the physical horizontal tangent object. The desired sequence is

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{W0: }&
(\Sigma_N,\mathcal A_N,\omega_N,\mathcal T_{N_2N_1})
&&\text{constructed wall family},\\
\text{W1: }&
G^{\mathrm{BKM}}_{\zeta\zeta}
&&\text{response on }H^{\mathrm{phys}}_{\Sigma,\mathrm{inh}},\\
\text{W2: }&
\mathcal K_{\mathrm{wall}}
=\mathcal W_{\mathrm{BKM}\to\mathrm{spatial}}
[G^{\mathrm{BKM}}_{\zeta\zeta}]
&&\text{spatial precision},\\
\text{W3: }&
\mathcal K_{\zeta_{\mathrm{cos}}}
=\mathcal R_*\mathcal K_{\mathrm{wall}}
&&\text{physical-curvature representation},\\
\text{W4: }&
\{\Gamma^{(n)}_{\zeta,\gamma}\}_{n\geq2}
&&\text{scalar, tensor, and higher vertices},\\
\text{W5: }&
\{C_\ell^{XY},P_m,B_\zeta,\ldots\}
&&\text{late observable records}.
\end{aligned}
$$

Here \(\mathcal R_*\) denotes the induced action of a spacetime realization map, not the Ruble crossing number \(\mathfrak R_c\). If a linear field map is written \(\zeta_{\mathrm{cos}}=R\zeta_{\mathrm{wall}}\), then on an invertible sector the precision transforms as

$$
\mathcal K_{\zeta_{\mathrm{cos}}}
=R^{-*}\mathcal K_{\mathrm{wall}}R^{-1}.
$$

The notation makes the domain change visible; equality of the two \(\zeta\) symbols is only the special case \(R=\mathbf1\) after their carriers have already been identified.

## The dimensional joint

The core's local areal response density and CWST's Fourier precision are not the same coefficient:

$$
[\chi_N(p)]=L^{-2},
\qquad
[\mathcal K_\zeta(k)]=L^{-3}
$$

in the registered three-dimensional Fourier convention. A common-geometry proposal must therefore supply an integration over a normal or history direction, a boundary-to-bulk map, a measure conversion, or another explicit soldering operation. [[conjectures/homogeneous-inhomogeneous-common-geometry|The common-geometry conjecture]] owns that stronger claim. Dimensional agreement after an unstated unit choice would not be enough; the carriers and measures must also agree.

## Failure localization

- Failure of W0 leaves both homogeneous and inhomogeneous wall programmes without a microscopic carrier.
- Failure of W1 can leave a wall family but no nondegenerate physical response direction.
- Failure of W2 rejects the causal-wall spectral weld without rejecting Fourier precision or holographic cosmology in their own domains.
- Failure of W3 rejects the proposed primordial interpretation while leaving a spatial state response possible.
- Failure of W4 makes the model unable to claim tensors or non-Gaussianity.
- Failure of W5 rejects observable adequacy or the imported-transfer route.
- Failure of the strong common-geometry conjecture prevents unification with the homogeneous areal response, but need not refute a weaker independently postulated spectral interface.

The status of every downstream claim is bounded by the weakest edge it uses.
