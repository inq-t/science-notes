# Yang–Mills Continuum Crossover

A regulated response estimate becomes a Yang–Mills mass-gap theorem only if its physical rate remains positive through volume removal and ultraviolet refinement, on a carrier that reconstructs the intended nontrivial continuum theory. Two sufficient routes have different inputs: transport a common static correlation exponent on an Osterwalder–Schrader-total local family, or bound complete midpoint-to-boundary prediction at one fixed physical slab thickness. The estimates below specify those routes and the renormalization losses they must control; the required four-dimensional asymptotically free crossover remains open.

## The rate must survive a change of scale

The [[strong-coupling-gap-and-continuum-crossover/inq|strong-coupling results]] establish genuine regulated gaps and provide interaction-preserving temporal-limit calibrations. Their coupling restrictions do not cover the weak-bare-coupling spatial continuum trajectory. At temporal spacing \(a_\tau\), a finite physical energy \(\Delta_E\) has normalized transfer contraction

$$
q(a_\tau)=\exp[-a_\tau\Delta_E/(\hbar c)].
$$

Thus the one-step deficit closes under refinement; the invariant target is a positive logarithmic rate per physical length, or a subunit contraction at a fixed positive physical thickness. [[mass-scale-calibration/inq|Scale calibration]] also requires a renormalization prescription independent of the unknown gap.

[[yang-mills-continuum-crossover/two-scale-rg-descent-and-the-crossover-lemma|Two-scale RG descent]] gives the exact entropy decomposition, the mobility induced by observable pullback, and the conditional-Fisher coercivity recursion. Recovering a fine auxiliary inequality from a coarse one requires controlled discarded fibers, the actual coarse marginal, and their coupling in declared metrics. A bounded loss at each step is insufficient when the number of ultraviolet steps diverges. The same note distinguishes this stronger functional-inequality transport from the static covariance route.

## Static localization and complete slab response

The [[yang-mills-continuum-crossover/asymptotically-free-response-crossover-lemma|asymptotically free response-crossover lemma]] asks exact gauge-covariant blocking to reach a fixed physical scale where the full generated law has both a uniform centered auxiliary \(L^2\) floor and a compatible influence bound. [[auxiliary-response-localization/inq|Auxiliary response localization]] eliminates the sampler clock from those two estimates. [[rg-covariance-residue/inq|RG covariance residue]] then accounts for the correlations of discarded fluctuations and transported sources. Terminal mixing alone cannot recover a physically soft source that blocking has forgotten.

If the resulting reflected correlations converge with one common positive physical exponent on an OS-total centered local family, spectral calculus excludes low positive energy in the reconstructed theory. The prefactor may depend on the source; the exponent must be common. This route need not bound prediction from a complete infinite boundary face.

[[yang-mills-continuum-crossover/collared-surface-response-to-the-clay-gap|Collared surface response to the Clay gap]] instead assumes a complete midpoint-to-two-boundary prediction norm \(\rho_*<1\), uniform in the regulator, at fixed physical half-thickness \(\ell_*\). The stationary bridge comparison yields

$$
H-E_0\geq
\frac{\hbar c}{\ell_*}\log(\rho_*^{-1})(I-P_0)
$$

after the stated passage to the limit. [[collared-quasi-factorization-and-surface-response/inq|Collared quasi-factorization]] and [[nonlinear-whole-law-surface-response/inq|nonlinear whole-law response]] formulate action-derived sufficient estimates for that norm. [[two-slice-innovation-geometry/inq|Complete innovation geometry]] explains why proper local marginals can miss a global slow distinction; the actual gauge-invariant vacuum complement must be covered.

## Reconstruction is a separate obligation

Neither branch constructs the continuum law merely by providing a positive number. The comparison must retain the intended vacuum and sources, reflection positivity, the remaining OS hypotheses, and nontrivial Yang–Mills short-distance behavior. An energy exclusion becomes an invariant-mass exclusion only after positive-energy Poincare reconstruction with the required joint spectrum and vacuum. The collared theorem states the form-and-carrier convergence contract; the response-crossover lemma states the correlation-convergence and totality contract. These are conditional implications with different hypotheses, and neither implication proves its required uniform Wilson estimate.
