# Relation to Entropic and Postquantum Gravity

The G-free first law sits in a populated neighbourhood: Jacobson's horizon thermodynamics, Verlinde's two entropic proposals, Padmanabhan's thermodynamic programme, and Oppenheim's postquantum (classical-quantum) gravity. This note places each against the reorganization of this module — what is shared, what is owed, what is rejected — and states the programme's standing ontological firewall: where those literatures say "random," this programme says "the necessitating reason for the fact is inaccessible from within." The audited expositions live in the vendor modules; nothing here re-grades them.

## Jacobson: the theorem this module leans on

[[vendor/entropic-gravity/jacobson-local-horizon-thermodynamics|The audited Jacobson result]] is the load-bearing import: Clausius at every wedge plus universal areal entropy yields Einstein dynamics. This module adds no physics to it — it *re-types* it, separating the dimensionless law $q=\delta\iota$ (G-free) from the presentation hypothesis $\iota=\eta A$ (all of $G$). The debt runs one way: if the Jacobson premises fail as universality claims (his own non-equilibrium caveat, [[vendor/entropic-gravity/jacobson-non-equilibrium-thermodynamics|audited here]]), the law survives only sectorwise and the failure typing of [[the-g-free-first-law]] takes over.

## Verlinde: right register, unfinished constitutive law

[[vendor/entropic-gravity/verlinde-entropic-force|The 2010 entropic force]] and [[vendor/entropic-gravity/verlinde-emergent-gravity|the 2016 elastic response]] both place gravity in the response register — the correct register by this module's audit — but neither constructs the ledger: screen entropies are postulated placements, and the 2016 elastic modulus is exactly the kind of constitutive coefficient the wall construction is supposed to *derive*. The de Sitter-entropy-displacement intuition of 2016 is the closest ancestor of the bath-crossover reading in [[contemporary-puzzles/galactic-rotation-anomaly/entry|the rotation-anomaly module]]; the vendor's empirical-status note owns its mixed record.

## Padmanabhan: the same split, asserted rather than typed

Padmanabhan's programme (Rep. Prog. Phys. **73**, 046901 (2010)) reads the field equations as thermodynamic identities and gravity's degrees of freedom as surface bookkeeping — congruent with the boundary-ledger reading here — but treats the entropy density as given. In this module's terms: it works downstream of the presentation $\iota=\eta A$ without asking what fixes $\mathrm dA/\mathrm d\iota$, which is precisely the value question quarantined in [[rulers-are-matter]].

## Postquantum gravity: the diffusion is a ledger in disguise

The audited construction ([[vendor/postquantum-gravity/entry|vendor]]; sources in the library cluster including [[library/covariant-cq-path-integrals/entry|the covariant path integral]] and [[library/renormalisation-of-postquantum-gravity/entry|the renormalisation paper]]) couples classical geometry to quantum matter at the price of a decoherence–diffusion trade. Three contacts matter for this module. First, the Onsager–Machlup term is an equation-of-motion-squared *cost functional* — a response-register object, not a dynamics — which is the vendor's "idea worth taking" and rhymes with the wall's response geometry. Second, the trade-off itself is an exchange rate between geometry-register noise and matter-register distinguishability: a sibling of $\mathrm dA/\mathrm d\iota$, and the right place to ask what in CQ language plays the ledger. Third, its rotation-curve and dark-matter applications ([[library/anomalous-galactic-rotation-curves/entry|Oppenheim–Russo]]; [[library/phantom-cold-dark-matter/entry|Oppenheim–Panella–Pontzen]]) are order-of-magnitude and candidly null respectively, per the vendor's empirical-status grading — they are cited in the rotation-anomaly module as convergent *scale* claims, never as established mechanisms.

## The randomness firewall

**[ADOPTED COMMITMENT]** The entropic and postquantum literatures speak of stochastic metrics, random screens, Brownian spacetime. The programme's standing commitment, owned by [[sufficient-reason/entry|sufficing and necessitating reason]]: there is no ontic randomness — a probability assignment is a *sufficing* reason (a measure), and the obtaining of a fact has a *necessitating* reason that need not be accessible from within the observable record. Appearances of chance are confusions of epistemic access with ontology.

The firewall has a technical anchor inside postquantum gravity itself, found in the vendor audit: at saturation of the decoherence–diffusion trade-off the quantum state is **pure conditioned on the classical trajectory** — the unconditioned mixedness is marginalisation over *which history obtains*, not destruction. In the programme's types: the classical trajectory is a character (necessitating ground), the Onsager–Machlup weight is a measure (sufficing ground), and "noise" is the shadow of an inaccessible pointing. Import rule for every consumer of these sources: never import "spacetime is random" as ontology; import "the metric's sufficing reason is a measure over histories, and each history obtains by a reason inaccessible from within."

Two disciplines keep the firewall from overreach. Bell–Kochen–Specker, owned by [[sufficient-reason/two-species-of-reason|two species of reason]], forbids reading the necessitating reason as a hidden assignment of context-free *values* — the reason is structural and global, not a secret scoreboard; a later fact may be necessitated by the whole without being retrodictable from the earlier record ([[sufficient-reason/algebraic-arrow-of-time|the arrow note]]). And the commitment is a philosophical axiom of the programme, not a theorem: it is stated here so that it is *cited* rather than smuggled.

From Jacobson: the theorem. From Verlinde: the register. From Padmanabhan: the boundary bookkeeping. From postquantum gravity: the exchange-rate structure and the conditional-purity anchor. From none of them: an ontology of chance.
