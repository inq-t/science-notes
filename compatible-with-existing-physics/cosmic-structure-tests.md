# Tests of Cosmic Structure

A theory whose object is a cosmos as a cosmos should be tested by structural relations among independently accessible aspects of the cosmic arena: scale-age, proper-time history, causal size, entropy, temperature, gravitational response, and the organization of records. The central discipline is to distinguish genuinely overdetermined relations from identities created by defining several quantities from the same measured input.

## Type the quantities before relating them

The words “age,” “entropy,” “temperature,” “size,” and “strength of gravity” each cover several inequivalent objects.

The programme's strength is its insistence that these registers require explicit maps. Its empirical case weakens whenever two of them are silently renamed as one variable.

## Scale-age and proper-time age

Scale-age is

$$
N:=\ln\frac{a}{a_*},
$$

not elapsed seconds. A background history relates it to proper time through

$$
\frac{\mathrm dN}{\mathrm d\tau}=H(N),
\qquad
t_0-t_*=\int_{N_*}^{N_0}\frac{\mathrm dN}{H(N)}.
$$

A rigid prediction for $H(N)$ therefore determines distances, lookback times, the age assigned to cosmic events, and the chronology of acceleration. These may be compared jointly with clocks, standard rulers and candles, recombination and nucleosynthesis epochs, and the ages of long-lived objects. Agreement of one distance curve is weaker than agreement of the whole chronology.

The horizon allocation developed in [[causal-scale-theory/horizon-clock|the horizon clock]] relates scale change to surface-gravity rapidity and horizon-entropy change under stated geometric assumptions. It is not independent evidence if every term is reconstructed from the same $H(N)$.

$$

$$

This is a powerful soldering identity among causal, thermodynamic, quantum, and gravitational units. ## Dimensionful constants are soldering data

The numerical values assigned to $c$, $G$, $\hbar$, and $k_B$ depend on units. A fundamental claim should therefore concern their structural roles or dimensionless combinations rather than a bare numerical coincidence.

- $c$ compares causal order with metric duration and length. Observable tests concern the equality of matter, light, and gravitational causal cones, dispersion, and dimensionless speed ratios.
- $\hbar$ converts quantum phase or modular generators into action and energy--time units.
- $k_B$ converts dimensionless information measures into thermodynamic entropy units.
- $G$ converts stress or information capacity into curvature, area, and gravitational energy scales.

The current horizon bridge uses $G$ in both the entropy and critical-density formulas; it does not derive $G$. A stronger future theory would relate the local Newton coupling, cosmological background coupling, growth/lensing response, gravitational-wave propagation, and entropy-area coefficient as independently accessible faces of one structure.

## Evidence must be layered

[[causal-scale-theory/empirical-status|The empirical-status ledger]] distinguishes four evidential levels:

1. symbolic identities;
2. numerical consequences of stated premises;
3. reproducible phenomenological likelihoods; and
4. first-principles calculations of the wall state and its capacity.

An identity cannot confirm its own premises. A fitted background cannot establish the microscopic meaning of its fitted parameters. Conversely, failure of the rigid background history is already empirically serious even before the wall construction is available.

The clean programme is therefore to combine independent determinations of expansion, age, distance, growth, lensing, temperature history, and primordial correlations; test the rigid global relations jointly; and separately calculate the state-geometric quantities that are proposed to ground them.
