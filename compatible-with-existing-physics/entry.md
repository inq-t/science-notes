# Compatibility With Existing Physics

The causal-scale programme is best judged as a proposed theory of the constitutive structure of a cosmos, not as a microscopic successor required to derive General Relativity, quantum field theory, or the Standard Model. Its intended relation to established physics is **surrounding**: GR and QFT supply tested local metric and quantum descriptions, while Causal Scale Dynamics and Causal-Wall Spectral Theory propose global scale, state, accessibility, correlation, and history relations by which those local descriptions belong to one arena of facts. The programme must preserve their empirical content wherever its domain overlaps theirs and must define the interface coherently, but a Newton-within-GR recovery theorem is required only if GR or QFT is claimed to emerge from the new theory rather than being taken as input.

## Compatibility is a typed relation

Several questions were compressed into the word “compatible” in the archived conversation. [[relations-among-theories]] separates them:

- **Non-contradiction:** the theories can be jointly stated without incompatible claims about the same observable in the same regime.
- **Conservative local restriction:** restricting the surrounding theory to an ordinary local causal context preserves the predictions of the imported local theory, within controlled errors.
- **Constitutive closure:** the new theory supplies a source, state, boundary condition, gluing law, or history law that the local theory accepts but does not itself select.
- **Recovery:** a controlled limit of the new theory produces the states, observables, equations, and predictions of the old theory.
- **Derivation:** the new theory explains why the old theory has its particular internal structure.

These relations do not form one ladder that every theory must climb. A theory can surround and constrain a local theory without deriving it. Conversely, sharing vocabulary or reproducing one fitted curve is not yet surrounding, embedding, or recovery.

## The division by object of inquiry

The relevant distinction is logical rather than a hierarchy of length scales:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{QFT and the Standard Model}
&:\quad \text{local observables, possibilities, correlations, and interactions},\\
\text{GR}
&:\quad \text{metric--causal representation and stress--geometry compatibility},\\
\text{cosmodynamics}
&:\quad \text{the constitution and history of the fact-bearing arena itself}.
\end{aligned}
$$

On this reading, QFT is not a provisional approximation that Causal Scale Dynamics must replace. It may be the correct local fiber theory. GR may remain the correct metric representation and compatibility law. The proposed new content is horizontal and global: which local states belong to one cosmos, how they compare across scale and horizons, how records acquire a common history, and which constitutive relations connect causal size, information capacity, temperature, entropy, and gravitational response.

The intended architecture is therefore schematically

$$
O\longmapsto (g_O,\mathcal A_O,\omega_O)
$$

for local causal contexts $O$, together with new comparison and gluing data over the family. The assignment of local fibers may be imported from GR plus QFT. The cosmodynamic claim concerns the structure that makes the family one cosmos rather than a disconnected inventory of local theories.

Within that architecture, CWST has a more specific role. It retypes the usual primordial “perturbation mechanism” question as a question about the correlation geometry of the whole cosmic state and its representation in local records. It need not reproduce the conventional local-event story for the origin of those correlations or derive the local field theories used in the transfer calculation. It must still produce the observed gauge-invariant correlation hierarchy, identify the local observables that carry it, and supply either the standard Einstein--Boltzmann descent or a comparably complete alternative. Equality of observable content may be required even when identity of mechanism is not.

## What must still be shown

Importing local physics is not a license to leave the joint theory undefined. [[local-physics-interface]] states the appropriate contract. In brief, the programme must:

1. identify the physical metric, causal domains, local observable algebras, and states being imported;
2. define the horizontal scale/state and overlap maps without introducing contradictory local dynamics;
3. preserve local causal propagation, conservation laws, gauge consistency, stable evolution, and established laboratory predictions in the regimes assigned to GR and QFT;
4. state where genuinely global corrections enter and how they are bounded or measured; and
5. distinguish a local theory taken as input from any later claim that the same theory has been derived.

This is a **restriction and preservation** obligation, not necessarily a derivation or reduction obligation. An effective-action decoupling limit is one possible implementation. An algebraic statement that every tested local restriction is equivalent to the established local net is another.

## Where the programme should be tested

The positive empirical case should not be built by repeating successful particle-physics calculations. Its natural tests concern relations that become visible only when a cosmos is considered as a history-bearing whole. [[cosmic-structure-tests]] separates the relevant registers and the danger of counting definitions as predictions.

The most characteristic targets include:

- the relation between scale-age $N$, proper-time age, and the expansion history $H(N)$;
- the relation between causal size, horizon entropy, modular or horizon temperature, and gravitational response;
- a dimensionless capacity law connecting state distinguishability to horizon entropy;
- the topology and timing of the acceleration history;
- correlations among the self-dual state, the response maximum, the $w=-1$ crossing, and the dark--ordinary density ratio; and
- the perturbation and record structure by which one global state appears through local metric and quantum observables.

Numerical relations involving $c$, $G$, $\hbar$, or $k_B$ are meaningful only after their types and units are fixed. The stronger targets are dimensionless invariants, coincidence relations among independently defined structures, and overdetermined predictions that cannot be recreated by redefining one fitted function.
