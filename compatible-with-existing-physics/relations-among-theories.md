# Relations Among Theories

Compatibility, recovery, and surrounding name different logical relations. Recovery is appropriate when a proposed theory claims to reproduce an older theory as a controlled approximation; surrounding is appropriate when established local theories are retained as inputs and a new theory supplies the global arena, comparison maps, or constitutive closure in which they jointly operate.

## Exact reformulation

An exact reformulation is an invertible change of variables,

$$
\mathcal T\;\cong\;\widetilde{\mathcal T},
$$

with no change in physical solution space or observable content. Writing a physical metric as a conformal class plus a positive scale section can have this status. The reformulation may expose a missing structural slot, but it is not evidence for any new law proposed to fill that slot.

## Domain compatibility

Two theories are domain-compatible when their claims agree, or at least do not conflict, on every observable for which both claim authority. If $D$ is the shared regime, then schematically

$$
\bigl|\operatorname{Pred}_{1}(O)-\operatorname{Pred}_{2}(O)\bigr|
\leq \varepsilon_D(O)
$$

for the tested observables $O$, with $\varepsilon_D$ below the relevant empirical and theoretical tolerance.

This is stronger than verbal coexistence and weaker than an embedding or reduction. It does not say why the agreement holds.

## Conservative extension and local restriction

A surrounding theory is conservative over an imported local theory when the new global structure adds no contrary local consequences inside the old theory's declared domain. If $\mathfrak C$ denotes the cosmodynamic structure and $O$ a tested local causal context, the desired form is

$$
\operatorname{Res}_{O}(\mathfrak C)
\simeq
(g_O,\mathcal A_O,\omega_O)_{\mathrm{GR+QFT}}.
$$

The equivalence can be an equality of effective equations, an equivalence of local observable nets and states, or a controlled approximation with explicit corrections. Because the local fiber is input, this is not a derivation of it. The work lies in showing that the global gluing does not make the imported fibers inconsistent.

## Constitutive closure

A constitutive closure supplies data that an existing dynamics uses but does not determine by itself. Examples include

- an equation of state accepted by a conservation law;
- a global source law accepted by Einstein's equation;
- a state or boundary condition accepted by local QFT;
- a correlation law represented as primordial data in an FLRW reconstruction; or
- a horizontal comparison law among local states.

The explanatory test is independence. A closure reconstructed backward from the desired solution is a redescription. A closure specified from the structure of causal scale and then yielding several linked consequences is a new physical principle, even if no microscopic derivation is yet known.

## Surrounding

Surrounding is a relation of logical role. A surrounding theory does not put the old theory inside a larger spatial container. It supplies the base, gluing, admissibility, or whole-state structure over a family of local descriptions:

$$
\begin{array}{c}
\{(g_O,\mathcal A_O,\omega_O)\}_{O\in\mathcal C}
\\[3pt]
\text{local metric and quantum fibers}
\end{array}
\qquad
\xleftarrow{\quad\text{restriction and gluing}\quad}
\qquad
\mathfrak C_{\mathrm{cosmos}}.
$$

The new object concerns why the fibers constitute one causal, metric, accessible, and history-bearing whole. Its native laws may relate quantities that no single local fiber treats as one object: causal scale, horizon capacity, state distinguishability, record orientation, and cosmic history.

Surrounding becomes empty rhetoric unless the domains, fibers, overlap maps, and new global constraints are specified. It becomes an emergence claim only if the fibers themselves are said to follow from the surrounding structure.

## Reduction and recovery

A reduction is a controlled limiting map from a new theory to an old one. For a parameter $\epsilon$,

$$
R_\epsilon(\mathcal T_{\mathrm{new}})
\longrightarrow
\mathcal T_{\mathrm{old}}
\qquad (\epsilon\to0)
$$

must carry the relevant states, observables, equations, and dynamics to their old-theory counterparts with controlled errors. Recovery adds physical interpretation and empirical completeness in the validated old domain.

General Relativity has a genuine Newtonian limit: suitable families of relativistic solutions converge, under weak-field and slow-motion conditions, to Newtonian gravitational dynamics. [[library/ehlers-newtonian-limit/entry|The Ehlers frame-theory literature]] makes this a precise relation. Thus “Newton inside GR” is technically stronger than “GR built on Newton.”

The closer analogy for Causal Scale Dynamics may be GR's relation to matter theories. GR does not derive the electromagnetic field, the Standard Model gauge group, or the particle spectrum. It supplies a metric--causal arena and a universal gravitational compatibility law in which those matter theories can be formulated. Causal Scale Dynamics proposes a still broader constitutive arena for metric, state, scale, entropy, and history.

## When recovery becomes mandatory

The strong recovery test returns whenever the programme claims any of the following:

- GR, QFT, or the Standard Model **emerges** from more primitive causal-scale data;
- the Standard Model's gauge group or matter representations are **derived**;
- local quantum probabilities or scattering amplitudes are replaced by a new dynamics;
- the physical metric or local causal cones differ from those used by the imported theories; or
- global constraints induce observable corrections inside a domain previously assigned to GR or QFT.

Absent those claims, the correct questions are whether the local restriction is conservative, whether the surrounding maps exist, and whether the new global relations are independently predictive.
