# Cosmological Balance and the Vacuum Gap

The conjecture is that one deeper global–local law constrains both cosmological dark-response/matter balance and the positive spectral threshold of a recovered local gauge vacuum. These would be distinct presentations of one structural invariant, not equal energies, reciprocal numbers, or interchangeable meanings of “mass.” The proposed relationship is quarantined from the premises of the existing mathematical gap results. No cosmological ratio, numerical grain, exponent or matching function is selected here.

## The portable method is a common intermediate object

The [[deriving-g-v2/the-g-free-first-law|G-free first law]] rewrites a supplied horizon's Clausius relation as \(q=\delta\iota\), with \(\iota=S/k_B\) and \(q=\delta Q/(k_BT)\). Its useful method is to distinguish a dimensionless ledger from the independently normalized maps presenting that ledger as heat or area. Its stated horizon, state, temperature and geometric premises remain necessary.

[[mass-scale-calibration/mass-and-g-as-dual-exchange-rates|The dual-exchange-rate analysis]] identifies the corresponding difficulty for mass: a first-order entropy differential and a positive projective transfer rate have different algebraic types. Sharing units does not identify them. A comparison needs either specified ledger paths for physical rays or a common quadratic-response carrier and a genuine form comparison. A static entropy balance does not select its dynamical rate.

The cosmological conjecture asks for a construction of the following form:

\[
\boxed{
I(\mathfrak U)
=\Phi_{\mathrm{cos}}\!\left(R_{\mathrm{cos}}(\mathfrak U)\right)
=\Phi_{\mathrm{YM}}\!\left(R_{\mathrm{YM}}(\mathfrak U)\right)
\in\mathfrak I.}
\tag{BV1}
\]

Here \(\mathfrak U\) is the proposed whole-law object, \(R_{\mathrm{cos}}\) and \(R_{\mathrm{YM}}\) are independently constructed cosmological and vacuum returns, and \(\Phi_{\mathrm{cos}},\Phi_{\mathrm{YM}}\) compare their outputs in one declared invariant type \(\mathfrak I\). A normalized response form or transported cocycle class is a possible type; neither has been selected by this notation. The maps must specify states, domains, observable algebras and transformation laws. They are not automatically functors.

The cosmological output needs a history or selected cut, a defined matter/dark-response decomposition, and residual-sector data. The vacuum output needs its physical carrier, actual ground-state projection, translations and energy form. [[cosmological-reconvergence-contract|The reconvergence contract]] owns these two return requirements. Equation (BV1) adds the specific conjecture that their common structure also constrains cosmological balance. Both maps must be derived without using the output they are supposed to predict.

## A dimensionless relation comes only after calibration

Suppose a cosmological calibration \(\mathsf{Cal}_{\mathrm{cos}}\) has independently defined a dimensionless balance observable \(B_{\mathrm{cos}}\), including its epoch, state and sector convention. Suppose also that a vacuum reference energy \(E_*>0\) has been fixed independently of the target gap. For example, it could be an independently specified Yang–Mills transmutation scale in a declared renormalization convention.

Only then is a schematic relation of the following type meaningful:

\[
B_{\mathrm{cos}}
=\mathsf{Cal}_{\mathrm{cos}}\!\left(R_{\mathrm{cos}}(\mathfrak U)\right),
\qquad
\delta_{\mathrm{YM}}=\frac{\Delta_E}{E_*},
\qquad
\mathcal F_{\mathfrak I}(B_{\mathrm{cos}},\delta_{\mathrm{YM}})=0.
\tag{BV2}
\]

The function \(\mathcal F_{\mathfrak I}\) is an unconstructed consequence demanded of (BV1), not a free function to fit. No density ratio or power law has been chosen for it. Defining \(E_*\) from the measured gap, adjusting the selected cosmic cut to match it, or absorbing disagreement into a new calibration would make the relation circular.

Constants such as \(c,G,k_B\), and any action-to-clock conversion can enter a recovered presentation with declared meanings. They do not themselves identify its carriers. [[cosmological-scale-selection/cosmological-selection-of-the-yang-mills-yardstick|Cosmological yardstick selection]] separately distinguishes selection of a dimensional member from proof that its dimensionless local gap coefficient is positive.

## Positivity has several different meanings here

A positive cosmological susceptibility is a statement about a response form. A positive dark-response density is a statement about one selected source component. Neither fixes the sign of a total cosmological constant or a residual vacuum sector. In [[causal-scale-theory/vacuum-residual-sector|the residual-sector analysis]], the schematic decomposition
\[
\rho_{\mathrm{tot}}=\rho_m+\rho_r+\rho_X+\rho_{\mathrm{res}}
\tag{BV3}
\]
leaves \(\rho_{\mathrm{res}}\) to a further global law; positivity of \(\rho_X\) does not choose it. Acceleration also requires the appropriate pressure and gravitational return, not merely positivity of one component.

There is an exact reason not to read that missing scalar sector from a centered local gap. [[general-causal-action/closed-normalization-and-cosmic-response|Closed normalization]] distinguishes an additive shift of the Hamiltonian from the centered spectrum and from variation of a gravitational source. The first leaves the excitation gap unchanged; the last can detect it. A law relating the gap to the residual sector therefore needs information absent from the centered spectrum alone.

Likewise, a positive scalar response is not a uniform spectral floor. Even a positive operator with \(Le_n=n^{-1}e_n\) has positive response on every nonzero vector but has infimum zero. The vacuum branch must instead prove a positive lower bound for its energy form on the entire nonvacuum physical carrier, uniformly through the required limits. One cosmological tangent, one integrated balance or one selected trace channel does not establish that quantifier.

Quark masses must also remain distinct from the pure Yang–Mills gap. Quark masses belong to a matter-containing theory and its specified normalization. The [[contemporary-puzzles/yang-mills-mass-gap/clay-contract-and-scale-assumptions|pure-gauge target]] contains no quarks or Higgs sector; its gap concerns gauge-invariant nonvacuum excitations. Connecting cosmic material loading to that target needs a theory-changing comparison, not a substitution of a quark mass or thermal transition scale.

## A positive balancing term can produce an unstable equilibrium

[[library/cosmological-considerations-in-the-general-theory-of-relativity/inq|Einstein's 1917 construction]] is a useful negative control. For the closed homogeneous dust model, let \(\rho\) be mass density, \(a\) the curvature radius and \(\Lambda\) fixed. The equilibrium conditions are
\[
\Lambda=\frac{4\pi G\rho_E}{c^2}=\frac1{a_E^2},\qquad
\frac{\ddot a}{a}=-\frac{4\pi G}{3}\rho+\frac{\Lambda c^2}{3}.
\tag{BV4}
\]
They relate the model's inputs; they do not select an absolute scale. Conserving dust mass and setting \(a=a_E(1+\varepsilon)\) gives \(\rho=\rho_E(1-3\varepsilon)+O(\varepsilon^2)\). Linearization yields
\[
\boxed{\ddot\varepsilon=\Lambda c^2\varepsilon.}
\tag{BV5}
\]
The positive balancing term therefore permits an unstable homogeneous mode. Its rate \(c\sqrt{\Lambda}\) is an instability rate, not a positive excitation-energy gap. Staticity, balance, stability and spectral rigidity require distinct operators.

This does not contradict [[library/asymptotic-behavior-of-homogeneous-cosmological-models-in-the-presence-of-a-positive-cosmological-constant/inq|Wald's expanding-cosmology theorem]], whose initial expansion, matter and curvature hypotheses define a different problem. Nor is the Einstein matter-filled static spacetime the same geometry as an expanding presentation of de Sitter space. A valid global/local comparison must specify the actual solution and its domain.

Einstein's Newtonian screening analogy must also be distinguished from the weak-field cosmological term. In the weak static spherical field, the metric coefficient \(g_{tt}=-(1+2\Phi/c^2)\) gives
\[
\Phi(r)=-\frac{GM}{r}-\frac{\Lambda c^2r^2}{6},
\qquad
\nabla^2\Phi=4\pi G\rho-\Lambda c^2.
\tag{BV6}
\]
This has a constant source contribution, not the screened operator \((\nabla^2-\lambda)\Phi\). The latter's Yukawa scale cannot be read as a graviton mass or Yang–Mills gap supplied by \(\Lambda\).

The related [[general-causal-action/preparation-compression-and-the-returned-potential|preparation-compression witness]] makes the dynamical distinction inside the algebraic programme: its returned positive potential raises the raw ground and excited levels, but initially lowers their difference. These examples support seeking one law that determines several outputs together; positivity of one output cannot replace the missing calculation of the others.

## The common law must return a self-contained local sector

The pure Yang–Mills target has no independent cosmological or gravitational coupling. This does not exclude a more comprehensive upstream origin. It requires the proposed origin to recover the target rather than replace it with a different gravity-coupled problem.

One possibility is that cosmology selects the realized scale member while the local dimensionless gap remains intrinsic to the recovered theory. A stronger coupling proposal needs an actual reducing sector or controlled decoupling construction: its observable algebra, state, translations, ground-state projection and energy form must return pure Yang–Mills, with a positive dimensionless floor in fixed local normalization. A cosmic horizon cannot simply be imposed as an external confinement box. The reconvergence contract states the corresponding decoupling obligations.

The nearest substantive test is one specified candidate for (BV1): construct both returns and their comparison maps, freeze their calibrations, and show that the common invariant constrains a second output not used to choose the first. A successful regulated example would support the method, not complete the continuum theorem. Until such a construction exists, cosmological balance is a research clue and a proposed additional explanatory return—not an established premise, a numerical predictor, or a substitute for the local vacuum-gap proof.
