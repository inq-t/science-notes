# Sources for Complex Presentation Without Polarization

The conditional \(S^6\) manuscript supplies the motivating geometry; established primary sources supply the complex-analytic, quantum-representational, and BKM distinctions. The project interpretation connecting those structures is not asserted by any cited author.

## Conditional local source

- *A compact complex threefold fibred by tori over the projective line, and the six-sphere*, anonymous 108-page manuscript archived as the [[library/complex-structure-on-s6/s6.pdf|local source PDF]] and summarized in [[library/complex-structure-on-s6/inq|the library module]]. It claims the complex-threefold construction and computes the signature-\((1,1)\) Hodge form in Remark 3.23. Its lack of attribution, review status, and conflict with published work are recorded in [[algebra/s6-manuscript-branch|the source audit]].

## Primary mathematical sources

- A. Newlander and L. Nirenberg, [*Complex Analytic Coordinates in Almost Complex Manifolds*](https://doi.org/10.2307/1970051), *Annals of Mathematics* 65 (1957), 391--404. This is the integrability-to-complex-coordinates theorem used to distinguish almost-complex from complex geometry.
- V. Bargmann, [*On a Hilbert Space of Analytic Functions and an Associated Integral Transform, Part I*](https://doi.org/10.1002/cpa.3160140303), *Communications on Pure and Applied Mathematics* 14 (1961), 187--214. This is the primary holomorphic Hilbert-space model used in [[holomorphic-wavefunctions]].
- B. Kostant, [*Quantization and Unitary Representations*](https://doi.org/10.1007/BFb0079068), in *Lectures in Modern Analysis and Applications III*, Lecture Notes in Mathematics 170 (1970), 87--208. This supplies the prequantum-line and polarization setting; it does not quantize the conditional \(S^6\) construction.
- E. Witten, [*Analytic Continuation of Chern--Simons Theory*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1001.2933), arXiv:1001.2933. This is a primary physical example of complexified integration cycles and Picard--Lefschetz theory, not a source for the \(S^6\) claim.
- M. R. Grasselli and R. F. Streater, [[library/uniqueness-of-chentsov-metric-quantum-information-geometry/inq|*On the Uniqueness of the Chentsov Metric in Quantum Information Geometry*]], *Infinite Dimensional Analysis, Quantum Probability and Related Topics* 4 (2001), 173--182. This supplies the finite-dimensional BKM score and density-tangent framework used to type the positive state geometry.
- F. Campana, J.-P. Demailly, and T. Peternell, [*The Algebraic Dimension of Compact Complex Threefolds with Vanishing Second Betti Number*](https://doi.org/10.1112/S0010437X19007802), *Compositio Mathematica* 156 (2020), 679--696. Its Corollary 2.3 is the published result with which the anonymous manuscript explicitly conflicts.
