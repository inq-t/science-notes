# Yang–Mills Scale and Gravity Decoupling

A pure Yang–Mills gap must be positive relative to its independently fixed renormalized gauge scale. Dimensional transmutation explains a possible scale parameter but does not prove the gap coefficient positive. A construction using gravity must additionally recover the pure-gauge carrier and hold that gauge scale fixed while gravity decouples.

## A renormalized scale is not a gap proof

[[library/trace-and-dilatation-anomalies-in-gauge-theories/inq|Collins, Duncan, and Joglekar]] derive the beta-function contribution to the renormalized stress-tensor trace. Their pure-gauge term is \(\beta(g_R)N[F^2]/(2g_R)\) in physical on-shell matrix elements at nonzero insertion momentum; off-shell identities also require operator-mixing and equation-of-motion terms (Eqs. 3.19–3.21). This supplies a scale-anomaly precedent, not a nonperturbative existence or gap theorem.

In renormalized four-dimensional pure Yang--Mills, the nonzero beta function produces a flat-space trace or dilatation anomaly, so a fixed quantum theory is not expected to possess the [[dilation-covariant-spectra/inq|exact unitary dilation symmetry]]. The running coupling at a reference scale is traded for a scheme-labelled RG-invariant parameter \(\Lambda_{\mathrm{YM}}^{(\mathsf s)}\), taken here in an energy-valued convention. An inverse-length convention requires the factor \(\hbar c\). This still does not prove a gap: one must establish

$$
\Delta_E
=
\kappa_{\mathsf s}\Lambda_{\mathrm{YM}}^{(\mathsf s)},
\qquad
\kappa_{\mathsf s}>0,
$$

with the scheme dependence cancelling in the physical product. RG evolution is not itself a same-carrier unitary dilation group, and neither the anomaly nor asymptotic freedom proves \(\kappa_{\mathsf s}>0\).


The [[scale-torsor-and-the-global-local-gap-invariant#A one-scale family and its coefficient|one-scale family theorem]] makes the homogeneity and scheme-conversion statements precise. The [[gravitational-and-compton-lengths|gravitational and Compton identities]] translate a mass once it is given.

## Recover the pure-gauge theory

The [[contemporary-puzzles/yang-mills-mass-gap/clay-contract-and-scale-assumptions|Clay contract]] for pure Yang--Mills theory on Minkowski spacetime contains no dynamical \(G\); the requested gap belongs to that \(G\)-free theory. Any proposed embedding in a larger gravitational theory must therefore supply either an exact decoupled pure-Yang--Mills subtheory or observable net with the required carrier and dynamics, or a controlled limit

$$
G\longrightarrow0
$$

with its renormalized gauge scale held fixed and

$$
\alpha_G(m_{\mathrm{YM}})
=
\frac{Gm_{\mathrm{YM}}^2}{\hbar c}
\longrightarrow0.
$$

Here \(m_{\mathrm{YM}}\) is a fixed positive gauge reference mass, for example \(\Lambda_{\mathrm{YM}}^{(\mathsf s)}/c^2\), or a mass already obtained on the recovered carrier. It is not an unknown gap assumed positive as an input. Such a limit must recover the pure Yang--Mills vacuum representation, its translation generator and spectrum condition, its gauge-invariant local net or a reconstruction-complete set of correlation functions, its gauge identities, and its renormalized scale. With \(\lambda_{\mathrm{YM}}=\hbar c/\Lambda_{\mathrm{YM}}^{(\mathsf s)}\) fixed in the chosen scheme, the background curvature must also disappear in Yang--Mills units, for example through \(|R|\lambda_{\mathrm{YM}}^2\to0\) and \(|\Lambda_{\mathrm{cosmo}}|\lambda_{\mathrm{YM}}^2\to0\). If the larger theory has arbitrarily soft gravitons, its full gravity-plus-matter Hamiltonian remains gapless even when \(\alpha_G(m_{\mathrm{YM}})\to0\); the desired gap must therefore be proved on the recovered pure-gauge carrier, not on the full gravitational Hilbert space. Neither \(\ell_G\to0\) nor convergence of a few correlators is a recovery theorem. Thus \(G\) may translate a previously derived Yang--Mills gap into gravitational geometry, or fix an external unit convention in a larger theory, but it cannot be an indispensable premise of a proof of the Clay pure-gauge gap unless the larger construction also proves the required gravity-decoupling and scale-matching theorem.

The observable content of any claim that \(G,\hbar,c\), or a cosmic scale varies lies in dimensionless ratios such as \(\alpha_G(m)\), \(mc^2/\Lambda_{\mathrm{YM}}^{(E)}\) when \(\Lambda_{\mathrm{YM}}^{(E)}\) denotes an energy scale, or a clock-rate ratio. A simultaneous change of all dimensional yardsticks can be a change of units. A physical evolving-scale theory must specify the dynamical field or section, the reference standards, and a dimensionless prediction.
