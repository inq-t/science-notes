# Two Nats per E-Fold

The holographic acceptance number of [[deriving-g-v2/closure-family-and-kills|the closure note]] is the exponential of a log — and making the log's variable precise turns it into a growth law with the codimension as exponent, an exact tie to the deceleration parameter, and a conditional dimension selector. A second, independent counter — the Fisher volume of scale families — has the same hyperbolic shape. Both are stated with their typed limits.

## The ledger as exponential of resolution depth

Define the **resolution depth** of the apparent horizon,

$$
N_P:=\ln\frac{R_A}{\ell_P},
\qquad
R_A=\frac cH .
$$

Then the ledger is exactly

$$
\iota=\frac{A}{4\ell_P^2}=\pi e^{2N_P}:
$$

**two nats per e-fold of resolution depth** — the growth exponent is the codimension of the cut, the same 2 that runs through [[nilpotency-and-the-wall/dimension-drop-as-group-change|the wall module]]. Numerically $N_P=140.29$ today and $140.14$ at flat-$\Lambda$CDM matter–$\Lambda$ equality, $H=H_0\sqrt{2\Omega_\Lambda}\simeq78.9\,\mathrm{km\,s^{-1}\,Mpc^{-1}}$, giving $\iota=2.27\times10^{122}$ and $1.65\times10^{122}$ nats (receipts); at the programme's own crossing rate $H_c=83.1058$ of [[the-grain-of-causal-scale/inq|the grain module]] the values are $N_P=140.08$ and $\iota_c=1.49\times10^{122}$. Note the type: $N_P$ is *not* the scale e-fold $N=\ln a$ of [[cosmodynamics/scale-age|scale age]]. The exact relation between the two clocks is

$$
\boxed{\frac{\mathrm dN_P}{\mathrm dN}=1+q,
\qquad
\frac{\mathrm d\iota}{\iota}=2(1+q)\,\mathrm dN,}
$$

with $q$ the deceleration parameter (from $\dot H=-(1+q)H^2$): ledger growth per scale e-fold is $2(1+q)$ nats — $0.95$ today, $1.50$ at the crossing, $\to0$ in the de Sitter limit, where the horizon freezes and the ledger saturates. Bookkeeping, but exact, and it welds three registers in one line: area growth, deceleration, and nats.

## The conditional dimension selector

**[PROPOSED READING]** In a homogeneous negatively curved space $H^d$, boundary spheres grow as $e^{(d-1)R}$ in the radial coordinate. If resolution depth is read as a genuine radial coordinate — the tensor-network/MERA reading of "scale is a dimension" (Swingle, Phys. Rev. D **86**, 065007 (2012)) — then matching the ledger's exponent forces $d-1=2$: **the counting selects a three-dimensional carrier.** Typed honestly: the flat reading (sphere areas in ordinary 3-space also grow as $R^2$) makes the same arithmetic trivially, so the exponent's invariant content is "codimension two," and the *selector* reading is exactly as strong as the radial hypothesis. What the counting genuinely excludes is any homogeneous radial reading with $d\ne3$.

## The second counter: Fisher volume of scale families

**[STANDARD, exact]** The Fisher information geometry of a location-scale family is hyperbolic: for Gaussians, $\mathrm ds^2=(\mathrm d\mu^2+2\,\mathrm d\sigma^2)/\sigma^2$, constant curvature $-\tfrac12$; with $D$ location parameters and one scale, the model manifold is $H^{D+1}$ — for fields over three-space, **four-dimensional hyperbolic space**. Two typed remarks. First, this is the Levi-Civita face of the same family whose $\pm1$-connections are flat — dually flat *and* LC-hyperbolic are compatible statements about different connections, per [[basic-concepts/hessians/inq|the Hessian module]] and [[hessian-response-geometry/inq|the response geometry]]. Second, Fisher volume *counts distinguishable models* (Balasubramanian, Neural Comput. **9**, 349 (1997)): hyperbolic volume growth means the number of distinguishable states grows exponentially in scale-distance — the same counting shape as the ledger, obtained from statistics with no horizon anywhere. The $H^4$-for-three-space observation is filed as RHYME with the Euclidean signature stated; nothing Lorentzian is claimed.

## Failure conditions

The exact rearrangements die only with their inputs (flat FLRW apparent-horizon kinematics). The selector dies with the radial hypothesis. The rhyme is killed if the wall's constructed state family fails to be location-scale type — in which case its Fisher geometry should be computed rather than assumed hyperbolic, and this note's second counter retires.
