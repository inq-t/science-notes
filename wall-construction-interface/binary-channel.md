# The Binary Channel Obligation

Reducing a wall's horizontal response to a single two-outcome generator is an identification, and it carries four separable debts: that the reduction exists as a specified channel, that the geometric reflection exchanging the two null orientations is not silently the Tomita conjugation, that the reference weights are balanced, and that the discarded modes are suppressed. The exact information geometry that follows once these are granted is not restated here; this note isolates what must be proved before it may be invoked.

## What the two orientations give, and what they do not

A spacelike codimension-two cut $\Sigma$ has normal bundle $N(\Sigma)=L_+\oplus L_-$ with two null-normal rays. Let $P_\pm$ be the spectral projections of a proposed orientation observable and set

$$
Q=P_+-P_-,
\qquad
Q^2=1 .
$$

Geometric normal reflection $J_{\rm refl}$ exchanges the rays,

$$
J_{\rm refl}QJ_{\rm refl}^{-1}=-Q .
$$

**[IDENTIFICATION]** The homogeneous, reflection-odd horizontal response is assumed to factor through the channel generated by $Q$.

Three inferences are *not* licensed by the above. Two null rays do not show that the full wall Hilbert space is two-dimensional; the microscopic algebra remains type III and the claim concerns an infrared sufficient statistic, not a dimension count. A two-dimensional Lorentzian normal plane does not carry a $1+1$-dimensional conformal field theory, so no Cardy argument follows from the normal geometry alone. And $Q^2=1$ does not enforce equal reference weights; see the balance obligation below.

## Reflection is not Tomita conjugation

Suppose $\mathcal A_N$ is a factor and $Q\in\mathcal A_N$ is noncentral. Tomita conjugation satisfies $J^{\rm TT}\mathcal A_NJ^{\rm TT}=\mathcal A_N'$, so

$$
J^{\rm TT}QJ^{\rm TT}\in\mathcal A_N' .
$$

If one additionally writes $J^{\rm TT}QJ^{\rm TT}=-Q$ with the **same** $Q\in\mathcal A_N$, then

$$
Q\in\mathcal A_N\cap\mathcal A_N'=\mathbb C\mathbf 1,
$$

contradicting noncentrality. The reflection relation as usually displayed is therefore a statement about $J_{\rm refl}$, not about $J^{\rm TT}$, and the two symbols must not be merged.

A doubled realization avoids the obstruction:

$$
Q_L\in\mathcal A_N,
\qquad
Q_R\in\mathcal A_N',
\qquad
J^{\rm TT}Q_LJ^{\rm TT}=-Q_R,
$$

together with an explicit geometric intertwiner relating left and right orientation data. **[OPEN]** Which realization is intended — a single noncentral $Q$ with a purely geometric reflection, or a doubled pair related by modular conjugation — is part of the missing construction, and the two have different consequences for what "self-duality" names.

## The channel must be specified, not gestured at

The reduction should be a physically specified map

$$
\mathcal E_N:\mathcal A_N\longrightarrow\mathcal B_N
$$

whose reflection-odd homogeneous part is generated by $Q$. The candidates for $\mathcal B_N$ are not interchangeable:

| Reading | What it requires | What it would license |
|---|---|---|
| subalgebra | $\mathcal B_N\subset\mathcal A_N$ with $Q\in\mathcal B_N$ | restriction of states; no automatic normalization |
| quotient | a two-sided ideal to divide by | equivalence classes, not expectation values |
| sufficient statistic | a recovery map for the retained data | information-geometric collapse to the reduced family |
| conditional-expectation image | a normal faithful projection $\mathcal E_N^2=\mathcal E_N$ onto $\mathcal B_N$ | pullback of states with preserved normalization |
| effective code subspace | an isometry and a declared error tolerance | approximate reduction with a bounded discard |

The word "reduction" hides this choice. Only the conditional-expectation and sufficient-statistic readings straightforwardly transport a faithful state and its relative entropy; the others require separate argument that the reduced object still carries the information metric being differentiated.

**[OPEN]** For a type-III local algebra, existence of a normal conditional expectation onto a chosen subalgebra is a genuine constraint, not a formality. A construction that needs one must exhibit it.

## Balance is a separate premise

The familiar reduced formulas presuppose that the two outcomes carry equal reference weight. On a larger code subspace with degeneracies, the deformed state must be defined relative to a declared balanced reference $\rho_0$,

$$
\rho_\theta
=\frac{e^{\theta Q/2}\rho_0e^{\theta Q/2}}
{\operatorname{tr}\!\left(\rho_0e^{\theta Q}\right)},
\qquad
\operatorname{tr}(\rho_0P_+)
=\operatorname{tr}(\rho_0P_-)=\frac12 .
$$

For a reflection-invariant $\rho_0$ commuting with $Q$ the denominator is $\cosh\theta$ and the induced outcome law is the balanced binary law. If instead $\operatorname{tr}(\rho_0P_+)\ne\operatorname{tr}(\rho_0P_-)$, the log-partition function acquires a shift, the intrinsic center of the family moves away from $\theta=0$, and the event that the consumers call self-duality is no longer at the same place as the extremum they identify it with.

Therefore: **[OPEN]** either reflection symmetry is shown to *enforce* balance for the constructed $\rho_0$, or balance is admitted as an additional premise alongside the reduction itself. It is not a normalization convention.

## Where the exact algebra lives

Once a balanced binary channel is granted, the reduced exponential family, its dual coordinate, its BKM metric, the moment identity, the finite Fisher traversal, and the symmetrized relative entropy of reflected states are exact and are developed in [[causal-scale-master/binary-geometry|normal chirality and binary information geometry]]. The operator factorization generated by the same log-partition potential is exact in the same sense and is kept in [[causal-scale-master/witten-pair|the binary Witten–Darboux pair]].

Those results are not evidence for the reduction. They are consequences of it, and the recurrence of one $\operatorname{sech}^2$ profile across the metric, the zero-mode density, and the partner potential is one mathematical structure seen in several registers rather than several independent confirmations.

## Upgrade and failure

**Upgrade.** Derive a canonical conditional expectation or sufficient channel onto $Q$ from the wall algebra; show that reflection enforces equal reference weights; bound the contribution of the discarded reflection-odd modes; and exhibit which of the two reflection realizations obtains.

**Failure.** Additional reflection-odd modes remain unsuppressed, so the response is not rank one in the required sense; the channel is unbalanced, so the intrinsic center shifts; no normal conditional expectation exists; or the reduced state metric is not the binary information geometry assumed. A non-$\operatorname{sech}^2$ reduced metric falsifies the balanced binary reduction directly, and no fit downstream should be used to restore it.
