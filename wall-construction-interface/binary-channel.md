# The Binary Channel Obligation

Reducing a wall's horizontal response to a single two-outcome generator is an identification, and it carries five separable debts: that the reduction exists as a specified channel, that its two retained outcomes are exhaustive in the selected image, that the geometric reflection exchanging the null orientations is not silently the Tomita conjugation, that the reference weights are balanced, and that neutral or discarded modes are controlled. The exact information geometry that follows once these are granted is not restated here; this note isolates what must be proved before it may be invoked.

## What the two orientations give, and what they do not

A spacelike codimension-two cut $\Sigma$ has normal bundle $N(\Sigma)=L_+\oplus L_-$ with two null-normal rays. Let \(P_+\) and \(P_-\) be orthogonal spectral projections for two proposed orientation sectors in an ambient algebra and set

$$
Q=P_+-P_-,
\qquad
P_{\mathrm{or}}:=P_++P_-.
$$

Before binary exhaustivity has been proved, the correct ambient-algebra identity is

$$
\boxed{Q^2=P_{\mathrm{or}},}
\qquad
P_0:=\mathbf1-P_{\mathrm{or}}.
$$

The projection \(P_0\) collects every neutral or additional sector not seen by the proposed orientation statistic. A geometric normal reflection \(J_{\rm refl}\) may exchange \(P_+\) and \(P_-\) while preserving \(P_{\mathrm{or}}\), so that

$$
J_{\rm refl}QJ_{\rm refl}^{-1}=-Q .
$$

**[IDENTIFICATION]** The homogeneous, reflection-odd horizontal response is assumed to factor through a selected algebra \(\mathcal B_N\) generated by the two retained outcomes. Only after the construction proves

$$
P_++P_-=\mathbf1_{\mathcal B_N}
$$

may one conclude

$$
Q^2=\mathbf1_{\mathcal B_N}.
$$

Four inferences are *not* licensed by the normal geometry alone. Two null rays do not show that the full wall Hilbert space is two-dimensional; the microscopic algebra remains type III and the claim concerns an infrared sufficient statistic, not a dimension count. They do not show that \(P_0=0\): [[a2-ternary-response/inq|the \(A_2\) ternary test]] gives an exact finite model in which a neutral projection remains. A two-dimensional Lorentzian normal plane does not carry a \(1+1\)-dimensional conformal field theory, so no Cardy argument follows from the normal geometry alone. Finally, even the selected-algebra identity \(Q^2=\mathbf1_{\mathcal B_N}\) does not enforce equal reference weights; see the balance obligation below.

## Reflection is not Tomita conjugation

Suppose $\mathcal A_N$ is a factor and $Q\in\mathcal A_N$ is noncentral. Tomita conjugation satisfies $J^{\rm TT}\mathcal A_NJ^{\rm TT}=\mathcal A_N'$, so

$$
J^{\rm TT}QJ^{\rm TT}\in\mathcal A_N' .
$$

If one additionally writes $J^{\rm TT}QJ^{\rm TT}=-Q$ with the **same** $Q\in\mathcal A_N$, then

$$
Q\in\mathcal A_N\cap\mathcal A_N'=\mathbb C\mathbf 1,
$$

contradicting noncentrality. The reflection relation as usually displayed is therefore a statement about $J_{\rm refl}$, not about $J^{\rm TT}$, and the two symbols must not be merged.

A doubled realization avoids the obstruction:

$$
Q_L\in\mathcal A_N,
\qquad
Q_R\in\mathcal A_N',
\qquad
J^{\rm TT}Q_LJ^{\rm TT}=-Q_R,
$$

together with an explicit geometric intertwiner relating left and right orientation data. **[OPEN CONSTRUCTION]** Which realization is intended — a single noncentral $Q$ with a purely geometric reflection, or a doubled pair related by modular conjugation — is part of the missing construction, and the two have different consequences for what "self-duality" names.

## The channel must be specified, not gestured at

The reduction should be a physically specified map

$$
\mathcal E_N:\mathcal A_N\longrightarrow\mathcal B_N
$$

whose reflection-odd homogeneous part is generated by \(Q\). If \(Q\) is first defined in \(\mathcal A_N\), the construction must say what happens to \(P_0\). If it is defined only after \(\mathcal E_N\), then \(Q^2=\mathbf1_{\mathcal B_N}\) is a property of the declared binary image rather than a consequence of the ambient normal bundle. The candidates for \(\mathcal B_N\) are not interchangeable:

| Reading | What it requires | What it would license |
|---|---|---|
| subalgebra | $\mathcal B_N\subset\mathcal A_N$ with $Q\in\mathcal B_N$ | restriction of states; no automatic normalization |
| quotient | a two-sided ideal to divide by | equivalence classes, not expectation values |
| sufficient statistic | a recovery map for the retained data | information-geometric collapse to the reduced family |
| conditional-expectation image | a normal faithful projection $\mathcal E_N^2=\mathcal E_N$ onto $\mathcal B_N$ | pullback of states with preserved normalization |
| conditioned corner | the corner \(P_{\mathrm{or}}\mathcal A_NP_{\mathrm{or}}\) and renormalization by the success probability | a selective binary family, not an everywhere deterministic channel |
| spectral decoupling | a family in which the \(P_0\) sector acquires a controlled diverging gap | a binary limiting family with a stated approximation bound |
| effective code subspace | an isometry and a declared error tolerance | approximate reduction with a bounded discard |

The word "reduction" hides this choice. The conditional-expectation and sufficient-statistic readings can transport normalization deterministically under their own hypotheses. Conditioning is selective, while spectral decoupling is a limit. The other readings require separate argument that the reduced object still carries the information metric being differentiated. Merely merging \(P_0\) with one oriented outcome does not generically return the balanced \(\operatorname{sech}^2\) family.

**[OPEN CONSTRUCTION]** For a type-III local algebra, existence of a normal conditional expectation onto a chosen subalgebra is a genuine constraint, not a formality. A construction that needs one must exhibit it.

## Balance is a separate premise

The familiar reduced formulas presuppose that the two exhaustive outcomes in \(\mathcal B_N\) carry equal reference weight. In a finite or trace-class representative, the state transported, pushed forward, or conditioned into that image must therefore be a declared normalized reference \(\rho_0^{\mathcal B}\),

$$
\rho_\theta
=\frac{e^{\theta Q/2}\rho_0^{\mathcal B}e^{\theta Q/2}}
{\operatorname{tr}_{\mathcal B}\!\left(\rho_0^{\mathcal B}e^{\theta Q}\right)},
\qquad
\operatorname{tr}_{\mathcal B}(\rho_0^{\mathcal B}P_+)
=\operatorname{tr}_{\mathcal B}(\rho_0^{\mathcal B}P_-)=\frac12 .
$$

For a reflection-invariant \(\rho_0^{\mathcal B}\) commuting with \(Q\), the denominator is \(\cosh\theta\) and the induced outcome law is the balanced binary law. If an ambient state has nonzero \(P_0\)-weight, conditioning it on \(P_{\mathrm{or}}\), gapping \(P_0\), or mapping it through a sufficient channel are different constructions and need not induce the same family. If instead the two retained weights are unequal, the log-partition function acquires a shift, the intrinsic center of the family moves away from \(\theta=0\), and the event that a binary consumer calls self-duality is no longer at the same place as the extremum it identifies with.

Therefore: **[OPEN CONSTRUCTION]** either reflection symmetry is shown to *enforce* balance for the constructed \(\rho_0^{\mathcal B}\), or balance is admitted as an additional premise alongside the reduction itself. It is not a normalization convention.

## Where the exact algebra lives

Once a balanced binary channel is granted, the reduced exponential family, its dual coordinate, its BKM metric, the moment identity, the finite Fisher traversal, and the symmetrized relative entropy of reflected states are exact and are developed in [[binary-information-geometry/inq|binary information geometry]]. The operator factorization generated by the same log-partition potential is isolated in [[binary-information-geometry/witten-darboux|the binary Witten–Darboux pair]].

Those results are not evidence for the reduction. They are consequences of it, and the recurrence of one $\operatorname{sech}^2$ profile across the metric, the zero-mode density, and the partner potential is one mathematical structure seen in several registers rather than several independent confirmations.

## Upgrade and failure

**Upgrade.** Derive a canonical conditional expectation, sufficient channel, controlled conditioning, or spectral-decoupling limit from the wall algebra; prove that the selected image has \(P_{\mathrm{or}}=\mathbf1_{\mathcal B_N}\); show that reflection enforces equal reference weights there; bound every neutral or discarded response; and exhibit which of the two reflection realizations obtains.

**Failure.** A neutral sector remains dynamically active; additional reflection-odd modes remain unsuppressed, so the response is not rank one in the required sense; the channel is unbalanced, so the intrinsic center shifts; the required expectation or sufficient channel does not exist; or the reduced state metric is not the binary information geometry assumed. A non-\(\operatorname{sech}^2\) reduced metric falsifies the balanced binary reduction directly, and no fit downstream should be used to restore it.
