# State Labels, Comparison Data, and Local Fields

A coordinate labeling a family of states, a coefficient returned by transporting one state to another, and a local spacetime field are different mathematical objects. A wall construction may relate them, but no identification follows from their sharing the symbol \(\theta\).

The three candidate types are

$$
\begin{array}{rcl}
\theta(N)&:&\text{a coordinate on a selected family of states},\\
\Theta(N_2:N_1)&:&\text{a relational coefficient returned by cross-fiber comparison},\\
\theta(p)&:&\text{a local field on a spacetime region or causal cut}.
\end{array}
$$

A flat comparison rule with controlled holonomy may allow

$$
\Theta(N_2:N_1)
=\theta(N_2)-\theta(N_1).
$$

Without that result, a state label is not yet an invariant comparison datum. [[cross-fiber-transport|The transport note]] owns the connection and holonomy obligations, while [[basic-concepts/soldering/affine-scale-state|the affine comparison theorem]] begins only after a scalar compositional coefficient has been obtained.

Neither of the first two types implies the third. Collective variables such as inverse temperature, chemical potential, and order parameters can label or compare states and influence a constitutive response without being fundamental canonical scalar fields. This analogy supplies a legitimate type, not an equation of motion.

A local-field realization would additionally require an action or constitutive system, field domain, gauge and constraint structure, kinetic operator, characteristic cone, and stress tensor. For CST that burden remains in [[causal-scale-theory/conjectures/covariant-response-sector|the covariant-response conjecture]]. The simplest positive-kinetic scalar realization is excluded by [[causal-scale-theory/no-gos/positive-kinetic-field-crossing|the crossing no-go]], but collective, constrained, nonlocal, multicomponent, and geometric completions remain open.

Finally, no version of \(\theta\) may be defined only by rearranging the solved cosmological history if it is meant to explain that history. [[elimination-test|The elimination test]] states the anti-circularity requirement.
