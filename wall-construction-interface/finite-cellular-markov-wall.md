# The Finite Cellular Markov Wall

A finite tensor-product wall gives an exact nonzero scale-indexed state family, coherent fiber transport, Connes cocycles, BKM response blocks, and a state-preserving readout without using measured gravity or a fitted history. Its infinite-cell limit also exposes the obstruction that the finite benchmark hides: distinct homogeneous product states become disjoint; the finite-volume response density remains finite while the global faithful-normal comparison fails.

## Cellular carrier and reversible fiber transport

Let \(\Sigma\) be a finite set of \(m\) cells. Choose

$$
q=q^*=q^{-1}\in M_2(\mathbb C),
\qquad
\operatorname{tr}_2(q)=0,
$$

where \(\operatorname{tr}_2=\operatorname{Tr}/2\). For \(O\subseteq\Sigma\), define

$$
\mathcal A(O)
=\bigotimes_{x\in O}M_2(\mathbb C)
\otimes\mathbf1_{\Sigma\setminus O}.
$$

This is an exact finite cellular net: inclusions give isotony and algebras on disjoint cell sets commute. It is not yet a relativistic local net. Let \(\mathcal A_N\) be a labelled copy of \(\mathcal A(\Sigma)\), with presentation isomorphism \(j_N:\mathcal A(\Sigma)\to\mathcal A_N\). Define

$$
\boxed{
\iota_{N_2:N_1}:=j_{N_2}j_{N_1}^{-1}.}
$$

These maps compose exactly and have zero holonomy. They compare carriers; they do not cause the state change below.

## A non-circular state-selection semigroup

Let

$$
P_0(a):=\operatorname{tr}_2(a)\mathbf1,
\qquad
C_t:=e^{-t}\operatorname{id}+(1-e^{-t})P_0
\quad(t\geq0).
$$

Because \(P_0^2=P_0\),

$$
C_{s+t}=C_sC_t.
$$

The maps are unital, completely positive, trace preserving, and self-adjoint for the trace pairing. Their tensor powers \(C_t^\Sigma:=\bigotimes_{x\in\Sigma}C_t\) form the corresponding cellular semigroup.

Choose only dimensionless initial data \(0<|r_*|<1\), take \(N\geq0\), and set

$$
r_N:=r_*e^{-N},
\qquad
\rho_N:=\frac12(\mathbf1+r_Nq),
\qquad
\rho_N^\Sigma:=\bigotimes_{x\in\Sigma}\rho_N.
$$

The faithful state on the labelled fiber is

$$
\omega_N(j_N(a)):=\operatorname{Tr}(\rho_N^\Sigma a).
$$

Self-duality of \(C_t^\Sigma\) gives the exact law

$$
\boxed{
\omega_{N+t}=\omega_N\circ C_t^\Sigma}
$$

after the labelled fibers have been identified by \(j_N\). Thus reversible presentation transport and irreversible state selection are different arrows. The law contains neither a measured \(G\), an expansion history, nor a target spectrum. Calling \(N\) logarithmic cosmic scale would nevertheless require an additional solder; here it is Markov depth.

## Relative modular data and BKM response

Put \(q_x=q\) in cell \(x\),

$$
Q_\Sigma:=\sum_{x\in\Sigma}q_x,
\qquad
\theta_N:=\operatorname{artanh}r_N.
$$

Then

$$
\rho_N^\Sigma
=\frac{e^{\theta_NQ_\Sigma}}{(2\cosh\theta_N)^m}.
$$

On Hilbert--Schmidt standard form,

$$
\Delta_N=L_{\rho_N^\Sigma}R_{(\rho_N^\Sigma)^{-1}},
\qquad
J(X)=X^*.
$$

After pulling both states to the reference copy, their Connes cocycle is

$$
\boxed{
[D\omega_{N_2}:D\omega_{N_1}]_s
=\exp\!\left{
is\left[
(\theta_{N_2}-\theta_{N_1})Q_\Sigma
-m\log\frac{2\cosh\theta_{N_2}}{2\cosh\theta_{N_1}}\,\mathbf1
\right]
\right}.}
$$

The noncentral comparison and the central normalization are therefore visibly distinct. Since

$$
\Psi_\Sigma(\theta)
:=m\log(2\cosh\theta),
\qquad
\Psi_\Sigma''(\theta_N)=m(1-r_N^2),
$$

and

$$
\theta_N'=-\frac{r_N}{1-r_N^2},
$$

the exact homogeneous BKM response is

$$
\boxed{
G^{(m)}_{NN}
=m(1-r_N^2)(\theta_N')^2
=m\frac{r_N^2}{1-r_N^2}>0.}
$$

This is **[EXACT FINITE MATHEMATICS]**. The equality between the log-partition Hessian and the BKM metric is the finite faithful-state theorem owned by [[basic-concepts/hessians/symmetrized-relative-entropy-hessian|the relative-entropy Hessian note]].

## Homogeneous and mean-zero blocks from one potential

Introduce dimensionless cell score parameters \(\zeta_x\) through

$$
\rho_{N,\zeta}
\propto
\exp\!\left[
\sum_x(\theta_N+\zeta_x)q_x
\right].
$$

At \(\zeta=0\), the common log-partition potential gives

$$
G_{\zeta\zeta}(f,g)
=(1-r_N^2)\sum_xf_xg_x,
$$

and

$$
G_{N\zeta}(f)
=-r_N\sum_xf_x.
$$

Hence the homogeneous direction is exactly orthogonal to every mean-zero score \(\sum_xf_x=0\). The extensive homogeneous response defines the finite additive set function

$$
\mu_N(U)
:=|U|\frac{r_N^2}{1-r_N^2}
$$

on cell subsets \(U\subseteq\Sigma\). This is a response measure relative to counting measure, not a derived geometric area.

## Exact readout balance

Let \(\mathcal B\) be the diagonal maximal abelian algebra generated by the \(q_x\), and let

$$
E_q
:=\bigotimes_x\frac12(\operatorname{id}+\operatorname{Ad}q_x)
:\mathcal A(\Sigma)\to\mathcal B.

Then \(E_q\) is a faithful state-preserving conditional expectation for every \(\omega_N\). The horizontal tangent and all \(\zeta_x\) tangents above are retained by \(E_q\); off-diagonal tangents form the lost block in the exact BKM Pythagorean split of [[spectral-wall-descent/conditional-expectation-balance|conditional-expectation balance]]. ## It does not manufacture the desired binary curve

The benchmark's selected response is

$$
G^{(1)}_{NN}=\frac{r_*^2e^{-2N}}{1-r_*^2e^{-2N}},
$$

not the \(\operatorname{sech}^2N\) curve of [[binary-information-geometry/entry|the balanced binary exponential family]]. This difference is informative. A time-homogeneous two-state Markov generator forces the polarization to obey an affine equation

$$
m'=c-\lambda m,
$$

whereas \(m=\tanh N\) obeys \(m'=1-m^2\). The latter may be imposed by exponential tilting, a time-dependent channel, or postselection, but the required instrument or rate law is then additional interface data. Exact binary information geometry and homogeneous CP descent are not the same construction.

## The thermodynamic obstruction

The finite systems embed into the quasi-local UHF algebra by \(a\mapsto a\otimes\mathbf1\); the product states and channels are compatible with these embeddings. For \(N_1\neq N_2\), however, the corresponding homogeneous infinite-product states are disjoint rather than quasi-equivalent. Their central supports in the universal bidual are orthogonal; there is no common factorial folium in which both are faithful normal, cross-state relative entropy is infinite, and the faithful unitary Connes cocycle needed for a global W0 comparison is unavailable.

Local finite-volume cocycles remain valid, and the thermodynamic response per cell is exactly

$$
\lim_{m\to\infty}\frac1mG^{(m)}_{NN}
=\frac{r_N^2}{1-r_N^2}.
$$

Thus the benchmark locates a real continuum requirement: a physical wall needs a local response-density or renormalized-relative-entropy construction rather than an unqualified global product-state cocycle.
