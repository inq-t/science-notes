# Higher Relative-Entropy Derivatives Are Not Cumulants

The coincidence Hessian of relative entropy is an information metric, but its third and higher derivatives are Bregman combinations rather than the connected cumulants generated by a log-partition functional. This no-go already appears in a one-dimensional commuting exponential family, so neither commutativity nor symmetrization repairs the identification.

## A classical calculation

Let

$$
p_\theta(x)
=\exp\!\bigl(\theta T(x)-\psi(\theta)\bigr)p_0(x)
$$

be a regular one-parameter exponential family, with the reference at \(\theta=0\). Derivatives of the log-partition function are connected cumulants in the reference state:

$$
\kappa_n=\psi^{(n)}(0).
$$

The two directed relative entropies are

$$
D(p_\theta\Vert p_0)
=\theta\psi'(\theta)-\psi(\theta)+\psi(0),
$$

$$
D(p_0\Vert p_\theta)
=\psi(\theta)-\psi(0)-\theta\psi'(0).
$$

Direct differentiation gives

$$
\left.\frac{\mathrm d^2}{\mathrm d\theta^2}
D(p_\theta\Vert p_0)\right|_0
=\psi''(0),
$$

but

$$
\left.\frac{\mathrm d^3}{\mathrm d\theta^3}
D(p_\theta\Vert p_0)\right|_0
=2\psi'''(0),
$$

whereas the reverse orientation gives

$$
\left.\frac{\mathrm d^3}{\mathrm d\theta^3}
D(p_0\Vert p_\theta)\right|_0
=\psi'''(0).
$$

For the symmetrized divergence

$$
\mathscr J(\theta)
=D(p_\theta\Vert p_0)+D(p_0\Vert p_\theta)
=\theta\bigl(\psi'(\theta)-\psi'(0)\bigr),
$$

one obtains

$$
\boxed{
\mathscr J''(0)=2\psi''(0),
\qquad
\mathscr J'''(0)=3\psi'''(0).}
$$

Thus the second derivative is twice the Fisher metric, as stated in [[basic-concepts/hessians/symmetrized-relative-entropy-hessian|the symmetrized-Hessian theorem]], while the third derivative is not the third cumulant. Higher orders contain their own order-dependent Bregman coefficients and, for multiparameter families, additional tensor symmetrizations.

This counterexample establishes **[NO-GO]**:

> Higher derivatives of a relative-entropy divergence cannot be identified with source cumulants merely because the coincidence Hessian equals an information metric.

## Quantum and field-theoretic strengthening

In a noncommutative exponential family, derivatives of \(\psi\) already carry Kubo--Mori orderings. Derivatives of relative entropy then contain further ordered combinations. Commutativity removes ordering ambiguity but, as the calculation above shows, does not remove the Bregman coefficients.

In field theory, three additional hierarchies must be separated:

1. derivatives of \(W[J]=\log Z[J]\), which generate connected source correlators;
2. derivatives of a relative-entropy functional, which compare a state pair; and
3. derivatives of the Legendre effective action \(\Gamma[\phi]\), which are 1PI vertices.

Connected correlators beyond second order are obtained from 1PI vertices by propagator contractions and exchange diagrams. They are not raw higher derivatives of \(\Gamma\), and neither hierarchy is supplied by higher derivatives of \(D\).

## What an upgrade would require

A model may relate these hierarchies through additional structure: a declared source family, a wavefunctional or probability construction, a Legendre transform, analytic continuation, and the relevant Ward identities and semilocal terms. Such a map is a new theorem. It is not an extension of the Hessian identity by pattern recognition.

For cosmological applications, the controlled holographic three-point machinery in [[library/cosmological-3-point-correlators-from-holography/entry|McFadden--Skenderis]] and [[library/holography-for-inflation-using-conformal-perturbation-theory/entry|Bzowski--McFadden--Skenderis]] illustrates how much additional data the cubic step actually requires.
