# Open Problems

The programme becomes a theory of scale as a modular observable only when the state-space construction is made independently of the cosmology it predicts and when its homogeneous response is lifted to a consistent spacetime system.

## Construct the observable

1. Specify the FLRW causal region or codimension-two cut associated with each $N$.
2. Construct the corresponding local algebras and faithful states. If the regions change with $N$, provide canonical inclusions or identifications rather than silently treating them as one fixed algebra.
3. Compute the Connes cocycle between the scale-indexed states.
4. Prove or falsify dependence on the scale ratio alone, identify any modular holonomy, and establish regularity in that ratio.
5. Extract $\theta(N)$ without using an already fitted $H(z)$ or $w(z)$.

A successful calculation would turn the architecture in [[observable-map]] into an actual observable map. A non-binary active quotient, nonlinear $\theta(N)$, or uncontrolled path dependence would falsify the current minimal form.

## Justify the reduction and normalization

- Show why the homogeneous reflection-odd tangent factors through the two null-normal chiralities while transverse and other algebraic sectors decouple.
- Decide whether unit slope is a fundamental representation postulate, an emergent result, or a parameter to be measured. Conformal-weight integrality cannot do this work.
- Derive or falsify the weak integrated crossing law

  $$
  \mathfrak R_c
  =\frac{k_B}{S_c}G_{NN}^{\perp}(N_c)=1.
  $$

- Derive the appropriate horizontal temperature for a dynamical FLRW wall and keep it distinct from the vertical Kodama--Hayward temperature.

## Build the response tensor

The scalar history $\rho_X(N)$ is not yet a covariant source. A completion needs a stress tensor or equivalent response object that

- reduces to the proposed density in homogeneous flat FLRW;
- is covariantly conserved or participates in a specified exchange law;
- has a regular pressure through $w_X=-1$;
- has well-defined scalar, vector, and tensor perturbations;
- is hyperbolic and stable; and
- introduces no arbitrary function that destroys the background model's rigidity.

This is the main gap between the homogeneous ansatz and cosmological dynamics.

## Lift the internal operator

The [[binary-information-geometry/witten-darboux|binary Witten pair]] must be connected to physical spacetime variables. One must identify its Hilbert space, inner product, gauge constraints, causal evolution parameter, and map to the response tensor. Reflectionlessness in $\theta$-space is not yet a statement about transmission of cosmological perturbations.

## Close the trace sector

Central shifts have zero length in normalized state geometry, and pure-trace sources drop from the trace-free Einstein equation. A complete account must still specify the scalar/global trace equation, the residual flux sector, radiative stability, and whether a nonzero residual is dynamically selected. The coasting future follows only on the zero-residual branch.

## Test the background and perturbations

- **Completed at the background-profile level:** [[causal-scale-theory/receipts/fit-generalized-background|the generalized CST-B2 receipt]] now releases \((\nu,\mathfrak R_c)\), profiles the admitted branch coordinate, and replaces the missing historical `P1/` parameter tables as the current calculation. Its canonical row uses the fully released 2025 likelihood. The unit point is compatible but not selected, and information criteria favor retaining the frozen unit member; the separate 2026 Ly\(\alpha\) row remains a provisional published-Gaussian reconstruction.
- [[causal-scale-theory/receipts/fit-calibrated-background|The calibrated receipt]] shows that retaining the local Cepheid scale requires an early ruler near \(136\ \mathrm{Mpc}\) and incurs conditional age pressure when the late-time ansatz is extrapolated beyond its fitted domain. Construct that ruler without importing the target \(H_0\), then test it jointly against BBN, stellar ages, and primary CMB data.
- Repeat the frozen and generalized comparisons when DESI releases the full 2026 Ly\(\alpha\) likelihood and cross-covariances, using current released supernova reductions rather than treating a transcribed two-distance block as final.
- Test the invariant

  $$
  9(1+w_X)^2+6w_X'=4
  $$

  as a correlated functional constraint, not by differentiating noisy point estimates naively.
- Implement the derived perturbation system in a Boltzmann solver before drawing conclusions from primary CMB, lensing, growth, or neutrino-mass fits.
- Freeze and test a genuinely different member of [[causal-scale-theory/response-family-interface|the response-family interface]]. Profiling \(\nu\) and \(\mathfrak R_c\) inside CST-B2 tests that member's shape and soldering parameters, not the selection of CST-B2 from the wider family.

## Completion criterion

The minimal programme is complete only if the observable map, the closure law, and a conserved perturbative response are independently specified and jointly reproduce the rigid homogeneous history. Until then, the exact binary identities remain useful modules, while the cosmology remains conditional.
