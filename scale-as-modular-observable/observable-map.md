# Observable Map

To call scale a modular observable is to posit a map from physical scale to quantum state space and to read its horizontal response intrinsically. The proposal needs more than a change of variables in an already chosen cosmological fluid.

## Scale and state coordinates

Let

$$
N:=\ln\frac{a}{a_0}
=-\ln\frac{\sigma}{\sigma_0}
$$

be the physical Weyl coordinate of flat FLRW, where $\sigma\propto a^{-1}$ is a scale section. A scale-indexed state family is a map

$$
\Phi:N\longmapsto\omega_N.
$$

The ordinary modular automorphism group $\sigma_s^{\omega_N}$ moves observables at fixed $(\mathcal A,\omega_N)$. By contrast, $\mathrm d\omega_N/\mathrm dN$ compares different states. The former is a vertical modular-flow direction; the latter is the horizontal direction relevant here. See [[causal-scale-theory/modular-flow]].

This distinction also prevents three parameters from being conflated:

- $N$ is logarithmic cosmological scale;
- $s$ is a modular-flow parameter at fixed state;
- $\theta$ is a coordinate along the proposed horizontal state family.

None of them is automatically proper time.

## Relative modular comparison

For two states associated with scales $\sigma_1$ and $\sigma_2$, relative modular theory supplies a Connes cocycle

$$
u_t(\sigma_2,\sigma_1)
:=[D\omega_{\sigma_2}:D\omega_{\sigma_1}]_t.
$$

Suppose, as additional structure, that its active noncentral quotient

1. is generated by one score $Q$;
2. depends on the two scales only through $r=\sigma_2/\sigma_1$;
3. obeys the cocycle composition law without extra noncentral holonomy; and
4. is measurable as a function of $r$.

Then the noncentral coefficient obeys

$$
\theta(r_1r_2)=\theta(r_1)+\theta(r_2),
$$

so

$$
\theta(r)=-\varrho_\perp\ln r,
\qquad
\theta=\varrho_\perp(N-N_c).
$$

The logarithmic form is a conditional theorem. Its slope is not fixed by the functional equation. Moreover, sigma-weak continuity of $u_t$ in the cocycle parameter $t$ does not by itself prove measurability with respect to the external scale ratio $r$; that regularity must be supplied or derived separately.

## Binary normal reduction

A codimension-two spacelike cut has two null-normal rays. The programme proposes that its active homogeneous, reflection-odd response factors through their chirality grade

$$
Q=P_+-P_-,
\qquad
Q^2=1.
$$

This gives the exact reduced binary geometry in [[causal-scale-theory/binary-geometry]]. It does not mean that the full local quantum field theory is a two-level system, nor does the two-dimensional normal vector space automatically carry a $1+1$-dimensional CFT.

Choosing

$$
\varrho_\perp=1
$$

identifies one Weyl e-fold with one unit of the fundamental normal character. This is a physical representation choice. Real conformal density weights allow real $\varrho_\perp$; they provide no integrality theorem that selects one.

## Elimination test

The construction is explanatory only if $\Phi$, or equivalently its cocycle and tangent, can be obtained without first inferring it from the expansion history it is meant to predict. A satisfactory completion therefore needs:

- a specified causal region or cut for each scale;
- algebras and faithful states, or canonical identifications between changing algebras;
- a demonstrated ratio-dependent relative cocycle;
- a derivation of its active binary quotient and regularity; and
- a calculation of $\theta(N)$ independent of $H(z)$ or an assumed $w(z)$.

The current programme states these ingredients but does not yet construct them for a dynamical FLRW state. It therefore passes the elimination test as a proposed architecture, not yet as an explicit calculation.
