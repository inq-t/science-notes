Causal Scale Dynamics v7.0 is a working, non-peer-reviewed research programme for a conditional homogeneous cosmology. It separates causal/conformal geometry from metric scale, models scale-indexed state change with horizontal modular information geometry, and closes the proposed late-time response with an explicit scale--capacity equivalence principle. This page maps the reusable pieces of the argument while keeping established mathematics, physical postulates, conditional deductions, and open work visibly distinct.

> [!warning] Scope
> The master closes a homogeneous background model only after several structural choices. It does **not** derive those choices from established mathematics, construct the required dynamical FLRW wall state, supply covariant perturbations, settle the global vacuum sector, or provide a full CMB/lensing/growth likelihood.

## Dependency structure

The argument is best read as a directed chain rather than as a single monograph:

1. Causal order leaves metric scale undetermined; scale-tractor calculus describes that missing calibration covariantly.
2. Vertical modular flow must be separated from horizontal change of a scale-indexed state family.
3. A proposed fundamental normal-chirality reduction produces a binary exponential family and its exact BKM shape.
4. A rank-one, ratio-dependent, measurable Connes cocycle makes the horizontal coordinate affine in logarithmic scale; choosing the fundamental character sets its slope to one.
5. The Scale--Capacity Equivalence Principle fixes the entropy-normalized peak squared BKM norm of the horizontal tangent; the modular free-energy source law converts BKM curvature into a proposed density; and the Hawking--Friedmann identity supplies physical units.
6. These inputs fix the density profile, and separate conservation then derives its equation-of-state history.
7. The same binary potential generates a Witten--Darboux pair, but no physical FLRW perturbation operator has yet been derived from it.

The conformal/tractor modules and several information-geometric modules remain meaningful even if the proposed cosmological closure fails.

The extracted notes use one convention throughout:

$$
N:=\ln\frac{a}{a_0},
\qquad
N_c:=\ln\frac{a_c}{a_0},
\qquad
x:=N-N_c=\ln\frac{a}{a_c}

Thus $N=0$ today and $x=0$ at the crossing. The horizontal state coordinate is $\theta=\varrho_\perp x$ in general and becomes $\theta=x$ only on the selected unit-slope branch.
