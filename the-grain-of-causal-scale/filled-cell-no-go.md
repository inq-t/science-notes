# The Causal Grain Is Not a Filled Particle Cell

The causal-grain length may be presented as a reduced-Compton mass scale, but it cannot simultaneously be interpreted as one ordinary particle of that mass occupying every grain volume. On the displayed CMB-conditioned branch, that literal reading exceeds the crossing critical energy density by about \(8.2\times10^{40}\). The exact contradiction forces a separation between a response or correlation cell, a particle pole, and a material energy density.

Using

$$
\lambda_*=4.264\,\mathrm{fm},
\qquad
m_*c^2=46.27\,\mathrm{MeV},
\tag{F1}
$$

the literal filled-cell energy density would be

$$
u_{\mathrm{cell}}
:=\frac{m_*c^2}{\lambda_*^3}
=9.5622\times10^{31}\,\mathrm{J\,m^{-3}}.
\tag{F2}
$$

For the separately supplied diagnostic crossing rate

$$
H_c=83.1058\,\mathrm{km\,s^{-1}\,Mpc^{-1}},
\tag{F3}
$$

the flat-Friedmann critical energy density is

$$
u_{\mathrm{crit},c}
=\frac{3H_c^2c^2}{8\pi G}
=1.16595\times10^{-9}\,\mathrm{J\,m^{-3}}.
\tag{F4}
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{
\frac{u_{\mathrm{cell}}}{u_{\mathrm{crit},c}}
=8.2012\times10^{40}.}
\tag{F5}
$$

Two material occupants per cell would double the mismatch. No uncertainty in the quoted cosmological inputs can repair forty orders of magnitude.

The contradiction does **not** invalidate the common-count identity. It invalidates a change of type:

$$
\text{effective response volume}
\not\equiv
\text{occupied particle volume}.
\tag{F6}
$$

A correlation length can be represented in the mass register by \(m_*:=\hbar/(c\lambda_*)\) without asserting that each correlation cell contains rest energy \(m_*c^2\). A genuine particle reading would require a pole or asymptotic sector, its residue and quantum numbers, an occupation law, and a stress-energy map. A genuine causal-grain reading instead requires the response carrier, count measure, and whole-to-local realization map.
