# A Quantum of Causal Realization

The proposed rigorous target for a “quantum of causality” is not a tiny piece of substance moving through pre-existing spacetime. It would be one oriented unit of positive realization at the wall: a construction in which a scale-free pre-wall translation passes to a different carrier and acquires a finite correlation pole with a center, linewidth, residue, and selection rules. The current programme has an exact rank-jump prototype and an exact conditional scale, but it does not yet have the operator correspondence that makes them one spectrum; compression or descent alone is not known to produce the pole.

## Facticity rather than a smallest voxel

The proposed grain is a candidate atom of **becoming factical**. Before the wall there is a family of possibilities and presentation symmetries. At the wall, one additional branch crosses into positive support and becomes available to a realized record. The atom is therefore an oriented change of support, not an indivisible metric cube.

This distinction is forced by the operator algebra. A type-III factor has no nonzero finite trace and no minimal projections. A “minimal packet” cannot be a smallest localized projection of the raw type-III algebra. Finite multiplicity can appear only after a corner, inclusion, crossed-product core, conditional expectation, or contextual descent supplies a different carrier.

The proposed terminology is:

| Term | Typed meaning |
|---|---|
| causal quantum | one oriented unit of positive spectral flow or boundary index |
| causal grain | the finite correlation length or pole created in the descended positive carrier |
| rank-two cell | the two-dimensional positive-support sector after the one-to-two jump |
| unit response | one nat-like unit of log-scale Fisher response per retained slot; not automatically thermodynamic entropy |

## The pre-wall no-gap theorem

For a half-sided modular inclusion, the Borchers--Araki--Zsidó structure gives

$$
\Delta^{it}U(a)\Delta^{-it}
=
U(e^{-2\pi t}a),
\qquad
U(a)=e^{iaP},
\qquad
P\ge0.
$$

Therefore

$$
\Delta^{it}P\Delta^{-it}
=
e^{-2\pi t}P.
$$

Unitary conjugation preserves spectrum, so

$$
\sigma(P)=e^{-2\pi t}\sigma(P)
\qquad
\text{for every }t\in\mathbb R.
$$

If \(P\ne0\), any one positive spectral value generates every positive value under scaling; closure adds zero. Hence

$$
\boxed{P=0\quad\text{or}\quad\sigma(P)=[0,\infty).}
$$

On a separable Hilbert space, \(P\) has no nonzero point eigenvalue: one such eigenvalue and the dilation covariance would produce uncountably many distinct, mutually orthogonal eigenvectors. Thus an isolated \(46\,\mathrm{MeV}\) “causal eigenvalue” cannot live in the unbroken translation generator.

This is a scoped conceptual gain. The particular HSMI translation generator above cannot carry a nonzero gap while its exact dilation law remains unbroken. A finite isolated scale in that same generator therefore requires failure of the covariance hypothesis, or passage to a different constructed carrier and generator. The theorem does not establish that every object called “pre-wall” is gapless or that a box, corner, threshold, boundary condition, record, or matter correlation automatically supplies a gap. ## The exact finite prototype: one branch becomes positive

Consider the trace-zero Schwarzschild--de Sitter cubic

$$
P_m(r)=r^3-L^2r+2mL^2.
$$

At \(m=0\), its ordered roots are

$$
(-L,0,L).
$$

The branch through zero satisfies

$$
\left.\frac{\mathrm dr_0}{\mathrm dm}\right|_{m=0}
=
-\left.\frac{\partial_mP_m}{\partial_rP_m}\right|_{(0,0)}
=2.
$$

Pass to an associative diagonal representative \(D_m\) of the ordered real frame and define the strict-positive support

$$
\Pi_m:=\mathbf1_{(0,\infty)}(D_m).
$$

Then

$$
\operatorname{Tr}\Pi_0=1,
\qquad
\operatorname{Tr}\Pi_m=2
\quad(m>0).
$$

After declaring the analytic extension to \(m<0\), the central branch crosses zero upward:

$$
\boxed{
\operatorname{Sf}(D_m;-\epsilon\le m\le\epsilon)=+1.
}
$$

For \(m>0\), the positive projector represents a rank-two class in \(K_0(\mathbb C^3)\), and the normalized trace is

$$
\tau_3(\Pi_m)=\frac13\operatorname{Tr}\Pi_m=\frac23.
$$

This is the clean mathematical prototype of “one packet becomes factical”:

$$
\boxed{

\longrightarrow
\text{two positive branches},

\Delta\operatorname{Tr}\Pi=+1.

$$

Two boundaries are essential. Physical Schwarzschild--de Sitter has \(m\ge0\), so without the declared negative-parameter extension this is a one-sided rank jump, not a spectral flow. And the \(A_2\) Nariai fold is an eigenvalue collision away from zero; it becomes a zero-crossing only after a spectral cut is independently chosen. ## Why the octonionic rhyme is serious but incomplete

On the unit imaginary octonions \(S^6\), the standard almost-complex structure satisfies, in the repository's convention,

$$
\boxed{
N_{J,u}(v,w)=2[u,v,w].
}
$$

Alternativity makes each two-generated context associative and gives \(J^2=-1\); the associator measures the obstruction to globally gluing all such local complex contexts. This supplies a precise philosophical picture:

$$
\text{globally nonassociative possibility}
\longrightarrow
\text{locally associative complex presentation}.
$$

Complex amplitudes can therefore belong to the local presentation by which the global structure becomes observable without requiring the global ontology itself to be an ordinary complex Hilbert space. [[the-grain-of-causal-scale/inbox/black-holes-as-jordan-spectra/black-holes-as-jordan-spectra.md|Black holes as Jordan spectra]] owns the octonionic calculation.

Two firewalls remain. The octonionic nearly Kähler structure on \(S^6\) is non-integrable, whereas an integrable complex structure on \(S^6\), if supplied by the manuscript studied elsewhere in the corpus, is a different structure and cannot also be Kähler because \(b_2(S^6)=0\). And \(\mathfrak h_3(\mathbb O)\) is an exceptional Euclidean Jordan algebra, not the self-adjoint part of an associative complex-Hilbert operator algebra. Its spectral decomposition is exact; its literal “wavefunction” and resonance dynamics are open constructions.

## What causal spectral lines would predict

A hydrogen-grade result must predict more than one numerically attractive center. It must specify:

- line centers \(E_n\);
- linewidths \(\Gamma_n\);
- residues or oscillator strengths;
- multiplicities and degeneracy splitting;
- selection rules, perhaps organized by the six oriented \(A_2\) root differences; and
- the physical instrument map by which a wall transition affects an observable.

The smooth \(\operatorname{sech}^2\) expansion history need not become a staircase. Hydrogen's stationary spectrum is seen through driven transitions; likewise, causal lines may appear in susceptibilities, ringdown poles, area-changing transitions, or controlled residual response rather than as discontinuous everyday expansion.

The clean susceptibility target is

$$
\chi_J(z)
:=
\langle J,(\mathcal D_{\mathrm{causal}}-z)^{-1}J\rangle,
$$

with presentation-invariant poles. A successful theory should recover the same dimensionless line ratios in at least two observational registers without retuning the center.

[[library/quanta-of-geometry/inq|Chamseddine--Connes--Mukhanov]] provide an important primary-source precedent: a higher Heisenberg relation and index formula quantize volume, area, and black-hole emission lines in their noncommutative-geometric construction. That result makes geometry spectroscopy mathematically respectable. It does not establish this programme's causal grain, rank-two projection, or crossing linewidth.

The most economical backward programme is:

1. construct the one-sided wall extension or self-adjoint Fredholm path;
2. prove the \(+1\) positive-support boundary class;
3. build a trace-preserving correspondence to the realized area projection;
4. construct the associative compressed operator and its resolvent;

7. derive selection rules and at least one higher-line ratio; and
8. only then use Baum--Connes assembly to test whether the constructed class is invariant under the relevant reversible presentation groupoid.

For the modular \(\mathbb R\)-action, Connes--Thom is already the closer theorem. For the one-sided wall, Toeplitz, Wiener--Hopf, relative K-theory, or Cuntz--Pimsner machinery is closer than bare Baum--Connes. Assembly transports a class after its groupoid and coefficient algebra exist; it does not create the wall or set a metric gap.

## Falsifiers

The proposal fails in its present form if any of the following occurs:

- the supposed isolated scale is assigned to the raw half-sided modular generator, contradicting dilation covariance;
- the pole disappears under the declared presentation or Morita equivalences;
- no common correspondence carries both rank and normalized trace to the physical wall;
- the line center is selected only by inserting measured \(G\) and then rediscovered;
- a fixed particle grain is imposed as a live all-epoch law and makes \(G\propto H\);
- the proposed spectrum cannot predict widths, residues, or higher ratios; or
- different observational registers require independent retuning.

The charitable philosophical conclusion is therefore exact enough to guide construction: **causality before the wall is scale-free; causal scale can become quantized only in positive, factive realization.**
