# The Grain of Causal Scale

The causal-scale grain is the positive correlation length at which the crossing's effective bulk-cell response and its areal ledger are one count. Conditional on the programme's unit-response and rank-two constructions, it obeys \(\lambda_*^3=(8/3)\ell_P^2R_c\) and appears in the mass register as \(46\)--\(47\,\mathrm{MeV}/c^2\). This is an exact UV/IR consequence of the proposed closure, not yet the discovery of a particle: either the crossing address or a microscopic correlation pole must still select the scale without reading it backward from measured \(G\).

## The meaning: one scale, many presentations

The grain is not first of all a small object located inside an already given spacetime. It is the scale at which two descriptions of one realized causal region agree:

$$
\text{areal response count}
=
\text{weighted bulk-cell count}.
$$

It is therefore better called a **common-count scale** than a particle mass. Once a length \(\lambda_*\) exists, [[cosmodynamics/soldering-constants|the soldering constants]] present it as a duration, frequency, energy, momentum, mass, or temperature. Those are different registers of one scale, not a list of new entities.

The stronger thought—that the grain is a quantum of causality, a minimal packet by which spacetime becomes factical—is developed in [[the-grain-of-causal-scale/causal-spectrum|a quantum of causal realization]]. Its disciplined version is not “a smallest spacetime voxel.” It is a one-sided spectral-flow event whose positive realization has a finite correlation scale. [[the-grain-of-causal-scale/material-realizations|Material realizations and Fisher response]] records two suggestive low-energy rhymes without promoting either one to an identification.

Repeated appearances of \(2\), \(2/3\), and a sixth power are not automatically the same theorem.

## The common-count lemma

Let

$$
N_{\mathrm{cells}}
:=
\frac{(4\pi/3)R_c^3}{\lambda_*^3}
$$

be the effective number of correlation volumes in the flat spherical bulk. Until a microscopic construction supplies actual cells and an integrality theorem, this is an effective dimensionless count—not necessarily an integer.

Assume **common-count saturation**:

$$
\iota_c
=
\gamma s_*N_{\mathrm{cells}}.
$$

Equating the bulk and areal presentations gives

$$
\frac{\pi R_c^2}{\ell_P^2}
=
\gamma s_*\frac{4\pi R_c^3}{3\lambda_*^3}.
$$

Hence

$$
\boxed{
\lambda_*^3
=
\frac{4\gamma s_*}{3}\ell_P^2R_c
=
4\zeta\ell_P^2R_c.
}
$$

This equation is the module's core lemma. It says that the grain is a mixed UV/IR geometric mean. It does **not** say that the cell is Planck-thin in two physical directions and Hubble-long in a third; such an anisotropic interpretation would require tensorial geometry not present in the scalar identity.

## Square root, cube root, sixth root

Write the crossing address as

$$
D:=\ln\iota_c.
$$

The areal presentation reads the address by a square root:

$$
\ell_P
=
\sqrt{\pi}\,R_c e^{-D/2}.
$$

The bulk presentation reads it by a cube root:

$$
\lambda_*
=
R_c\left(4\pi\zeta e^{-D}\right)^{1/3}.
$$

Define

$$
E_c:=\hbar H_c,
\qquad
E_P:=\frac{\hbar c}{\ell_P},
\qquad
E_*:=\frac{\hbar c}{\lambda_*}.
$$

Then

$$
\frac{E_P}{E_c}
=
\frac{e^{D/2}}{\sqrt\pi},
\qquad
\frac{E_*}{E_c}
=
\left(\frac{e^D}{4\pi\zeta}\right)^{1/3},
$$

and the hierarchy between the Planck and grain registers is a sixth root:

$$
\boxed{
\frac{m_P}{m_*}
=
\left(\frac{16\zeta^2e^D}{\pi}\right)^{1/6}.
}
$$

On the unit, rank-two branch this becomes

$$
\boxed{
\iota_c
=
\frac{9\pi}{64}
\left(\frac{m_P}{m_*}\right)^6
=
\left|
\frac{3\sqrt\pi}{8}
\left(\frac{m_P}{m_*}\right)^3
\right|^2.
}
$$

The last form makes a mathematically precise rhyme available to the programme: a cubic amplitude can acquire a sixth-power capacity under a Born square. It does not yet prove that the cubic norm of \(\mathfrak h_3(\mathbb O)\) is this amplitude. That identification belongs to the operator construction in the companion spectral note.

## The soldered registers

Define

$$
\tau_*:=\frac{\lambda_*}{c},
\qquad
\omega_*:=\tau_*^{-1},
\qquad
p_*:=\frac{\hbar}{\lambda_*},
\qquad
E_*:=\hbar\omega_*=p_*c=m_*c^2.
$$

Here \(c\) solders causal duration to metric length, while \(\hbar\) solders phase frequency to energy and inverse length to momentum. Their numerical values depend on units; their typed roles do not. The optional thermodynamic presentation

$$
T_*:=\frac{E_*}{k_B}
$$

does not by itself say that the grain is a constituent of a literal thermal bath.

On the unit, rank-two branch the common-count lemma is equivalently

$$
\boxed{
\tau_*^3=\frac83t_P^2H_c^{-1},
\qquad
E_*^3=\frac38E_P^2(\hbar H_c).
}
$$

This is the precise dimensional reduction: one cube of the intermediate scale is the product of two Planck factors and one cosmic factor. It is not dimensional analysis alone, because the coefficient follows from the conditional common-count normalization.

## Direction of explanation

There are three materially different achievements:

1. If \((D,H_c)\), \(s_*\), and \(\gamma\) are independently derived, then

   $$
   t_P
   =\frac{\sqrt\pi}{H_c}e^{-D/2},
   \qquad
   G
   =\frac{\pi c^5}{\hbar H_c^2}e^{-D},
   $$

   and the theory predicts \(\ell_P,G,\lambda_*\), and \(m_*\). The one-channel origin \(\iota_{\mathrm{birth}}=1\) fixes the zero of the ledger, but it does not by itself calculate the depth \(D\simeq281.3\).

2. If a microscopic construction independently selects \((m_*,s_*,\gamma)\) and the crossing selector supplies \(H_c\), then the closure predicts \(G,D\), and \(\iota_c\). This is the alternative backward route.

3. If measured \(G\) and \(H_c\) are both inserted, the calculation is an exact consistency diagnostic. Calling it a derivation would be circular.

This is the distinction between an end-state worth working backward from and a completed theory. ## A crossing value is not a running particle mass

The canonical closure is evaluated once at a presentation-natural crossing and pulled back as a flat scalar on the whole solution, as formalized by [[crossing-evaluated-flat-modulus/entry|the crossing-evaluated flat modulus]]. It does not assert

$$
G(N)=\frac{3\hbar^2H(N)}{8c\,m_*^3}
$$

at every epoch. That live law would make fixed \(m_*\) imply \(G\propto H\), in conflict with the intended Einstein fiber and with [[bianchi-protection-of-the-areal-modulus/entry|Bianchi protection]].

One may instead define a cut-dependent diagnostic resolution

$$
m_{\mathrm{cap}}(N)
:=
\left(\frac{3\hbar^2H(N)}{8cG}\right)^{1/3}.
$$

It is a presentation scale, not automatically a running Standard Model pole mass. Cosmic evolution in \(N\), modular or KMS flow, thermal observer dependence, and renormalization-group flow in \(\mu\) must remain distinct.

## What would make this a quantum of causality

The philosophical proposal becomes mathematical only if the programme constructs all of the following:

- a one-sided wall family with an integer positive-support jump or spectral-flow class;
- a correspondence carrying that class to the physical wall projection while preserving the relevant trace;
- a state--geometry map identifying the descended response with the areal ledger;
- a metric or resolvent construction selecting the finite pole or correlation scale \(m_*\);
- a flux or record law that fossilizes the crossing value without turning capacity into an undeclared conserved charge; and
- a spectrum with more content than one fitted center: widths, residues, multiplicities, selection rules, and higher-line ratios.

[[flux-record-and-top-form-realizations/entry|Flux, records, top forms, and assembly]] explains where Baum--Connes, Connes--Thom, and one-sided boundary maps can help. Assembly may transport a constructed class across presentation groupoids; it cannot supply the metric gap, \(V/A\), the absolute trace, or \(G\).
