- the wall expectation, OS quotient, and transfer semigroup cannot be related by an explicit intertwiner;
- the coercive estimate is not uniform in infinite volume and along continuum removal;
- another physical sector lies below the proposed scalar threshold;
- held-out ratios such as \(m_{2^{++}}/m_{0^{++}}\), \(m_{0^{++}}/\sqrt\sigma\), or the \(SU(n)\) dependence fail; or
- resolving the cosmological branch and one consistent scale-setting map moves \(m_{0^{++}}/E_*\) decisively away from thirty-six.

## Provisional verdict

The direct equation

$$
m_*=m_{0^{++}}
$$

was badly typed. It treated a causal common-count anchor as though it were already a particle or confinement correlation length. The more promising relation is

$$
\boxed{
\text{causal-scale anchor}
\times
\text{dimensionless physical coercivity}
=
\text{Hamiltonian gap}.
}
$$

The exact \(A_2\) weight supplies a non-arbitrary route to a dimensionless six, and the one-sided Witten construction shows that an explicitly chosen discriminant amplitude can turn it into a genuine threshold thirty-six. The integrable complex structure on \(S^6\) adds an exact predecessor: complex rank three produces a cubic determinant amplitude whose invariant square has weight six. It also exposes the strongest objection, because the standard Hilbert half-density then has weight three and threshold nine. The glueball arithmetic becomes a reason to decide this carrier fork and attempt the carrier-changing theorem, not permission to skip either.

The upside-down formulation is therefore viable in a precise sense: mass need not be imagined as a substance that later bends an independently existing space. A realized geometry and its translation representation can define the invariant called mass. But the decisive word is *realized*. Causal directedness, metric scale, quadratic capacity, physical time translation, and Poincare mass are still different structures. The research contribution would be the commutative diagram that makes them one construction.
