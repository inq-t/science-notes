# The Schrödinger Discipline

Positing a signature is a respectable founding act with a specific historical anatomy, and the anatomy is a contract. Schrödinger did not derive his equation; he selected a signature (first order in time, complex-linear, unitary-generating, one new constant) by correspondence and confirmed it by solving hydrogen *in the first paper*. The interpretation — what the wave function is, when outcomes obtain — was not settled by the posit and has not been settled since. Each clause has an exact analogue here, and together they say what "calling it a day" legitimately covers.

## The three disciplines

**Rigidity.** A good signature has no tunable functions: all freedom quarantined in the member. The pre-wall passes today — the core is canonical, both slopes forced, freedom confined to (pointing, $d$). The failure mode is *signature creep*: if consumers ever adjust the axioms per observation, the posit has died of the freedom-displacement disease [[program-core/explanatory-economy|explanatory economy]] names, and no amount of fit quality revives it.

**The Balmer requirement.** A posited signature earns its keep with a non-retrofitted number, delivered early. Care: the logistic $d$ was selected knowing the sech-squared target, exactly as the Coulomb potential predated 1926 — so the pulse itself is retrodiction, and legitimate as such. The honest Balmer candidates are the parameter-free $(w_0,w_a)$ tangent, the one-nat-per-channel reading with [[hyperbolic-counting/counting-rate-of-the-wall|its survivor table]], and the fossil $\dot G/G$ discriminant — run blind, per the promotion path of [[causal-scale-theory/empirical-status|CST empirical status]] and within the scopes of [[wall-construction-interface/elimination-test|the elimination tests]]. A signature without its Balmer line within a reasonable horizon is a definition, not a discovery.

**The Born-rule warning.** The posit will not close the record layer, and quantum mechanics' own century says this is not a defect — it is the boundary between law and facthood. The programme is unusually well-prepared for this: [[cosmodynamics/fact-record-history|fact, record, and history]] was built to hold exactly this remainder. What the discipline forbids is *counting the open interpretation against the signature* — and equally, *claiming the signature settled it*.

## What "calling it a day" covers

The law layer: yes — posit it, stop apologizing for not deriving the core, publish the axioms with their failure contract. The member layer: yes, permanently — $d$ and $\nu$ are empirical the way Hamiltonians are, and demanding their derivation from the signature is a category error. The correspondence layer: **no** — without items 1 and 3 the posit does not touch nature, and no day may be called. The interpretation layer: no, and never by this route — but that day was never this programme's to call by construction alone; it is the day the philosophy spine exists to work toward.
