# Octonionic Hopf Descent and the Complex Purification

The octonionic Hopf map connects the sphere-to-ball construction to an ordinary complex purification by an exact factorization. A selected complex unit identifies \(\mathbb O^2\) with \(\mathbb C^2\otimes\mathbb C^4\); taking its qubit partial trace is the same map as first taking the octonionic Hopf image in \(S^8\), then retaining a three-plane. The normalized spherical generators and pushed-forward measures follow this same diagram. Quadratic readout removes the parent's linear modes and raises the first visible spectral edge. This is a common realization of two previously separate presentations, not a physical mass calibration or a four-dimensional field theory.

## The square map has genuine fibers

Use the normed octonion multiplication fixed in [[octonionic-associator-and-branch-forgetting|the associator construction]]. For \(a,b\in\mathbb O\), put
\[
z=(a,b)\in S^{15},\qquad |a|^2+|b|^2=1,
\]
\[
\boxed{h(a,b)=(t,c):=(|a|^2-|b|^2,\;2a\bar b)\in S^8\subset\mathbb R\oplus\mathbb O.}
\tag{OH1}
\]
Norm composition proves
\[
t^2+|c|^2=(|a|^2+|b|^2)^2=1.
\]
Equivalently the rank-one Jordan matrix
\[
\begin{pmatrix}|a|^2&a\bar b\\ b\bar a&|b|^2\end{pmatrix}
\]
has trace one and vanishing determinant. Its idempotency follows inside the associative subalgebra generated by \(a,b\). The [[the-grain-of-causal-scale/inbox/de-sitter-box-and-the-octonionic-ladder/de-sitter-box-and-the-octonionic-ladder|preserved ladder packet]] records this Hopf clue; the argument here develops the maps and their response without importing that packet's cosmological identifications.

For a specified \((t,c)\in S^8\) with \(t<1\), set \(s=\sqrt{(1-t)/2}>0\). Every preimage is
\[
b=su,\qquad a=\frac{cu}{2s},\qquad |u|=1.
\tag{OH2}
\]
Indeed \((cu)\bar u=c\) by alternativity and \(|u|=1\), so \(2a\bar b=c\). Conversely \(a\bar b=c/2\) implies \(a=(cb)/(2|b|^2)\). The norms give \(|a|^2=(1+t)/2\). At \(t=1\), the fiber is \(\{(a,0):|a|=1\}\). Thus the map is onto and every fiber is a great \(S^7\), the intersection with a real eight-dimensional plane. The alternate chart using nonzero \(a\) covers the remaining pole.

These fibers are not orbits of an ordinary group of unit octonions acting by simultaneous right multiplication. In the declared Cayley–Dickson convention, take
\[
a=\frac35i,\qquad b=\frac45j,\qquad u=\ell.
\]
Then
\[
2a\bar b=-\frac{24}{25}k,\qquad
2(au)\overline{bu}=+\frac{24}{25}k.
\tag{OH3}
\]
The naive action changes the Hopf image. A parameterization of each fiber by \(S^7\) is not a principal \(S^7\)-group action. No claim is made that fibers therefore lack groupoid or homogeneous descriptions.

## Clifford composition computes the descended response

Let \(e_1,\ldots,e_7\) be orthonormal imaginary octonion units, and \(L_ea=ea\). Norm composition and alternativity give
\[
L_{e_i}^T=-L_{e_i},\qquad
L_{e_i}L_{e_j}+L_{e_j}L_{e_i}=-2\delta_{ij}I.
\tag{OH4}
\]
For the second identity, polarize \(L_e^2=-|e|^2I\) on imaginary elements. The product on the left is ordinary associative composition of real-linear maps, not a claim that octonion multiplication is associative. The related [[contemporary-puzzles/yang-mills-mass-gap/octonionic-clifford-completion-of-the-color-normal|color-normal Clifford construction]] uses this distinction on its own representation carrier.

On \(\mathbb O\oplus\mathbb O\), define nine symmetric matrices
\[
P_{\mathrm d}=\begin{pmatrix}I&0\\0&-I\end{pmatrix},\qquad
P_{\mathrm r}=\begin{pmatrix}0&I\\I&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
P_i=\begin{pmatrix}0&L_{e_i}\\-L_{e_i}&0\end{pmatrix}.
\]
They satisfy
\[
\operatorname{Tr}P_\alpha=0,\qquad
P_\alpha P_\beta+P_\beta P_\alpha=2\delta_{\alpha\beta}I.
\tag{OH5}
\]
The coordinates of \(h\) are precisely
\[
h_\alpha(z)=z^TP_\alpha z.
\]
For example \(\langle a\bar b,e_i\rangle=\langle a,e_i b\rangle\), so the corresponding coordinate is \(2\langle a,L_{e_i}b\rangle\).

Write \(\Delta_{15}\) for the nonpositive Laplacian of the unit \(S^{15}\). Each \(h_\alpha\) is the restriction of a homogeneous harmonic quadratic polynomial on \(\mathbb R^{16}\), hence
\[
\Delta_{15}h_\alpha=-32h_\alpha.
\]
Its spherical gradient is \(2(P_\alpha z-h_\alpha z)\), giving
\[
\nabla_Sh_\alpha\cdot\nabla_Sh_\beta
=4(\delta_{\alpha\beta}-h_\alpha h_\beta).
\tag{OH6}
\]
The chain rule therefore proves
\[
\boxed{\frac14\Delta_{15}(f\circ h)=(\Delta_8f)\circ h.}
\tag{OH7}
\]
One may check it using any smooth ambient extension of \(f\) near \(S^8\); both sides depend only on the restriction. The factor four is fixed by the quadratic map, not chosen after comparing two gap values.

## The measure and operator domain descend with the same map

Let \(\sigma_{15}\) be normalized round measure and \(\eta=h_*\sigma_{15}\). Equation (OH7) gives
\[
\int_{S^8}\Delta_8 f\,d\eta
=\frac14\int_{S^{15}}\Delta_{15}(f\circ h)\,d\sigma_{15}=0.
\]
Every nonconstant spherical harmonic has zero \(\eta\)-mean, because its Laplace eigenvalue is nonzero. Polynomials restricted to the sphere are finite harmonic sums and are dense in continuous functions. Consequently
\[
\boxed{h_*\sigma_{15}=\sigma_8.}
\tag{OH8}
\]
Thus \(U_hf=f\circ h\) is an isometry \(L^2(S^8,\sigma_8)\to L^2(S^{15},\sigma_{15})\).

If \(f\) is a spherical harmonic of degree \(j\) on \(S^8\), its pullback is an eigenfunction of \(-\Delta_{15}/4\) with eigenvalue \(j(j+7)\). These eigenfunctions span the closed pullback range. That range is therefore reducing for the self-adjoint parent generator, and the eigenfunction expansions transport its form and operator domains. In particular
\[
e^{t\Delta_{15}/4}U_h=U_he^{t\Delta_8},\qquad t\ge0.
\tag{OH9}
\]
No group action on the \(S^7\) fibers is required for this domain argument.

## A complex choice makes the same readout a partial trace

Choose a unit imaginary octonion \(e\). The real-linear operator
\[
J=L_e,\qquad J^2=-I,\qquad J^T=-J
\tag{OH10}
\]
makes \(\mathbb O\) a four-dimensional complex Hilbert space. Choose a complex orthonormal basis and express \(a,b\) as complex row vectors \(a_j,b_j\), \(1\le j\le4\). With these conventions,
\[
\sum_j a_j\bar b_j
=\langle a,b\rangle+i\langle a,Jb\rangle.
\]
Define the normalized amplitude and its row Gram matrix
\[
\Psi=\begin{pmatrix}a_1&\cdots&a_4\\b_1&\cdots&b_4\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
Q(\Psi)=\Psi\Psi^*=\rho.
\tag{OH11}
\]
This is the partial trace of a pure vector in \(\mathbb C^2\otimes\mathbb C^4\) over the second factor. It has trace one and is positive.

In the standard Pauli convention, \(\rho=(I+x\cdot\sigma)/2\) has upper-right entry \((x_1-ix_2)/2\). Hence
\[
\boxed{x=(\operatorname{Re}c,\;-\langle c,e\rangle,\;t)=q_e(h(a,b)).}
\tag{OH12}
\]
The minus sign is essential with this chosen Pauli basis. Thus the two-stage octonionic map and the complex row-Gram map are identical:
\[
\boxed{
S^{15}\subset\mathbb O^2
\ \xrightarrow{\ h\ }\ S^8
\ \xrightarrow{\ q_e\ }\ \overline B^3\cong\mathcal D_2,
\qquad Q=q_e\circ h.}
\tag{OH13}
\]
Here \(\mathcal D_2\) is the space of qubit density matrices.

The common parent is the amplitude sphere, not its selected complex projectivization. The final Gram map is invariant under a common complex phase, but \(h\) need not be. For \(e=i\), take \(a=3j/5\), \(b=4\ell/5\). The phase \(i\) acts as \((a,b)\mapsto(L_i a,L_i b)\); it changes the full Hopf coordinate \(c\) from \(-24j\ell/25\) to \(+24j\ell/25\), while leaving \(Q\) unchanged. Thus \(Q\) also factors through \(\mathbb{CP}^7\), but \(h\) does not generally do so. Octonionic fiber forgetting and complex global-phase forgetting are distinct quotients of the same parent.

The choices \(e\) are parameterized by \(S^6\). Equation (OH10) is a complex structure on the real eight-dimensional vector space \(\mathbb O\), not a complex structure on \(TS^6\). It neither assumes nor proves integrability of an almost-complex structure on that six-sphere.

Octonion automorphisms fixing \(e\) preserve (OH12). The [[contemporary-puzzles/yang-mills-mass-gap/octonionic-slice-groupoid-and-orientation-torsor|selected color stabilizer]] therefore acts vertically for this qubit readout. Its existence does not make the three qubit modes into color-field excitations; a nontrivial color response needs the appropriate retained carrier.

## Both routes return the same law and spectrum

Combining (OH7)–(OH13) with [[sphere-to-ball-descent-and-the-jacobi-response|sphere-to-ball descent]] yields
\[
Q_*\sigma_{15}
=\frac{105}{32\pi}(1-|x|^2)^2\,dx,
\]
\[
\frac14\Delta_{15}(f\circ Q)
=\left[(\delta_{ij}-x_ix_j)\partial_i\partial_jf-8x\cdot\nabla f\right]\circ Q.
\tag{OH14}
\]
The full retained law, not just its first moment or its lowest eigenvalue, is the same in both presentations. [[purification-descent-and-the-matrix-response|Matrix purification descent]] obtains this as \(d=2,K=4\) in a construction for every finite matrix size.

The parent and retained edges should not be conflated:
\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{gap}(-\Delta_{15}/4)=15/4,\qquad
\operatorname{gap}(-\Delta_8)=
\operatorname{gap}(-\mathcal L_{9,3})=8.}
\tag{OH15}
\]
All parent linear functions have zero fiber average, since \(h(z)=h(-z)\). Quadratic Hopf coordinates are the first nonconstant retained modes. Thus the higher visible edge is a consequence of the readout's admissible function space, with the same parent parameter. The last \(S^8\to\overline B^3\) step retains modes attaining eight. Neither step creates a gap from a gapless whole.

The total readout fiber over a faithful qubit state is also a \(U(4)\) orbit of purifications, with stabilizer \(U(2)\). Its real dimension is \(16-4=12\), consistent with the seven-dimensional Hopf fiber followed by the five-dimensional ball fiber. An ordinary unitary orbit description of the composite does not turn its intermediate octonionic fibers into a principal \(S^7\) action.

For bounded positive input densities, [[finite-entropy-cost-and-rank-changing-readout|conditional entropy loss]] composes through the same diagram:
\[
\mathcal L_Q^{\sigma_{15}}(f)
=\mathcal L_h^{\sigma_{15}}(f)
+\mathcal L_{q_e}^{\sigma_8}(g_h),
\qquad g_h=\mathbb E[f\mid h].
\tag{OH16}
\]
The second loss uses the actual processed density. This is a state-indexed balance of forgotten distinctions, not two charges that can be assigned independently of the input.

The construction supplies a common parent and exact comparison of the octonionic and complex realizations. It does not uniquely select that parent from the retained data, derive the round evolution from a directed primitive, or identify its parameter with physical time. The coordinates remain state parameters; nonassociativity, noninjective readout, entropy deficit and a particle mass are distinct structures.
