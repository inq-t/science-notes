# Interface to the Program Core

The present `program-core` is best understood as a causal-response realization downstream of the algebraic pre-core. Its quotient discipline, BKM geometry, finite conditional-expectation identities, fact/state distinction, Noether gap, and claim ledger remain valuable. What changes is their logical position: causal cuts, metric scale, complex associative operator algebras, states, transports, observables, and area are inputs to the response realization until the algebra-to-cosmos gates construct them independently. GR enters only the gravitational promotion branch.

## What the response core already assumes

Before its first response calculation, the core has granted a tuple schematically of the form

$$
(\Sigma,\sigma,\mathcal A,\omega,\mathcal T,\mathcal O),
$$

with a causal cut $\Sigma$, scale section $\sigma$, associative complex algebra $\mathcal A$, state $\omega$, cross-fiber transport $\mathcal T$, and observable family $\mathcal O$. It later adds differentiability, a relative-entropy Hessian, a countably additive localization, and independently normalized area. These are legitimate ingredients of a constitutive branch. They are not yet return values of pure algebra.

The upstream interface must therefore supply or explicitly leave imported:

1. the scalar and algebraic doctrine;
2. a category of presentations and processes with a chosen reversible core;
3. generalized probes and an adequate observable family;
4. a site and effectivity theorem wherever strict descent is claimed;
5. an algebraic scale category not defined by an already-metric $N$;
6. the analytic enrichment producing algebras, states, channels, and transports;
7. factual instruments and record objects; and
8. any causal, metric, area, or gravitational carrier claimed as emergent.

## Where the quotient theorem lives

[[program-core/physical-quotient|The physical quotient]] is the sole owner of the observational equivalence relation, its kernel-pair formulation, the staged consequence family, and the horizontal tangent. The algebraic pre-core adds no second quotient. It changes the dependency order: the presentation object (X), generalized probes, target objects, and category in which the kernel pair or effective quotient is taken must be constructed or declared before the core theorem applies.

The Set-level quotient is one realization of that theorem. When nilpotents, stabilizers, higher arrows, or derived tangents are physically relevant, the upstream doctrine must instead supply the category in which a groupoid quotient, stackification, localization, or derived quotient is the appropriate return. This interface owns only that translation between levels.

## The scale--geometry loop

The current response branch begins with metric or Weyl scale $N$, maps its tangent into state space, and then seeks a map from state response back to gravitational response:

$$
\text{metric scale}
\longrightarrow
\text{state tangent}
\dashrightarrow
\text{geometry or gravity}.
$$

This is a coherent constitutive law on an imported geometry. It becomes a derivation of geometry only after an independent algebraic scale object and a theorem identify its realized parameter with Weyl scale. Likewise, dividing a BKM measure by area produces an inverse-area modulus because area was supplied; it does not derive area or dimension from BKM geometry.

## Name the operation

[[program-core/operation-registers|The operation register]] is the canonical ledger for arrows formerly compressed into the word *descent*: gluing, strict descent, physical or categorical quotient, conditional expectation, contextual readout, factual pointing, and record extension have different data and return values. Historical module names may retain the older word, but canonical claims should name the actual arrow. In particular, strict descent supplies coherent comparison and effectivity; it does not produce entropy, time, or facthood.

[[program-core/contextual-descent-from-homogeneity|Contextual realization from homogeneity]] is a promising branch once its readout family is typed, but differentiation resides in the state, representation, Dirac data, context, boundary, or pointing—not in a structureless homogeneous algebra.

## What remains load-bearing

The following parts of the core should remain authoritative inside the causal-response realization:

- the status vocabulary and weakest-edge rule in [[program-core/axioms-and-principles|the axiom ledger]];
- the distinction between observable equivalence and physical completeness in [[program-core/physical-quotient|the physical quotient]];
- the exact no-response lemma for state-preserving changes of presentation;
- the separation of state, fact, and record;
- the finite conditional-expectation entropy and BKM split;
- the same-tangent, independent-normalization requirements for a gravity weld;
- the distinction between Casimir identity, capacity, Noether charge, and action; and
- the branch and failure discipline in [[program-core/claim-and-failure-contract|the claim contract]].

These are not false starts. They are a well-typed downstream laboratory whose inputs have now become visible.

## Revised construction order

The interface is

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\text{algebraic doctrine and generalized probes}\\
&\longrightarrow
\text{presentation/process category and strict descent}\\
&\longrightarrow
\text{analytic causal-scale realization}\\
&\longrightarrow
\text{physical quotient and horizontal state response}\\
&\longrightarrow
\begin{cases}
\text{gravity branch},\\
\text{factive-record branch},\\
\text{variational-charge branch}.
\end{cases}
\end{aligned}
$$

The branches remain independent. In particular, an exact $A_2$ cover does not choose a wall state; a KO-degree does not choose ordinary dimension; a conditional expectation does not choose a fact; and a response modulus does not become gravity without the same-carrier weld and independent area calibration.

## Upgrade condition

The algebraic pre-core should replace an imported core datum only when it constructs that datum functorially and passes the conservative-interface tests for GR and QFT. Until then, the honest status is **coexistence with an imported local fiber** or **constitutive response on an imported carrier**. This lets the foundational programme deepen without sacrificing the exact work already accomplished downstream.
