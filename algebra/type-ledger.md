# Dimension and Structure Type Ledger

The recurring integers two, three, six, and ten in the algebra chats belong to different invariants: dimension, cover degree, bundle rank, group cardinality, root count, monodromy order, Milnor number, and KO-degree. Numerical equality is legitimate evidence only after a map is constructed that preserves the structures those invariants classify. This ledger prevents a mnemonic rhyme from becoming an ontological identification.

## The several threes and sixes

| Expression | Type | Meaning and status |
|---|---|---|
| $\dim_{\mathbb C}\mathbb C^3=3$ | complex dimension | the announced Keller map has three complex input variables, hence six real dimensions |
| degree $\pi=3$ | cover degree | conditional on the local unreviewed analysis, a generic inverse fiber contains three sheets |
| $\operatorname{rank}E=3$ | module rank | $E=\pi_*\mathcal O_X$ for the conditional degree-three finite étale cover |
| $\dim_{\mathbb C}X=3$ | complex dimension | a complex structure on the smooth real manifold $S^6$ would make it a complex threefold |
| $\dim_{\mathbb R}X^\tau=3$ | real fixed-locus dimension | exact when a complex threefold carries an antiholomorphic involution with nonempty fixed locus; the involution and physical interpretation are additional data |
| physical $3+1$ | real Lorentzian dimension | three real spatial directions plus one real temporal direction |
| $\mu(A_2)=2$ | Milnor number | rank of the vanishing-cycle lattice for the simple $A_2$ singularity |
| $\lvert\Phi(A_2)\rvert=6$ | root count | six roots: three positive and three negative |
| $\lvert W(A_2)\rvert=\lvert S_3\rvert=6$ | group cardinality | the Weyl group has six elements; the conditional inverse cover has this full sheet group |
| $h(A_2)=3$ | Coxeter number | an $A_2$ Coxeter element has order three in the standard root representation |
| order $(-T)=6$ | plane-cusp Milnor order | for $x^3+y^2$, the Milnor operator is the negative of the order-three root Coxeter operator |
| one further quadratic suspension | suspension parity | adding $z^2$ toggles the sign back to the order-three operator $T$ |
| $\dim_{\mathbb R}S^6=6$ | real manifold dimension | the ordinary dimension of the six-sphere |
| $S_6$ | permutation group | permutations of six letters, of cardinality $6!=720$; not the sphere $S^6$ |
| $\operatorname{KOdim}F=6\pmod8$ | real spectral-triple degree | mod-eight sign data encoded by the relations among $J$, $D$, and $\gamma$, not six coordinates |
| metric dimension of $F=0$ | spectral dimension | Connes' finite internal geometry has finite spectrum and no positive metric dimension |

The displayed $A_2$ Coxeter matrix in the chats,

$$
T=
\begin{pmatrix}
-1&1\\
-1&0
\end{pmatrix},
$$

satisfies $T^3=I$ and has characteristic polynomial $t^2+t+1$. For the plane cusp $x^3+y^2$, the classical Milnor operator is $-T$, with characteristic polynomial $t^2-t+1$ and order six. One further quadratic suspension changes the sign again. The fact that $W(A_2)$ has six elements does not determine any of these operator orders.

## The two meanings of ten

For the almost-commutative product, KO-degrees add modulo eight:

$$
4+6\equiv2\pmod8.
$$

This is graded sign bookkeeping. It is not a statement that a four-dimensional manifold has been multiplied by a smooth six-dimensional internal manifold. The finite internal algebra remains metric-dimensional zero. [[ko-dimension-as-morita-class/inq|KO-dimension as a Morita class]] owns the full distinction and develops the project's adopted graded-Clifford/Morita interpretation; that interpretation should not be silently promoted beyond the status assigned there.

## The constant $c$ is not an identification

Writing \(x^0=ct\) expresses a temporal coordinate in units of length. It does not turn a timelike direction into a spacelike one, erase Lorentzian signature, or derive why physical spacetime has one temporal and three spatial directions. A realization map must separately recover the causal cones, signature, and relation between record order and metric time.

## A real form is a genuine bridge, not automatic halving

For a complex threefold \(X\), an antiholomorphic involution \(\tau:X\to X\) has a real three-dimensional fixed locus whenever that locus is nonempty. This gives the typed implication

$$
(X,\tau)
\longmapsto
X^\tau,
\qquad
6_{\mathbb R}\longmapsto3_{\mathbb R}.
$$

[[algebra/real-forms-and-factive-spacetime|The real-form note]] owns the proof and the proposed history functor. The involution \(\tau\) is not Connes' antiunitary real-structure operator \(J\), and \(X^\tau\) is not automatically a spatial slice of a Lorentzian cosmos. The first additional bridge would relate the two real structures through a represented function algebra; the second would realize factive processes as \(3+1\)-dimensional histories.

## The overloaded symbol $A_2$

| Use of $A_2$ | Object |
|---|---|
| simple singularity | the analytic germ $x^3+y^2$ up to the usual equivalence |
| deformation discriminant | the cusp $4a^3+27b^2=0$ for $u^3+au+b$ |
| root system | six roots in a rank-two lattice |
| Weyl group | $W(A_2)\cong S_3$ generated by two reflections |
| Milnor or Coxeter monodromy | an automorphism of vanishing homology, with convention and suspension dependence |
| sheet monodromy | a permutation representation of the fundamental group of a discriminant complement |
| toric $A_2$ triangulation | the triangular lattice used in a fan or degeneration construction |

These objects are related in specific singularity-theoretic constructions, but the phrase “has $A_2$” does not identify them. In particular, the toric $A_2$ triangulation in the local $S^6$ manuscript is not evidence that its family is governed by the Keller inverse cusp.

## The bridge rule

A shared invariant may motivate a bridge search. Promotion requires a typed map

$$
F:\mathcal X\longrightarrow\mathcal Y
$$

and a proof that the relevant structure is transported:

$$
I_{\mathcal Y}(F(x))
=\varphi(I_{\mathcal X}(x))
$$

for a declared comparison $\varphi$. Equal integers without $F$ and $\varphi$ are salience, not derivation. This rule applies equally to $S^6$, KO-degree six, $S_3$, $SU(3)$, the six $A_2$ roots, and the six purported internal dimensions.
