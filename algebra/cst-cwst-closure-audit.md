# Algebraic Closure Audit of CST and CWST

The algebraic pre-core establishes one exact categorical obstruction and one exact conditional geometric implication, but it does not yet close a full CST or CWST realization. Genuine forgetting cannot be a cross-register equivalence; a complex threefold with a selected antiholomorphic involution has a three-real-dimensional fixed locus. The audit also refines the wall into presentation, correspondence, quotient, response, fact, and record layers. The selected wall family, \(3+1\) Lorentzian realization, BKM-to-spatial transfer, gravitational weld, covariant dynamics, and factual actuality rule remain open.

## What “physics is mathematics” changes

The audit no longer distinguishes a mathematical source from a supposedly more real physical target. It distinguishes mathematical types:

$$
\text{internal algebraic reality}
\xrightarrow{\;\text{realization}\;}
\text{factive Lorentzian reality}.
$$

An internal dimension, spectrum, state, or monodromy is as real as a spatial coordinate. The burden is nevertheless exact: a claim that they are two presentations of one structure requires a functor, natural transformation, quotient, or recovery theorem with declared domains. Philosophical realism removes a false ontological hierarchy; it does not turn equality of numerals into equality of invariants.

## Newly exact results

### Nonunitarity of a genuinely forgetful wall

[[algebra/nonfaithful-realization|The nonfaithfulness theorem]] proves

$$
\text{nonfaithful realization}
\Longrightarrow
\text{no categorical or unitary equivalence across the wall}.
$$

This closes the conceptual question of whether global information-preserving unitarity must govern a map whose defining role is to identify distinctions. It need not. Local unitary evolution may still hold inside the realized QFT register. Under finite tracial conditional-expectation hypotheses, [[spectral-wall-descent/conditional-expectation-balance|the exact entropy and BKM Pythagorean theorem]] quantifies the retained and erased response without postulating a hidden reservoir.

### A rigorous six-to-three carrier mechanism

[[algebra/real-forms-and-factive-spacetime|The real-form theorem]] proves that the nonempty fixed locus of an antiholomorphic involution on a complex threefold is a real three-manifold:

$$
\dim_{\mathbb R}X=6,
\qquad
\dim_{\mathbb R}X^\tau=3.
$$

This is an exact candidate for the spatial part of \(6\to3+1\). The time direction is a separate process type: a functor from factive processes to four-dimensional Lorentzian histories between three-dimensional Cauchy objects. No candidate \(S^6\) complex structure, Connes real structure, or KO-degree-six datum currently supplies the needed \(\tau\) and history functor.

### Strict descent and loss are no longer conflated

Strict descent glues compatible presentations. A conditional expectation, quotient, coarse-moduli map, localization, or nonfaithful realization forgets declared distinctions. An instrument selects an outcome. A record inclusion preserves obtained values. These are four operations, not four names for one arrow. This type separation resolves several verbal ambiguities while leaving the corresponding constructions open.

The final factive arrow is not W5, and the first gluing arrow is not the nonfaithful wall. This common chain removes two duplications that previously obscured both programmes.

## Highest-value theorem sequence

The shortest noncircular route now has five targets.

1. Construct a selected internal complex threefold or another six-real-dimensional spectral carrier together with an antiholomorphic real structure \(\tau\); calculate the topology and geometry of \(X^\tau\).
2. Build the context site and correspondence prestack over that carrier, prove effective gluing, and show how it fills the canonical wall-interface slots without using the target expansion history or primordial spectrum.
3. Construct the nonfaithful observational realization, its kernel/effective image, faithful unconditioned state family, conditional expectation or instrument, and physical response quotient.
4. Construct the functor from factive processes to \(3+1\) Lorentzian histories and prove the record--scale soldering criterion; then derive the scalar and TT constrained phase spaces.
5. Calculate the common response and its mixed jet, the CST covariant source, the CWST W2/W3 transfers, and the independent state--area--gravity weld before fitting any cosmological return value.

The first target can fail cleanly if no suitable real structure exists or its fixed locus has the wrong topology. The second can fail if the local wall data do not glue. The third can fail if all admissible readouts are reversible or response-null. The fourth can fail if record order and scale disagree. The fifth can fail independently in the homogeneous, scalar, tensor, gravity, or factive branch.

## Present verdict

The paradigm shift survives the audit in a stronger form. A timeless internal mathematics can be ontologically fundamental; ordinary \(3+1\) spacetime can be its factual presentation; the wall can be genuinely nonunitary; and a persistent structural class can endure while observable symmetry is broken and distinctions are forgotten. What has not yet been proved is that one selected mathematical object realizes all of these structures, or that its record order, entropy increments, horizon growth, mass response, and cosmic acceleration are natural images of one law.

That missing result is no longer a verbal mystery. It is a finite list of functors, naturality squares, cocycle identities, constrained phase spaces, and independent normalization theorems.
