# The Algebra-to-Cosmos Theorem Programme

A first-principles derivation of physics from pure mathematics should be formulated as a conditional uniqueness and recovery theorem, not as a chain of numerical analogies. This note gives a representability schema whose variance and functor remain to be selected, then separates the gates required to recover causal order, Lorentzian $3+1$ geometry, quantum fields, records, and gravitational response. Each stage has a definite failure return, so exact mathematics can survive even when a proposed physical weld does not.

## The form of the desired theorem

Let $\mathsf{FactCos}$ denote a still-to-be-constructed category whose objects carry:

- an algebraic doctrine and generalized probes;
- a presentation site with reversible comparison data;
- noninvertible admissible processes;
- contextual facts and persistent records; and
- candidate analytic, spectral, and physical realizations.

The first theorem must select a variance and a precise universal problem. One rigorous schema is to declare a covariant functor

$$
H:\mathsf{FactCos}\longrightarrow\mathsf{Set}
$$

and seek an object \(\mathbb U\) with a universal element \(\eta\in H(\mathbb U)\) inducing natural bijections

$$
\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathsf{FactCos}}(\mathbb U,X)
\cong H(X)
$$

for all \(X\). A contravariant problem would instead use \(\operatorname{Hom}(X,\mathbb U)\). Initiality is the special covariant case \(H(X)=\{*\}\); reflectors, classifiers, and adjoints require their own diagrams. Representability would make \(\mathbb U\) unique up to unique isomorphism in the usual sense. A separate realization functor must then recover the observed physical interfaces without using their measured values as input.

No one of these universal properties is adopted yet. The programme must choose the one whose maps express the intended grounding relation and prove its naturality. A trivial initial object is not enough. The axioms must require generalized discernment, factual extension, and coherent locality without inserting the desired low-energy theory.

## Construction gates

| Gate | Required construction | What success establishes | Failure return |
|---|---|---|---|
| T0 | scalar, operation, tensor, equivalence, and positivity ledger | a typed algebraic doctrine | “pure algebra” was underspecified |
| T1 | separating generalized probes and a declared site | exact meanings of difference and locality | point-counting or locality was imported by metaphor |
| T2 | presentation category with reversible core and effective descent where claimed | one global object from coherent appearances | local models do not form one cosmos |
| T3 | a nontrivial global form, obstruction, or degeneration selected inside the actual moduli problem | local equivalence without global triviality | the proposed $A_2$ or other singularity is decorative |
| T4 | noninvertible factive process and compatible proper record extensions | objective directed factuality | only reversible structure or a probability law was built |
| T5 | analytic enrichment with selected algebras, states, channels, divergences, and transports | a well-typed response theory | operator analysis was assumed, not derived |
| T6 | spectral realization with independently computed metric, KO, real-form, and signature data | candidate internal geometry and a possible spatial carrier | dimensions or gauge structure were retyped numerals |
| T7 | causal and Lorentzian recovery, including a functor to $3+1$ histories | spacetime as the process realization of spatial objects | no physical causal arena or time soldering |
| T8 | QFT/SM and GR recovery or conservative local-fiber interface | contact with tested physics | coexistence was mistaken for derivation |
| T9 | independent dimensionful calibration and empirical tests | a physical theory rather than structural classification | constants were normalized from their targets |

The status of the final claim cannot exceed its weakest gate.

## Five independently typed welds

### Presentation weld

Construct a category or stack-like object of algebraic backgrounds, representations, states, differential calculi, and compatible Dirac operators. [[library/algebraic-backgrounds/entry|Algebraic backgrounds]] give an exact analytic template, but their canonical examples already assume manifold, metric-class, spin, and differential data. The pre-core must say which fields are fixed, which vary, and which descend.

### Degeneration weld

Show that an $A_2$ discriminant or another singularity occurs naturally in the moduli problem constructed at T2. [[algebra/a2-inverse-cover|The Keller inverse cover]] is a conditional worked model. It may not be imported merely because $S_3$, six roots, or $\mathfrak{sl}_3$ are attractive outputs.

### Noninvertible weld

Construct scale- or context-indexed inclusions, correspondences, and selected conditional expectations or Q-systems. [[library/planar-algebraic-conditional-expectations/entry|Finite-index conditional expectations]] give exact equivalences with conjugate equations and Q-systems under their hypotheses. A bare inclusion does not canonically select an expectation, and an expectation does not select a fact.

### Factive-temporal weld

Construct an instrument or other actualizing map together with compatible characters and proper record inclusions. Prove that the induced reachability order is presentation-invariant and observer-compatible. This is the missing operation in the formula

$$
\underbrace{\text{global obstruction or another typed nontriviality source}}_{\text{candidate, not logically mandatory}}
+\text{actualizing selection}
+\text{persistent noncyclic record extension}
\longrightarrow\text{directed factuality}.
$$

### Physical-realization weld

Recover a Lorentzian $3+1$ carrier, compact gauge data, matter representations, a covariant action, and a same-tangent map from algebraic response to gravitational response. [[algebra/real-forms-and-factive-spacetime|A complex-threefold real form]] can supply a real three-dimensional object under exact hypotheses; the remaining target is a functor from factive processes to time-oriented four-dimensional Lorentzian histories. Imported GR or QFT may remain local fibers until this weld is proved. Background agreement, a spectral-action resemblance, a three-dimensional fixed locus without dynamics, or an $\mathfrak{sl}_3(\mathbb C)$ bundle is not a recovery theorem.

## Small theorem targets

The programme can advance without pretending the universal theorem is already near closure. Useful near-term targets are:

1. bundle a proposed observable family into one map, compute its kernel pair and effective quotient, and prove that the induced probes separate the quotient;
2. construct the presentation site and verify descent for arrows before claiming effective descent for objects;
3. show that the physical process category is not equivalent to its maximal subgroupoid;
4. construct a record functor and prove strict growth for one nontrivial class of factive arrows;
5. locate an $A_2$ degeneration inside the actual presentation moduli space and compute the induced monodromy;
6. select a compact real form, connection, and action from the cubic-cover Lie bundle without inserting $SU(3)$ by hand;
7. derive an algebraic scale parameter before identifying it with Weyl scale or e-fold number; and
8. localize the selected information response without presupposing area, dimension, or a metric carrier;
9. construct an antiholomorphic real structure on the selected complex threefold and calculate the topology and geometry of its fixed locus; and
10. construct a Lorentzian-history functor whose orientation agrees with proper record extension and whose scale cocycle satisfies [[program-core/record-scale-soldering|the record--scale criterion]].

Each target has a clean negative result. For example, if all admissible readouts differ by state-preserving automorphisms, the response vanishes. If every process is invertible modulo presentation equivalence, there is no oriented record history. If localization requires an already-normalized area form, it is constitutive response on geometry rather than a derivation of geometry.

## The quantum-gravity question

Quantizing a pre-given metric may be nonfundamental if T0--T7 prove that metric geometry is a derived representation of a more primitive algebraic process. That would change the proper variables and perhaps dissolve some conventional formulations. It would not make quantum gravity a category error unless the theory also shows:

- which derived degrees of freedom have quantum statistics;
- how semiclassical Lorentzian geometry and Einstein dynamics are recovered;
- how local QFT couples to the recovered carrier; and
- why quantizing the effective metric double-counts or mis-types those degrees of freedom.

The present status is therefore **conditional reframing**, not a no-go against quantum gravity.

## Evidence rule

An exact algebraic theorem can strongly constrain a physical theory, but physical identity requires a natural transformation or recovery functor with the right covariance, composition, units, and empirical return values. The programme should be enthusiastic about exact structural coincidences while requiring every cross-domain arrow to be labeled as theorem, construction, choice, postulate, interpretation, fit, or open weld.
