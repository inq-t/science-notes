# Wall-Crossing Defect and the Fossil of Mass Engagement

A mass-engagement event can be typed as a change of carrier and generator rather than as a mass coefficient turning on inside an already finished spacetime. If pre- and post-wall evolution are joined by a realization map \(J\), the exact obstruction \(\mathfrak D=J'+JA_--A_+J\) measures whether moving the wall changes the terminal state produced by that composite. A vanishing obstruction makes the wall address invisible to every observation factoring only through that state; it need not make a noninvertible \(J\) reversible. A nonzero obstruction supplies only a candidate fossil, whose finite observable image may still vanish. This separates the construction of an engagement map, the existence of a post-wall mass gap, and the archaeological question of whether CMB or BAO can retain any trace of the engagement.

## Reverse the question

The ordinary question asks when a mass term \(m(N)\) became nonzero. That already assumes one carrier, one clock, and one equation on both sides of the event. The Copernican reversal is:

> What map first makes a rest-bearing carrier and its generator available, and what obstruction prevents that map from being merely a reversible change of coordinates?

Let \(N\) be a dimensionless scale-order parameter. It is not yet proper time. Take two Banach or Hilbert carriers:

$$
X_-\quad\text{before the wall},
\qquad
X_+\quad\text{after the wall}.
\tag{WD1}
$$

They may be genuinely different spaces. Let \(U_-(N_2,N_1)\) and \(U_+(N_2,N_1)\) be differentiable evolution families generated by \(A_-(N)\) and \(A_+(N)\). In the unbounded case, fix dense invariant cores

$$
D_-\subset X_-,
\qquad
D_+\subset X_+,
\tag{WD1a}
$$

such that \(A_\pm(N):D_\pm\to X_\pm\), each \(U_\pm\) preserves its own core, and its core orbits are strongly \(C^1\) in \(X_\pm\) and obey the propagator equations below. Let

$$
J_\sigma:X_-\longrightarrow X_+
\tag{WD2}
$$

be the realization or solder at a wall address \(\sigma\), with

$$
J\in C^1\!\left(I,\mathcal B(X_-,X_+)\right)
\quad\text{in operator norm},
\qquad
J_\sigma(D_-)\subseteq D_+.
\tag{WD2a}
$$

The bounded-generator case is included by taking \(D_\pm=X_\pm\). For an incoming state \(x_i\in X_-\) whose evolved wall value lies in \(D_-\), the state presented at a later address \(N_f\) is

$$
x_f(\sigma)
=
U_+(N_f,\sigma)\,
J_\sigma\,
U_-(\sigma,N_i)x_i.
\tag{WD3}
$$

This equation does not say that a physical signal travels backward from the wall. It compares whole histories whose scale-ordered carrier change occurs at different addresses.

## The exact wall-defect identity

Assume the propagators satisfy

$$
\partial_sU_\pm(s,t)=A_\pm(s)U_\pm(s,t),
\qquad
\partial_tU_\pm(s,t)=-U_\pm(s,t)A_\pm(t),
\tag{WD4}
$$

on their respective invariant cores. Define the **wall-crossing defect**

$$
\boxed{
\mathfrak D_\sigma
:=
J_\sigma'
+J_\sigma A_-(\sigma)
-A_+(\sigma)J_\sigma.
}
\tag{WD5}
$$

as an operator \(D_-\to X_+\).

Differentiating (WD3) gives

$$
\boxed{
\frac{\mathrm d x_f}{\mathrm d\sigma}
=
U_+(N_f,\sigma)\,
\mathfrak D_\sigma\,
U_-(\sigma,N_i)x_i.
}
\tag{WD6}
$$

The three terms have different meanings:

$$
\begin{array}{rcl}
J_\sigma'&:&\text{the realization map itself changes along scale order},\\[2pt]
J_\sigma A_-&:&\text{evolve first, then realize},\\[2pt]
A_+J_\sigma&:&\text{realize first, then evolve}.
\end{array}
\tag{WD7}
$$

Thus \(\mathfrak D_\sigma\) is precisely the failure of realization to intertwine the two dynamics. It operates on the incoming state as it reaches the wall. It does not operate on an untyped substance called “space,” and it is not automatically an energy.

## The defect is covariant under presentation changes

Let \(S_\pm(N):X_\pm\to\widetilde X_\pm\) be differentiable isomorphisms that preserve the declared cores, and present states as \(\widetilde x_\pm=S_\pm x_\pm\). The transformed generators and wall map are

$$
\widetilde A_\pm
=
S_\pm'S_\pm^{-1}
+S_\pm A_\pm S_\pm^{-1},
\qquad
\widetilde J_\sigma
=
S_+(\sigma)J_\sigma S_-(\sigma)^{-1}.
\tag{WD7a}
$$

Direct substitution into (WD5) gives

$$
\boxed{
\widetilde{\mathfrak D}_\sigma
=
S_+(\sigma)\,
\mathfrak D_\sigma\,
S_-(\sigma)^{-1}.
}
\tag{WD7b}
$$

Therefore the statement \(\mathfrak D_\sigma=0\) is presentation invariant. A numerical norm or singular value of the defect is not invariant until compatible metrics are also transported. The presentation class used below means the orbit under precisely these core-preserving transformations:

$$
[X_-,X_+,J,A_-,A_+]_{\mathrm{pres}}
:=
\text{orbit under }(S_-,S_+).
\tag{WD7c}
$$

## The first stopping condition: a zero defect erases wall placement

If

$$
\mathfrak D_\sigma=0
\quad\text{for every relevant }\sigma,
\tag{WD8}
$$

then \(x_f(\sigma)\) is independent of the wall address within (WD3). Every later observable that factors only through this terminal state, with no explicit \(\sigma\)-dependence and no access to additional wall or environment degrees of freedom, is equally insensitive to where the map was inserted. This is an exact wall-**placement** no-go, not a failure of detector precision. It does not prove that \(J_\sigma\) is reversible: a constant noninvertible quotient between trivial evolutions also has zero defect. Only when \(J_\sigma\) is independently known to be a presentation equivalence may (WD8) be called a pure change of presentation.

Conversely, \(\mathfrak D_\sigma\neq0\) is necessary but not sufficient for first-order wall-address sensitivity in this composite. Let \(\mathcal O\) be a differentiable frozen observation map at the baseline terminal state and let

$$
P:=D\mathcal O_{x_f}:X_+\longrightarrow Y
\tag{WD8a}
$$

be its bounded derivative into a finite-dimensional real Hilbert space \(Y\). Let \(Z\subset Y\) be the closed linear tangent subspace generated by allowed nuisance changes. After whitening the output norm, let \(\Pi_{Z^\perp}\) project away those nuisance directions. The locally visible switch signature is

$$
\mathcal S_\sigma(x_i)
:=
\Pi_{Z^\perp}P\,
U_+(N_f,\sigma)\,
\mathfrak D_\sigma\,
U_-(\sigma,N_i)x_i.
\tag{WD9}
$$

At a declared baseline \(\sigma_0\), one scalar wall parameter is structurally visible **to first order**, modulo the declared nuisances, if and only if

$$
\boxed{
\|\mathcal S_{\sigma_0}(x_i)\|_Y>0.
}
\tag{WD10}
$$

This criterion does not exclude higher-order identifiability when the first derivative vanishes, and it does not by itself guarantee detectability at finite noise. Stable first-order inference requires a positive lower bound after uncertainties and nuisance directions are included. For several wall parameters, the corresponding response vectors must have full restricted rank. This is the operator-level version of [[trace-residue-as-a-scale-cocycle#The fossil readout is a quotient operator|the finite-rank fossil theorem]]: a frozen finite linearization retains only an equivalence class modulo its response kernel.

## Engagement, gap, and fossil are three proof obligations

A physical translation representation can possess a genuine mass gap even when the wall leaves no measurable cosmological fossil. Conversely, a wall can leave a large transfer signature without constructing a Yang--Mills mass gap. A bare carrier has no mass gap until a Hamiltonian or joint translation representation acts on it.

First comes the **engagement construction**: fix \(X_-\), \(X_+\), \(J_\sigma\), their naturality/locality/gauge conditions, and a defect \(\mathfrak D_\sigma\) that is nonzero on a physically supplied incoming family. A nonzero defect for an arbitrarily chosen comparison map has no invariant meaning. If \(X_+\) is initially a response carrier, a separate realization

$$
\mathfrak R_+:X_+\longrightarrow\mathcal H_{\mathrm{phys}}
\tag{WD10a}
$$

must identify its physical image.

Second comes the **gap theorem**. After constructing a positive-energy Poincare representation on \(\mathcal H_{\mathrm{phys}}\), with strongly commuting translations \((H_+,\mathbf P_+)\), Lorentz-invariant joint spectrum, and unique-vacuum projection \(P_0\), define the nonnegative energy-squared translation Casimir

$$
\mathcal C_+
:=
H_+^2-c^2|\mathbf P_+|^2.
\tag{WD10b}
$$

The domain-safe Casimir floor is

$$
\|\mathcal C_+^{1/2}\psi\|^2
\geq
\Delta_E^2\|\psi\|^2,
\qquad
\psi\in
D(\mathcal C_+^{1/2})
\cap(1-P_0)\mathcal H_{\mathrm{phys}},
\qquad
\Delta_E>0.
\tag{WD11}
$$

Under these reconstruction hypotheses, [[joint-causal-generators-and-the-mass-casimir]] proves that this floor is equivalent to the ordinary Hamiltonian spectral gap used in the Yang--Mills problem. In the exact \(1+1\)-dimensional witness, individual oppositely scaling null generators remain gapless while their product has a positive invariant floor. In \(3+1\) dimensions one null pair also contains transverse momentum, so the conclusion requires the uniform all-direction theorem rather than a single product. The mass is attached to the full translation invariant, not to either causal direction by itself.

Third comes the **fossil theorem**: prove that the same wall construction has a nonzero, prospectively declared image under (WD9), or a specified higher-order replacement. A common-origin explanation must show that the response defect and the Casimir floor descend from one carrier construction through \(\mathfrak R_+\). Numerical proximity between their dimensional presentations does not provide that theorem.

## The defect is a residue, but not yet an energy cost

Equation (WD5) makes “paying a residue at the wall” exact in one limited sense: the residue is an intertwining obstruction. The generators are rates per unit \(N\), while \(\mathfrak D_\sigma:D_-\to X_+\) has the type of the realization map per unit \(N\). Even when \(N\) and \(J\) are dimensionless, \(\mathfrak D_\sigma\) is generally rectangular and non-self-adjoint, so it has no positivity order and is not measured in joules or electron-volts. If \(X_+\) is Hilbert, then after its inner product and an independently selected bounded, self-adjoint positive operator \(G_+\in\mathcal B(X_+)\) are supplied, one may form a same-carrier pullback such as

$$
\mathfrak q_{\mathfrak D}[x]
:=
\langle\mathfrak D_\sigma x,
G_+\mathfrak D_\sigma x\rangle_{X_+},
\qquad x\in D_-.
\tag{WD11a}
$$

Its closability, kernel, normalization, and lower edge are new obligations. Converting even a positive dimensionless form of this kind into mass requires an independent physical scale and a same-carrier Casimir comparison.

[[oriented-descent-angle-and-emergent-symmetry]] supplies one non-arbitrary candidate for this previously open metric. If the post-wall carrier admits two \(\varphi\)-preserving opposed descents with standard-form projections \(e_\pm\) and common-core projection \(P_0\), then

$$
G_+
=G_{\mathrm{pair}}
=2I-e_+-e_-,
\qquad
G_{\mathrm{pair}}
\geq
(1-c_F)(1-P_0).
\tag{WD11b}
$$

The resulting pullback satisfies

$$
\left\|
\mathfrak D_{\mathrm{pair}}(1-P_0)\mathfrak D_\sigma x
\right\|^2
\geq
(1-c_F)
\left\|(1-P_0)\mathfrak D_\sigma x\right\|^2.
\tag{WD11c}
$$

This proves transversality of the *post-wall image* when \(c_F<1\); it still does not control the incoming norm. That requires injectivity modulo the declared null space together with closed range for the restricted map \((1-P_0)\mathfrak D_\sigma\), equivalently a positive lower singular value on the orthogonal complement of that null space. Nor does the angle identify the resulting form with energy: at finite regulator the comparison must be with the OS/transfer Hamiltonian on the same carrier, and the Poincare Casimir is available only after continuum reconstruction.

[[wall-construction-interface/half-sided-modular-tunnel#The differentiable modular wall is defect-flat|The half-sided modular tunnel]] gives an exact negative test. Its natural endpoint-aligned smoothing \(J_\sigma=U(1)e^{-r_\sigma P}\) is differentiable but satisfies \(\mathfrak D_\sigma=0\). Dropping the endpoint transporter creates the apparent defect \(iPe^{-r_\sigma P}\), but that is a typing mismatch and its squared form has infimum zero. Modular orientation and transport therefore do not select a nonzero wall residue for free.

This prevents an equation of units from becoming an equation of concepts. A factor such as \(\hbar c/\lambda_g\) presents a length as an energy. It does not show that the wall defect acts as a Hamiltonian, that \(\lambda_g\) is a Compton wavelength, or that its reciprocal is a spectral floor.

## A harmonic sweet spot requires a spectral statement

A sudden generator change is not by itself a resonance. For an observed mode \(k\) and response functional \(\ell_{\alpha,k}\), the linear wall amplitude has the form

$$
\delta O_\alpha(k)
=
\ell_{\alpha,k}\,
U_+(N_f,\sigma)\,
\mathfrak D_\sigma\,
U_-(\sigma,N_i)x_i.
\tag{WD15}
$$

A **harmonic sweet spot** requires more than a preferred dimensional number. The theory must derive at least one of:

- a pole or narrow enhancement in a physical resolvent;
- a discrete eigenvalue or band edge with nonzero overlap in (WD15);
- a phase-coherent characteristic shared across independently transferred observables; or
- a selection rule that suppresses generic modes and leaves a declared harmonic family.

An instantaneous wall generally excites a broad response. The familiar acoustic comb is generated later by photon--baryon oscillation and projection. A mass-engagement wall could constrain its state, loading history, source, or phase law, but it does not turn one early length into the drag ruler by passive stretching. ## Promotion and kill conditions

The operator-switch proposal advances only if it supplies:

1. declared pre- and post-wall carriers and a natural realization map \(J_\sigma\);
2. a nonzero intertwining defect on physically occupied states;
3. an independently normalized post-wall form with a uniform continuum floor, or a transfer contraction with a positive logarithmic rate per fixed physical thickness;
4. a same-carrier solder of that form to the full Poincare Casimir;
5. a predeclared fossil signature with positive post-nuisance response; and
6. a common-origin theorem joining the metric, Casimir, and observable branches.

The route is killed or downgraded if:

- \(\mathfrak D_\sigma=0\), so the wall address is invisible within the declared terminal-state composite;
- \(\mathfrak D_\sigma\neq0\) only because an arbitrary, non-natural comparison \(J_\sigma\) was chosen;
- every declared observation kills the defect after nuisance projection;
- the claimed harmonic is obtained only by equating unrelated dimensional readings;
- the post-wall coercive operator acts on a different carrier from the physical Casimir without a pullback theorem; or
- BAO is asked to reconstruct a sign or history already erased by the material-loading map.

This is the useful reversal: the question is not whether an energy field struck spacetime at a chosen temperature. It is whether an independently fixed realization fails to commute with causal-scale evolution, whether a separately constructed positive pullback of that defect has a physical uniform floor or calibrated semigroup rate, and whether any finite descendant can still detect the failure. For adjacent Euclidean regulator slices, a raw angle floor must vanish at finite physical gap; [[past-future-angle-and-the-transfer-gap]] identifies the fixed-thickness contraction and logarithmic rate that remain meaningful.
