# Mass-Gap No-Gos

The reclassification in [[register-audit]] has positive content only if it forbids something. This note states what it forbids, at the scope the programme's canon allows, and then turns the same obstructions on the programme itself. A lower-dimensional heuristic precedent is recorded last, together with the reasons it does not presently transfer to four dimensions.

## NG1 — A fixed-cutoff gap is insufficient

**[STANDARD]** A UV cutoff does not by itself create a mass gap. The relevant precedent is narrower: Wilson lattice gauge theory is reflection positive at every coupling and is rigorously gapped in its strong-coupling regime. That fixed-cutoff result is not the continuum theorem. A proposed grain mechanism must first prove a regulator-level gap, then prove volume uniformity and persistence in fixed physical units along a tuned \(a\to0\) trajectory. If \(\lambda_*\) remains as a fundamental physical grain, the construction targets a different theory from the continuum theory on \(\mathbb R^4\).

**Kill condition.** Any vault claim to solve the Clay problem is disqualified if its bound is only at fixed \(\lambda_*\), is not uniform in spatial volume, or has no controlled continuum trajectory. A fixed-grain theory may still be interesting, but it must be named as a different target.

## NG2 — HSMI dilation covariance forbids a gap in its translation generator

**[EXACT -- SCOPED]** [[the-grain-of-causal-scale/causal-spectrum|The HSMI no-gap theorem]]: if the positive translation generator \(P\) of a half-sided modular inclusion satisfies \(\Delta^{it}P\Delta^{-it}=e^{-2\pi t}P\), then \(P=0\) or \(\sigma(P)=[0,\infty)\), with no nonzero point spectrum on a separable Hilbert space. A nonzero gap in that *same generator* requires the covariance hypothesis to fail or passage to a different constructed carrier and generator. The theorem does not establish that every pre-wall spectrum is gapless or that a wall automatically supplies a gap. Classical Yang--Mills scale covariance and its quantum trace anomaly are a compatible standard analogy, not an identification of its Hamiltonian with this \(P\).

**Kill condition.** A proposed gap located in this unbroken dilation-covariant HSMI generator contradicts the exact theorem. The condition says nothing until the proposed physical Hamiltonian is actually identified with that generator.

## NG3 — Dimensionless data cannot supply the yardstick

**[EXACT]** [[deriving-value-of-g/obstructions-to-an-unconditional-proof|The dimensional obstruction]] proves that dimensionless structure together with \(c\), \(\hbar\), and \(k_B\) fixes no scale. In \(3+1\) dimensions the Yang--Mills coupling \(e^2\) is dimensionless, so dimensionless algebraic data alone — Casimir, index, rank, cohomology class — cannot output a mass. At one loop the transmuted scale has the schematic form \(\Lambda\sim\mu\exp[-1/(2\beta_0g^2(\mu))]\); its exact scheme-labelled definition uses the beta-function integral. Dimensional transmutation is not a violation of the obstruction but the trade it permits: one dimensionless coupling for one dimensionful scale, input count unchanged.

**Consequence for the programme.** Without an independent scale-setting input, the correctly typed target of an algebraic argument is a pure number computed within one convention: \(m_{0^{++}}/\sqrt\sigma\approx3.5\)–\(3.6\) (Lucini--Teper, continuum), \(m_{2^{++}}/m_{0^{++}}\approx1.4\), or a scheme-labelled ratio such as \(m_{0^{++}}/\Lambda_{\overline{\mathrm{MS}}}\). An output in MeV with no such input has mistyped the problem; so has a ratio assembled from two conventions.

## NG4 — A running grain is dead, and transmutation is a running phenomenon

**[NO-GO — RECEIPT, imported]** [[the-grain-of-causal-scale/inbox/the-grain-in-every-register/entry|The grain-in-every-register note]] shows that if the grain closure were a live law, \(G_{\mathrm{BBN}}/G_0\approx3\times10^{17}\) against a nucleosynthesis bound of \(0.2\). The grain must be a frozen address, evaluated once. Dimensional transmutation, by contrast, is the statement that the coupling runs and the scale is an invariant of that running. The two are different jobs; the grain cannot do the second.

## NG5 — Emergent space owes the axiom

**[STANDARD]** The Wightman axioms take exact Poincaré covariance on \(\mathbb R^4\) as given. This is a statement about the *observable* register — the axioms are axioms about observables, and "four-dimensional" is a property of the arena in which the observations are made, not a claim about the ontology beneath it. The programme is entitled to hold that the ontology is not four-dimensional at all; [[program-core/ontological-registers|the register declaration]] already separates the two, and [[ko-dimension-as-morita-class/inq|the KO-dimension module]] lists "why the metric dimension is 4" as open. But the problem is posed in the observable register, so a programme in which the \(3+1\) arena and its symmetries are emergent must derive exact Poincaré covariance of that arena before it is entitled to state the problem, let alone attack it. This is not a defect of the programme; it is a statement about which problem is downstream of which. The mass-gap problem is downstream of a fixed observable spacetime, and an emergent-space programme is upstream of one. The dimension is load-bearing for the one realized mechanism, too: the Karabali–Nair parametrization below exists because two spatial dimensions carry a complex structure.

## A lower-dimensional structural analogue

**[STANDARD -- non-rigorous calculation, lattice-corroborated]** In \(2+1\) dimensions Karabali and Nair pass from the connection to a gauge-invariant matrix variable; the Jacobian of that change of variables is \(e^{2c_A S_{\mathrm{WZW}}}\), where \(c_A\) is the adjoint Casimir and \(2c_A\) is the level of the hermitian WZW action the change of variables induces. In their approximation this measure factor helps make the vacuum wavefunctional normalizable and produces a mass parameter

$$
m=\frac{e^2c_A}{2\pi}
$$

at lowest order in their scheme; Karabali, Kim, and Nair (1998) obtain a string tension \(\sqrt\sigma=e^2\sqrt{c_Ac_F/4\pi}\) close to lattice values for \(\mathrm{SU}(2)\) through \(\mathrm{SU}(5)\). The pattern resembles a nonzero measure residue multiplying a Casimir and an available yardstick. Because the method is approximate, it is a realized heuristic analogue, not an executed theorem and not proof of the programme's wall conjecture.

**Why it does not presently transfer.** First, and the one NG3 owns: in \(2+1\) dimensions \(e^2\) has the dimension of mass, so the coupling is its own yardstick; in \(3+1\) dimensions \(e^2\) is dimensionless and the scale must come from the renormalization trajectory. Second, the Karabali--Nair parametrization \(A=-\partial M\,M^{-1}\) exploits the complex structure of two spatial dimensions; no comparably controlling parametrization in three spatial dimensions is supplied here. Third, \(2+1\)-dimensional Yang--Mills is super-renormalizable, while control of the four-dimensional ultraviolet limit is the unsolved construction. These are independent missing bridges, not a theorem that every conceivable generalization is impossible.

## The converse the programme would need

**[OPEN]** Buchholz--Wichmann nuclearity is a separate high-energy phase-space condition on the maps \(A\mapsto e^{-\beta H}A\Omega\). A mass gap can improve model-specific large-\(\beta\) estimates once nuclearity is independently known, but neither condition implies the other. Suitable nuclearity hypotheses imply the split property, and a standard split inclusion supplies a canonical intermediate type-I factor; a conditional expectation onto it still requires additional state or modular data. The programme would need a *stronger infrared wall-capacity theorem* that implies \(\lVert e^{-aH}(1-P_0)\rVert<1\). No such converse is present.
