# Mass-Gap No-Gos

The reclassification in [[register-audit]] has positive content only if it forbids something. This note states what it forbids, at the scope the programme's canon allows, and then turns the same obstructions on the programme itself. The single realized precedent for the programme's conjecture is recorded last, together with the exact reason it does not transfer to four dimensions.

## NG1 — A cutoff gap is the trivial case

**[STANDARD]** On a lattice of spacing \(a\), Wilson's gauge action is reflection positive at every coupling and has a mass gap at strong coupling by convergent cluster expansion (Osterwalder–Seiler 1978). Any construction that supplies a gap by supplying an ultraviolet grain has therefore reproduced the 1978 result. The problem's entire content is the clause that the gap, in physical units, survive \(a\to0\). The programme's causal grain \(\lambda_*\) is an ultraviolet correlation length; a mechanism for the Yang–Mills gap that depends on \(\lambda_*\) is not a candidate.

**Kill condition.** Any vault argument for a gap that does not survive the formal removal of \(\lambda_*\) is disqualified by this note.

## NG2 — Scale covariance forbids a gap

**[EXACT]** [[the-grain-of-causal-scale/causal-spectrum|The pre-wall no-gap theorem]]: if a positive generator \(P\) satisfies \(\Delta^{it}P\Delta^{-it}=e^{-2\pi t}P\), then \(\sigma(P)\in\{\{0\},[0,\infty)\}\) and \(P\) has no nonzero point spectrum on a separable Hilbert space. A gap requires that dilation covariance be broken. This is the programme's statement of why classical Yang–Mills, which is exactly dilation covariant in four dimensions, has no scale, and why the quantum theory's scale must enter through the failure of covariance under quantization — the trace anomaly.

**Kill condition.** A proposed gap located in an unbroken dilation-covariant generator contradicts an exact theorem of the vault.

## NG3 — Dimensionless data cannot supply the yardstick

**[EXACT]** [[deriving-value-of-g/obstructions-to-an-unconditional-proof|The dimensional obstruction]] proves that dimensionless structure together with \(c\), \(\hbar\), and \(k_B\) fixes no scale. In \(3+1\) dimensions the Yang–Mills coupling \(e^2\) is dimensionless, so no algebraic invariant of the theory — Casimir, index, rank, cohomology class — can output a mass. The scale enters as \(\Lambda=\mu\exp(-1/2\beta_0g^2(\mu))\), an address on the renormalization flow, and dimensional transmutation is not a violation of the obstruction but the trade it permits: one dimensionless coupling for one dimensionful scale, input count unchanged.

**Consequence for the programme.** The correctly typed target of any algebraic argument about this problem is a pure number computed within one scale-setting: \(m_{0^{++}}/\sqrt\sigma\approx3.5\)–\(3.6\) (Lucini–Teper, continuum), \(m_{2^{++}}/m_{0^{++}}\approx1.4\), \(m_{0^{++}}/\Lambda_{\overline{\mathrm{MS}}}\approx7\). An output in MeV has mistyped the problem; so has a ratio assembled from two conventions.

## NG4 — A running grain is dead, and transmutation is a running phenomenon

**[NO-GO — RECEIPT, imported]** [[the-grain-of-causal-scale/inbox/the-grain-in-every-register/entry|The grain-in-every-register note]] shows that if the grain closure were a live law, \(G_{\mathrm{BBN}}/G_0\approx3\times10^{17}\) against a nucleosynthesis bound of \(0.2\). The grain must be a frozen address, evaluated once. Dimensional transmutation, by contrast, is the statement that the coupling runs and the scale is an invariant of that running. The two are different jobs; the grain cannot do the second.

## NG5 — Emergent space owes the axiom

**[STANDARD]** The Wightman axioms take exact Poincaré covariance on \(\mathbb R^4\) as given. This is a statement about the *observable* register — the axioms are axioms about observables, and "four-dimensional" is a property of the arena in which the observations are made, not a claim about the ontology beneath it. The programme is entitled to hold that the ontology is not four-dimensional at all; [[program-core/ontological-registers|the register declaration]] already separates the two, and [[ko-dimension-as-morita-class/inq|the KO-dimension module]] lists "why the metric dimension is 4" as open. But the problem is posed in the observable register, so a programme in which the \(3+1\) arena and its symmetries are emergent must derive exact Poincaré covariance of that arena before it is entitled to state the problem, let alone attack it. This is not a defect of the programme; it is a statement about which problem is downstream of which. The mass-gap problem is downstream of a fixed observable spacetime, and an emergent-space programme is upstream of one. The dimension is load-bearing for the one realized mechanism, too: the Karabali–Nair parametrization below exists because two spatial dimensions carry a complex structure.

## What the programme's conjecture looks like when it works

**[STANDARD — non-rigorous calculation, lattice-corroborated]** In \(2+1\) dimensions the conjecture that "the residue of the descent is the gap, and the algebra forces it nonzero" is not a conjecture. Karabali and Nair (1996) pass from the connection to a gauge-invariant matrix variable; the Jacobian of that change of variables is \(e^{2c_A S_{\mathrm{WZW}}}\), where \(c_A\) is the adjoint Casimir and \(2c_A\) is the level of the hermitian WZW action the change of variables induces. That residue makes the vacuum wavefunctional normalizable and produces a mass parameter

$$
m=\frac{e^2c_A}{2\pi}
$$

at lowest order in their scheme; Karabali, Kim, and Nair (1998) obtain a string tension \(\sqrt\sigma=e^2\sqrt{c_Ac_F/4\pi}\) that agrees with lattice values for \(\mathrm{SU}(2)\) through \(\mathrm{SU}(5)\) to within a few percent. Residue of descent; forced nonzero by representation theory; equal to a Casimir times a yardstick. The method is not rigorous, has known approximations, and is recorded at that grade.

**Why it does not transfer — three independent obstructions.** First, and the one NG3 owns: in \(2+1\) dimensions \(e^2\) has the dimension of mass, so the coupling is its own yardstick; in \(3+1\) dimensions \(e^2\) is dimensionless, the Jacobian keeps its Casimir and loses its yardstick, and the scale must come from the flow. Second, the Karabali–Nair parametrization \(A=-\partial M\,M^{-1}\) uses the complex structure of two spatial dimensions, which has no analogue in three. Third, \(2+1\)-dimensional Yang–Mills is super-renormalizable, so the measure on the gauge-invariant variables needs no ultraviolet renormalization, whereas the four-dimensional measure is the unsolved problem. Any one of the three blocks the transfer by itself. The programme's contribution is to have the first one already typed as an obstruction; it has nothing to say about the other two.

## The converse the programme would need

**[OPEN]** The one place the programme's machinery and a known result are the same species is nuclearity: a mass gap improves the Buchholz–Wichmann nuclearity bounds, nuclearity implies the split property, and the split property supplies a canonical type-I corner with a canonical conditional expectation — the programme's type-I product cell, arriving from the field-theory side. The arrows run from the gap to the structure. The programme's conjecture is the converse: a capacity condition on a wall that implies a gap. No such theorem exists.
