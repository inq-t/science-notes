# General Causal Action: A Copernican Programme for Yang–Mills

Find an economical abstract construction whose axioms imply the Yang–Mills existence and mass-gap theorems required by Jaffe and Witten. The proposed strategy is a geometry of directed processes and compatible realizations: observable algebras, positive state structures and dynamics should be constrained together, with the gap arising from a rigidity theorem rather than being inserted as spectral data. Processes, relations of access and changing presentations replace a mandatory field-on-spacetime starting point. Their interpretation as arena, time or mass is a guide to discovery, not an extra hypothesis of the mathematical target.

This is a **research schema**, not a completed axiom system or a proof. Its balance identities are exact in their stated analytic realizations; the claim that a deeper law selects those realizations and forces a Yang–Mills gap is the conjecture.

## Mathematical reconstruction is the governing test

The task is to construct mathematical objects satisfying the specified return theorems. Throughout this schema, **physical carrier** means the Hilbert space, observable representation and translation structure appearing in those theorems. It does not mean that an intermediate construction must first receive an empirical or ontological interpretation.

A candidate may be diagrammatic, categorical, noncommutative, nonassociative, analytic, or a new combination. It may posit a positive pairing, a state or a compositional rule as an axiom, provided the resulting programme is noncircular and has nontrivial realizations. We are not requiring a derivation of every axiom from no assumptions. Nor does Clay require uniqueness of all dimensionless data or a derivation of why nature has four observable dimensions: it requires the stipulated four-dimensional construction.

The stronger Copernican ambition is to make formerly independent structures consequences of fewer joint principles. That ambition guides which constructions we prefer; it must not become an additional barrier to recognizing a valid theorem. An explanation of factual actualization, the ontology of probability, the origin of every constant, or cosmological history is not an admission test for the Yang–Mills proof.

## The reversal: explain the arena and its action together

The question is not only which trajectories are allowed inside a given arena. It is what makes an arena of mutually distinguishable events available, what a local description retains, and why its transformations admit the familiar quantum grammar.

The foundational wager is that several structures usually supplied separately are representations of one law: causal compatibility, observable localization, positive state evaluation, clock evolution, and the invariant cost of excitation. A successful theory would not add an arena-making field to an otherwise unchanged arena. It would explain why fields, geometry and their dynamical relations become available together.

Start with directed composition as a candidate primitive, rather than universal reversibility. Seek local symmetry as the automorphism or stabilizer structure of a realization. This does not mean that an asymmetric object explains itself: the rule selecting its asymmetry is part of the construction. Nor does it identify unitarity, time-reversal symmetry, symplectic geometry and integrability; these are different predicates whose relations must be proved.

The reason for pursuing this wager is its possible explanatory economy, not a proof that existing axioms are inconsistent. The hard [[clay-contract-and-scale-assumptions|Clay return contract]] remains fixed: for every compact simple gauge group, construct the stipulated quantum Yang–Mills theory, its gauge-invariant observables and short-distance behavior, and a positive physical gap on the continuum, infinite-volume carrier. The choice of construction primitives is open. A derivation of why nature selects a particular gauge group is a further question, not a substitute for that family of constructions.

## No privileged whole, no privileged local container

Use **whole** for the register in which compatibility among presentations is posed, and **local** for a specified register of accessible distinctions. Neither initially means a spatial size. The whole need not be a largest observable algebra, a global observer, or a preferred simultaneous assignment of all values.

Global and local are not assumed to be two coordinate charts related by an invertible transformation. Restriction can genuinely lose distinctions; different presentations can carry different constraints. The proposed invariance is that one law governs their comparison and composition without privileging either description as an absolute inventory of what exists.

Thus absence of a global section need not mean absence of a global object or state. A boundary charge is not the same type as a local mass parameter. A closed-system constraint is not an assertion that nothing happens. [[program-core/operation-registers|The operation register]] and [[algebra/local-global-individuation|local–global individuation]] own these distinctions.

The guiding principle is **covariance within equivalent presentations, and a controlled realization law across inequivalent ones**. The latter may be noninvertible without being arbitrary.

## A candidate master object: a geometry of processes and comparisons

Seek a compositional structure \(\mathsf P\), with a declared wide subgroupoid \(W\) of presentation equivalences, contextual access maps, and coherent comparison squares. Its objects are initially presentations or boundary conditions, not spacetime points. Its arrows distinguish physical processing, access restriction and mere changes of presentation.

A square compares processing followed by readout with readout followed by the corresponding represented process. Whether the square commutes, commutes up to a specified comparison, or carries a controlled defect is mathematical data. It cannot be decided by calling every arrow “descent.”

A category, bicategory or double category is a candidate language for this structure, not a prescribed answer. A stack may organize reversible gluing; it does not itself create irreversible dynamics. Octonionic products may occur inside a candidate, but a nonzero associator does not provide either an arrow of time or the coherence laws for process composition.

The strategic target is a **joint realization**:

\[
\mathfrak C
\longmapsto
\bigl(\text{access structure},\text{observable algebra},
\text{positive pairing},\text{state},\text{dynamics}\bigr).
\]

This is a return signature, not a theorem about a bare category. Each candidate must expose its primitive rules and all remaining choices. If an arbitrary Hamiltonian can still be appended without changing those rules, the claimed joint selection has not occurred; this does not invalidate a separately proved construction that acknowledges the Hamiltonian as input. The [[directed-realization-and-foundational-restart|foundational restart]] supplies the existing worked examples and their unresolved dependencies.

The observable entry must retain the invariant contractions of the
declared multiplication, not just its positive metric. A realization
can preserve every clock readout while forgetting such contractions.
Thus temporal compatibility and algebraic coverage are separate tests
of the same proposed object; neither can be used to define away a
sector required by the other.

## The invariant to seek: composition with accounted loss

The most concrete available template is [[algebra/moving-response-balance-and-a-ruble-operator-signature|moving response balance]]. In a finite-dimensional analytic realization, let \(V_X\) carry a positive response form \(\Gamma_X\), and let a contextual comparison have tangent map \(J:V_X\to V_Y\). When the construction proves response contractivity, define

\[
D_J:=\Gamma_X-J^*\Gamma_YJ\geq0.
\]

This form acts on incoming distinctions. It measures their lost response, not an energy or a factual outcome. For composable \(J\) and \(K\), the exact identity is

\[
\boxed{D_{KJ}=D_J+J^*D_KJ.}
\]

The accumulated defect is independent of how the same composite map is subdivided, provided each contribution is transported to the same incoming carrier. This is a candidate grammar for an invariant across changing access: the retained contribution alone need not be conserved, but its relation to the defect composes lawfully.

For a non-null tangent \(v\), the dimensionless fraction

\[
\eta_J(v)=\frac{D_J[v]}{\Gamma_X[v]}
\]

survives a common change of response units and compatible changes of presentation. It is not yet a universal constant or a mass. The law is algebraically available for many unrelated systems; its physical content would come from a primitive geometry forcing \(J\), the forms, and their normalization together.

The [[descent-loss-cocycle-and-recovery-fork|relative-entropy loss cocycle]] supplies one exact information-geometric realization. Its recovery fork is essential: completely erased distinctions have no retained tangent, while exactly recoverable distinctions can have zero loss. Mass cannot be assigned to either class by simply renaming that one-channel defect.

The [[directed-isometric-residue-completion/inq|retained-plus-residue completion]] preserves a norm in an enlarged mathematical carrier. This neither makes the discarded data accessible nor proves a unitary ontology. In infinite-dimensional physical realizations, positivity of a form difference does not alone imply closability; domains, quotient compatibility and completion must be proved.

## The infinitesimal question includes the changing yardstick

On a smooth positive response family, write \(\dot v=B_uv\). Here \(u\) labels a process path, not yet elapsed clock time. The finite balance has the differential form

\[
\boxed{\dot\Gamma+B^*\Gamma+\Gamma B+R=0,
\qquad R\geq0.}
\]

The response \(\Gamma\) and the represented motion \(B\) enter together. Decaying coordinate components need not mean intrinsic loss if the response changes compensatingly. Conversely, an unchanged coordinate can acquire a different response. This is the precise opening for investigating the arena of measurement alongside what appears to move in it.

The displayed identity is not offered as a new field equation already solved. Given arbitrary \(\Gamma\) and \(B\), it merely defines a candidate \(R\), whose positivity is an additional condition. A new constitutive law must constrain the entire package, including the reversible part of \(B\) that the loss does not determine.

An operator-shaped **Ruble candidate** compares two independently returned forms on the same tangents. If \(C\) is positive definite and has the same process-density weight as \(R\), then

\[
\mathscr R=C^{-1/2}RC^{-1/2},
\qquad
\lambda_*=
\inf_{v\ne0}\frac{R[v]}{C[v]}.
\]

The generalized spectrum is invariant under compatible frame changes and increasing reparameterizations. This supplies the requested type of quotient: a response compared with a response, not merely two dimensionally compatible numbers. Choosing \(C=R\) would make the answer tautological; the same primitive law must select the denominator without reference to the desired edge. Closed physical realizations require the domain and comparison theorems in [[internal-yardstick-as-a-generalized-rate-edge|the generalized-rate-edge construction]].

## Time and stationary action become representation questions

Distinguish precedence of processes from an additive clock parameter. An optional fact-bearing extension would also construct record extensions that preserve earlier readouts and cannot be undone by an admissible return. Such a record law is not inferred from entropy monotonicity or nonassociativity, and is not an extra requirement of Clay.

The [[algebra/quotient-clock-and-stationary-action|quotient-clock theorem]] gives a concrete precedent for the reversal. A one-sided process, an invariant complex positive semidefinite pairing, continuity and quotient-surjectivity can return

\[
qT_s=U_sq,
\]

with a unitary quotient group \(U_s\). Its positive complex structure supplies a symplectic state form, and its generator supplies a stationary state action. That action does not determine the discarded histories. The theorem derives this representation from specified inputs; it does not yet derive those inputs, positive energy, spatial locality, or four-dimensional Yang–Mills.

The objective is therefore not to abolish stationary action, but to investigate whether it is a local representation of a more general law. Returning a valid Yang–Mills theory does not additionally require deriving every familiar variational formulation. Nor will entropy-gradient flow be imposed as a universal replacement. A susceptibility metric does not select a vector field or its pace. Modular flow, scale flow, Euclidean attenuation and Lorentzian translations must be connected by constructed maps rather than called one time.

## Mass as joint rigidity; the gap as exclusion of soft escape

After a positive-energy Poincare representation exists, mass belongs to its joint translation invariant, not to whichever generator has a convenient positive spectrum. The [[joint-causal-generators-and-the-mass-casimir|opposed-null witness]] shows how two individually gapless causal directions can possess a positive invariant product. Its mass is supplied as input; the programme must explain why the physically realized sectors exclude arbitrarily small values.

The central conjecture is **uniform joint rigidity of physical realization**: the primitive composition and access law permits no sequence of normalized nonvacuum physical distinctions whose invariant dynamical cost tends to zero. This is stronger than a unique vacuum, nonzero loss, a monotone entropy, or a trivial limiting description. It is not a pixel size or a demand that all excited masses be discrete.

The exact return test is conditional. Let \(P_0\) project onto the unique vacuum, and let

\[
\mathcal C:=H^2-c^2\mathbf P^2
\]

be the reconstructed energy-squared Casimir. Let \(\mathcal D\) be a form core for \(\mathfrak c[\psi]=\|\mathcal C^{1/2}\psi\|^2\) on the complete physical vacuum complement. Let the dimensionless closed response form \(\mathfrak d\) have domain containing \(\mathcal D\). For an independently calibrated \(E_*>0\), seek independent derivations on \(\mathcal D\) of

\[
\mathfrak d[\psi]\geq\kappa\|(I-P_0)\psi\|^2,
\qquad
\|\mathcal C^{1/2}\psi\|^2
\geq\eta E_*^2\mathfrak d[\psi],
\qquad \kappa,\eta>0.
\]

The [[global-local-response-reconstruction/qft-recovery-contract|recovery contract]] owns the carrier and limiting obligations. With the required domains and Poincare spectrum condition, these estimates imply

\[
H\geq E_*\sqrt{\eta\kappa}\,(I-P_0).
\]

The challenge is to derive the estimates, not postulate them under geometric names. The physical norm must come from the observable realization, not be defined to equal \(\mathfrak d\). The reference \(E_*\) must be calibrated independently of the unknown mass gap. For regulator constructions, a positive lower bound in that fixed calibration must survive the required limits alongside a nontrivial continuum theory.

Any proposed proof must fail on a hidden arbitrarily soft physical sector even if a coarse description forgets it. The [[two-scale-rg-descent-and-the-crossover-lemma|two-scale analysis]] supplies the existing rigorous framework for testing that failure. A lossless visible sector can also remain soft: complete retained-plus-residue accounting does not by itself charge it.

## Constants and cosmology remain questions, not concealed inputs

Keep quantity lines and conversion maps visible. Setting \(\hbar=c=1\) is a permitted coordinate convention after the relevant structures exist; it is not an explanation of their origin. The [[hbar-clock-and-the-calibration-firewall|clock and calibration analysis]] separates an abstract spectral parameter, an observable duration, and an action-to-phase comparison.

The programme neither assumes that \(\hbar\) is ontologically primitive nor concludes that it can be discarded. Any proposed variability must have an operational consequence in dimensionless comparisons, not merely a different choice of units. Similarly, uncertainty inequalities constrain observable statistics; they do not, by themselves, require an ontology of vacuum jitter. The programme remains open about underlying determinism while retaining the obligation to recover quantum observations.

Cosmology is an admissible second realization of the same upstream structure. Ask whether the same process family returns both cosmic response and localized excitation structure, and whether a dimensionless relation links them. Do not exclude that question because one realization appears large and another small.

But a fitted Hubble history, horizon size, temperature or gravitational constant cannot secretly select the pure Yang–Mills gap. A proposed cosmological parent must recover the Clay member in a stated regime without weakening its flat-spacetime and infinite-volume requirements. [[global-local-response-reconstruction/cosmological-reconvergence-contract|Cosmological reconvergence]] states the separate comparison problem. Numerical coincidence is a clue to test, not the construction of a common origin.

## The next construction: joint selection with restricted primitives

Begin from a worked whole-to-local member, not from a fresh catalogue of suggestive objects. [[algebra/primitive-overlap-refinement-and-the-emergent-diffusion|Primitive overlap refinement]] already derives a complete diffusion from a specified Jordan orbit and comparison rule. It still imports the compact arena and symmetric law; its refinement is not a four-dimensional field limit.

The next experiment is to construct one nontrivial process–context comparison square in which a directed primitive rule fixes the readout, positive pairing and compatible observable dynamics together. Seek a noncommutative algebraic realization and account for distinctions invisible in one context but visible in another. Derive one dimensionless relation among the resulting responses or generators that was not used to choose the inputs. No prior identification with an actual laboratory or cosmological process is required.

Then perform the **independent-retuning test**: keep the complete primitive data fixed and try to change one claimed output clock, relative response or spectral relation. If this remains freely possible, the construction has not made that joint selection. This is a test of the claimed derivation, not a demand that no legitimate family parameters exist. A relation fixed only by choosing its coefficients after observing the output does not pass.

Two newer constructions make this test executable. [[gauge-boundary-frame-gluing/shared-driver-response-and-the-nested-holonomy-clock|The shared-driver member]] derives a clock from an actual path-shift entropy Hessian and its declared dualization, but its state, shift action and prescription remain inputs. [[algebra/oriented-projection-cycles-and-joint-response|Oriented projection cycles]] compute loss and coherent phase from one ordered geometry, with a sharp relation between them. However, their converse theorem realizes every pair in a sectorial cone, and smooth refinement can remove loss while retaining phase.

There is therefore a second, **disguised-input test**: does the allowed primitive family merely encode arbitrary desired output operators? Passing the fixed-primitive test does not answer this. The next law must restrict admissible geometry, comparison or refinement independently enough to constrain a returned relation; it need not eliminate legitimate family parameters.

[[algebra/unbounded-projection-cycles-and-inherited-spectra|The closed-graph theorem]] proves that this is not just a finite-matrix concern. The same ordered triangle realizes every nonnegative self-adjoint operator, including arbitrary continuous excitation thresholds. Its strong limits and finite-loss domain are controlled, but its spectrum is inherited from the graph input. The analytic construction succeeds while the spectral selection remains open.

[[trace-dirichlet-descent/conditional-score-shorting-and-observable-lifts|The conditional-score construction]] tests a different selecting law: compatibility with normalized restriction to a declared boundary sector, including its inherited source family. supplies known concave spherical components and an explicit
posterior variance budget. It also rules out a uniform
fractional margin: the two terms grow together with
preparation time, while their difference stays finite.
The lesson for primitive selection is to constrain the
correlated cancellation, not to posit separately bounded
loss and response terms or identify either with mass.

[[coarse-response-memory/certified-ground-marginal-and-late-preparation|A certified additive estimate]]
now controls the full compact ground marginal and an entire
preparation trajectory at one explicit coupling. Its proof
ties harmonic multiplication, the inherited kinetic blocks and
conditional integration together; exact finite residuals control
all omitted modes and entire time intervals, including the cubic
initial cancellation. The gain is a proved constraint on an actual
prepared state, not a new choice of dynamical primitive.
[[coarse-response-memory/pointed-preparation-stability-and-the-volume-test|The normalization audit]]
shows why the successful global norm cannot simply be reused
uniformly in volume: it grows even on independent gapped copies.
[[coarse-response-memory/connected-preparation-and-local-normalization|The connected assembly equation]]
instead cancels independent contributions exactly.
[[coarse-response-memory/kinetic-smoothing-and-connected-fourier-control|Its kinetic majorant]]
now constructs the actual connected preparation in a small-source
regime, uniformly under spatial extension. Nonabelian Casimirs
control the derivative cost before matrix channels and supports
are summed. The instantaneous map fails in the same norm;
the kinetic response cannot be independently discarded.
[[coarse-response-memory/kinetic-hessian-bootstrap-and-uniform-response|The complementary Hessian estimate]]
then gives direct uniform conditional-response control in its
own explicit \(SU(2)\) regime. These are constraints derived
from the shared supplied dynamics, not selection of a new
primitive Hamiltonian. The remaining task is a law that retains
such control relative to interacting blocks and along the
continuum trajectory, where the present smallness conditions
do not apply.

[[coarse-response-memory/interacting-reference-and-spectral-product-control|The first interacting-block extension]]
preserves the exact assembly equation but exposes the additional
compatibility needed: a block gap does not control multiplication
of its eigenfunctions. A checked gapped reference violates the
Haar spectral triangle. Re-centering works under a separate
reference-drift bound; it is not an automatic escape from smallness.
The next certificate must govern products, response and full
boundary-charge channels together. This makes the selecting
obligation more concrete without deriving a new primitive.
[[coarse-response-memory/block-spectral-moments-and-connected-assembly|The replacement assembly theorem]]
reduces this particular estimate to four one-block weighted product
moments and explicit derivative bounds. It permits high-energy
product channels and prevents a loss factor multiplying once per
block. These hypotheses still require proof on the full Wilson
blocks; their tensorization is no longer the open step.

[[coarse-response-memory/first-order-lift-and-spectral-product-tails|The first-order product test]]
then removes two unnecessary assumptions for fixed insertions:
neither an interacting gap nor scalar eigenbasis summation is
needed to control transfer between energy windows. The weighted
gradient and its adjoint factor the actual kinetic operator;
the multiplication commutator supplies an all-coupling bound.
[[coarse-response-memory/charged-link-probes-and-vacuum-spectral-width|Its charged vacuum test]]
also fixes spectral width from the state's link score, uniformly
across representation labels. The foundational gain is a shared
constraint on product, pairing and response. The factorization
still starts from a supplied kinetic geometry; it does not select
that geometry, produce nonassociative dynamics or close the
nonlinear assembly merely by changing the carrier.

The next tests distinguish norm choice from state choice.
[[coarse-response-memory/transition-score-and-lipschitz-product-control|Transition-score covariance]]
controls genuine nonlinear response in a derivative quotient,
where a second-order Hilbert replacement fails by concentration.
But [[coarse-response-memory/product-boundary-frames-and-crossing-susceptibility|the boundary-state test]]
shows that exact independent regional vacua still leave an
unsuppressed neutral crossing response. Whole gauge projection
cannot add correlations to an already invariant product vector.
The sharper primitive target is therefore a compatible joint
boundary state and response law—not merely independently
solvable pieces with a larger local spectral number.

The remaining continuum and physical-gap tests cannot be replaced by
the fixed-graph construction. The decisive controls are already
available: a massless field, a massive field with known threshold,
a finite-box gap that closes, and a coarse map hiding soft excitations.
These test different failures; none is replaced by another finite
algebraic coefficient check.

Exceptional Jordan geometry, \(S^6\), \(A_2\) singularities, global inversion obstructions and twisted spectral geometry remain candidate suppliers of the primitive rule. Each must earn its place by forcing a composition, pairing, compatibility or rigidity result. Their names, dimensions and obstruction classes are not themselves mass spectra.

## How the programme learns

Treat the workspace as a working mathematical dependency graph. Improve existing definitions and proofs before adding new terminology; lift genuinely shared results into reusable modules; preserve raw chats, attachments and superseded derivations as immutable evidence. Keep the present schema as the ordered proposal, with proofs owned by the linked specialist notes.

Read primary sources to establish exact hypotheses, discover reusable mathematics and test novelty—not to inherit an ontology unexamined. The failure to solve Clay does not make every existing theorem suspect, and unfamiliar mathematics does not make a proposal foundational. Apply the [[program-core/claim-and-failure-contract|same type, carrier, tangent, normalization, prescription and scope tests]] equally to the literature and to our own ideas.

The programme succeeds philosophically when one law explains why several formerly independent structures belong together. It contributes mathematically when that law forces a new, checkable consequence. It solves the Clay problem only when the specified Yang–Mills theory exists and its physical gap has been proved.

The Copernican wager is not that mass is a new substance beneath spacetime. It is that **mass is an invariant of the law by which a physical arena and its distinctions become jointly realizable**—and that the gap is forced by the impossibility of making that realization arbitrarily soft.
