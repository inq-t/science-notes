# Directed Realization: A Foundational Restart

The next foundational task is to find one law that jointly selects an observable quotient, its positive form and its nontrivial dynamics. The existing mathematics shows how irreversible processing can coexist with reversible observable evolution, but also proves that forgetting alone leaves the spectrum arbitrary. Progress now means reducing an independent choice in that interface, rather than adding another sufficient estimate to a supplied Wilson theory.

## The minimum distinction between the two kinds of time

Begin with a category \(\mathsf P\) of presentations and admissible processes, and a declared wide subgroupoid \(W\) of presentation equivalences. A stack may organize reversible gluing. Genuine directed processes require additional arrows; a torsor or groupoid alone is not a one-way dynamics.

[[algebra/local-global-individuation|Local–global individuation]] supplies the factual return signature

\[
\mathcal R:\mathsf P\longrightarrow\mathsf{Rec}_{\rm mono}.
\]

A fact-producing arrow must properly extend records while preserving earlier readouts, with no admissible return that undoes that extension modulo the declared equivalences. This gives precedence, not elapsed seconds. A record law is an obligation of the *factive* programme, not an extra axiom silently added to Clay.

The observable return is a different construction. Schematically, a candidate primitive law \(\mathfrak C\) must determine

\[
\operatorname{Real}(\mathfrak C)
=\bigl(\mathcal K,g,N=\operatorname{rad}g,\mathcal H_g,
\text{state},\text{observable action},\text{dynamics}\bigr).
\]

Here \(\mathcal K\) is a specified linear presentation carrier, \(g\) a positive semidefinite form, and \(\mathcal H_g\) the completion of \(\mathcal K/N\). This is an interface to be constructed, not a claim that the tuple already follows from a bare category. [[program-core/response-registers|The response declaration contract]] specifies what each form and operator must act on.

There is no need to add a literal fifth spacetime coordinate. Before realization, context variation and process composition may be different categorical directions. Only a further theorem can identify a returned parameter with the fourth coordinate of Lorentzian spacetime.

## The exact bridge worth starting from

Suppose a one-sided semigroup \(T_s\) of linear maps on \(\mathcal K\), \(s\geq0\), preserves \(g\), is strongly continuous in its seminorm, and satisfies

\[
T_s\mathcal K+N=\mathcal K.
\]

Then [[algebra/quotient-unitarity-and-kernel-stabilization|the quotient theorem]] makes its induced maps a strongly continuous unitary semigroup on \(\mathcal H_g\). The quotient inverses extend it to a group even if no upstream inverse exists. Thus reversible observable transport need not be a primitive property of the whole presentation.

This is a real algebraic realization of the proposed reversal. It does **not** determine the sign of the generator, its physical calibration, a vacuum, spacelike locality or a mass gap. Nor does a semigroup with an externally supplied real parameter derive clock duration merely by passing to a quotient.

One can instead seek positive-energy evolution through reflection-positive reconstruction, or by a direct Lorentzian construction. [[algebra/os-descent-naturality-and-clock-no-go|OS naturality]] controls the former route once its input is supplied. It does not select the input law. A realization map may intertwine translations; an individual localized event need not commute with a fixed clock, because translation generally moves the event and its region.

## Why a residue is not yet a mass

The same OS audit gives a decisive negative control. Fix the forgetting map

\[
r:\mathcal H\oplus\mathcal N\twoheadrightarrow\mathcal H,
\qquad r(x,n)=x.
\]

For arbitrary nonnegative self-adjoint \(K\) and \(L\),

\[
r\bigl(e^{-sK}\oplus e^{-sL}\bigr)=e^{-sK}r.
\]

The kernel, its dimension and nonfaithfulness are unchanged while the local spectrum can be gapped or gapless. Therefore “there is a forgotten background” cannot by itself select mass. Likewise, an idempotent forgetting map cannot descend to a nontrivial unitary clock: its descended map remains idempotent, and an invertible idempotent is the identity.

The missing ingredient is a **law relating composition to retained response** that disallows this independent replacement of \(K\). It could be introduced as a rigid constitutive principle before metaphysical necessity is established. It must constrain more than the vocabulary of the carrier.

## What could be conserved across the relation?

The [[directed-isometric-residue-completion/inq|defect composition law]] supplies an exact candidate grammar. For contractions \(A_p:\mathcal H_x\to\mathcal H_y\) and \(A_q:\mathcal H_y\to\mathcal H_z\), set \(A_{qp}:=A_qA_p\) and define

\[
B_p=I-A_p^*A_p.
\]

Then

\[
B_{qp}=B_p+A_p^*B_qA_p.
\]

This relates retained response and accumulated defect under composition. It is more informative than calling either side “non-being.” But it presupposes its norms and contractions; the equation alone does not identify a defect with entropy, an actual record, energy or gravity. A successful foundational law would select the carriers and forms and make such a balance constrain the reconstructed dynamics.

[[program-core/record-scale-soldering|Record–scale soldering]] offers a different candidate: compositional scale ratios \(r(qp)=r(q)r(p)\), or additive logarithms. A scale cocycle, a defect form and an entropy increment are not interchangeable. Their relation needs a natural map on the *same process family*. This is the appropriate place to include a cosmological realization, without using a fitted cosmic history to select the desired local spectrum.

## What the geometric clues must supply

The [[algebra/cst-cwst-closure-audit|CST/CWST closure audit]] already identifies the critical missing arrows. A selected real locus, for example, does not select its involution or produce a Lorentzian history. The same discipline applies to the proposed primitive geometries:

- An \(A_2\) monodromy is invertible transport. A declared one-sided process through its geometry would be additional structure.
- An \(S^6\) construction must specify which complex presentation, reduction and realization map it uses. The name of the manifold does not determine a physical positive form or translation generator.
- A locally invertible but globally noninjective map can obstruct reconstructing a branch. Even granting the proposed map, this is not yet a probability law, a factual selection rule or spectral coercivity.
- An octonionic associator records failure of associativity. It does not by itself orient a history or define an irreversible operator.

[[sufficient-reason/sufficing-and-necessitating-reason-MASTER|Sufficing and necessitating reason]] motivates the useful distinction between a law of possible readouts and an obtained fact. Its stronger transitions remain hypotheses: Gleason representation does not select a unique state, existence of characters does not select an obtained character, and an initial-algebra structure isomorphism is not a theorem of temporal reversibility. The exact separation in [[algebra/local-global-individuation|individuation]] should govern their use here. An inaccessible reason is compatible with this programme; its inaccessibility is not a construction of it.

## The shape of the eventual bound

The required rigidity is quantitative, not just a prohibition on perfect reversibility. After the physical positive generator \(K\) and vacuum \(\Omega\) are reconstructed, the target is

\[
\inf_{\substack{\psi\perp\Omega,\ \|\psi\|=1\\
\psi\in\operatorname{Dom}(K^{1/2})}}
\|K^{1/2}\psi\|^2>0.
\]

Every nonvacuum direction having some positive cost is weaker: normalized directions may still have costs approaching zero. The primitive law must rule out this escape and retain that exclusion under the gluing, refinement and volume limits required by the physical return. This is a useful meaning of a **scale gap**: a uniform lower response relative to the realized normalization, not a minimum coordinate interval. The displayed inequality states the target; it is not a replacement axiom that proves it.

## A finite restart with a stopping condition

1. **Freeze the return contract.** Use [[clay-contract-and-scale-assumptions|the attached-statement audit]]. Deriving a larger ontology is permitted; recovering only a cutoff approximation is not completion of Clay.
2. **Choose one existing primitive candidate.** Write its carrier, composition, equivalences and proposed invariant explicitly. Declare every state, kernel, form and parameter still free. Do not merge all candidate geometries by analogy.
3. **Make one joint selection.** Construct a worked member in which the same law constrains realization and nontrivial observable dynamics. Test whether the local spectrum can still be changed arbitrarily without changing the stated primitive data. If so, the claimed selection has failed.
4. **Calculate an independent return.** Derive one normalized spectral or two-point relation not used to choose the member. Factive claims additionally require persistent records; positive energy and locality require their own return theorems. A nontrivial quotient alone is not this result.
5. **Re-enter the physical estimates only when warranted.** Ask whether the selected dynamics recovers Yang–Mills and excludes arbitrarily soft nonvacuum excitations through its required limits. Use the existing Wilson and response results where their hypotheses actually match.

The immediate target is not a glueball number or another name for a lower-frame assumption. It is one explicit example where the proposed global–local law fixes something previously independent. [[program-core/explanatory-economy|The independent-return standard]] and [[program-core/claim-and-failure-contract|claim and failure contract]] make this test precise. A rigid new posit with a worked return is admissible; freely appending the desired Hamiltonian is not an explanation of it. That is a modest enough first construction to attempt, and a strong enough one to change the direction of the programme.
