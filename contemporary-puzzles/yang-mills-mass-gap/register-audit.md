# Register Audit

The audit asks, for each object in the conventional presentation of the mass-gap problem, which register it lives in, and where the presentation changes register without saying so. Three such changes are found. Each converts an ordinary theorem-shaped hole into an apparent paradox. Removing them does not shorten the proof; it removes the sense that a proof should not be needed.

## Error 1 — the register of the fields

The Yang–Mills Lagrangian is written in terms of a connection \(A\). Perturbation theory quantizes \(A\), finds massless gluon propagators, and then must explain why no massless states appear. But \(A\) is not an observable: it transforms under the gauge group, and the physical algebra is the invariant quotient — Wilson loops, local gauge-invariant composites, and their descendants. The statement "the gluon is massless" is a statement about the pre-quotient register. In the observable register there is no gluon and nothing is massless. **[STANDARD]**

The paradox is therefore an artifact of computing in a register that contains no facts. [[program-core/physical-quotient|The physical quotient]] declares gauge directions removed before any physical statement is made, and this problem is the sharpest instance of why that declaration matters. The mainstream mechanism literature independently locates the gap at the boundary of the quotient: gauge fixing on the full space of connections has Gribov copies, restriction to the fundamental modular region introduces a horizon, and the horizon condition deforms the gluon propagator to a positivity-violating form with a mass parameter (Gribov 1978; Zwanziger 1989; the refined scenario adds condensates). That is a wall in configuration space producing a scale — the programme's grammar — and it is a scenario, not a theorem. **[CONJECTURE — external scenario]**

## Error 2 — the register of the vacuum

The perturbative vacuum is the Fock vacuum of the free theory. Haag's theorem states that two Wightman theories whose sharp-time fields are related by a unitary at one time are unitarily equivalent throughout; hence an interacting Wightman theory cannot share the free theory's Fock representation, and the interaction picture does not exist. **[CONDITIONAL THEOREM]** For Yang–Mills the statement is conditional twice over: the interacting Wightman theory is the object whose existence the problem asks for, so its inequivalence to Fock is asserted of an unconstructed theory; and gauge-fixed perturbation theory lives in an indefinite-metric space with ghosts, outside Haag's hypotheses, so the theorem diagnoses the perturbative register from the outside rather than governing it. What survives the conditions is the diagnosis: if the theory exists, a gap is a property of its GNS representation, and every tool that can calculate lives in a different one. Renormalized perturbation theory shifts poles order by order; what it cannot do is change folium.

The programme's phrase "the symmetric model cannot capture the pre-observable ground" is a statement of this kind, and Haag's theorem is its rigorous, conditional form. Note the direction: this does not make the vacuum mysterious. An interacting Wightman vacuum is a cyclic, separating state on every local algebra, the most structured object in the theory, and the difficulty is purely that it lives where the computable tools do not.

## Error 3 — the register of the proof

The gap is a statement about the spectrum of a Hamiltonian, a Lorentzian, real-time object. Every method that has ever produced a gap rigorously is Euclidean: a statistical measure, a cluster expansion, a transfer matrix. The two registers are welded by exactly one structure, reflection positivity, which is a one-sided pairing across a time-slice — a wall in the programme's sense. The Hilbert space is the half-space algebra modulo the null vectors of \(\langle\theta F,F\rangle\ge0\); positivity of energy and the Hamiltonian are outputs of the wall, not inputs of the measure. **[STANDARD]**

Across that wall the transfer operator \(T=e^{-aH}\) is a positive non-invertible map from one slice to the next, and the gap is its contraction residue, \(ma=-\ln(\lambda_1/\lambda_0)\). [[lorentzian-spectral-envelope/inq|The envelope module]] owns the dictionary. The conventional presentation states the gap in the Lorentzian register and searches for it there; the proof, if it comes, will be a statement that a Euclidean residue is uniformly bounded away from zero as the lattice is removed. Stating the target in the wrong register is why the problem reads as a paradox rather than as an estimate that nobody can make.

## Where the audit places causal scale theory

The programme has one exact theorem in this neighbourhood and one correctly typed identification. The theorem is that a dilation-covariant generator has no gap ([[the-grain-of-causal-scale/causal-spectrum|the pre-wall no-gap theorem]]): before a wall breaks scale covariance there is nothing to find, and classical Yang–Mills in four dimensions is dilation covariant. The identification is that the mass gap is the residue of a descent across a wall — which in the transfer-matrix register is the definition of the gap, not a discovery, and is graded as a definition wherever it appears in these notes. Both are correctly placed. Neither shortens the proof.

## The condition under which the charge reverses

The audit's three errors have a common form: a quantity is computed in a register that cannot contain it. The programme commits the same error if it proposes a gap mechanism whose scale is supplied by its own ultraviolet grain, because on a lattice a gap is the trivial case and the problem's content is the grain's removal; if it asserts a mass in MeV from an algebraic structure, because [[deriving-value-of-g/obstructions-to-an-unconditional-proof|the dimensional obstruction]] forbids it and the correctly typed output is a pure number; or if it states the gap in a Lorentzian register while its wall construction remains Euclidean or absent, since [[deriving-g-v2/inq|the Lorentzian realization of the core pre-wall]] is an open item in the programme's own ledger. [[mass-gap-no-gos]] states these as obstructions with their kill conditions.
