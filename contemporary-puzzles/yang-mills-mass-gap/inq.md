# Yang–Mills Existence and the Mass Gap

The popular paradox — “the Lagrangian has massless gluons, so what gives them mass?” — contains a real category error: it demands that a gauge-invariant spectral gap appear as a mass term or pole of a gauge-dependent field. The clean geometric replacement is now explicit at finite lattice regulator: after Gauss descent and a ground-state transform, the physical gap is exactly a Poincare constant of the vacuum-weighted electric-flux form. Its real multiplication sector has a classical Fisher-score realization through the Hellinger embedding. None of this makes the Yang--Mills existence-and-gap theorem a category error. It leaves the hard task intact: construct the continuum theory and retain a positive coercivity bound in physical RG units, uniformly in volume and regulator removal.

Claim labels follow [[program-core/axioms-and-principles#Status vocabulary|the programme-wide status vocabulary]] and its composition rule. The puzzle statement in [[puzzle-as-posed]] is sourced to the literature and can be used by a reader who rejects everything below it.

## The argument in order

**1. The problem is an existence theorem, not a puzzle.** The Clay statement asks for a nontrivial quantum Yang--Mills theory on \(\mathbb R^4\), for any compact simple gauge group, satisfying axioms at least as strong as the standard Wightman framework, with a spectral gap \(\Delta>0\) above the vacuum. Numerical evidence is strong: lattice calculations place the pure-\(\mathrm{SU}(3)\) \(0^{++}\) glueball near \(1.65\)–\(1.73\,\mathrm{GeV}\) after a scale convention is chosen, and compute low-lying dimensionless ratios to a few percent. What is missing is a construction and proof. [[puzzle-as-posed]] separates existence, gap, nontriviality, and numerical evidence.

**2. A gap is not a failure of continuum mathematics.** The statement \(E_H((0,\Delta))=0\) is expressible without strain on continuous \(\mathbb R^4\). A free massive field already supplies an elementary continuum example: the vacuum is isolated, a one-particle shell occurs at positive mass, and multiparticle spectrum lies above it. A mass gap therefore requires neither discrete spacetime nor an ultraviolet grain.

**3. A gauge-dependent massless pole is not the physical gap target.** **[STANDARD]** The connection \(A\) and a gauge-fixed gluon propagator are not gauge-invariant observables. The physical target belongs to the vacuum representation of the gauge-invariant observable algebra, or to an equivalent constrained or BRST construction, and is not determined by the absence of an \(A^2\) term or by a gauge-dependent propagator pole. This removes the demand that a physical gap must look like a generated gluon mass. It does *not* make gauge reduction a gap mechanism: free Maxwell theory has gauge-equivalence classes and gauge-invariant field strengths while remaining massless. [[program-core/physical-quotient|The physical quotient]] types the reduction; the observable state and Hamiltonian must still supply the dynamics. **[CONJECTURE -- external scenario]** Gribov--Zwanziger restricts a gauge-fixed configuration space and modifies the gauge-dependent gluon propagator, typically with reflection-positivity violation. This may diagnose the absence of an asymptotic one-gluon state; it is neither a gauge-invariant spectral-gap theorem nor evidence that its mass parameter equals the physical gap.

**4. The perturbative Fock representation does not determine the interacting vacuum sector.** **[CONDITIONAL THEOREM]** If a positive-metric Wightman Yang--Mills theory exists, Haag's theorem obstructs the ordinary interaction-picture identification of its sharp-time fields with free fields in one Fock representation. The application is conditional on the very theory being constructed, while gauge-fixed perturbation theory uses an indefinite-metric auxiliary space outside Haag's hypotheses. The safe conclusion is narrower: perturbative poles and the free Fock vacuum do not by themselves determine the spectrum of the interacting observable GNS representation. This is compatible with the programme's register warning, but is not a theorem about its proposed pre-observable ground.

**5. The OS quotient constructs a carrier; transfer contraction measures the gap.** **[STANDARD]** In the Euclidean route, reflection positivity and the remaining Osterwalder--Schrader hypotheses construct the physical Hilbert space and positive-energy evolution. The OS map from positive-time functionals to the completed Hilbert space is a quotient and can be noninjective. The descended transfer operator \(T_a=e^{-aH}\), however, is injective; its inverse is generally unbounded. After normalizing the vacuum eigenvalue and writing \(P_0\) for the vacuum projection, define

$$
\widetilde T_a:=\lambda_0(T_a)^{-1}T_a,
\qquad
r_a:=\left\|\widetilde T_a(1-P_0)\right\|,
\qquad
\boxed{\Delta_a=-\frac1a\ln r_a.}
$$

At finite volume, when the next transfer eigenvalue exists, \(a\Delta_a=-\ln(\lambda_1/\lambda_0)\). [[lorentzian-spectral-envelope/inq|The Lorentzian spectral envelope]] owns the dictionary and the invertible Ising debugging model. A conditional expectation, the OS quotient, and the transfer semigroup are three different maps. Calling them all “descent” is a proposed analogy until an explicit intertwiner is built. The unresolved estimate is a positive \(\Delta_{a,L}\) uniform through infinite volume and the tuned continuum limit. For a fixed physical gap, \(1-r_a\sim a\Delta\to0\); the raw per-slice deficit does not stay bounded away from zero.

**6. The regulated gap is weighted flux coercivity.** **[EXACT -- FINITE REGULATOR]** On a finite spatial cell complex, the Kogut--Susskind electric term is a sum of compact-group link Laplacians and the magnetic term is a plaquette potential. Its unique positive vacuum \(\psi_0\) defines the gauge-invariant probability measure \(\mathrm d\nu=\psi_0^2\mathrm d\mu_{\mathrm{Haar}}\). Conjugation by \(\psi_0\) turns \(H-E_0\) into a weighted Witten/Bakry--Emery Laplacian, and the physical gap is exactly its Poincare constant. For real multiplication excitations, the denominator is also the classical Fisher norm of a mean-zero score under the Hellinger embedding; this does not represent phase directions or the full quantum BKM tangent space. [[gauge-descent-flux-fisher-coercivity]] proves the regulated identity and states the uniform continuum target. [[carrier-first-reversal]] gives the reversal tactic: construct the carrier and closed flux form first, recover the generator by the representation theorem, and derive intrinsic configuration geometry from its carré du champ afterward. [[physical-distinction-coercivity]] isolates the exact common grammar with measurement: projections ask exclusive questions, instruments govern conditioned state change, while the mass gap is coercivity on the centered physical GNS carrier. It also gives a staged operator-algebraic route: Connes--Takesaki core maps can make different presentations comparable, and Takesaki-admissible expectations can decompose a fixed faithful carrier into distinction shells, but only one independently proved joint electric-flux bound on all shells yields the gap. Orbit-space curvature is one sufficient route to the inequality, not the definition of the gap and not a necessary condition.

**7. One particular unbroken dilation-covariant generator cannot be gapped.** **[EXACT -- SCOPED]** [[the-grain-of-causal-scale/causal-spectrum|The HSMI no-gap theorem]] states that the positive translation generator \(P\) of a dilation-covariant half-sided modular inclusion has \(P=0\) or \(\sigma(P)=[0,\infty)\). A nonzero gap in that same generator therefore requires the covariance hypothesis to fail or passage to a different carrier and generator. The theorem does not say that every “pre-wall” object is gapless or that a wall supplies a gap. **[STANDARD]** Classical four-dimensional Yang--Mills is scale covariant, while the quantum scale enters through the trace anomaly, \(T^\mu{}_{\mu}\propto\beta(g)F^2\). A scale is still not a gap: in the expected chirally broken phase of massless-quark QCD, dimensional transmutation coexists with Goldstone modes.

**8. A lower-dimensional calculation supplies a structural analogue, not the theorem.** **[STANDARD -- non-rigorous calculation, lattice-corroborated]** In \(2+1\) dimensions Karabali and Nair pass to gauge-invariant variables whose Jacobian contains \(e^{2c_A S_{\mathrm{WZW}}}\); their approximation produces \(m=e^2c_A/2\pi\), and Karabali--Kim--Nair obtain string tensions close to lattice values. This resembles the programme's grammar: a change of variables leaves a nonzero measure factor, with a Casimir multiplying an available yardstick. It is a realized heuristic analogue, not a rigorous gap construction and not an execution of the four-dimensional wall conjecture.

**9. Why it does not transfer, in three parts.** **[EXACT -- dimensional, for the first]** In \(3+1\) dimensions \(e^2\) is dimensionless, so the Casimir no longer comes with a mass yardstick; the scale arises from the renormalization trajectory, schematically \(\Lambda=\mu\exp[-1/(2\beta_0g^2(\mu))]\). [[deriving-value-of-g/obstructions-to-an-unconditional-proof|The dimensional obstruction]] correctly says that dimensionless algebra alone cannot output a mass. The other differences are structural rather than impossibility theorems: the Karabali--Nair parametrization exploits the complex structure of two spatial dimensions, with no comparably controlling three-dimensional parametrization supplied here, and \(2+1\)-dimensional Yang--Mills is super-renormalizable whereas the four-dimensional ultraviolet construction is the problem itself. An algebraic contribution can legitimately target dimensionless ratios such as \(m_{0^{++}}/\sqrt\sigma\), but a prediction in MeV needs an independent scale-setting convention. [[mass-gap-no-gos]] states these obligations.

**10. The programme's present grain does not solve this problem.** **[NO-GO -- three ways]** The diagnostic \(\lambda_*\approx4.18\)–\(4.26\,\mathrm{fm}\) is thirty-five to thirty-seven times coarser than \(\hbar c/m_{0^{++}}\approx0.11\)–\(0.12\,\mathrm{fm}\), so it is not a plausible ultraviolet regulator for the displayed glueball dynamics. More fundamentally, a fixed physical grain does not furnish a theory on continuum \(\mathbb R^4\); a regulator proposal must prove a gap, volume uniformity, and persistence on the tuned removal trajectory. Reflection positivity at finite spacing does not supply those estimates. A running-grain law is already excluded by the programme's nucleosynthesis check, while dimensional transmutation is intrinsically a running-coupling phenomenon. Finally, an emergent-spacetime programme owes the exact observable Poincare covariance assumed by the problem. [[mass-gap-receipt-ledger]] records the numerical mismatch and chance-rate census without treating either as a proof about the grain. The mismatch excludes the direct identification and cutoff readings; it does not exclude the separately typed, still exploratory factorization “frozen anchor times cross-sector matching times dimensionless coercivity” in [[the-grain-of-causal-scale/inbox/causal-grain-and-the-yang-mills-gap/entry|the causal-grain gap packet]].

**11. What the reclassification predicts, and what it does not.** An argument fails structurally if it identifies a gauge-dependent gluon pole with the gauge-invariant Hamiltonian gap, treats the free Fock vacuum as the already-constructed interacting sector, or infers a Lorentzian spectrum from Euclidean data without OS reconstruction or another bridge. Absence of a reflection wall is not itself fatal to a direct Hamiltonian construction. Nuclearity is a separate high-energy phase-space condition: it can imply the split property under suitable hypotheses, but neither a gap nor nuclearity implies the other. A stronger wall-capacity condition that forces an infrared gap would be new mathematics and is correctly recorded as open.
