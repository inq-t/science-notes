# Yang–Mills Existence and the Mass Gap

The mass-gap problem is routinely presented as a paradox — a theory of massless fields that nonetheless has no massless states — and the presentation is a category error three times over. The masslessness belongs to a register that contains no observables; the perturbative vacuum belongs to a representation that, if the interacting theory exists, cannot contain the interacting one; and the gap is a Lorentzian statement proved, where it is proved at all, by Euclidean means across a reflection wall. Removing the three errors removes the paradox and leaves the theorem exactly as hard as before: no one has constructed a nontrivial four-dimensional quantum field theory at all, and the gap is a property of a construction. Causal scale theory contributes one exact theorem about where a gap cannot live, one correctly typed statement of what a gap is — which turns out to be the definition of the transfer-matrix gap, not a discovery — and one realized precedent in lower dimension; it does not contribute a mechanism for the four-dimensional gap, and the module records why its own grain cannot be one.

Claim labels follow [[program-core/axioms-and-principles#Status vocabulary|the programme-wide status vocabulary]] and its composition rule. The puzzle statement in [[puzzle-as-posed]] is sourced to the literature and can be used by a reader who rejects everything below it.

## The argument in order

**1. The problem is an existence theorem, not a puzzle.** The Clay statement asks for a nontrivial quantum Yang–Mills theory on \(\mathbb R^4\), for any compact simple gauge group, satisfying the Wightman axioms, with a spectral gap \(\Delta>0\) above the vacuum. Nothing in it is in empirical doubt: lattice computations put the pure-\(\mathrm{SU}(3)\) gap, the \(0^{++}\) glueball, at \(1.65\)–\(1.73\,\mathrm{GeV}\), and the low-lying dimensionless ratios of the spectrum are measured to a few percent. What is missing is a construction. [[puzzle-as-posed]] separates the existence clause, the gap clause, the nontriviality clause, and the measured facts.

**2. A gap is not a failure of continuum mathematics.** A spectrum \(\{0\}\cup[\Delta,\infty)\) is expressible without strain in continuum quantum field theory; a free massive field has exactly that spectrum on continuous \(\mathbb R^4\). Discrete spectral structure in continuous spacetime is the ordinary case. The intuition that the gap requires a spatial grain is the first error [[register-audit]] disposes of, and it is disposed of by an elementary example.

**3. The masslessness that makes the gap look paradoxical lives in a register that has no observables.** **[STANDARD]** The connection \(A\) is not an observable; the physical algebra is the gauge-invariant quotient. Gluons are massless only in the pre-quotient register. In the observable register nothing was ever massless, so there is no massless object whose mass the theory must generate. [[program-core/physical-quotient|The physical quotient]] types this. **[CONJECTURE — external scenario]** The mainstream mechanism literature places the gap where the programme would: the Gribov horizon, the boundary of the fundamental domain that gauge fixing cannot avoid, is a wall in configuration space, and restricting the functional integral to it deforms the propagator into a gapped, positivity-violating form (Gribov 1978; Zwanziger 1989). That scenario is not a theorem and is recorded as one.

**4. The perturbative vacuum lies in the wrong representation.** **[CONDITIONAL THEOREM]** If a Wightman Yang–Mills theory exists, Haag's theorem forbids its vacuum representation being unitarily equivalent to the Fock representation of the free theory, and the interaction picture does not exist. The statement is conditional on the very existence clause the problem asks for, and Haag's hypotheses — sharp-time fields in a positive-metric Hilbert space — are not met by gauge-fixed perturbation theory, which lives in an indefinite-metric space; so the theorem diagnoses the situation rather than governing the perturbative register directly. What it does say is that a gap, if the theory exists, is a property of the interacting GNS representation, and every tool that can calculate lives elsewhere. The programme's sentence "the symmetric model cannot capture the pre-observable ground" has this as its rigorous, conditional form.

**5. The gap is, by definition, the contraction residue of a descent across a reflection wall.** **[STANDARD]** Reflection positivity constructs the Hilbert space, positivity of energy, and the Hamiltonian from a one-sided pairing across a time-slice; Osterwalder and Seiler proved it for Wilson's lattice gauge action at every coupling. **[DEFINITION]** The transfer operator \(T=e^{-aH}\) is the positive non-invertible map across one slice, and

$$
\boxed{ma=-\ln\frac{\lambda_1(T)}{\lambda_0(T)}.}
$$

[[lorentzian-spectral-envelope/inq|The Lorentzian spectral envelope]] owns the dictionary and its finite debugging model. In the programme's vocabulary the mass gap is the residue of the descent across the wall — and this is the definition of the transfer-matrix gap, not a finding. Its value is that the programme's conjecture arrives already typed. The Millennium content is the single further clause that this residue stay bounded away from zero in physical units as \(a\to0\).

**6. Before any wall there is no gap; without broken scale covariance there is not even a scale.** **[EXACT]** [[the-grain-of-causal-scale/causal-spectrum|The pre-wall no-gap theorem]]: dilation covariance of a half-sided modular inclusion forces \(\sigma(P)=\{0\}\) or \([0,\infty)\) with no nonzero point spectrum. **[STANDARD]** Classical Yang–Mills in four dimensions is dilation covariant; the quantum theory's *scale* is the failure of that covariance to survive quantization — the trace anomaly, \(\langle T^\mu{}_\mu\rangle=\tfrac{\beta(g)}{2g}F^2\neq0\). A scale is not a gap: QCD with massless quarks has a transmuted scale and massless pions. The exact part is only the theorem; the rest is the programme's facticity threshold coinciding with a standard result.

**7. The mechanism the programme conjectures is realized one dimension down.** **[STANDARD — non-rigorous calculation, lattice-corroborated]** In \(2+1\) dimensions Karabali and Nair (1996) pass from the connection to gauge-invariant variables, and Karabali, Kim, and Nair (1998) extract the string tension; the Jacobian of the change of variables is \(e^{2c_A S_{\mathrm{WZW}}}\), with \(c_A\) the adjoint Casimir and \(2c_A\) the WZW level the change of variables induces, and it is this forced residue that makes the vacuum wavefunctional normalizable and delivers a mass parameter \(m=e^2c_A/2\pi\) at lowest order, with string tensions within a few percent of lattice values for \(\mathrm{SU}(2)\) through \(\mathrm{SU}(5)\). Residue of descent, forced nonzero by the algebra, equal to a Casimir times a yardstick: the conjecture, executed, at the grade of a non-rigorous but corroborated calculation.

**8. Why it does not transfer, in three parts.** **[EXACT — dimensional, for the first]** In \(3+1\) dimensions \(e^2\) is dimensionless, so the Jacobian keeps its Casimir and loses its yardstick, and the scale must be manufactured by the renormalization flow: \(\Lambda=\mu\exp(-1/2\beta_0g^2(\mu))\), an address of the flow, not an invariant of the algebra. [[deriving-value-of-g/obstructions-to-an-unconditional-proof|The dimensional obstruction]] already proves that dimensionless data cannot supply this; transmutation is not a counterexample but the trade the obstruction permits — one dimensionless coupling for one dimensionful scale, input count unchanged. Two further obstructions are independent of it: the Karabali–Nair parametrization uses the complex structure of two spatial dimensions, which has no analogue in three; and \(2+1\)-dimensional Yang–Mills is super-renormalizable, so its measure needs no ultraviolet renormalization. The correctly typed target for any algebraic contribution in four dimensions is therefore a pure number — \(m_{0^{++}}/\sqrt\sigma\approx3.5\)–\(3.6\) in consistent lattice units, \(m_{2^{++}}/m_{0^{++}}\approx1.4\) — never a mass in MeV. [[mass-gap-no-gos]] states the obstructions this imposes on the programme itself.

**9. The programme's grain is not a scale in this problem.** **[NO-GO — three ways]** The grain \(\lambda_*\approx4.18\)–\(4.26\,\mathrm{fm}\) is thirty-five to thirty-seven times coarser than the gap's correlation length \(\hbar c/m_{0^{++}}\approx0.11\)–\(0.12\,\mathrm{fm}\); it is not an ultraviolet cutoff for this theory at all, and if it were, a cutoff gap would be the trivial case since Osterwalder–Seiler, the Millennium clause being precisely the cutoff's removal. A running grain is dead by eighteen orders of magnitude at nucleosynthesis, and transmutation is a running phenomenon. And an emergent-space programme owes exact Poincaré covariance before it may state the problem, since the problem takes it as an axiom. The numerical companion, [[mass-gap-receipt-ledger]], adds that the grain has no rhyme with any QCD scale above the empirically measured chance rate, and that the most seductive coincidence in the neighbourhood is a unit artifact.

**10. What the reclassification predicts, and what it does not.** The three category errors make one falsifiable claim: any argument for the gap that is stated in the pre-quotient register, in the Fock folium, or without a reflection wall will fail structurally rather than by insufficient effort. They make no claim that the theorem is easy. The one avenue in which the programme's machinery is the same species as a known partial result is nuclearity — a capacity of an inclusion with a state, controlled by the gap — and the direction of that implication is presently from gap to structure, not the converse the programme would want. That converse is the correctly typed open problem, and it is recorded as such rather than as a result.
