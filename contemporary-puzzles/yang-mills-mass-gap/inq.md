# Yang–Mills Existence and the Mass Gap

The popular paradox — “the Lagrangian has massless gluons, so what gives them mass?” — contains a real category error: it demands that a gauge-invariant spectral gap appear as a mass term or pole of a gauge-dependent field. The clean finite-regulator operator-theoretic reformulation is now explicit: after Gauss descent and a ground-state transform, the physical gap is exactly the dimensionless Poincare constant of the vacuum-weighted electric-flux form times its kinetic energy coefficient. A deeper causal-frame synthesis lower-bounds the sought gap by a canonically normalized dimensionless completeness constant, a dimensionless energy-solder coefficient, and an independently calibrated energy scale. None of this makes the Yang--Mills existence-and-gap theorem a category error. It leaves the hard task intact: construct the continuum theory and retain the bounds in physical RG units, uniformly in volume and regulator removal.

Claim labels follow [[program-core/axioms-and-principles#Status vocabulary|the programme-wide status vocabulary]] and its composition rule. The puzzle statement in [[puzzle-as-posed]] is sourced to the literature and can be used by a reader who rejects everything below it.

## The argument in order

**1. The problem is an existence theorem, not a puzzle.** The Clay statement asks for a nontrivial quantum Yang--Mills theory on \(\mathbb R^4\), for any compact simple gauge group, satisfying axioms at least as strong as the standard Wightman framework, with a spectral gap \(\Delta>0\) above the vacuum. Numerical evidence is strong: lattice calculations place the pure-\(\mathrm{SU}(3)\) \(0^{++}\) glueball near \(1.65\)–\(1.73\,\mathrm{GeV}\) after a scale convention is chosen, and compute low-lying dimensionless ratios to a few percent. What is missing is a construction and proof. [[puzzle-as-posed]] separates existence, gap, nontriviality, and numerical evidence.

**2. A gap is not a failure of continuum mathematics.** The statement \(E_H((0,\Delta))=0\) is expressible without strain on continuous \(\mathbb R^4\). A free massive field already supplies an elementary continuum example: the vacuum is isolated, a one-particle shell occurs at positive mass, and multiparticle spectrum lies above it. A mass gap therefore requires neither discrete spacetime nor an ultraviolet grain.

[[mass-as-casimir-and-realization]] sharpens what is nevertheless right in the “discrete mass” intuition. Momentum components are translation generators; on an elementary relativistic representation, mass is one Poincare-Casimir label of the orbit rather than another canonical coordinate. Measurement, spontaneous symmetry breaking, and a gap share only a higher realization ledger—alternatives, optional weighting, optional pointing and record, and optional dynamical cost. Only the last slot gives \(H\geq\Delta(1-P_0)\). The same note separates metrological unit lines from conformal scale densities and exhibits the exact scale triangle \(\ell_G=Gm/c^2\), \(\lambda_C=\hbar/(mc)\), and \(\ell_G\lambda_C=\ell_P^2\). A nonzero inverse-Compton representative is dual to length only after comparison by \(c\) and \(\hbar\); it does not automatically choose a Weyl scale. The note also proves that a nonzero positive operator with exact same-carrier unitary dilation covariance has spectrum \([0,\infty)\), so a genuine gap marks the failure of that covariance or a change of reconstructed carrier. The Clay pure-gauge target still contains no \(G\): gravitational reinterpretation cannot replace a \(G\)-independent Yang--Mills coercivity and scale theorem.

[[horizon-saturation-and-entropic-distinction]] adds the entropy lane. For fixed areal radius, Schwarzschild compactness saturates at \(M_R=c^2R/(2G)\), while the area law gives \(S_{\mathrm{BH}}=k_BA/(4\ell_P^2)\); neither is an absolute maximum without the boundary scale. The note treats the classical interior as one representational continuation rather than mandatory primitive ontology and states a no-interior reconstruction target in which a black hole is an exterior boundary sector. Relative entropy then measures unresolved distinctions, temperature supplies energy per nat, and a Yang--Mills gap still requires a uniform entropy-Hessian lower bound plus a same-carrier energy comparison. Exactly, \(H\geq\Delta_E(1-P_0)\) implies a Euclidean distinction-decay length \(\xi_{\mathrm{gap}}=\hbar c/\Delta_E\); that is a correlation or persistence scale, not a spatial pixel.

**3. A gauge-dependent massless pole is not the physical gap target.** **[STANDARD]** The connection \(A\) and a gauge-fixed gluon propagator are not gauge-invariant observables. The physical target belongs to the vacuum representation of the gauge-invariant observable algebra, or to an equivalent constrained or BRST construction, and is not determined by the absence of an \(A^2\) term or by a gauge-dependent propagator pole. This removes the demand that a physical gap must look like a generated gluon mass. It does *not* make gauge reduction a gap mechanism: free Maxwell theory has gauge-equivalence classes and gauge-invariant field strengths while remaining massless. [[program-core/physical-quotient|The physical quotient]] types the reduction; the observable state and Hamiltonian must still supply the dynamics. **[CONJECTURE -- external scenario]** Gribov--Zwanziger restricts a gauge-fixed configuration space and modifies the gauge-dependent gluon propagator, typically with reflection-positivity violation. This may diagnose the absence of an asymptotic one-gluon state; it is neither a gauge-invariant spectral-gap theorem nor evidence that its mass parameter equals the physical gap.

**4. The perturbative Fock representation does not determine the interacting vacuum sector.** **[CONDITIONAL THEOREM]** If a positive-metric Wightman Yang--Mills theory exists, Haag's theorem obstructs the ordinary interaction-picture identification of its sharp-time fields with free fields in one Fock representation. The application is conditional on the very theory being constructed, while gauge-fixed perturbation theory uses an indefinite-metric auxiliary space outside Haag's hypotheses. The safe conclusion is narrower: perturbative poles and the free Fock vacuum do not by themselves determine the spectrum of the interacting observable GNS representation. This is compatible with the programme's register warning, but is not a theorem about its proposed pre-observable ground.

**5. The OS quotient constructs a carrier; transfer contraction measures the gap.** **[STANDARD]** In the Euclidean route, reflection positivity and the remaining Osterwalder--Schrader hypotheses construct the physical Hilbert space and positive-energy evolution. The OS map from positive-time functionals to the completed Hilbert space is a quotient and can be noninjective. Taking \(a\) here to be a Euclidean length step, the descended transfer operator \(T_a=e^{-aH/(\hbar c)}\) is injective; its inverse is generally unbounded. After normalizing the vacuum eigenvalue and writing \(P_0\) for the vacuum projection, define

$$
\widetilde T_a:=\lambda_0(T_a)^{-1}T_a,
\qquad
r_a:=\left\|\widetilde T_a(1-P_0)\right\|,
\qquad
\boxed{\Delta_E=-\frac{\hbar c}{a}\ln r_a.}
$$

At finite volume, when the next transfer eigenvalue exists, \(a\Delta_E/(\hbar c)=-\ln(\lambda_1/\lambda_0)\). [[lorentzian-spectral-envelope/inq|The Lorentzian spectral envelope]] owns the dictionary and the invertible Ising debugging model. A conditional expectation, the OS quotient, and the transfer semigroup are three different maps. Calling them all “descent” is a proposed analogy until an explicit intertwiner is built. The unresolved estimate is a positive \(\Delta_{a,L}\) uniform through infinite volume and the tuned continuum limit. For a fixed physical gap, \(1-r_a\sim a\Delta_E/(\hbar c)\to0\); the raw per-slice deficit does not stay bounded away from zero.

[[vacuum-boundary-gluing-and-wall-response]] supplies the missing time-zero sewing theorem. At finite regulator, integrating a Euclidean half-space over histories with fixed boundary data gives a boundary amplitude; normalized transfer through infinite depth projects it onto \(\psi_0\); and gluing two reflected halves converges in total variation to \(\psi_0^2\mu_{\mathrm{Haar}}\). The exact Gaussian member identifies the linearized boundary response as a Dirichlet-to-Neumann square root: its boundary lower edge, logarithmic-density Hessian, and Hamiltonian gap are related by explicit unit-bearing factors but are not the same concept. This is a concrete wall-response construction, not wave-function collapse. It also exposes a regulator firewall: a Wilson transfer matrix determines \(H_T=-(\hbar/a_\tau)\log T\); equality with the canonical Kogut--Susskind Hamiltonian requires either defining \(T\) from that semigroup or taking a controlled temporal-continuum limit.

[[causal-patch-boundary-and-two-times]] sharpens the locality reversal. A bounded causal patch is not a UV cutoff or a finite-dimensional system; locality may be demoted as primitive ontology only if microcausality, regional independence, finite propagation, and local covariance are recovered as observable theorems on an atlas of patches. The note separates factive record order, one-sided CP relaxation, Euclidean preparation, Lorentzian clock evolution, and modular flow. It proves a range--kernel no-go: the defect of the map that forms an observable carrier vanishes on that carrier and therefore cannot itself be its mass-gap form. The viable sequence is carrier formation, tangential boundary response, then clock Hamiltonian. Finally, a cross-carrier comparison theorem says that if an independently constructed dimensionless response generator has gap \(\kappa_C\), the comparison map has lower coverage \(b_J\), and the physical energy form satisfies the noncircular solder \(h\geq\eta_{\mathrm{sol}}E_*\mathfrak d_C\), then \(\Delta_E\geq\eta_{\mathrm{sol}}\kappa_Cb_JE_*\). This is a theorem schema, not a Yang--Mills realization.

**6. The regulated gap is weighted flux coercivity.** **[EXACT -- FINITE REGULATOR]** On a finite spatial cell complex, the Kogut--Susskind electric term is a sum of compact-group link Laplacians and the magnetic term is a plaquette potential. Its unique positive vacuum \(\psi_0\) defines the gauge-invariant probability measure \(\mathrm d\nu=\psi_0^2\mathrm d\mu_{\mathrm{Haar}}\). Conjugation by \(\psi_0\) turns \(H-E_0\) into a weighted Witten/Bakry--Emery Laplacian, and the physical gap is its dimensionless optimal Poincare constant multiplied by the kinetic energy coefficient. On the unweighted pure-gauge Haar carrier of a connected graph containing a cycle there is now a sharp theorem: the optimal invariant electric-flux constant is \(g(\Gamma)\lambda_G\), graph girth times the smallest nonzero Casimir allowed by the global form of the gauge group. It does not deteriorate with volume and is constant on fixed-girth families such as ordinary square-plaquette lattices; it expresses closed spin-network support, not three-dimensional knotting. A global transfer to the interacting vacuum carries the generally useless factor \(e^{-\operatorname{osc}(-2\log\psi_0)}\). A local conditional Poincare bound plus a Dobrushin influence radius \(q<1\) would give the volume-uniform constant \(\lambda_{\mathrm{loc}}(1-q)\). The boundary analysis exposes a second route: a Euclidean bulk Poincare or logarithmic-Sobolev inequality for a product carré du champ passes to a time-zero coordinate marginal equipped with the inherited coordinate form, without requiring a local boundary potential; it does not pass to an arbitrary marginal carrying an unrelated form. The remaining energy-form comparison and survival along the asymptotically free continuum trajectory are indispensable. For real multiplication excitations, the denominator is also the classical Fisher norm of a mean-zero score under the Hellinger embedding; this does not represent phase directions or the full quantum BKM tangent space. [[gauge-descent-flux-fisher-coercivity]] proves the regulated identities and finite-graph theorem and states the uniform continuum target. [[carrier-first-reversal]] gives the reversal tactic: construct the carrier and closed flux form first, recover the generator by the representation theorem, and derive intrinsic configuration geometry from its carré du champ afterward. [[physical-distinction-coercivity]] isolates the exact common grammar with measurement: projections ask exclusive questions, instruments govern conditioned state change, while the mass gap is coercivity on the centered physical GNS carrier. [[causal-frame-coercivity]] now gives the operator signature that a pre-QFT construction would have to realize: a canonically normalized causal family of dimensionless distinction maps has a lower frame bound \(\kappa_{\mathrm{fr}}\), and the physical form obeys \(h\geq\eta_{\mathrm{sol}}E_*\mathfrak d\), yielding \(H\geq\kappa_{\mathrm{fr}}\eta_{\mathrm{sol}}E_*(1-P_\Omega)\) in quadratic-form sense. Its factivity bridge separates the type change of choosing an outcome, the dimensionless defect of forgetting distinctions, and the energetic spectral gap; the same wall architecture may relate them, but none is identified with another. It also proves that ordinary nested AQFT region algebras cannot furnish a proper vacuum-preserving expectation tower under Reeh--Schlieder hypotheses; regulator, RG, or different-carrier shells are required. Orbit-space curvature is one sufficient route to the energy inequality, not the definition of the gap and not a necessary condition.

[[knotting-as-dimensional-presentation/inq|Knotting as three-dimensional presentation]] supplies a separate topological lane. Its conditional selection of three spatial dimensions requires a primitive ordinary $S^1$ carrier, faithful ambient embedding, nontrivial ambient isotopy, and a minimal-ambient-dimension rule; higher-dimensional codimension-two knots remain possible. Faddeev--Skyrme theory separately proves that topology plus a scale-balancing form can make nontrivial sectors energetically costly. Neither result is a Yang--Mills gap: knot sectors need not exhaust the physical carrier, and positive soliton energy can coexist with massless fluctuations in the topologically trivial sector.

**7. One particular unbroken dilation-covariant generator cannot be gapped.** **[EXACT -- SCOPED]** [[the-grain-of-causal-scale/causal-spectrum|The HSMI no-gap theorem]] states that the positive translation generator \(P\) of a dilation-covariant half-sided modular inclusion has \(P=0\) or \(\sigma(P)=[0,\infty)\). A nonzero gap in that same generator therefore requires the covariance hypothesis to fail or passage to a different carrier and generator. The theorem does not say that every “pre-wall” object is gapless or that a wall supplies a gap. **[STANDARD]** Classical four-dimensional Yang--Mills is scale covariant, while the quantum scale enters through the trace anomaly, \(T^\mu{}_{\mu}\propto\beta(g)F^2\). A scale is still not a gap: in the expected chirally broken phase of massless-quark QCD, dimensional transmutation coexists with Goldstone modes.

**8. A lower-dimensional calculation supplies a structural analogue, not the theorem.** **[STANDARD -- non-rigorous calculation, lattice-corroborated]** In \(2+1\) dimensions Karabali and Nair pass to gauge-invariant variables whose Jacobian contains \(e^{2c_A S_{\mathrm{WZW}}}\); their approximation produces \(m=e^2c_A/2\pi\), and Karabali--Kim--Nair obtain string tensions close to lattice values. This resembles the programme's grammar: a change of variables leaves a nonzero measure factor, with a Casimir multiplying an available yardstick. It is a realized heuristic analogue, not a rigorous gap construction and not an execution of the four-dimensional wall conjecture.

**9. Why it does not transfer, in three parts.** **[EXACT -- dimensional, for the first]** In \(3+1\) dimensions \(e^2\) is dimensionless, so the Casimir no longer comes with a mass yardstick; the scale arises from the renormalization trajectory, schematically \(\Lambda=\mu\exp[-1/(2\beta_0g^2(\mu))]\). [[deriving-value-of-g/obstructions-to-an-unconditional-proof|The dimensional obstruction]] correctly says that dimensionless algebra alone cannot output a mass. The other differences are structural rather than impossibility theorems: the Karabali--Nair parametrization exploits the complex structure of two spatial dimensions, with no comparably controlling three-dimensional parametrization supplied here, and \(2+1\)-dimensional Yang--Mills is super-renormalizable whereas the four-dimensional ultraviolet construction is the problem itself. An algebraic contribution can legitimately target dimensionless ratios such as \(m_{0^{++}}/\sqrt\sigma\), but a prediction in MeV needs an independent scale-setting convention. [[mass-gap-no-gos]] states these obligations.

**10. The programme's present grain does not solve this problem.** **[NO-GO -- three ways]** The diagnostic \(\lambda_*\approx4.18\)–\(4.26\,\mathrm{fm}\) is thirty-five to thirty-seven times coarser than \(\hbar c/m_{0^{++}}\approx0.11\)–\(0.12\,\mathrm{fm}\), so it is not a plausible ultraviolet regulator for the displayed glueball dynamics. More fundamentally, a fixed physical grain does not furnish a theory on continuum \(\mathbb R^4\); a regulator proposal must prove a gap, volume uniformity, and persistence on the tuned removal trajectory. Reflection positivity at finite spacing does not supply those estimates. The programme's tested live closure law $G\propto H/m_*^3$ is excluded by its nucleosynthesis check; this does not exclude every conceivable running-grain construction. Dimensional transmutation is independently a running-coupling phenomenon. Finally, an emergent-spacetime programme owes the exact observable Poincare covariance assumed by the problem. [[mass-gap-receipt-ledger]] records the numerical mismatch and chance-rate census without treating either as a proof about the grain. The mismatch excludes the direct identification and cutoff readings; it does not exclude the separately typed, still exploratory factorization “frozen anchor times cross-sector matching times dimensionless coercivity” in [[the-grain-of-causal-scale/inbox/causal-grain-and-the-yang-mills-gap/entry|the causal-grain gap packet]].

**11. What the reclassification predicts, and what it does not.** An argument fails structurally if it identifies a gauge-dependent gluon pole with the gauge-invariant Hamiltonian gap, treats the free Fock vacuum as the already-constructed interacting sector, or infers a Lorentzian spectrum from Euclidean data without OS reconstruction or another bridge. Absence of a reflection wall is not itself fatal to a direct Hamiltonian construction. Nuclearity is a separate high-energy phase-space condition: it can imply the split property under suitable hypotheses, but neither a gap nor nuclearity implies the other. A stronger wall-capacity condition that forces an infrared gap would be new mathematics and is correctly recorded as open.
