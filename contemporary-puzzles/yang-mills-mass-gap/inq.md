# Yang–Mills Existence and the Mass Gap

The Yang--Mills mass-gap problem asks for a nontrivial continuum quantum theory whose complete physical vacuum complement begins at positive invariant mass. The popular question “how do massless gluons acquire mass?” contains a category error because it demands that a gauge-invariant spectral property of the reconstructed observable carrier appear as a mass term or pole of a gauge-dependent field. The carrier-first reversal is now mathematically sharp at finite regulator, but the existence, regulator-uniform coercivity, continuum reconstruction, and Poincare realization required by the Clay problem remain open.

The most concrete geometric bridge is the stationary past--future construction: the Friedrichs cosine of disjoint history cuts is the transfer contraction across their slab, its supported positive return recovers the transfer Hamiltonian by a logarithm per calibrated thickness, and a gap is equivalent to a finite uniform Euclidean-dwell ceiling. If \(R(\ell)=-\log\|e^{-\ell H/(\hbar c)}(1-P_0)\|\), then \(m_{\mathrm{gap}}=(\hbar/c)\,\mathrm dR/\mathrm d\ell=(\hbar/c^2)(\mathrm d/\mathrm d\tau)R(c\tau)\) only after Poincare reconstruction. Here \(R\) is dimensionless attenuation depth, not yet a number of facts; a factification reading additionally needs a record-producing process and a noncircular same-carrier solder.

Claim labels follow [[program-core/axioms-and-principles#Status vocabulary|the programme-wide status vocabulary]] and its composition rule. The puzzle statement in [[puzzle-as-posed]] is sourced to the literature and can be used by a reader who rejects everything below it.

## The argument in order

**1. The problem is an existence theorem, not a puzzle.** The Clay statement asks for a nontrivial quantum Yang--Mills theory on \(\mathbb R^4\), for any compact simple gauge group, satisfying axioms at least as strong as the standard Wightman framework, with a spectral gap \(\Delta>0\) above the vacuum. Numerical evidence is strong: lattice calculations place the pure-\(\mathrm{SU}(3)\) \(0^{++}\) glueball near \(1.65\)–\(1.73\,\mathrm{GeV}\) after a scale convention is chosen, and compute low-lying dimensionless ratios to a few percent. What is missing is a construction and proof. [[puzzle-as-posed]] separates existence, gap, nontriviality, and numerical evidence.

As of September 2026, Clay still lists the problem as unsolved. [[library/reflection-positive-construction-of-four-dimensional-su-n-yang-mills-theory-with-mass-gap-and-confinement/inq|Faizal and Shabir (2026)]] present a published claimed reflection-positive \(SU(N)\) construction with a multiscale continuum gap. It is useful because its theorem architecture names the right bridge—fixed-scale clustering, summably controlled interlacing, OS reconstruction, and ultraviolet identification—but it is not treated here as an accepted solution. A primary-source audit finds that its gap transport needs total error smaller than the starting gap rather than mere summability, its final proof uses the opposite operator inequality from the one stated, and its strong/weak trajectory comparison invokes an \(O(g_k^2)\sim O(1/k)\) bound as summable while presuming entry into a shared controlled domain.

**2. A gap is not a failure of continuum mathematics.** The statement \(E_H((0,\Delta))=0\) is expressible without strain on continuous \(\mathbb R^4\). A free massive field already supplies an elementary continuum example: the vacuum is isolated, a one-particle shell occurs at positive mass, and multiparticle spectrum lies above it. A mass gap therefore requires neither discrete spacetime nor an ultraviolet grain.

[[mass-as-casimir-and-realization]] sharpens what is nevertheless right in the “discrete mass” intuition. Momentum components are translation generators; on an elementary relativistic representation, mass is one Poincare-Casimir label of the orbit rather than another canonical coordinate. Measurement, spontaneous symmetry breaking, and a gap share only a higher realization ledger—alternatives, optional weighting, optional pointing and record, and optional dynamical cost. Only the last slot gives \(H\geq\Delta(1-P_0)\). The same note separates metrological unit lines from conformal scale densities and exhibits the exact scale triangle \(\ell_G=Gm/c^2\), \(\lambda_C=\hbar/(mc)\), and \(\ell_G\lambda_C=\ell_P^2\). A nonzero inverse-Compton representative is dual to length only after comparison by \(c\) and \(\hbar\); it does not automatically choose a Weyl scale. The note also proves that a nonzero positive operator with exact same-carrier unitary dilation covariance has spectrum \([0,\infty)\), so a genuine gap marks the failure of that covariance or a change of reconstructed carrier. The Clay pure-gauge target still contains no \(G\): gravitational reinterpretation cannot replace a \(G\)-independent Yang--Mills coercivity and scale theorem.

[[horizon-saturation-and-entropic-distinction]] adds the entropy lane. The note treats the classical interior as one representational continuation rather than mandatory primitive ontology and states a no-interior reconstruction target in which a black hole begins as an exterior equivalence class; sector status requires an additional GNS criterion. Relative entropy defines a dimensionless distinguishability functional between declared states on the exterior algebra, while a faithful Gibbs reference converts it into a free-energy difference. Fiber multiplicity, conditional entropy, and relative entropy remain different constructions. A Yang--Mills gap still requires a uniform entropy-Hessian lower bound plus a same-carrier energy comparison. Exactly, \(H\geq\Delta_E(1-P_0)\) implies the universal norm-decay length \(\xi_{\mathrm{gap}}=\hbar c/\Delta_E\) of the vacuum-complement Euclidean semigroup when \(\Delta_E\) is the optimal threshold; only additional locality, spectral, and coupling hypotheses relate it to the correlation scale of a particular observable. It is not a spatial pixel or entropy-production rate.

[[mass-as-a-calibrated-distinction-rate]] isolates what is exact in “mass as a rate.” The numerator is logarithmic transfer attenuation; division by Euclidean length or duration gives an inverse-length or inverse-time rate, and \(\hbar c\) or \(\hbar\) returns clock energy before the Casimir interpretation returns mass. [[hbar-clock-and-the-calibration-firewall]] makes the dependency explicit: this is a downstream retyping, because a spectrum becomes a frequency only after a normalized clock parameter exists, and \(\hbar\) converts action to phase rather than creating time. Entropy may there be identified as a capacity proportional to a rate ratio, not an event-production rate. A literal factification rate still requires an instrument, obtained value, persistent record, a dimensionless settling exponent, independent clock and action solders, and a physical-carrier comparison.

[[cosmic-geon-hypothesis-and-horizon-rate-ledger]] tests the whole-cosmos version of the reversal. Radiation in a fixed comoving cell obeys \(-\mathrm d\log E_\gamma/\mathrm dt=H\), while a flat Einstein--FLRW apparent-horizon sphere obeys \(2GM_A/(R_Ac^2)=1\) and an exact conditional rate ledger among its Misner--Sharp energy, canonical horizon temperature, area entropy, and signed energy supply. Type-III structure permits trace-free modular comparison but supplies neither a fact nor a gap. The raw forgetting operator vanishes on the carrier it forms, so a distinct tangential response operator must be uniformly coercive before clock, action, and Casimir solders can retype its rate as mass.

[[pointed-facts-and-the-shorted-response]] separates the two contrasts around a local fact. Relative-entropy Fisher information equals gravitational canonical energy in one controlled holographic regime, proving that an information-to-energy solder can be a real same-tangent theorem, but neither that precedent nor Unruh normalization supplies a Yang--Mills scale. Verlinde's screen reversal is suggestive but underdetermined, and stochastic gravity supplies covariance and retarded response rather than an actuality selector or gap generator.

**3. A gauge-dependent massless pole is not the physical gap target.** **[STANDARD]** The connection \(A\) and a gauge-fixed gluon propagator are not gauge-invariant observables. The physical target belongs to the vacuum representation of the gauge-invariant observable algebra, or to an equivalent constrained or BRST construction, and is not determined by the absence of an \(A^2\) term or by a gauge-dependent propagator pole. This removes the demand that a physical gap must look like a generated gluon mass. It does *not* make gauge reduction a gap mechanism: free Maxwell theory has gauge-equivalence classes and gauge-invariant field strengths while remaining massless. [[program-core/physical-quotient|The physical quotient]] types the reduction; the observable state and Hamiltonian must still supply the dynamics. [[register-audit]] expands the correction into a full register stack: presentation versus object, formal series versus nonperturbative function, ultraviolet limit versus fixed theory member, coefficient versus spectrum, and finite cutoff versus continuum trajectory. **[CONJECTURE -- external scenario]** Gribov--Zwanziger restricts a gauge-fixed configuration space and modifies the gauge-dependent gluon propagator, typically with reflection-positivity violation. This may diagnose the absence of an asymptotic one-gluon state; it is neither a gauge-invariant spectral-gap theorem nor evidence that its mass parameter equals the physical gap.

**4. The perturbative Fock representation does not determine the interacting vacuum sector.** **[CONDITIONAL THEOREM]** If a positive-metric Wightman Yang--Mills theory exists, Haag's theorem obstructs the ordinary interaction-picture identification of its sharp-time fields with free fields in one Fock representation. The application is conditional on the very theory being constructed, while gauge-fixed perturbation theory uses an indefinite-metric auxiliary space outside Haag's hypotheses. The safe conclusion is narrower: perturbative poles and the free Fock vacuum do not by themselves determine the spectrum of the interacting observable GNS representation. This is compatible with the programme's register warning, but is not a theorem about its proposed pre-observable ground.

$$

\qquad

For a fixed physical gap, \(1-r_a\sim a\Delta_E/(\hbar c)\to0\); the raw per-slice deficit does not stay bounded away from zero.
