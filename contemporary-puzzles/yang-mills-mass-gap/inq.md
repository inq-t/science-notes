# Yang–Mills Existence and the Mass Gap

The Yang--Mills mass-gap problem asks for a nontrivial continuum quantum theory whose complete physical vacuum complement begins at positive invariant mass. The popular question “how do massless gluons acquire mass?” contains a category error because it demands that a gauge-invariant spectral property of the reconstructed observable carrier appear as a mass term or pole of a gauge-dependent field. The carrier-first reversal is now mathematically sharp at finite regulator, but the existence, regulator-uniform coercivity, continuum reconstruction, and Poincare realization required by the Clay problem remain open.

The most concrete geometric bridge is the stationary past--future construction: the Friedrichs cosine of disjoint history cuts is the transfer contraction across their slab, its supported positive return recovers the transfer Hamiltonian by a logarithm per calibrated thickness, and a gap is equivalent to a finite uniform Euclidean-dwell ceiling. If \(R(\ell)=-\log\|e^{-\ell H/(\hbar c)}(1-P_0)\|\), then \(m_{\mathrm{gap}}=(\hbar/c)\,\mathrm dR/\mathrm d\ell=(\hbar/c^2)(\mathrm d/\mathrm d\tau)R(c\tau)\) only after Poincare reconstruction. Here \(R\) is dimensionless attenuation depth, not yet a number of facts; a factification reading additionally needs a record-producing process and a noncircular same-carrier solder.

The cleanest exact pre-QFT model of the proposed Copernican mechanism is now the triangle-character theorem. On the hyperbolic orbifold of \(\Delta^+(3,4,\infty)\), the local Casimir remains an ordinary smooth differential operator, but discrete global equivariance restricts its domain. The complex-\(S^6\) winding defect has the phase shadow \(\chi(c)=e^{2\pi ip/12}\); every nontrivial such character removes the invariant cusp Fourier channel, so the twisted Laplacian has compact resolvent, no zero mode, and a positive lowest eigenvalue. This proves that a globally discrete gluing law can open a coercive sector without discretizing the local continuum. It also proves a useful direct-lift obstruction: every $SU(3)$ cusp holonomy fixes at least a rank-two Cartan subspace in the adjoint representation, so ordinary color holonomy alone cannot remove all gluonic zero channels by this mechanism. The automorphic carrier is still two-dimensional, the neutral sector and continuum limit remain untreated, and no physical scale or same-carrier comparison has been derived.

[[augmentation-descent-and-the-neutral-sector-firewall]] removes the arbitrary choice of one phase by taking the canonical augmentation representation \(I_{C_{12}}=\mathbf1^\perp\subset\ell^2(C_{12})\). It contains all eleven relative sheet modes and no cusp-invariant vector, so its twisted Laplacian has one positive floor. But this stronger packaging yields an exact neutral-sector no-go: every deck-equivariant operator splits into invariant and augmentation blocks, and fiber averaging kills the augmentation carrier, so its coercivity imposes no bound on invariant observables. If the nontrivial characters label separate sectors, their positive bottoms do not cover neutral glueballs; if one twisted sector is the whole carrier, its lowest eigenvalue is vacuum energy and the mass gap is the next spacing. The missing cocycle must therefore be a spectrum-independent analysis map from every physical vacuum-complement direction into the constrained descent carrier, with a uniform energy-form comparison—not a raw quotient, global charge, or modular Hamiltonian.

[[global-discreteness-kazhdan-rigidity-and-the-gap]] sharpens what the local-continuous/global-discrete rhyme can actually mean. For a flat-bundle Laplacian, topology identifies the parallel kernel, whereas a gap is the quantitative closed-range condition for the covariant derivative. Kazhdan property \((T)\) is exactly the representation-uniform version for a discrete group comparison Laplacian. But \(\Delta^+(3,4,\infty)\cong C_3*C_4\) has a finite-index free subgroup \(F_6\) and therefore lacks property \((T)\); an explicit irreducible two-dimensional family has cusp eigenvalues approaching one. The eleven \(C_{12}\) characters remain an exact finite coercive family, but their separation does not extend to all descent representations. Thus discreteness is the grammar, while quantitative rigidity on the actual carrier is the gap-bearing predicate.

[[triangle-presentation-descent-on-the-neutral-adjoint-carrier]] supplies the first finite exact way around the direct charge mismatch. For the irreducible two-dimensional triangle presentation, pass from \(V\) to the neutralized operator carrier \(V\otimes\bar V\simeq\operatorname{End}(V)\), then average conjugation over the \(C_3\) and \(C_4\) generators. The two trace-preserving expectations have common range \(\mathbb CI\), and their summed restriction-loss Hessian has sharp relative floor \(1-|\cos2\theta|\) on every traceless finite-algebra density tangent. The same ordered pair has the reversal-odd commutator spectrum \(\{0,\pm\sin4\theta/4\}\), so handedness and stiffness arise from one relative presentation without being identical. This is a finite algebraic prototype of the global-presentation-to-local-entropy arrow, not its physical completion: its affine mixed-state paths are not localized inner-unitary QFT paths. One three-dimensional matrix frame also cannot cover an infinite-dimensional Yang--Mills form core; the full conditional target therefore requires an \(\ell^2\)-family with uniform floor \(\kappa_{\mathrm{pres}}>0\), a natural complex-linear analysis map, and one genuine localized restriction loss whose positive Hermitian extension dominates the entire summed frame on a complex energy-form core. With those hypotheses and the isotropic whole-to-part yardstick, Longo's comparison would give \(\Delta_E\geq b_J\kappa_{\mathrm{pres}}(\gamma s_*)^{-1/3}E_{A,c}\iota_c^{-2/3}\). The discrete relations alone are insufficient because the one-fibre floor tends to zero as the presentations align.

[[twisted-holonomy-and-the-neutral-hessian]] tests the same idea inside gauge theory. A center twist charges lines rather than point-local glueball operators; on a twisted finite torus it gives a neutral opposite-flux threshold \(4\pi/(NL)\), which vanishes as \(L\to\infty\). The stronger positive precedent is center-stabilized Yang--Mills on \(\mathbb R^3\times S^1\): holonomy gaps charged \(W\)-modes but leaves Cartan photons perturbatively massless, while monopole-instantons generate a cosine potential whose Hessian is positive on the physical neutral modes after the diagonal \(U(1)\) direction is removed. [[localized-relative-entropy-and-the-energy-solder]] supplies a newly located exact bridge. Differentiating a localized local-unitary path under an explicit energy-form regularity condition gives the real inequality \(q_B\leq(2\pi R/\hbar c)\mathfrak h\); a restriction-loss Hessian lies below \(q_B\). ## The argument in order

**1. The problem is an existence theorem, not a puzzle.** The Clay statement asks for a nontrivial quantum Yang--Mills theory on \(\mathbb R^4\), for any compact simple gauge group, satisfying axioms at least as strong as the standard Wightman framework, with a spectral gap \(\Delta>0\) above the vacuum. Numerical evidence is strong: lattice calculations place the pure-\(\mathrm{SU}(3)\) \(0^{++}\) glueball near \(1.65\)–\(1.73\,\mathrm{GeV}\) after a scale convention is chosen, and compute low-lying dimensionless ratios to a few percent. What is missing is a construction and proof. [[puzzle-as-posed]] separates existence, gap, nontriviality, and numerical evidence.

As of September 2026, Clay still lists the problem as unsolved. [[library/reflection-positive-construction-of-four-dimensional-su-n-yang-mills-theory-with-mass-gap-and-confinement/inq|Faizal and Shabir (2026)]] present a published claimed reflection-positive \(SU(N)\) construction with a multiscale continuum gap. It is useful because its theorem architecture names the right bridge—fixed-scale clustering, summably controlled interlacing, OS reconstruction, and ultraviolet identification—but it is not treated here as an accepted solution. A primary-source audit finds that its gap transport needs total error smaller than the starting gap rather than mere summability, its final proof uses the opposite operator inequality from the one stated, and its strong/weak trajectory comparison invokes an \(O(g_k^2)\sim O(1/k)\) bound as summable while presuming entry into a shared controlled domain.

**2. A gap is not a failure of continuum mathematics.** The statement \(E_H((0,\Delta))=0\) is expressible without strain on continuous \(\mathbb R^4\). A free massive field already supplies an elementary continuum example: the vacuum is isolated, a one-particle shell occurs at positive mass, and multiparticle spectrum lies above it. A mass gap therefore requires neither discrete spacetime nor an ultraviolet grain.
