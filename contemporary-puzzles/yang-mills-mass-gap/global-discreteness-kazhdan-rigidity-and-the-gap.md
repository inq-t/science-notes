# Global Discreteness, Kazhdan Rigidity, and the Gap

A discrete global gluing law can alter the domain of a smooth local differential operator without discretizing the local geometry, but discreteness is only a qualitative restriction. The analytic content of a gap is quantitative closed range: every nonconstant direction must pay a uniformly positive comparison cost. Kazhdan property \((T)\) is the exact group-level version of that demand across all unitary carriers. The triangle group \(\Delta^+(3,4,\infty)\cong C_3*C_4\) does not have property \((T)\), so its finite \(C_{12}\) character gap is a genuine model of local-continuous/global-discrete confinement but cannot, by group discreteness alone, become a representation-uniform physical mass-gap theorem.

## What a geometric gap actually says

Let \(X\) be a connected complete finite-volume Riemannian orbifold, let \(E_\rho\to X\) be the flat Hermitian bundle of a finite-dimensional unitary representation \(\rho\), and let

\[
q_\rho(s)=\|\nabla_\rho s\|_2^2,
\qquad
\Delta_\rho=\nabla_\rho^*\nabla_\rho
\]

denote the Friedrichs form and operator. If \(K_\rho=\ker\Delta_\rho\), then the following are equivalent:

\[
q_\rho(s)\geq\delta\|s\|_2^2
\quad(s\perp K_\rho),
\]

\[
\operatorname{Ran}\nabla_\rho\text{ is closed},
\]

\[
0\text{ is isolated in }\sigma(\Delta_\rho).
\]

The optimal constant is the square of the reduced minimum modulus

\[
\gamma(\nabla_\rho)
:=
\inf_{s\in\operatorname{Dom}\nabla_\rho\cap K_\rho^\perp}
\frac{\|\nabla_\rho s\|_2}{\|s\|_2}.
\]

Finite volume identifies the kernel with the parallel sections,

\[
K_\rho\cong V^{\rho(\pi_1^{\mathrm{orb}}X)}.
\]

Topology determines which directions cost exactly zero. A gap is the stronger assertion that nonzero-cost directions cannot approach zero cost. For a family of geometries or regulators, the real stopping condition is

\[
\inf_r\gamma(\nabla_{\rho_r})>0,
\]

not merely the nontriviality of every \(\rho_r\).

## The cusp theorem is confinement, not discreteness alone

On a two-dimensional exact hyperbolic cusp, a cusp eigenvector with monodromy \(e^{2\pi i\theta}\) has separated radial channels of the form

\[
-\partial_r^2+\frac14+e^{2r}(n+\theta)^2.
\]

If no cusp monodromy has eigenvalue one, every channel rises at the end and the twisted Laplacian has compact resolvent. If a fixed vector survives, its \(n=0\) channel restores essential spectrum starting at \(1/4\). This sharpens [[triangle-character-cusp-coercivity]]: the nontrivial character removes escape to infinity and makes the spectrum discrete. It is stronger than what is required merely to isolate zero on one fixed finite-area surface, because the surviving scalar cusp continuum still begins at positive \(1/4\).

The same holonomy defect behaves differently on different ends. For

\[
g=\mathrm dr^2+a(r)^2h_N,
\]

a positive vertical eigenvalue \(\mu\) contributes \(a(r)^{-2}\mu\). It diverges on a shrinking cusp, stays a Kaluza--Klein threshold on a cylinder, and falls to zero on an expanding or conic end. The discrete label is therefore not the gap. The gap belongs to the label together with the metric incidence by which it enters the operator.

## Property (T) is the representation-uniform form

Let \(\Gamma\) be generated by a finite symmetric set \(S\), and let \(\pi\) be any unitary representation. Define

\[
\Delta_{S,\pi}
:=
|S|I-\sum_{s\in S}\pi(s).
\]

Then

\[
2\langle\xi,\Delta_{S,\pi}\xi\rangle
=
\sum_{s\in S}\|\pi(s)\xi-\xi\|^2,
\]

and \(\ker\Delta_{S,\pi}=\mathcal H_\pi^\Gamma\). The group has Kazhdan property \((T)\) exactly when one constant \(\varepsilon_S>0\) works in every unitary representation:

\[
\Delta_{S,\pi}
\geq
\varepsilon_S(1-P_\pi^\Gamma).
\]

This is the algebraic meaning of **no almost-undifferentiated nontrivial direction**. It is a uniform Poincare inequality for global comparison, not a claim that the base space has pixels. [[library/kazhdans-property-t-via-semidefinite-optimization/inq]] records the standard spectral characterization and an exact group-ring certificate in a group that does possess this rigidity.

The correct exceptional upgrade is categorical rather than a search for a better discrete group. For a rigid \(C^*\)-tensor category, admissible fusion-algebra representations retain annular or tube-algebra positivity that the bare fusion rules forget. [[quantum-g2-categorical-rigidity-and-the-carrier-firewall]] records the corresponding universal averaging operator and the theorem that the quantum-\(G_2\) categories have property \((T)\) for \(q>0\), \(q\neq1\). This produces a genuine dimensionless minimum distinction cost, while the classical \(q=1\) member retains positive statistical dimension but loses the edge. The result strengthens the mathematical operator without solving the physical-carrier problem: the categorical invariant subspace must still pull back to the Yang--Mills vacuum rather than to all neutral states.

## The \((3,4,\infty)\) triangle group is not Kazhdan

For

\[
\Gamma=C_3*C_4,
\]

the commutator subgroup \(K=\ker(\Gamma\to C_{12})\) has index twelve and is torsion-free. Its Euler characteristic is

\[
\chi(K)
=12\left(\frac13+\frac14-1\right)
=-5,
\]

so \(K\) is the free group \(F_6\), equivalently the group of the once-punctured genus-three cover already appearing in [[s6-descent-defect-and-the-chirality-firewall]]. If \(\Gamma\) had property \((T)\), its finite-index subgroup \(F_6\) would have it. But \(F_6\) surjects onto \(\mathbb Z\), property \((T)\) passes to quotients, and \(\mathbb Z\) has unitary characters approaching the trivial character without becoming invariant. Hence

\[
\boxed{C_3*C_4\text{ does not have property }(T).}
\]

There is also an explicit two-dimensional warning. Put

\[
\omega=e^{2\pi i/3},
\qquad
A=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&\omega\end{pmatrix},
\]

\[
B_\theta
=R_\theta
\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&i\end{pmatrix}
R_\theta^{-1},
\qquad
0<\theta<\frac\pi2,
\]

with \(R_\theta\) the real rotation matrix. Then \(A^3=B_\theta^4=I\), so these define unitary representations of \(C_3*C_4\). They are irreducible for the displayed range and have no global fixed vector. Yet for the cusp word \(c=(AB_\theta)^{-1}\), one eigenvalue approaches one as \(\theta\to0\). Thus even irreducibility and nontrivial global descent do not provide a uniform cusp displacement. [[receipts/triangle-almost-invariant-receipt.py|The finite receipt]] checks this degeneration directly; its [[receipts/triangle-almost-invariant-receipt-output.txt|stored output]] shows the minimum displacement falling quadratically over the sampled angles.

The eleven nontrivial characters of \(C_{12}\) avoid this problem only because they form a fixed finite family with phases separated by \(1/12\). [[augmentation-descent-and-the-neutral-sector-firewall]] correctly packages that finite family, but it cannot promote its minimum into a bound for every representation of the full triangle group.

## What must act on the Yang--Mills carrier

Property \((T)\) would still not be a mass-gap theorem. Its operator acts on a supplied unitary representation and measures disagreement under group generators. If the group acts as an ordinary gauge or center charge, neutral glueballs lie in its invariant subspace and the comparison form vanishes on the very carrier that must be gapped.

The viable Copernican use is different. Global lifts or presentations must induce comparison maps on **localized physical state tangents**, not superselection charges. If \(J\) transports those tangents into a representation \(\pi\), the required assertion is

\[
\ker J=\mathbb C\Omega,
\qquad
\langle J\Psi,\Delta_{S,\pi}J\Psi\rangle
\geq b\|(1-P_0)\Psi\|^2
\]

with \(b>0\) uniform through the regulator and volume limits. Property \((T)\) can supply the middle spectral constant only after a separate lower frame bound for \(J\) and only if the invariant vectors of \(\pi\) do not contain nonvacuum images.

[[localized-relative-entropy-and-the-energy-solder]] supplies the next exact arrow: a lower bound on the resulting local distinction Hessian can be converted into a Hamiltonian lower bound by locality, positive energy, and finite width. The missing theorem has therefore narrowed to a same-carrier comparison between global presentation disagreement and a regional relative-entropy or descent-loss Hessian.

## Stopping condition

The phrase “globally discrete, locally continuous” becomes physically sufficient only when all four assertions hold:

1. the discrete comparison law is represented on localized gauge-invariant state tangents rather than charged color vectors;
2. its joint invariant subspace on that carrier is exactly the vacuum direction;
3. its comparison derivative has regulator-uniform closed range;
4. its positive form is dominated by a regional distinction Hessian whose localized tangent vectors form an energy-form core.

This is a quantitative global-to-local incidence theorem. Discreteness names the grammar; closed range is the gap-bearing predicate.
