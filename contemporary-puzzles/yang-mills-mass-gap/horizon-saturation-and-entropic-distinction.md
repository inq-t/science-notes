# Horizon Saturation and the Entropic Cost of Distinction

A horizon supplies a precise model of boundary-relative saturation: for fixed areal radius, gravitational compactness reaches unity at a definite mass, the semiclassical entropy scales with boundary area, and an exterior observer loses the ability to distinguish any putative realizations that agree on the exterior algebra. This does not force the ontology of a classical black-hole interior. It suggests a sharper pre-QFT programme in which a horizon is a terminal object for localization, a black hole is an exterior representation sector rather than a hidden material ball, entropy measures unresolved multiplicity within that sector, temperature converts dimensionless entropy into energy, and a mass gap requires the further theorem that every nonvacuum distinction has a uniform positive energetic cost.

## What is actually saturated

Let (R) be areal radius, so a spherical surface has area (A=4\pi R^2), and define the compactness

$$
\chi(M,R)
:=
\frac{2GM}{Rc^2}.
$$

For the Schwarzschild family, the horizon condition is

$$
\boxed{
\chi=1
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
M_R=\frac{c^2R}{2G}.}
\tag{H1}
$$

This is the rigorous kernel of “maximum mass for the space occupied.” Within a declared class of spherical configurations whose boundary of areal radius (R) remains outside a trapped region, (M_R) is the compactness-saturation scale. It is not a universal maximum mass: asymptotically flat black holes can have arbitrarily large (M) by having larger (R), and charge, angular momentum, matter assumptions, and cosmological curvature change the bound.

At the Schwarzschild saturation point, semiclassical black-hole thermodynamics gives

$$
S_{\mathrm{BH}}
=
\frac{k_BA}{4\ell_P^2}
=
\frac{\pi k_BR^2}{\ell_P^2}
=
\frac{4\pi k_BGM^2}{\hbar c},
\qquad
\ell_P^2=\frac{\hbar G}{c^3},
\tag{H2}
$$

and

$$
k_BT_H
=
\frac{\hbar c^3}{8\pi GM}
=
\frac{\hbar c}{4\pi R}.
\tag{H3}
$$

Consequently,

$$
\boxed{T_HS_{\mathrm{BH}}=\frac12Mc^2.}
\tag{H4}
$$

The area law is therefore a boundary-saturation benchmark at fixed (R), not the statement that one universal object has both maximum mass and maximum entropy. A cosmological scale can make the relativity explicit. For positive cosmological constant, the Schwarzschild--de Sitter family has a Nariai endpoint

$$
M_{\mathrm N}
=
\frac{c^2}{3G\sqrt{\Lambda}},
$$

but this is a maximum black-hole parameter only after the external scale \(\Lambda^{-1/2}\) has been supplied. It is not an absolute mass bound, and the total horizon entropy at the Nariai endpoint is smaller than that of empty de Sitter space at the same \(\Lambda\).

## No interior is a coherent reconstruction target

The conservative empirical datum is not a visible interior object. It is a family of exterior responses: asymptotic charges, lensing, orbital dynamics, absorption, scattering, ringdown, merger radiation, and, semiclassically, thermal emission. Classical GR extends the exterior solution through a horizon and assigns geometry to an interior. A deeper theory is not logically required to promote that continuation into primitive ontology if it reproduces the entire exterior observable structure.

Let \(\mathcal X_{\mathrm{pre}}\) be a candidate pre-observable carrier and let

$$
\pi_{\mathrm{ext}}:
\mathcal X_{\mathrm{pre}}
\longrightarrow
\mathcal X_{\mathrm{ext}}
$$

retain exactly the data represented by an exterior observable algebra \(\mathfrak A_{\mathrm{ext}}\). Define

$$
x\sim_{\mathrm{ext}}y
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\pi_{\mathrm{ext}}(x)=\pi_{\mathrm{ext}}(y).
\tag{H5}
$$

The exterior black-hole object can then be the equivalence class or representation sector \([x]_{\mathrm{ext}}\), labelled at minimum by the exterior charges that survive the quotient. No set of localized interior “things” appears in the target category. In this precise sense, “nothing inside” means **no independently individuated interior object in the exterior observable theory**. It does not mean that an empty subset of an already assumed interior manifold has been proved to exist.

This is structurally like the modern meaning of particle. A particle is not fundamentally a tiny visible bead; it is identified through a representation of the observable symmetry and scattering structure. A black hole may analogously be reconstructed as a massive boundary or asymptotic sector whose internal realization is not part of the exterior localization functor.

The hypothesis incurs a strict recovery debt. It must reconstruct all exterior predictions, causal response, conserved charges, horizon thermodynamics, perturbative ringdown, and unitary or otherwise explicitly stated information accounting. Calling the GR singularity a symptom is philosophically admissible; replacing the interior is physics only after those maps and predictions exist.

## Entropy measures unresolved alternatives, not non-being

If a finite fiber of \(\pi_{\mathrm{ext}}\) has \(N\) equiprobable preimages, its dimensionless entropy is \(s=\log N\) nats and its thermodynamic entropy is \(S=k_Bs\). The semiclassical area law suggests the capacity

$$
N_{\mathrm{eff}}
\sim
\exp\!\left(\frac{A}{4\ell_P^2}\right),
\tag{H6}
$$

but no finite microstate set is thereby constructed. In an operator-algebraic theory, relative entropy, subfactor index, modular data, or another declared invariant may replace literal cardinality.

The phrase “nothing in particular” is captured by the fiber before a point is selected: exterior facts determine an equivalence class, not one underlying realization. Entropy quantifies the unresolved alternatives relative to that coarse observable presentation. It is not the quantity of non-being, and it does not itself select which realization obtains.

This also corrects the strongest form of “facts require entropy.” A pure state can have zero von Neumann entropy and still support definite records. What fact formation requires is an algebra of alternatives, a state or weighting when probabilities are used, an obtained point, and a persistent record. Positive entropy enters when distinctions remain unresolved relative to a chosen algebra or are discarded by a noninjective process.

## The exact entropy--energy solder

Entropy and energy are different types. In finite dimensions, let \(\sigma\) be a faithful reference state and define its dimensionless modular Hamiltonian

$$
K_\sigma:=-\log\sigma.
$$

For another state \(\rho\), write \(s(\rho)=-\operatorname{Tr}(\rho\log\rho)\). Then quantum relative entropy satisfies

$$
\boxed{
D(\rho\Vert\sigma)
=
\Delta\langle K_\sigma\rangle
-
\Delta s
\geq0,}
\tag{H7}
$$

where differences are taken from \(\sigma\) to \(\rho\). For a Gibbs reference

$$
\sigma
=
Z^{-1}e^{-H/(k_BT)},
$$

equation (H7) becomes

$$
\boxed{
k_BT\,D(\rho\Vert\sigma)
=
\Delta\langle H\rangle
-
T\Delta S.}
\tag{H8}
$$

This is the clean algebraic role of temperature: \(k_BT\) is an energy per nat that solders a dimensionless information distinction to an energetic free-energy cost. For local type-III QFT algebras there need be no density matrix or finite absolute entropy; Araki relative entropy and the relative modular operator are the correct objects. [[library/relative-entropy-and-the-bekenstein-bound/inq|Casini's formulation of the Bekenstein bound]] uses precisely

$$
\Delta S\leq\Delta\langle K\rangle
$$

for a state and the vacuum restricted to a region. This avoids interpreting separately divergent local entropy and energy as finite quantities.

The operator ledger matters:

| Object | Operates on | Returns |
|---|---|---|
| restriction or channel | global states or observables | exterior/coarse state or algebra |
| entropy | a state relative to an algebra or reference | a dimensionless uncertainty or distinguishability number |
| modular operator | the standard/GNS carrier of an algebra and state | dimensionless modular flow |
| clock Hamiltonian | the physical Hilbert carrier | energy and reversible time translations |
| temperature or another yardstick | entropy units and clock normalization | an energy comparison |

An identification \(K=H/(k_BT)+\text{constant}\) holds for a declared thermal clock, not for every modular Hamiltonian. Near stationary horizons, surface gravity provides the comparison

$$

=
\frac{\hbar\kappa}{2\pi c},
\tag{H9}
$$

with the Unruh formula obtained by replacing \(\kappa\) with proper acceleration. This displays all four types: geometric acceleration, \(c\) as the space--time unit comparison, \(\hbar\) as frequency--energy conversion, and \(k_B\) as nat--temperature conversion. The acceleration or surface-gravity normalization remains an input unless a deeper geometry selects it.

## Irreversible order is not clock time

Unitary Lorentzian evolution preserves the fine-grained entropy of a closed state. The exact irreversible arrow instead appears under restriction or a quantum channel \(\Phi\):

$$
D(\rho\Vert\sigma)
\geq
D(\Phi_*\rho\Vert\Phi_*\sigma).
\tag{H10}
$$

Information distinguishability cannot increase after declared distinctions are forgotten. A nested family of exterior algebras or coarse-graining channels can therefore carry a directed record order even when the microscopic clock dynamics is reversible. A horizon is a natural place for this distinction because exterior accessibility changes what can be compared. But neither a partial trace nor an algebra restriction proves that something ontologically exists on the discarded side. It only defines the equivalence relation seen by the retained algebra.

## The mass gap as a scale of distinguishability decay

Now let \(H\geq0\) be the physical vacuum Hamiltonian with vacuum projection \(P_0\). If

$$
H\geq\Delta_E(1-P_0),
$$

then Euclidean clock evolution satisfies the exact contraction bound

$$
\left\|
e^{-\tau H/\hbar}(1-P_0)
\right\|
\leq
e^{-\tau\Delta_E/\hbar}.
\tag{H11}
$$

Writing \(a=c\tau\) and \(m_{\mathrm{gap}}=\Delta_E/c^2\) gives

$$
\boxed{
\xi_{\mathrm{gap}}
=
\frac{\hbar c}{\Delta_E}
=
\frac{\hbar}{m_{\mathrm{gap}}c},
\qquad
\left\|
e^{-aH/(\hbar c)}(1-P_0)
\right\|
\leq
e^{-a/\xi_{\mathrm{gap}}}.}
\tag{H12}
$$

This is a rigorous sense in which mass supplies scale: the gap is the inverse Euclidean persistence length of every nonvacuum physical direction. Under additional locality and spectral hypotheses it is related to exponential clustering of separated observables. It is not a spatial pixel, a minimum observable distance, or entropy production under real-time unitary evolution.

The entropy route to a Yang--Mills gap would therefore need a lower bound, not merely an entropy ceiling. A candidate construction must produce a state-perturbation map and a dimensionless relative-entropy Hessian \(\mathfrak d_{\mathrm{ent}}\) on the entire vacuum complement such that

Then

$$

The possible role of temperature is now exact: \(E_*\) could be a canonically selected \(k_BT_*\). An Unruh or Hawking temperature does not suffice unless the programme independently derives its acceleration or surface gravity, maps its modular carrier to the Yang--Mills vacuum carrier, and proves the two inequalities in (H13). Positivity of relative entropy gives an upper entropy bound; it does not by itself give \(\kappa_{\mathrm{ent}}>0\).
