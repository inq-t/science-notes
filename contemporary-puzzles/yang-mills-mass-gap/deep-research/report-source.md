# Deep Research Source: Causal-Frame Coercivity and Yang--Mills

This source record owns the evidence, claim boundaries, and unresolved-obligation matrix behind [[contemporary-puzzles/yang-mills-mass-gap/causal-frame-coercivity|causal-frame coercivity]]. The research question is whether a pre-QFT algebraic or geometric construction can explain a positive Yang--Mills gap by independently supplying canonically normalized dimensionless distinction, a dimensionless energy comparison, a dimensional yardstick, and uniform coercivity while recovering ordinary local QFT below a declared UV threshold. A secondary question is whether the same wall process can underlie factive measurement without identifying outcome selection, information loss, and spectral energy.

## Research scope and success condition

The target is not a numerical coincidence or a reinterpretation of a known Hamiltonian inequality. Success requires:

- a carrier and vacuum constructed without using the desired low spectrum;
- physical distinction maps fixed by causal, gauge, topological, or descent data;
- a uniform lower-frame or Poincare estimate on the full vacuum complement;
- an independently calibrated energy, inverse-length, or clock-rate scale;
- a derived dimensionless form comparison between distinction and physical energy after the yardstick is fixed;
- a typed separation of descent, nonfaithful forgetting, outcome selection, record formation, and spectral coercivity;
- control through infinite volume and continuum removal; and
- either conservative local restriction to Yang--Mills QFT or a full recovery theorem.

Selection of $SU(3)$, Standard Model matter, or numerical MeV values is outside the compatibility claim. The Clay statement is indexed by every compact simple gauge group.

## Derived no-go: ordinary local expectation shells

Let $\mathcal N\subseteq\mathcal M$ be nested local observable algebras on the vacuum Hilbert space. Assume $\Omega$ is cyclic for $\mathcal N$ and separating for $\mathcal M$. If $E:\mathcal M\to\mathcal N$ is a vacuum-preserving conditional expectation, then for $n\in\mathcal N$ and $a\in\mathcal M$,

$$
\langle n\Omega,(a-Ea)\Omega\rangle
=
\omega(n^*a)-\omega(n^*Ea)
=0.
$$

Because $\mathcal N\Omega$ is dense, $(a-Ea)\Omega=0$; because $\Omega$ is separating for $\mathcal M$, $a=Ea$. Hence $\mathcal N=\mathcal M$. Under the usual Reeh--Schlieder hypotheses, a nontrivial vacuum-preserving expectation tower cannot be the ordinary nested region net. Regulator/RG block algebras, altered carriers or states, or controlled inequalities are required.

This is stronger than the generic warning that modular invariance is restrictive and is a decisive routing constraint for the proposed framework.

## Derived type separation: factivity is not spectral coercivity

For a finite commutative context $\mathcal C\cong C(X)$, a state restricts to a probability law on $X$, while a fact is a character $\chi_x(f)=f(x)$. A completely positive instrument fixes probabilities and conditional states but, absent a further actuality rule, still does not supply the obtained $x$. A record is a further directed extension preserving the chosen character. These are separate from a coarse map $E$, whose defect $1-E$ can measure lost distinctions, and separate again from the physical energy form $h$.

The proposed common process therefore has four typed arrows:

$$
\text{descent/gluing}
\ ;\quad
\text{nonfaithful forgetting}
\ ;\quad
\text{factive pointing and record}
\ ;\quad
\text{energy coercivity}.
$$

Only the last arrow defines a spectral gap. The useful new hypothesis is that the earlier wall operations construct and canonically normalize the same physical distinction family whose integrated defect form satisfies the lower-frame estimate. This remains a construction target, not a consequence of measurement theory.
