# Beginning Again on the Yang–Mills Problem

The Clay statement is a well-typed question asked from inside one presentation: a fixed Minkowski arena whose metric already orders scales, a time that is a coordinate to be reflected and continued, a gauge group supplied in advance, and a Hilbert space with a unique vacuum taken as the ontology. It defines the theory at the end where it has no scale and asks about the end where it has one. The existing mass-gap module has already typed every one of these assumptions correctly, but it did so across seventy notes with no single object of construction, and its last three weeks of growth were re-notation of one comparison lemma. This note fixes the mood for a restart: it catalogues the statement's assumptions against the vault's own vocabulary, states in eleven propositions what the asymmetry-first reading can already claim at theorem grade and what it cannot, names the one new mathematical question the reading generates, and orders a sequence of steps each of which can fail.

## 1. What the statement assumes, typed

The statement is candid about one thing: "one does not yet have a mathematically complete example of a quantum gauge theory in four-dimensional space-time, nor even a precise definition of quantum gauge theory in four dimensions" (p. 3). Everything else it treats as settled grammar. The grammar has eight load-bearing assumptions. Each is stated with the register it lives in ([[program-core/ontological-registers|register declaration]]) and the vault note that already types it, so that the charge "wrong framework" is a list of checkable type assignments rather than a mood.

Three remarks sharpen the charge rather than soften it.

First, the statement is not naive about units. "Δ > 0" is a scale-covariant statement, and since pure Yang–Mills has no dimensionful parameter other than the one it generates, its content is the dimensionless κ = Δ/Λ_YM > 0 (register-audit R2); dimensional transmutation is well understood as the trade of one dimensionless coupling for one scheme-labelled invariant ([[contemporary-puzzles/yang-mills-mass-gap/mass-gap-no-gos|NG3]]). So the God's-eye assumption is not a hidden *unit*. It is a hidden *ordering* and a hidden *definition*: the arena's metric orders scales before any theory exists, and the theory is then defined at the scale-free end (its short-distance behaviour is fixed to be free Yang–Mills with logarithmic approach, p. 6) and asked about at the scale-bearing end. That is the "scale gap" in one sentence: **the theory is specified where it has no scale and interrogated where it has one.** The bridge between the two ends is the renormalization-group trajectory, and the 2026-09-03 assessment found that controlling that crossover is exactly the missing analytic step. The philosophical diagnosis and the technical diagnosis point at the same place.

Second, the condition "m < ∞" on p. 6 is a non-emptiness clause. If the spectrum of H on Ω^⊥ were empty, every Δ would be admissible and m = ∞; the requirement says there must be excitations at all. (Nontriviality in the sense of interaction is the separate clause typed in `puzzle-as-posed`: a free massive field is gapped and non-empty and still not the theory asked for.) The statement therefore asks for a theory with *some* scale-bearing content and *no* content below the scale: both halves of "scale gap", with interaction demanded on top.

Third, A2 does not *forget* the asymmetry; it *presupposes* it and then quotients out the memory. Reflection positivity is a positivity of a pairing across a chosen hyperplane, with a chosen positive side; the Hilbert space is the quotient of the positive half by the null space; H ≥ 0 and the unitary clock are outputs. The Wick-rotated formulation thus already has the shape "asymmetric input, symmetric output". What it lacks is any account of *why* that input is available; the output carries the input only as the spectrum condition H ≥ 0. [[algebra/os-descent-naturality-and-clock-no-go|The OS-descent note]] types this exactly: the whole law is an object, the null quotient is a nonfaithful arrow, Euclidean transfer is a positive contraction, clock evolution is a unitary functional calculus on the quotient; (ON9) and (ON12) prove their compatibility and (ON17) forbids identifying them.

## 3. What the reading can already claim, in eleven propositions

The guiding intuitions of the restart — asymmetry first, two kinds of time, descent across a wall with a cost, scale gap rather than mass gap, invariant laws across the whole and the local registers, a fifth "pointing" dimension — each have a theorem-grade home or a theorem-grade correction. The propositions are labelled B1–B11 (the constitution owns the identifiers P1–P4b); each uses standard results and, where noted, its neighbours; the status of each is its weakest edge.

**B1. [STANDARD] Every local algebra is the same algebra; the gap is invisible in the objects.** In any theory with a nontrivial scaling limit the local algebras are factors of type III₁ (Fredenhagen 1985), and under split and nuclearity hypotheses each is isomorphic to the unique hyperfinite III₁ factor tensored with its centre (Buchholz–D'Antoni–Fredenhagen 1987; Haagerup's uniqueness). A gapped net and a gapless net have isomorphic local algebras. Therefore **no local algebra can see the mass gap. The gap lives entirely in the arrows — the inclusions A(O₁) ⊂ A(O₂) — together with the vacuum state.** This is the exact content of "the local perspective cannot see scale": in the category of local algebras all objects are isomorphic, and all physics is in the morphisms and the pointing. The second half matters (B3): the arrows alone do not carry it either.

**B2. [STANDARD] Every local algebra is intrinsically scale-free, gap or no gap.** The Connes invariant of a III₁ factor is S(M) = [0,∞): its modular spectrum contains every positive scale (Connes 1973). Type III_λ factors, with S(M) = {0} ∪ λ^ℤ, are the algebras in which scale is quantized; type III₁ is the one in which it is not. Local algebras of relativistic QFT are III₁. So the "here and now" of any bounded region contains no yardstick as a matter of algebraic type, whether or not the theory has one. **Scale-free local algebras inside a scale-bearing global theory is the normal situation, not a paradox.** The vault's [[wall-construction-interface/core-spectral-wall|core spectral wall]] states a related fact on the canonical core: "the scale-covariant whole is a weight, while every normal L¹ state points."

**B3. [STANDARD for the conformal half; CONJECTURE for the massive half; OPEN as to which invariant] Mass is the failure of the net's pointed inclusions to be dilation-equivariant.** The bare inclusion carries nothing: under the split property every double-cone inclusion A(O_r) ⊂ A(O_R) is abstractly isomorphic to every other, in every theory and for every mass (Doplicher–Longo standard split inclusions; the relative commutant is again hyperfinite III₁). What can carry scale is the *pointed* inclusion — the triple (A(O_r), A(O_R), Ω) up to vacuum-preserving unitary equivalence. In a conformal net, dilations are unitarily implemented and fix Ω, so pointed inclusions with equal cross-ratios are equivalent and the double-cone modular flow is geometric (Hislop–Longo 1982; Brunetti–Guido–Longo 1993). In a massive net there is no dilation, and the conjecture is that the pointed class depends on Δr and ΔR separately, not on R/r alone. **Mass is an invariant of pointed inclusions — arrows together with the state — not of algebras.** This is the same statement as the core spectral wall's "every normal state points", read at the level of the net. What is open is *which* computable invariant of the pointed inclusion detects it: the modular nuclearity index (Buchholz–D'Antoni–Longo 1990), the relative entropy of localized coherent excitations, or the non-geometric part of the modular generator (B4 below). This is the question of §4.

**B4. [STANDARD for the wedge and conformal cases; INTERPRETATION for the reading; numerical evidence only for the massive double cone] The "vertical" time is the part of the modular flow of a bounded region that no spacetime symmetry implements.** For a wedge, the modular flow of the vacuum is the boost in every Wightman theory (Bisognano–Wichmann 1975/76): a state-relative, thermal flow that happens to be a spacetime symmetry, hence absorbed into the four dimensions. For a connected double cone in a *conformal* theory the modular flow is again geometric (Hislop–Longo; conformal covariance is what is used, not dilation invariance alone, and disjoint unions of regions have non-geometric flows even there). For a double cone in a massive theory it is not known to be geometric; Saffary (2006) studied the massive generator for light cones and double cones, and Bostelmann–Cadamuro–Minz (2023) give numerical evidence that the double-cone modular operator depends on the mass and on angular momentum. (Longo–Morsella's announced massive results were withdrawn; do not import them.) Comparing a massive generator with the massless one presupposes an identification of one-particle spaces — time-zero data in both studies — so "the non-geometric part" is relative to that choice and is not canonical. The reading: **the second component of time the restart points at is the part of the modular flow of a bounded region that no spacetime flow implements. It is absent for connected double cones of a conformal theory and is conjectured to be the algebraic shadow of the gap when the theory is massive.** A CGMA-type argument (Buchholz–Dreyer–Florig–Summers 2000) makes the converse plausible: if the double-cone modular flows all acted geometrically they would generate additional spacetime symmetries, which mass forbids; this is a sketch, [CONJECTURE], not a theorem. The wedge is the only standard region in a massive theory whose modular flow is known to be geometric, and it is the one whose modular flow can be a Poincaré transformation at all (a boost preserving the wedge and its causal complement; light cones are excluded by Borchers' theorem with timelike translations, double cones by the absence of a fixing one-parameter subgroup of the right type). So, as a reading, the set of regions with geometric modular action measures the symmetry group, and its shrinkage from {wedges, light cones, connected double cones} in the conformal case to {wedges} in the massive case would be the algebraic signature of scale. [CONJECTURE] for the shrinkage; [STANDARD] for each cited endpoint.

**B5. [STANDARD, and EXACT in the vault] One arrow is scale-free; only two opposed arrows can carry scale.** A single half-sided modular inclusion generates a positive light-ray translation P whose spectrum is all of [0,∞) — the dilation covariance Δ^{it}PΔ^{−it} = e^{−2πt}P forbids any gap ([[the-grain-of-causal-scale/causal-spectrum|the pre-wall no-gap theorem]]; Borchers 1992; Wiesbrock 1993; Araki–Zsidó 2005). The Casimir needs two opposed generators: in two dimensions M² = P₊P₋, and under the boost P₊ and P₋ scale oppositely while their product is fixed. **Mass is the invariant of a pair of opposed directednesses under the boost; a gap, when there is one, is a floor on the one quantity that flow cannot move.** The module already holds this in `joint-causal-generators-and-the-mass-casimir` and in the paired filtration M_𝓕 = (K₊K₋)^{1/2} of `paired-scale-filtrations-and-the-invariant-incidence-wall`. It is the exact reason the restart's slogan "scale requires distinguishing here-and-now from then-and-there" is true: one direction alone has a dilation symmetry; the comparison of two does not.

**B6. [STANDARD for each link with its declared inputs; INTERPRETATION for the reading] Asymmetry-first is a theorem chain in algebraic QFT, not a hope — but no link is input-free.** (i) A one-sided inclusion N ⊂ M with a common cyclic separating vector and half-sided modular covariance yields a positive-energy translation group with U(a)MU(a)* ⊂ M for a ≥ 0; the spectrum condition is *derived* from the inclusion (Borchers 1992; Wiesbrock 1993; Araki–Zsidó 2005). (ii) The Poincaré group of four-dimensional Minkowski space is *reconstructed* from modular data — but the known theorems presuppose the wedge geometry: Borchers 1996 builds the group from the modular groups of wedge algebras "provided the groups act on the algebra of every double cone like the associated Lorentz boosts", and Buchholz–Dreyer–Florig–Summers 2000 start from a wedge-indexed net with geometric modular action; only Kähler–Wiesbrock 2001 claim to build the wedge net itself from finitely many algebras in prescribed modular positions with one state, and their exact hypotheses must be verified before that claim is filed. (iii) From the superselection sectors of an observable net with localizable charges — cone-localizable when the charged sector has an isolated mass shell (Buchholz–Fredenhagen 1982) — the compact gauge group is *reconstructed* as the dual of the sector category (Doplicher–Roberts 1990). So the modern structure of the subject runs arrows → spectrum condition → spacetime group → gauge group, with declared inputs at each link, and the Clay statement's "for any compact simple G" quantifies over a presentation-register object that, in the theory's own logic, is downstream. For pure Yang–Mills the point is sharper still: if colour is confined there are no charged sectors, so G is not visible in the observable net's superselection structure at all; its observable shadows are ratios such as m₀₊₊/√σ. The object the literature calls a **Borchers triple** (M, U, Ω) — a wedge algebra, a positive-energy translation representation, a vacuum — is already the asymmetry-first presentation of a QFT. It is a fair name for the restart's "QFToid". What it does not deliver is stated in B7 and §5.

**B7. [EXACT compatibility; EXACT no-go; the load-bearing step OPEN] The OS wall is the model of descent with a cost, and the cost does not select the gap.** Reflection positivity across the time-zero hyperplane is necessary for the clock: it gives H ≥ 0 and, by continuation, the unitary group. The null quotient is a nonfaithful arrow. What is exact ([[algebra/os-descent-naturality-and-clock-no-go|ON9, ON12]]): an OS-bounded, translation-covariant realization of a whole law onto a local law intertwines the reconstructed clocks even when noninjective, and if surjective it deletes exactly those spectral bands whose whole-carrier subspaces lie in its kernel (ON12a). What is also exact (ON17, ON23–ON25): an idempotent expectation cannot descend to a nontrivial unitary, and a bare noninjective quotient is compatible with *every* local gap value — nonfaithfulness selects nothing. **So the restart's claim "the residue of crossing the wall makes the reversible algebra possible" is true in the weak sense (positivity across the wall is what makes the clock exist) and false in the strong sense (the kernel of the wall does not determine the spectrum).** For the cost to be load-bearing, ker D̂ must be constructed geometrically as the band of soft whole-modes the wall renders invisible, without reading the desired spectrum backwards. No such construction exists.

**B8. [EXACT algebra; INTERPRETATION for the physical identification] The fifth coordinate is the trace-scaling direction of the canonical core.** The Falcone–Takesaki core M̃ = M ⋊_σ ℝ of a III₁ factor is a II_∞ factor carrying a semifinite trace and a one-parameter dual action that scales it: τ∘θ_s = e^{−s}τ. It adds exactly one real parameter to the algebra, and it is a register in which a "God's-eye" uniform measure exists — and that measure is scale-*covariant*, not scale-*fixed*. The four-dimensional local algebra is the fixed-point algebra of the dual action: it has no trace, only states; and on the core every normal L¹ state breaks the trace-scaling covariance ([[wall-construction-interface/core-spectral-wall|core spectral wall]]). The reading: **the yardstick the Clay statement tacitly uses lives in the core and is mislocated in the local algebra; "scale requires pointing" is the theorem that the scale-covariant whole is a weight while every normal state points.** The [[inbox/radical-copernicanism/commentary-part-2/time-parameter-type-audit|time-parameter type audit]] forbids identifying the dual parameter s with any other time without a seven-item soldering receipt; this proposition identifies nothing. It locates the "5-dimensional" intuition at the one place in the mathematics where a fifth real parameter with a trace actually appears.

**B9. [STANDARD for the anomaly and its cohomological form; INTERPRETATION for the descent reading] The trace anomaly is the obstruction that forces a scale; the gap is the separate dynamical half.** Classical pure Yang–Mills in four dimensions is dilation invariant. The quantum theory cannot be defined without pointing a scale — a renormalization condition — and the anomaly is the cohomology class (Wess–Zumino consistency; the Weyl-cocycle cohomology of local functionals, Bonora–Pasti–Bregola 1986) that says no *invariant* pointing exists: ⟨T^μ_μ⟩ ∝ (β(g)/g)·F², with β₀ = 11C_A/3 a group-theoretic number. The torsor of presentations has sections (any subtraction point μ); what it lacks is a dilation-invariant one. Λ_YM is the scheme-labelled point the quantum theory selects, and the renormalization-group transition law between presentations is the cocycle. This is not the H¹-of-the-base obstruction of MASTER §1 (no section at all); it is the weaker and exactly located obstruction to an invariant section. **So "scale from asymmetry" splits into a half that is already a theorem in the perturbative register — that a scale exists is cohomological and structural, needing no scalar field — and a half that is open — that every excitation engages it, κ = Δ/Λ_YM > 0.** This vindicates the complaint that scalar fields are invented rather than explained, but only for the first half. Caveat in the vault's own terms ([[contemporary-puzzles/yang-mills-mass-gap/register-audit|register audit]]): β₀ ≠ 0 is a fact about a formal series, and the operator identity for the trace in the constructed theory is part of what must be proved; the Clay statement itself lists the stress tensor among the desiderata (p. 6).

**B10. [EXACT under stated OS hypotheses] The gap is uniform past–future distinguishability.** Under stationary reversible Markov and OS realization hypotheses, the Friedrichs angle between the past and future half-space algebras at Euclidean separation ℓ is c_F(ℓ) = ‖e^{−ℓH₀}(1 − P₀)‖ = e^{−ℓΔ} in natural units ([[contemporary-puzzles/yang-mills-mass-gap/past-future-angle-and-the-transfer-gap|past–future angle]]). A gap is c_F(ℓ) < 1 for one, hence every, ℓ > 0. In a gapless theory there exist states for which "then" and "now" are indistinguishable at every finite separation. **This is the temporal form of "if here and now is indistinguishable from then and there, there is no scale and no measurement", and it is the definition of "scale gap" that should be adopted**: not "the theory has a scale" (massless-quark QCD has a scale and no gap; an infrared fixed point has a flow scale and no gap) but "every non-vacuum distinction engages the scale uniformly".

**B11. [EXACT — AFTER REDUCTION for the A₂ items; EXACT in the vault for the rest; a correction to the framing] The A₂ singularity, S⁶, and Keller maps supply global–local obstruction, not direction.** The vault has already recorded: the A₂ inverse cover is "an exact algebraic-geometric model of local invertibility without global single-valued invertibility" whose monodromy "is reversible" — exact, by that note's own hedge, only "conditional on the announced three-variable Jacobian counterexample and the local unreviewed analysis"; the octonionic S⁶ is nearly Kähler and non-integrable while the vault's integrable S⁶ (the Engel/Alpöge branch it treats as established, with auxiliary invariants under review) is a different structure that cannot be Kähler; Keller maps are étale, so an arrow built from them "would be globally real and locally undetectable, which is the opposite of how time is encountered" ([[sufficient-reason/sufficing-and-necessitating-reason-MASTER|MASTER §4]]). These objects are the right laboratory for **existence as a cohomology class** — local sections everywhere, no global section — which is the vault's best idea and the correct form of the global/local invariant the restart wants. They are the wrong laboratory for irreversibility. The arrow's home is the one-sided inclusion (B5, B6); their home is the obstruction (B9, and the torsor of B8). Keep both; do not ask either to do the other's work.

Two consequences of the eleven together. The slogan "time is two-dimensional" has an exact reading: the horizontal component is the translation (light-ray or clock) generated from the inclusion, the vertical component is the modular flow of the region: geometric for wedges in every theory and for connected double cones in conformal theories, and conjectured non-geometric for bounded regions of a gapped theory (B4). And the higher relativity principle the radical-Copernican inbox was looking for has the corrected form the commentary already gave it — a groupoid fibred over a monoid: democracy of presentations within a level (Connes cocycle; weight groupoid), one-sidedness across levels (inclusions; restriction of states) — with B3 adding the specific content that *scale is the invariant carried by the one-sided arrows and invisible to the groupoid of objects*.

## 4. The one new question the reading generates

Everything in §3 is either standard, or already in the vault, or a reading attached to a standard result. The reading generates exactly one question that the survey of 2026-09-05 did not find posed in the vault or, in this form, in the literature it checked:

> **Q\*.** Is there an invariant of a *pointed* bounded inclusion (A(O) ⊂ A(Ô), Ω) in a Haag–Kastler net — or of the double-cone modular flow relative to what the spacetime symmetries can implement — that (a) depends only on the conformal class of the pair of regions in every conformal net, (b) depends on the absolute size of the regions in every massive net, and (c) has a large-region behaviour whose *rate* detects the gap: exponential in the region size with exponent set by Δ when the net is gapped, and slower than any exponential when it is not?

Clause (c) is deliberately a rate, not a floor: any invariant satisfying (a) and continuous in the mass looks conformal on regions much smaller than the Compton length, so no gapped theory can have a uniform floor over all sizes, and the vault's own model — the past–future angle c_F(ℓ) = e^{−ℓΔ} of B10 — reads the gap off a rate. If Q\* has a positive answer, the Clay gap becomes a statement purely about pointed arrows and the vertical flow — the programme's ontology with nothing lost — and the theorem proving the equivalence would be new mathematics of the *reformulation*, not a proof of the gap. If Q\* has a negative answer for every natural candidate, the two-times reading of the gap is wrong and should be retired. Candidates, in order of computability: the defect of the massive double-cone modular generator relative to the conformal one under a declared one-particle identification (B4); the large-region asymptotics of the modular nuclearity index of the pointed inclusion (Buchholz–D'Antoni–Longo 1990 give mass-dependent bounds); the relative-entropy response of the vacuum to localized coherent excitations as a function of region size against Compton length. (A Hilbert-space angle between A(O)Ω and its boosted image is not a candidate: Reeh–Schlieder makes both dense.) The laboratory is the free scalar field, massive against massless, in 1+1 and 3+1 dimensions, where every candidate is computable at one-particle level (Bostelmann–Cadamuro–Minz's scheme is a working numerical method). The kill battery is §5.

## 5. What the reading cannot claim, and the kills a restart must respect

The reading does not shorten the analytic problem. The one case in which a gap has been proved for a perturbatively massless gauge theory — three-dimensional compact U(1) for all couplings (Göpfert–Mack 1982, via duality to a ℤ-ferromagnet and a Coulomb gas; the gap is exponentially small in 1/g²) — and the one case in which it has been convincingly computed without proof (Karabali–Kim–Nair in 2+1 dimensions, lattice-corroborated) both worked by changing the *carrier* so that the gap became structural on the new carrier. That is the asymmetry-first move in practice, and it is also the register audit's item 2 ("find a presentation or dual carrier on which the infrared distinction form is structurally positive"). Nothing about the reading tells one which carrier; it tells one what kind of object to look for.

The Borchers-triple grammar (B6) takes the one-particle mass as an *input* — the representation of the translations is chosen. In every constructed model of that type (Lechner's integrable 1+1 models; Buchholz–Lechner–Summers deformations in four dimensions, whose local algebras are not known to be nontrivial) the gap is assumed, not derived. The programme differs from that literature in exactly one demand: that the *scale* also be an output of the arrows. B7 shows a bare quotient cannot supply it. That is where the novelty and the difficulty both sit.

Kills already on record that any restart inherits, with their owners: a fixed-cutoff gap is not the theorem (NG1); the unbroken half-sided generator cannot carry a gap (NG2; B5); dimensionless data plus c, ħ, k_B fix no scale, and a G-based gap answers a different problem (NG3); Poincaré covariance of an emergent arena must be derived before the problem can be stated in it (NG5); retyping does not solve (NG6); no vacuum-preserving expectation exists between proper nested local algebras (NG7, Reeh–Schlieder); descent, forgetting, factivity and gap are four arrows (NG8); density ratios and entropy ceilings do not give spectral floors (NG9, NG10); the wall projection's form vanishes on the formed carrier (range–kernel no-go); an idempotent is not a clock and a positive contraction is not its continuation (ON17, ON20–22); unit rate is not derivable from character theory (`basic-concepts/soldering/continuous-character-no-go`); on a totally ordered wall chain there is no base cohomological obstruction and the III₁ flow of weights is a point (`inbox/radical-copernicanism/stack-of-weight-groupoids`); changing the weight cannot toggle a core between II₁ and II_∞; a fibre of state restriction is not automatically a torsor; the relative commutant is not the kernel of restriction (`inbox/radical-copernicanism/commentary-part-2/correction-ledger`); "weight = frame" holds only inside gauged-modular-flow constructions (`inbox/radical-copernicanism/modular-theory-inventory`, `commentary/audit-findings`). The kill battery of 2026-09-03 applies to any proposed mechanism and to any candidate for Q\*: U(1)₄ at weak coupling must come out gapless, U(1)₃ gapped, two-dimensional Yang–Mills without glueballs, and any bound must be flat in g (a bound analytic in g² at g = 0 is not the gap).

Finally, the survey turned up a few statements in the module that a restart should not carry forward without checking: the vault treats Engel's "self-contained geometric proof of smooth existence" of an integrable complex structure on S⁶ as established (`s6-descent-defect-and-the-chirality-firewall`; `algebra/s6-manuscript-branch`, with only the manuscript's auxiliary invariants under review) — the existence question has a long history of withdrawn proofs, so any load-bearing use should carry an independent verification receipt; a dependence on a 2026 preprint (Wirth) in `kazhdan-markov-process-carrier`; and the recurring elevation of finite identities to [EXACT] beside [OPEN] physics, which inflates the appearance of progress. This note is not exempt: its B11 was caught doing the same to the A₂ inverse cover in draft.

## 6. The plan

One object, one question, seven steps numbered 0–6. Each step names what would kill it. Nothing downstream of a killed step is written.

**Step 0 — Freeze and re-head.** Add this note to the module and rewrite the first paragraph of its `inq.md` so that the target is (S12) of the scale-torsor note together with B1: *construct one scale-equivariant whole whose local presentations recover pure Yang–Mills and whose natural dimensionless response has a uniform positive floor on the complete vacuum complement; the gap is a property of pointed inclusions, not of algebras.* Consolidate the thirty comparison-lemma instances into one note with an instance table (recommended on 2026-09-03; not yet done). Move the cosmological-yardstick numerology to a separate calibration module or the junk drawer with a one-line pointer explaining PC5. Kill: none; this is hygiene. Cost: a day.

**Step 1 — The assumption catalogue as a standing note.** Promote §1 to `clay-statement-assumptions.md`, one row per assumption, with page references and the register each assumption lives in, so that "the framework is wrong" is henceforth a list of type assignments that a reader can dispute row by row. Include the remark that the statement is scale-covariant and that its blind spot is an ordering and a definition, not a unit. Kill: none. Cost: half a day.

**Step 2 — The anomaly as descent obstruction.** Write B9 as a note in the torsor and cohomology vocabulary the vault already owns (`basic-concepts/torsors`, `descent`, `stacks`; MASTER §1): the scale torsor of presentations of the regularized theory; the anomaly as the class obstructing a dilation-invariant section; Λ_YM as the cocycle; β₀ as group theory; the formal-series caveat. State plainly that this settles the *existence* of scale in the perturbative register and leaves the *engagement* (κ > 0) open. Kill: if the standard Wess–Zumino cohomology cannot be typed as an obstruction class on the vault's scale torsor without violating PC1, the note is withdrawn. Cost: two or three days; it is retyping of standard material, and it should cite Collins–Duncan–Joglekar and the Stora–Zumino/Bonora line as its sources.

**Step 3 — Scale as an inclusion invariant, computed.** This is the spine, and the only step that produces mathematics not already in the vault. For the free scalar field in 1+1 and 3+1 dimensions: (a) record that the double-cone modular flow is geometric at m = 0 in 3+1 dimensions (Hislop–Longo) and for the chiral or derivative nets in 1+1 dimensions (Brunetti–Guido–Longo 1993; the massless scalar itself has no vacuum representation in 1+1, and the m → 0 limit is singular there), and that at m > 0 the modular operator depends on m (Bostelmann–Cadamuro–Minz 2023, numerical; Saffary 2006 for the generator), so that B4 is typed at its true grade; (b) compute at one-particle level at least one candidate from §4 as a function of m·r and m·R — the non-geometric defect of the double-cone modular generator against the conformal one, and the Friedrichs angle between past and future slabs — with a stdlib-only receipt script in the module's receipt contract, an output file beside it, and a nonzero exit on failure; (c) verify that each candidate depends only on the conformal class at m = 0 and on absolute size at m > 0, and record its rate for m·R ≫ 1. Kill: a candidate whose large-region rate is independent of m, or that fails to distinguish the massive from the massless pointed inclusion at all, kills "scale lives in the pointed arrows" for that candidate; if every natural candidate dies, B3 is demoted to a slogan and Q\* is answered in the negative for the free field. Cost: two to three weeks of actual computation.

**Step 4 — The kill battery on the surviving candidate.** Before any surviving candidate is called an "algebraic form of the gap", evaluate it on U(1)₃ (must detect the Göpfert–Mack gap and its exponential smallness in 1/g²; this test is heuristic, since the theorem is a lattice theorem and no continuum net is available to carry the invariant), U(1)₄ at weak coupling (must report no gap rate: Coulomb phase, Guth 1980; Fröhlich–Spencer 1982), two-dimensional pure Yang–Mills (no local excitations at all — the candidate must return the empty-spectrum value, and a candidate that reports a finite rate there is measuring something else), and the free massive field at several masses (rate must be monotone in m). Kill: a candidate that reports a gap rate for U(1)₄ at weak coupling, or whose rate is analytic in g² at g = 0 for a non-abelian theory, is not the gap.

**Step 5 — The asymmetry-first grammar, stated once.** Write B6 as a single note: the theorem chain from a one-sided inclusion to the spectrum condition, to the four-dimensional Poincaré group, to the gauge group, with exact hypotheses and sources verified into the library (Borchers 1992 and 1996; Wiesbrock 1993; Araki–Zsidó 2005; Kähler–Wiesbrock 2001; Buchholz–Dreyer–Florig–Summers 2000; Doplicher–Roberts 1990; Buchholz–Fredenhagen 1982). Mark where pure Yang–Mills' G sits (presentation register, no sectors) and mark the gap as the one thing the chain does not supply. This note becomes the canonical statement of "symmetry as the stabilizer of prior asymmetric data"; the exceptional and octonionic stabilizer notes become *instances* of it, as `oriented-descent-angle` already frames them, and the module stops re-deriving the principle from the Albert algebra. Kill: Borchers 1996 and BDFS are already known to presuppose the wedge geometry (B6); if Kähler–Wiesbrock's hypotheses also do, then no theorem builds the wedge net from bare modular data, the link "arrows → spacetime group" is restated with the wedge geometry as declared input, and the reading loses its strongest claim.

**Step 6 — The reformulated conjecture, and its equivalence to the Clay gap.** Only if Step 3 leaves a candidate alive: state the conjecture *for the pure Yang–Mills net, assuming existence* — the large-region rate of the candidate invariant of every pointed bounded inclusion is bounded below, for double cones of fixed shape as their size grows, by a positive multiple of the scheme-labelled Λ_YM — and prove its equivalence to the Clay gap as stated on p. 6. The equivalence is a theorem to be proved, not assumed; the vault already has the analogous equivalence for the past–future angle (B10). Success means the Clay problem has been restated in the programme's ontology with nothing lost and the open problem is located: the uniform vertical defect. Kill: a gapped theory whose candidate rate vanishes, or a gapless theory with a positive rate, kills the two-times reading of the gap outright.

What this plan does not promise is a proof. If the programme survives Step 4, the next question — whether the uniform vertical rate can be proved for the interacting theory — is the crossover problem in new clothing, and the plan will have earned the right to say so with a specific object in hand rather than a comparison lemma.
