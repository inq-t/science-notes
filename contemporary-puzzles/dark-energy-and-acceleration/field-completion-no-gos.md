# Why the Direct Field Lifts Fail

The reclassification is not merely a preference in vocabulary, because it predicts something the substance reading does not: that attempts to write the response as an ordinary field should fail structurally rather than by bad luck. A scoped no-go discharges the prediction for the two most direct completions. Its scope is narrower than the archive's earlier reading of it, and the same computation is a live threat to the framework, because a response with no covariant completion at all is not a physical sector either.

## The standard obstruction, first

The difficulty is not novel and does not require this framework. For a single minimally coupled canonical scalar,

$$
\rho_\phi+p_\phi=\dot\phi^2\ge0,
$$

so a canonical scalar of *positive energy density* obeys $w_\phi\ge-1$ and cannot be phantom at all, let alone cross the divide. The positivity hypothesis is not decoration: with $V$ sufficiently negative the density itself goes negative and the bound on $w$ flips, which is why the canonical statement is the unconditional one about $\rho+p$ rather than a bound on the ratio. Reversing the kinetic sign buys $w<-1$ at the cost of a ghost. Crossing $w=-1$ requires the kinetic coefficient to pass through zero, which is exactly where the perturbation normalization degenerates. This is why the literature's crossing models are two-field quintom constructions, non-canonical kinetic theories, or modified-gravity effective descriptions rather than single scalars.

The relevance is immediate. [[puzzle-as-posed|P4]] is the observation that the data now prefer a history which crosses the divide near $z\simeq0.4$. The most-favoured feature of the data is the feature least available to the simplest candidate.

## The no-go, at the scope the canon gives it

**[NO-GO — POSITIVE-KINETIC SCALAR]** [[causal-scale-theory/no-gos/positive-kinetic-field-crossing|The no-go note]] owns the calculation. The response satisfies

$$
1+w_X=\frac{2\nu}{3}\tanh(\nu x),
$$

so $1+w_X<0$ for $x<0$, vanishes at $x=0$, and is positive for $x>0$. Any positive-definite sigma model has

$$
\rho+p=G_{IJ}\dot\phi^I\dot\phi^J\ge0,
$$

with the canonical scalar the special case $\rho_\phi+p_\phi=\dot\phi^2$. Neither can cover the pre-crossing branch, which is the entire past of the crossing. The failure is therefore not a defect at an isolated point that a field redefinition might absorb.

In the single-field Mukhanov construction the kinetic normalization is proportional to $1+w_X$ and changes sign at the crossing, and the pump field develops singular crossing behaviour of the form

$$
\frac{z''}{z}\sim-\frac1{4\theta^2}.
$$

The coefficient is worth noting: $1/4$ is the critical value of the inverse-square problem, the boundary at which the operator ceases to be bounded below. But the current no-go note does *not* draw a robustness conclusion from that, and neither should this one. An earlier archive layer read the marginality as showing that small deformations of one-field models cannot repair the obstruction; that reading is not carried by the canonical note, and it is not adopted here.

The canon's own scoping is explicit: this excludes the most direct canonical-field lift, not all covariant completions, and it names live alternatives — constrained systems, non-minimal couplings, effective nonlocal response, or a genuinely geometric stress tensor. Each needs its own stability analysis.

## What the internal spectral structure does not supply

There is a standing temptation here, and the no-go register closes it off in one line: internal Darboux positivity does not imply spacetime perturbative stability.

The binary channel contains an exact Witten–Darboux pair with a $\operatorname{sech}^2$ partner potential, and the response density carries the same $\operatorname{sech}^2$ factor. [[binary-information-geometry/witten-darboux|The Witten pair note]] is emphatic that this is an internal factorization of the binary profile and not a derived spacetime perturbation operator; the archived master spells the point out further, denying in turn that the growth equation is the Pöschl–Teller operator, that the response density is a wave potential, that reflectionlessness fixes a signal speed, or that factorization fixes a kinetic sign.

One $\operatorname{sech}^2$ appearing in the metric, the zero-mode density, and the partner potential is one mathematical structure seen three times, not three independent confirmations.

## What the no-go establishes, and what it does not

It establishes that the two most direct completions fail exactly, for reasons internal to the profile.

It does not establish that no covariant completion exists. There is no theorem to that effect, and [[causal-scale-theory/conjectures/covariant-response-sector|the covariant response sector]] is carried as an open conjecture with its own criteria. Nor does it license the inference "therefore it is state geometry"; that identification is a separate proposal with separate obligations.

What it supports positively is only an elimination, and the elimination is all that is canonical. Not a propagating local field, which this no-go excludes for the direct lifts. Not a function reconstructed from the metric, which [[causal-scale-theory/no-gos/background-reconstruction-is-not-wall-construction|the reconstruction no-go]] excludes. [[causal-scale-theory/anchored-response-density-postulate|The source law]] supplies the constitutive step but does not itself type the state coordinate.

The label usually attached to the surviving slot — a collective constitutive quantity of the same type as an inverse temperature, a chemical potential, or an order parameter — comes from the archived Revision 2 monograph and is listed in the refactor plan's salvage ledger as not yet migrated into the canon. It is a useful description and it is cited here as archival rather than canonical. Either way the slot is reached by elimination and not by construction, which is weaker than a derivation and should be described that way.

## The same computation cuts against the framework

This is the honest reading and it should not be buried. **[OPEN CONSTRUCTION]** [[causal-scale-theory/conjectures/covariant-response-sector|The covariant-response conjecture]] records that until the response sector is constructed, stability, growth, lensing, and CMB predictions are not properties of the homogeneous pulse alone. If no conserved covariant $T^X_{ab}$ realizes the background, the homogeneous response is not a physical sector at all.

So the no-go is simultaneously the module's best evidence and its sharpest danger. It vindicates the reclassification against the field reading, and it narrows the space in which the reclassification must find its own covariant realization. A reader who takes it as pure vindication has read half the result.

The asymmetry that keeps this from being a wash is worth stating. A field theory that cannot be written down has no fallback: its whole content was the field. A constitutive response that cannot yet be covariantly completed retains the exact background identities, the invariant, and the type argument, and loses its claim to be a physical sector until the completion arrives. That is the sense in which this failure localizes rather than propagating, which [[causal-scale-theory/inq|the present standing]] names as the programme's structural virtue.

A second exclusion from the same register belongs here for symmetry, because it cuts against a story this module might otherwise be tempted to tell: [[binary-information-geometry/reflected-divergence#Reflection|the reflected divergence is even]] in $\theta$ and therefore cannot orient cosmic evolution. The pulse's temporal asymmetry — phantom first, quintessence-like after — is not explained by the state-space geometry. It is imposed by the expanding branch.
