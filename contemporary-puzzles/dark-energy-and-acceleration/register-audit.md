# Register Audit of the Acceleration Inference

Auditing the inference from measurement to "dark energy" one step at a time shows that the argument changes register exactly once, at the application of the Einstein equation, and that the change is a soldering rather than a report. The substance reading is the image of a scale-register fact under that map, and the inventory question demands a preimage. Naming that gap is only worth doing if the naming is falsifiable, so the audit ends with the condition under which the charge of category error reverses onto this framework instead.

## The method

[[cosmodynamics/registers-and-type-discipline|Type discipline]] requires that causal order, conformal geometry, metric scale, proper time, modular flow, state deformation, probability, outcome, energy, and entropy be given different mathematical types, and that any claim making one determine another exhibit its map. The method is not a prohibition. It is a bookkeeping requirement, and its whole value is that it converts a vague unease into a located obligation.

That note also lists the characteristic type errors, two of which are about to become relevant verbatim:

$$
\text{choice of energy zero}\ne\text{gravitational source},
\qquad
\text{phenomenological fit}\ne\text{microscopic derivation}.
$$

## The one exact step

**[EXACT]** [[conformal-scale-geometry/flrw-scale-section-kinematics|The scale-section identities]] give, with $\sigma=1/a$ and conformal time $\eta$,

$$
\mathcal R=\frac{c}{aH}=-\frac{c\,\sigma}{\mathrm d\sigma/\mathrm d\eta},
\qquad
q=\frac{\mathrm d^2(\ln\sigma)/\mathrm d\eta^2}{\left[\mathrm d(\ln\sigma)/\mathrm d\eta\right]^2},
$$

and therefore

$$
\ddot a>0
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
q<0
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\frac{\mathrm d^2\ln\sigma}{\mathrm d\eta^2}<0 .
$$

This is exact kinematics and holds without Einstein's equation or any matter model. The 1998 discovery, stated in the register in which it was actually made, is: *the logarithm of the scale section is concave in conformal time.*

Note what has and has not been established. **[STANDARD]** [[conformal-scale-geometry/causal-order-and-metric-scale|Causal order fixes a conformal class, not a unique metric]], so $\sigma$ is genuine independent data and not a coordinate artifact — [[conformal-time/entry|conformal time]] is explicit that factoring out $a^2$ removes neither expansion nor redshift nor curvature. But log-concavity of a section is not by itself evidence that scale is *dynamically sourced*; that is the substantive claim, and it is not yet in evidence at this line.

## Where the register changes

The step from $q_0<0$ to "there is a component with $p<-\rho/3$" runs through the Friedmann acceleration equation, which is the Einstein equation specialized to FLRW. [[cosmodynamics/soldering-constants|Constants as soldering structures]] is the right frame: $G$ is a conversion between the curvature register and the stress register, and the equation carrying it is a compatibility law, not an observation within either register.

What makes the conversion interesting rather than routine is that it is not injective. **[EXACT — REFORMULATION]** [[conformal-scale-geometry/scale-tractor-transport|Scale-tractor geometry]] splits the Einstein equations into a trace-free transport equation and a scalar norm equation, and [[causal-scale-theory/vacuum-residual-sector|the residual-sector note]] carries the identity showing the transport channel is blind to metric-proportional shifts:

$$
T_{ab}\longmapsto T_{ab}-\lambda g_{ab}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
(T_{ab}-\lambda g_{ab})^\circ=T^\circ_{ab}.
$$

So "what accelerates the universe" is already two questions in two equations before any new physics is proposed. The obstruction to parallel scale transport lives in the trace-free channel and cannot see $\lambda$; the common scalar part must be carried by the norm equation, boundary data, or a global constraint. Demanding one inventory entry that answers both is what fuses [[puzzle-as-posed|P1 and P2]] to [[puzzle-as-posed|P3 and P4]] and guarantees that no candidate satisfies all of them.

The same note carries a second, independent instance of the same blindness on the state side, and keeps the two apart carefully: adding a multiple of the identity to a modular Hamiltonian cancels from the normalized density operator. Two blindnesses in two registers are suggestive, and the note is explicit that cancellation from a normalized comparison does not imply cancellation from gravity.

## The error, stated precisely

Let $\mathcal E$ denote the soldering induced by the Einstein equation, carrying a source configuration to a scale history. The measurement determines a scale history $h$. Standard practice reports the discovery as a source configuration $s$ with $\mathcal E(s)=h$, and then asks what $s$ is made of.

> "Dark energy" names $\mathcal E^{-1}(h)$ read as though it were a single physical constituent. The inventory question presupposes that the preimage is nonempty, that it is unique, and that its members are of matter type. None of the three is established; the second fails in the trace-free channel, which annihilates metric-proportional shifts; and the third is what [[field-completion-no-gos|the field-completion no-go]] rules out for the two most direct candidates.

Stated in the vault's own error list: the argument slides from a fact about metric scale to a claim about gravitational source, using an equation that relates the two registers, and then treats the result as though the source register had been observed directly. It is the same shape as reading a thermodynamic response function as a mechanical force because both appear in the same equation of state.

This is not the complaint that dark energy is "only effective." Effective descriptions are respectable and often all one needs. The complaint is narrower: the *explanatory* question has been posed in a register the measurement never entered, and that is why it has resisted answer for a quarter century while the *effective* description has been stable and successful the whole time.

## What follows for the reclassification

If the acceleration is a scale-register fact, the thing to construct is a law for the scale register — something that says why $\ln\sigma$ should be concave over a bounded interval — and to let the source description be whatever $\mathcal E$ makes of it. That is the shape of causal scale theory: the response is the free-energy curvature of a scale-indexed state family, and $\rho_X$ is its image under the soldering, not its substance.

The typing then carries a consequence that a fluid picture obscures. A response to *changing* scale is intrinsically transient: it is large where the change is extremal and decays as the state settles. A substance with $w=-1$ has no such structure. Permanence is a property of constants; a pulse is what a response looks like. Everything rigid in the profile — one maximum, a mandatory phantom crossing, a finite acceleration episode — descends from that single type distinction rather than from a fitted shape.

The register change also has to be made honestly in the other direction, and [[wall-construction-interface/vertical-and-horizontal-motion|the vertical/horizontal distinction]] is where that is enforced: scale indexes a *family* of states, and comparing members of a family requires an actual cross-fiber transport rather than a derivative symbol. Retyping the puzzle buys nothing if the new register's own maps are left implicit.
