# Perturbation and Symmetry Breaking

The perturbation problem is to explain how a symmetric law or state supports gauge-invariant differentiated observables and their evolution without confusing symmetry of the substrate with constancy of every realized record. This module presently marks that open puzzle: [[program-core/contextual-descent-from-homogeneity|contextual descent]] owns the logical distinction, while CWST's independent W2 and W3 maps and [[causal-wall-spectral-theory/open-questions/lorentzian-evolution-and-matching|Lorentzian evolution and matching]] own the physical construction obligations.
