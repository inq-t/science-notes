That report is a register conversion, and the converted puzzle promptly grew a scandal the inventory language cannot state: the anomaly organizes itself around one *acceleration*, $g_\dagger\approx1.2\times10^{-10}\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}$, numerically at the acceleration of the cosmological horizon.
