# Register Audit

The inference "flat rotation curves, therefore missing matter" changes register in its second clause, exactly as the dark-energy inference does: a kinematic residual measured in the acceleration register is converted into an inventory claim by pushing it through the gravitational source map and demanding a preimage. This note performs the audit, types the acceleration register as thermal-modular, and states the crossover reading — with the condition under which the audit reverses and the substance answer wins.

## Where the register changes

**Step 1 (measurement).** What is measured is $g_{\rm obs}(r)$, a centripetal acceleration — an acceleration-register quantity — together with $g_{\rm bar}(r)$, the Newtonian field of the observed baryons. The datum is a relation between two accelerations, and its empirical form is one curve with one scale $g_\dagger$ ([[contemporary-puzzles/galactic-rotation-anomaly/puzzle-as-posed|the puzzle as posed]], P2).

**Step 2 (conversion).** "There exists unseen matter with density $\rho_{\rm DM}(r)$" applies the source map $g\mapsto\rho$ of Newton/GR. This is a soldering across registers, per [[cosmodynamics/soldering-constants|the soldering reading of the constants]]: it converts an acceleration-register fact into an inventory-register claim. Dark matter, at galactic scales, names the *image* of the measured relation under that map. The inventory question "which particle?" demands a preimage that nothing in the disk data guarantees — the same audit step as [[contemporary-puzzles/dark-energy-and-acceleration/register-audit|the dark-energy audit]], with acceleration in place of scale curvature.

**Step 3 (the tell).** If the residual were an inventory fact, its organizing variable should be inventory-like — a mass, a length, a formation history. It is instead an *acceleration*, $g_\dagger$ — a variable of the register the inference left. A substance answer must explain why the substance arranges itself to hide behind one acceleration scale in every disk; the halo literature carries that burden under the names diversity and RAR-tightness.

## Typing the acceleration register

**[STANDARD]** Acceleration is not inventory data; it is thermal-modular data. An accelerated frame is a KMS state at $T_U=\hbar a/2\pi ck_B$ (Unruh; Bisognano–Wichmann), and the cosmos independently carries a bath at $T_{dS}=\hbar H/2\pi k_B$ (Gibbons–Hawking) — both G-free, per [[deriving-g-v2/the-modular-register-is-g-free|the modular audit]]. The two baths cross where

$$
a\sim cH ,
\qquad
\frac{cH_0}{2\pi}=1.04\times10^{-10}\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-2}} .
$$

**[COINCIDENCE — RECEIPT]** The measured $g_\dagger=1.20\times10^{-10}$ sits within fifteen percent of this crossover. The typed reading: below $g_\dagger$ a wedge's local modular structure no longer dominates the cosmological bath — the deep-MOND regime is *the regime where the global register owns the local frame*. Milgrom's own 1999 vacuum reading and Verlinde's 2016 de Sitter-entropy displacement ([[vendor/entropic-gravity/verlinde-emergent-gravity|audited]]) are prior art for this placement; the stochastic-gravity anti-correlation of [[library/anomalous-galactic-rotation-curves/inq|Oppenheim–Russo]] reaches the same scale by cutting its diffusion at the Hubble radius — a convergence of *scale*, not yet of mechanism.

## What the reading does and does not assert

It asserts that the organizing variable ($g_\dagger$), its numerical value ($\sim cH/2\pi$), and its register (acceleration = temperature) are what a bath crossover would look like, and that the inventory inference is therefore not forced by disk data. It does *not* assert a mechanism (no covariant sector exists in this programme), does not touch P1/P4's cluster and CMB evidence, and does not make the cosmological nonbaryonic component go away. The reading competes for P2 and P3 only.

## The reversal condition

The audit reverses — the substance answer wins and the register reading becomes the epicycle — if any of: a direct detection of a dark-matter particle with the right abundance; a demonstration that feedback-shaped halos generically produce the RAR with its measured tightness *and* the $cH$ coincidence; or a measured redshift-independence of $g_\dagger$ where the crossover reading requires drift ([[bath-crossover-no-gos]]). Symmetrically, the substance reading loses P2/P3 if $g_\dagger$ is measured to drift with $H(z)$.
