# Receipt Ledger

The evidence for the bath-crossover reading is one arithmetic coincidence, one register argument, and two pieces of prior art — nothing more, and this ledger says so before anyone is tempted to say otherwise. Layers are kept separate per the programme rule: statement, receipt, comparison, and microscopic claim never certify one another.

## Layer 1 — Assertions with receipts

## Layer 2 — What is imported, at what grade

The Unruh and Gibbons–Hawking laws are STANDARD and G-free ([[deriving-g-v2/the-modular-register-is-g-free|modular audit]]). The RAR and its scale are measured (McGaugh–Lelli–Schombert 2016). ## Layer 3 — Everything wrong with this comparison

The $2\pi$ is one order-one convention among several; the honest statement is $g_\dagger=O(1)\times cH_0$ with the specific convention chosen a priori by the Unruh form. The comparison inherits the $H_0$ tension at ten percent. $g_\dagger$ itself carries a twenty-percent systematic from disk inclinations and mass-to-light ratios. The reading has no covariant sector, so it predicts no RAR *shape*, no cluster physics, and no CMB — the concessions of NG1 and NG2 are load-bearing, not fine print. And the coincidence has been noticed for forty years without becoming a mechanism; novelty here is only the register typing and the drift prediction N1.

## Layer 4 — What would count as more

In order of cheapness: a rederivation of the RAR normalization drift $E(z)$ from any independent high-$z$ kinematic sample; a covariant response sector from the wall programme whose low-acceleration limit fixes the interpolation shape with no new scale; a cluster prediction distinguishing "two baths plus cosmological component" from "one substance everywhere." Until at least the first exists, this module's claim grade is COINCIDENCE with a falsifiable rider, and its citation grade in other modules must not exceed that.
