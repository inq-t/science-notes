# Bath-Crossover No-Gos and Predictive Content

If the rotation anomaly is a thermal-bath crossover rather than an inventory, the reading is falsifiable: it fixes the scale's value, forbids it from being a fundamental constant, and inherits scope boundaries it cannot excuse. This note states the positive content at the scope the canon allows, then the no-gos, then the kill list. Everything is downstream of the register audit; nothing here constructs a covariant sector, and the grades say so.

## Positive content

**N1 — The scale is not a constant.** **[CONDITIONAL PREDICTION]** If $g_\dagger\sim cH/2\pi$, then $g_\dagger$ is epoch-dependent:

$$
g_\dagger(z)=g_\dagger(0)\,E(z),
\qquad
E(z)=\sqrt{\Omega_m(1+z)^3+\Omega_\Lambda},
$$

so $E(0.5)=1.32$, $E(1)=1.79$, $E(2)=3.03$ (receipts). A radial-acceleration relation measured in $z\gtrsim1$ disks should show a normalization drift by these factors. Fundamental-constant MOND predicts no drift. This is the cleanest divergence between the two non-substance readings, and it is observational, not philosophical.

**N2 — The scale is not adjustable.** **[COINCIDENCE — RECEIPT]** $cH_0/2\pi=1.042\times10^{-10}$ against $g_\dagger=1.20\times10^{-10}$: ratio 1.15, zero fitted parameters under the stated convention. Honesty items: the $2\pi$ is one choice among order-one conventions (though it predates the precision RAR measurement, via the Unruh form $T\propto a/2\pi$); with $H_0=73$ the ratio improves to 1.06, so the comparison inherits the $H_0$ tension at the ten-percent level; the pure-$\Lambda$ form $c^2\sqrt{\Lambda/3}/2\pi$ gives ratio 1.39. The receipt asserts the order-one window and *also* asserts that dropping the $2\pi$ misses by a factor 5.5 — the match is convention-sensitive and the script says so, per the receipt discipline.

**N3 — Embedded systems feel the larger bath.** **[QUALITATIVE]** A subsystem accelerated within a larger field sits in the larger system's frame — the analogue of MOND's external field effect, which is itself claimed detected in SPARC data (Chae et al. 2020; contested). A bath reading makes the EFE natural rather than anomalous: the relevant temperature is set by the total wedge, not the isolated subsystem.

## No-gos

**NG1 — No cluster or CMB rescue.** The crossover reading owns P2 and P3 of [[contemporary-puzzles/galactic-rotation-anomaly/puzzle-as-posed|the puzzle as posed]] and *concedes* P1's cosmological component and P4's cluster residual: a bath crossover with no covariant sector cannot source the third acoustic peak or the Bullet Cluster offset. Whoever imports this module must not present it as a dark-matter solution; it is a reclassification of the disk-scale regularity only. (The stochastic-gravity literature attempts the missing pieces — [[library/phantom-cold-dark-matter/inq|phantom CDM]] is the candid null so far, thirty-one orders short.)

**NG2 — No mechanism without a sector.** The reading supplies register, scale, and value — not dynamics. Deriving the *shape* of the interpolation (the RAR exponential) requires the covariant response sector this programme has not constructed; until then any shape agreement is numerology and must not be claimed.

**NG3 — Solar-system silence.** **[RECEIPT]** Every solar-system acceleration sits far above the crossover (Saturn: $6.5\times10^{-5}\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}$, five orders above $g_\dagger$), so the reading predicts exactly nothing there — which is required, since nothing is seen.
