# Pointed CP Fusion Residue

A supplied normal completely positive map has a canonical carrier-changing
$W^*$-GNS realization. Under composition, the GNS carrier of the composite
embeds isometrically as the cyclic summand generated by the two pointed
carriers; the adjoint is a coisometric endpoint descent, and its orthogonal
kernel is the fusion residue. A CP semigroup therefore gives a cyclic
inclusion system, equivalently a subproduct system, and becomes a product
system at this level exactly when every fusion residue vanishes. The residue
is exact operator-algebraic data, but it is not by itself a transfer defect,
entropy, clock, record, fact, or Yang--Mills excitation.

**Status: [ESTABLISHED MATHEMATICS] for the pointed $W^*$-GNS construction,
cyclic fusion inclusion, coisometric descent, residue splitting,
associativity, and subproduct criterion; [EXACT FINITE WITNESS] for the
two-state Markov calculation; [OPEN PHYSICAL REALIZATION] for the channel,
state, local net, causal cut, normalization, record interpretation, and
Yang--Mills transfer identification.**

## The pointed $W^*$-GNS carrier

Let $A$ and $B$ be von Neumann algebras and let

$$
\Phi:A\longrightarrow B
\tag{PF1}
$$

be normal and completely positive. On the algebraic tensor product
$A\odot B$, set

$$
\langle a\otimes b,a'\otimes b'\rangle_B
=b^*\Phi(a^*a')b'.
\tag{PF2}
$$

After quotienting by the null space, completing, and taking the self-dual
extension, one obtains the $W^*$-correspondence

$$
{}_A E_\Phi{}_B
=\overline{(A\odot B)/\mathcal N_\Phi}^{\,\mathrm{sd}}.
\tag{PF3}
$$

The right action is inherited from $B$, while the normal left action is

$$
\lambda_\Phi(x)[a\otimes b]=[xa\otimes b].
\tag{PF4}
$$

With

$$
\xi_\Phi=[1_A\otimes1_B],
\tag{PF5}
$$

the map is recovered as a coefficient:

$$
\boxed{
\Phi(a)=\langle\xi_\Phi,\lambda_\Phi(a)\xi_\Phi\rangle_B.}
\tag{PF6}
$$

The self-dual closed span of $A\xi_\Phi B$ is all of $E_\Phi$. If $\Phi$ is
unital, then $\langle\xi_\Phi,\xi_\Phi\rangle_B=1_B$; otherwise the
distinguished vector need not be a unit vector. This is the construction of
[[library/inner-product-modules-over-b-star-algebras/inq|Paschke]].

Given a faithful normal representation $\rho:B\to B(H_B)$, it also gives the
Stinespring carrier

$$
K_\Phi=E_\Phi\bar\otimes_\rho H_B,
\qquad
W_\Phi h=\xi_\Phi\otimes h,
\qquad
\pi_\Phi(a)=\lambda_\Phi(a)\otimes I,
\tag{PF7}
$$

for which

$$
W_\Phi^*\pi_\Phi(a)W_\Phi=\rho(\Phi(a)).
\tag{PF8}
$$

For a UCP map, $W_\Phi$ is an isometry. This changes carrier exactly once the
CP map is given; it does not derive or physically select that map.

## Variance must be declared

The same datum has several directions that must not be silently identified.

| Presentation | Direction |
|---|---|
| normal CP algebra map | $\Phi:A\to B$ |
| Paschke correspondence by its actions | $\,_A E_\Phi{}_B$ |
| state-space predual | $\Phi_*:B_*\to A_*$ |
| workspace convention $\,_{\mathrm{target}}X_{\mathrm{source}}$ | $E_\Phi$ is a one-morphism $B\to A$ |

Thus the correspondence is covariant with the algebra map if an $A$--$B$
correspondence is called an arrow $A\to B$, but contravariant under the
target-left/source-right convention used by
[[spectral-wall-descent/scale-correspondence-stack|the scale-correspondence
prestack]]. All formulas below display the left and right algebras so that no
choice of arrow vocabulary can reverse the composition.

## Composition is a cyclic inclusion

Let

$$
\Phi:A\to B,
\qquad
\Psi:B\to C
\tag{PF9}
$$

be normal CP maps and put

$$
\mathcal F_{\Phi,\Psi}
:=E_\Phi\bar\otimes_B E_\Psi.
\tag{PF10}
$$

The rule

$$
\boxed{
V_{\Phi,\Psi}:
E_{\Psi\circ\Phi}\longrightarrow\mathcal F_{\Phi,\Psi},
\qquad
V_{\Phi,\Psi}\bigl(a\xi_{\Psi\circ\Phi}c\bigr)
=a\xi_\Phi\otimes\xi_\Psi c}
\tag{PF11}
$$

extends to an isometric correspondence map. Indeed,

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\left\langle
a\xi_\Phi\otimes\xi_\Psi c,
a'\xi_\Phi\otimes\xi_\Psi c'
\right\rangle_C\\
&\qquad
=c^*\Psi\!\left(
\langle a\xi_\Phi,a'\xi_\Phi\rangle_B
\right)c'
=c^*\Psi\!\left(\Phi(a^*a')\right)c'.
\end{aligned}
\tag{PF12}
$$

Its range is exactly the self-dual closed cyclic subcorrespondence

$$
\mathcal F^{\mathrm{cyc}}_{\Phi,\Psi}
=\overline{A(\xi_\Phi\otimes\xi_\Psi)C}^{\,w^*}.
\tag{PF13}
$$

Self-duality now matters. Every bounded module map between self-dual Hilbert
modules is adjointable, so

$$
p_{\Phi,\Psi}:=V_{\Phi,\Psi}V_{\Phi,\Psi}^*
\tag{PF14}
$$

is the orthogonal projection onto the cyclic range, and

$$
\boxed{
U_{\Phi,\Psi}:=V_{\Phi,\Psi}^*:
\mathcal F_{\Phi,\Psi}\twoheadrightarrow E_{\Psi\circ\Phi}}
\tag{PF15}
$$

is a coisometry. In particular,

$$
U_{\Phi,\Psi}V_{\Phi,\Psi}=I,
\qquad
V_{\Phi,\Psi}U_{\Phi,\Psi}=p_{\Phi,\Psi}.
\tag{PF16}
$$

The **fusion residue** is

$$
\boxed{
\mathcal R_{\Phi,\Psi}
:=(I-p_{\Phi,\Psi})\mathcal F_{\Phi,\Psi}
=\ker U_{\Phi,\Psi}.}
\tag{PF17}
$$

Hence

$$
\mathcal F_{\Phi,\Psi}
=V_{\Phi,\Psi}E_{\Psi\circ\Phi}
{}\bar\oplus{}
\mathcal R_{\Phi,\Psi}.
\tag{PF18}
$$

After standard-form realization, the internal tensor product in (PF10) is
the corresponding Connes--Sauvageot fusion:

$$
\bigl(E_\Phi\bar\otimes_B L^2(B)\bigr)
\boxtimes_B
\bigl(E_\Psi\bar\otimes_C L^2(C)\bigr)
\cong
\bigl(E_\Phi\bar\otimes_BE_\Psi\bigr)
\bar\otimes_C L^2(C).
\tag{PF18a}
$$

The cyclic inclusion therefore refines, rather than replaces, the fusion law
already required by the correspondence prestack.

For norm-complete Hilbert $C^*$-modules, an isometric module map need not be
adjointable and its range need not be orthogonally complemented. Without the
self-dual $W^*$-completion, (PF11) still gives the relevant cyclic isometry
when defined, but (PF14)--(PF18) do not follow formally. This is the exact
caveat emphasized in [[library/subproduct-systems/inq|Shalit and Solel]].

## The commutative bridge specialization

For dominated commutative Markov kernels, the scalarized fused carrier
disintegrates over endpoint pairs into conditional middle-state bridge
fibers. The cyclic composite is the constant line in each bridge fiber, so
the fusion residue is its conditional-mean-zero subspace. Differentiating
the normalized bridge half-density gives a canonical residue tangent, while
a middle-slice exponential tilt gives a bounded history-sensitive analysis
of the complete slice \(L^2\) carrier. [[bridge-score-fusion-geometry/inq|Bridge-Score Fusion Geometry]]
proves the direct-integral identification, score and Fisher normalizations,
Doob-invariance formula, middle-insertion comparison with transfer defect,
and the remaining lower-frame obligation.

## Associativity and the subproduct criterion

For a third normal CP map $\Omega:C\to D$, tensor associativity identifies

$$
\boxed{
(V_{\Phi,\Psi}\otimes I)V_{\Psi\circ\Phi,\Omega}
=(I\otimes V_{\Psi,\Omega})V_{\Phi,\Omega\circ\Psi}.}
\tag{PF19}
$$

Both sides send $a\xi_{\Omega\circ\Psi\circ\Phi}d$ to
$a\xi_\Phi\otimes\xi_\Psi\otimes\xi_\Omega d$. Taking adjoints gives the
associative coisometric multiplication law.

Consequently, if $(\Theta_t)_{t\geq0}$ is a semigroup of normal contractive
CP maps on $M$, with
$\Theta_{s+t}=\Theta_t\circ\Theta_s=\Theta_s\circ\Theta_t$, then

$$
F_t:=E_{\Theta_t},
\qquad
V_{s,t}:F_{s+t}\hookrightarrow F_s\bar\otimes_MF_t,
\qquad
U_{s,t}:=V_{s,t}^*
\tag{PF20}
$$

is a cyclic inclusion system in the $V$-direction and a subproduct system in
the $U$-direction. The distinguished vectors satisfy

$$
V_{s,t}\xi_{s+t}=\xi_s\otimes\xi_t,
\qquad
\Theta_t(a)=\langle\xi_t,a\xi_t\rangle_M.
\tag{PF21}
$$

They form a unital unit exactly when the semigroup is UCP. The pointwise GNS
family is a product system precisely when

$$
\boxed{
V_{s,t}\text{ is onto}
\Longleftrightarrow
U_{s,t}\text{ is unitary}
\Longleftrightarrow
\mathcal R_{s,t}=0
\quad\text{for every }s,t.}
\tag{PF22}
$$

Theorem 3.2 of Shalit and Solel proves (PF20)--(PF21). It does not say that
the pointwise GNS fibers of an arbitrary CP semigroup already form a product
system.

## Endpoint unitarity lives in the retained corner

Write $E=E_{\Psi\circ\Phi}$, $\mathcal F=\mathcal F_{\Phi,\Psi}$,
$V=V_{\Phi,\Psi}$, $U=V^*$, and $p=VV^*$. If
$q\in\mathcal L(E)$ is unitary, then

$$
\widetilde q_p:=VqV^*\in p\mathcal L(\mathcal F)p
\tag{PF23}
$$

is canonically unitary in the retained corner, whose identity is $p$:

$$
\widetilde q_p^*\widetilde q_p
=\widetilde q_p\widetilde q_p^*=p.
\tag{PF24}
$$

Under (PF18), every unitary
$r\in\mathcal U(\mathcal L(\mathcal R_{\Phi,\Psi}))$ gives a full-carrier
extension

$$
\widehat q=(VqV^*)\bar\oplus r.
\tag{PF25}
$$

The corner lift is canonical relative to $V$; the full extension is not,
because its residue block is arbitrary. Conversely, if
$W\in\mathcal U(\mathcal L(\mathcal F))$ preserves the retained projection in
the reducing sense

$$
Wp=pW,
\tag{PF26}
$$

then

$$
\boxed{q=UWU^*}
\tag{PF27}
$$

is unitary on the endpoint. Mere forward invariance of $p\mathcal F$ is not
enough; reduction is what makes the compression surjective as well as
isometric.

Equations (PF23)--(PF27) are statements in the adjointable right-module
operator algebras. They are correspondence automorphisms only when the
chosen unitaries also intertwine the left action.

This is the coisometric version of the corner mechanism in
[[algebra/retract-corners-and-local-unitarity|retract corners and local
unitarity]]. It constrains only the retained cyclic subcorrespondence. The
block-diagonal hypothesis forbids the residue from causing the endpoint
unitary, and the free choice of $r$ shows that endpoint dynamics does not
determine residue dynamics.

## A two-state path witness

Let $M=\mathbb C^2$, let $E:M\to M$ average the two coordinates, and set

$$
\Theta_t=e^{-t}\operatorname{id}+(1-e^{-t})E.
\tag{PF28}
$$

This is a point-norm-continuous normal UCP semigroup. With the convention
$(\Theta_tf)_k=\sum_iP_t(k,i)f_i$,

$$
P_t=\frac12
\begin{pmatrix}
1+e^{-t}&1-e^{-t}\\
1-e^{-t}&1+e^{-t}
\end{pmatrix}.
\tag{PF29}
$$

For $t>0$, all four entries are positive. If $e_0,e_1$ are the minimal
projections of $M$, then the four classes $[e_i\otimes e_k]$ are nonzero and
mutually orthogonal in the GNS module, so

$$
\dim_{\mathbb C}F_t=4.
\tag{PF30}
$$

The fusion $F_s\bar\otimes_MF_t$ has one normalized basis vector for each
length-two path $i\to j\to k$, hence dimension $8$. On normalized endpoint
and path vectors, the cyclic inclusion is

$$
V_{s,t}|i,k\rangle
=\sum_{j=0}^1
\sqrt{
\frac{P_s(j,i)P_t(k,j)}{P_{s+t}(k,i)}
}
\,|i,j,k\rangle.
\tag{PF31}
$$

Chapman--Kolmogorov makes each column a unit vector. Different endpoints have
disjoint path supports, so $V_{s,t}^*V_{s,t}=I_4$, while

$$
\dim_{\mathbb C}\mathcal R_{s,t}=8-4=4.
\tag{PF32}
$$

Thus even this continuous normal UCP semigroup has proper pointwise GNS
inclusions. [[pointed-cp-fusion-residue/receipts/finite_classical_path_receipt.py|The
finite classical-path receipt]] checks the semigroup law, the isometry and
coisometry identities, the rank-four residue projection, three-step
associativity, and a sample of (PF23)--(PF27). Its
[[pointed-cp-fusion-residue/receipts/finite-classical-path-receipt-output.txt|stored
output]] records the numerical errors.

## The residue has one exact type

The fusion residue is the orthogonal complement of the cyclic carrier for a
composite CP coefficient map. Nothing in its definition supplies the extra
structures in the following ledger.

| Candidate identification | Exact mismatch | Additional datum required |
|---|---|---|
| Hilbert transfer defect $I-A^*A$ | positive operator on the input of a declared contraction; $\mathcal R_{\Phi,\Psi}$ is a subcorrespondence of a two-step fusion carrier | an explicit unitary or quadratic-form comparison; see [[directed-isometric-residue-completion/inq|directed isometric residue completion]] |
| entropy or information loss | residue dimension and norm are state-free and trace-free | a normal state and an admissible trace, weight, relative entropy, or instrument |
| physical clock | the CP-semigroup parameter labels composition of channels | a reconstructed strongly continuous automorphism or unitary group, with physical calibration |
| record or fact | $\ker U$ is not an obtained outcome and has no persistence law | an instrument, selected outcome or character, record algebra, and stable record inclusion |
| expectation or Q-system | GNS realizes a CP map already supplied; it does not select that map from a bare correspondence | inclusion-plus-expectation, Q-system, or another explicit channel constructor |
| causal wall | no regions, isotony, locality, cut, or area appear in (PF1)--(PF22) | the local net, state family, causal cut, source map, and independent normalization required by [[minimal-cosmodynamic-closure/transport-provider-ledger|the transport-provider ledger]] |
| Yang--Mills transfer or mass gap | the two-state witness is a finite classical Markov channel, not an OS-positive gauge transfer matrix | a gauge-field measure, reflection positivity, continuum reconstruction, physical Hilbert carrier, Hamiltonian identification, and coercive spectral bound |

In particular, endpoint unitarity is compatible with a nonzero
fusion residue but is neither generated nor causally oriented by that
residue. Likewise, the coisometric endpoint map is exact descent of a cyclic
coefficient carrier, not [[program-core/physical-quotient|the physical
quotient]], [[trace-dirichlet-descent/inq|Dirichlet trace descent]], or factual
forgetting. Those identifications remain separate theorem targets.
