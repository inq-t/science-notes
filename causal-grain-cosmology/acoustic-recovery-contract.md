# Acoustic Recovery Contract

A causal-grain explanation of the acoustic sky must construct a primordial state and hot history, then return the observed sound-horizon ratio and its correlated temperature, polarization, lensing, and matter readouts. The existing frozen test constrains one acoustic coordinate inside standard transfer physics. Deriving the acoustic operator, controlling post-search choices, and predicting a genuinely uninspected observable remain separate obligations.

The [[causal-grain-cosmology/acoustic-characteristic-and-chiral-calibration|candidate characteristic]] fixes one compressed acoustic scalar. The stronger target here is a state and history that determine the related readouts together.

## Perturbation need not mean ontic randomness

The early [[causal-wall-spectral-theory/whole-state-correlation-reading|whole-state reading]] predates the grain. It observes that a covariance can be the relational geometry of one globally constrained state; it need not describe independent little kicks with no determining structure. Standard cosmological perturbation equations already evolve each mode deterministically once a coherent growing-mode state and its statistics are supplied.

The new proposal strengthens that research direction:

$$
\text{one global causal state}
\longrightarrow
\text{one primordial covariance and phase relation}
\longrightarrow
\text{different TT, TE, EE, lensing, and matter readouts}.
$$

It does not yet prove that observed fluctuations are nonrandom in every sense. It still owes the scalar amplitude, tilt, higher correlations, outcome statistics, Born probabilities, and the formation of definite records. The live-cut grain identity

$$
\Delta_\zeta^2(k)
=\frac{1}{\epsilon_H(k)\iota_H(k)}
=\frac{3}{8\pi\epsilon_H(k)}
\left(\frac{\lambda_g}{R_H}\right)^3
$$

is presently a conditional rewriting of imported single-clock physics, not a new amplitude prediction. The detailed calculation is in [[causal-grain-cosmology/junk-drawer/source-records/causal-grain-cmb-spectroscopy/grain-primordial-precision.md|Grain Ratio as Primordial Precision]].

The absence of a derivation of the Born rule is not, by itself, a reason to discard a sharply economical law. The proper demand is stricter and more empirical: freeze the law, prevent data leakage, and ask whether it returns more independent structure than it consumed.

## The noncircular test

The programme distinguishes the following construction and validation steps. [[causal-grain-cosmology/cmb-likelihood-test|The recorded frozen test]] supplies the current empirical result; a genuinely held-out validation remains open:

1. derive or explicitly postulate one oriented acoustic operator with characteristic

   $$
   \mathscr P_{3,+}(q)=q\left(q^2+\frac83\right);
   $$

2. construct the unit-wall map identifying one primitive response interval with \(\delta\sigma_g=1\);
3. derive the spectral label \(L_g=\Sigma_c+3\) rather than selecting it from the observed difference;
4. fix \(Q_c\) from a wall construction or the frozen local chiral input without using the acoustic data under test;
5. replace the standard acoustic parameter in a full TT/TE/EE+lensing likelihood, with the same nuisance model;
6. reserve at least one genuinely uninspected polarization, BAO, lensing, or future acoustic determination as the holdout; and
7. forbid branch switching between the economical chiral prescription and the closer pion-midpoint prescription after unblinding.

Step 5 has now been partially executed in two transparent spectrum-transfer likelihoods and approximated for the official full Planck stack through its archived chain geometry. The newly run Planck fit omits low-\(\ell\), the lensing likelihood, and explicit Plik nuisances; ACT has no separate lensing likelihood; and the full Planck result remains a Gaussian surrogate rather than a constrained minimization. Step 6 remains entirely open: ACT is an independent instrument but was already known to the search, so it is a cross-instrument consistency check rather than a blind holdout.

A compact theorem target is

$$

(\Sigma_c,d,\delta\sigma_g,\zeta)
\longmapsto
(q_*,\mathscr P_{3,+}).
$$

A stronger hot-history map must return
