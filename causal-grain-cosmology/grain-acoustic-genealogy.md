# Grain Acoustic Genealogy

The common-count grain, its rank-two coefficient, the chiral-scale comparison, and the acoustic cubic entered the programme at different stages. This provenance distinguishes conditional derivation from subsequent structural choices and numerical postdiction: the grain was not tuned to the acoustic angle, but its quoted absolute value used a CMB-conditioned crossing rate, and the acoustic characteristic was selected after inspecting its target.

## Working backward from the grain

The repository trail is more informative backward than forward because each prior note explains one factor in the final coefficient. The raw [[causal-grain-cosmology/junk-drawer/source-records/causal-grain-cmb-spectroscopy/repository-genealogy.md|Repository Genealogy of the Causal-Scale Grain]] owns the detailed claim-by-claim inheritance; the chain below gives its argumentative line.

### The canonical metric grain is a common-count scale

[[the-grain-of-causal-scale/inq#The common-count lemma|The common-count lemma]] owns the areal and spherical bulk-cell ledgers, their saturation hypothesis, and the resulting coefficient

$$
\lambda_*^3=\frac{4\gamma s_*}{3}\ell_P^2R_c.
$$

This is a conditional effective cell scale; a microscopic correlation length, pole length, particle radius, or cutoff requires a separate carrier or two-point theorem. Historically, [[causal-grain-cosmology/junk-drawer/source-records/the-ledger-level/the-ledger-level.md|the ledger-level note]] first reread the horizon-area count as a Hubble-bulk correlation count rather than merely as the familiar \(10^{122}\) hierarchy.

### The denominator three is geometry

[[bulk-area-cell-normalization/inq|Bulk–area cell normalization]] owns the three-ball identity \(V/(AR)=1/3\), which gives \(\zeta=\gamma s_*/3\). The denominator comes from ordinary spatial geometry; it is not an octonionic multiplicity, an information convention, or a fitted cosmological coefficient.

### The one is a unit response, not one bit

The raw [[causal-grain-cosmology/junk-drawer/source-records/the-unit-nat-channel/the-unit-nat-channel.md|The Unit-Nat Channel and the $\gamma$ Ladder]] composes the unit rate and unit matching clauses

$$
\mathfrak R_c=\frac{\nu^2}{s_*},
\qquad
(\nu,\mathfrak R_c)=(1,1),
$$

and therefore returns

$$
s_*=1.
$$

Under the unit-rate exponential/KMS presentation, the weight \(s_*=1\) can be written as one nat because logarithms linearize multiplicative composition. That is an optional entropy presentation, not the definition of the response weight. It is also not a hidden qubit count: one qubit has capacity \(\ln2\) nats, while the later factor \(2\) is a proposed multiplicity of positive area-writing channels. Response weight, channel number, entropy, and number of binary outcomes remain different types.

### The two is an \(A_2\) positive-support proposal

The raw [[inbox/the-rank-two-cell/the-rank-two-cell]] proposes that one effective causal cell is represented by the positive part of a trace-zero triple. [[algebra/a2-positive-completion|The \(A_2\) positive completion]] supplied the antecedent finite geometry; its \(\mathbb C^3\), \(\mathbb C^2\), and \(\mathbb C\) fiber types are not themselves a canonical chain of wall homomorphisms. The later [[the-grain-of-causal-scale/causal-spectrum|causal-spectrum canonicalization]] made one support jump exact: at the Schwarzschild--de Sitter parameter crossing \(m=0\), using a declared negative-\(m\) analytic extension, strict-positive support changes from rank one to rank two. This is neither the physical cosmological crossing nor historical evidence used to choose \(\gamma\). The ordinary positive-support count is two, and its normalized trace in the three-state carrier is \(2/3\).

The proposed physical identification is

$$
\gamma=2.
$$

This does not follow merely from the phrase “\(A_2\) has rank two.” The Lie-theoretic rank of \(A_2\), the two positive Schwarzschild--de Sitter radii, and the rank-two strict-positive support are three different facts. A trace-compatible wall-to-area correspondence must prove that the positive spectral slots are precisely the area-writing channels counted by the common-count law. Until that map exists, \(\gamma=2\) is a principled discrete postulate with an exact finite model, not a theorem of nature.

### The \(A_2\) triple sharpened the broad grain window

The broad femtometre window appeared first. The Jordan and de Sitter notes then supplied the \(A_2\) organization before the final rank-two \(4.2\,\mathrm{fm}\) value was fixed. The raw notes [[inbox/black-holes-as-jordan-spectra/black-holes-as-jordan-spectra]] and [[inbox/de-sitter-box-and-the-octonionic-ladder/de-sitter-box-and-the-octonionic-ladder]] found the same trace-zero cubic organization in two places:

- the trace-free characteristic cubic of elements of the exceptional Jordan algebra \(\mathfrak h_3(\mathbb O)\); and
- the Schwarzschild--de Sitter horizon polynomial, with two positive horizons and one negative balancing root.

Both carry the three-root, six-difference, \(W(A_2)\cong S_3\) structure. During the grain search this supplied a candidate reason why a three-entry carrier might have exactly two positive geometric channels. That promotion to \(\gamma=2\) is a post-search structural choice relative to the already visible scale window, not an independent confirmation of it. [[inbox/the-box-spectrum-functor/the-box-spectrum-functor]] records the missing proof that these appearances are presentations of one invariant construction.

### The scale first appeared while trying to derive \(G\)

The numerical ancestor of the grain was not a CMB harmonic. [[deriving-g-v2/closure-family-and-kills|The closure-family audit]] found, while searching for a noncircular route to Newton's constant,

$$
\lambda_*^3=4\zeta\ell_P^2R_c,
$$

and therefore a broad \(40\)--\(63\,\mathrm{MeV}\) matter-ruler window. The exponent \(a=1\) in that monomial family was not selected by geometry alone: it was named the “Fossil Weinberg” branch and was already compared with chiral/QCD scales. Thus QCD numbers do not occur in the displayed closure identity, but QCD-scale awareness belongs to its branch-selection provenance. [[deriving-g-v2/rulers-are-matter|Rulers are matter]] had already made the conceptual demand: an absolute length cannot emerge from scale-free geometry without a physical ruler. The subsequent ledger, carrier, unit-nat, and rank-two notes explained the formerly free \(\zeta\) as

$$
\zeta=\frac{\gamma s_*}{3}=\frac23.
$$

The earlier [[deriving-value-of-g/inq|\(G\)-derivation programme]] had also imposed the decisive anti-circular standard. Rewriting the Einstein area law in response units is only a retyping of measured \(G\) unless a state construction independently returns the same areal modulus. The grain inherits that standard.

The legacy Causal Scale Dynamics package preserved under `causal-scale-theory/sources/legacy/` already supplied the binary BKM wall, unit-response crossing, and scale-state solder. The current [[causal-scale-theory/inq|Causal Scale Theory]] entry is a later canonical synthesis that organizes the homogeneous branch as

Its binary BKM member supplied the exact complementarity

$$

and the intensive equality \(g(0)=1\) at the balanced crossing. It does not by itself imply the extensive channel weight \(s_*=1\); that later conclusion also uses unit rate, unit matching, and channel additivity. Historically, [[causal-wall-spectral-theory/inq|Causal-Wall Spectral Theory]] began after legacy CSD but before the current CST synthesis. It asked whether the nonconstant response of one global state could descend to primordial spatial precision. Once a positive, isotropic, dilation-covariant precision operator on a three-dimensional cut exists, [[critical-scale-kernels/inq|critical scale kernels]] prove its principal symbol is proportional to \(|k|^3\).

The early synoptic theories therefore already contained the later ingredients in uncombined form: unit scale response, a finite wall, whole-state correlation, logarithmic scale, and critical order three. What they did not contain was the common-count grain or the acoustic characteristic.
