# Causal-Grain Cosmology

Causal-grain cosmology asks whether one conditional scale construction can constrain an acoustic observable or leave a material-history fossil. The first branch combines common-count calibration with a post-search chiral acoustic characteristic; its frozen Planck and ACT tests remove one continuous acoustic coordinate at little likelihood cost inside standard transfer physics. The second branch seeks a map from a directed wall class to rest-bearing matter and then to observable acoustic memory. Calibration, trace history, baryon loading, entropy production, and spectral mass remain distinct quantities, and neither branch derives a Yang–Mills gap or the complete acoustic sky.

## From conditional calibration to an acoustic characteristic

[[the-grain-of-causal-scale/inq|The common-count grain]] owns the conditional law \(\lambda_*^3=(8/3)\ell_P^2R_c\), with its spherical-cell, unit-response and rank-two assumptions. [[causal-grain-cosmology/grain-acoustic-genealogy|The grain–acoustic genealogy]] records how those ingredients preceded the later acoustic search, while retaining the QCD-scale awareness and CMB-conditioned normalization in their provenance. [[causal-grain-cosmology/causal-grain-and-local-rulers|Causal grain and local rulers]] distinguishes the proposed primitive realization from its dimensional presentations and defines

$$
Q_c=\frac{R_c}{\lambda_*},\qquad
\Sigma_c=\ln Q_c,\qquad
q_*:=\frac{D_M(z_*)}{r_s(z_*)}.
$$

A direct insertion of \(\lambda_*\) into the ordinary acoustic phase cancels. The [[causal-grain-cosmology/acoustic-characteristic-and-chiral-calibration|acoustic characteristic]] instead conjectures

$$
q_*\left(q_*^2+\frac83\right)
\stackrel{?}{=}(\Sigma_c+3)^3.
$$

Its primary chiral package supplies \(Q_c\) from measured \(G\) and a frozen laboratory pion-decay input. The cubic was selected after inspecting the acoustic target. [[causal-grain-cosmology/exceptional-scale-phase-descent|Exceptional scale-phase descent]] gives exact algebraic precedents for the candidate; selecting the physical descent and deriving its acoustic image remain open.

## Empirical return and the remaining recovery

[[causal-grain-cosmology/cmb-likelihood-test|The first frozen likelihood test]] reports the paired Planck and ACT TT/TE/EE fits. Fixing the primary physical acoustic angle reduces the continuous base freedom from six to five, with recorded \(\Delta\chi^2\simeq0.06\)--\(0.34\); the full Planck-chain comparison is a Gaussian surrogate. The nuisance treatment is held fixed within each pair. These tests establish a narrow parameter reduction, with nominal conditional AIC benefit, before accounting for post-search choices. They do not independently predict peak heights, damping, primordial amplitude or tilt, or the full spectra.

[[causal-grain-cosmology/acoustic-recovery-contract|The acoustic recovery contract]] separates the missing operator and primordial-state constructions from a prospectively frozen observational test. ACT supplies cross-instrument consistency, but was already known during the search. A galaxy-BAO holdout requires its own frozen mapping, statistic and nuisance model. The [[causal-scale-theory/data-consistency|separate DESI BAO-plus-supernova CST test]] profiles the background scale \(c/(H_0r_d)\); it does not test the grain characteristic.

## Mass engagement, trace history, and acoustic fossils

[[causal-grain-cosmology/causal-grain-as-a-mass-engagement-fossil|Mass engagement]] asks for two descendants of one wall class: a common-count calibration and a transported material history. A wall index does not itself generate a positive physical Casimir edge. The late-time crossing addresses used for calibration are also not early mass-generation dates.

[[causal-grain-cosmology/pair-annihilation-quotient-and-the-baryon-acoustic-carrier|The baryon pair quotient]] supplies a material template: pair reactions preserve a charge excess, while a separately supplied mass-per-charge map turns survivor magnitude into acoustic loading. Standard acoustic transfer forgets its sign and detailed annihilation history. [[causal-grain-cosmology/trace-residue-as-a-scale-cocycle|The trace-residue cocycle]] instead records an additive stress history; neither a nonzero residue nor an entropy change guarantees that the history survives the finite acoustic readout.

The [[cosmological-scale-selection/wall-crossing-defect-and-the-fossil-of-mass-engagement|wall-crossing defect]] tests whether a change of realization leaves an observable image after transport. [[crossing-evaluated-flat-modulus/inq|A crossing-evaluated calibration fossil]], a trace-history functional and an acoustic record therefore require different maps. Their common origin remains a construction problem.

## Diagnostic scales retain their inputs

[[causal-grain-cosmology/standard-model-trace-fossil-diagnostic|The thermal trace diagnostic]] and the pair-exposure calculation test specific thermal readings of the grain. Their negative controls do not date the primitive wall event. [[causal-grain-cosmology/numerical-diagnostics|Horizon-ratio diagnostics]] distinguish late-time calibration, equality cuts, geometric degeneracy and dissipation. [[causal-grain-cosmology/mass-scale-rhyme-sweep|The mass-scale rhyme sweep]] retains a numerical search over supplied physical scales and the limits of its interpretation.
