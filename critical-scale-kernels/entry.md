# Critical Scale Kernels

A dimensionless scalar on a homogeneous, isotropic three-dimensional carrier has an inverse-covariance kernel of degree three when exact dilation covariance is imposed. This module isolates that classification, its conditional curved \(P_3\) representative, the fixed-point trace obstruction, and the limited conclusions that tilt, running, and unrestricted positive response functions support; it does not identify that carrier with a horizon cut or any scalar with a physical wall or cosmological perturbation.

Begin with the exact operator distinction in [[basic-concepts/hessians/fourier-covariance-and-precision|Fourier covariance and precision]]. Once translation invariance has diagonalized a positive precision and the constant or other null modes have been handled by an independently justified physical quotient, [[flat-weight-zero-precision|the flat weight-zero theorem]] gives

$$
\mathcal K_\zeta(k)=C|k|^3,
\qquad C\geq0,
$$

from exact spatial dilation covariance. The theorem selects a shape class. It supplies neither the coefficient, the physical field, nor the dynamics that prepare its covariance.

On a suitable conformal three-manifold with declared filling or scattering data, [[curved-p3-representative|the fractional \(P_3\) construction]] is a natural curved representative. Its flat symbol and round-sphere spectrum reproduce the critical degree-three behavior. Filling dependence, self-adjoint domain, kernel, and positivity remain part of the construction rather than consequences of the name *conformal*.

A separate issue arises when the proposed source is the trace of a three-dimensional stress tensor. [[trace-fixed-point-null-no-go|The trace-fixed-point no-go]] shows that a strict improved conformal fixed point supplies no nonlocal scalar trace response under the stated hypotheses. If precision is identified solely with that response, it becomes null rather than invertible. The null direction is not automatically gauge; an independent constraint or quotient theorem is required before it may be discarded as redundant.

Away from the exact critical point, [[tilt-and-running-identities|the tilt and running identities]] relate logarithmic variation of a positive response coefficient to the scalar spectral index. A constant exponent gives zero running because that member assumes a constant exponent. No general \(\delta^2\) running bound follows from critical scaling alone.

Finally, [[unrestricted-response-no-go|the unrestricted-response no-go]] records the empirical boundary. An arbitrary positive response function can encode any positive scalar two-point spectrum. Predictive content begins only when independent structure computes the function, restricts it to a genuine finite-dimensional family, or relates it to tensor, higher-point, background, or other observables.

The causal-wall programme may consume these results after it has identified its physical scalar and response carrier. Those identifications remain downstream obligations governed by [[program-core/entry|the programme core]] and [[wall-construction-interface/entry|the wall-construction interface]].
