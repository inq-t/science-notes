# Paired-Wall Casimir Comparison

Two complementary distinction responses can have gapless marginals and a positive joint product. A physical application requires strongly commuting operators on one complete complex carrier, a lower bound for their product, and an independently normalized comparison with the full Poincare Casimir. Reciprocal scaling alone establishes an invariant of that one-parameter action, not a physical mass or a Poincare scalar.

## Why the solder must target the joint invariant

[[mass-scale-calibration/joint-causal-generators-and-the-mass-casimir|The joint-causal-generator theorem]] owns the spectral argument: exact same-carrier scaling of a nonzero positive null generator forces its spectrum down to zero, while a strongly commuting complementary generator can scale oppositely and leave their product invariant. Its rapidity-space witness has two gapless marginals and fixed positive product.

In natural units in \(1+1\) dimensions, or on a declared zero-transverse-momentum sector, put \(H=(P_++P_-)/2\) and \(M^2=P_+P_-\) for strongly commuting positive self-adjoint generators. Let \(P_0\) reduce both and let both annihilate its range. The canonical theorem's joint-functional-calculus argument gives

$$
M^2\geq m_*^2(1-P_0)
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
H\geq m_*(1-P_0).
\tag{D19a}
$$

In \(3+1\) dimensions, \(P_\pm=H\pm cP_z\) instead give \(P_+P_-=\mathcal C+c^2\mathbf P_\perp^2\). A fixed directional product is not the full Casimir. After positive-energy Poincare reconstruction, however, a floor for one physical null-pair product on the **entire** vacuum complement implies an energy gap and hence a full Casimir floor by [[mass-scale-calibration/joint-causal-generators-and-the-mass-casimir#Energy gap and Casimir floor are equivalent only after Lorentz reconstruction|the Lorentz-orbit theorem]]. An abstract distinction product still needs a noncircular same-carrier comparison with that physical pair, an all-direction family, or directly with \(\mathcal C\).

## The paired-wall operator signature

The same reversal can be stated one level before energy. Suppose two complementary wall constructions yield retained-output response forms that have been pulled back, complexified, and identified with one common complete complex physical carrier. Assume a positive-energy Poincare representation on that carrier, with Hamiltonian $H$, full energy-squared Casimir $\mathcal C=H^2-c^2\mathbf P^2\geq0$, and vacuum projection $P_0$. Assume their closed forms are represented there by strongly commuting positive self-adjoint **dimensionless** operators $K_+$ and $K_-$, each annihilating the vacuum. Their product is then the positive self-adjoint operator defined by joint spectral calculus. Only after this common-carrier theorem does $K_+K_-$ have a meaning.

If a unitary same-carrier scale or boost action satisfies

$$
U_sK_+U_s^*=e^sK_+,
\qquad
U_sK_-U_s^*=e^{-s}K_-,
\tag{D19b}
$$

then either directional operator may have spectrum down to zero, while

$$
U_s(K_+K_-)U_s^*=K_+K_-.
$$

Let $P_0$ reduce $H$, the full Poincare Casimir $\mathcal C$, and $K_+K_-$. On the common form domain

$$
\mathcal D_{\leftrightarrow}
:=
\operatorname{Dom}\!\left((K_+K_-)^{1/2}\right)
\cap
\operatorname{Dom}(\mathcal C^{1/2}),
$$

assume the **paired completeness** estimate

$$
\left\|(K_+K_-)^{1/2}\Psi\right\|^2
\geq
\kappa_{\leftrightarrow}^2
\left\|(1-P_0)\Psi\right\|^2
\tag{D19c}
$$

and a separately normalized same-carrier Casimir solder

$$
\left\|\mathcal C^{1/2}\Psi\right\|^2
\geq
\eta_{\leftrightarrow}E_*^2
\left\|(K_+K_-)^{1/2}\Psi\right\|^2.
\tag{D19d}
$$

for every $\Psi\in\mathcal D_{\leftrightarrow}$, with $\kappa_{\leftrightarrow},\eta_{\leftrightarrow},E_*>0$. If this domain is a core for the closed quadratic form of $\mathcal C$, positivity of the translation spectrum gives the **[CONDITIONAL EXACT IMPLICATION]**

$$
H
\geq
E_*\sqrt{\eta_{\leftrightarrow}}\,
\kappa_{\leftrightarrow}(1-P_0).
\tag{D19e}
$$

The two independent estimates are the substantive construction target. The directional $K_\pm$ operate on physical distinction tangents, not on spacetime; $P_\pm$ operate on the physical Hilbert space as translation generators; and $\mathcal C$ is their joint Poincare invariant. Invariance of $K_+K_-$ under (D19b) alone does not make it a full Poincare scalar. Equations (D19c) and (D19d) do not identify these layers. They state the two missing theorems: complementary walls must leave no jointly soft nonvacuum direction, and their invariant distinction product must be controlled by the physical mass Casimir without using the desired spectrum to normalize it.

The product route differs from [[trace-dirichlet-descent/shorted-response-filtration-and-the-leak-cocycle#Paired walls produce an exact tangential response|the sum-then-short two-projection theorem]]. That theorem relaxes hidden lifts after adding two incoming losses on one source; it does not assert strong commutation or construct the product $K_+K_-$. Either mechanism still requires its own physical carrier and energy comparison. The more general [[physical-response-coercivity/retained-output-casimir-bound|retained-output Casimir bound]] accepts a declared response form without assuming this product factorization.
